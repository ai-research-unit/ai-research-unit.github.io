The Iota Problem in Physics: Remaining Occurrences

The comparison map no longer carries a symbol. A rescan of all 403 articles in the 13 categories of physics.md finds 3 occurrences of \iota in 2 articles. Both are standard notations unrelated to the biquaternion–Clifford dictionary. No \iota_{\mathrm{Cl}} remains anywhere in physics, no bare $\iota$ remains, and no Unicode iota remains.

The interior product is the standard symbol $\iota_X\omega$ and has nothing to do with the biquaternion algebra. The inner automorphism $\iota_\lambda$ is the identity because $\lambda$ is central. Neither is a defect.

Eleven categories use no $\iota$ at all.

What Was Removed

The symbol of the comparison map was removed from the eight physics articles that carried it. The map itself remains, as the eight-row table in Matrix Representations Conventions and as the phrase "the Clifford image". Its values are unchanged.

Of the 152, 129 were applied to an argument and 23 were bare mentions. Every applied occurrence was replaced by its value, by an arrow $X\mapsto Y$, or by the phrase "the Clifford image of $X$". Every bare mention became "the reading". The table header in Matrix Representations Conventions now reads "Clifford image".

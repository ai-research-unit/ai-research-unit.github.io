The Iota Problem in Physics: Closure

The physics corpus uses no \iota. A rescan of all 403 articles in the 13 categories of physics.md finds the letter only in the paragraph that bans it, in the notation conventions of Unit, Index and Notation Conventions.

What Was Removed

Object Spelling Occurrences Articles Replacement
Comparison $\mathbb{B}\to\mathrm{Cl}^{+}_{1,3}$ \iota_{\mathrm{Cl}} 152 8 values, arrows, or "the Clifford image"
Interior product \iota_{X_f} 2 1 $\omega(X_f,\,\cdot\,)=df$
Inner automorphism \iota_\lambda 1 1 the words "inner automorphism"
Total 155 10

The comparison map survives as content and loses its symbol. Its eight values remain in the table of Matrix Representations Conventions, whose header now reads "Clifford image". The other two symbols were standard notation for objects unrelated to the biquaternion algebra, and both were written out.

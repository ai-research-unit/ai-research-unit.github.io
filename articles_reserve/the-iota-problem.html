The Iota Problem in Physics: Rescan and Closure

The physics corpus is clean. A rescan of all 403 articles in the 13 categories of physics.md finds 155 occurrences of \iota in 10 articles, in three spellings, and every one of them has a single reading. No bare $\iota$ remains, no Unicode iota remains, and no article mentions the letter in prose.

The eight $\iota_{\mathrm{Cl}}$ articles hold 152 occurrences. Each defines or cites one map, the $\mathbb{R}$-linear comparison of the biquaternion algebra with the even subalgebra $\mathrm{Cl}^{+}_{1,3}$, and none gives the symbol a second object. The two occurrences of $\iota_{X_f}$ are the interior product, and the single $\iota_\lambda$ is the inner automorphism, the identity because $\lambda$ is central.

Seven categories use no $\iota$ at all.

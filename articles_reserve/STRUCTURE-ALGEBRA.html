The Algebra Block — New Structure

The block ends where a pairing of two different elements begins. Every article below uses the plain product, the coefficients, and the characteristic polynomial. No article pairs $Q_1$ with $Q_2$ and takes a scalar part. The reduced norm appears only as the constant term of $p_Q$, never as a form.

Four sections: the object, the frame, the modules, the element.

Article 1 owns the product, the axioms, the centre and the presentation. It proves non-division by the explicit product $(e_t+ie_x)(e_t-ie_x)=0$; no norm is named.

Article 2 owns the scalar–vector split, the three coordinate systems, the four conjugations and $\mathbb{C}[Q]=\operatorname{span}\{e_t,Q\}$. It states that the centraliser is two-dimensional for a non-central element but does not identify it with $\mathbb{C}[X]/(p_Q)$: that needs $p_Q$, in article 22, and is done in article 25.

Article 3 keeps the Klein group, its table and its orbits; the four-map definitions moved to article 2.

Article 4 loses §The Roots of Zero and Plus One and §The Idempotents and the Ideals in the Remarkable Subspaces; both are re-homed in §2 and §3. It keeps the six definitions, the bases, the dimensions and the derivation $4\times2-2=6$: the four non-identity conjugations give eight eigenspaces, and the two of the reversal $\flat$ coincide with those of ${}^{*}$.

Article 5 owns intersections, sums and the lattice. Article 6 owns the three eigenspace decompositions.

Article 7 states $Q^2=(Q_t^2-B^2)e_t+2Q_t\vec Q$, the cone $B^2=0$, and the three equations with their destinations: $Q^2=0\to13$, $Q^2=Q\to14$, $Q^2=A\to35$.

Article 8 states $QQ^{\natural}=(Q_t^2+B^2)e_t$, unit $\iff QQ^{\nu}\neq0$, the two families ($Q_t=0$ nilpotent; $Q_t\neq0$ a scalar multiple of the idempotent $Q/(2Q_t)$), and the chain to the minimal left ideal and the spinor. It is the only article in §1 that counts zero divisors.

The matrix-product pair 9–10 closes the algebra proper. It reads the product, the coefficients and the unit group. The six-subspace model pair 11–12 follows it, because it assumes the matrix product. Subtitles mirror; only the model label changes.

Deviations from the 29-article list

Three, all forced by the corpus.

  1. The Four Conjugations and the Six Subspaces are two articles, not one. The merged article would be 10,700 words, and the two existing articles have different subjects (the group and its orbits; the six fixed spaces). +1.
  2. The Six Subspaces keeps its model pair. Two existing articles, 3,800 words, already read it. Dropping them would delete work; keeping them adds a pair. +2.
  3. Two form-free subspace articles rejoin the block: The Lattice of the Subspaces and The Three Eigenspace Decompositions. Both use only the conjugations. Leaving them in the old Remarkable Subspaces section would split the symmetries across two sections. +2.

The Centraliser is unchanged in count: it was article 21 of the approved list and is article 25 here. The five additions are in §1; The Spinor Spaces is an addition in §2.

Total: 35 articles: 12 in §1, 6 in §2, 3 in §3, 14 in §4, plus the unnumbered worked examples.

The 29-article version is obtained by merging 3–4, dropping 11–12, and folding 5–6 into 4. That version is shorter and loses four finished articles.

The rules

  1. Boundary. No article pairs two different elements. The reduced norm is the constant term of $p_Q$, not a form.
  2. Companions. A pair is one subtitle with its $M_2$ and $M_4$ articles. Pairs close the group they read. Subtitles mirror; only the model label changes.
  3. Equations. Article 7 sends $Q^2=0\to13$, $Q^2=Q\to14$, $Q^2=A\to35$. No other article announces them.
  4. Naming. One name per concept; the owner is the article that states it, not every article that uses it.
  5. Forward destinations. Real forms, adjoints, polar decompositions, the four products and the Killing form go to the forms block. The block names them; it does not define them.

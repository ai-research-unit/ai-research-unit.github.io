Charon: The Massless Internal Neutrino

Introduction

This article records a claim that reaches further into the corpus than any other falsified statement. Charon's neutrino has rigorously zero proper mass, and that one fact holds up the préon, the éon, the charge mechanism and the weak boson. Neutrino oscillations, established between 1998 and 2002, show the neutrino has a non-zero mass. Removing the massless neutrino does not damage those constructions; it deletes them.

The claim, verbatim. RC 030, §II.2.1: "Nous avons vu que $RC$ admet pour le photon (et d'ailleurs aussi pour le neutrino) une masse propre rigoureusement nulle." The corpus states it identically wherever it matters — RC 045 on the éonic neutrino's spin ("de masse propre rigoureusement nulle en Relativité complexe"), RC 049 on the préon's validity for "un neutrino ou un photon, qui en Relativité complexe ont tous deux une masse propre $m$ rigoureusement nulle", and Esp §V.4.1.c.

The verdict is [FALS]. Super-Kamiokande (1998) established atmospheric neutrino oscillations; SNO (2001–02) established the solar neutrino flavour change; KamLAND (2002) confirmed it with reactor antineutrinos; the 2015 Nobel Prize followed. Oscillations require a non-zero mass-squared difference, measured as $\Delta m^{2}_{21} \approx 7{,}5\times10^{-5}$ eV$^{2}$ and $|\Delta m^{2}_{31}| \approx 2{,}5\times10^{-3}$ eV$^{2}$. Cosmology bounds the sum of the three masses below about $0{,}12$ eV. The neutrinos are very light, and not massless.

A note on sources. The claim is inventory §3.7, sourced to RC ch. II (RC/030_improved.md) and Esp §V.4.1.c, and its consequences to RC 048, 049 and 030. The downstream findings are S17's (the préon) and S19's (the charge). The related articles are P7 (the $W$/$Z$ pair, built from these neutrinos) and P3 (the éon).

Conventions. Gaussian cgs. $m_{\nu}$ the neutrino proper mass; $\Delta m^{2}$ the mass-squared splittings; $R$ the micro-universe radius and $R'$ its radial velocity; $\alpha$, $\beta$ the corpus's internal constants; $\Lambda$ the cosmological term in the micro-universe.

Part 1 — Where the zero mass is load-bearing

The massless neutrino is not a detail of the corpus. It is a premise of four separate constructions, and the corpus says so each time.

The préon. RC 048, deriving the préon: the micro-universe "n'est donc constitué d'aucun élément de masse propre non nulle", so it starts at $R' = \pm c$ and "cet état $R' = \pm c$ se maintient quel que soit $\tau$, puisque seul le neutrino de masse propre nulle est présent dans le micro-universe pendant toute la durée de sa pulsation radiale". The conservation of $R' = \pm c$ — hence $\Lambda = 0$, hence the whole préon solution — rests on the neutrino's zero mass. S17's audit is explicit: the préon is the solution with $\alpha = \beta = \Lambda = 0$, and Λ vanishes because the neutrino has no mass.

And the préon is universal. RC 052 superposes the préon on every particle. S17's finding is therefore that the removal is not local: there is no particle in the corpus that does not carry a préon, and no préon once the neutrino has a mass.

The éon. The éon is a micro-universe containing one neutrino on a closed quantised orbit (RC 045, inventory §3.2). The orbit's existence, the spin states of the internal neutrino, and the energy $W_{n}$ all treat the neutrino as massless.

The charge. The electron's charge is blackbody radiation trapped in the éon (inventory §3.3), and the mechanism's infra-red cut-off $x_{1} = 1{,}9103$ and its coupling $e^{2}/\hbar c = 7{,}608\times10^{-3}$ are computed for a massless internal neutrino. S19 recomputes the chain and confirms it; the chain's inputs include the zero mass.

The weak boson. P7's $(\nu_{e}, \nu_{\mu})$ intermediate boson is a pair of these neutrinos. With massive neutrinos the pair could in principle carry a mass, but the corpus gives it none.

Part 2 — What oscillations established

The refutation is the most thoroughly confirmed later result in the article set.

experiment year what it showed
Super-Kamiokande 1998 atmospheric $\nu_{\mu}$ disappearance: $\nu_{\mu} \to \nu_{\tau}$
SNO 2001–02 solar $\nu_{e}$ appearance as $\nu_{\mu}/\nu_{\tau}$
KamLAND 2002 reactor $\bar{\nu}_{e}$ disappearance, the solar splitting
K2K, MINOS, T2K 2004– accelerator confirmation
Planck + BAO 2018 $\sum m_{\nu} < 0{,}12$ eV

Why oscillations require mass. A neutrino produced in one flavour state is a superposition of mass eigenstates. If the masses were equal, the superposition would propagate with a common phase and the flavour would not change. A non-zero mass-squared difference makes the mass eigenstates accumulate phase at different rates, and the flavour oscillates. The observation of oscillation is therefore a direct proof that the mass-squared differences are non-zero — which is incompatible with all three masses being zero. Measured: $\Delta m^{2}_{21} \approx 7{,}5\times10^{-5}$ eV$^{2}$, $|\Delta m^{2}_{31}| \approx 2{,}5\times10^{-3}$ eV$^{2}$.

How light they are, and why that matters for the corpus's standing. The cosmological bound puts the sum below about $0{,}12$ eV — five to eight orders of magnitude below the electron's mass. So Charon was wrong about "rigorously zero", but not absurdly wrong about "very small". This is the honest framing, and it is the reason this article is in Part P rather than among the corpus's blunders.

Part 3 — Refuted by later data, not contradicted at publication

Part P holds two kinds of error, and this article should be placed in the right one.

P23 is the first kind. The corpus gives the muon spin $3/2$. The muon was known to be spin-½ a quarter of a century before the book, so that claim was contradicted at publication; no new experiment was ever needed.

P8 is the second kind. Massless neutrinos were the standard assumption in 1983 and 1987. The Standard Model as usually presented had them massless, and the solar neutrino deficit — Davis's experiment, running since the 1960s — was an anomaly without a confirmed explanation until Super-Kamiokande and SNO. Charon assumed what the field assumed. The claim is [FALS], and the falsification came fifteen to twenty years after publication, from a kind of experiment (large water Cherenkov and heavy-water detectors) that did not then exist.

Why that distinction matters. It changes what a reissue should say. For P23 the correct note is that the corpus contradicted long-standing data. For P8 the correct note is that the corpus adopted the consensus of its day and the consensus was later overturned — which is not a failure of the corpus's judgment but a fact about the state of neutrino physics. The failure to update is a real one for the 1987 book only in the weaker sense that the solar anomaly was known.

Part 4 — The deletion, stated precisely

S17 states the consequence, and it should be quoted because it is stronger than "damage".

The chain. The préon requires $\Lambda = 0$; $\Lambda = 0$ requires $R' = \pm c$ for all $\tau$; that requires the micro-universe to contain no element of non-zero proper mass; that requires the neutrino to be massless. Remove the last link and the first is gone. The corpus's own sentence is the proof: the state is maintained "puisque seul le neutrino de masse propre nulle est présent".

The universality. Because the préon is superposed on every particle (RC 052), the deletion is not confined to the neutrino sectors. Every particle's préon component is removed. The corpus's own statement that every point of the Real carries a préon becomes, with a massive neutrino, a statement about a solution that does not exist.

What this does to §3.8. Inventory §3.8 records the claim that the neutrino's "rest energy" is non-zero although its proper mass is zero — an artefact of the pulsating frame. Once the mass is non-zero the claim is vacuous, and the k_S coefficient built on it has no subject.

Summary

The corpus's neutrino has rigorously zero proper mass, and that one fact holds up the préon, the éon, the charge mechanism and the weak boson. Neutrino oscillations — Super-Kamiokande 1998, SNO 2001–02, KamLAND 2002, Nobel 2015 — prove non-zero mass-squared splittings ($\Delta m^{2}_{21} \approx 7{,}5\times10^{-5}$ eV$^{2}$, $|\Delta m^{2}_{31}| \approx 2{,}5\times10^{-3}$ eV$^{2}$), with the cosmological sum bounded below $0{,}12$ eV. The consequence is deletion, not damage: the préon's $\Lambda = 0$ requires $R' = \pm c$, which requires a massless internal neutrino, so the préon solution ceases to exist — and since the préon is superposed on every particle, the removal is universal. Unlike P23, the claim was the consensus of its day and the refutation came fifteen years later. Verdict [FALS], settled by neutrino oscillation experiments.

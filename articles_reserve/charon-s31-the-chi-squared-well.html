Charon — The $\chi$-Squared Well (S31)

Audits the numerical method of the Système $L$: Esp 057 §1–§6 and the recovered paragraph at the head of Esp 058 (scans 057–058), with the solution table of Esp 058. The method behind S32's table and S34's ledger.

Introduction

The method is honest, well posed and reported with unusual frankness; the number it reports does not mean what it looks like it means, and what it does mean is smaller and worth having. The corpus's numerical claim is that a system of twenty equations, "ne dépendant d'aucun paramètre" (Esp 056), has a "profond puits de minimisation" at the measured values of the six principal constants. The method section, two pages later, begins by putting those six values in by hand and confines the search to $\pm10\,\%$ of them — and the corpus says so in its own words. So the result is not a derivation of the constants and not a fit to data; it is a self-consistency test, and this article says exactly how strong a test it is.

What the test is, stated precisely, because the corpus's own notation blurs it. The reduced system $L'$ is solved forward: given six seeds, $\alpha$ comes from L7 $+$ L8, $\beta$ from L8 $-$ L7, $T$ from L8 at $n = 1,2$, and $K = +1$, $A$ and $R(R_{0})$ from L2–L6 and L1. The six equations dropped from $L$ are then evaluated at that solution, and their normalised misfit is the scalar $\chi$. There is no least-squares fit against measured data anywhere in the procedure: there is a map from six seeds to an internal-inconsistency residual, and the search locates its minimum. That is a real and non-trivial experiment, and it is not the experiment the corpus's reputation claims.

Three results this article adds.

(1) The residual $\chi_{L} = 2\,\%$ has no absolute meaning, and the invariant is the depth $50$. The corpus defines $\chi^{2} = \frac{1}{6}\sum(F_{i}/\varepsilon_{i})^{2}$ and never specifies the scales $\varepsilon_{i}$, while also writing $\chi$ rather than $\chi^{2}$ in the ratio that gives the well's depth. So $2\,\%$ is a level in a normalisation chosen by the author, and the scale-free statement the corpus makes is that the best of the 728 other parameter sets is $50$ times worse. Both of those are stated as they are, with the arithmetic consequences.

(2) The depth is a sharpness measurement, and it is the corpus's strongest numerical result. The nearest other sets in the grid are $\pm10\,\%$ away along one axis, so $\min\chi_{i}/\chi_{L} = 50$ says that moving any single one of the six seeds by $10\,\%$ makes the six dropped equations at least $50$ times worse. The well is narrower than $10\,\%$ in every direction. That is a statement of local rigidity, it is measurable, and it is the honest headline of the corpus's numerical chapter — not the "computation of the constants".

(3) The corpus's arithmetic is sound, and it is now verified in three independent ways. $T$, $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are recomputed here from closed forms that follow from the corpus's own L7 and L8 by addition and subtraction, and they reproduce the printed table to $0{,}3\,\%$–$1{,}8\,\%$; and $\beta$ is confirmed by a second, completely different route — the blackbody temperature range of Esp 058 §17 with the corpus's own (245) — to $1{,}2\,\%$ (§4.3). The arithmetic is not where the model is weak; the inference is.

And one structural finding that belongs here rather than in S32's table: the six equations the residual is over include two definitions. They are L11, L12 at $n = 1,2$, L14, L15 and L17 (Esp 057 §2). L15 is the spin–pulse equipartition and L17 is the definition of the elementary length. So the corpus's self-consistency well is, among other things, a test of $\xi$'s "suggestion" against the lepton's geometry — and S30's independent computation of L17's misfit, $2{,}6\,\%$ in $l_{0}$, is the same order as the reported $\chi_{L} = 2\,\%$ (§3.5).

What the Corpus Claims

Esp 057 §1–§6, the method, in the corpus's words:

1) Donnons-nous un ensemble de six valeurs numériques, dans un système d'unités physiques donné (cgs par exemple), correspondant respectivement aux valeurs des six paramètres : $h, c, m, \mu, \tau_2$ et $e$. Appelons $P_0$ l'ensemble de ces six paramètres initialement choisis.

2) On constate alors que, $P_0$ étant donné, on peut rechercher la solution exacte non pas du Système $L$ mais d'un Système $L'$ obtenu en supprimant six des équations de $L$, à savoir : $L11, L12\ (n=1,2), L14, L15$ et $L17$. Cette solution $L'$ est obtenue facilement sur ordinateur; on commence par calculer $\alpha$ par addition de $L7$ et $L8$, puis $\beta$ par soustraction de $L7$ à $L8$, en se servant de $L16$. $L8$, considéré avec $n = 1$ et $n = 2$, donne aussi : $T = 2h/[c^{2}(\tau-\mu)]$.

3) Soient $F_i = 0$ ($i = 1$ à $6$) les six équations dont le système $L'$ ne tient pas compte […] On peut calculer l'« écart » entre la solution $L'$ et la solution $L$ en introduisant une fonction d'écart $\chi$ définie comme suit : $\chi^{2} = \frac{1}{6}\sum_{i=1}^{6}(F_i/\varepsilon_i)^{2}$ où $F_i/\varepsilon_i$ est non dimensionnel.

4) […] choisissons un ensemble $P_0$ numériquement identique aux valeurs numériques expérimentales que l'on obtient pour ces six paramètres. Puis choisissons pour la variation de ces paramètres des « plages » comprises entre le double et la moitié de la valeur « naturelle » de $P_0$. Enfin discrétisons le calcul en choisissant, par exemple, cinq valeurs régulièrement espacées entre $P_0$ et les deux extréma des plages de variation. Nous disposons ainsi maintenant de $11^{6} = 1\,771\,561$ ensembles $P_i$. […] Le rapport $\min\chi_{i}/\chi_{L}$ ($i \ne L$) sera une mesure de la « profondeur » du puits.

6) Nous avons procédé au calcul effectif sur ordinateur […] cependant, essentiellement pour des raisons de temps de calcul,

Esp 058, the recovered paragraph that completes §6 — and the frankest page in the book:

[…] nous nous sommes limité à choisir pour chaque paramètre des plages de variation de seulement $\pm10\,\%$ par rapport aux valeurs numériques connues expérimentalement, avec une seule valeur des paramètres aux deux extréma de chaque plage de variation. Cela conduit pratiquement à tester $3^{6} = 729$ ensembles de paramètres $P_i$. On constate bien effectivement un profond puits de minimisation de la fonction d'écart $\chi$ pour l'ensemble des six paramètres $P_i$ correspondant aux valeurs expérimentales de ces paramètres. L'approximation de la solution $L$ calculée vaut : $\chi_{L} = 2\,\%$. Par ailleurs, ce puits est unique dans la région analysée, et le rapport exprimant la « profondeur » du puits vaut : $\min\chi_{i}/\chi_{L} = 50$.

Tags. The experiment is [MATH]: a parameterised map, a box, a grid, a scalar residual, a reported minimum. The inference drawn from it — that the System $L$ computes the constants — is [SPEC] and is what S33 and S34 refuse. Note also what the paragraph does not claim: it does not claim a global minimum, it does not claim the residual is zero, and it does not claim the search was exhaustive. All three abstentions are to the corpus's credit.

Scope. S30 states the system; S32 gives the constants one row each; S34 counts inputs and outputs; S33 tests the parameter-freedom claim as such. This article audits the method and recomputes the published solution's arithmetic.

1. The Method, Step by Step

The procedure is complete and reproducible in principle, and this section states it in the order it is used so that S32's table can be checked against it.

step what the corpus does what it needs what it returns
1 chooses the six seeds $P_{0}$ the measured $h, c, m, \mu, \tau_{2}, e$ a starting point
2 solves $L'$ = $L$ minus (L11, L12 at $n=1,2$, L14, L15, L17) $P_{0}$ $\alpha$, $\beta$, $T$, $K$, $A$, $R(R_{0})$ and every integral of the system
2a $\alpha$ by addition of L7 and L8 $m$, $\mu$, $h$, $c$, $T$ $\alpha$
2b $\beta$ by subtraction of L7 from L8 $\mu$, $m$, $h$, $c$, $T$ $\beta$
2c $T = 2h/[c^{2}(\tau-\mu)]$ from L8 at $n = 1,2$ $\mu$, $\tau$, $h$, $c$ $T$
2d $K = +1$, $A(R_{0})$, $R(R_{0})$ from L2–L6 and L1 the above the waveform and the geometry
3 evaluates the six dropped equations as $F_{i}$ the $L'$ solution $\chi^{2} = \frac{1}{6}\sum(F_{i}/\varepsilon_{i})^{2}$
4 searches a box about $P_{0}$ on a grid a box, a grid, $\chi$ $\min\chi_{i}$ and the depth ratio
5 refines about the best $P_{L}$ and repeats — convergence "vers la solution exacte"
6 reports the executed run — $\chi_{L} = 2\,\%$, depth $50$, uniqueness in the region analysed

Two features of the method are worth isolating because they decide what the result can mean.

(a) It is forward computation plus residual evaluation, not fitting. Once $P$ is given, everything in $L'$ is computed — there is no iterative adjustment inside step 2. So $\chi$ is a function of the six seeds, and the search is over a six-dimensional parameter space of inputs, not of fitted coefficients. The corpus's language — "la solution numérique du Système L" — is therefore fair: what is being located is a self-consistent point of a system, and the point it finds is the experimental one.

(b) The six dropped equations are not an arbitrary six. They are L11, L12 at $n = 1,2$, L14, L15 and L17, and S30 gives what each one is. The list is dominated by the pulse side of the model (L11, L12 = the electron's and the heavy leptons' pulse actions; L14 = the pulse relativistic coefficient $k_{P}$), plus L15, the equipartition $k_{S} = k_{P}$, plus L17, the definition of the elementary length. The spin side (L9, L10, L13) is solved, the pulse side is tested.

2. The Seeds, and the Window

2.1 The six, and what three of them are

The seeds are $h, c, m, \mu, \tau_{2}$ and $e$ — Esp 057 §1, read from the scan and confirmed on the printed book (register §13.4). Three of the six are the measured charged-lepton masses ($m$ the electron, $\mu$ the muon, $\tau_{2}$ the tau, by the corpus's own (244)); one is Planck's constant at its 1970s value; one is the speed of light; and one is the elementary charge.

That list is the whole of the constants claim, and it is stated before the search begins. S34's ledger is the consequence: a system whose six inputs are the six constants it is claimed to compute cannot, by itself, compute them. The reader who wants the shortest possible evidence for rule R1 has it here, in the corpus's step 1.

2.2 $e$ is among the seeds, and the corpus's "5 + 1" is about §18.2

Esp 056 says the system computes five principal constants directly and "conduit également au calcul de la charge électrique élémentaire $e$" by way of §18.2. Esp 057 §1 puts $e$ in the seed set. Both statements are in the corpus; they are consistent only if the computation of $e$ is the §18.2 chain and the seed is its starting value. S35 owns that chain and its $2{,}3\,\%$/$4{,}8\,\%$; the point for this article is that the numerical search never computes $e$ at all. $\chi$ does not contain $e$; the dropped six are the pulse and normalisation equations. So the well's depth says nothing about the elementary charge, favourably or unfavourably.

The corpus describes three windows and executes the narrowest. This matters, and it is stated plainly on the corpus's own page.

And the published number comes from the $729$-point grid, not from the $1{,}77$-million-point one. The corpus says why ("essentiellement pour des raisons de temps de calcul") and does not pretend otherwise. So the claim under test is a local claim, and the corpus's own wording limits it correctly: "ce puits est unique dans la région analysée".

3. The Residual: What the $2\,\%$ Is and Is Not

3.1 The definition, and the two ambiguities in it

$$\chi^{2} = \frac{1}{6}\sum_{i=1}^{6}\left(\frac{F_i}{\varepsilon_i}\right)^{2}, \qquad \frac{F_i}{\varepsilon_i}\ \text{non dimensionnel}$$

The $\varepsilon_{i}$ are normalisation scales, and the corpus never says what they are. Two consequences, and both must be stated because the corpus's own sentence about $2\,\%$ depends on them.

  1. The level of $\chi$ is not a physical quantity. A residual of $2\,\%$ in units of $\varepsilon_{i}$ becomes $20\,\%$ if the $\varepsilon_{i}$ are ten times smaller, and $0{,}2\,\%$ if they are ten times larger. The only reading of the $2\,\%$ that is scale-free is the reading in which each $\varepsilon_{i}$ is the natural magnitude of its own equation — in which case "the six dropped equations are satisfied to about $2\,\%$ of their own size" is what the number asserts. The corpus never says that, and it is the charitable reading; it is offered here as such.
  2. The ratio $50$ is invariant and is the well's content. Whatever the $\varepsilon_{i}$, $\min\chi_{i}/\chi_{L}$ is a ratio of two residuals in the same normalisation. So the corpus's scale-free statement is the depth, not the level.

The second ambiguity is the square. The corpus defines $\chi^{2}$ and then writes "le rapport $\min\chi_{i}/\chi_{L} = 50$" and "$\chi_{L} = 2\,\%$". If $\chi$ in those sentences means the same object as $\chi^{2}$ above, the depth is $50$ in the squared quantity; if it means its square root, the depth is $50$ in the residual and $2\,500$ in the square. Both readings are recorded; the article uses $50$ in $\chi$ and says $2\,500$ in $\chi^{2}$ where the distinction matters, since $50$ is what the corpus prints.

3.2 What the residual is a residual of

A residual is only as meaningful as the equations it measures, and here they can be named. They are L11, L12 at $n = 1,2$, L14, L15 and L17 — that is:

dropped equation what it is what its residual measures
L11 the electron's pulse action ($\leftrightarrow$ (229)) whether the pulse action closes at $\frac{1}{2}h$
L12 ($n=1,2$) the heavy leptons' pulse actions ($\leftrightarrow$ (230)) the same at $\left(n+\frac{1}{2}\right)h$
L14 $k_{P} = \overline{(1-R'^{2}/c^{2})^{-1/2}}$ the pulse relativistic coefficient
L15 $k_{S} = k_{P}$ — the equipartition whether spin and pulse share the action equally
L17 $\int dt/R^{3} = T/l_{0}^{3}$ — the definition of $l_{0}$ whether the lepton's geometry returns the elementary length taken from $M_{0}$

Three things follow, and the third is the one the register does not yet carry.

(a) The masses are not what $\chi$ measures. L7 and L8 are solved, not residual: $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $T$ are computed from them, so the lepton masses are satisfied identically by construction. The corpus's deviations on $m$, $\mu$ and $\tau_{2}$ (S32's table) are therefore not residuals of this fit at all — they are the distances between the seeds and the measurements, which is a different thing and is dealt with in S34.

(b) The equipartition is being tested. $k_{S} = k_{P}$ is one of the six, and it is the corpus's own "facteur 2" — the term that makes the kinetic energy $2(n+\frac{1}{2})h/T$, hence the tower step $2h/(c^{2}T)$ (register §13.2). So the well says the spin–pulse equipartition holds to the $\chi_{L}$ level at the experimental point and is bad elsewhere: the equipartition is not an identity in the corpus's system but a satisfied constraint. That is a positive result for the corpus's internal economy, and it has never been stated.

(c) The elementary length is inside the residual, and S30 computes its misfit independently. L17 is the equation that would compute $l_{0}$ from the lepton's own radii; the corpus's $l_{0}$ was taken from the nucleon mass as a "suggestion" (Esp 057). S30 §3.3 computed that misfit without any fitting: with the printed $R_{0}$, $\mathcal{R}$ and a constant-speed cycle, L17 returns $l_{0} = 1{,}7127\times10^{-14}$ cm against the printed $1{,}7577\times10^{-14}$ cm — $2{,}6\,\%$. That is the same order as $\chi_{L} = 2\,\%$, which is exactly what one expects if L17 is one of the residual's six equations and its $\varepsilon$ is the equation's own scale. Two independent computations of the same defect agree, and the agreement is a check on both.

3.3 The depth, and the sharpness it implies

The depth is the article's most useful number, and it can be read in one more way than the corpus reads it. On the executed $3^{6}$ grid, the points nearest to the centre are one axis-step away, i.e. $\pm10\,\%$ in a single seed; all the other $728$ are further. So

$$\min_{i \ne L}\frac{\chi_{i}}{\chi_{L}} = 50 \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \text{a }10\,\%\text{ shift in any single seed worsens the six dropped equations by at least a factor }50$$

so the self-consistent point is pinned to better than $10\,\%$ in every direction, and in the residual's own measure it is pinned considerably tighter. In $\chi^{2}$ terms the nearest rivals are $2{,}500$ times worse. This is a measurement of local rigidity, it involves no assumption about $\varepsilon_{i}$, and it is the strongest numerical statement the corpus makes about the lepton model. It is also a statement about the model, not about nature — see §5.

3.4 The uniqueness claim, correctly scoped, and the grid's granularity

The corpus's uniqueness claim is "ce puits est unique dans la région analysée", and it is correctly scoped — but "the analysed region" is narrower than the box suggests. With three points per axis, the region analysed is the $3^{6}$ grid, not the continuous $\pm10\,\%$ box; a second shallow minimum between grid points would not be seen. Two things follow.

  • The claim as printed is safe and should be quoted with its qualifier. Unique in the analysed region is what the search supports.
  • The granularity is coarse enough that "the minimum is at the experimental point" should be read as "the central grid point is the best of the $729$". The corpus's step 5 is exactly the iterative refinement that would sharpen this, and whether the refinement loop was ever run to convergence is not stated on either page. Recorded as an open item: the corpus reports the first grid's result and describes the refinement without saying it was executed.

One further caution, in the corpus's favour rather than against it. A depth of $50$ over $729$ points would be unimpressive if the other $728$ points were far away and the function smooth; it is impressive here precisely because the nearest neighbours are $10\,\%$ away and the depth is $50$. No statistical model of "expected depth" is offered here, because the corpus's $\chi$ is a deterministic function of the six seeds and not a random variable.

4. The Arithmetic, Recomputed

4.1 The three closed forms the method implies

Esp 057 §2 says $\alpha$ comes from adding L7 and L8, $\beta$ from subtracting them, and $T$ from L8 at $n = 1,2$. Carrying those operations out exactly, with L16 to evaluate $\int dt/R = 2\pi/c$, gives (S30 §5.1):

$$T = \frac{2h}{c^{2}(\tau-\mu)}, \qquad \alpha = \frac{m + 3\mu - 2\tau}{4\pi^{2}}, \qquad \beta = -\frac{h}{4\pi^{3}c}\left(1 - \varepsilon\right), \qquad \varepsilon = \frac{\mu-m}{\tau-\mu} = 0{,}06292$$

These are exact algebraic consequences of the corpus's own three instructions — not a model of them and not a fit. Note that $\alpha$ is a pure mass combination: $T$ cancels. That is the algebraic statement of rule R1's verdict for $\alpha$: the lepton model's internal density constant is a restatement of the three measured lepton masses.

Read the middle column, because it is the fair comparison: the corpus's printed table is computed from the corpus's printed masses, and against those its arithmetic is right to a third of a per cent in $\alpha$ and to a hundredth of a per cent in $T$. The first column shows what the same formulae give with today's masses — which is the number S35 needs, because $\beta$ is what fixes $\alpha$ there. S21's $\beta$ — reached from the éon's system — is the same expression, so the two chapters agree exactly on the internal constant that carries the $\alpha$ agreement.

4.3 $\beta$ confirmed by a second, independent route

The recomputation above uses the lepton mass difference. Esp 058's own temperature range can be used instead, and it agrees. The table prints the blackbody temperature varying between $\theta_{0} = 1{,}526\times10^{13}$ K at $R_{0}$ and $\theta_{\max} = 2{,}479\times10^{12}$ K at $\mathcal{R}$; the corpus's (245) is $D_{E} = \beta c^{2}/R^{4} = \pm a\theta^{4}$. Eliminating $D_{E}$ and using the printed radii:

$$|\beta| = \frac{a\,\theta_{0}^{4}R_{0}^{4}}{c^{2}} = 1{,}6897\times10^{-39}, \qquad |\beta| = \frac{a\,\theta_{\max}^{4}\mathcal{R}^{4}}{c^{2}} = 1{,}6876\times10^{-39}$$

The two ends of the printed temperature range give the same $\beta$ to $0{,}1\,\%$ of each other — which is itself a check that $\theta \propto 1/R$, as $D_{E}\propto R^{-4}$ requires — and $1{,}6897\times10^{-39}$ is $1{,}2\,\%$ from the closed form's $-1{,}66995\times10^{-39}$. So the corpus's own blackbody data reproduce the internal constant that its own lepton-mass difference gives. This is a third independent verification of the published solution's arithmetic, and $\beta$ is now the best-corroborated number in the lepton sector.

Two remarks, so that the check is not over-read. The agreement is between two printed quantities and one derived one, so it corroborates the corpus's internal consistency and not the physics of a blackbody inside a closed micro-universe. And the printed $\rho$ range is the $\alpha$ check in the same sense (S32): $\rho = |\alpha|/R^{3}$ gives $3{,}10\times10^{17}$ and $1{,}33\times10^{15}$ g cm⁻³, which are the printed "$3{,}1\times10^{17}$ et $1{,}33\times10^{15}$" exactly. The table of Esp 058 is internally consistent to a part in a few hundred, and that is now established on three independent pairs.

5. What the Corpus Gives Away, and Why It Helps

The recovered paragraph at the head of Esp 058 is the frankest passage in the corpus, and it should be quoted wherever the constants are discussed.

  • It says the search was limited to $\pm10\,\%$ (the corpus's word is "limité");
  • it says the ranges are "par rapport aux valeurs numériques connues expérimentalement";
  • it says the minimum is found "pour l'ensemble des six paramètres $P_{i}$ correspondant aux valeurs expérimentales de ces paramètres";
  • it reports the residual as $2\,\%$, not as zero;
  • it restricts uniqueness to "la région analysée".

Every one of those five sentences is a limitation the corpus states against itself. S34 draws the ledger from them, and this article's contribution is to say what they do not damage: the experiment is well posed and its result — a sharp self-consistency well at the measured lepton parameters — is real.

What the well does establish, in one sentence. Given the six measured constants, the lepton model's twenty equations are mutually consistent to the per-cent level, and that consistency point is isolated in its own parameter space by a factor $50$ per $10\,\%$. What it does not establish: that the constants are computed. The distance between those two sentences is rule R1, and it is measured here.

6. The Two Grids, and What the Choice Costs

The corpus is explicit that the wider grid was not run, and the cost of that choice can be stated exactly.

$\pm100\,\%$ grid (proposed) $\pm10\,\%$ grid (executed)
points $1\,771\,561$ $729$
what it could find a second well anywhere in $[0{,}5P_{0},\,2P_{0}]$ a second well at grid resolution inside $\pm10\,\%$
cost in the corpus's own terms "essentiellement pour des raisons de temps de calcul" —
consequence for the claim global uniqueness (not claimed) local uniqueness (claimed, and supported)

So the corpus's claim is exactly as strong as its grid, and it says so. A reader who wants the stronger claim can price the missing run: the $\pm100\,\%$ grid is $2\,430$ times the executed one. Nothing in this article suggests the corpus should have run it: the honest claim is the one printed.

One consequence of the choice that is not on the corpus's page, and it is the interesting one. Because the box is $\pm10\,\%$ about the measured values, the well's existence inside the box is guaranteed by construction for any model whose residual is smooth and smallest near the seeds. What is not guaranteed, and what the depth $50$ measures, is that the well is sharp. So the corpus's own result, read strictly, is a statement about sharpness — which is a statement about the model's rigidity — and it is the only reading of the numerical chapter that survives without the $\varepsilon_{i}$.

7. Verdict

The method is well posed, honestly reported, correctly scoped, and its arithmetic verified; its inference is not entitled, and its strongest result is not the one it advertises.

  • Confirmed (§13.3): the seeds are six measured values (Esp 057 §1, §4); the search was $\pm10\,\%$ (Esp 058); $3^{6} = 729$ and $11^{6} = 1\,771\,561$ are exact; $\chi_{L} = 2\,\%$ and $\min\chi_{i}/\chi_{L} = 50$ as printed; uniqueness only "dans la région analysée".
  • New — the $\chi_{L} = 2\,\%$ level has no absolute meaning because the normalisation scales $\varepsilon_{i}$ are never specified; the invariant is the depth $50$. Both readings of the corpus's $\chi$ vs $\chi^{2}$ ambiguity are given, and the depth is $50$ in $\chi$, i.e. $2\,500$ in $\chi^{2}$.
  • New — the depth is a sharpness measurement, and it is the corpus's strongest numerical result: a $10\,\%$ error in any single seed worsens the six dropped equations by a factor $\ge 50$. The well is narrower than $10\,\%$ in every direction.
  • New — $\chi$ is not about the masses and not about $e$. L7 and L8 are solved, not residual; the six dropped equations are the pulse actions, $k_{P}$, the equipartition L15 and the elementary-length definition L17. So the well tests the spin–pulse equipartition and the $\xi$ suggestion, and it never touches the elementary charge.
  • New — the corpus's arithmetic is verified three ways: the closed forms $T = 2h/[c^{2}(\tau-\mu)]$, $\alpha = (m+3\mu-2\tau)/(4\pi^{2})$, $\beta = -h(1-\varepsilon)/(4\pi^{3}c)$ reproduce the printed table to $0{,}3\,\%$–$1{,}8\,\%$ from the corpus's own printed masses; $\beta$ is confirmed independently from the printed blackbody temperature range via (245), to $1{,}2\,\%$; and $\rho = |\alpha|/R^{3}$ reproduces the printed density range exactly.

Summary

The method is well posed, honestly reported and correctly scoped; what it proves is a sharpness property, not the computation of the constants. The six seeds are the six measured values (Esp 057 §1), the search is $\pm10\,\%$ (Esp 058), $3^{6} = 729$ and $11^{6} = 1\,771\,561$ are exact, $\chi_{L} = 2\,\%$, and uniqueness is claimed only "dans la région analysée". Because the normalisation scales $\varepsilon_{i}$ are never specified, the level $2\,\%$ has no absolute meaning; the scale-free invariant is the depth, and a depth of $50$ against neighbours $10\,\%$ away says that moving any single seed by a tenth worsens the six dropped equations by a factor $\ge 50$. That is the corpus's strongest numerical result. The six dropped equations are named — the pulse actions, $k_{p}$, the equipartition L15 and the elementary-length definition L17 — so the residual never measures the lepton masses and never touches $e$. And the published arithmetic is verified three ways: $T$, $\alpha$, $\beta$ from closed forms ($0{,}3$–$1{,}8\,\%$), $\beta$ again from the printed blackbody range via (245) ($1{,}2\,\%$), and $\rho = |\alpha|/R^{3}$ exactly. S30's independent L17 misfit, $2{,}6\,\%$, is the same order as $\chi_{L}$.

Summary of Notation

  • $P_{0}$, $P_{i}$, $P_{L}$ — the seed set (the measured six), a grid point, and the best grid point. $P_{0}$ is "numériquement identique aux valeurs numériques expérimentales".
  • $L'$ — the reduced system, $L$ minus L11, L12 ($n=1,2$), L14, L15, L17. Solved forward in step 2.
  • $F_{i}$ — the six dropped equations, evaluated at the $L'$ solution; $F_{i} = 0$ at the exact solution.
  • $\varepsilon_{i}$ — the corpus's normalisation scales, never specified; they make $F_{i}/\varepsilon_{i}$ non-dimensional. The $\chi_{L} = 2\,\%$ level is meaningless without them; the depth $50$ is not.
  • $\chi$, $\chi_{L}$ — the residual, defined as $\chi^{2} = \frac{1}{6}\sum(F_{i}/\varepsilon_{i})^{2}$; $\chi_{L} = 2\,\%$ at $P_{L}$ ($= P_{0}$). The corpus writes $\chi$ where it defines $\chi^{2}$; both readings are given in §3.1.
  • $\min\chi_{i}/\chi_{L} = 50$ — the well's depth; $i \ne L$ over the $728$ other grid points. $2\,500$ in the squared quantity.
  • $\alpha$, $\beta$, $T$ — the internal constants and the pulsation period; this article's closed forms are $T = 2h/[c^{2}(\tau-\mu)]$, $\alpha = (m+3\mu-2\tau)/(4\pi^{2})$, $\beta = -h(1-\varepsilon)/(4\pi^{3}c)$.
  • $\varepsilon$ — the abbreviation $(\mu-m)/(\tau-\mu) = 0{,}06292$; S37's, used here for $\beta$'s closed form.

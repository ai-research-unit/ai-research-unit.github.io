Charon — Energy, Momentum and Negative Proper Mass (S13)

Audits §2.10–2.12 of CHARON_IDEAS_AND_CLAIMS.md, against RC ch. III §1–2 and §2.1 (scans 029, 030).

Introduction

The corpus allows energy, proper mass and proper density each to be positive, negative or zero — and it does so with an argument, in the open, knowing what it costs. That distinguishes it from the usual crank move of positing negative energy and hoping nobody notices. The corpus has a reason, the reason is a good one, and the reason is also where the trouble is.

This article states the licence, gives the corpus its argument, and then shows the one structural point that matters: the licence is granted in the corpus's geometric sector and spent in its physical one. The cosmology's negative vacuum — and through it S50's provenance chain and S51's bounce — depends on that transfer. So this page is not a curiosity about signs; it is the load-bearing licence of the whole cosmology, examined at the point where it is issued.

What the Corpus Claims

§2.10 — energy, proper mass and proper density may each be positive, negative or zero. RC ch. III §1–2. Tag: [SPEC].

§2.11 — negative-frequency photons, the "antiphotons", of energy $-h\nu$. RC ch. III §2.1. Tag: [SPEC]. Verbatim from RC 030: "nous avons deux signes $W_+$ et $W_-$ autorisés. C'est le résultat que va adopter la Relativité complexe, introduisant ainsi la notion d'antiphoton d'énergie $-h\nu$, à côté de celle de photon, d'énergie $+h\nu$. ... cette notion d'antiphoton sera très utile ... pour effectuer la distinction entre les charges électriques positives et négatives."

§2.12 — a composite particle can be lighter than the sum of its constituents, because positive and negative proper energies partly cancel. RC ch. III §2.2. Tag: [MIX].

The Corpus's Argument, and It Is a Good One

The corpus's defence of the licence rests on the difference between a continuity equation that is linear in the density and one that is quadratic in it, and the argument deserves to be stated in full because it is the best thing on this page.

  • In general relativity the continuity law is $\nabla_{\alpha}\rho u^{\alpha} = 0$ — RC (2-53) — and it is linear in $\rho$. Read at face value, the corpus observes, it appears to permit a positive density $\rho_{+}$ and a negative density $\rho_{-}$ to be created from nothing ($W = 0$), or to meet and disappear into nothing again. And experiment says no: matter and antimatter annihilate into energy equal to the sum of the absolute values, and a particle–antiparticle pair cannot emerge from the vacuum without an energy at least that large.
  • The corpus's own continuity law is different. RC (2-54) reads $\nabla_{\alpha}Ru^{\alpha} = \nabla_{\alpha}\rho^{2}u^{\alpha} = 0$ — the continuity of the square of the density. The corpus's comment on it is exact: "L'«addiction» de $\rho_{+}^{2}$ et $\rho_{-}^{2}$ est ici toujours positive, et ne conduit pas aux «paradoxes» de voir sortir de l'énergie du néant." A squared density cannot sum to zero, so the creation-from-nothing reading does not arise.

This is a real argument and it is worth crediting. It identifies a genuine awkwardness in the naive reading of $\nabla_{\alpha}\rho u^{\alpha} = 0$ — the reason general relativity attaches a conserved current to $\rho u^{\alpha}$ and then treats $\rho$ itself as positive by fiat — and it proposes a coherent alternative: make the conserved quantity $\rho^{2}u^{\alpha}$. The corpus did not stumble into the negative universe; it built a formalism in which the density's sign is inert in the conservation law by construction.

And the corpus is explicit that the sign freedom lives in one sector only. RC 029 splits RC (2-52) into two relations: (2-52-a) is "purement physique", and its squares "ne sont donc pas autorisés ... à être, ou devenir, négatifs"; (2-52-b) is "purement géométrique" and its curvature elements $T_{\alpha}^{\ \alpha}$ "peut donc être positif, négatif ou nul (théorème de Schur)". The corpus's own conclusion: the geometric formalism is more general than a physical one, and the distinction is what buys the generality.

The Findings

1. The licence is granted in the geometric sector and spent in the physical one

This is the article's central point and it needs no new calculation, only the corpus's own two sentences read together.

  • By (2-52-b) and RC 029, the freedom to change sign belongs to the curvature elements $T_{\alpha}^{\ \alpha}$, whose square roots need not be real — that is Schur's theorem doing the work, and it is a statement about tensors.
  • By (2-54), the conserved object is $\rho^{2}$, and the corpus's own gloss is that $\rho_{+}^{2} + \rho_{-}^{2}$ is always positive. So in the corpus's conservation law, $\rho$'s sign is not a physical quantity at all: the physical object is $\rho^{2}$, and $\rho$ is one of two square roots of it.
  • And then the cosmology makes the sign physical. RC 084 states the conclusion: "on est conduit ... à une densité $\rho$ de l'espace «vide» qui est négative"; RC 080 §3.3 calls the vacuum "une forme d'antimatière"; and §8.5's [FALS] verdict on the vacuum is a verdict on a negative density, quoted as $\rho = -2{,}9\times10^{-32}$ g cm$^{-3}$. S50 traces $\Lambda \le 0$ and the bounce to precisely this sign.

So the corpus's sign is a geometric label in chapter II and a physical cause in chapter V. Under (2-54) that transfer is not licensed: if $\rho^{2}$ is what is conserved and what enters the geometry — $R = \xi^{2}\rho^{2} + 4\Lambda$ contains $\rho^{2}$, not $\rho$ — then a "negative vacuum density" is a statement about the sign of a coefficient ($\alpha < 0$ in $\rho = \alpha/R^{3}$), not about a physically negative energy. Whether $\alpha < 0$ is then fixed by the corpus's zero-total-energy identity, which is itself a stipulation (S50) — not by the negative-energy licence this article is auditing. And that is the finding: the licence the corpus argues for so carefully is not the licence its cosmology actually uses.

2. The licence, taken at face value, would not help the cosmology anyway

Suppose the licence is granted in the physical sector as well, generously. Even then, the corpus's negative vacuum is not of the kind the sign freedom provides. The formal negative-energy objects of relativistic theory — negative-frequency modes, the Dirac sea — are mode labels, not static densities; and the corpus's own (5-46) uses $D_{\rho}$ as a signed square root, $D_{\rho}/c^{2} = \pm\sqrt{\rho^{2} + P_{\rho}^{2}/\Omega_{0}^{2}c^{2}}$, which is a choice of root, not a different density. A signed square root is a bookkeeping device; a negative energy density of the vacuum is a physical claim. The corpus needs the second and its formalism supplies the first. S50 makes the same point from the other side: drop the stipulation, and $\rho$'s sign is not forced at all — so the negative vacuum was never a consequence of the licence.

3. The antiphoton is a label, not a particle

RC 030 introduces $\overline{\gamma}$ of energy $-h\nu$ and states its job: to distinguish positive from negative electric charge. In established physics the photon is its own antiparticle — charge conjugation acts on the one-photon state as a phase, not as a particle exchange — and negative-frequency modes are the same field's negative-energy solutions, not a second particle. So the corpus's antiphoton is a relabelling of the standard negative-frequency sector.

The relabelling is not absurd, and this is worth saying: using a signed photon energy to carry a sign of charge is coherent bookkeeping, and nothing in the corpus's charge assignments is thereby made wrong. But it is a designation, not a mechanism — the register's §2.11 says exactly this — and the corpus then builds its strongest matter–antimatter claim on it: the 303-Myr antimatter era, which §8.4 marks [FALS] on its gamma signature. So the device's most consequential use has already failed for an independent, observational reason.

4. The mass-defect explanation is a relabelling of binding energy, and the corpus's version has a counterexample

§2.12's phenomenon is real and is [EST]: a bound state weighs less than its separated constituents, by the binding energy. The corpus's explanation — that negative proper energies partly cancel positive ones — is [SPEC], and the standard explanation is different in kind: potential-energy bookkeeping in electromagnetism, and for the nucleons the QCD trace anomaly. Where the corpus's version would predict, it does not: the mass defect is present in systems with no negative-energy component whatsoever — the deuteron ($2{,}2$ MeV), the helium nucleus, and every hadron — and the corpus's own strongest case, the nucleon, is P24's subject and dies there. So the reading is a restatement of the phenomenon in the corpus's vocabulary, not a derivation of it.

5. What the observational data actually require is negative pressure, not negative energy

This is the cleanest statement of why the corpus's negative vacuum is not the thing the data ask for. The 1998 discovery of accelerated expansion is fitted by a positive energy density with equation of state $w \simeq -1$ — that is, the acceleration requires $\rho + 3p < 0$, i.e. sufficiently negative pressure, and it is satisfied by a positive $\rho_{\Lambda}$ (S52 computes $\Lambda_{\rm obs} = +1{,}10\times10^{-56}$ cm$^{-2}$). So the corpus chose the one sign of the wrong quantity: it needs $\Lambda < 0$ for its existence conditions, while observation supplies $\Lambda > 0$ at $200\times$ the corpus's magnitude — with a positive energy density and negative pressure, not a negative energy density. A modern cosmology with negative energy density is not merely unfavoured; it is not what the data look like.

What Modern Physics Says, Fairly

The corpus's device is not absurd, and the fair statement of its standing is worth two sentences.

  • Negative energy densities do exist, quantum-mechanically. The Casimir configuration has negative energy density between the plates; what bounds it is not positivity but the quantum inequalities (Ford–Roman), which limit the magnitude and the duration of any negative-energy excursion. The corpus's $\rho$ is unbounded, cosmological and static — which is exactly the regime the inequalities forbid.
  • And the energy conditions are the reason the singularity theorems work — Penrose–Hawking need the strong or null condition, and a fluid with negative $\rho$ violates them. So the corpus's generalised sign freedom does not merely sit outside observation; it removes the very theorems that give general relativity its predictive structure, including the singularity theorems whose conclusion S51 accepts.

Verdict

A licence with a good argument, spent in the wrong sector, and not needed by the sector it was spent in.

  • What is genuinely to the corpus's credit. It saw the "energy from nothing" awkwardness in $\nabla_{\alpha}\rho u^{\alpha} = 0$ before most people would; it proposed a coherent fix — conserve $\rho^{2}$ (2-54) — and stated the price honestly; and it kept the sign freedom where it belongs, in the geometric sector, with Schur's theorem as the justification. §2.10's [SPEC] tag is right for the claim about the world, and the corpus's reasoning about it is better than the tag suggests.
  • Where it fails. (a) The cosmology reads a physical sign into $\rho$ although the corpus's own conservation law makes $\rho$'s sign non-physical. (b) The sign it needs is then fixed by a stipulation (S50), not by the licence. (c) The observational conflict (§8.5) is not with negative energy as such but with the sign of $\Lambda$, and the data need negative pressure. (d) The antiphoton is bookkeeping, and its strongest use (P9) is [FALS]. (e) The mass-defect explanation restates the phenomenon and dies with P24.
  • Net. §2.10 stays [SPEC] as a statement about the world; §2.11 stays [SPEC] as a relabelling; §2.12's phenomenon is [EST] and its explanation is [SPEC]. And this article's contribution is to relocate the corpus's licence: the negative vacuum is not a consequence of the negative-energy principle, and no repair of the corpus's cosmology can be made by arguing about that principle at all.

Summary of Notation

  • $\rho$ — the proper density of matter; the corpus allows either sign, but (2-54) conserves $\rho^{2}$, so the sign is a labelling.
  • $\rho_{+}, \rho_{-}$ — positive and negative proper densities.
  • $W_{\pm}$ — positive and negative energy; $M_{0}$ — proper mass, $M_{0} = \Omega_{0}\rho$; each allowed either sign.
  • $T_{\alpha}^{\ \alpha}$ — the diagonal curvature elements of the geometric tensor; their sign is free by Schur's theorem, and this is where RC 029 locates the licence.
  • $\overline{\gamma}$ — the corpus's "antiphoton", energy $-h\nu$. A negative-frequency mode, not a distinct particle.
  • $\rho^{2}$ — the conserved quantity of RC (2-54), $\nabla_{\alpha}\rho^{2}u^{\alpha} = 0$.

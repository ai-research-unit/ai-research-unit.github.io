Charon — What Survived (S62)

Consolidation, fifth article. What the corpus reaches that established theory does not: three items, and none of them is a constant.

Introduction

The question this article answers is asked in a natural form: "Charon computes the constants of nature — is that not a result no one else has?" The honest answer needs both halves. Established theory does not compute $\alpha$, the electron mass or $G$ either, so a theory that computed one would be a major result — and this one does not compute any. The negative half is settled elsewhere (S34's seed list; S35's $\alpha$ chain; S59's ledger). What this article records is the positive half: the content that is genuinely beyond established theory, so that later readers do not lose it and do not mistake it for the constants claim.

A theory computes a constant when a number it could have got wrong comes out right, at a point it did not start from. By that test the corpus computes none. Each of the six values it reports is one of the values it started from (Esp 057 §4: "numériquement identique aux valeurs numériques expérimentales"), and the executed search is a ±10 % box about those values (Esp 058: $3^{6} = 729$ sets, "essentiellement pour des raisons de temps de calcul"). The corpus's own output table at Esp 058 lists $c = 3\times10^{10}$ and $h = 6{,}61\times10^{-27}$ among its "computed" values — they are seeds.

So the corpus's novelty is structural and predictive, not numerical, and the three items below are the whole of it. They are a live prediction, a genuine disproof, and an open structure — and the fact that the programme produced all three kinds is itself the summary of what it was.

1. $\dot G/G \simeq 5\times10^{-16}$ yr$^{-1}$ — the one live prediction

Beyond established theory, because general relativity has $G$ constant. A fractional change of Newton's constant with cosmic time is a prediction no established theory makes: a measured rate of change of $G$ would falsify general relativity, not refine it. The corpus derives $\dot G/G \simeq 5\times10^{-16}$ yr$^{-1}$ — about one part in $2\times10^{15}$ per year, or 0,05 % over the age of the universe — from the complexified field equations, not by fitting it to anything.

Status: unconfirmed, testable, and not excluded. Present bounds on a varying $G$ do not reach $10^{-16}$ yr$^{-1}$: lunar-laser-ranging and binary-pulsar timing constrain it at the $10^{-12}$–$10^{-13}$ level, so the prediction sits two to four orders below the present frontier. That makes it the one claim in the corpus that could still be right without the rest of the programme being right, and therefore the one worth spending effort on. Whether the derivation chain supports the number is S40's subject — the one entry in the list whose subject is a prediction rather than a fit, with the chain S7 → S8 → S10 → S50 → S51 → S40.

The caveat, stated as plainly as the claim: unconfirmed means unconfirmed. The prediction is a live lead, not a result, and it is recorded here as such so that it is not quietly dropped for being unfashionable. Its testability has one internal condition — S40's open factor of 32 in the present rate — and one presentational condition: the value must be converted into the currency LLR and pulsar timing actually report (S61).

2. A periodic internal structure for the lepton — genuinely novel, and dead

Beyond established theory, because established theory says the electron is point-like. The corpus gives the lepton an internal clock and an internal size: a pulsation period $T = 4{,}9\times10^{-24}$ s, an elementary length $l_0 = 1{,}76\times10^{-14}$ cm, and radii of order $10^{-15}$–$10^{-14}$ cm coming from the minimum and maximum radii $R_0$ and $R$ of the pulsation. None of this exists in the Standard Model, where the electron is a structureless point with no internal period.

Status: disproved. Direct searches and precision measurements have ruled out an electron of that size: the $g-2$ agreement with the point-particle prediction and the point-like limits from collider and atomic physics leave no room for a $10^{-14}$ cm structure. The register records it as dead, with nothing found to retain.

It is listed here not as a lead but as the second kind of novelty: content genuinely beyond established theory and genuinely falsified. The distinction matters, because it is evidence that the corpus was making predictions and not merely relabelling: it made them, and this one was tested and failed. A programme that fails a real test is more informative than one that never risks a number — and the corpus risked numbers. $Z_{\max}$, the lepton tower, Axiom 6 and the electron's size were all stated sharply enough to be checked, and all four were checked.

The contrast with §1 and §3 is the article's shape: the same programme produced one claim still allowed, one claim refuted, and one structure with no test at all. A theory is not characterised by its ambition but by which of those three boxes its claims land in, and the corpus fills all three.

3. A structure for an inter-sector coupling — the framework's missing dynamics

Beyond established theory, and beyond the biquaternion framework too. The third item is structural rather than numerical, and it is stated in full in S64. The framework has no dynamics coupling its informational sector $\mathbb{M}_+$ to its material sector $\mathbb{M}_-$; its own account is that the split "is a grading of the vector space and not a splitting of the algebra", under which "multiplication mixes them". The corpus's whole theory is a proposal for exactly that coupling — its RÉEL is the material sector, its IMAGINAIRE the informational one, and its formalism is the machinery joining them. Whatever its numbers do, the structure of the join is content the framework does not have and established theory does not address at all.

Status: open, and it is a research direction rather than a computation. It cannot be imported into a dynamics the host does not possess, so it is a candidate structure — the one item in this list with neither a measurement to check it against nor a proof that it fails. The framework's work order carries it as the framework side's open item, together with the spin-two lead (S64): whether Charon's $T_{\alpha\beta} = \xi^{2}\rho^{2}u_\alpha u_\beta$, whose symmetric traceless part must be carried, can be written as a frame perturbation — the framework carries exactly that object through $h_{\mu\nu} = \langle\varepsilon_\mu,\delta\tilde E_\nu\rangle + \langle\delta\tilde E_\mu,\varepsilon_\nu\rangle$ with $16 = 10+6$.

Two structural warnings about this item, so that it is not oversold. First, it is not a derivation of anything: $R = \xi^{2}\rho^{2}+4\Lambda$ is an ansatz, and giving it as a dynamics is future work. Second, the corpus's own version of the coupling is a hypothesis of the same family as the closed $K=+1$ section and the second sheet — posited and licensed, not forced. What survives is the shape of the question, and the shape is genuinely the framework's missing piece.

4. What is deliberately not in this list

Four exclusions, each with its reason, because the natural failure of an article like this is inflation.

  • The Born rule. The register marks its derivation [SPEC]: "curvature = Born probability" is speculation, and only the Cartan part it rests on is [EST]. A speculation resting on a known result is not a fourth novel item, and the criticism is that it derives no number anyway.
  • The other five constants. They are the seeds. S34 and S35 give the verdict, and S59 tabulates it.
  • The lepton mass tower. It reproduces three measured masses with $T_0$ fitted — a fit, retained as a fit, and never a prediction. The tower beyond the three is dead (P1).
  • The cosmology, the cycles and the bounce. Mostly dead, with the bounce retained as an idea under S51. Their novelty was never the issue; their conflict with observation was.
  • The ψ-wave and EPR material. Dead as a mechanism; the ontology is kept separately under S54 and is not a physics claim.

And one addition, which the register states and this article must repeat because it is the whole point: the corpus's own output table lists $c$ and $h$ among its computed values. A reader who has been told that Charon computed the constants of nature should be shown that table. Nothing in this article's three items required the constants claim, and the constants claim required none of them.

Summary

The corpus reaches beyond established theory in three places, and none of them is a constant.

First: $\dot G/G \simeq 5\times10^{-16}$ yr$^{-1}$. A fractional change of Newton's constant with cosmic time is a prediction general relativity does not make, so a measured rate would falsify GR rather than refine it. The corpus derives it from the complexified field equations rather than fitting it. It is unconfirmed, testable and not excluded — two to four orders below the present lunar-laser-ranging and pulsar-timing frontier — which makes it the one claim that could still be right without the rest of the programme being right. The caveat is that unconfirmed means unconfirmed; it is a live lead, recorded so that it is not quietly dropped.

Second: a periodic internal structure for the lepton — genuinely novel and dead. An internal clock ($T = 4{,}9\times10^{-24}$ s), an elementary length ($l_0 = 1{,}76\times10^{-14}$ cm) and radii around $10^{-14}$ cm do not exist in the Standard Model, where the electron is a point. They are ruled out: $g-2$ agrees with the point-particle prediction to ten digits. It is listed as the second kind of novelty — content genuinely beyond established theory and genuinely falsified — and as evidence that the corpus made predictions rather than relabelling.

Third: a structure for an inter-sector coupling. The framework has no dynamics joining $\mathbb{M}_+$ to $\mathbb{M}_-$, and the corpus's whole theory is a proposal for exactly that coupling. The status is open: it is a research direction rather than a computation, with no measurement to check it against and no proof that it fails. Related, the spin-two lead: whether Charon's symmetric traceless source can be written as a frame perturbation.

And the negative half, which is not repeated but is not forgotten: established theory does not compute $\alpha$, the electron mass or $G$ either — so a theory that computed one would be a major result, and this one computes none. Its six values are its six inputs, and its own output table lists $c$ and $h$ among the computed. Deliberately excluded from this list: the Born rule, the other five constants, the lepton tower, the cosmology, and the ψ-wave and EPR material — each with its reason given above.

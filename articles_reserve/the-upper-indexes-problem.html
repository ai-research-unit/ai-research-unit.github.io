The Upper-Index Problem: The Caret Rule and the Corpus

The corpus has 403 physics articles and 2,407 mathematical ones. It writes a coordinate, a field component, a frame vector, a gamma and a sign symbol with a low index, and reserves the caret for what the convention calls a power or a name. This note states the rule, defines the screens that apply it, and records what fails.

The Rule

Operator and Calculus Conventions states the rule in one sentence:

A caret marks a power or a name, never a frame slot: $e^\lambda$, $2^k$ and $(-1)^k$ are powers, and $\mathrm{Cl}^i$, $K^0$, $M_4^\sigma$ and $\mathbb{B}^\sigma$ are names; a coordinate, a field component, a basis vector, a gamma and a sign symbol are written with a low index — $x_\mu$, $A_\mu$, $\vec e_i$, $\gamma_i$, $\varepsilon_{ijk}$ — and a contraction is written as an explicit sum with the sign vector or as the quaternionic product that carries it.

Index, Notation and Corpus Conventions states the same rule for mathematics, with the form that replaces the contraction.

The rule bans one use of the caret, not the caret. Three uses are legitimate, and each has a test a reader can apply without knowing the author's intent:

Use Test Examples
Power a repeated product $2^k$, $(-1)^k$, $e^\lambda$
Grade the degree of a grading $\mathrm{Cl}^i$, $\Omega^k$, $K^0$
Parameter the argument of a construction $A^\sigma$, $M_4^\sigma$, $\mathbb{B}^\sigma$

A caret outside these three marks a frame slot. The slot is low: $x_\mu$, $A_\mu$, $\vec e_i$, $\gamma_i$, $\varepsilon_{ijk}$. Every contraction is an explicit sum. The one further exception is the representation index, which keeps its height because it labels a representation rather than a component; the three physics cases are collected at the end.

The Screens

Three screens answer three disjoint questions.

  • Greek. A caret whose exponent is one of $\mu,\nu,\rho,\lambda,\kappa,\sigma,\tau$, on a base that could be a coordinate, a derivative, a field component or a metric. Mathematics has 1,514, on 98 distinct bases, each read; physics has 8.
  • Latin. A caret whose exponent is one of $i,j,k,l,a$, on fifteen component bases: $x$, $dx$, $dq$, $\partial$, $q$, $Q$, $e$, $v$, $\xi$, $g$, $\pi$, $F$, $R$, $\Gamma$, $\theta$ and $\omega$. 856 carets in mathematics and 0 in physics.
  • Internal. A caret on a colour or isospin index $a,b,c$ in a gauge article: 376 carets in 29 physics articles.

The Latin screen over-counts, and the count below removes what it over-counts: 115 powers, 2 cohomological degrees and 17 coframe carets. What remains is 722 frame slots.

The Greek Screen: Zero

Physics carries eight carets on the seven letters, and every one is legitimate: three integration limits $\int_0^\tau$ in The Electro-Gravimagnetic Field and the Magnetic Charge–Mass Hypothesis, one exponent $(\alpha/\pi)^\lambda$ in The Anomalous Magnetic Moment, one sign label $\nabla^\sigma$ in Bispinor Fields and the Fundamental Solution, and three from the sentence that states the rule: the power $e^\lambda$ and the two specimens $M_4^\sigma$ and $\mathbb{B}^\sigma$.

Mathematics carries 1,514 on 98 bases, and none marks a coordinate, a derivative, a field component or a metric. Four families cover them.

  • Fixed sets. $A^\sigma$, $G^\sigma$, $X^\sigma$, $F^\sigma$ and $R^\sigma$ are the invariant part under an involution. The caret names the involution.
  • Labels of a partition. $S^\lambda$, $\chi^\lambda$, $x^\lambda$ and $D^\mu$ index a partition, not a component of a vector.
  • Parameters. $\Sigma^\alpha_{u,u^{-1}}$ and $\Theta^\alpha_{a,b}$ are the signed sandwiches; the low slots carry the arguments, the caret carries the sign rule $\alpha$.
  • Multi-indices. $\partial^\alpha$ with $|\alpha|\le n$ is a derivative of order $\alpha$, a power in the notation of several variables.

Three near misses show why the screen was read and not grepped. $\varphi^\mu_t$ is the flow of the family $f(\cdot,\mu)$, with $\mu$ a parameter and $t$ the time. $D^\mu$ is the irreducible module attached to a partition $\mu$. $x^\mu$ in the theory of symmetric functions is a monomial whose exponent is the partition $\mu$. Each has the shape of a component; none is one.

The Word "Name"

The word "name" covers two different things in the rule's own examples, and neither is a name in the sense of a proper label.

  • $\mathrm{Cl}^i$ and $\Omega^k$ are grades: the caret is the degree of a grading.
  • $K^0$ is the degree-zero K-theory group: the caret is a cohomological degree.
  • $M_4^\sigma$ is the $\sigma$-fixed part and $\mathbb{B}^\sigma$ the $\sigma$-twist: the caret is a parameter.
  • $A^\sigma$, $G^\sigma$, $X^\sigma$ and $F^\sigma$ are fixed-point constructions, a role the rule never mentions.

The list in the rule contains no name. It contains two grades and two parameters.

Four costs follow.

  1. There is no test. Nothing separates $A^\sigma$ from $A^\mu$ except the article. The same letter $\sigma$ is a frame slot in physics, where the pass lowered it to $\sigma_i$, and an involution label in mathematics, where it stays high.
  2. The word carries the verdict. A caret is cleared when it is called a name, and the same caret would be debt under another word.
  3. It cannot be screened. A regression check cannot fire on a new $Q^\sigma$, because "name" can always be claimed.
  4. It is unstable. An earlier draft of this note lowered the specimens to $\mathrm{Cl}_i$, $K_0$ and $B^\sigma$. The convention is right about the height and wrong about the reason.

The repair is to drop "name" and keep three words that can be checked: power, grade, parameter. The convention can then list the permitted carets, and the gate can fire on everything else.

The Coframe Exception

Seventeen of the 856 are on $e^i$, and in each case $e^i$ is the dual of the frame $e_i$, with $e^i(e_j)=\delta^i_j$. Lowering them would collide with the frame and erase the pairing. The rule does not cover a dual basis, and the seventeen are not debt: either the convention adds a coframe exception, or the articles rename the dual to $\epsilon^i$. They sit in Geometric Calculus and the Vector Derivative (11), Symmetric Tensors and Spherical Harmonics (3), Hermitian Connections and the Adjoint (1), Exterior Powers (1) and The Coboundary Operator (1).

What Was Removed

115 powers: 65 powers of the field order $q$ in the finite-field articles, 16 powers in Part VI, 11 powers of a quaternion variable, 13 powers of a generating function, 10 powers in the cohomology operations. 2 degrees: $Q^i$ in a resolution $Q^0\to Q^1\to\cdots$ in Derived Functors. 17 coframes, as above.

The Internal Screen: 376 in 29

The physics corpus carries a class that the rule does not name. 376 carets stand on internal colour or isospin indices — $\mathcal{A}_\mu^a$, $A_\mu^a$, $c^a$, $B^a$, $T^a$, $\tau^a$, $\sigma^a$, $\pi^a$, $F^a_{\mu\nu}$ and $f^{abc}$ — in the Yang–Mills, BRST, anomaly, pion and gluon articles.

The class is open, and the corpus is not consistent inside it. Four articles write both heights in the same text: The Gluon ($T^a$ 4, $T_a$ 12), The Yang–Mills Path Integral (8 and 16), The W and Z Bosons (5 and 7) and Electroweak Theory (2 and 6). $\pi^a$ is a field component, which the rule sends low. If the height records the representation, the class is an exception like Okubo's; if it records a component, it is debt.

The Indices That Stay

Three physics rows keep their heights, because the height labels a representation: Okubo's $A^i{}_k$ in SU(3) Representations and the Gell-Mann–Okubo Mass Formula, the dimensions $\mathbb{C}^k$ and $\mathbb{C}^N$ in The ADHM Construction and Biquaternion Instanton Data, and the Bhabha pair $\Lambda^l{}_j$ and $\Lambda^{\bar l}_j$. Lowering them would make a mixed object look Euclidean.

The Next Pass

  1. The mathematics Latin pass. 722 slots. Start with Lagrangian and Hamiltonian Systems (164) and Poisson Geometry (54), then the connections and the tensors.
  2. The internal class. Decide whether a colour or isospin index is a representation label or a component, and write the decision into the convention.
  3. The convention article. Replace "name" with grade and parameter, add the coframe case, and keep the specimens high.
  4. The gate. Screen with an explicit list of permitted forms, not with a word.

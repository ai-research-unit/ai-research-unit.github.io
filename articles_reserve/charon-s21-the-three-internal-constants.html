Charon — The Three Internal Constants (S21)

Audits §3.5 and §4.8 of CHARON_IDEAS_AND_CLAIMS.md, against RC ch. III (scans 051, 052, 053) and Esp §VI (scan 058).

Introduction

The register's verdict on §4.8 — "FALS as stated: six fundamental constants computed should be read as one output, five inputs" — is right, and this article proves it instead of counting it.

The corpus's three "internal constants" of the imaginary sheet are, in closed form,

$$\alpha = \frac{m + 3\mu - 2\tau}{4\pi^{2}}, \qquad \beta = -\frac{h}{4\pi^{3}c}\cdot\frac{\tau + m - 2\mu}{\tau - \mu}, \qquad T_{0} = \frac{2h}{c^{2}(\tau-\mu)}$$

where $m$, $\mu$, $\tau$ are the three measured charged-lepton masses. The decisive line is the first: $\alpha$ contains no $h$ and no $c$. The $h/c^{2}T_{0}$ term in (3-68) is identically $(\tau-\mu)/2$, because $T_{0}$ is that term inverted — so the two cancelling is not a coincidence, it is the algebra of a definition. A constant that emerges from a system containing $h$ and $c$ cannot be independent of them unless the system was solved rather than derived, and here the solution is one line of substitution.

So the register's "five inputs" is not a count that happens to come out that way; it is what the printed equations say, and this article exhibits it.

What the Corpus Claims

§3.5 — the three internal constants are computed from the three measured charged-lepton masses: $\alpha = -1{,}46\times10^{-25}$ g, $\beta = -1{,}74\times10^{-39}$ g cm, $T_{0} = 2h/[c^{2}(\tau-\mu)]$. RC (3-68). Tag: [MATH] (definition), [SPEC] (physics). The register's gloss: "Note the word 'computed': they are defined by inverting the equations using measured m, μ, τ. This is important for judging the 'parameter-free' claim (§10.1)."

§4.8 — the lepton model is "entirely devoid of arbitrary parameters". Esp §VI. Tag: [FALS] as stated. The register: "Count the inputs honestly: c, h, m_e, m_μ, m_τ are measured and used; α, β, T₀ are then defined by those inputs; ξ, l₀ are fixed by one closure condition. The genuine outputs are e (§7.1) and the lepton tower (falsified). 'Six fundamental constants computed' should be read as 'one output, five inputs'."

1. The Corpus Counts Its Own Inputs

The corpus states the count itself, in the same section that prints (3-68), and this is the article's textual anchor. RC 051:

"Une première analyse du Système $E$ montre que, pour calculer l'ensemble des solutions particulières, nous avons besoin des valeurs de cinq des paramètres mentionnés ci-dessus, avec bien entendu leurs valeurs propres des trois leptons chargés connus ($m$, $\mu$ et $\tau$). Les équations (3-53) du Système $E$ fournissent alors facilement, en fonction de ces paramètres bien mesurés par l'expérience, les valeurs des trois principaux paramètres inconnus, à savoir $\alpha$, $\beta$ et $T_{0}$."

Five measured parameters in, three constants out, and the corpus says so before it prints them. The five are $c$, $h$, $m$, $\mu$, $\tau$ — cosmology's two universal constants and the three lepton masses the model is meant to explain.

And RC 053 says what "computed" means, in the corpus's own word: "Rappelons que les constantes $\alpha$, $\beta$ et $T_{0}$ du modèle éonique ont été calculées (3-68) en supposant que les 3 premières valeurs $n = 0$, $1$ et $2$ … correspondaient respectivement au positron, au muon et au tau." "Calculées" here is "solved for", and the assumption named is the identification of the three leptons with the first three integers. So the corpus's "computed" and the register's "defined" are the same claim in two vocabularies, and the corpus's is the more precise of the two.

2. The Inversion, and Its Closed Form

Transcribed (3-68), RC 051:

$$(3\text{-}68)\quad \left\{ \begin{aligned} \alpha &= \frac{m + \mu}{4\pi^{2}} - \frac{h}{\pi^{2}c^{2}T_{0}} = -1{,}46\cdot10^{-25}\ \mathrm{g} \\ \beta &= \frac{(\mu - m)c^{2}T_{0} - 2h}{8\pi^{3}c} = -1{,}74\cdot10^{-39}\ \mathrm{g\,cm} \\ T_{0} &= \frac{2h}{c^{2}(\tau-\mu)} = 4{,}93\cdot10^{-24}\ \mathrm{s} \end{aligned} \right.$$

$T_{0}$ is given outright, so the system is triangular and the elimination takes two lines. Substituting $T_{0}$ into $\alpha$ and $\beta$:

$$\frac{h}{c^{2}T_{0}} = \frac{h}{c^{2}}\cdot\frac{c^{2}(\tau-\mu)}{2h} = \frac{\tau-\mu}{2} \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \boxed{\ \alpha = \frac{m + 3\mu - 2\tau}{4\pi^{2}}\ }$$

$$\beta = \frac{(\mu-m)\frac{2h}{\tau-\mu} - 2h}{8\pi^{3}c} = \frac{2h\left[\frac{\mu-m}{\tau-\mu} - 1\right]}{8\pi^{3}c} \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \boxed{\ \beta = -\frac{h}{4\pi^{3}c}\cdot\frac{\tau + m - 2\mu}{\tau-\mu}\ }$$

Both are exact, and both are the same expressions S37 reached from the Système L side. S37's $\beta = -h(1-\varepsilon)/(4\pi^{3}c)$ with $\varepsilon = (\mu-m)/(\tau-\mu)$ is this expression, since $1 - \varepsilon = (\tau + m - 2\mu)/(\tau-\mu)$. Two independent routes — S37 through L7, L8 and L16, this article through (3-68) — give the same closed form, which is a real internal consistency of the corpus.

3. The Decisive Line: $\alpha$ Contains No $h$ and No $c$

This is the finding, and it is a one-line observation with a long reach.

Every occurrence of $h$ and $c$ in the $\alpha$ equation comes through the single term $h/(\pi^{2}c^{2}T_{0})$ — and $T_{0}$ was defined as $2h/[c^{2}(\tau-\mu)]$ precisely so that this term is the pure number $(\tau-\mu)/(2\pi^{2})$. So the corpus's first internal constant of the imaginary sheet is a linear combination of the three lepton masses divided by $4\pi^{2}$, and nothing else:

$$\alpha = \frac{m + 3\mu - 2\tau}{4\pi^{2}}$$

No $h$. No $c$. No $\xi$. No $l_{0}$. No solution of any system. If $\alpha$ had been derived from the Système $E$ — a system whose equations contain $h$ and $\xi = \Omega_{0}c/\hbar$ — the result would carry them, or the cancellation would be a theorem of the system that the corpus would have had to prove. It is not a theorem; it is the definition of $T_{0}$ substituted back.

The corollary is worth stating because it is the sharpest form of §4.8's failure. $\alpha$ is not merely fitted; it is nothing but the lepton masses. Its value moves whenever they are remeasured, and the corpus's own two books already disagree about it for exactly that reason (§4).

And evaluated at the corpus's own 1983 masses ($\tau = 1784{,}2$ MeV):

Three things follow, and all three belong in the register.

  1. RC's printed $\alpha = -1{,}46\times10^{-25}$ is the value today's masses give, not the value its own decade's masses give (which is $-1{,}4680\times10^{-25}$, printing as $-1{,}47$). Recorded as an observation, not as an error: $\alpha$ is a mass combination, so a book published in 1987 and a measurement programme that ran for forty years after it disagree at the third figure, and the corpus's figure is the one that aged well.
  2. Esp's printed $\alpha = -1{,}472\times10^{-25}$ is $0{,}27\,\%$ from the 1983 mass value, and RC's is $0{,}55\,\%$ from it. The two books' $\alpha$'s differ by $0{,}8\,\%$ while the underlying mass inputs differ by $0{,}44\,\%$ in $T_{0}$ — so the discrepancy between the books is the mass measurement's, amplified by the fact that $\alpha$ is a difference of two nearly cancelling terms (§5).
  3. The printed $\beta = -1{,}74\times10^{-39}$ is not reproduced by the printed formula at any mass set: it is $4{,}0\,\%$ from the closed form, and S37 has already priced that $4{,}0\,\%$. This article does not re-open it; the point here is that the same $4{,}0\,\%$ is what makes $\beta$ the corpus's best near-miss rather than a hit (S37: carrying the derived $\beta$ turns G-C's $+4{,}38\,\%$ into $+2{,}11\,\%$).

5. The Nesting: None of the Three Carries Independent Information

The register's count said "five inputs, one output". The closed forms say something slightly different and slightly worse for the corpus: the three constants are nested restatements of the five, with no independent content at any level.

constant decomposition dominant term
$\alpha$ $(m+\mu)/(4\pi^{2}) - (\tau-\mu)/(2\pi^{2})$ = $+4{,}794\times10^{-27} - 1{,}5093\times10^{-25}$ the mass difference, $31{,}5\times$ the other term
$\beta$ $-h/(4\pi^{3}c)$ corrected by $(\tau+m-2\mu)/(\tau-\mu)$ $h/c$ alone, to $6{,}3\,\%$
$T_{0}$ $2h/[c^{2}(\tau-\mu)]$ $h$, $c$, and the mass difference: nothing else

So $\alpha$ is $97\,\%$ a mass difference, $\beta$ is $94\,\%$ the pure constant combination $h/c$ (S37's finding), and $T_{0}$ is $h$, $c$ and one mass difference exactly. $\alpha$'s two terms are a small mass sum and a large mass difference, which is why $\alpha$ inherits the sensitivity of a subtraction and why §4's three values move together.

And the tower closes the argument, because (3-76) turns the input back into a "result" in one line. RC 053's (3-76) is $m_{n} = \mu + 2(n-1)h/(c^{2}T_{0})$, and its spacing is

$$\frac{2h}{c^{2}T_{0}} = \frac{2h}{c^{2}}\cdot\frac{c^{2}(\tau-\mu)}{2h} = \tau - \mu \qquad\text{identically}$$

So the charged-lepton tower's step is $\tau-\mu$ by definition, and the "arithmetic progression with equal spacing $\approx 1{,}67$ GeV" that §4.3 marks [FALS] is the fitted input printed back as a prediction. This article does not own §4.3 or R7; it records that the closed form shows why the tower cannot be evidence, which is the missing sentence in the register's R7 entry.

6. A Third Internal Inconsistency, and It Is in $T_{0}$'s Own Section

RC 053's (3-75) and (3-75-a) give the éon neutrino's energy as

$$W_{n} = 2\left(n + \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\frac{h}{T_{0}} \qquad\text{printed numerically as}\qquad W_{n} = 2\left(n + \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\times 0{,}832\ \mathrm{GeV}$$

which requires $h/T_{0} = 0{,}832$ GeV. From the printed $T_{0} = 4{,}93\times10^{-24}$ s,

$$\frac{h}{T_{0}} = 0{,}83888\ \mathrm{GeV}$$

a difference of $0{,}82\,\%$ — so the corpus's own printed $T_{0}$ and its own printed energy coefficient do not close at the printed precision. Either (3-75-a)'s coefficient carries a transcription digit, or the $T_{0}$ used for it was $\approx 4{,}97\times10^{-24}$ s. Filed in the check queue against scans 053 and, for the parallel table, Esp 058. It is mild in itself and it is the third instance of the same pattern in this block — S20's $\mathcal{R}$, S20's $R''$, and now $h/T_{0}$ — which is why it is worth recording rather than smoothing over: the éon's numbers are quoted at a precision their own equations do not support.

7. What a Derivation of $\alpha$ Would Have Looked Like

The register's S6 requires the success criteria to be stated in advance, so this article states the one that applies here — not for $\alpha$'s gate, which is S35's and S37's, but for the ledger's sake.

A derivation of $\alpha$ from the Système $E$ would be an expression for $\alpha$ containing $h$ and $\xi$ in a combination that is not forced to cancel, obtained from (3-53) without first defining $T_{0}$ in terms of $\tau-\mu$. The corpus's route cannot produce such an expression, because it begins by fixing $T_{0}$ from the measured mass difference and then eliminates. So the honest description of RC (3-68) is: three equations, one of which is a definition, solved for the other two. That is precisely §3.5's "[MATH] (definition), [SPEC] (physics)", and the article's contribution is to show the definition is load-bearing rather than incidental.

The positive half, which must be stated with it. The inversion itself is competent, the closed forms are exact, and S37's Système L route gives the same $\beta$ by a different path — so the corpus's constant algebra is internally coherent. What it is not is parameter-free, and the two printed sentences that claim otherwise (§4.8's, and Esp's "entièrement dépourvue de paramètres arbitraires") are the claims to correct.

8. Verdict

§3.5 and §4.8 both stand; the article supplies the proof of §4.8 and the corrected wording of §3.5.

  • Confirmed, with closed form: $\alpha = (m+3\mu-2\tau)/(4\pi^{2})$ — no $h$, no $c$, no $\xi$; $\beta = -h(\tau+m-2\mu)/[4\pi^{3}c(\tau-\mu)]$, agreeing with S37's Système L result; $T_{0} = 2h/[c^{2}(\tau-\mu)]$.
  • Confirmed from the corpus's own text: the five inputs are counted by RC 051 itself, and RC 053's "calculées" means solved-for.
  • New: $\alpha$ is $97\,\%$ a mass difference and $\beta$ $94\,\%$ the pure $h/c$ combination, so none of the three constants carries information beyond $h$, $c$, $m$, $\mu$, $\tau$ — and (3-76)'s tower spacing is $\tau-\mu$ identically, which is why the tower cannot be evidence for the model.
  • Recorded: RC's $\alpha$ is the value today's masses give and not its own decade's; the two books' $\alpha$'s differ by the mass measurement's own drift; the printed $\beta$ is $4{,}0\,\%$ from the corpus's formula (S37's pricing stands, not reopened here).
  • Check queue: (3-75-a)'s $0{,}832$ GeV against the printed $T_{0}$ ($0{,}82\,\%$ apart).
  • §4.8 wording: keep [FALS] as stated; replace the register's "read as one output, five inputs" with "the five inputs are stated by the corpus, the three constants are their nested restatements, and $\alpha$'s independence of $h$ and $c$ is the algebra of the definition."
  • §3.5's tag is unchanged — [MATH] (definition), [SPEC] (physics) — and the article's result is that "definition" is the operative word, not a formality.

Part S / Part P. Part S: the inversion, the closed forms, the counting, the comparison with S37's route — audited algebra. Part P: the claim that these constants are properties of an imaginary micro-universe — because the object whose constants they are is the one §3.7 deletes (S17, P8). The algebra survives; the physics does not.

Summary of Notation

  • $m$, $\mu$, $\tau$ — the measured electron, muon and tau masses. Charon writes the positron's mass $m_{0}$ for $m$; (3-68) uses $m$ and $m_{0}$ interchangeably. The series writes $m$.
  • $\alpha$, $\beta$ — the éon's two internal constants. $[\![\alpha]\!] = \mathrm{g}$ (a mass), $[\![\beta]\!] = \mathrm{g\,cm}$ (a mass-length).
  • $T_{0}$ — the éon's pulsation period, claimed common to all charged leptons by Axiom 7 (§10.1). Fitted: $2h/[c^{2}(\tau-\mu)]$.
  • $\varepsilon$ — S37's abbreviation, $(\mu-m)/(\tau-\mu) = 0{,}0629$. $\beta = -h(1-\varepsilon)/(4\pi^{3}c)$; S37's form and this article's are the same expression.
  • $n$ — the integer labelling the charged leptons, $n = 0, 1, 2$ for $e$, $\mu$, $\tau$. The corpus's "assumption" in RC 053.
  • (3-53) — the Système $E$; (3-68) — the three constants; (3-76) — the tower; (3-75-a) — the energy coefficient $0{,}832$ GeV.
  • "calculées" — the corpus's word at RC 053. Translated as "solved for", which is what the equations show.

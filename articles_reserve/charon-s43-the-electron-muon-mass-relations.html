Charon — The Electron and Muon Masses from (218)–(221) (S43)

Audits inventory §4.6 (the lepton masses computed from a potential energy $(\alpha \pm \beta/R)$ plus spin and pulse kinetic terms, with equipartition). Sources: Esp 050 §8, eqs (218)–(221); Esp 051 §9–§11; Esp 052 §10–§11, eqs (229)–(234); RC 046–RC 048, eqs (3-31)–(3-53); RC 051 (3-68).

Introduction

The register's §4.6 verdict is "consistent, but not reproducible: two integrands are unreadable in the scan" — and the integrands have been recovered. The equation pair is not merely consistent: it closes on the corpus's own numbers, it forces its own branch assignment, and it contains a cancellation that decides where the electron mass comes from. The recovered integrands are (220) and (221) from Esp 050 §8 and Esp 051, together with the spin and pulse equations (223), (224), (229), (230) that fix the kinetic terms. What the pair does, verified: it determines $\alpha$ — and the value it determines agrees with the corpus's printed (3-68) $\alpha$ to $0{,}02\,\%$ — and it determines the product $\beta I$, where $I = \int_{0}^{T_{0}} dt/R$ is the lepton's own trajectory integral. With $\beta$ taken from the corpus's independent route, $I$ follows, and the mean inverse radius it implies is $\langle R \rangle = 2{,}35\times10^{-14}$ cm — inside the éon's own turning-point band $[0{,}80, 4{,}82]\times10^{-14}$ cm. That is a necessary-condition check, and it passes.

The second finding is the one that matters, and it is a precision statement rather than a numerical one. In the electron's equation the three contributions are $-1{,}2831\times10^{-26}$, $+6{,}2094\times10^{-27}$ and $+6{,}6261\times10^{-27}$ erg s, and the left-hand side they must reproduce is $4{,}0363\times10^{-30}$: the electron's mass is the residual of a cancellation of one part in $3{,}2\times10^{3}$. The corpus's constants are carried to three figures. So §4.6's obstruction is not the missing integrand; it is that the model's electron mass is a difference of two large terms, and the model states its own terms to three digits. The same structure appears one level down: the muon–electron splitting is the difference of $2h = 1{,}3252\times10^{-26}$ and $4\pi^{2}c^{2}|\beta|I = 1{,}2419\times10^{-26}$, i.e. a $6{,}3\,\%$ cancellation. The lepton sector's two light masses are both residuals, and the corpus never says so.

The third finding closes a loop the corpus leaves open. Esp 052 §11 says the pair "cannot be jointly extended" and that only (221) may be used for the heavy leptons, and gives as its reason the observed $\tau \to \mu$ transition. That the electron's branch cannot be extended is also what S44's sign convention implies — the electron is the corpus's antimatter member — and the two reasons agree. Once (221)'s integrand is reused for the tau, the potential integral cancels identically between the two mass equations and the ladder falls out exactly: $m_{0\tau} - m_{0\mu} = 2(n_{\tau} - n_{\mu})h/(c^{2}T_{0})$, which is RC (3-76) and the register's R7. The cancellation is exact, and it is also the reason the ladder is not a prediction: the step and the definition of $T_{0}$ are the same number.

The verdict, per rules R3 and R6. R3: the pair can now be recomputed to the point where only one unexhibited object remains — the integral $\int dt/R$ — and even that is checkable through its two turning-point bounds. R6: coherence — the pair closes and the branch assignment is forced; reproducibility — the pair's $\alpha$ is the printed $\alpha$ and $I$ implies a radius inside the trajectory; correspondence — the pair makes no contact with experiment at all, because the éon is unobservable and the one number that could be read off it, the electron mass, is the corpus's own input. §4.6's [SPEC] tag stands and is upgraded in one respect: what the equations show is a competent inversion, not a fit of independent numbers.

What the Corpus Claims

The two energies, from Esp 050 §8:

a) Une énergie potentielle : $E_{p} = 2\pi^{2}R^{3}c^{2}(\frac{\alpha}{R^{3}} \pm \frac{\beta}{R^{4}}) = 2\pi^{2}c^{2}(\alpha \pm \frac{\beta}{R})$, où $2\pi^{2}R^{3}$ est le volume riemannien d'un espace sphérique de rayon $R$ ($K = +1$). […] Le signe $-$ correspond à l'énergie potentielle de l'électron, le signe $+$ à celle du muon.

b) Une énergie cinétique : $E_{ce} = \frac{S_{e}}{T} + \frac{P_{e}}{T} = \frac{2(n_{e}+\frac{1}{2})h}{T}$. […] Le facteur 2 indique l'équipartition du spin et du pulse du modèle leptonic.

The mass equations, (220) and (221):

$$\int_{0}^{T} 2\pi^{2}c^{2}\left(\alpha - \frac{\beta}{R}\right)dt + S_{e} + P_{e} = m_{0}c^{2}T \qquad \text{(220, électron)}$$

$$\int_{0}^{T} 2\pi^{2}c^{2}\left(\alpha + \frac{\beta}{R}\right)dt + S_{\mu} + P_{\mu} = \mu_{0}c^{2}T \qquad \text{(221, muon)}$$

The corpus's time-averaged, $n = 0$ form of (220), which is the equation S44's convention acts on (RC 048, (3-52)):

$$\frac{1}{T}\int_{0}^{T} 2\pi^{2}c^{2}\left(\alpha - \frac{\beta}{R}\right)d\tau + \frac{h}{T} = m_{0}c^{2}$$

The corpus's own refusal to extend the pair, Esp 052 §11:

L'analyse des équations (220) et (221) fournissant respectivement les masses propres de l'électron et du muon ne peuvent pas conjointement être prolongées pour s'appliquer aux masses d'autres particules éventuelles […] mais qu'on ne peut utiliser qu'une seule des deux, à savoir l'équation (221) qui permet la transition $\tau \to \mu$ expérimentalement.

And its consequence, Esp 052 §11 and RC 053 (3-76):

$$m_{0\tau} = m_{0\mu} + \frac{2(n_{\tau}-n_{\mu})h}{c^{2}T} \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad m_{n} = \mu + \frac{2(n-1)h}{c^{2}T_{0}}$$

1. The two terms, their signs, and their dimensions

The potential term is a Riemannian-volume integral of a density, and its two signs come from the fundamental equation, not from a choice. (200)/(3-32) carries $\alpha^{2}$ and $\beta^{2}$ — the corpus says so explicitly at Esp 050 §8 ("Etant donné que (200) comporte les carrés $\alpha^{2}$ et $\beta^{2}$, il y a deux énergies potentielles possibles") — so once $\alpha$ and $\beta$ have definite signs, the energy density $2\pi^{2}R^{3}c^{2}(\alpha/R^{3} \pm \beta/R^{4})$ has two branches, and the corpus assigns the minus branch to the electron and the plus branch to the muon.

Dimensions, verified. $\alpha$ carries grams and $\beta$ carries g cm, so $2\pi^{2}c^{2}(\alpha \pm \beta/R)$ is an energy; (220) equates a time-integral of an energy (an action, g cm$^{2}$ s$^{-1}$) to $m_{0}c^{2}T$ (also an action). The equation is a balance of actions over one pulsation, not of energies — which is why $T$, and not $T^{2}$, appears on the right. The corpus's printed values, $\alpha = -1{,}46\times10^{-25}$ g and $\beta = -1{,}74\times10^{-39}$ g cm (RC 051 (3-68); Esp 058's table gives $-1{,}472\times10^{-25}$ and $-1{,}7\times10^{-39}$), are in these units.

The kinetic term is the corpus's equipartition, and it is the same for every equation in the pair. $S = P = (n+\frac{1}{2})h$ by (223)/(229) and (224)/(230), hence $S + P = 2(n+\frac{1}{2})h$; the factor 2 is the equipartition of spin and pulse (axiom 4, audited in S11 and S32). So the two equations differ in exactly two places: the sign of the $\beta/R$ branch, and the integer $n$ — $n_{e} = 0$, $n_{\mu} = 1$ (Esp 052 §11: électron : n = 0 […] muon : n = 1 […] lepton tau : n = 2).

2. The integral the corpus never writes

Neither book exhibits the trajectory $R(\tau)$, so neither exhibits $\int dt/R$; what the corpus gives instead is the trajectory's two turning points. RC (3-72) computes $R_{0} = 8{,}01\times10^{-15}$ cm at $\tau = 0$ and $\mathcal{R} = 4{,}82\times10^{-14}$ cm at $\tau = T_{0}/2$ (Esp 058's table gives $7{,}8\times10^{-15}$ and $4{,}8\times10^{-14}$), and these are the lepton model's own numbers. They bound the integral without evaluating it:

$$\frac{T_{0}}{\mathcal{R}} \le I = \int_{0}^{T_{0}} \frac{dt}{R} \le \frac{T_{0}}{R_{0}} \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad R_{0} \le \langle R \rangle := \frac{T_{0}}{I} \le \mathcal{R}.$$

So every statement the pair can make about $I$ is checkable against $[0{,}80, 4{,}82]\times10^{-14}$ cm, and the article's numerical work consists of reducing the pair to objects of that kind. This is the sharpened form of §4.6's flag: the integrand is no longer missing (Esp 050 §8 and Esp 051 §10 carry it), the primitive is not given, and one necessary condition on the primitive survives.

3. What the pair determines, and the agreement with (3-68)

Add and subtract the two equations — permissible because both use the same $T = T_{0}$ and the same trajectory $R(\tau)$ — and the integrals separate.

Adding, the $\beta$ terms cancel and $2\pi^{2}c^{2}\alpha T_{0}$ doubles:

$$\alpha = \frac{(m_{0}+\mu_{0})c^{2}T_{0} - 4h}{4\pi^{2}c^{2}T_{0}}$$

Subtracting, the $\alpha$ terms cancel and the $\beta$ terms double:

$$|\beta|\,I = \frac{2h - (\mu_{0}-m_{0})c^{2}T_{0}}{4\pi^{2}c^{2}}$$

Evaluated with the corpus's own masses, $h$, $c$ and $T_{0} = 2h/(c^{2}(\tau-\mu)) = 4{,}9298\times10^{-24}$ s:

The third row is a check and only a check. $I$ is not printed by the corpus, $\beta$ is taken from the corpus's independent route, and the resulting mean radius is required merely to lie between the turning points. That is a shape remark, not a check, and it is labelled as one.

The branch assignment is forced, and this is a finding rather than a convention. If the electron were placed on the plus branch and the muon on the minus branch, the two equations would give $I = -2{,}10\times10^{-10}$ s cm$^{-1}$ — negative, hence impossible for a positive $R(\tau)$. So of the four ways to assign two branches to two leptons, only the corpus's assignment survives the requirement that the mean inverse radius be positive, and the corpus never states the argument. With that assignment fixed, the pair is a complete statement of the two mass equations: given the masses, it fixes $\alpha$ and the product $\beta I$; it leaves $\beta$ and $I$ individually free, and it is the tau that closes the system (RC (3-68) determines $T_{0}$, hence $\beta$, hence $I$).

The cancellation is one part in $3{,}2\times10^{3}$: the potential and kinetic parts cancel to $0{,}03\,\%$ and the electron's mass is what is left. Two consequences follow, and both are the article's, not the corpus's.

(a) The corpus's three-figure constants cannot support a four-figure cancellation. $\alpha$ is printed as $-1{,}46\times10^{-25}$ but computed as $-1{,}4672\times10^{-25}$; the third figure alone moves the first term by $0{,}4\,\%$, i.e. by ten times the residual. The electron mass therefore comes out of the corpus's inputs, not out of its equation: the equation is satisfied because $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $T_{0}$ were fitted to $m$, $\mu$ and $\tau$ in the first place. This is S34's and S21's finding reached from the equation's side, and it is sharper here: the mechanism by which the fit is hidden is a cancellation.

(b) The same happens to the muon–electron splitting. Subtracting the equations gives $(\mu_{0}-m_{0})c^{2}T_{0} = 2h - 4\pi^{2}c^{2}|\beta|I = 1{,}3252\times10^{-26} - 1{,}2419\times10^{-26} = 8{,}3336\times10^{-28}$ erg s, a $6{,}3\,\%$ cancellation. So both quantities the pair produces — the electron's mass and the $\mu$–$e$ splitting — are differences of nearly equal large terms, and the ratio $\mu/m = 207$ is the ratio of two such residuals. The corpus presents the pair as the computation of two masses; it is the computation of two residuals from inputs that already contain them.

5. What the pair produces once it is extended: the ladder, exactly

The first two members of the ladder cannot be wrong, and the members above the tau never appear — LEP excluded charged leptons to $\sim100$ GeV (R7, P1). So the pair's extension is not a prediction that failed; it is a definition that was extrapolated, and the article records that the potential-integral cancellation is what makes the extrapolation look like a computation.

Two smaller consistencies are worth recording. (i) The tau's branch is the muon's — Esp 052's empirical reason (the observed $\tau \to \mu$ transition) and S44's structural reason (the electron is the corpus's antimatter member, so the matter ladder starts at the muon) agree, and the agreement is not an accident of wording. (ii) The éon neutrino's energy quantum $W_{n} = 2(n+\frac{1}{2})h/T_{0}$ (RC 053, (3-75)) and the mass step are the same quantity in two units: the printed $0{,}832$ GeV doubles to $1{,}664$ GeV against the ladder's $1{,}6778$ GeV, i.e. the corpus's own $0{,}8\,\%$ rounding of $T_{0}$.

6. Verdict

§4.6's tag [SPEC] stands, with the flag cleared and replaced. The integrands are recovered; the equation pair closes; the branch assignment is forced by positivity; the pair's $\alpha$ is (3-68)'s $\alpha$ to $0{,}002\,\%$, and the pair's other output puts the trajectory's mean inverse radius at $2{,}35\times10^{-14}$ cm inside the corpus's own turning points. What remains is not a missing integrand but a missing primitive, and the primary object of the pair — the electron's mass — is a one-part-in-$3{,}2\times10^{3}$ cancellation of terms the corpus prints to three figures.

Per rule R6, the three questions are answered separately. Coherence: passes, and does so in a form the corpus does not claim — the pair and (3-68) are one constraint. Reproducibility: passes at the level of $\alpha$ and the bound on $\langle R \rangle$; it cannot pass at the level of the electron mass, and the reason is structural, not clerical. Correspondence: fails, and not for a numerical reason — the pair's objects are the éon's internal radius, temperature and trajectory, none of which is observable, and the one externally meaningful number it touches is the electron mass, which the corpus put in. So §4.6 is not a falsified claim and not an established one: it is a competent inversion of the lepton spectrum into the éon model, whose numerical content is the spectrum itself.

Summary

The register's §4.6 entry — the electron and muon masses computed from a potential energy plus spin and pulse kinetic terms — was flagged as unreproducible because the integrands of (220) and (221) were missing from the scan. They are recovered (Esp 050 §8, Esp 051 §10), and the article recomputes the pair; the findings are four.

First, the pair closes, and the corpus's two books say the same thing twice. Adding and subtracting (220) and (221) determines $\alpha$ and the product $\beta I$, and the $\alpha$ so determined agrees with RC's printed (3-68) $\alpha$ to $0{,}002\,\%$ — with an exact identity behind it, $\alpha = (m+3\mu-2\tau)/4\pi^{2}$ (S21). With $\beta$ from the corpus's independent route, $\beta I$ gives a trajectory integral $I = \int dt/R$ of $2{,}10\times10^{-10}$ s cm$^{-1}$, i.e. a mean inverse radius $\langle R \rangle = 2{,}35\times10^{-14}$ cm, inside the éon's own turning points $[0{,}80, 4{,}82]\times10^{-14}$ cm.

Second, the branch assignment is forced, not chosen. Putting the electron on the $\alpha + \beta/R$ branch makes $I$ negative; only the corpus's assignment gives a positive mean inverse radius. So the two signs are constrained by the physics and the corpus never says so.

Third, the electron mass is a residual of a cancellation of one part in $3{,}2\times10^{3}$, and the $\mu$–$e$ splitting of one part in $16$. The corpus's constants are carried to three figures; the third figure of $\alpha$ moves the leading term by ten times the residual. The pair's numerical content therefore comes from the fitted inputs and not from the equation — which is S34's finding, here located in the arithmetic.

Fourth, the extension to the heavy leptons is exact and empty. With (221)'s integrand reused, the potential integrals cancel identically and the ladder $m_{n} = \mu + 2(n-1)h/(c^{2}T_{0})$ falls out — the same equation that defines $T_{0}$ from $\tau-\mu$. The step is $1{,}6778$ GeV and the corpus's own $\tau-\mu$ is $2{,}9910\times10^{-24}$ g to every digit printed, so the ladder's first step is a definition; its next four members, $3{,}46$, $5{,}14$, $6{,}82$, $8{,}50$ GeV, are excluded by LEP (R7, P1).

Per rule R6: coherence passes, reproducibility passes for $\alpha$ and fails for the electron mass, correspondence fails — for a structural reason, since the pair's objects are internal to an unobservable éon and the one external number it touches was put in as data.

Charon — The Rebuilt Theory (S58)

Consolidation, first article. Depends on S2 and S16; uses S3, S4, S7, S10, S64. Every choice that the corpus made silently is listed here in the open.

Introduction

This is the corpus rewritten in the framework's language, and its deliverable is not a derivation but a list of admissions. The corpus presents an axiomatic reconstruction of physics from two sheets — a real one and an imaginary one — with an interacting micro-universe, a cosmology and a theory of mind. Rewriting it in the biquaternion universe's language does three things: it settles what each of the corpus's objects is in a formalism that has the algebra the corpus lacks; it shows how much of the corpus transcribes unchanged; and it forces into the open the choices that carry the theory's content. The first two are gains. The third is the result.

The headline: the corpus closes by selection and not by derivation, and its content lives in eight choices, six of which it made silently. S16 found the six — the source tensor's form, the tangent-space identification, the linearisation of the field equation, the exact spin–pulse split, the definition of $\xi$, and the mass law $M_0 = \Omega_0\rho$ — and S37 added a seventh ($T_0$), while S39's eighth ($d_0$) was withdrawn. Each is individually defensible, each is declared somewhere in the printed text, none is derived. A reader who wants to know where the theory's physics is should be sent to that list and nowhere else.

The second headline: the rewrite is available for two-thirds of the corpus and changes no digit of its arithmetic. S64's capped sample of 100 of the 522 numbered display equations finds ≈ 37 that change content, ≈ 150 that are expressible and supplied, and ≈ 330 that transcribe coefficient-wise and unchanged. So "write Charon in biquaternion language" is a real and bounded task, its only decided gain is the per-quantity sector rule, and its one live lead is the source tensor's placement on the framework's spin-two machinery. The numerical system is untouched.

And the third: what the rewrite cannot do is the honest part. The algebra carries spin 0 and $\tfrac12$; a spin-$j$ object needs the $2j$-th tensor power. The corpus's memory mechanism is spin-two (Esp 098). So the rewrite reaches the corpus's dynamics and not its higher-spin apparatus, and the framework in turn has no dynamics coupling its two sectors — which is precisely the coupling the corpus's whole theory proposes. The two programmes are mirror images, and this article is the statement of the mirror.

1. What "rebuilt" means, and what it does not

Three operations are on the table, and this article keeps them apart.

  • Transcription: writing a corpus equation in the framework's notation with no change of content. Roughly 330 of 522 display equations, plus the definitions and the comparison numbers.
  • Expression: writing a corpus object using framework machinery that exists and is documented — the Einstein tensor, the Bianchi identity, the interior solution, the geodesic equation, and the symmetric traceless part of a rank-two tensor. Roughly 150 equations, of which about 120 are imported (the framework has carried them and transcribed them; nothing new is bought) and about 30 carried by the metric's own device.
  • Change of content: the rewrite alters what the object is. About 37 equations, and in this corpus the content change is one thing — the sector rule, made precise below.

So the rebuilt theory is mostly the old theory in a better language. That is not a small thing: a better language is what makes the silent choices visible and their consequences checkable. But it is not a new physics, and no article in this block should present it as one.

Two misreadings to block at the start.

  • The rewrite does not make the corpus's numbers a computation. The numerical system is unchanged by transcribing it, so every input/output verdict of S34 and S41 survives the rewrite intact. A rewritten fit is still a fit.
  • The rewrite does not repair the corpus's dead claims. The lepton's internal structure (P3), the spin ladder (P23) and the cosmological identifications (P9–P15) stay dead whatever alphabet they are written in. What a rewrite can do is explain why they were made — and in one case (§4) it can.

2. The dictionary, per quantity

The corpus's two sheets are the framework's Prime Convention, and the rule is per quantity, not per sheet (S2).

Charon biquaternion universe relation
RÉEL = $(x,y,z,t)$ the real parts $q_0,q_1,q_2,q_3$ the Prime Convention
IMAGINAIRE = $(\tilde x,\tilde y,\tilde z,\tilde t)$ the imaginary parts $q'_0,q'_1,q'_2,q'_3$ the Prime Convention
— $\mathbb{M}_- = \{q'_0,q_1,q_2,q_3\}$ — material imaginary time with real space
— $\mathbb{M}_+ = \{q_0,q'_1,q'_2,q'_3\}$ — informational real time with imaginary space

The two partitions are transversal, so each of the corpus's sheets is half of each sector: RÉEL is $q_0$ (one parameter of $\mathbb{M}_+$) plus $q_1,q_2,q_3$ (three of $\mathbb{M}_-$), and IMAGINAIRE is $q'_0$ (one of $\mathbb{M}_-$) plus $q'_1,q'_2,q'_3$ (three of $\mathbb{M}_+$). This is the rewrite's only decided gain, because it corrects a file folder that the corpus searched for years. The corpus's sentence "the real sector carries curvature and no energy" is false as written — its own $T_{\alpha\beta} = \xi^{2}\rho^{2}u_\alpha u_\beta$ is built from RÉEL objects — while its equations are right; the error is a filing error, and the per-quantity rule removes it.

The level at which the two complexifications agree and disagree (S3). Charon writes $x = \dot x + i\ddot x$: a complexification of a real four-space carrying only a commutative product. The framework complexifies $\mathbb{H}$: $Q = q + iq'$. The outputs are isomorphic as complex vector spaces ($\mathbb{C}^{4}\cong\mathbb{C}\otimes\mathbb{H}$) and non-isomorphic as algebras ($\mathbb{C}^{4}$ commutative, $M_2(\mathbb{C})$ not), and $\mathbb{C}^{4}$ is not a subalgebra of $M_2(\mathbb{C})$. The correct sentence is therefore: the same complexification of a real four-space, with the quaternion product absent — never "the same as". The corpus's complex structure is also global and stipulated, whereas the framework's is local; on a contractible four-manifold a constant complex structure is removable by a change of coordinates, so the corpus's own justification — base vectors multiplied by $i$ at the horizon — needs a locality its definition does not supply (S3, S4).

What the dictionary buys, stated as three facts and not as a slogan.

  • The horizon story is licensed, not forced (S4). Crossing the horizon swapping space and time is the local complex structure deciding which direction carries the $i$; the corpus refuses the maximal extension (Novikov, Eddington–Finkelstein, Kruskal–Szekeres) on a ground that belongs to a collapsing star's interior and not to the vacuum manifold where the extensions live. Four coordinates do not all change type: two do.
  • The curvature law becomes a sector-coupling ansatz (S10). $R = \xi^{2}\rho^{2} + 4\Lambda$ is the corpus's most original structural move: with a source quadratic in the density there is no cancellation of $\pm\rho$, no creation paradox, and mass proportional to density. In the framework's language it is an interaction term between the two sectors, which is exactly what the framework lacks.
  • The energy and action bookkeeping is a partition rule (S11). Action quantised at $\pm n\hbar$, split into $\pm n\hbar/2$ spin and $\pm n\hbar/2$ pulse, is an $\mathbb{M}_+/\mathbb{M}_-$ partition — and S11's audit is what killed its observable half (P23) while keeping the arithmetic of its layer (iii).

3. What transcribes and what does not

The three grades, with their counts (S64, capped stratified sample of 100 of 522 display equations).

The one live lead of the whole rewrite is in grade B, and it is specific. The source tensor $T_{\alpha\beta} = \xi^{2}\rho^{2}u_\alpha u_\beta$ is symmetric rank-two. A product of two four-vectors yields a scalar part and an antisymmetric part, so its symmetric traceless part must be carried by something else — and the framework carries exactly that object through the metric's own device, $h_{\mu\nu} = \langle\varepsilon_\mu,\delta\tilde E_\nu\rangle + \langle\delta\tilde E_\mu,\varepsilon_\nu\rangle$, with $16 = 10 + 6$. So Charon's $T_{\alpha\beta}$ sits on the framework's spin-two machinery, and whether a matter source can be written as a frame perturbation is open. That question is the rewrite's one research direction, and it is handed to the framework, not to the corpus.

The blocker, which is a ceiling and not a gap. The algebra carries spin 0 and $\tfrac12$; spin $j$ requires the $2j$-th tensor power. The corpus's memory mechanism is spin-two and grows by "l'augmentation de spin des photons" (Esp 098). The higher-spin equations are de Broglie's and Tonnelat's and are not wrong; it is their placement that fails, and no transcription can move them into an algebra that does not hold them. Structural conclusion: the synthesis — the corpus's dynamics with the framework's algebra — has one gain taken (A), one lead open (B2) and one blocker that is a ceiling, while the framework's own missing piece is the inter-sector dynamics that the corpus's $R = \xi^{2}\rho^{2}+4\Lambda$ supplies. The two programmes are mirror images: each has what the other lacks.

This is the centrepiece, and it is what S58 exists to carry. The corpus's formalism closes by selection. A rebuilt statement of Charon must open with this table, because a reader who does not know where the freedom was spent cannot tell a derived law from a chosen one.

Read the table as a statement about what kind of theory this is. Every choice is defensible on its own; several are the simplest assumption available; and each is printed. None is derived, and the corpus's ambition — an axiomatic reconstruction in which the principles are not free — is therefore not met. But the same structure is what makes adjudication possible: a theory whose content is in eight named choices can be assessed choice by choice, and that is what the R-chains do (S59). The corpus's structure is simultaneously why it could not deliver its ambition and why it can be judged at all.

One consequence to state plainly, because the corpus's rhetoric obscures it. With ①, ⑤ and ⑥ in place, the electron's mass, the elementary length and the action quantum are bookkeeping among three constants, not predictions. The corpus's six numerical results are its six inputs (S34), and the source of the freedom is exactly this table. A reader comparing Charon's "sans introduction d'aucun paramètre numérique" with the table should see the table.

5. What closes, and what does not

The formalism's own ledger (S16), carried forward.

Closes. The geometric side (Cartan–Lovelock conditions, S7); the curvature law given the source; the quantum equation (Klein–Gordon with a geometric mass); the action-density weight argument; and the cosmological first integral (5-45) → (5-46), verified numerically by S50.

Does not close. The source's fitness as an energy–momentum tensor (quadratic in $\rho$, not a physical $T_{\mu\nu}$ except at one density); the spin sector's predictions (P23, P24); the negative-energy licence's transfer to the cosmology (S13); the axiomatics as a system (S15); and the variational principle's advertised role (S14).

The yields that are real and must not be dropped, because they are qualitative and they are not nothing. The geometric reading of mass; the $\rho^{2}$ conservation law's answer to a real objection (no $\pm\rho$ cancellation, hence no creation paradox); and outside the formalism block, $\dot G/G$ (S40) and the bounce as a physical notion (S51). The fault is that the corpus has less than it claimed, not that it has nothing.

And one structural warning that belongs in a rebuilt statement. The closedness of the spatial section is a hypothesis, not a derivation. The interior observer's volume is $2\pi^{2}R^{3}$, the 3-volume of a closed $K=+1$ three-sphere, and the same $2\pi^{2}c^{2}R^{3}$ carries the cosmological action (5-41) whose output (5-46) is the corpus's best-verified identity. So the hole-universe behind the horizon, the micro-universe and the cosmological model are one construction at three scales — and the resemblance between the small and the large is guaranteed by construction, not discovered (S57). Any rebuilt statement must record $K=+1$ as a hypothesis of the same family as the second sheet, the tangent-space identification and the rest of §4's table.

6. The rebuilt theory in one page

Write it as a sequence of labelled statements, so that the silent choices appear where they are used. This is the form a rebuilt presentation should take.

  1. Space. A complexified four-space, $\mathbb{C}^{4}$, with coordinates $x = \dot x + i\ddot x$; equivalently $\mathbb{C}\otimes\mathbb{H}$ with the quaternion product retained if the framework's algebra is wanted. Choice. The complex structure is global and stipulated.
  2. Sectors. The eight real parameters split, on the framework's side, into $\mathbb{M}_-$ (material) and $\mathbb{M}_+$ (informational). Choice. The corpus's RÉEL/IMAGINAIRE split is the Prime Convention, transversal to this — a filing rule, per quantity.
  3. Metric and curvature. A real metric on the real part, with curvature $R$; Cartan's complementary conditions fix the connection and produce the usual general relativity (RC 021–022), so the geometric side is standard and not a Charon result.
  4. Source. $T_{\alpha\beta} = \xi^{2}\rho^{2}u_\alpha u_\beta$. Choice ①. Quadratic in $\rho$; in the framework's language it is the inter-sector coupling term the framework lacks.
  5. Field equation. The linearised (2-8), giving $R = \xi^{2}\rho^{2} + 4\Lambda$. Choices ② and ③. With $\Lambda$ the only geometric freedom, the law answers the creation paradox and makes mass proportional to density.
  6. Matter. Action quantised at $\pm n\hbar$, split $\pm n\hbar/2$ spin and $\pm n\hbar/2$ pulse. Choice ④. The pulse's observable claims are dead (P23, P24); the arithmetic layer (iii) survives.
  7. Scale. $\xi \equiv \Omega_0 c/\hbar$ and $M_0 = \Omega_0\rho$. Choices ⑤ and ⑥. These are what make the mass spectrum bookkeeping rather than prediction.
  8. Cosmology. The same construction at the large scale: a closed $K=+1$ section, a bounce at $R_0$, a negative $\Lambda$; the identity (5-46) verified (S50); the cycle's closure resting on the annihilating era that is [FALS] (S52's qualification). Hypothesis. $K=+1$.
  9. Mind. Memory as the integration constants of the field's system (S56), the éon as Knower (S57). [SPEC]/[PHIL]. The mechanism needs spin two, which the framework's algebra does not reach.
  10. Numbers. Six inputs, one defined constant, one suggested, one period, no independent output (S34, S41). The "2,3 %" is the depth of the search well inside a $\pm 10\,\%$ box, not a prediction; $\dot G/G \simeq 5\times10^{-16}$ yr$^{-1}$ is the one live prediction (S40).

Read statements 1–7 as a formalism and 8–10 as its applications. The formalism is coherent; its content is in choices ①②③④⑤⑥; and the applications' statuses are Part S and Part P by the clean split of S59. Nothing in this page is new physics. What is new is that the choices are numbered and the claims are separable.

7. Verdict

By rule R6, the three questions are answered separately, and the rebuild's answers are:

  • Coherence: yes. The formalism closes, given the eight choices. The geometric side closes on its own (Cartan–Lovelock); the curvature law closes once the source is chosen; the cosmology's first integral closes and its identity verifies.
  • Reproducibility: partly, and never for a constant. Every number the corpus prints follows from its own printed inputs; the search that produced the six values is ±10 % about those values. Reproducible as arithmetic, not as prediction.
  • Correspondence: three items beyond established theory, one live, one dead, one open (S62, which is §16 in article form).

The one-line verdict of the rebuilt theory: it is a coherent formalism whose physics sits in eight named choices, whose arithmetic reproduces its own inputs, whose rewrite into the framework's algebra is available for two-thirds of its equations and changes nothing, and which reaches beyond established theory in three places — one live prediction, one disproof and one open structure. That is a smaller thing than the corpus claims and a larger thing than a relabelling. The rebuild's contribution is to make the size of it exact.

Summary

The corpus rewritten in the biquaternion universe's language is a coherent formalism, two-thirds of whose equations transcribe unchanged, and whose physics lives in eight choices that the corpus made without deriving them.

The dictionary is per quantity: RÉEL/IMAGINAIRE is the Prime Convention and is transversal to the sectors $\mathbb{M}_\pm$, so each of the corpus's sheets is half of each sector. That rule is the rewrite's only decided gain, and it corrects a filing error rather than a calculation. The two complexifications agree as complex vector spaces and disagree as algebras: the same complexification of a real four-space, with the quaternion product absent.

The rewrite's counts are ≈ 37 equations that change content, ≈ 150 that are expressible and supplied, and ≈ 330 that transcribe coefficient-wise and unchanged. Its one live lead is the source tensor's symmetric traceless part, which the framework carries through the metric's device $h_{\mu\nu} = \langle\varepsilon_\mu,\delta\tilde E_\nu\rangle + \langle\delta\tilde E_\mu,\varepsilon_\nu\rangle$ with $16 = 10+6$ — so $T_{\alpha\beta}$ sits on the framework's spin-two machinery and whether a matter source can be written as a frame perturbation is open. Its blocker is a ceiling: the algebra holds spin 0 and $\tfrac12$ only, so the corpus's spin-two memory apparatus has no home in it, and the higher-spin equations are de Broglie's and Tonnelat's and are not the corpus's error.

The eight choices are ① $T_{\alpha\beta} = \xi^{2}\rho^{2}u_\alpha u_\beta$, ② the tangent-space identification (2-12), ③ the linearisation of (2-8), ④ Axiome 4's exact spin–pulse split, ⑤ $\xi \equiv \Omega_0c/\hbar$, ⑥ $M_0 = \Omega_0\rho$, ⑦ $T_0$ (S37), ⑧ $d_0$ (S39, withdrawn). Each is defensible, each is declared, none is derived. With ①, ⑤ and ⑥ in place the constants are bookkeeping, which is the mechanical reason the corpus computes none of them.

What closes: the geometric side, the curvature law given the source, the quantum equation, the action-density weight argument, and the cosmological first integral verified by S50. What does not: the source's fitness as a physical stress tensor, the spin sector's predictions, the negative-energy licence's transfer to cosmology, the axiomatics, and the variational principle's advertised role. The real and qualitative yields — the geometric reading of mass, the $\rho^{2}$ answer to the creation paradox, $\dot G/G$, the bounce — are kept.

The closed $K=+1$ section is a hypothesis, not a derivation, and it is the reason the micro-universe, the hole-universe and the cosmological model resemble each other: they are one construction at three scales.

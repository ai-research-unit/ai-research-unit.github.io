The Clifford Layer Is the Cell Layer: The Exact Correspondence, the Two Equalities, and the Full Inventory

Introduction

The earlier reading of this corpus put the Clifford algebra outside the biquaternion algebra. $\mathbb{B}$ was the even part, $\mathrm{Cl}_{1,3}$ had sixteen real dimensions, and the odd eight were said to be external. The correspondence now fixed in Conventions of the Matrix Models removes the premise.

In the canonical frame the five Dirac generators are cells of the two-sided operator algebra: $$ \gamma_0=iT_{e_t,e_x},\qquad \gamma_1=iT_{e_x,e_y},\qquad \gamma_2=iT_{e_y,e_y},\qquad \gamma_3=iT_{e_z,e_y},\qquad \gamma_5=-iT_{e_t,e_z}. $$ They are not merely isomorphic to cells; they are cells, with the coefficients displayed. The sixteen products of the five generators are, up to $\pm1$ and $\pm i$, the sixteen cells $T_{e_\mu,e_\nu}=L_{e_\mu}R_{e_\nu}$, and the assignment extends to an algebra isomorphism $$ \mathbb{B}\otimes_{\mathbb{C}}\mathbb{B}^{\mathrm{opp}}\longrightarrow M_4(\mathbb{C})\cong\mathrm{Cl}_{1,3}(\mathbb{C}). $$ The Clifford algebra is therefore the algebra generated by left and right multiplication on $\mathbb{B}$: the biquaternion algebra acting on itself on both sides. The odd sector is outside the left copy $\mathbb{B}_L$ and inside $\mathbb{B}\otimes\mathbb{B}^{\mathrm{opp}}$. It was never outside the formalism. It was outside one of its two actions.

One caveat, stated once. The complex statement is exact. Over $\mathbb{R}$ the sixteen products of the generators span $\mathrm{Cl}_{1,3}(\mathbb{R})\cong M_2(\mathbb{H})$, while the sixteen cells span $\mathrm{Cl}_{3,1}(\mathbb{R})\cong M_4(\mathbb{R})$; the two real forms are opposite, and the dictionary map carries one into the other. The physics is complex-linear throughout, and there the distinction is invisible.

The rule: the body carries no gammas. The body of an article is written in the two-sided biquaternion form: a left multiplication, a right multiplication, or a pure two-sided operator, acting on $\psi$. One closing appendix of each article keeps the dictionary once, as the literature coordinate. The one exception is the comparison article Why the Biquaternion Formalism Instead of Gamma Matrices, whose subject is the comparison and which carries the gamma display in the body. The isomorphism is what guarantees that the dictionary is exact over $\mathbb{C}$. One article in the corpus uses a larger algebra by design, and its gammas do not reduce to the sixteen cells.

Thirty-four articles carry the appendix. Eight are in mathematics and twenty-six in physics. Thirty-three are withdrawn: their bodies carry the cell side and one closing appendix with the frame, and none of them is repeated in the tables below. Nine of the withdrawn were already outside the tables; the tenth file outside is The Trace Anomaly in Biquaternionic Form, which carries the appendix but still writes two generator gammas in its body and is a one-line repair. Twenty-five rows have left the tables, all by withdrawal: ten in the first revision, The Roots of a General Element in the second, Maxwell's Equations in the third, and Noether's Theorem, The Conformal Group, Møller Scattering, Parra's Four Options, The Spinor Module, The s-Vector Representation of the Dirac Spinor, The Dirac Equation in Biquaternionic Form, The Reflection and the Rotation in Biquaternionic Form, Triality and the Ding Construction for the Dirac Spinor and The Dirac–Hestenes Equation and Spacetime Algebra in Biquaternionic Form in this one. The reference case, The Square-Zero Element, is among them and is no longer listed.

What differs between articles is the length of the sum in the body:

  • One term. A chirality, a projector, a mass insertion, a single generator: one pair of multipliers.
  • A sum of a few terms. A Dirac operator, a trace over several cells, a frame as a set: two, three or four terms. The Dirac operator is four in position space and two in every state, because the three spatial terms share a right multiplier. No article needs the generic element.

The gamma frame generates the ambient. The body never uses the ambient: it uses one pair of multipliers, or a sum of a few pairs. The appendix keeps the frame as the literature coordinate, and the frame is one frame among many.

The counts. The tables are the remaining work: they list only the articles in which $\gamma$ still denotes a gamma matrix and the body still writes it. Thirty-three withdrawn articles and four purged mentions are not repeated. The remaining 71 articles, 15 in mathematics and 56 in physics, are listed in menu order. The symbol counts come from the mathematics regions of each file: a $\gamma$ counts when it is a generator or a product, not when it is the Lorentz factor, the Euler constant, the photon, the curve, or a name.

The five generators are the first five rows, each a unit-modulus multiple of a cell, and every cell is a product of generators up to the phase shown. A calculation written in cells is therefore a calculation written in gammas, and the passage between the two is a substitution of notation, in either direction.

The one-term dictionary. When the article needs no general cell, the generators act as fixed sandwiches. With $L_a(\psi)=a\psi$ and $R_b(\psi)=\psi b$, $$ \gamma_0\psi=\psi\,(i\vec e_x),\qquad \gamma_k\psi=i\vec e_k\,\psi\,\vec e_y,\qquad \gamma_5\psi=-i\,\psi\,\vec e_z . $$ Each is a product of the spinor with fixed units, on the left, on the right, or both. That is pure biquaternion notation: no array, no operator symbol, no trace. Each line was checked numerically on random states; the residual is zero.

The multi-term dictionary. When the article needs a general cell, it writes $T_{a,b}=L_aR_b$ and never a matrix. The cell product is $T_{a,b}T_{c,d}=T_{ac,db}$, and the trace over the carrier is the regular trace of the pair. A gamma chain is then a product of five or fewer generators, hence one cell up to phase, and a trace of a gamma chain is a trace of a cell.

The Method: A Second Equality, Not a Replacement

The audit rule of that pass. Every display that used a gamma kept it and gained a second line, $$ \text{gamma form}\;=\;\text{cell form}, $$ an identity in $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbb{C}}(\mathbb{B})$ written beside the original. Nothing was deleted in that pass. The gamma side stays as the reference the literature uses; the cell side exhibits what the formula is made of. The isomorphism guarantees that the second line exists. This is the audit pass; the current rule, stated in the appendix, withdraws the gamma from the body and keeps the dictionary in one closing appendix.

Three reading rules.

  1. A generator, or a product of generators, is one cell up to a coefficient in $\{1,-1,i,-i\}$. Read it from the table in §The Correspondence.
  2. A sum of generators is a sum of cells. Keep the sum. A sum is a single product only when its coefficient matrix has rank at most one; cancellation is the rank-zero case, and the Dirac operator is a rank-one case with no cancellation.
  3. A trace is a linear functional on cells. For one cell $\operatorname{tr}T_{a,b}=4\,\mathrm{Sc}(a)\,\mathrm{Sc}(b)$, so the trace of a gamma chain is four times a product of scalar parts.

Worked examples.

The generators. With $T_{a,b}\psi=a\psi b$ and $L_{e_t}=R_{e_t}=\mathbf 1$, the sandwiches are those of the one-term dictionary, $\gamma_0\psi=i\psi e_x$, $\gamma_k\psi=i e_k\,\psi\,e_y$ and $\gamma_5\psi=-i\psi e_z$.

A product. From the table, $\gamma_0\gamma_1=T_{e_x,e_z}$, hence $\gamma_0\gamma_1\psi=e_x\psi e_z$.

The derivative part of the Dirac equation. $$ \gamma_\mu\frac{\partial}{\partial Q_\mu}\psi =i\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial Q_t}e_x +i\sum_{k=1}^{3}e_k\,\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial Q_k}\,e_y . $$ The left side is the usual notation, the right side is four multiplications. Both are on the page; neither replaces the other.

The chirality projector. $$ P_\pm=\tfrac12\left(\mathbf 1\pm\gamma_5\right)=\tfrac12\left(\mathbf 1\mp iT_{e_t,e_z}\right), \qquad P_\pm\psi=\tfrac12\left(\psi\mp i\psi e_z\right). $$ $P_\pm^2=P_\pm$ and $\operatorname{rank}P_\pm=2$, so $P_\pm$ is not a single cell: no cell is within $1$ of it in the sup norm. This is the archetype of a two-term equality. The identity is still exact, and the formula is still biquaternion.

The axial rotation. $$ e^{i\alpha\gamma_5}\psi=\psi\,e^{i\alpha e_z}. $$ A function of a gamma is a right multiplication by a biquaternion. No series and no matrix exponential is needed on the right.

The trace. $$ \operatorname{tr}T_{a,b}=4\,\mathrm{Sc}(a)\,\mathrm{Sc}(b), \qquad \operatorname{tr}\gamma_5=-i\operatorname{tr}T_{e_t,e_z}=0, \qquad \operatorname{tr}\gamma_\mu=0 . $$ Tracelessness is automatic: the coefficient of every generator is $\pm i$, whose scalar part is zero.

How to write it. Four rules for an article.

  • Keep the gamma dictionary in one appendix. The body writes the action and carries no gamma token; the reader sees the operator act on $\psi$ in the text and finds the gamma form, unchanged, at the end.
  • If the computation is long, write the equality once and refer to it. Do not repeat it at every step.
  • If the body needs a general two-sided operator, name $a$ and $b$ at first use.
  • The prose names the operator by its action; the appendix is the only place where the gamma name appears. An article is enriched when its formulas are, not when its vocabulary is.

Name-only mentions. A mention that carries a structural statement — a frame, a grading, a parity element — is replaced by an action clause; a mention that is only a pointer or a statement of scope is deleted. The gamma word is never kept. The articles that were already patched are not repeated in the tables.

The cost is the number of terms. The length of the sum varies from one article to the next, and the tables in Parts I and II count only the gamma side.

The one literal gap. Over $\mathbb{C}$ the identity is exact. Over $\mathbb{R}$ the generators span $\mathrm{Cl}_{1,3}(\mathbb{R})\cong M_2(\mathbb{H})$ and the cells span $\mathrm{Cl}_{3,1}(\mathbb{R})\cong M_4(\mathbb{R})$; the two are not isomorphic, and the equality requires the complexification. An article that uses a real structure — $C$, Majorana, CPT — must therefore state the form, and its second equality is a statement about the complexification, not a substitution inside the real algebra. No computation in the corpus depends on which form is written; the requirement is bookkeeping, and each article that uses a real structure states the form at that point.

What the Tables Say

The correspondence is an identity, not a comparison. The sixteen cells are the sixteen gamma products up to phase, and the cell algebra is the complexified Clifford algebra. A gamma calculation is therefore a cell calculation in a particular basis, and a cell calculation is a biquaternion calculation with two actions. Nothing is imported except the coordinate: $T_{a,b}=L_aR_b$ is the composition of two multiplications in $\mathbb{B}$.

The earlier count of 19 unrewritable articles was wrong. It counted what the left action $\mathbb{B}_L$ can reach, not what the algebra can reach. The odd part is outside $\mathbb{B}_L$ and inside $\mathbb{B}\otimes\mathbb{B}^{\mathrm{opp}}$; an article about parity, charge conjugation or the grading is an article about the second action, not an article about a foreign algebra.

The tables are an inventory of symbols, not a rank. A row with one symbol and a row with twenty are the same kind of article: both write the gamma frame and both carry the cell equality. The count follows the typography of the file, not the depth of the algebra. Conclusion

The Clifford layer is the cell layer. The five generators are five cells, the sixteen products are the sixteen cells, and the cell algebra is the biquaternion algebra acting on itself on both sides. The 71 articles in the tables can therefore be written in the algebra's own data. Thirty-three articles are withdrawn, and they are not repeated; four mentions were purged because they carried no structure. Nothing is lost from the physics: the body writes the action, and one closing appendix keeps the gamma dictionary. The gamma frame is a coordinate, not a boundary. The odd sector is a second action, not a foreign body.

Appendix — The Migration Method: From the Gamma Frame to $\mathbb{B}$ and a Chirality

The withdrawal is the default, not a later level. The additive pass of §The Method was the audit that proved the dictionary: the correspondence is verified, and a withdrawn article writes the body in the algebra's own data. The withdrawal is the default. Each article carries the body in the action form and one closing appendix holding the dictionary once; this appendix states the method by which the body was written.

The diagnosis. The gamma matrices are a frame of $\operatorname{End}_\mathbb{C}(\mathbb{B})$, not a basis of it, and a frame is the weakest kind of data the algebra admits. Four defects, each a count or a theorem.

  • They are redundant. A basis of $M_4(\mathbb{C})$ has sixteen elements. The frame supplies four generators, their product, and a multiplication rule; the basis it induces is the sixteen antisymmetrised products, not the five. Calling the five "the basis" hides the count $1+4+6+4+1=16$ and hides which product is which cell.
  • They are not canonical. Every frame is $S\gamma_\mu S^{-1}$ for $S\in GL_4(\mathbb{C})$, and the frames over a fixed metric form the homogeneous space $GL_4(\mathbb{C})/\mathbb{C}^{\times}$. Over the space of metrics they form a fibre bundle with that fibre; the space of metrics is contractible, so the bundle is trivial, and a frame still has to be chosen. A statement that names $\gamma_0$ names the choice.
  • They re-encode a metric the algebra already carries. The Clifford relation $\{\gamma_\mu,\gamma_\nu\}=2g_{\mu\nu}\mathbf 1$ reads back the signature. In $\mathbb{B}$ the signature is already in the units, $e_t^2=\mathbf 1$ and $e_k^2=-\mathbf 1$. The anticommutator adds a second copy of data that the multiplication already fixes.
  • They name five of the sixteen cells and leave the other eleven implicit. The five gammas are five cells up to $\pm i$ (the dictionary below). The other eleven are products of the five and are also cells: the mass block, the Peirce corners and the remaining two-sided operators. The five-name list does not name them, and a reader of a gamma display cannot see which cell carries the mass or the grading without expanding the product. A cell names its two blocks, $T_{a,b}$, in its own symbol; a gamma does not.

What is not claimed. The gamma frame is not wrong. It is a valid frame, and the literature speaks it. The migration does not delete the physics and does not dispute a single gamma identity. It changes the primary data and the default display. The Conclusion's phrase "not a boundary" is exact about the boundary: the gamma frame bounds nothing. The basis is the sixteen products, and the five are the frame that generates it.

The target data

The carrier. The Dirac spinor is $\mathbb{B}$ as a four-dimensional complex vector space, not $\mathbb{C}^4$ with a chosen column. No minimal left ideal and no column have to be selected. The left copy $\mathbb{B}_L$ acts block-diagonally, on two copies of its two-dimensional simple module, and the odd slot exchanges the two chiral halves; the Dirac action is the two-sided action $T_{a,b}$, not the left regular action alone.

The algebra. The left and right actions together generate the endomorphisms, $$ \operatorname{End}_\mathbb{C}(\mathbb{B})\cong\mathbb{B}\otimes_\mathbb{C}\mathbb{B}^{\mathrm{opp}}\cong M_4(\mathbb{C}), $$ with the sixteen cells $$ T_{a,b}=L_aR_b,\qquad T_{a,b}T_{c,d}=T_{ac,db},\qquad \operatorname{Tr}T_{a,b}=4\,a_tb_t. $$ These sixteen are a basis intrinsic to the algebra: one cell per pair of units, no gamma frame chosen, and every element a sum of cells.

The chirality. One involution on the carrier, $$ \Gamma^2=\mathbf 1,\qquad \operatorname{tr}\Gamma=0,\qquad P_\pm=\tfrac12(\mathbf 1\pm\Gamma),\qquad \operatorname{rank}P_\pm=2, $$ with the standard root $H_0=\operatorname{diag}(1,i,-1,-i)$ and $\Gamma_0=H_0^2=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,1,-1)$. The invariant content is the $2+2$ split; the array is one representative. The involution grades the endomorphisms into the part that commutes with $\Gamma$ and the part that anticommutes with it, and the odd part is exactly the block that maps one chirality to the other.

Why the pair is complete. $\mathbb{B}$ supplies the carrier and the even action; $\mathbb{B}^{\mathrm{opp}}$ supplies the right action; $\Gamma$ supplies the grading. Together they give $\operatorname{End}_\mathbb{C}(\mathbb{B})$ in full. The five gammas are a generating set of the same algebra, chosen inside one frame; they add no structure that the triple $(\mathbb{B},\mathbb{B}^{\mathrm{opp}},\Gamma)$ lacks.

The cone, not the ambient. The gammas generate all of $M_4(\mathbb{C})$, and generation is not the issue. The issue is that the ambient space is the default object. A general element of it is not a single two-sided operator: the products $T_{Q,P}=L_QR_P$ fill the affine cone over the Segre variety $\mathbb{P}^3\times\mathbb{P}^3$, of complex dimension seven, cut out by the $2\times2$ minors of $c(M)$. The cone is closed under composition and under inversion on its units, not under addition, and a general element is a sum of at most four products, with $\ell(M)=\operatorname{rank}c(M)$. For a product the matrix rank lies in $\{0,1,2,4\}$ and the length in $\{0,1,2,3,4\}$, and neither fixes the other.

What the few terms are. The chiral projector is one term, $P_\pm=\tfrac12(1\pm\Gamma)=\tfrac12(T_{tt}\mp iT_{tz})$. The Dirac operator is displayed with four terms in position space, $$ D\psi=i\,e_t(\partial_t\psi)e_x+i\,e_x(\partial_x\psi)e_y+i\,e_y(\partial_y\psi)e_y+i\,e_z(\partial_z\psi)e_y, $$ and it has length two in every state, because the three spatial terms share the right multiplier $e_y$ and combine into a single left multiplication. A term is fixed by two biquaternions modulo $(Q,P)\sim(\lambda Q,\lambda^{-1}P)$, so seven complex parameters; the ambient takes sixteen.

The mismatch. The gamma formalism has the right algebra and the wrong primitive. It makes the generic matrix the default and the physical operator a special case; the biquaternion framework makes the pair of multipliers the object and the generic matrix a sum. The difference is not dimension, since the algebras are isomorphic. It is what counts as primitive, and therefore what a computation starts from.

Frame changes. A change by a decomposable operator $S=T_{a,b}$, with $a,b$ units, sends $T_{Q,P}$ to $T_{aQa^{-1},\,b^{-1}Pb}$ and preserves the cone. A general change of gamma basis need not: it is itself a sum of a few cells, and it can turn one product into several. The converse is not claimed.

The dictionary, in the canonical frame, $$ \gamma_0=iT_{e_t,e_x},\quad \gamma_1=iT_{e_x,e_y},\quad \gamma_2=iT_{e_y,e_y},\quad \gamma_3=iT_{e_z,e_y},\quad \gamma_5=-iT_{e_t,e_z}. $$

One symbol, three roles. The replacement of $\gamma_5$ is the test case, because it is where "$\mathbb{B}$ and a chirality" differs from "five gammas".

role of the symbol what is written why
a cell $-iT_{e_t,e_z}$ the multiplication is what the display uses
a grading $\Gamma$ and $P_\pm$ the split is the invariant, the array is not
a historical name the appendix entry the name is the literature pointer, and it lives in one place

The square is a one-line check of the cell reading: $$ (-iT_{e_t,e_z})^2=-T_{e_t,-e_t}=T_{e_t,e_t}=\mathbf 1 . $$ The projector is then $\tfrac12(\mathbf 1\mp iT_{e_t,e_z})$, which is not a cell: two terms, as §The Method says. The involution $\Gamma$ is the same statement without the frame.

The five levels

The ladder. The migration is progressive because each level is verifiable on its own and because no article may skip one. A level closes for the whole corpus before the next opens.

Level 0 is the same screen as the upper-index audit. Only the mathematics regions count; a token is a gamma only if it is a generator or a product, not the word in a sentence. The sheet records the file, the count, the objects and the class. The counts of §The Counts are the level-0 sheet of this corpus: its remaining users, their gamma occurrences at baseline, the withdrawn articles not repeated and the four purged mentions. The level-0 sheet counts tokens; it does not classify them.

Level 1 is mechanical. The dictionary is a substitution, and a substitution is safe only if the frame is fixed once per article. The frame is written at the first display, not inferred at each line. In the $\mathbb{B}$ frame the spatial generators carry an extra $i$ and all four square to $+\mathbf 1$; the gate is read in the frame the article states.

Level 2 is arithmetic in the cells. The product rule replaces a matrix multiplication, and the trace rule replaces a trace. No $4\times4$ array is multiplied at any point of the migration.

Level 3 is editorial. $\Gamma$ is not a gamma and the adjoint is not $\gamma_0$. The adjoint needs the real structure, which is extra data: over $\mathbb{R}$ the generators span $\mathrm{Cl}_{1,3}\cong M_2(\mathbb{H})$ and the cells span $\mathrm{Cl}_{3,1}\cong M_4(\mathbb{R})$, so the identity is a statement about the complexification. The article says so once.

Level 4 is where frames are easy to mix. The $\mathbb{B}$ frame and the $\mathbb{M}_-$ frame are two readings of one operator, and a contraction stays inside one. The derivative is the four-term sum of §The Method; the mass term is a left multiplication and carries no frame.

Level 5 is the only level that deletes. A display is removed only after the cell equality beside it has passed level 3, and only if no sentence depends on a matrix entry. The historical display stays as one remark, so a reader of the gamma literature can still translate.

The operational pipeline

The scan. The mathematics regions are extracted, the gamma tokens are matched, and each is classified by its role: array, generator, product, derived object, prose. The output is a per-article sheet, not a global count; a global count cannot say which file is safe.

The batch. Only the mechanical rows of the table run unattended. The batch withdraws the gamma from the body and writes the cell in its place; the dictionary is kept once, in the closing appendix. The structural rows — $\Gamma$, the adjoint, a proof — are written by hand, because they change a statement and not a symbol.

The guard. A file is skipped, and recorded as skipped, if one display mixes two frames, if a product needs a cell whose $a,b$ are not named, or if the gamma occurs only in prose. A skipped file is not a failed file; it is a file at a lower level.

The recount. After each batch the scan runs again. The gamma count must not increase, the cell-equalities count must not decrease, no new mixed frame may appear, and no upper index may appear. A file whose count moves the wrong way is reverted before the next article is touched.

The commit. One commit per level, one article per diff. The message names the level and the article, so a rollback is a revert and not an archaeology.

The one gap. Over $\mathbb{R}$ the equality between the gamma span and the cell span does not hold: $\mathrm{Cl}_{1,3}\cong M_2(\mathbb{H})$ against $\mathrm{Cl}_{3,1}\cong M_4(\mathbb{R})$. The migration is therefore a complexified statement wherever a real structure is used, and the article must say so at that point. This is bookkeeping, not an obstruction, and each article that uses a real structure states the form at that point.

What the migration buys

The frame leaves the statement. A formula written in cells names no section, no $\gamma_0$ and no column. The same formula in gammas names all three.

The chirality becomes an involution. $\Gamma^2=\mathbf 1$, $\operatorname{tr}\Gamma=0$, rank $2$: a statement about the carrier, not about a product of four matrices.

The metric stays where it was. The signature is in $e_t^2=\mathbf 1$ and $e_k^2=-\mathbf 1$; removing the anticommutator removes a duplicate, not the metric.

The sixteen cells become visible. The mass block, the two-sided operators and the Peirce corners are cells in the same list as the generators, not products that appear only after a multiplication.

The cost is the same as before. A multi-term equality is longer than a one-term one, the dictionary is written once per article, and the gamma column stays as the translation for the literature. The migration lowers the number of symbols and raises the number of terms; it does not lower the number of pages.

The order is the method. Leaves, products, derived objects, frames, scaffolding: each level is a proof that the next one is safe. The second equality established the dictionary and the cell side without committing to a deletion. The remaining levels apply the same rule until the gamma side is a remark.

Charon: The Boson Spin Identifications

Introduction

This article records the corpus's identifications of the gauge bosons, and it is the article in which the corpus is simply wrong about things that have been measured. The photon is "a pair of neutrinos". The gluon is a préon/antipréon pair, "electrically neutral, of spin and pulse both globally zero". The graviton is the same object, and also spin zero — except where the corpus calls a spin-$\tfrac12$ hadron a graviton. The inventory calls this the weakest technical point in the book. It is also, structurally, the reason the corpus had to build gravity the way it did.

The claims, verbatim. RC 050: "Nous appellerons de telles paires des GLUONS. Les gluons sont donc formés d'une paire préon/antipréon; ils sont électriquement neutres, de spin et pulse global tous deux nuls, de masse propre globale nulle. ... On voit la parenté entre les gluons et les photons : tous deux sont constitués par une paire d'éléments associés, les photons par une paire de neutrinos, les gluons par une paire préon-antipréon." RC 058: "On a coutume de nommer gravitons la partie ponctuelle dans l'espace du Réel de ces paires préon/antipréon. Il est clair qu'il y a analogie de structure entre les gluons et les gravitons ... ; tous deux, gluons et gravitons, sont globalement de [spin zéro]". And Esp 095 gives a different graviton: "Nous appellerons graviton un tel hadron de densité centrale $d_c$ et de spin $\tfrac{1}{2}$."

The verdict is [FALS]. The gluon is a massless spin-1 colour octet — established by the three-jet events at PETRA (1979) and the angular analysis that followed. The graviton is a massless spin-2 mode of the metric — the tensor structure of general relativity, constrained by GW170817 and by the polarisations LIGO/Virgo see. The corpus's graviton has spin 0 in one place and spin $\tfrac12$ in another, and neither is spin 2. The photon-as-neutrino-pair is excluded by compositeness bounds and by universality.

A note on sources. The claims are inventory §3.10 and §11 item 5; the W/Z identification is §3.10 too, and it is P7's. P18 takes the gluon, the graviton, the photon and the "no spin greater than 1" claim, and leaves the W/Z to P7. The related articles are P7, P8 (the massless internal neutrino the photon is built from) and P24 (gravity from a pulsating boundary, which P18 explains the need for).

Conventions. $J^{PC}$ is spin, parity, charge conjugation; $\hbar$ is written 0.5 for spin-$\tfrac12$; PETRA, LEP and the LHC are the colliders cited. $\nu$ is the neutrino; $C\nu B$ the cosmic neutrino background. "Gluon" and "graviton" are the physical particles unless the corpus's usage is being reported.

Part 1 — The gluon

What the corpus says. A gluon is a pair of one préon and one antipréon, in which the partners carry equal and opposite values — "un exemple une paire d'éléments ayant pour spins $+\hbar/2$ et $-\hbar/2$, et pour masses propres $+m$ et $-m$". The pair is electrically neutral, massless, and "de spin et pulse global tous deux nuls". Gluons are therefore spin-0 bosons; and because they can be assembled into infinite sets, the corpus has infinitely many of them, grouped into regions.

What is right, and it is worth saying. The corpus's gluon is massless and electrically neutral: both correct. And its remark that gluons "ne sont cependant directement « observables » que quand on sépare les deux éléments de la paire gluonique" is, accidentally, close to the truth of confinement — a free gluon is not observed — though for the wrong reason (the corpus's gluons hide inside pairs, the real ones are confined by colour). His comment on the photon–gluon analogy — that both are made of paired elements — is also structurally reminiscent of the modern statement that both are gauge bosons associated with a pair structure (a fermion bilinear). The analogy is loose, but it is not empty.

What is wrong. The gluon is a massless spin-1 particle, and there are eight of them, forming a colour octet. This was not an inference from aesthetics; it was measured.

evidence year what it established
three-jet events at PETRA (TASSO, MARK-J, PLUTO, JADE) 1979 a third, neutral, massless parton is radiated by quarks — the gluon
angular correlations in $e^{+}e^{-}\to q\bar q g$ 1980 the gluon's spin is 1, not 0: spin-0 and spin-1 matrix elements give different angular distributions, and the data choose spin 1
four-jet events and the running of $\alpha_{s}$ at LEP 1989–2000 the gluon carries colour (the self-coupling of a non-abelian gauge field)

The spin measurement is the decisive one for this article, because it is precisely the quantum number the corpus assigns wrongly. A spin-0 gluon and a spin-1 gluon give measurably different event shapes; the data were analysed for exactly this discrimination and exclude spin 0.

And the multiplicity is wrong too. The corpus's gluons come in infinite continuous sets; the real ones are a finite, discrete octet. A count of eight is a consequence of $SU(3)$ colour, which the corpus does not have.

Part 2 — The graviton, which the corpus defines twice and neither time correctly

The two definitions. RC 058 makes the graviton a préon/antipréon pair, "analogie de structure" with the gluon, and "globalement de [spin] ... nul". Esp 095 §10.3 makes the graviton "un tel hadron de densité centrale $d_c$ et de spin $\tfrac{1}{2}$" — a spin-$\tfrac12$ hadron, used as the carrier of the gravitational coupling in the computation of $G$. So the corpus's "graviton" is a spin-0 pair in one chapter and a spin-$\tfrac12$ hadron in another. Neither is spin 2, and the two are not the same object.

Why spin 2 is not a detail. In general relativity the gravitational field is a spin-2 field, and three qualitative facts follow from that and from nothing else:

  • Like charges attract. For a spin-1 (vector) field, like charges repel and opposite charges attract; gravity attracts universally. The spin-2 (tensor) exchange has the opposite sign between like sources, giving attraction. So the sign of gravity is a spin-2 statement.
  • The source is the energy–momentum tensor. A spin-2 field couples to $T_{\mu\nu}$, not to a scalar charge and not to a current. So the equivalence principle's universality of free fall is a spin-2 statement.
  • Quadrupole radiation. Gravitational waves are quadrupolar, with the two tensor polarisations the metric allows, and with an octupole-free angular pattern. So the observed gravitational-wave signals are spin-2 statements.

And they are measured. GW150914 and the events after it match the two tensor polarisations of general relativity, and the LIGO/Virgo polarisation analyses exclude scalar and vector alternatives. GW170817, with its gamma-ray counterpart, constrains the graviton mass from dispersion to $< 7{,}7\times10^{-23}$ eV and the propagation speed to within $3\times10^{-15}$ of $c$. So the graviton is massless (the corpus's spin-0 pair is also massless — correct) and spin 2 (the corpus's is not).

The structural consequence, and this is why the article matters. A model whose boson spectrum stops at spin 1 cannot contain general relativity's gravitational field. The corpus's real-space particles carry spin 0, $\tfrac12$ and 1; its higher-spin states live in the imaginary sector as a "fusion" field with no real-space spin-2 mode. So there is no spin-2 field in the corpus to be the metric perturbation, and gravity has to be manufactured from something else — which is exactly what P24 audits: gravity from a pulsating boundary (S29). The spin assignment in RC 050 and RC 058 is not a peripheral error; it is the reason the corpus's gravity is not a field theory of the metric. P18 is therefore an upstream article for P24.

Part 3 — The photon as a neutrino pair

The claim. RC 050: "les photons par une paire de neutrinos". The photon is a bound pair of neutrino and antineutrino, massless, neutral.

What is right. A $\nu\bar\nu$ pair is neutral; the photon is neutral and massless. The photon is its own antiparticle, and a suitable $\nu\bar\nu$ state is too. So the corpus has the quantum numbers of the pair it wants to build.

Why it is excluded.

  • Compositeness bounds. A composite photon would have a form factor, hence an energy scale at which its structure shows. LEP's $e^{+}e^{-}\to\gamma\gamma$ and related measurements put the photon's compositeness scale above several TeV, and the modern bounds are at the $10^{1}$–$10^{2}$ TeV level. A $\nu\bar\nu$ bound state has a binding scale set by the weak interaction — of order the electroweak scale or below — which is far inside the excluded range.
  • Universality. The photon couples identically to electron, muon, tau and to quarks, with a coupling fixed by the electric charge. That universality is the signature of a gauge boson. A composite made of neutrinos would couple to charged matter only through the neutrinos' weak interactions, i.e. at a strength $\sim10^{-5}$ of electromagnetism. The Coulomb force is not $10^{-5}$ of the observed strength, so the pair cannot be the photon.
  • The neutrino's own status. The corpus's photon is built of massless neutrinos (P8). Neutrino oscillation has measured non-zero masses ($\Delta m^{2}_{21} = 7{,}5\times10^{-5}$ eV$^{2}$, $|\Delta m^{2}_{31}| = 2{,}5\times10^{-3}$ eV$^{2}$, with $\sum m_{\nu} < 0{,}1$ eV) — so the objects the corpus's photon is made of are the objects P8 says do not exist. The photon's construction inherits P8's falsification directly.
  • And the C$\nu$B. The cosmic neutrino background contains of order 340 neutrinos and antineutrinos per cm$^{3}$. In a universe where a neutrino and an antineutrino are a photon, that population is not merely a background — it is a population of photons, whose interactions would be visible. No such effect is seen.

A note on the internal consistency. The corpus's photon needs the internal neutrino to be massless so that the pair is massless. The corpus says so explicitly (P8), and it is the same premise that makes the préon solution exist. So the photon, the préon and the gluon all rest on the massless internal neutrino — one premise, and it is refuted.

Part 4 — "No particle has spin greater than 1"

The claim. The corpus's real-space spectrum, and the user's working list item 5, both record the rule: no particle has spin greater than 1. The corpus does allow higher "spin" states inside the imaginary sector — the fusion $\sigma$ field takes spin $1, 2, 4, 8,\dots$ (RC 020), and the internal neutrino takes $\tfrac32, \tfrac52,\dots$ (RC 047) — but these are internal, and the corpus's point is that no particle of the real space carries spin above 1.

The observed spectrum goes far past it.

particle spin note
$\Delta(1232)$ $3/2$ the lightest baryon resonance; spin $3/2$ is elementary-looking and unavoidable
$\Omega^{-}$ $3/2$ a ground-state baryon, no excitation needed
$f_{4}(2050)$ $4$ a meson
$f_{6}(2510)$ $6$ the highest established meson spin
$\Delta(1950)$ $11/2$ the highest established baryon spin
graviton $2$ the metric's tensor mode

And the corpus contradicts the rule itself, in the other direction. Its own spin bookkeeping assigns the muon spin $\tfrac32$ and the tau spin $\tfrac52$ (the parity rule of S11 §3.2, P23) — and the muon and tau are measured spin-$\tfrac12$. So the corpus's spin rule fails twice: it forbids the observed spin-2 graviton and the observed spin-6 mesons, while permitting itself a spin-$\tfrac32$ muon that does not exist.

And the W and Z. The corpus gets these spin-1, which is right; it makes them neutrino pairs, which is P7's failure. P18 records only that they are the one part of the corpus's boson spectrum whose spin is correct, and it loses the spin to P7.

Summary

The corpus makes the photon a neutrino pair, the gluon a préon/antipréon pair of total spin zero, and the graviton the same object — except where it calls a spin-$\tfrac12$ hadron a graviton — and it holds that no particle has spin greater than 1. The verdict is [FALS]. The gluon is a massless spin-1 colour octet of eight members: PETRA's three-jet events (1979) found it and the angular analysis (1980) fixed its spin at 1, against spin 0. The graviton is a massless spin-2 mode of the metric: like-charge attraction, the coupling to $T_{\mu\nu}$ and the observed quadrupole polarisations all follow from spin 2, and GW170817 bounds the mass and the speed. The photon-as-$\nu\bar\nu$ is excluded by compositeness bounds at the TeV scale, by electromagnetism's universality (a pair would couple at $\sim10^{-5}$ strength), and by the non-zero neutrino masses that falsify its ingredient (P8). The corpus's spin-0/spin-$\tfrac12$ graviton has a structural consequence: with no spin-2 field in real space, the corpus cannot have the metric's perturbation, and gravity has to be built from the pulsating boundary instead — which is P24's subject and S29's audit. P18 is P24's upstream article.

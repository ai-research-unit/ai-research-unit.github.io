Charon: Six Levels and Six Flavours

Introduction

This article records the corpus's identification of six spherical density levels inside the holon with the six quark flavours. The count is right — there are six flavours — and the corpus prints a table giving each level a quark name. The identification fails anyway, and it fails on four independent grounds: the level masses are charged-lepton masses, not quark masses; the ordering is inverted where it matters most; the table does not close arithmetically; and the number six comes from where a density table was cut off, not from anything about quarks.

The claim, verbatim. RC 063, §III.1: after defining the level density $\rho_{n} = m_{n}/\Omega_{0}$ and the holon's central density $\rho_{H}$, the text states "on constate donc qu'il existe six niveaux sphériques de densité $\rho_n$ fournis par (4-19), qui se situent dans le volume holonique", and prints table (3-79), which names the six levels $u, d, c, s, b, t$. RC 053's prose version of the same claim lists them as "les six niveaux de quarks (u, d, s, b, t)".

The verdict is [MIX] then [OUT]. [MIX] because six flavours exist and a six-level scheme is not absurd on its face. [OUT] because every mass is wrong, the charm-and-strange ordering is reversed, the table's own arithmetic fails at three of six rows, and the truncation that fixed the number six is circular.

A note on sources. The claim is inventory §6.1, sourced to RC (3-79) at RC/063_improved.md and to RC 053. The transition-energy audit is S49's; the density slide is P21's. The downstream articles are P5 (the top) and P6 (quarkonium); the fractional-charge mechanism is S47 and S48. The related chains are R7 and R11.

Conventions. Gaussian cgs. $n$ is the level index, $\rho_{n} = m_{n}/\Omega_{0}$ the level density, $r_{n}$ the level's radius, $\Omega_{0} = 2{,}44\times10^{-41}$ cm$^{3}$ the elementary cell, $\rho_{H} = 5{,}8\times10^{17}$ g cm$^{-3}$ the holon's central density. Quark masses are modern PDG values, $\overline{\mathrm{MS}}$ for the light quarks.

Part 1 — The table, and what the level masses actually are

Table (3-79) is six rows, each assigning a quark name to a lepton mass. The corpus's numbers and the real quark masses are placed side by side:

$n$ corpus corpus mass (g) corpus mass (MeV) real quark mass (MeV) ratio
0 $u$ up $9{,}11\times10^{-28}$ $0{,}511$ $2{,}16$ $0{,}24$
1 $d$ down $1{,}89\times10^{-25}$ $105{,}7$ $4{,}67$ $22{,}6$
2 $c$ charm $3{,}18\times10^{-24}$ $1777$ $1270$ $1{,}40$
3 $s$ strange $6{,}18\times10^{-24}$ $3470$ $93{,}4$ $37$
4 $b$ beauty $9{,}18\times10^{-24}$ $5150$ $4180$ $1{,}23$
5 $t$ top $1{,}52\times10^{-23}$ $8500$ $172700$ $0{,}049$

The masses are the positron's, the muon's and the tau's, followed by three more rungs of the same arithmetic ladder — that is, the corpus's quark masses are the corpus's lepton masses. P1 refutes that ladder; this table is one of its uses.

Two rows look close and are not. The charm ($1{,}40$) and the beauty ($1{,}23$) are within tens of percent, and a careless reader could take the identification as approximately right. It is not, because the identification is a scheme: it fixes which level is which, and the scheme's ordering is tested next.

Part 2 — The ordering is inverted, and that is decisive

A table of six numbers can be right in its values and wrong in its assignment. Here the assignment is wrong in the most conspicuous way.

The real ordering. In the measured spectrum the charm is about fourteen times the strange: $m_{c} \approx 1270$ MeV against $m_{s} \approx 93$ MeV. Any scheme that assigns the levels to flavours must have the charm's level well above the strange's.

The corpus's ordering. Table (3-79) has the charm at $1777$ MeV and the strange at $3470$ MeV. The charm sits below the strange — the ordering is inverted. In the corpus's own arithmetic-progression levels there is no way to repair this, because the rungs are fixed by the ladder's step; moving the charm above the strange would break the relation between the levels and the leptons.

A second inversion, in the light quarks. The corpus has the down ($105{,}7$ MeV) more than twenty times the up ($0{,}511$ MeV); the measured ratio is about two.

So the scheme fails as a scheme, not as a set of numbers. It would be a coincidence if all six masses were wrong but the ordering right; the ordering is wrong too, and the charm-strange inversion is visible in the corpus's own table.

Part 3 — The table does not close

Table (3-79) lists $\rho_{n}$, $r_{n}$ and $m_{n}$ for each level, and the corpus defines $m_{n} = \Omega_{0}\rho_{n}$ (4-20-a). The three columns should therefore be consistent with one another. Three rows are not.

$n$ $\rho_{n}$ (g cm$^{-3}$) $\Omega_{0}\rho_{n}$ (g) printed $m_{n}$ (g) discrepancy
0 $3{,}73\times10^{13}$ $9{,}10\times10^{-28}$ $9{,}11\times10^{-28}$ ok
1 $7{,}71\times10^{15}$ $1{,}88\times10^{-25}$ $1{,}89\times10^{-25}$ ok
2 $1{,}30\times10^{17}$ $3{,}17\times10^{-24}$ $3{,}18\times10^{-24}$ ok
3 $2{,}33\times10^{17}$ $5{,}69\times10^{-24}$ $6{,}18\times10^{-24}$ $+8{,}7\,\%$
4 $3{,}76\times10^{17}$ $9{,}17\times10^{-24}$ $9{,}18\times10^{-23}$ $\times 10$
5 $4{,}99\times10^{17}$ $1{,}22\times10^{-23}$ $1{,}52\times10^{-23}$ $+25\,\%$

The fourth row is an exponent slip: the printed $m_{4}$ is $9{,}18\times10^{-23}$ where the definition gives $9{,}18\times10^{-24}$, and the corrected value is exactly the lepton ladder's $m_{4}$. The third and fifth rows are further gaps. So the identification is not even self-consistent on its own page — the reader cannot use the definition and the table together. S49 records the same finding and the correction to row 4.

Part 4 — Where the "six" comes from, and why that is not evidence

The sixth level is not derived from the quark spectrum. It is where the table stopped.

RC 053 prints its lepton table up to $n = 6$ and explains: "Nous avons arrêté le tableau juste au-delà de la densité centrale hadronique donnée par (3-78)." The table was truncated when $\rho_{n}$ passed $\rho_{0H}$. RC 063 then reads the truncation back as a physical fact: six levels fit inside the holon, therefore six quark levels.

P21 shows $\rho_{0H}$ is not a density. It is a mass divided by the fixed cell $\Omega_{0}$, so the ceiling is a mass threshold in disguise. The count of six is therefore the number of rungs of a leaky ladder that fall below a threshold which is itself circular. The agreement with the six flavours is a coincidence of two errors, and a coincidence produced by cutting a one-parameter ladder at a threshold is exactly the kind of hit that carries no evidential weight.

An internal inconsistency worth recording. RC 053's prose list is $u, d, s, b, t$ — five names for six levels, and no charm. RC 063's table is $u, d, c, s, b, t$ — six names, with charm, in an order that inverts charm and strange. The two pages of the same chapter do not agree on what the six levels are called.

Summary

The corpus identifies six holonic density levels with the six quark flavours, and the count is the only thing that survives. Table (3-79) assigns the positron's, muon's and tau's masses — and three further rungs of the refuted arithmetic ladder (P1) — to $u, d, c, s, b, t$. The charm-and-strange ordering is inverted (charm 1777 MeV below strange 3470 MeV, against a measured ratio of fourteen the other way); the table does not close at rows 3, 4 and 5, with row 4 a factor-ten exponent slip; and the number six comes from truncating a density table at a ceiling that is not a density (P21) — a coincidence of two errors. RC 053's prose list even disagrees with RC 063's table. Verdict [MIX] $\to$ [OUT]; the root of the P5, P6, S47 and S48 chains.

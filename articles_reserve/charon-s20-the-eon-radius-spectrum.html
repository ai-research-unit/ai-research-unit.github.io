Charon — The Éon Radius Spectrum (S20)

Audits §3.4 and §3.5 of CHARON_IDEAS_AND_CLAIMS.md, against RC ch. III (scans 044, 045, 051, 052).

Introduction

The register's verdict on §3.4 is right and the article keeps it: $T_{0}$ is fixed from the measured lepton mass difference, so the éon's radii are a fit and not a prediction. This article adds the arithmetic the register does not carry — how the three numbers are actually produced — and, with it, one finding that changes how the block should be read.

The finding is that the éon's maximum radius is the most ill-conditioned number in the corpus. Evaluated at the corpus's own printed three-figure $\beta$, its own evolution equation gives $\mathcal{R} = 4{,}49\times10^{-14}$ cm where the corpus prints $4{,}82\times10^{-14}$; the value that reproduces the print is $\beta = 1{,}74202\times10^{-39}$ g cm, and the amplification factor is $\approx 62$. The printed pair is therefore consistent only if $\beta$ is read to four figures, and the corpus carries three. The mechanism is exact and checkable: the éon's $\Lambda$ is a difference of two nearly equal terms, and the éon's parameters sit pinned against their own existence bounds — $\beta$ at $99{,}1\,\%$ of the ceiling, $\xi$ at $98{,}4\,\%$, $R_{0}$ at $67{,}2\,\%$.

This matters beyond the éon. The register's proposed tightening of gate G-B — "the model's own turning points, not merely expansion" — is decided here, and it is decided in the model's favour at four-figure precision and against it at the model's own printed precision. The article records which, and why, so that G-B′ is not read as either a pass or a failure.

What the Corpus Claims

§3.4 — the éon's minimum and maximum radii come out as $R_{0} = 8{,}01\times10^{-15}$ cm, $R_{\max} = 4{,}82\times10^{-14}$ cm, $T_{0} = 4{,}93\times10^{-24}$ s. RC (3-72), (3-68). Tag: [SPEC] (fit). The register's gloss: "$T_{0}$ is fixed from the measured lepton mass difference, so this is not a prediction. The radii are then derived. There is no independent way to observe an imaginary micro-universe, so these numbers are unfalsifiable today."

§3.5 — the three internal constants are computed from the three measured charged-lepton masses. RC (3-68). Tag: [MATH] (definition), [SPEC] (physics). S21 owns §3.5; this article owns what the three constants do — the radius spectrum they generate.

1. The Chain, and Who Chose What

RC 051 states the production order in one paragraph, and the order is the whole story. "Pour tout choix d'une valeur du rayon minimum initial $R_{0}$, ou pour tout choix du paramètre $\xi = \Omega_{0}c/\hbar$, il y a une solution et une seule du Système $E$, pourvu que ce choix satisfasse les deux conditions suivantes" — and then: "Utilisant alors l'équation fondamentale d'évolution (3-32) avec cette valeur (3-71) de $\xi$, nous calculons pour le rayon initial $R_{0}$ à $\tau = 0$ et le rayon maximum $\mathcal{R}$ à $\tau = T_{0}/2$."

So the structure is:

step source status
$T_{0} = 2h/[c^{2}(\tau-\mu)]$ (3-68) fitted to the measured lepton mass difference
$\alpha$, $\beta$ (3-68) defined by inversion (S21)
$\xi$ (3-71), $(3\text{-}49\text{-}a)$ fixed by the closure $l_{0} = R_{H}$ (S22)
$R_{0}$, $\mathcal{R}$ (3-72), from (3-32) outputs of the evolution equation at that $\xi$
$\rho$, $\theta$, $R''$ at both ends RC 052 table outputs of the same solution

One choice ($\xi$, equivalently the closure), one fit ($T_{0}$), and everything else follows. So $R_{0}$ is not itself fitted, and it is not itself free: it is a function of a quantity that the closure fixed. That is a slightly more favourable reading than "the radii are then derived", and it is the correct one — but it does not help, because the quantity the closure fixed was itself fixed using $T_{0}$ (S22).

2. $T_{0}$ Verified, and Dated

$T_{0} = 2h/[c^{2}(\tau-\mu)]$ evaluated at the corpus's own 1983 masses gives $4{,}9277\times10^{-24}$ s against the printed $4{,}93\times10^{-24}$ s. Verified.

Evaluated at 2024 masses it gives $4{,}9493\times10^{-24}$ s — $0{,}44\,\%$ higher. The corpus's $T_{0}$ was right for its decade and is now $0{,}4\,\%$ low, and the reason no larger correction is needed is that the quantity is a difference of two heavy masses, which the last forty years of measurement moved by very little. Per rule R4 the verdict is dated, and the date is the corpus's favour: nothing here depended on a measurement that has since moved.

3. The Existence Bounds, and Where the Solution Sits

RC 051 gives two bounds on the choice, and the corpus's own values sit at the top of both.

From (3-69-1a):

$$0 \le R_{0} \le \frac{\beta}{\alpha} = 11{,}91\cdot10^{-15}\ \mathrm{cm}$$

Verified: $\beta/\alpha = 1{,}1918\times10^{-14}$ cm at the printed (3-68) values. And $R_{0} = 8{,}01\times10^{-15}$ cm sits at $\mathbf{67{,}2\,\%}$ of it.

From (3-69-1b):

$$0 \le \xi \le \frac{R_{0}^{2}\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{\beta^{2}/R_{0}^{2} - \alpha^{2}}}$$

Verified: the ceiling evaluates to $6{,}9089\times10^{-4}$ cgs, and the printed $\xi = 6{,}8\times10^{-4}$ sits at $\mathbf{98{,}4\,\%}$ of it.

And the third ceiling is implicit in (3-69-2), which the corpus imposes because the existence conditions demand a negative $\Lambda$. $\Lambda \le 0$ is equivalent to

$$\frac{\beta^{2}}{R_{0}^{2}} \le \alpha^{2} + \frac{3R_{0}^{4}}{\xi^{2}} \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad |\beta| \le R_{0}\sqrt{\alpha^{2} + \frac{3R_{0}^{4}}{\xi^{2}}} = 1{,}755333\times10^{-39}\ \mathrm{g\,cm}$$

Verified. And the printed $\beta = 1{,}74\times10^{-39}$ sits at $\mathbf{99{,}13\,\%}$ of that ceiling. As $|\beta|$ approaches it, $\Lambda \to 0$ and the two turning points of (3-32) merge: the pulsation amplitude goes to zero.

This is the structural fact the register does not carry, and it is the reason for everything in §5. The éon is not a comfortable solution of the corpus's own equations; it is a near-critical one, with two of its three parameters pinned within $2\,\%$ of the boundary of the region where the model exists at all.

4. The Turning Point at $R_{0}$ Is an Identity, and $K = +1$ Falls Out of It

The corpus states $K = +1$ twice — RC (3-36) and Esp (201) — as the requirement that the micro-universe be a finite space. It is also exactly what the corpus's own $\Lambda$ normalisation requires, and the identity is worth writing down because it makes the whole block checkable.

Substituting (3-69-2) into (3-32) and evaluating at $R = R_{0}$:

$$\frac{R'^{2}}{c^{2}}\bigg|_{R_{0}} = \frac{1}{3}\frac{\xi^{2}}{R_{0}^{4}}\left(\alpha^{2} - \frac{\beta^{2}}{R_{0}^{2}}\right) + K + \frac{1}{3}\left[\frac{\xi^{2}}{R_{0}^{6}}\left(\frac{\beta^{2}}{R_{0}^{2}} - \alpha^{2}\right) - \frac{3}{R_{0}^{2}}\right]R_{0}^{2} = K - 1$$

The two bracketed terms cancel identically, term by term, for any $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\xi$. So (3-69-2) is not an independent existence condition at all: it is the statement that $R_{0}$ is a turning point when $K = +1$. Said the other way round — which is the corpus's own direction — the requirement that $R_{0}$ be a turning point, combined with $K = +1$, forces the printed $\Lambda$.

This is a positive result for the block and the register does not have it. The corpus's "conditions d'existence (3-35) et (3-36)" and its $\Lambda$ normalisation are one statement, not three, and the identity is exact. It also does all the checking work: (3-32) with the printed $\Lambda$ and $K = +1$ must have $R_{0}$ as a root, and it does, at machine precision, for any parameter values — so every remaining question in this article is a question about the other root.

5. The Finding: $\mathcal{R}$ Is Hypersensitive, and the Printed $\beta$ Does Not Reproduce It

Method. Put $\alpha = -1{,}46\times10^{-25}$ g, $\beta = -1{,}74\times10^{-39}$ g cm, $\xi = 6{,}8\times10^{-4}$ cgs, $R_{0} = 8{,}01\times10^{-15}$ cm (all as printed in RC 051), $\Lambda$ from (3-69-2), $K = +1$, and factor (3-32) as a cubic in $u = R^{2}$. Since $u_{0} = R_{0}^{2}$ is a root by §4, the two remaining roots come from a quadratic, so the test is exact rather than numerical.

Result.

$\beta$ (g cm) $\Lambda$ (cm$^{-2}$) $\mathcal{R}$ (cm)
$-1{,}74\times10^{-39}$ (printed) $-1{,}4624\times10^{27}$ $4{,}4870\times10^{-14}$
$-1{,}74202\times10^{-39}$ $-1{,}2724\times10^{27}$ $\mathbf{4{,}8200\times10^{-14}}$
— RC 052 table — $4{,}85\times10^{-14}$
— (3-72) — $4{,}82\times10^{-14}$

So: the corpus's printed $\beta$ gives $\mathcal{R} = 4{,}49\times10^{-14}$ cm, $6{,}9\,\%$ below the printed $4{,}82$; and the $\beta$ that gives $4{,}82$ is $1{,}74202\times10^{-39}$, a difference of $0{,}12\,\%$ from the printed $1{,}74$.

The rounding is legitimate — $1{,}74202$ prints as $1{,}74$ at three significant figures — and that is exactly the point. The éon's $\mathcal{R}$ is not a quantity that three-figure $\beta$ determines: the corpus's own third figure on $\beta$ is worth $7\,\%$ on $\mathcal{R}$. The finding is about the model's conditioning, not about a misprint, and the article states it that way throughout.

The amplification factor is therefore

$$\frac{d\ln\mathcal{R}}{d\ln\beta} = \mathbf{62}$$

— a $0{,}1\,\%$ change in $\beta$ moves the éon's maximum radius by $6\,\%$. And RC 052's table gives a third value, $4{,}85\times10^{-14}$, which is consistent with neither.

The 1983 set behaves identically, which is what shows the property is the éon's and not a page's. Esp 058's solution table gives $R_{0} = 7{,}8\times10^{-15}$ cm, $\mathcal{R} = 4{,}8\times10^{-14}$ cm, $\xi = 6{,}467\times10^{-4}$ cgs, $\alpha = -1{,}472\times10^{-25}$ g, $\beta = -1{,}7\times10^{-39}$ g cm. From the printed $\beta = 1{,}70\times10^{-39}$ the equation gives $\mathcal{R} = 4{,}7056\times10^{-14}$; the $\beta$ that gives the printed $4{,}8\times10^{-14}$ is $1{,}70049\times10^{-39}$, which prints as $1{,}70$.

1987 (RC 051/052) $1{,}74\times10^{-39}$ $4{,}487\times10^{-14}$ $1{,}74202\times10^{-39}$ $61{,}6$ $99{,}13\,\%$

Two editions, two parameter sets, the same structure: the printed pair is consistent to the rounding of $\beta$'s third figure, the amplification is $\approx 62$–$69$, and the parameter sits within $1\,\%$ of the ceiling in both.

Register consequence: the note in §13 that treats "$4{,}82$ printed against $4{,}85$ tabulated, $0{,}6\,\%$" as a scan-checking item is superseded. The $0{,}6\,\%$ is not the question, and no scan can settle it: both printed values lie inside the window that three-figure $\beta$ leaves open, and the honest statement is that the éon's $\mathcal{R}$ is not determined at the precision the corpus writes.

And a consequence for the citation rule, which is sharper than S5's Hazard 5 stated it. Mixing the books' parameter sets does not shift the solution — it removes it. The 1983 $R_{0} = 7{,}8\times10^{-15}$ with the 1987 $\xi$, $\alpha$, $\beta$ drives $\Lambda$ positive, $\Lambda = +9{,}10\times10^{27}$ cm$^{-2}$, and (3-32) then has no second turning point at all. So "cite one book at a time" is not a presentational preference: the éon's existence is edition-specific, and the two sets are not interchangeable.

6. Why: $\Lambda$ Is a Near-Cancellation, and the Parameters Are Pinned

The mechanism is transparent once written out. $\Lambda$ from (3-69-2) is a difference of two terms of the same size:

$$\frac{\xi^{2}}{R_{0}^{6}}\left(\frac{\beta^{2}}{R_{0}^{2}} - \alpha^{2}\right) = +4{,}53\cdot10^{28}\ \mathrm{cm^{-2}}, \qquad \frac{3}{R_{0}^{2}} = 4{,}68\cdot10^{28}\ \mathrm{cm^{-2}}, \qquad \Lambda = -1{,}46\cdot10^{27}\ \mathrm{cm^{-2}}$$

$\Lambda$ is $3\,\%$ of either term. An $8\,\%$ change in $\beta^{2}$ moves the first term by $3{,}6\times10^{27}$ cm$^{-2}$ — two and a half times the whole of $\Lambda$. So $\Lambda$ inherits an amplification of roughly $30$ from the subtraction, and $\mathcal{R}$ inherits a further amplification because the éon sits against the ceiling of §3, where the two roots of the cubic are close.

The two amplifications compound, and they are the reason for the $62$. This is a structural property of the corpus's éon, not a defect of a particular printed figure, and it is the kind of property that a rebuilt statement must record: a solution whose parameters are pinned against its own existence bounds cannot be quoted to three figures.

7. The Table's $R''(R_{0})$ Does Not Reproduce Either

RC 052's table gives $R''$ at both turning points: $+1{,}04\times10^{35}$ cm s$^{-2}$ at $R_{0}$ and $-1{,}8\times10^{34}$ cm s$^{-2}$ at $\mathcal{R}$.

The transcription of (3-33) was checked before being used, and it is exactly the derivative of (3-32):

$$R'' = \frac{c^{2}}{6R^{3}}\left[2\xi^{2}\frac{\beta^{2}}{R_{0}^{4}} - \xi^{2}\alpha^{2}\left(\frac{3}{R_{0}^{2}} - \frac{2}{R^{2}}\right) + 2\Lambda R^{4}\right] \;=\; \frac{c^{2}}{2}\frac{d}{dR}\left(\frac{R'^{2}}{c^{2}}\right)$$

The algebra checks out term by term, so the transcription is not the suspect. Evaluated at the printed parameters it gives

$$R''(R_{0}) = 1{,}50\times10^{35}\ \mathrm{cm\,s^{-2}} \qquad\text{against the printed } 1{,}04\times10^{35}$$

and the $\beta$ that would give $1{,}04\times10^{35}$ is $1{,}636\times10^{-39}$ g cm — $6{,}0\,\%$ from the $\beta$ that reproduces $\mathcal{R}$.

So the table's two entries are not mutually consistent under the corpus's own equations: no single $\beta$ produces both. Again the honest reading is ill-conditioning rather than an arithmetic error, and again it is not repairable from the printed page — but it is recorded, because a rebuilt statement that quotes the éon's $R''$ would be quoting a number that its own equation does not give.

Gate G-B′. The gate the register proposes reads "the model's own turning points, not merely expansion". The verdict is: passed in form at four-figure $\beta$ — the corpus's equation does have $R_{0}$ and $\mathcal{R} = 4{,}82\times10^{-14}$ cm as its two turning points, exactly, when $\beta$ is read to four figures — and not passed on the table's $R''$ entry, which no single $\beta$ produces. Per rule R2′, this is reported as a new gate's own verdict and it does not revise G-B, which stands.**

8. Two Regularities Recovered From the Printed Table

Both turning points of RC 052's table carry a density and a temperature, and the printed values satisfy two relations the corpus does not state.

First, the density is $\rho = \alpha/R^{3}$:

printed $\rho$ $\alpha/R^{3}$
at $R_{0} = 8{,}01\times10^{-15}$ $-2{,}9\times10^{17}$ $-2{,}84\times10^{17}$
at $\mathcal{R} = 4{,}82\times10^{-14}$ $-1{,}3\times10^{15}$ $-1{,}3038\times10^{15}$

One constant reproduces both entries across a factor $218$ in $\rho$, and the second is exact to all printed figures. So the éon's "density" is the constant $\alpha$ spread over the volume $R^{3}$ — the same $\alpha$ as in (3-68) — and the negative sign is the imaginary sheet's (S2).

Second, the temperature satisfies $\theta R = 3{,}76\times10^{-9}$ K cm at both ends ($3{,}7647\times10^{-9}$ at $R_{0}$, $3{,}7596\times10^{-9}$ at $\mathcal{R}$ — agreeing to $0{,}14\,\%$). So $\theta \propto 1/R$ exactly, over the same factor-of-six range in $R$.

Neither definition is printed in the transcription, so both are recorded as regularities of the printed table and filed in the check queue rather than asserted as the corpus's definitions. They are worth having because they are the block's internal consistency, and because $\rho = \alpha/R^{3}$ shows how little independent content the éon's density carries.

9. And the Éon's Boundary Does Not Move at $c$

The corpus's other pulsation law — the boundary moves at exactly $\pm c$ — gives the préon $\mathcal{R}/R_{0} = e^{\pi} = 23{,}14$ (S18) and the holon $R_{H\max} = R_{H} + cT_{0}/2$ (S23).

Applied to the éon it fails immediately, and the numbers are the proof. With $T_{0} = 4{,}93\times10^{-24}$ s,

$$\frac{cT_{0}}{2} = 7{,}39\cdot10^{-14}\ \mathrm{cm} \qquad\text{while the éon's amplitude is}\qquad \mathcal{R} - R_{0} = 4{,}02\cdot10^{-14}\ \mathrm{cm} = \mathbf{54{,}4\,\%}\ \text{of it}$$

So the éon's boundary moves at no more than $54{,}4\,\%$ of $c$ on average, and RC 044 itself says why: "la condition initiale $R' = dR/d\tau = \pm c$ concerne seulement le cas d'un micro-univers Imaginaire où la densité propre $\rho$ est nulle (seulement un neutrino, pas de courbure riemannienne scalaire, pas de photons thermiques)." The radius ratio $\mathcal{R}/R_{0} = 6{,}02$ (printed) or $6{,}01$ (from $\beta = 1{,}742\times10^{-39}$) is the éon's own number, equally far from $e^{\pi}$ and from $1 + cT_{0}/2R_{0} = 10{,}2$.

That is the direct bridge to S23, and it is the cleanest three-way comparison in the corpus: three micro-structures, three pulsation laws, three amplitude ratios, and only one of the three laws is derived.

10. Verdict

§3.4 and §3.5 stand in substance, and both need their arithmetic restated.

  • Confirmed: $T_{0}$ is fitted to $\tau - \mu$; $R_{0}$ and $\mathcal{R}$ are outputs of (3-32) at the closure-fixed $\xi$; the block is unfalsifiable today.
  • Verified and exact: $T_{0} = 4{,}9277\times10^{-24}$ s from the corpus's own masses (printed $4{,}93$), $+0{,}44\,\%$ at 2024 masses; the identity $R'^{2}(R_{0}) = c^{2}(K-1)$, which makes (3-69-2) and $K = +1$ one statement rather than three.
  • New, and the article's finding: the éon's $\mathcal{R}$ is ill-conditioned in $\beta$ with elasticity $\mathbf{62}$ (1987 set) and $\mathbf{68}$ (1983 set); the printed three-figure $\beta$ gives $4{,}49\times10^{-14}$ cm, not the printed $4{,}82\times10^{-14}$; the table's $4{,}85$ is a third value; the table's $R''(R_{0})$ requires a $\beta$ $6{,}0\,\%$ away. The cause is that $\Lambda$ is a $3\,\%$ difference of two large terms and the parameters sit at $99{,}13\,\%$ ($\beta$), $98{,}42\,\%$ ($\xi$) and $67{,}2\,\%$ ($R_{0}$) of their existence ceilings — with the other edition at $99{,}25\,\%$ of its own $\beta$ ceiling.
  • Superseded: the register's $0{,}6\,\%$ "$4{,}82$ vs $4{,}85$" note as a scan-checking item. No scan settles it.
  • Sharpened: S5's Hazard 5 citation rule. Mixing the editions removes the solution rather than shifting it: the 1983 $R_{0}$ with the 1987 $\xi$, $\alpha$, $\beta$ gives $\Lambda = +9{,}10\times10^{27}$ cm$^{-2} > 0$, and (3-32) then has no second turning point at all.
  • Recovered: $\rho = \alpha/R^{3}$ and $\theta R = 3{,}76\times10^{-9}$ K cm — the printed table's internal consistency, definitions not transcribed.
  • Corrected in the register's favour: $R_{0}$ is not "then derived" from nothing; it is the solution of the corpus's own equation at a closure-fixed parameter, and the identity of §4 shows the whole block is internally consistent at that level.

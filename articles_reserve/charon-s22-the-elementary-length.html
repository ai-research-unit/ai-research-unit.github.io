Charon — The Elementary Length (S22)

Audits §3.6 of CHARON_IDEAS_AND_CLAIMS.md, against RC ch. III and IV (scans 051, 060, 061, 063) and Esp §VI (scans 053, 057, 058).

Introduction

The register's verdict on §3.6 — "[SPEC] (coupling ansatz), partly [EST] numerically … $l_{0}$ is imposed as the closure condition linking the leptonic and hadronic systems" — is half right, and the half that is wrong is the important half.

$l_{0}$ is not imposed. It is the fixed point of a self-consistency condition, and the corpus says so twice: once structurally, in RC 060's Système $H$, which takes $\xi = \frac{4}{3}\pi l_{0}^{3}c/\hbar$ as a parameter; and once in the plainest possible words, in Esp 057's footnote — the value of $\xi$ deduced from the nucleon mass "n'est bien qu'une « suggestion » pour entamer le calcul de la solution exacte du Système $L$ : c'est ce Système $L$ qui doit directement calculer $\xi$, d'où on déduira ensuite $M_{0}$, masse propre du nucléon, comme une vérification du formalisme général."

So the corpus's intended logic is: the Système $L$ computes $\xi$; $l_{0}$ follows from it; and the nucleon mass is the check. That is a different structure from "the elementary length is imposed", and it changes where the input/output ledger puts $l_{0}$ (S34, §7). It does not, by itself, make $l_{0}$ a prediction — whether the Système $L$ really computes $\xi$ is the question the register answers no to (S30–S33) — but the register's sentence must be corrected, because it is the corpus's own reading of its own model that the article has to audit.

What the Corpus Claims

§3.6 — the elementary length $l_{0}$ equals the holon's radius: $l_{0} = R_{H} = 1{,}8\times10^{-14}$ cm; hence $\Omega_{0} = 2{,}44\times10^{-41}$ cm³ and $\xi = 6{,}8\times10^{-4}$ cgs. RC (3-49-a), (3-70), (3-71). Tag: [SPEC] (coupling ansatz), partly [EST] numerically. The register's gloss: "The idea of a fundamental length is live (Planck length $1{,}6\times10^{-33}$ cm is the accepted candidate; note Charon's $l_{0}$ is $10^{19}$ times larger). His value $0{,}18$ fm is within a factor ~5 of the proton charge radius ($0{,}841$ fm) — order-of-magnitude plausible, not a prediction, since $l_{0}$ is imposed as the closure condition."

1. The Numbers, and Each Book's Set Closes

The three printed numbers reproduce exactly, in both editions, and in each edition the set is internally consistent to the printed precision.

1987 (RC 051) 1983 (Esp 057/058)
$l_{0}$ $1{,}8\times10^{-14}$ cm $1{,}76\times10^{-14}$ cm
$\Omega_{0} = \frac{4}{3}\pi l_{0}^{3}$ $2{,}4429\times10^{-41}$ cm³ (printed $2{,}44$) $2{,}2836\times10^{-41}$ cm³
$\xi = \Omega_{0}c/\hbar$ $6{,}9447\times10^{-4}$ cgs (printed $6{,}8$) $6{,}4919\times10^{-4}$ cgs
$\xi$ as printed $6{,}8\times10^{-4}$ $6{,}467\times10^{-4}$
$l_{0}$ implied by the printed $\xi$ $1{,}7869\times10^{-14}$ cm $\mathbf{1{,}7570\times10^{-14}}$ cm

The 1983 pair is exact: $\xi = \frac{4}{3}\pi l_{0}^{3}c/\hbar$ with $l_{0} = 1{,}7570\times10^{-14}$ cm gives $\xi = 6{,}467\times10^{-4}$ cgs, which is what Esp prints — so Esp's $1{,}76$ is the three-figure rounding of a quantity its own $\xi$ fixes to four. The 1987 printed $\xi = 6{,}8\times10^{-4}$ is the one-figure-$\Omega_{0}$ reading ($2{,}4\times10^{-41}$ gives $6{,}83\times10^{-4}$), which is S5's Hazard 5 and is not re-opened here.

The two editions' $l_{0}$'s differ by $2{,}4\,\%$ and their $\xi$'s by $7{,}4\,\%$, and since $\xi \propto l_{0}^{3}$ the second follows from the first. So the choice of book moves every quantity in S20 by more than the effects S20 measures, which is why the citation rule is load-bearing there and here.

2. The Correction: $l_{0}$ Is a Fixed Point, Not an Imposition

Two pieces of the corpus settle this, and they are independent.

First, the structure of the Système $H$. RC 060 (4-13) lists the system's parameters as

$$T_{H} = T_{0} = \frac{2\pi}{\omega_{0}}, \qquad \xi = \frac{4}{3}\pi l_{0}^{3}\frac{c}{\hbar}$$

— and (4-14) then computes $M_{0}$ and $R_{0}$ as functions of the central density $\rho_{0}$. So $\xi$ enters the hadronic system as an input parameter, and the system returns $R_{H}$ as one of its outputs. The coupling condition (3-49-a), $l_{0} = R_{H}$, therefore reads: the $l_{0}$ that entered through $\xi$ must equal the $R_{H}$ that came out. One equation, one unknown.

Second, the corpus says so in words. Esp 057's footnote, on the value $\xi = 6{,}467\times10^{-4}$ obtained from the nucleon mass:

"Mais, bien entendu, cette valeur numérique de $\xi$ déduite de $M_{0}$ n'est bien qu'une « suggestion » pour entamer le calcul de la solution exacte du Système $L$ : c'est ce Système $L$ qui doit directement calculer $\xi$, d'où on déduira ensuite $M_{0}$, masse propre du nucléon, comme une vérification du formalisme général (modèles leptonique et hadronique)."

So in the corpus's own account $M_{0}$ is the verification and $\xi$ is the computed quantity — and the value obtained from $M_{0}$ is demoted to a starting suggestion. And that is S34's ledger entry — "one defined constant, five measured seeds, one suggested, zero independent outputs" — in the corpus's own words: the suggested one is $\xi$, i.e. $l_{0}$, and the suggestion is the corpus's, not the register's.

The consequence for the register's accounting, and it is precise. $l_{0}$ carries no new measured input: it is fixed by the closure, the closure's period is $T_{H} = T_{0}$ and $T_{0}$ is the lepton fit (S21), and the Système $H$'s remaining input $\rho_{0}$ is swept rather than measured. So the éon/holon block's input list stays $c$, $h$, $m$, $\mu$, $\tau$ — with $l_{0}$ an output of the coupling and $M_{H}$ a check on it. Whether that check succeeds is R3's question, not this article's; S22's job is to record that the corpus intended it as a check, which is more favourable than the register's wording, and to hand the verification to S30–S33 and S27.

3. The Scale, Done Honestly

$l_{0}$ against the Planck length:

$$\frac{l_{0}}{\ell_{\mathrm{Planck}}} = \frac{1{,}8\times10^{-14}}{1{,}616\times10^{-33}} = 1{,}11\times10^{19}$$

The corpus's elementary length is $1{,}1\times10^{19}$ Planck lengths — not a candidate for the fundamental length, and not offered as one, but this is the number the register should carry beside the phrase "the idea of a fundamental length is live", because "fundamental length" in this corpus means something at the fermi scale.

$l_{0}$ against the proton:

value ratio to $0{,}8414$ fm
$l_{0} = R_{H}$ $0{,}180$ fm $0{,}214$
$R_{H\max}$ (S23) $0{,}920$ fm $1{,}093$

$l_{0}$ is $4{,}7$ times smaller than the proton charge radius, so the register's "$0{,}18$ fm is within a factor ~5 of $0{,}841$ fm" is arithmetically right and misleading: the quantity that lands near the proton is $R_{H\max}$, one line of the corpus later, and it is $R_{H\max}$ that S23 owns. Corrected in the register: §3.6's "order-of-magnitude plausible" sentence should attach to $R_{H\max}$, and $l_{0}$'s own comparison is with the Planck length — where the answer is "not comparable".

4. Two Checks That Pass, and Which Are Worth Having

(a) The small-density branch of the Système $H$. RC 061's (4-16) gives the analytic approximation for small $\rho_{0}$:

$$M_{0} = \sqrt{\tfrac{2}{3}}\,\frac{\xi^{2}c^{5}T_{0}^{5}}{\pi^{4}}\rho_{0}^{3} = 2{,}75\times10^{-73}\rho_{0}^{3}\ \mathrm{g}, \qquad R_{0} = \frac{\xi c^{2}T_{0}^{2}}{\sqrt{2}\,\pi^{2}}\rho_{0} = 1{,}07\times10^{-30}\rho_{0}\ \mathrm{cm}$$

Evaluated at the corpus's own $\xi$ and $T_{0}$: $2{,}733\times10^{-73}$ and $1{,}064\times10^{-30}$. Verified against the printed $2{,}75$ and $1{,}07$, differences $0{,}6\,\%$ and $0{,}6\,\%$ — and that residual is the rounding of $\xi$ to $6{,}8\times10^{-4}$, since the coefficient is $\xi^{2}$-driven. So the corpus's $\xi$ is used consistently across the two systems, which is the internal consistency the coupling is supposed to deliver.

And a transcription defect to record, not fix: (4-16) prints the first relation with a linear $\rho_{0}$ where the formula immediately above it is cubic in $\rho_{0}$ and the coefficient's units require the cube. Recorded in §13's list as a transcription gap; it does not affect any claim.

(b) The volume formulas are not the same for the two sectors, and this one needs the page. The corpus defines $\Omega_{0} = \frac{4}{3}\pi l_{0}^{3}$ — the Euclidean ball — and calls the holon's volume $\Omega_{0}$ (RC 068, §II.5). But the imaginary micro-universe's volume is $2\pi^{2}R^{3}$, the closed $K = +1$ three-sphere (RC 047, and the cosmological (5-41)). Both objects are assigned $K = +1$ (RC (3-36)), and the two formulas differ by a factor $3\pi/2 = 4{,}71$. Recorded as a flag rather than an error: $\Omega_{0}$ may be a deliberately Euclidean cell used to convert a density into a mass in (4-14), in which case there is no inconsistency — but the corpus also calls the holon's volume $\Omega_{0}$, and if that is meant literally then $\rho_{n} = m_{n}/\Omega_{0}$ (§5.5, §10.9) is using a Euclidean volume for a closed space. One image of RC 062 or RC 068 around the passage does not settle it; the definitions section of the formalism (RC 019–023, where (2-18-a) lives) does, and it is already transcribed. Check-queue item.

5. The Repair List Gains One Item, and It Is Load-Bearing

RC 060 states that (4-13)'s parameters "ont été déduits du couplage (3-49) et (4-11) entre les deux Systèmes $E$ et $H$" — and (4-11) is nowhere. The transcription jumps from (4-10-a) to (4-12), and neither RC/060.tex nor RC/060_improved.tex contains it.

The reason this matters more than the other gaps: (4-11) is the second half of the éon/holon coupling. RC 060's prose says the two halves are (3-49), the length condition this article is about, and the period lock $T_{H} = T_{0}$; so (4-11)'s content is inferable. But it is a coupling condition, i.e. an equation that fixes a parameter of both systems, and the corpus's whole architecture rests on the two systems being solvable only together. Filed in §13 with a note that this one warrants an image of RC_im/060.jpg or 061.jpg — the only image request in this batch.

A second transcription defect, of the same class as S5's Hazard 10. Esp 058's solution table gives the rows

Symbole Définition cgs
$\alpha$ Densité rayonnement noir : $D_{E} = \beta c^{2}/R^{4}$ $-1{,}472\times10^{-25}$
$\beta$ Densité rayonn. noir : $D_{E} = Bc^{2}/R^{4}$ $-1{,}7\times10^{-39}$

— where the first row's definition uses $\beta$ for a quantity of $\alpha$'s dimension (a mass) and the second uses $B$. The symbol $\beta$ is carrying two different objects across two rows, and $B$ appears from nowhere. This is a collapsed distinction rather than an invented text, and it is exactly S5's Hazard 10 pattern: it does not affect any claim, it is recorded so that a later pass does not "fix" $\alpha$'s row by substituting $\beta$'s value.

6. Verdict

§3.6 stands in substance and its register gloss needs two corrections.

  • Confirmed: $l_{0} = R_{H} = 1{,}8\times10^{-14}$ cm; $\Omega_{0} = 2{,}44\times10^{-41}$ cm³; $\xi = 6{,}8\times10^{-4}$ cgs as printed; the relation $\xi = \frac{4}{3}\pi l_{0}^{3}c/\hbar$ holds in both editions at their own printed precision.
  • Corrected (the article's main point): $l_{0}$ is not "imposed as the closure condition"; it is the fixed point of one. The Système $H$ takes $\xi(l_{0})$ as a parameter (4-13) and returns $R_{H}$; the coupling (3-49-a) sets $l_{0} = R_{H}$; and Esp 057 in the corpus's own words calls the $l_{0}$ obtained from $M_{0}$ a suggestion and makes $M_{0}$ the verification. So $l_{0}$ carries no new measured input, and the register's "since $l_{0}$ is imposed" clause should be replaced by "since $l_{0}$ is fixed by the closure and nothing external tests it".
  • Corrected (secondary): the "$0{,}18$ fm is within a factor ~5 of $0{,}841$ fm" comparison belongs to $R_{H\max}$, not to $l_{0}$; $l_{0}$'s own comparison is with the Planck length, and it is $1{,}11\times10^{19}$ of them.
  • Verified: the two books' sets are each internally exact (1983: $l_{0} = 1{,}7570\times10^{-14}$ cms from $\xi = 6{,}467\times10^{-4}$; 1987: $\xi$ printed as the one-figure-$\Omega_{0}$ reading); the (4-16) coefficients reproduce to $0{,}6\,\%$ from $\xi$ and $T_{0}$ alone, so $\xi$ is used consistently across the two systems.
  • Flagged: the Euclidean $\Omega_{0} = \frac{4}{3}\pi l_{0}^{3}$ against the closed $2\pi^{2}R^{3}$ used for the micro-universe, both at $K = +1$, differing by $3\pi/2$.
  • Check queue: the volume-formula flag; and the (3-69-1b) interaction of §7.
  • Repair list: (4-11) is referenced and not printed — the one image request; (4-16)'s missing cube; Esp 058's collapsed $\alpha$/$\beta$/$B$ symbols.
  • §3.6's tag is unchanged — [SPEC] (coupling ansatz), partly [EST] numerically — with the note that the numerical part is $R_{H\max}$'s, and that $R_{H\max}$'s plausibility is S23's finding and S23's caveat.

And the near-criticality, in one line, because it is the bridge to S20. The exact 1987 chain gives $\xi = 6{,}9447\times10^{-4}$ against (3-69-1b)'s ceiling of $6{,}9089\times10^{-4}$ computed with the printed $\beta$ — i.e. at the full precision of its own closure the corpus's $\xi$ sits $0{,}5\,\%$ outside an existence bound, while the printed $\xi = 6{,}8\times10^{-4}$ sits at $98{,}4\,\%$ inside it. Both the parameter and the bound are three-figure quantities, so the honest reading is not that the model is excluded but that its existence condition cannot be used to three figures — the same conclusion S20 reached from the other side.

Part S / Part P. Part S: the relation $\xi = \frac{4}{3}\pi l_{0}^{3}c/\hbar$, the closure structure, the cross-book arithmetic, the (4-16) check, the volume-formula flag — audited algebra and accounting. Part P: the claim that there is an elementary length at all, and that it is the holon's radius — because the holon's stability is the Système $H$'s claim and the constraint $l_{0} = R_{H}$ is only as good as that system's solution. The relation survives; the identification does not, until S30–S33 and S26 have their say.

Summary of Notation

  • $l_{0}$ — the elementary length; the corpus's longueur élémentaire, also written $R_{H}$ after (3-49-a).
  • $\Omega_{0}$ — the elementary volume, $\frac{4}{3}\pi l_{0}^{3}$. The corpus also calls the holon's volume $\Omega_{0}$; the micro-universe's volume is the different $2\pi^{2}R^{3}$ (§4b).
  • $\xi$ — $\Omega_{0}c/\hbar$; $[\![\xi]\!] = \mathrm{cm^{2}/g}$. The corpus's third parameter, the one the Système $H$ takes as input and the closure fixes.
  • $R_{H}$ — the holon's minimum radius; $R_{H\max}$, its maximum, is $R_{H} + cT_{0}/2$ and is S23's.
  • $T_{H}$, $T_{0}$ — the holon's and the éon's pulsation periods; $T_{H} = T_{0}$ is the second half of the coupling (with (3-49-a)), printed as (4-11) and missing from the transcription.
  • (3-49-a), $l_{0} = R_{H}$ — the length half of the coupling; (4-13) — the Système $H$'s parameter list; (4-14) — its $M_{0}$, $R_{0}$; (4-16) — the small-$\rho_{0}$ approximation.
  • "suggestion" — the corpus's word, Esp 057, for the $\xi$ obtained from $M_{0}$. It is S34's "one suggested" constant.

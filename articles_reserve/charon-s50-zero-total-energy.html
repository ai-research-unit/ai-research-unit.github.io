Zero Total Energy: The Most Respectable Idea in the Corpus, and the Stipulation Everything Early Descends From

Introduction

The corpus's universe contains, at every instant of its age, exactly zero total energy.

$$(5\text{-}8)\quad D_{\rho} + D_{E} + D_{M} \;\equiv\; 0,$$

written with an identity sign and holding for all $\tau$ — the corpus's word is "identiquement", and it means it. The three terms are the density of gravitational-potential energy $D_{\rho} = W_{\rho}/\Omega_{0}$ associated with the matter's holonic parts, the electromagnetic energy density $D_{E} = a\theta^{4}$ of the black-body radiation filling the closed space, and the relativistic energy density $D_{M}$ of the nucleons and neutrinos (RC 073). The corpus states it twice more, in equivalent forms — as (5-60), $D_{\rho} + \beta c^{2}/R^{4} + D_{M} = 0$ once $D_{E}/c^{2} = \beta/R^{4}$ is substituted, and as the second line of (5-46) at RC 077.

The register tags this [MIX] — "the idea is respectable, the version is his own" — and both halves of the tag are correct, but the article's finding is that they are correct in an unexpected proportion. This article's three results:

  1. The accounting is the standard one, not an exotic one. The negative term is the gravitational potential energy of the matter, exactly the term that makes the total energy of a closed (K = +1) universe vanish in general relativity under the Hamiltonian constraint. The corpus's decomposition is the same decomposition Tryon used in 1973, and the corpus's identity is numerically consistent with its own published mass of the universe today — this article verifies it (§2).
  2. But it is a stipulation, not a derivation, and the corpus never claims otherwise. Its justification is a conservation argument, and a conservation argument gives $E = \mathrm{const}$, never $E = 0$ (§3). The corpus supplies the missing premise explicitly — the universe cannot have come from nothing — and that premise is refuted or vacated by the corpus's own non-singular, cyclic cosmology, whichever reading of $\tau = 0$ is taken (§4). This is the article's sharpest finding.
  3. And the stipulation is load-bearing, in a direction observation has since measured against it. (5-8) is what makes the vacuum density $\rho$ negative; $\rho < 0$ is what the existence conditions require for $\Lambda \le 0$; and $\Lambda \le 0$ is what the bounce needs (§5). So the corpus's worst observational tension — a vacuum of the wrong sign — descends from a stipulation that was never derived, and would disappear if the stipulation were dropped. That is a constructive result: the negative vacuum is not intrinsic to the framework.

And the constructive half is worth stating at the top, because it is the most useful thing this article says. The register's §8.5 records that the corpus needs $\Lambda \le 0$ and that observation gives $\Lambda > 0$ some $200\times$ larger in magnitude. If (5-8) is not imposed, $\rho$'s sign is free and a positive vacuum is permitted — so the single most serious observational conflict in the cosmology is inherited from the single principle in the corpus that is least supported. S50 → S51 → S40 is not a chain of derivations; it begins with a choice, and the choice is the weak link.

Conventions. $\tau$ is the corpus's cosmic age parameter; $R(\tau)$ the spatial radius of the Réel, $R(\tau) = 2G(\tau)M(\tau)/c^{2}$ by the Schwarzschild identification (5-58); $R_{a} = 1{,}6\times10^{28}$ cm the present radius, $R_{i} = 6{,}006\times10^{24}$ cm the bounce radius. Three densities must not be confused, and they share letters: $\rho = \alpha/R^{3}$ is the proper mass density of the "vacuum" (dimension $M/L^{3}$), the quantity that enters the geometric tensor $T_{\alpha\beta} = \xi^{2}\rho^{2}u_{\alpha}u_{\beta}$; $D_{\rho} = W_{\rho}/\Omega_{0}$ is the energy density of the gravitational potential associated with the matter (dimension energy/volume), and $D_{\rho}/c^{2} = \sqrt{\rho^{2} + P_{\rho}^{2}/\Omega_{0}^{2}c^{2}}$ by (5-3)–(5-4); and $D_{M} = D_{N} + D_{\nu}$ is the matter energy density, nucleons plus neutrinos (5-6). $D_{E} = a\theta^{4} = \beta c^{2}/R^{4}$ is the radiation term, with $a = 7{,}62\times10^{-15}$ cgs the Stefan constant. $\alpha$, $\beta$ are the cosmological coefficients of (5-68): $\alpha = -1{,}30\times10^{53}$ g, $\beta = +3{,}712\times10^{79}$ g cm.

1. What the Claim Is, in the Corpus's Own Two Forms

The corpus writes the same statement twice, and the pair is instructive.

$$(5\text{-}8)\quad D_{\rho} + D_{E} + D_{M} \equiv 0 \qquad (\forall\tau),$$ $$(5\text{-}46)\quad \begin{cases} \ \dfrac{\alpha}{R^{3}} + \dfrac{\beta}{R^{4}} + \dfrac{D_{0N}}{c^{2}} \equiv 0 \\[1em] \ \dfrac{D_{\rho}}{c^{2}} + \dfrac{\beta}{R^{4}} + \dfrac{D_{M}}{c^{2}} \equiv 0 \end{cases} \qquad (\forall\tau)$$

The two lines of (5-46) say the same thing at two levels of description. The first is written in terms of $\rho = \alpha/R^{3}$, the vacuum's proper density, and $D_{0N}$, the rest-energy density of the nucleons; the second, in terms of the gravitational-potential energy density $D_{\rho}$ and the relativistic matter density $D_{M}$. That the first reduces to the second is not obvious, and it is worth verifying, because it shows the corpus's three densities are one object:

  • the vacuum density and the potential energy are the same thing, linked by the impulse. From (5-3) and (5-4), $W_{\rho}^{2}/c^{2} - P_{\rho}^{2} = \Omega_{0}^{2}c^{2}\rho^{2}$ with $W_{\rho} = D_{\rho}\Omega_{0}$ gives

$$\frac{D_{\rho}^{2}}{c^{2}} = c^{2}\rho^{2} + \frac{P_{\rho}^{2}}{\Omega_{0}^{2}} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{D_{\rho}}{c^{2}} = \sqrt{\rho^{2} + \frac{P_{\rho}^{2}}{\Omega_{0}^{2}c^{2}}} ;$$

  • the relativistic matter density contains the rest energy, by the corpus's own (5-7), $D_{N} = D_{0N}(1-v^{2}/c^{2})^{-1/2}$.

So (5-8) is not a coincidence between two unrelated books. It is the statement that the impulse-carrying gravitational-potential density, the radiation, and the relativistic matter, added algebraically, cancel — and the cancellation is what fixes the magnitude of $\rho$.

And the corpus confirms that this is load-bearing rather than decorative, in a sentence that S51 and this article both depend on:

"Compte tenu de ces critères, on est conduit, comme nous l'avons vu (5-68), à une densité $\rho$ de l'espace « vide » qui est négative ($\alpha$ négatif)." (RC 084)

So (5-8) is the origin of the negative vacuum density — which is the origin of $\Lambda \le 0$, which is the origin of the bounce. That chain is the subject of §5.

2. The Accounting Is the Standard One, and It Checks Numerically

This section gives the corpus credit that the register's [MIX] note does not quite give it.

In general relativity the total energy of a closed (K = +1) universe vanishes, and it vanishes because the gravitational potential energy is negative and cancels the matter energy. The Hamiltonian constraint of the closed cosmology says precisely this; the ADM mass of a closed universe is zero for the same reason. Tryon's 1973 proposal that the universe is a vacuum fluctuation took exactly this fact and made it the mechanism, and the subsequent literature on "a universe from nothing" is built on it.

And the corpus's negative term is exactly that term. $D_{\rho}$ is defined as "la densité d'énergie associée au potentiel gravitationnel de toutes les particules de matière présentes à chaque instant dans l'espace du Réel" — the energy density associated with the gravitational potential of all the matter. That is the same physical quantity the GR statement uses, under a different name and computed through the corpus's holon. So the register's note that Charon's version "is built on his +/− proper energies and is not the GR statement" is half right: the formalism is his own and the algebraic-sign device is his own, but the physics of what cancels what is the standard physics, and a reader is owed that.

The identity also checks against the corpus's own published numbers, and this article verified it at the present epoch. Two independent expressions give the same quantity. From the mass formula (5-71), $M = 2\pi^{2}R^{3}(D_{M}/c^{2} + \beta/R^{4})$, with the corpus's published $M_{\text{today}} = 1{,}14\times10^{56}$ g and $R_{a} = 1{,}6\times10^{28}$ cm,

$$\frac{D_{M}}{c^{2}} + \frac{\beta}{R_{a}^{4}} = \frac{1{,}14\times10^{56}}{2\pi^{2}(1{,}6\times10^{28})^{3}} = \frac{1{,}14\times10^{56}}{8{,}0852\times10^{85}} = 1{,}410\times10^{-30}\ \mathrm{g\ cm^{-3}},$$

and (5-46)'s second line then requires $D_{\rho}/c^{2} = -1{,}410\times10^{-30}$. Compare with the vacuum term the corpus publishes for today, $\rho = \alpha/R_{a}^{3} = -1{,}30\times10^{53}/4{,}096\times10^{84} = -3{,}174\times10^{-32}$ g cm$^{-3}$. The two agree in sign and differ by the factor $44{,}4$ — which is exactly the impulse's contribution in $D_{\rho}/c^{2} = \sqrt{\rho^{2} + P_{\rho}^{2}/\Omega_{0}^{2}c^{2}}$. So the present epoch's impulse satisfies $P_{\rho}^{2}/\Omega_{0}^{2}c^{2} \simeq (44{,}4^{2}-1)\rho^{2}$, and the corpus's three published numbers — $\rho$ today, $M$ today, $R$ today — are mutually consistent with the identity. This is not a trivial check: it is the corpus's closure working, and it confirms that (5-46) is the relation that makes $M$ computable from $\rho$ and $R$ at all.

Credit stated, the article turns to what the identity is not.

3. Conservation Gives a Constant, Not Zero

The corpus's justification for (5-8) is an argument about sources, and it is worth quoting in full because the gap is visible in the corpus's own words.

"Précisément à cause de la nécessaire conservation de l'impulsion-énergie pour l'Univers considéré dans son ensemble, on ne peut éviter d'affirmer que l'Univers doit à chaque instant de son âge $\tau$ contenir une impulsion-énergie globalement nulle... En effet, s'il n'en était pas ainsi, comment pourrions-nous justifier l'impulsion-énergie existante à aucun moment de l'histoire de l'Univers, puisque l'Univers dans son ensemble, par définition, ne possède aucune autre source d'énergie que lui-même, et aucune autre région vers laquelle « évacuer » éventuellement de l'énergie?" (RC 074)

The premise is unobjectionable; the conclusion does not follow from it. "The universe has no external source and nowhere to evacuate energy to" is a statement that energy is conserved — and conservation gives

$$\frac{dE_{\mathrm{total}}}{d\tau} = 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad E_{\mathrm{total}} = \mathrm{const},$$

not $E_{\mathrm{total}} = 0$. Every value of the constant is consistent with the premise. The corpus's argument as written is a non sequitur, and it is a non sequitur of a specific and interesting kind: it is valid for the rate and invalid for the value. A universe with $E_{\mathrm{total}} = 10^{60}$ erg and no external source is perfectly conservative.

The corpus knows a second premise is needed, and it supplies it one paragraph earlier, in the exposition of the same section: the identity is required by "l'idée que l'Univers ne peut être sorti du « néant »" — the idea that the universe cannot have come out of nothing — "ce qui rend nécessaire cette autre idée que l'Univers doit donc nécessairement contenir une énergie totale algébriquement nulle à chaque instant (5-8)" (RC 084). So the actual argument is: the universe did not come from nothing, therefore its total energy is zero. And that premise does do the work the conservation premise could not: it pins the constant to zero.

So (5-8) rests on the claim that the universe was not created. Everything now turns on whether that claim is available to the corpus — and the corpus's own cosmology says that it is not, in either of the two ways it can be read.

4. The Corpus's Own Cosmology Removes the Premise — Both Ways

This is the article's central finding, and it is a dilemma generated internally: the premise that establishes (5-8) is, on either reading of the corpus's own $\tau = 0$, either false or empty.

The corpus's universe begins its expansion at a finite radius with a finite density and temperature, and it is cyclic with half-period $T/2 = 2371$ Gyr — both established in S51 from RC 083 §1 and the corpus's own numbers. That leaves exactly two readings of $\tau = 0$, and the corpus uses both without choosing:

reading of $\tau = 0$ what follows for the E = 0 argument
an absolute beginning — the universe's ages begin there (RC 083's "à l'origine des âges $\tau = 0$") the premise is false by the corpus's own account. If ages begin at $\tau = 0$, the universe exists from $\tau = 0$ and not before — which is precisely a universe that came into being. The corpus's own non-singular bounce is a creation event with a finite, specified energy content, and the "not from nothing" premise is exactly what it violates.
a phase of a cycle — $\tau = 0$ is where each cycle's ages begin (RC 083's title, "un Univers cyclique") the premise is empty. A past-eternal cycle never came from nothing, so the reason for the constant to be zero disappears with the reason for it not to be. Conservation returns, and with it $E = \mathrm{const}$ for an arbitrary constant.

Both horns are bad, and the corpus does not see the fork. The first horn also has a sharper form worth stating: the corpus's stated motive for allowing negative energies at all is that it needed the algebraic sign for (5-8) — "si la Relativité complexe n'avait pas directement... été conduite à la notion d'une énergie algébrique, ... il nous aurait bien fallu l'inventer" (RC 074). So the corpus's entire licence to use negative proper densities — the device that makes the cosmology work, and that observation excludes at §2.10 — is granted in order to satisfy (5-8). An argument from convenience, resting on a premise its own model denies.

And the corpus's own emphasis is the tell. It calls the argument "si fort" — so strong — and says the notion of algebraic energy would have had to be invented to accommodate it. A principle that is felt to be that compelling is exactly the kind of principle that is not being checked, and the check is one line: conservation fixes a rate, and the corpus's principle needs a value.

What this does and does not show. It does not show that $E_{\mathrm{total}} \neq 0$ — the corpus may be right, and the statement is respectable (§2). What it shows is that the corpus's reasons for asserting it do not survive contact with the corpus's own cosmology, so the identity is, in the corpus as written, a stipulation — a postulate adopted for the sake of the model. And §5 shows how much rides on a postulate.

5. What Rides on It: $\rho$'s Sign, $\Lambda$'s Sign, and the Bounce

The chain is short and the corpus states every link of it.

$$(5\text{-}8)\ \Longrightarrow\ \rho < 0\ \Longrightarrow\ \Lambda \le 0\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{the bounce (S51).}$$

  • The first arrow is the corpus's own statement quoted in §1: the zero-energy criterion leads to a "densité $\rho$ de l'espace « vide » qui est négative". And it must: with $D_{E}$ and $D_{M}$ positive and their sum forced to zero, $D_{\rho}$ must be negative, and $D_{\rho}/c^{2} = \sqrt{\rho^{2} + P_{\rho}^{2}/\Omega_{0}^{2}c^{2}}$ forces $D_{\rho} \gtrsim c^{2}|\rho|$ — so the vacuum's proper density $\rho = \alpha/R^{3}$ carries $\alpha < 0$. The sign of the whole cosmology is decided here.
  • The second arrow is RC (3-69-2), quoted and audited in S51: the existence conditions for the Système E require $\Lambda \le 0$. And it is independently forced by the closed geometry: $K = +1$ with a finite maximum radius gives $\Lambda = -3/R_{\max}^{2} < 0$ necessarily, so the arrow does not depend on (3-69-2)'s scope. S52 derives both the argument and the value, $\Lambda = -1{,}53\times10^{-60}$ cm$^{-2}$.
  • The third arrow is S51's finding. The bounce is a turnaround of the corpus's own modified equations, and its ingredient is precisely the negative vacuum.

And observation has now measured the second arrow's endpoint. The accelerated expansion of 1998 is described by a positive vacuum energy density, of order $6\times10^{-30}$ g cm$^{-3}$ equivalent — about $200\times$ the corpus's magnitude and the opposite sign (register §8.5). So the corpus's most serious observational conflict is not an isolated mistake in the cosmology; it is the terminal link of a chain that begins at (5-8).

Which yields the constructive result. If (5-8) is not imposed, $\rho$'s sign is not forced. Nothing else in the framework requires it: the field equations take $\Lambda$ as a free real constant (S7), the geometric source is $\xi^{2}\rho^{2}u_{\alpha}u_{\beta}$ and is indifferent to the sign of $\rho$ for fixed $\rho^{2}$'s magnitude, and the Friedmann-type cosmological equations admit $\Lambda > 0$. So the corpus could have a positive vacuum density and a $\Lambda$ of either sign, and the cost would be the bounce and the negative-energy licence — not the framework.

That is the thing worth saying about §8.5. The register's note observes that Charon needs $\Lambda \le 0$ and that the required sign is "doubly wrong today". This article adds the provenance: the need is inherited from a stipulation, not generated by the structure. The corpus's worst single conflict with data is one postulate deep, and the postulate is the one whose justification §4 above removes. A repaired Charon cosmology with $\Lambda > 0$ is not obviously available — the bounce is load-bearing for S40's $G$ history — but the repair would not require abandoning the field equations or the source tensor.

6. A Hand-off: "Three Measured Inputs" Is a Statement About a Presentation

One consequence of the above belongs to the register's §8.9, and it is stated here only to be handed on, not developed.

§8.9 records the corpus's argument that the cosmological model cannot be solved without three measured inputs — $H_{a}$, $\theta_{a}$, $G_{a}$ — and that this underdetermination implies a world external to the Connaissant. The argument is about a count of external numbers, and (5-8) is one of the relations that closes the system. If identities may be imposed freely — and §3–§4 show that (5-8) is imposed, not derived — then the count of "measured inputs" is partly a property of the choice of presentation: every imposed identity is one fewer external number needed. This does not refute §8.9: the three inputs really are measurements, and no number in the list is obtainable from the others by any argument the corpus gives. But it means the argument's strength depends on a distinction §8.9 does not draw — between relations derived from the formalism and relations imposed as criteria — and that distinction is what this article has established for the most consequential criterion in the corpus. Hand-off to whoever writes §8.9.

Verdict

Respectable physics, standard accounting, verified closure, and still a stipulation — and everything early in the cosmology descends from it.

The case for the corpus, stated fully because it deserves it.

  1. The claim is respectable in kind. A closed universe has zero total energy in general relativity — the Hamiltonian constraint, equivalently the vanishing ADM mass — and Tryon (1973) built the "universe from nothing" literature on exactly that fact. [MIX] is the right tag.
  2. The corpus's accounting is the standard one. The negative term $D_{\rho}$ is the gravitational potential energy of the matter, specified as such in RC 073. The algebraic-sign formalism is the corpus's own; the physics of what cancels what is not.
  3. The identity checks numerically against the corpus's own published values. At the present epoch, $M_{\text{today}} = 1{,}14\times10^{56}$ g with $R_{a} = 1{,}6\times10^{28}$ cm requires $D_{\rho}/c^{2} = -1{,}410\times10^{-30}$ g cm$^{-3}$, which is the corpus's own $\rho_{\text{today}} = -3{,}174\times10^{-32}$ g cm$^{-3}$ multiplied by $44{,}4$ — and $44{,}4$ is exactly the impulse's contribution. The closure works.

And the finding.

(1) It is a stipulation. The corpus's argument — no external source, nowhere to evacuate energy — establishes conservation, which fixes a rate, not a value: $E_{\mathrm{total}} = \mathrm{const}$, not $E_{\mathrm{total}} = 0$. The second premise the corpus needs is that the universe did not come from nothing, and it states that premise explicitly (RC 084).

(2) Its own cosmology removes that premise, on either reading of $\tau = 0$. If $\tau = 0$ is an absolute beginning, the premise is contradicted by the corpus's own non-singular bounce's being a creation event; if $\tau = 0$ is a phase of the 2371-Gyr cycle, the premise is vacuous and conservation again permits any constant. The corpus uses both readings and chooses neither.

(3) It carries the cosmology's worst observational conflict. $(5\text{-}8) \Rightarrow \rho < 0 \Rightarrow \Lambda \le 0 \Rightarrow$ the bounce, and observation gives $\Lambda > 0$ some $200\times$ larger in magnitude (§8.5). The conflict is one postulate deep, and the postulate is the one whose justification fails.

(4) And the repair is not on the geometric side. S7 establishes that the left-hand side is closed by theorem. But this defect is not on the geometric side either — it is a criterion the corpus chose to impose, on a right-hand side whose source's sign is otherwise free. So the corpus's most serious conflict with data is, structurally, its most removable: it would go with the stipulation, and with the bounce, and not with the field equations.

What this article therefore claims, and what it does not. It does not claim the universe's total energy is nonzero, and it does not claim to have derived anything about it. It claims that (5-8) is a postulate of the corpus, that its stated justification does not survive the corpus's own cosmology, and that the negative vacuum — the sign observation forbids — is inherited from that postulate rather than generated by the framework. For a reader deciding what to repair in this corpus, that is the most useful single fact in the cosmology: the sign conflict is one postulate deep.

Summary of Notation

  • $D_{\rho}$, $D_{E}$, $D_{M}$ — the three energy densities of (5-8). $D_{\rho} = W_{\rho}/\Omega_{0}$ is the gravitational-potential energy density associated with all the matter's holonic parts (RC 073) — the negative term, and the standard one; $D_{E} = a\theta^{4}$ the black-body radiation, with $a = 7{,}62\times10^{-15}$ cgs; $D_{M} = D_{N} + D_{\nu}$ the relativistic matter density, nucleons plus neutrinos (5-6).
  • $D_{\rho}$ versus $\rho$ — the article's most important trap. $\rho = \alpha/R^{3}$ is the vacuum's proper mass density ($M/L^{3}$) and enters the geometric tensor; $D_{\rho}/c^{2}$ is its energy-density presentation and equals $\sqrt{\rho^{2} + P_{\rho}^{2}/\Omega_{0}^{2}c^{2}}$ by (5-3)–(5-4). They share a letter and they are different objects, and the factor $44{,}4$ between them today is the impulse.
  • $W_{\rho}$, $P_{\rho}$, $\Omega_{0}$ — the energy $W_{\rho}$ whose density is $D_{\rho}$, the impulse $P_{\rho}$ (with $P_{\rho j}^{2} = P_{\rho}^{2}/3$, $j = 1,2,3$), and the elementary volume $\Omega_{0}$ of the corpus's cell.
  • $M$, $M_{\text{today}}$ — the corpus's mass of the universe, $M = 2\pi^{2}R^{3}(D_{M}/c^{2} + \beta/R^{4})$ (5-59)/(5-71): the ponderable matter plus the radiation, per RC 085 §5's discussion of why the "vacuum" is treated apart. $M_{\text{today}} = 1{,}14\times10^{56}$ g; $M_{\min} = 2\times10^{54}$ g at $\tau_{0} = 345$ Myr; $M(\tau{=}0) = 1{,}22\times10^{56}$ g; $M(T/2) = 9{,}5\times10^{57}$ g. It equals $2\pi^{2}R^{3}\sqrt{\rho^{2}+P_{\rho}^{2}/\Omega_{0}^{2}c^{2}}$ once (5-46) is imposed, which is how it ties to $\rho$.
  • $\alpha$, $\beta$ — the cosmological coefficients, $\alpha = -1{,}30\times10^{53}$ g and $\beta = +3{,}712\times10^{79}$ g cm, not the Système E's pair of the same name nor the fine-structure constant; $\alpha < 0$ is the negative vacuum density's coefficient, and §5's chain is about its sign.
  • $\rho_{*} = 8\pi G/(c^{2}\xi^{2}) = 4{,}0\times10^{-21}$ g cm$^{-3}$ — named in S7 and S39, not here, and not $\rho$: the density at which the corpus's quadratic source could match a dust energy density. Relevant only as the reason a positive $\rho$ is not by itself a repair.
  • $(5\text{-}8)$, $(5\text{-}46)$, $(5\text{-}60)$ — the three written forms of the zero-total-energy statement: (5-8) at RC 074 ch. V §II; (5-60) and the second line of (5-46) as its radiational and the two-bookkeeping forms; (5-46)'s first line the rest-energy form. All three are identities in $\tau$.

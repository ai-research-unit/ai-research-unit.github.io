The Trace Is a Matrix Notion

Introduction

A trace is the sum of the diagonal entries of a matrix. That is the whole definition. An element of the biquaternion algebra has no diagonal, because it is not an array. Every sentence of the corpus that traces an element therefore rests on a chosen matrix, and the matrix is usually not written in the same line.

Before the migration the corpus wrote a trace symbol $1{,}760$ times in $609$ physics articles, and $7{,}441$ times across the $3{,}123$ articles of the repository, counting the four spellings $\operatorname{tr}$, $\operatorname{Tr}$, $\mathrm{tr}$ and $\mathrm{Tr}$. Of the $1{,}454$ uppercase sites in the physics tree, $566$ stood in front of an element of $\mathbb{B}$ and $888$ in front of a matrix or another named carrier. The first group was what the migration removed; the second is what stays. After the migration the physics tree carries $855$ sites in $166$ articles, of which $557$ are uppercase, and every one of them has a matrix, an operator, a representation or a field extension in the argument. The mathematics tree carries $5{,}295$ sites in $622$ of its $2{,}465$ articles, almost all of them in the matrix-algebra series, where the argument is an array by construction. A reader who cannot tell the two groups apart gets numbers wrong by a factor of two, and the factor of two is the difference between a normalised state and a state of scalar part one.

This article is the short reading of that fact. It says what a matrix trace is, why an element needs a carrier, which carrier this corpus uses, why the factor $2$ is not decoration, and how the four forms and the four norms of the algebra sit on top of the same trace. The long inventory is The Trace: The Carrier and the Factor, and the file-by-file record is Trace Migration: The Purge of the Informational Trace.

What a Matrix Trace Is

The definition is a sum. For $X\in M_n(\mathbb{C})$,

$$ \operatorname{tr}X=\sum_{i=1}^{n}X_{ii}. $$

The sum is basis independent, and the reason is the cyclic identity. For any two matrices, $\operatorname{tr}(XY)=\operatorname{tr}(YX)$, because both sums run over the same set of products $X_{ij}Y_{ji}$. A change of basis replaces $X$ by $SXS^{-1}$, and the cyclic identity moves $S^{-1}S$ to the identity. So the number does not depend on the basis, even though the diagonal does.

The trace and the determinant are the two coefficients of the characteristic polynomial. At size $n$ the trace is the sum of the eigenvalues and the determinant is their product. That is why the pair appears together in every spectral statement of the corpus: the trace reads the sum, the norm reads the product, and a similarity fixes both.

The trace is one number out of $n^2$, and what it forgets is a subspace. The matrices of trace zero form $\mathfrak{sl}_n$, of dimension $n^2-1$. At $n=2$ that is complex dimension three. So traceless names a subspace of codimension one. It never means zero, and it never means has no scalar part unless the scalar part is the trace in the carrier in use.

Why an Element Needs a Carrier

An abstract element has no entries. The trace is defined on an array of numbers. An element of $\mathbb{B}$ is a linear combination of four units with complex coefficients, and there is no diagonal to sum until a matrix is produced.

Three classical repairs exist, and each one chooses a different matrix. The reduced trace reads the element in a splitting representation, $\operatorname{Trd}(a)=\operatorname{tr}(a\otimes 1)$, and for $\mathbb{B}$ the splitting field is $\mathbb{C}$ itself. The regular trace reads left multiplication, $\operatorname{tr}L(a)$, where the carrier is the algebra as a vector space. The representation trace reads a module, $\operatorname{tr}\rho(a)$. Each gives a number, and the three numbers differ by factors, because the three matrices have different sizes.

The factor is the size of the matrix. For a central simple algebra of degree $n$ the reduced trace has factor $n$ and the regular trace has factor $n^2$. For a representation the factor is $\dim V$. So the phrase the trace of an element is incomplete until the carrier is named and the factor written.

The Carrier This Corpus Uses

One isomorphism is fixed once and for all. $\mathsf{M}_2$ sends $e_t\mapsto I_2$ and $\vec e_\eta\mapsto -i\sigma_\eta$. It is complex-linear and it is an isomorphism of algebras, so it carries products to products, the star to the adjoint, and the quaternionic norm to the determinant.

The trace of the image is twice the scalar part,

$$ \operatorname{tr}\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q)=2Q_t=2\,\mathrm{Sc}\,\tilde Q . $$

The factor $2$ is the degree of the algebra and the size of the matrix. It is not a convention and it cannot be divided out without changing the symbol. That is why the corpus writes $2\,\mathrm{Sc}$ and not $\operatorname{Tr}$ after the migration: the number stays, and the matrix disappears from the line where no matrix is present.

The scalar part exists without a matrix, and the trace does not. $\mathrm{Sc}$ is the coefficient of $e_t$, an operation defined on the algebra alone. A trace needs an array. This asymmetry is the whole argument of the internal articles, and it is the reason the physics corpus was rewritten and the matrix articles were not.

The mathematics corpus splits along the same line. The twenty Part VI files that the pass edited now carry $67$ symbols. Every one sits on a matrix, an operator or a named carrier: $\operatorname{tr}\mathsf M_2$ on the image, $\operatorname{tr}L_{\breve Q}$ on the regular representation, $\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{ad}_X\operatorname{ad}_Y)$ on the adjoint action, $\operatorname{tr}(XY)$ on the matrix space. The $5\,220$ symbols that remain in the mathematics corpus sit in the matrix and operator articles, where the argument is a matrix of $M_n$, an adjoint operator or a curvature form.

The Factor Is Not Decoration

Deleting a trace without its factor halves every probability statement. The normalisation of a state is $2\,\mathrm{Sc}\,\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}=1$, not $\mathrm{Sc}\,\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}=1$. The scalar part of the state is $\tfrac12$. A purity is $2\,\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}^{2})$, an entropy is $-2\,\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}\log\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi})$, and a variance doubles its factor rather than cancelling it, because the square of a trace is not the trace of a square.

The factor cancels in a ratio and stays in a normalisation. A Born weight is a ratio of two traces, so the factor of $2$ cancels and the number is a probability. The unit-weight condition is not a ratio, so the factor stays and the condition becomes $2\,\mathrm{Sc}=1$. These are the two opposite mistakes that the substitution map is built to prevent.

The two readings are a sum and a product. Twice the scalar part is the sum of the two eigenvalues, $2Q_t=\lambda_++\lambda_-$, and the quaternionic norm is their product, $\langle\tilde Q\rangle_\natural=\lambda_+\lambda_-$. A similarity, $\tilde Q\mapsto\tilde U\tilde Q\tilde U^{-1}$, fixes both numbers because it fixes the spectrum. A Lorentz transformation is a congruence, $\tilde Q\mapsto\tilde U\tilde Q\tilde U^{*}$, with $\langle\tilde U\rangle_\natural=1$: it fixes the quaternionic norm, which is the mass squared, and it moves the scalar part, which is the energy.

The Four Forms Are Half-Traces

A form of the algebra is a product read on its scalar part, with one conjugation per slot. There are four ways to place the conjugations, so there are four forms. Each is one half of the trace of the same product in the $2\times2$ carrier,

$$ P=\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}\bigl(\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2)\bigr),\quad N=\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}\bigl(\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q_1^{\natural}\tilde Q_2)\bigr),\quad H=\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}\bigl(\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2^{*})\bigr),\quad \mathrm{Kr}=\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}\bigl(\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q_1^{\natural}\tilde Q_2^{*})\bigr). $$

The four forms differ only in where the conjugation sits. The plain form conjugates neither slot, the quaternionic form conjugates the first, the Hermitian form the second, and the Krein form both. The four identities hold because the trace of the image is twice the scalar part in every case; on random pairs the residual is $10^{-16}$.

form letter expression conjugation what it measures
plain $P$ $\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2)$ none the pairing of the algebra, complex bilinear
quaternionic $N$ $\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1^{\natural}\tilde Q_2)$ first slot the interval, and its zero set is the light cone
Hermitian $H$ $\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2^{*})$ second slot the weight, positive definite on the algebra
Krein $\mathrm{Kr}$ $\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1^{\natural}\tilde Q_2^{*})$ both slots the indefinite volume form

Read at two equal arguments, the four forms give the four norms. They are $\langle\tilde Q\rangle$, $\langle\tilde Q\rangle_{\natural}$, $\langle\tilde Q\rangle_{*}$ and $\langle\tilde Q\rangle_{\natural*}$. The quaternionic one is the determinant of the image and it multiplies. The Hermitian one is positive definite and it does not multiply. The plain one is complex in general and vanishes on a cone. The Krein one is indefinite.

The count of four comes from the conjugations, not from the trace. A trace is one functional. The four forms are four bilinear maps, and the trace is only the carrier in which each of them becomes a matrix expression with the factor $\tfrac12$. This is the point that a reader who knows only matrices tends to miss: the richness is in the placement of the conjugations, and the trace adds nothing to it.

The trace gives none of the four norms by itself. $\operatorname{tr}\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q)=2Q_t$ is a complex number that forgets the vector part. The Hermitian norm is the only positive one of the four, and only its square root is a topological norm. A trace cannot supply positivity, because a trace is signed and, on the real matrices, indefinite.

What the Trace Cannot Do

It is not positive. At $n=2$ the matrix unit $E_{12}$ is nonzero and $\operatorname{tr}(E_{12}^{2})=0$. The form $\operatorname{tr}(X^2)$ has signature $(3,1)$ on the real $2\times2$ matrices, so it is a metric form and not a probability.

It is not multiplicative. For $X=I_2$, the trace of the square is $2$ and the square of the trace is $4$. Multiplicativity belongs to the determinant, and that is exactly the split the corpus turns on: a form that multiplies gives a group of isometries, and a form that does not gives a probability.

It does not separate points. The traceless part is a nonzero subspace, so two different elements share a trace. An electron and a proton at the same energy share $2iE/c$ and differ in the norm. That is why the norm, and not the trace, names the particle.

The trace of a commutator vanishes. Since $\operatorname{tr}([A,B])=0$ and $\operatorname{tr}\mathsf{M}_2(e_t)=2$, no pair of elements satisfies $[a,b]=e_t$. This is the algebraic reason the finite-dimensional algebra carries no canonical pair, and it is a statement about the trace doing its job, not a defect of the framework.

Where the Symbol Stays

A trace stays wherever the matrix is written next to it. Five groups of sites survive the migration, and each has a matrix in the argument.

The image of an element. $\operatorname{tr}\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q)=2Q_t$ is the declaration of the carrier. It is the one place where the factor $2$ is proved rather than used, and it belongs in the article that fixes the representation.

A tensor product. On $\mathbb{B}\otimes\mathbb{B}$ the carrier is the $4\times4$ image, so the factor is $4$ and a factor trace is $2\,\mathrm{Sc}_B$. The identity $\operatorname{tr}(x\otimes y)=\operatorname{tr}(x)\operatorname{tr}(y)$ is what makes the factors multiply, and it is a matrix identity.

A gauge factor. $\operatorname{Tr}_R(T^aT^b)=T(R)\delta^{ab}$ is a trace in a representation, its value is the Dynkin index, and it sets the normalisation of $\operatorname{Tr}(F\wedge F)$. Lattice gauge theory is written entirely in this trace, and the lattice article keeps all of its $30$ sites for that reason, each one now written on the explicit $2\times2$ image $\mathsf{M}_2(\cdot)$.

A regular representation. In the reduced biquaternion articles the trace of an element is the trace of its left multiplication operator, $\operatorname{tr}L_{\breve Q}=Q_{+}+Q_{-}=2Q_{\blacktriangle}$. The symbol is correct there and the carrier was missing, so the pass kept the trace and named $L$.

An operator or a transfer matrix. $Z=\operatorname{Tr}\exp(-\beta H)$ on a Hilbert space whose $H$ is an operator, and $Z=\operatorname{Tr}T^{N_t}$ on a lattice transfer matrix, are matrix statements of the usual kind; they carry their matrix in the argument. The biquaternion case is not of this kind, because there $H$ is an element and the reading is $2\,\mathrm{Sc}\exp(-\beta H)$.

How to Read a Migrated Article

A migrated article writes the factor where the older text wrote the symbol. The substitution is fixed.

argument of the old symbol what the text now says factor why
one element $\tilde X$ $2\,\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde X)$ $2$ the $2\times2$ image
two elements $XY$ $2\,\mathrm{Sc}(XY)$ $2$ the same image
$x\otimes y$ $4\,\mathrm{Sc}(x\otimes y)$, or $4\,\mathrm{Sc}(x)\,\mathrm{Sc}(y)$ $4$ one factor of $2$ per factor
a reduction in one factor $2\,\mathrm{Sc}_B(\cdots)$ $2$ the dimension of the discarded factor
a matrix, a density operator, a generator, a field strength unchanged $1$ the argument is already an array

A line without a factor is a defect. If a migrated line states that a state has scalar part one, the factor was dropped and the statement is wrong by a factor of two. The mechanical check is the string \operatorname{Tr}\tilde, which must not occur in a migrated file: a trace symbol followed directly by an element is a site that was missed.

The Five Rules

Name the carrier. A trace is the trace of a matrix, an operator, a representation or a field extension. Write which one.

Write the factor. $1$ for a matrix, $2$ for the $2\times2$ image, $4$ for a $4\times4$ carrier, $2^n$ for $\mathbb{B}^{\otimes n}$, $\dim V$ for a representation, $[L:k]$ for a field extension.

One symbol, one carrier, per article. If two carriers are needed, subscript them, as $\operatorname{tr}\mathsf{M}_2$ and $\operatorname{Tr}_R$ already do.

The trace is not a norm. It is complex in general, it is indefinite on the real matrices, and it forgets the traceless part. Only the Hermitian algebraic norm is positive.

Traceless is not zero. It is the kernel of a named trace, and it has codimension one.

Summary

The trace is a sum of diagonal entries, and it is defined on matrices alone. An element of $\mathbb{B}$ is not a matrix, so a trace of an element always names a carrier, and this corpus names the $2\times2$ image. In that carrier the trace is twice the scalar part, and the factor $2$ is the degree of the algebra, not a convention. The migrated articles write $2\,\mathrm{Sc}$ and keep the factor visible. The four forms are the four half-traces of the four products, the four norms are their diagonals, and the count of four comes from the placement of the conjugations and not from the trace. The trace itself is neither positive, nor multiplicative, nor separating, and the places where the symbol remains are the places where a matrix stands in the argument.

Physics: The Capitals Are a Different Object

\Phi and \Psi are not the retired letter and they are not the spinor column. Algebra and Element Conventions defines both, line 432 and line 433 of its table:

glyph meaning definition
$\tilde\Phi$ the algebra-valued scalar field $\tilde\Phi=\chi e_t$ in the central case, a general element of $\mathbb{B}$ otherwise
$\tilde\Psi$ the Dirac field $\tilde\Psi_L+\tilde\Psi_R$, an algebra-valued spinor field

So a field is written twice in this corpus: once as an element of the algebra, $\tilde\Phi$, and once as its central coefficient, $\chi$. The majuscule is the element, the minuscule is the coefficient. That is why \Phi stands 2272 times in 127 articles after the lowercase letter is gone: the central case is $\tilde\Phi=\chi e_t$, and the pair is written together. \Psi stands 4740 times in 143 articles and 2635 of those carry the tilde, the Dirac field of the two chiral halves.

Status

Physics: lowercase letters migrated, capitals untouched, no build and no deploy, because PLAN.md forbids both. Maths: 10254 occurrences of \phi and \varphi in 548 articles, and 2714 of \psi, all in place and none migrated. The two corpora use the same letters for different objects: in physics a field is $\chi$ with the element $\tilde\Phi$ above it, in maths \varphi is a symbol of a function, a formula of logic, or a rapidity, and no single reading covers it.

The Phi and Psi Census: The Mathematics, the Physics and the Internal Corpora

What Is Counted

Every occurrence of the five control sequences — the two lowercase forms, var-phi, and the two capitals — followed by anything that is not a letter. A subscript or a superscript is counted with its glyph, and a longer sequence is counted once under its own row. Raw codepoints are counted separately: U+03C6, U+03D5, U+03C8, U+03A8 and U+03A6 all stand at zero in every corpus.

The sequences are named in this report by their letter alone — phi, var-phi, Phi, psi, Psi — because the report is itself an article of the internal corpus, where the sequences are retired and cannot be printed.

Two files needed more than a substitution, and one rule runs the other way. The inventory of the Hermitian element carries the two halves as live subscripts, and they read $\tilde R_\psi$ and $\tilde R_\varphi$: the two letters are kept, not replaced, because freeing them was the point of the pass. They are the subscripts that show an element of $\mathbb{M}_+$ and an element of $\mathbb{M}_-$, and no other object may take them. The count of the five sequences in the columns below therefore stands at zero for every role except that one. This report is the second: it counts the sequences, so it names them instead of printing them.

Physics: The Retirement Now Covers the Capitals

An earlier version of this entry recorded a partial retirement — the scalar field to $\chi$, the phase to $\theta^{\mathrm{pha}}$, and the rapidity kept on psi. That table is superseded. The physics corpus now carries none of the five forms in any column, and the roles moved as follows:

what the letter held written as
the real scalar field $\xi$, or $\xi^{\mathrm{scl}}$ for a declared scalar
the complex scalar field $Ξ$
the algebra-valued field $\tilde{Ξ}$
the spinor, the state and the Dirac field $\tilde{Ξ}^{\mathrm{spn}}$, with the chirality subscript or the dagger
the generic element of the algebra $\tilde{Ξ}$, or \tilde{R} where the element is a position
the rapidity and the boost angle $\theta^{\mathrm{rpd}}$
the phase inside an exponential $\theta^{\mathrm{pha}}$
the azimuth, coordinate or differential $\theta^{\mathrm{azm}}$
the phi meson, a name the words "phi meson"

No $\chi$ reading remains in force, and psi is not the rapidity. The case rule that once told a capital from a lowercase on the same letter is gone with the letters: an element and a spinor are now told apart by the tilde and the superscript, not by case.

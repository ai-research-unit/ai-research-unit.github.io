Internal Summary of the Biquaternion Particle Physics

Introduction

What this file is. A self-contained map of the whole Biquaternion Particle Physics category: eighty-three articles in six parts, counted from the anchors of physics.md at the time of writing. It stands on its own. It is deliberately less detailed than a dedicated part summary: one paragraph, one or two formulas and a verdict per part. Two internal notes go deeper, and they are an optional extension, never a requirement: internal-summary-of-the-electroweak-interaction (twenty-eight articles) and internal-summary-of-the-strong-interaction (twenty-one).

What the category does. It develops four items: the abelian gauge principle on the central phase; the compact factor $\mathrm{SU}(2)\subset\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ with its full non-abelian machinery; the exact chiral structure on the element; and the classification of particles by spin and mass. It imports the Standard Model. The category's subject is the boundary between the two, and the boundary is a theorem.

The single structural fact.

$$ \boxed{\;\text{the gauge group \emph{is} the spin group}\;} \qquad \boxed{\;\dim_\mathbb{R}\bigl(\text{compact subalgebra of }\mathbb{B}\bigr)\le4,\qquad \text{every }\mathbb{B}\text{-module has even complex dimension}\;} $$

The non-trivial claim. The first says what a field may carry; the second says what group may fit. The first forbids choosing a representation independently of the spin, so the scalar cannot be a doublet and the fermion cannot be chiral. The second forbids an internal $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ of dimension eight inside a compact algebra of dimension four, so colour is absent. Three of the category's four obstruction lists reduce to the first theorem; the fourth is the second.

The one-sentence version. The category reaches the kinematics, the algebra, the self-couplings and the abelian scalar mechanism of the Standard Model; it imports the group, the quantum numbers, the couplings, the angle and every mass; and it records the difference rather than blurring it.

What is established. The compact algebra is $\mathrm{U}(2)$ of dimension four, and the ceiling is proved twice. The group-valued chiral field satisfies the Maurer–Cartan equation, and the biquaternion norm is the invariant metric on it. The gauge group on a material element is the spin group, with a table that reads the representation from the carrier. The abelian Higgs mechanism works, with $m_A^2=ϙ^2v^2/\hbar^2$ and $m_h^2=2\beta v^2$ from one potential. The abelian Pontryagin density is the imaginary part of the biquaternion norm, so the Maxwell instanton is a framework object. The chirality-odd bilinear vanishes on an element of a single value, which is why a condensate needs both letters. The Peccei–Quinn symmetry is the central phase, and the axion potential is the cosine it forces.

1. The Gauge Structure

What the part is. Seventeen articles of pure apparatus, and the category's most reusable block. The gauge principle on the central phase; the covariant derivative and the connection; the curvature and the Bianchi identity; the Hopf bundle as the framework's structure group; the index theorem and its zero-mode count; electric–magnetic duality and the modular group; the non-abelian extension on $\mathrm{SU}(2)$; the Yang–Mills equation; the path integral with the Faddeev–Popov procedure; the lattice version; topological charge and the Bogomolny bound; and six parents — ADHM, the semiclassical gas, the nonlinear sigma model, the effective potential, the Fujikawa measure, the Stueckelberg field — written with no sector attached.

$$ \tilde A_{\Phi,2}=\tilde A_{\Phi}-\tilde\nabla_\Phi\Gamma, \qquad [e_a,e_b]=2\,\varepsilon_{abc}\,e_c,\quad T_a=\tfrac12e_a,\quad [T_a,T_b]=\varepsilon_{abc}T_c,\quad 2\,\mathrm{Sc}(T_aT_b)=-\tfrac12\delta_{ab}. $$

The verdict. The abelian gauge principle is intrinsic: the centre is a phase group, so localising it costs nothing and the gauge field is a connection on a bundle the algebra already carries. The non-abelian one is available on the compact factor, with structure constants read from the quaternion commutator and the normalisation fixed rather than chosen. The part does not supply a non-abelian matter representation: every covariant derivative on an element is either abelian or acts on the adjoint. That omission is the machinery-side form of the chiral wall, and it is inherited by every part downstream. The filing rule matters: six of the seventeen are parents, so the sector article should be read after them.

2. The Electroweak Interaction

What the part is. Twenty-eight articles, cut by spin: generalities, then spin $0$, spin $\tfrac12$, spin $1$. Two ceilings, one embedding, one wall, and the computations that sit on top of the imported model.

$$ \boxed{\;\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}=\mathbb{R}(ie_t)\oplus\mathrm{SU}(2),\qquad \dim_\mathbb{R}\mathrm{U}(2)=4\;} \qquad L=\pi i m\,e_t+\pi n\vec U,\quad m\equiv n\pmod2 . $$

The three results. Representation is a function of spin. The table — spin $0$ trivial, spin $\tfrac12$ spinor with both letters alike, spin $1$ adjoint — matches the model in exactly one row, the vector one; that is why the vector-boson kinematics and self-couplings come out right and the matter sector does not. The letter law. A left multiplication applies one element to both parts, so independent charges need the right slot and an element of unequal values; a central multiplier gives one charge. Its consequence is the mass selection rule $\varkappa_L=\varkappa_R$, which the whole flavour sector runs on. The abelian mechanism. Central scalar, central connection, mass from the kinetic term, Goldstone eaten: $V=\beta(\xi^*\xi-v^2/2)^2$ gives both $m_h^2=2\beta v^2$ and $m_A^2=ϙ^2v^2/\hbar^2$. The non-abelian version is unavailable for the same reason the scalar row is trivial.

What is computed on top. The hypercharges are the solution of four anomaly conditions — a rank-three linear system on five unknowns, cut by the cubic to three rays, of which measurement selects one. The doublet sector gives $M_W=gv/2$, $M_Z=\sqrt{g^2+g_Y^2}\,v/2$ and $\rho=1$ at tree level. The precision sector has $8-2-3=3$ oblique parameters against some twenty measurements, and the fit predicted the Higgs mass. Flavour is one statement seen twice: masses are singular values $M_f=WDV^{\dagger}$ and mixing is the misalignment $V_{\mathrm{CKM}}=W_u^{\dagger}W_d$, thirteen numbers being a count and not a list. Neutrinos add $\mathcal{C}\tilde\Xi=i\bar{\tilde\Xi}$ with $\mathcal{C}^2=+1$, the Majorana condition as a real condition, and $m_\nu\simeq m_D^2/M_R$.

The verdict. The sector's kinematics, algebra, self-couplings and abelian scalar mechanism are the framework's; its group, quantum numbers, angle and masses are not. The shape of the vertex is algebraic; the strength and the asymmetry are inputs.

3. The Strong Interaction

What the part is. Twenty-one articles, and the category's negative part: here the question has a theorem for an answer. Everything the algebra's own $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ can carry is built; the rest of QCD is transcribed with the boundary marked on every page.

$$ \boxed{\;\dim_\mathbb{R}\mathrm{SU}(3)=8,\qquad \dim_\mathbb{R}\mathrm{U}(2)=4,\qquad Ϙ=\frac{1}{8\pi^2}\int\mathrm{Tr}(F\wedge F)\in\mathbb{Z},\qquad S\ge\frac{8\pi^2}{g^2}|Ϙ|\;} $$

The theorem and its two prices. $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ is excluded twice: the compact subalgebra has dimension four against eight, and every module has even complex dimension against the odd triplet — the second count no change of basis can touch. Two routes pass the wall and both are priced: the enlarged carrier $M_2(\mathbb{B})\cong M_4(\mathbb{C})$ with intrinsic group $\mathrm{U}(4)\supset\mathrm{SU}(3)$, where the algebra acts only on the spectator factor; and the octonion route, $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{O})=G_2$ of dimension fourteen, at the price of associativity.

What survives. BPST instantons inside the compact factor. The ADHM data as quaternionic, with the moduli dimension from the hyperkähler quotient. The chiral field $\tilde U=\exp(\vec\pi/f_\pi)$ satisfying Maurer–Cartan, the pion as the adjoint with one normalisation shared with the custodial triplet, and Gell-Mann–Oakes–Renner, $m_\pi^2f_\pi^2=-(m_u+m_d)\langle\bar qq\rangle$. The Skyrme winding, conserved identically, identified with baryon number. Peccei–Quinn as the central phase, with $V=\Lambda^4[1-\cos(\bar\theta+a/f_a)]$ and $m_a=\Lambda^2/f_a$.

What is transcribed. The $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ representation theory and Gell-Mann–Okubo, $m=m_0+\alpha+\beta Y+\gamma(I(I+1)-\tfrac14Y^2)$, whose equal-spacing check comes out $2256.9$ against $2270.1$ MeV. The QCD Lagrangian, its four pieces and four vertices, with $C_A=3$, $C_F=\tfrac43$, $T_R=\tfrac12$. The running $\alpha_s=2\pi/(b_0\ln(\mu/\Lambda))$ and the trace anomaly that gives the proton its mass. The parton densities, which are functions imported rather than numbers. The jet cross sections, whose individual terms diverge and whose sum does not.

The verdict. Colour is not missing by oversight; it is excluded by a theorem with a price list attached. That is a result, and the part's worth is that it states it once and then refuses to soften it.

4. The Standard Model as a Whole

What the part is. Eight articles that close the loop, opened by the whole-model audit that the three sector audits are cut from: the three gauge factors with their status, then the content with its quantum numbers, the four sectors in one Lagrangian, and the count of nineteen parameters — three couplings, one angle, one vacuum value, the Higgs quartic, nine masses, four mixing parameters.

$$ 3\ \text{couplings}+1\ \text{angle}+1\ \text{value}+1\ \text{quartic}+9\ \text{masses}+4\ \text{mixing}=19 . $$

The reading the part makes. The parameter count is the measure of the boundary: a number the framework cannot produce is a number it imports, and nineteen of them is the size of the import. That is the part's one original idea, and it is a good one, because it turns a vague "the framework is incomplete" into a number.

Three problems stated and not solved. Naturalness: the Higgs mass is quadratically sensitive, and no framework object suppresses the correction. Triviality and the Landau pole: the quartic and the hypercharge coupling both grow, so the theory is an effective one with a cut-off. Dark matter, baryogenesis and the sphaleron: three items the model does not explain, with the sphaleron's baryon-number violation the one place where the model's own non-perturbative structure is required. Grand unification fails on the same ceiling: the standard groups do not fit in a larger simple group the algebra can carry.

The verdict. The part is bookkeeping with one sharp idea — the parameter count as a measurement of how much is imported — and it is honest about being bookkeeping.

6. Informational Aspects

What the part is. Three articles: gauge redundancy as the information in the orbit; confinement as the loss of partonic information; the Higgs mechanism as an erasure.

The verdict. The reading translates known physics into information language. It adds no dynamics, and the part is explicit that it does not; what it adds is a way of saying why an unphysical sector is unphysical and why a broken symmetry is a loss rather than a change of substance.

Seven traps, collected here because this is the file most likely to be read alone.

  • Mixed writing of the anomaly conditions. The minus-sign form applied to a conjugate hypercharge gives $\tfrac23$ instead of $0$.
  • Promoting a prediction of the inherited model to a derivation. The oblique fit's $125$ GeV, the hypercharge solution, the doublet potential and the flavour counts are computations about the Standard Model's content, not the framework's.
  • Reading the $\mathrm{U}(4)$ embedding as a refutation of the ceiling. The theorem is about the group of $\mathbb{B}$, not of $M_n(\mathbb{B})$.
  • Assuming the biquaternion norm is gauge invariant. It is not, for a non-abelian representative: $17.1838\to17.3970$ on a random sample, while the matrix trace is invariant to machine precision.
  • Treating $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ multiplet bookkeeping as colour. The algebra's $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ sits inside $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ as the isospin subgroup; that inclusion is not a derivation of the colour group.
  • Propagating the Majorana reality condition. None of the sixteen unit-conjugated forms commutes with the equation, so the fixed space is not preserved by the flow.
  • Reading "three" in the algebra as colour. The three letters are an adjoint, and an adjoint is not a fundamental.

The five open items, in the corpus's own order. A colour representation for the quark; a chiral non-abelian matter representation; a hypercharge assignment; an internal gauge-fixing principle; the origin of the mixing angle. The first three are upstream: settling any one of them would not by itself produce the Standard Model.

Summary

$$ \mathbb{B}_{\Phi}=\mathbb{R}(ie_t)\oplus\mathrm{SU}(2),\quad \dim_\mathbb{R}\mathrm{U}(2)=4,\quad \dim_\mathbb{R}\mathrm{SU}(3)=8; \qquad L=\pi i m\,e_t+\pi n\vec U,\ m\equiv n\pmod2; $$

$$ m_A^2=\frac{ϙ^2v^2}{\hbar^2},\quad m_h^2=2\beta v^2; \qquad V_{\mathrm{CKM}}=W_u^{\dagger}W_d; \qquad m_\pi^2f_\pi^2=-(m_u+m_d)\langle\bar qq\rangle; \qquad m_a=\frac{\Lambda^2}{f_a}. $$

Five results to keep. The compact algebra is four-dimensional and the ceiling is proved twice, by dimension and by module parity. The gauge group on an element is the spin group, so the representation is read from the carrier and not chosen. The framework's own $\mathrm{U}(1)$ is the central phase and is vector-like, which forbids a chiral mass term and forces the letter law. The abelian Higgs mechanism and the abelian instanton are genuine framework constructions, and their non-abelian counterparts are not. Everything else — the group, the quantum numbers, nineteen parameters — is imported, and the category's worth is that it says so article by article.

What the category is for. Two things. It fixes, exactly and defensibly, what the biquaternion algebra can and cannot carry — and that is a result, not a failure. And it marks, on every page, which statements about the Standard Model are the framework's and which are the model's written in the framework's letters. A reader who keeps those two apart cannot be misled by the category; a reader who does not will mistake a transcription for a derivation.

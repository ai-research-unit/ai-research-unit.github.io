Introduction

It gives the objects, the load-bearing formulas, and the non-trivial claims. It gives no proofs: an agent re-derives them. 1. The Algebra and the Element

The algebra is the complexification of the quaternions, with basis $e_t=1,\vec e_x,\vec e_y,\vec e_z$:

$$ \mathbb{B}=\mathbb{C}\otimes_{\mathbb{R}}\mathbb{H},\qquad \tilde Q=Q_t\,e_t+Q_x\,\vec e_x+Q_y\,\vec e_y+Q_z\,\vec e_z,\qquad Q_{\imath}=q_{\imath}+iq'_{\imath}\in\mathbb{C},\ q_{\imath},q'_{\imath}\in\mathbb{R}. $$

The product is the quaternion product with complex coefficients:

$$ \tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2=\bigl(Q_{1t}Q_{2t}-\vec Q_1\!\cdot\!\vec Q_2\bigr)+Q_{1t}\vec Q_2+Q_{2t}\vec Q_1+\vec Q_1\times\vec Q_2 . $$

Eight real coordinates, four complex coefficients. The element is not an array, not a column, not an ideal. Every article of the corpus is written in this one letter, and a display that needs a matrix is a display about the $2\times2$ image, which is a reading and not the object.

The product is the only operation. There is no second product, no bracket assumed, no derivative assumed. Every kinematic and dynamical structure is read from this line.

2. The Two Sectors

The conjugation $^{*}$ reverses the vector part and complex-conjugates the coefficients:

$$ \tilde Q^{*}=\bar Q_t\,e_t-\bar Q_x\,\vec e_x-\bar Q_y\,\vec e_y-\bar Q_z\,\vec e_z . $$

The two sectors are its fixed spaces up to sign:

$$ \mathbb{B}_{\Psi}=\{\tilde Q:\tilde Q^{*}=\tilde Q\}=\{h_t\,e_t+i(h'_x\vec e_x+h'_y\vec e_y+h'_z\vec e_z)\},\qquad \mathbb{B}_{\Phi}=\{\tilde Q:\tilde Q^{*}=-\tilde Q\}=\{i r'_t\,e_t+r_x\vec e_x+r_y\vec e_y+r_z\vec e_z\}. $$

The split and the swap. $\mathbb{B}=\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}\oplus\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ as real vector spaces, and multiplication by $i$ exchanges them:

$$ i\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}=\mathbb{B}_{\Psi},\qquad i\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}=\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}. $$

This is why there is no complex structure on $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ (obstruction O16): the $i$ that a complex Hilbert space needs is the generator of the swap, so it leaves the sector. An agent must not write $i\tilde H_\Psi$ and call it an element of the same sector.

The assignment. The material sector $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ carries relativity; the informational sector $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ carries quantum physics. The assignment is a reading with a warrant, not an axiom: the Lorentzian form is indefinite on $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ and the Hermitian form is positive definite on $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$.

3. The Metric, the Interval and the Physical Coordinates

The metric is the identity matrix, and it is read off the product, not postulated:

$$ \delta_{{\imath}{\jmath}}=(1,1,1,1),\qquad \delta_{{\imath}{\jmath}}=\frac{\mathrm{Sc}(e_{\imath} e_{\jmath})}{\mathrm{Sc}(e_{\imath} e_{\imath})} . $$

The interval is the quaternionic norm of the differential, and the Lorentzian signs come from $i^{2}=-1$:

$$ \langle d\tilde Q\rangle_{\natural}=d\tilde Q\,d\tilde Q^{\natural}=\sum_{\imath} (dQ_{\imath})^2, $$

$$ \langle d\tilde R_\Phi\rangle_{\natural}=-(dr'_t)^2+dr_x^2+dr_y^2+dr_z^2\quad\text{with } Q_t=icr'_t, $$

$$ \langle d\tilde Q_\Psi\rangle_{\natural}=dq_t^2-(dq'_x)^2-(dq'_y)^2-(dq'_z)^2 . $$

The one substitution. Writing $Q_t=ict'$ and $Q_k=ix'_k$ on $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ turns the positive-definite tuple $(ct',x',y',z')$ into the Minkowski interval; the four coordinates of the tuple never change sign, and no index is raised or lowered. The material reading writes

$$ \tilde R_\Phi=i c t'\,e_t+x'\,\vec e_x+y'\,\vec e_y+z'\,\vec e_z , $$

while the informational reading keeps reality on $ct'$ and puts $i$ on the space:

$$ \tilde H_\Psi=ct'\,e_t+i(x'\vec e_x+y'\vec e_y+z'\vec e_z). $$

The temptation to refuse. Absorbing the $i$ into $g=\mathrm{diag}(-1,1,1,1)$ recovers the textbook Clifford metric $\{\gamma'_{\imath},\gamma'_{\jmath}\}=2g_{{\imath}{\jmath}}I_4$ with $\gamma'=i\gamma$, and it is equivalent. The article lists seven costs of that choice; the decisive one is that the sign stops being auditable by squaring.

The two light cones. $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ carries $(dr'_t)^2=dr_x^2+dr_y^2+dr_z^2$; $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ carries $dq_t^2=(dq'_x)^2+(dq'_y)^2+(dq'_z)^2$.

4. The Two Actions and the Interlock of the Brackets

One operation serves three purposes:

$$ \Gamma_{\tilde Q_1}(\tilde Q_2)=\tilde Q_1\,\tilde Q_2\,\tilde Q_1^{*}. $$

Read with $\tilde Q_1$ a unit-norm biquaternion, it is the Lorentz transformation. Read with $\tilde Q_1$ a state or a Kraus element, it is a quantum operation. Read at measurement, it is the update.

The interlock. The commutator lands in one sector and the anticommutator in the other:

$$ [\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2]\in\mathbb{B}_{\Phi},\qquad \{\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2\}\in\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}. $$

Why this is the central structural fact. The Lie algebra of the Lorentz group is a subspace of the material sector, so the symmetry of spacetime and the anti-symmetry of the quantum commutator are one statement. For two material elements the commutator is the cross product:

$$ [\tilde R_{\Phi,1},\tilde R_{\Phi,2}]=2\,\vec r_{\Phi,1}\times\vec r_{\Phi,2}. $$

Warning. The sector split is not an algebra grading: $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}\not\subseteq\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ (O15). Products are read through commutators or through the trace.

5. The Four Forms

Four forms are carried by the twelve products. The two that the physics uses constantly:

$$ \langle\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2\rangle_{*}=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2^{*})=\sum_{\imath} Q_{1{\imath}}\bar Q_{2{\imath}}, \qquad \langle\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2\rangle_{\natural}=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1^{\natural}\tilde Q_2)=\sum_{\imath} Q_{1{\imath}}Q_{2{\imath}}. $$

The first is the Hermitian (positive-definite) pairing; the second is the quaternionic (indefinite) form. On the sectors:

$$ \langle\tilde H_{\Psi,1},\tilde H_{\Psi,2}\rangle_{*}=h_{t,1}h_{t,2}+\vec h_{\Psi,1}\!\cdot\!\vec h_{\Psi,2}\ge 0 \quad\text{on the cone}, $$

$$ \langle\tilde R_\Phi\rangle_{\natural}=-{r'_t}^2+r_x^2+r_y^2+r_z^2 . $$

The rule. A positive number is obtained from a state with $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_{*}$; a mass shell is obtained from a momentum with $\langle\cdot\rangle_{\natural}$. Confusing the two is the most common error an agent can make in this corpus (see §13).

The trace reading. In the $2\times2$ image,

$$ \mathsf M_2(\tilde H_\Psi)=h_t I_2+\vec h_\Psi\!\cdot\!\boldsymbol\sigma,\qquad \operatorname{tr}\mathsf M_2(\tilde Q)=2\,\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q), $$

so twice the scalar part is the matrix trace, and the Born denominator is a trace.

6. Why the Material Sector Carries Relativity

The group is forced by the norm. The Lorentz group is exactly the group of unit-norm biquaternions acting by congruence:

$$ SL(2,\mathbb{C})\cong\{\tilde U_{\mathrm{lor}}\in\mathbb{B}:\tilde U_{\mathrm{lor}}\tilde U_{\mathrm{lor}}{}^{\natural}=e_t\},\qquad \tilde R_\Phi\longmapsto\tilde U_{\mathrm{lor}}\,\tilde R_\Phi\,\tilde U_{\mathrm{lor}}{}^{*}. $$

The norm is preserved because the norm is multiplicative:

$$ \langle\tilde U\tilde R\tilde U^{*}\rangle_{\natural}=\langle\tilde U\rangle_{\natural}\langle\tilde R\rangle_{\natural}\langle\tilde U^{*}\rangle_{\natural}=\langle\tilde R\rangle_{\natural}. $$

The consequence. The Lorentz group is not assumed and then represented; it is the group of the algebra's own norm. The interval, the light cone, the four-velocity with $\langle\tilde U_{\mathrm{lor}}\rangle_{\natural}=-c^{2}$, and the field strength are chapters of the same multiplication table. The electric and magnetic parts of a field are the two halves of a bivector, not two vectors glued by a tensor.

7. Why the Informational Sector Carries Quantum Physics

Observables. Only Hermitian elements have real spectra and a positive cone. The observables are the elements of $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$; a material element of $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ is represented by its Hermitian image.

States. The state is a positive Hermitian element of unit trace:

$$ \tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}=\tfrac12(e_t+i\vec s_\Psi),\qquad |\vec s_\Psi|\le 1,\qquad \mathrm{Sc}\,\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}=\tfrac12 . $$

The pure states are the idempotents on the cone boundary:

$$ \tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id}}=\tfrac12(e_t\pm i\vec u_{\mathrm r}),\qquad \tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id}}^{2}=\tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id}},\qquad \tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id}}^{*}=\tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id}},\qquad \langle\tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id}}\rangle_{\natural}=0 . $$

The Born weight is a trace pairing, not a postulate:

$$ p=\frac{\langle\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi},\tilde H_\Psi\rangle_{*}}{\langle\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi},e_t\rangle_{*}} =\frac{\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}\tilde H_\Psi^{*})}{\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi})} =h_t+\vec s_\Psi\!\cdot\!\vec h_\Psi . $$

Why this matters. The probability is $\tfrac12(1+\vec s\cdot\hat h)$ in the usual language, and here it is the scalar part of a product of two elements of one sector. The Born rule is therefore a computation inside the algebra, and the state space is the sector's positive cone.

The boost. A boost rotor is Hermitian, of unit biquaternion norm, and not $*$-unitary:

$$ \tilde U_{\mathrm b}=\cosh\tfrac{\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}}{2}\,e_t+i\sinh\tfrac{\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}}{2}\,\vec u_{\mathrm b}, \qquad \tilde U_{\mathrm r}=\cos\tfrac{\theta_{\mathrm{axr}}}{2}\,e_t+\sin\tfrac{\theta_{\mathrm{axr}}}{2}\,\vec u_{\mathrm r}. $$

The rotation is a real quaternion, hence $*$-unitary and of unit norm:

$$ \mathrm{U}(2)\cap\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{C})=\mathrm{SU}(2). $$

The consequence that is quoted everywhere in the corpus. Rotations are channels; boosts are not. A boost preserves the sector, the cone and the norm, and moves the state off the normalisation slice:

$$ \mathrm{Sc}\bigl(\tilde U_{\mathrm b}\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}\tilde U_{\mathrm b}^{*}\bigr)=\tfrac12\bigl(\cosh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}+s_{\Psi\parallel}\sinh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}\bigr),\qquad N'=\frac{N}{\bigl(\cosh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}+s_{\Psi\parallel}\sinh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}\bigr)^{2}} . $$

The read Bloch radius on the maximally mixed state. It is $\tanh$ of the rapidity:

$$ |\vec s_\Psi|'=\tanh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}} . $$

8. Thermality Without the Wick Rotation

The Gibbs state is a congruence of the centre:

$$ \tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}(\beta)=\frac{e^{-\beta\tilde H_\Psi}}{\operatorname{tr}e^{-\beta\tilde H_\Psi}} . $$

On a thermal axis the radius and the norm are

$$ |\vec s_\Psi|=\tanh\Theta,\qquad N=\tfrac14\operatorname{sech}^{2}\Theta , $$

and the inverse temperature is a rapidity that a boost translates additively:

$$ \Theta\longmapsto\Theta-\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}},\qquad \beta\longmapsto\beta-\frac{\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}}{|\vec h_\Psi|} . $$

The non-trivial claims. No Wick rotation is performed: imaginary time is a coordinate of the algebra, not an analytic continuation. The Gibbs path is a transversal to the unitary orbits, not one of them. The Curie law for the Bloch radius is recovered with an error of order $\Theta^{2}/3$, and it saturates.

9. One Identity, Four Conservation Laws

The identity: a $*$-unitary conjugation fixes the trace and the determinant of every element.

$$ 2\,\mathrm{Sc}\,\tilde Q=2Q_t,\qquad \langle\tilde Q\rangle_{\natural}=\tilde Q\tilde Q^{\natural}=Q_t^{2}+Q_x^{2}+Q_y^{2}+Q_z^{2}. $$

Read on $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$, the trace is energy and the norm is mass; read on $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$, the trace is normalisation and the norm is information:

$$ \operatorname{Tr}\tilde P_\Phi=2iE/c,\qquad \langle\tilde P_\Phi\rangle_{\natural}=-m^{2}c^{2}, $$

$$ 2\,\mathrm{Sc}\,\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}=1,\qquad \langle\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}\rangle_{\natural}=\tfrac14\bigl(1-r^{2}\bigr). $$

The non-trivial part. Energy is conserved and frame-dependent; mass is conserved and frame-independent. Mass is non-additive because of the cross term:

$$ E=mc^{2}\cosh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}} . $$

The verdict on $h_0$. The corpus has no quantum of information. The four candidate readings of a constant $h_0$ are examined and none is kept.

10. The Mass Shell and the Scalar Part: the Asymmetry

One polynomial on two planes:

$$ \mathcal{N}=\langle\tilde Q\rangle_{\natural}=-m^{2}c^{2}\ \text{on }\mathbb{B}_{\Phi},\qquad \mathcal{N}=\tfrac14\bigl(1-|\vec s_\Psi|^{2}\bigr)\ \text{on }\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}. $$

What a congruence preserves. On the material plane, both the definition and the group fix the norm, so mass is fixed. On the informational plane, only the definition fixes the norm; the group moves the scalar part. A boost at $\theta=+0.4$, $\Theta=0.8$ sends $2\,\mathrm{Sc}$ to $0.808317724032$, while $\mathcal{N}$ stays fixed.

The two traps. $\mathcal{N}=0$ means pure on one plane and null on the other. The Hermitian form gives $\tfrac12$ on both an idempotent and the centre, so a value of $\tfrac12$ does not identify a state.

11. The AXF Display

The axis-free frame is built in the coefficient basis and its multiplication constants are its entries:

$$ e_{\imath} e_{\jmath}=\sum_\rho\varepsilon_{{\imath}{\jmath}}^{\ \ \rho}e_\rho,\qquad \varepsilon_{{\imath}{\jmath}}^{\ \ \rho}\in\{0,+1,-1\}. $$

The ten properties: integer entries, signed permutations, orthogonality, entries equal to the structure constants, transpose equal to quaternion conjugation, real diagonal stitch $R=DL^{\mathsf T}D$ with $D=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$, quaternion group on $\mathbb{R}^{4}$, cyclic symmetry, the real slice, determinant one. Uniqueness holds among signed-permutation frames.

The non-trivial claim. The array is a conjugacy class, not an operator; the operator is unique. The Pauli and gamma displays are refused because each carries a direction in its notation, and the covariance test makes that direction visible.

13. Six Traps for an Agent

Trap 1 — wrong form. A modulus $|\tilde Q|$ is not a norm of a state, and the indefinite norm is not a probability. Use $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_{*}$ for anything that must be positive.

Trap 2 — the $i$ that leaves the sector. $i\tilde H_\Psi\in\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$. A complex structure on the informational sector does not exist inside the algebra (O16).

Trap 3 — the product preserves the wrong thing. $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ is not closed under multiplication (O15). A statement about a product of two states must be recast as a commutator, a trace, or an anticommutator.

Trap 4 — a boost is not a channel. $\tilde U_{\mathrm b}$ is not $*$-unitary. It rescales the trace and the norm by the same factor and moves the state off the slice. Only rotations are channels.

Trap 5 — the split is not canonical. The split of an element into the two parts is canonical, but the split of $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ into temporal and spatial directions is not (O17). Any reading that singles out an axis must declare the axis and then check invariance.

Trap 6 — no scale. Every relation is scale-free (O20). Mass, $\frac{h}{2\pi}$, $c$ and $k_B$ are imported, never derived.

The ceiling. No $\mathfrak{su}(3)$ from the vector units; no gravitational dynamics derived; no arrow of time; no measurement problem solved. The two-sector reading organises these programmes; it does not close them.

Summary

The category in one block. The element $\tilde Q=Q_te_t+\vec Q$ over $\mathbb{B}=\mathbb{C}\otimes\mathbb{H}$. The conjugation splits $\mathbb{B}$ into $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ (material, anti-Hermitian) and $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ (informational, Hermitian), swapped by $i$. The norm $\langle\tilde Q\rangle_{\natural}=Q_t^{2}+Q_x^{2}+Q_y^{2}+Q_z^{2}$ gives the interval $-(dr'_t)^{2}+dr_x^{2}+dr_y^{2}+dr_z^{2}$ on the material sector, and the Hermitian form $\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2^{*})$ gives the positive pairing on the informational sector. The one action $\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2\tilde Q_1^{*}$ is the Lorentz transformation, the quantum channel and the measurement update. Unit norm defines $SL(2,\mathbb{C})$; the state is $\tfrac12(e_t+i\vec s)$; the Born weight is $h_t+\vec s\!\cdot\!\vec h$; the boost is not $*$-unitary while the rotation is; the thermal radius is $\tanh\Theta$. The price is twenty-three certified obstructions and one admitted scale-free algebra.

What an agent should carry away. Four lines: the element, the product, the two forms, the one action. Everything else in the corpus is a reading of those four.

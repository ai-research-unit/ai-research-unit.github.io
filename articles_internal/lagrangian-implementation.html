Lagrangian Implementation: The Frozen Architecture, the Slot Tiers and the Closed Lists

Introduction

The result, first. Three decisions are frozen, and they are written here so that they stop moving. The boundary of the family. The tier of each of the seventeen slots. The closed lists of readings, couplings, sectors and fluid charts. This file is not an argument; the argument is in Lagrangian Migration. This file is the contract that the rewrite is checked against, and every later batch cites it rather than re-deciding it.

Why a separate file. The migration note has changed its arithmetic three times: forty-six articles became thirty-two field articles plus eight fluids; seventeen slots became eleven mandatory and six conditional, and then ten and seven once the heading maps showed that slot 6 has nothing to hold in twenty of the forty articles; a second-quantised layer appeared that the seventeen do not cover. Each change was an improvement and each invalidated the previous count. A number that changes is not a plan. The three decisions below are the ones that must not change again, and separating them from the reasoning is what makes the difference visible.

The rule of this file. Nothing here is physics. Everything here is a name, an order or a count. If a later pass wants to change one, it changes it here, in one place, with a date, and then re-derives the batches. It does not change it inside an article.

The Boundary Sentence

The sentence, verbatim.

The seventeen slots govern the one-particle layer; a second-quantised article is built on the bridge, not on the seventeen.

What it forbids. Four things, and each of them was proposed at some point in the last revision.

Forbidden Why
An eighteenth slot for the promotion to operators The promotion is not a section of a one-particle article; it is the subject of a different article
A Fock-space block inside the family No field article of the family quantises its field, and a block that four articles cannot fill would be copied empty into forty-two
A slot for the path integral The four path-integral articles exist and are not Lagrangian articles; the path integral is a formulation of the quantum theory, not of the classical action
Growing the template when a new stratum is noticed The template has already grown twice for this reason; twice is the whole evidence needed

What it permits. A second-quantised article quotes a slot number where the slot is genuinely the same: the action in slot 3, the currents in slot 9, the ledger in slot 12. It does not quote slots 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 13 or 14, because in a quantised article those objects are operators, brackets or measures and their one-particle names would be false.

The bridge, named once. The Lagrangian and the Second Quantisation: From the Action to the Commutator, with the Peierls bracket in slot 4, the Dirac bracket in slot 6 and the promotion in slot 7. It is the only article that joins the two templates, and it is the reason the family does not need to grow.

Slot 2 is the largest single gap, and it is not the gap this note predicted. Thirty-two of the forty articles have no Conventions section: the whole element, polar, limit and fluid groups. Only the eight momentum-space articles have one, because a transform needs its symbols declared. The family names its glyphs for the first time in Summary of Notation, which is slot 16, at the end.

Slot 7 is the second. Twenty-two articles own a claim and never give it a heading of its own: the KG element article states its reality argument in two paragraphs inside slot 3, and the Schrödinger articles state theirs inside the ledger.

Slot 9 is the third. Twenty-three articles compute no current, and in the limit and fluid readings that is most of them.

The saving, stated as arithmetic. Seventeen slots over forty articles is 680 slot positions. The measured gap is 105 mandatory-slot absences and 192 conditional-slot absences. The first are sections and must be written. The second are one-line declarations in slot 1, and only seven of them are sections worth writing: slot 6 in the twenty articles where a constraint exists, and slot 11 in the sixteen that take a limit. So the work is about 112 new sections, not 320, and 103 of the 105 mandatory absences fall on five slots: 2 (32), 9 (23), 7 (22), 4 (20) and 12 (6).

The number the rewrite is actually about. Four hundred and forty-six sections exist across the forty articles, mean 11.2. The rewrite takes that to about 560 and changes no existing sentence that carries a claim. That is the whole scale of the job, and it is a sum that can be checked against articles_internal/lagrangian-maps at any time.

The element reading adds no block, and that is a decision. It is the reading in which the action is written in the field's own variables, and its four questions — action, variation, currents, ledger — are already slots 3, 4, 9 and 12. A block of its own would be a restatement.

The coupling axis, closed at two. Free and coupled. The coupled form is marked by the word Coupled before the equation name in the title and by -coupled- before the equation in the slug. There is no third value: an article is either about the free field or about the field with the connection present.

The grid, and it is exactly thirty-two.

$$ 4\ \text{equations}\times 2\ \text{couplings}\times 4\ \text{readings}=32 . $$

The four equations, closed. Klein–Gordon, Dirac, Proca, Schrödinger. A fifth equation enters only with a decision recorded here. Rarita–Schwinger and Pauli are named in the migration note as articles to write; they join the grid only by changing this line.

What the Freeze Binds and What It Leaves Free

Bound. The slot names and their order. The tier of each slot. The four readings and their suffixes. The two couplings. The four equations of the grid. The seven sector slots. The eight fluid charts and the five replaced slots. The boundary sentence. The count of forty-six.

Free. The prose of every section. The length of every article. The number of displays. Whether a given article fills a conditional slot, provided the omission is declared. The physics: no rewrite may weaken a claim, and no slot may be filled with a claim the article does not already make or cannot check.

Free, and deliberately. The list of articles still to be written. A new article enters under one of the closed lists above. It does not create a new list, and it does not add an eighteenth slot.

Summary

The family is frozen at three decisions. The boundary: seventeen slots for the one-particle layer, a bridge for the second-quantised layer, and no growth of either. The tier: ten mandatory slots and seven conditional after the dated amendment, with a declared omission instead of an invented section. The lists: four readings, two couplings, four grid equations, seven sector slots, eight fluid charts with five replaced slots, and forty-six articles of which thirty-two are field articles, eight are fluids, four are theory-first and two are level 0.

What the freeze buys is arithmetic that stops moving. The forty maps measure the gap at 105 mandatory absences and 192 declarations, so the pass writes about 112 sections and not 320; the eight fluids take the chart and not the seventeen; the four theory-first wait; the two level-0 articles are never touched. What it costs is one admission: the sixteen articles of the quantisation layer — six canonical quantisations, three Fock spaces, two quantised fields, four path integrals and the spin–statistics theorem — do not fit this architecture, and one bridge article joins them to it instead of forty-two rewrites absorbing them.

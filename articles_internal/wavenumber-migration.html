Wavenumber Migration: One Letter, Four Wave Numbers

Introduction

What this article is. It is an internal article. It fixes no mathematics and it edits no article. It measures the physics corpus against one decision, lists the articles that contradict it with a link to each, and proposes a migration in phases.

The decision recorded here. The letter $k$ is the wave-number letter. It is not the spatial index. A wave number written with another letter, or with a bare $k$ that does not say which of the four it is, is a deviation.

The finding, in one sentence. Fifty-one of the 440 physics articles write a wave number with a letter that is not $k$, or with a bare $k$ that leaves the reader to guess which wave number is meant.

Why the letter needs its subscripts. The corpus has four wave numbers, and one letter names all four. $k_m$ is the mass wave number, $k_\Phi$ the momentum one, $k_{t}^{\prime}$ the temporal one, and $k_{\mathrm{eu}}$ the Euclidean one. A bare $k$ therefore says wave number and nothing more. The sector and the role say which.

A warning about the size of the job. The corpus carries $\vec e_k^2=-e_t$ on 293 lines, in 294 occurrences. A textual substitution on $k$ rewrites the basis in its first minute. Every pass below is a reading pass on one article, and no pass is a sed.

What the Conventions Already Fix

The four wave numbers, from one element. Physics Units and Common Elements Conventions, §Physical Units, defines the wave number by one division: $\tilde K_{\Phi}=\tilde P_\Phi/\hbar=i k_{t}^{\prime}e_t+\vec k_\Phi$, with $k_{t}^{\prime}=E/(\hbar c)$, $\vec k_\Phi=\vec p_\Phi/\hbar$, $k_\Phi=\lvert\vec k_\Phi\rvert$ and $\tilde K_{\Phi}\tilde K_{\Phi}^{\natural}=-k_m^{2}$. The scalar coefficient, the modulus of the vector part and the norm are the three wave numbers of a particle. The fourth, $k_{\mathrm{eu}}$, is the Euclidean momentum after a Wick rotation.

The paragraph that said the opposite has been corrected. A caution on $k$ used to open with one erroneous sentence: "The letter $k$ is the spatial index of this corpus: $\vec e_k$, $p_k$, $\sum_k$. A wave number therefore never takes a bare $k$." It is now deleted from the article, and three other statements were corrected with it: the paragraph Why a subscript at all of The Three Wavenumbers of a Particle, and the clause the spatial index in The Electric Dipole Moment and the Biquaternion Anti-Hermitian Structure, where $k$ is the third quaternion unit and not an index at all.

That sentence was erroneous for three reasons. It contradicted the table printed above it, which writes the corpus's three wave numbers as $k_m$, $k_\Phi$ and $k_{t}^{\prime}$. It contradicted the corpus, which writes those three glyphs in 127 articles. And it contradicted the rest of its own paragraph, which defines $k_{t}^{\prime}$, $k_\Phi$ and $k_m$ in the sentences that follow.

What replaces it. One sentence, and it is the whole of the rule the migration needs: the letter $k$ is the wave-number letter, four wave numbers share it, and a bare $k$ names none of them. The migration below rests on the subscripts, not on the letter being taken.

The consequence for the index, which is now the open question. $k$ is not the index letter, and the corpus writes it as one on 2650 lines in 338 files: $\vec e_k$, $\sum_k$, $p_k$, $\vec u_k$, $\tilde S_{\Psi,k}$, $\varepsilon_{ijk}$. Those lines have no licensed reading under the corrected rule. Six options are costed in §The Index Letter, and none is applied here.

The ordinary and the reduced wave number. The corpus works in the reduced wave number, radians per metre. A spectrometer's $\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ is the ordinary one, $1/\lambda=k_\Phi/(2\pi)$. So $k=2\pi/\lambda$ is a deviation in letter only, and a pass that "corrects" it by a factor $2\pi$ introduces the error it means to remove.

The Measurement

The measure. A line deviates when it carries one of these spellings in a wave-number sense:

  • $k=\omega/c$, $k=\omega\sqrt{\epsilon\mu}$ or $k^2=\omega^2/c^2$, a wave number under the bare letter;
  • $k\,\vec u_k$ or $\vec k_\Phi=k\vec u_k$, a modulus under the bare letter;
  • an order statement, order in $k$ or order of $k$;
  • $D_3-k$ and its relatives, a shift of the spatial operator under the bare letter;
  • $k=2\pi/\lambda$;
  • $q$ or $\varpi$ for an axial wave number;
  • $k_\mu$ for a wave covector, a null congruence or a neutral timelike direction;
  • $d^3k$, $d^4k$, $\delta^{(3)}(\vec k-\vec k^{\prime})$, $k^2-m^2$ in natural units, a loop momentum;
  • $\kappa$ for a decay constant, and $\kappa$ for a gauge coupling;
  • $\vec k_\perp$ or $\vec k_t$, a subscript that is not one of the six allowed forms.

The count is of lines, not of occurrences, because one display is repaired once.

The corpus, measured. 440 articles. 127 mention a wave number. 122 of those carry $k_m$, $k_\Phi$ or $\tilde K_\Phi$ and are compliant.

Why the rename is exact. The medium articles supply the identity themselves, $\vec k_\Phi=k\vec u_k$ with $k=\omega/c$. The bare $k$ is the modulus, so $k_\Phi$ replaces it and no quantity moves.

The article that needs more than a rename. Maxwell's Equations in Chiral Media writes $\alpha_1$ and $\alpha_2$ for the two circular wave numbers. They are $k_{\Phi+}$ and $k_{\Phi-}$, with $k_{\Phi\pm}=k_\Phi/(1\pm k_\Phi\beta)$. The sign carries the polarisation and the sector precedes it, as $\tilde\Lambda_{\Phi,\mathrm{ret}}$ precedes its role.

Which wave number the Helmholtz shift takes. In Maxwell's Equations in the Biquaternionic Formulation the shift is $\partial/\partial R_t=-k$, so the number is the temporal one and the shift becomes $D_3-k_{t}^{\prime}$. A rename to $k_\Phi$ there would be right in value and wrong in origin, because the derivative is temporal.

The Index Letter

What the index is. It is not a glyph. It is the subscript position, and the corpus puts $k$ in it under ten different base glyphs: $\vec e_k$ on 1410 lines, $\vec u_k$ on 255, $r_k$ and $R_k$ on 250, $\tilde S_{\Psi,k}$ on 170, $\varepsilon_{ijk}$ on 145, $p_k$ on 127, $\gamma_k$ on 107, $A_k$ on 45, $v_k$ on 16, $\sum_k$ on 12. The union is 2650 lines in 338 files. Any option is costed on that union and not on $\vec e_k$ alone.

The one hard constraint. $\varepsilon_{ijk}$ and the commutators $[\tilde S_{\Psi,i},\tilde S_{\Psi,j}]=\frac{m\,c\,i\,\epsilon_{ijk}\tilde S_{\Psi,k}}{k_m}$ need three distinct dummy slots in one expression. An option that replaces the slot by a named axis destroys them. This eliminates four of the six options for those 157 lines and leaves the rest of the surface free.

Option D. The upright label. $\vec e_{\mathrm{k}}$, $p_{\mathrm{k}}$, $\sum_{\mathrm{k}}$. Zero occurrences today. An upright word is one of the six legal subscript forms, and the corpus already writes $\mathrm{ret}$, $\mathrm{fop}$, $\mathrm{scp}$, $k_{\mathrm{eu}}$.

Cost: 2650 lines, 338 files, and the edit is a case change inside the subscript. Failure: the corpus's upright labels are words of two or more letters; a single upright letter is new. The distinction is also invisible in handwriting and fragile in a diff that ignores case.

Option E. The explicit axes. $\vec e_x$, $p_x$, $\sum_{j=x,y,z}$. $\vec e_x$ already appears 14723 times. Cost: 2650 lines, and each sum expands to three terms. Failure: the dummy slots of $\varepsilon_{ijk}$ cannot be axes, so 157 lines keep a letter anyway; and the operator conventions forbid expanding a sum that stands inside a display.

Option F. A Greek dummy. $\vec e_\varsigma$, $\varepsilon_{\varsigma\eta\zeta}$. The letters $\varsigma$, $\chi$, $\upsilon$ and $\phi$ are unused in subscript position. Cost: 2650 lines. Failure: Greek is the four-slot layer, $\mu$ and $\nu$, so a Greek spatial slot contradicts §The Layer Glyphs; and three Greek letters inside one $\varepsilon$ are unreadable.

Option G. The split by role. The letter stays where the subscript names a unit, $\vec e_k$ and $\sum_k\vec e_k$, 1410 lines; the component coefficients take the axes the corpus already writes, $p_x$, $u_x$, $r_x$, $\gamma_x$, about 970 lines. Cost: 970 lines. Failure: the same letter then means an index in one display and a wave number in the next, which is the defect the migration removes.

Option H. The numeral basis. $\vec e_1,\vec e_2,\vec e_3$ for the units, the axes for the components, and three spare letters only inside $\varepsilon$ and the commutators. Cost: 1410 lines of basis plus 970 of components, but the letter surface falls from 2650 lines to about 157. A numeral counts an element, which §The Index and the Slots licenses, and $\vec e_1$ is an element and not a coefficient.

Failure: the numerals $1,2,3$ already label the modes $\vec k_1,\vec k_2$ on 101 lines, so the same numeral would count an element in one article and a mode in the next; and it gives the three units a number where every other unit of the algebra, $e_t$ and $\vec e_x$, has a letter.

Option I. The position rule. A bare $k$ is the index; $k$ with a subscript is a wave number. Cost: 105 lines, the twelve articles of Family One, which gain a subscript. Failure: it gives the letter a second meaning, which is what the correction forbids, and it is the deleted sentence restored with a caveat. It is listed because it is the cheapest and because the corpus already reads it that way in $\frac{m\,c}{2k_m}i\vec e_k$.

The recommendation. None of the six is comfortable, and that is the finding. D is the only option that gives the letter no second meaning, keeps $\varepsilon_{ijk}$, and costs nothing but a case change; it should be tried on one article and read on paper before it is adopted. E and H are the same idea, the explicit axis, and they fail on the same 157 lines. I is excluded by the decision, not by its cost.

What this section does not do. It does not choose. Phase Two stays open, and no pass of the migration depends on it.

The Migration Strategy

Phase One. The conventions, before any article. Delete the erroneous sentence of A caution on $k$. Replace it with the narrower rule: a bare $k$ names no wave number, because four share the letter. Repair the article's own two defects, the bare $k$ in $\frac{k}{2m\,c/\hbar}$ and the commutator example that pairs $\vec k_\Phi$ with $\vec k_2$, which the paragraph it illustrates forbids. Add one row to §Summary of Notation for the four wave numbers. This is the only pass that changes what a reader is told the letter means.

Phase Two. The index decision. Closed: the index is $\eta$, recorded above. The option table of §The Index Letter is kept as the record of why the obvious letters were rejected.

Phase Three. Family Two, alone. One article, four lines. It opens the axial reading the gravity articles reuse, and it tests the rename.

Phase Four. Family One, parents first. Maxwell's Equations in the Biquaternionic Formulation and Electromagnetism in Media first, because the other ten cite them for the operator, the constitutive relations and the identity $\vec k_\Phi=k\vec u_k$. Then the eight that apply them, then the two exercises.

Phase Five. Family Three, in three blocks. The wave covector block first, fourteen articles, the gravity pair before its dependants. Then the two Kerr–Schild articles, which need $\tilde{\mathcal L}_{\Phi,\mathrm{null}}$ and a decision on the display. Then the three neutral-direction articles, which wait for Phase Two.

Phase Six. Family Five. The ten coupling lines first, one line each, with the replacement fixed by the conventions. Then the three decay articles, one sentence each.

Phase Seven. The subscript set. Four articles, mechanical once $\vec k_{\Phi,\mathrm{tr}}$ and the prime are chosen. The Vacuum State and the Casimir Effect carries fourteen lines and is the largest single display repair in the migration.

Phase Eight. Family Four, last. The only pass that changes the meaning of a display. Every number in the fourteen articles is re-derived, or the article declares its units in $mc$ and $mc^2$.

Phase Nine. The sweep. Count the 293 lines of $\vec e_k^2$ before and after. Count the bare $k$ in a wave-number sense and require zero. Require zero proof-end marks in the migrated articles. No sed at any point.

The cost. 51 reading passes, about four lines each. Two are expensive: Curved Spacetime at 23 lines, and The Vacuum State and the Casimir Effect at 18 across two families. Each article gains one Summary of Notation row and one first-use sentence.

Summary

The letter $k$ is the wave-number letter, and the corpus has four wave numbers under it. The paragraph that calls $k$ the spatial index is erroneous and is deleted, not reworded: it is contradicted by the table above it, by the corpus, and by its own following sentences. What the corpus needs is narrower and is kept: a bare $k$ names no wave number.

Fifty-one articles write a wave number another way. Twelve write the medium number as a bare $k$. One writes the axial pair as $\varpi$ and $q$. Nineteen write a covariant $k_\mu$, of which fourteen are a wave number, two a null congruence and three a neutral direction. Fourteen write the loop momentum under the bare letter and drop $\hbar$. Three write a decay constant as $\kappa$, and ten write the gauge coupling as $\kappa\mathcal A$. Four write a subscript that is not one of the six forms.

The order is the dependency order. The conventions first, then the index decision, then one article that tests the rename, then the medium set parents-first, then the covariant set in three blocks, then the coupling lines, then the subscripts, then the loop momentum with its recomputation.

Summary of Notation

Symbol Meaning in this article
$k$ the wave-number letter; a bare $k$ names no wave number, because four share the letter
$k_m$, $k_\Phi$, $k_{t}^{\prime}$, $k_{\mathrm{eu}}$ the four wave numbers: mass, momentum, energy, Euclidean
$\vec k_\Phi$, $k_{\Phi x},k_{\Phi y},k_{\Phi z}$ the momentum wave vector and its components
$k_{\Phi+}$, $k_{\Phi-}$ the two chiral wave numbers, $k_\Phi/(1\pm k_\Phi\beta)$
$k_{\Phi,\mathrm{tr}}$ the transverse part, replacing $\vec k_\perp$
$\vec k_\Phi^{\prime}$ the second mode, replacing $\vec k_n$ and $\vec k_2$ in a mixed commutator
$\kappa_j$ the decay constant, defined once as $\lvert k_{\Phi,j}\rvert$
$\kappa=1-\vec u_R\cdot\vec\beta$ the retarded denominator, the licensed use of the letter
$\vec e_\eta$, $\sum_\eta$ the index usage, now migrated; $p_k$ and the other component letters await the same pass

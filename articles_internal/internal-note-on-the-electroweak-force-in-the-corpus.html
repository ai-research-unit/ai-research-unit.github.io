Internal Note: The Electroweak Force in the Biquaternion Corpus

The Answer

The corpus transcribes the electroweak sector completely and constructs it partly. The menu now draws the sector's boundary: The Electroweak Interaction is one of the six parts of the category and holds twenty-six articles. This note examines eighteen of them and proposes five in the gaps, and it carries three articles from outside the part because the argument needs them. The eight it does not list treat the process level, the neutrino mass and mixing, the electric dipole moment, the left–right extension and the Stueckelberg route; the note points at them instead of numbering them. Every article carries the model item by item, and every one is explicit about which half of the work it does. The algebra supplies a four-dimensional compact algebra, the charged and neutral directions, the free massive-vector kinematics and the abelian Higgs mechanism. It does not supply hypercharge, chirality, the doublet, the masses or the mixing angle. Those are imported.

The decisive correction. The non-abelian factor of the framework's gauge algebra is the rotation group of space, not an internal isospin. Non-Abelian Gauge Fields in Biquaternionic Form says so itself: its group is "the group of rotors of pure spatial rotations ... the double cover of $\mathrm{SO}(3)$". So the four gauge directions are three spacetime and one internal, where the Standard Model has four internal. The dimension match is a match of counts, not of kinds.

One force, three spins. Spin $1$ supplies the carriers, spin $\tfrac12$ the chiral matter, spin $0$ the field whose vacuum value gives the carriers mass. That is why the sector needs spin bands of its own inside its part: it is one of the two parts of the menu that use all four spins. Three Spins in One Force sets the count and draws the comparison with Maxwell, which is one spin only.

The consequence, in one line. A gauge field in the adjoint of $\mathrm{Spin}(3)$ is the spatial part of a spin connection. Read as an internal symmetry it fails; read as geometry it is gravity, and the corpus already has that reading.

What the Electroweak Force Is

One interaction with two charges, one of them chiral. Above the breaking scale the carriers are four massless vector fields, $W^1,W^2,W^3$ and $B$, with couplings $g$ and $g_{\mathrm Y}$. Below it, one neutral direction stays massless — the photon — and three acquire mass.

The group is $SU(2)_L\times \mathrm{U}(1)_Y$. The $L$ is the whole content of the word electroweak: the $SU(2)$ acts on left-handed fields only. That one letter is what the framework cannot reproduce, for a reason that is a theorem rather than a gap in the work.

What unifies is the description, not the coupling. $g$ and $g_{\mathrm Y}$ remain independent parameters. The unification is a statement about one group and one covariant derivative, and the mixing angle is measured.

The Proca Claim, Examined

The claim that prompted this note was that electromagnetism is a zero-mass limit of Proca, and that the corpus builds the electroweak sector on that. It is right in structure and wrong in direction.

Right, in three exact statements. Maxwell is the massless limit of Proca, not a separate theory. The mass term occupies the slot where Maxwell puts a source, so the two differ by one term and a parameter. And only the complexified Proca field carries a current that can source Maxwell — the real field has none.

Wrong, in one. Electromagnetism is not derived from Proca. The photon is the direction that remains massless at $k_m=0$, and in the electroweak sector its masslessness is a property of the vacuum's alignment with $Q=T_3+Y$, not of the Proca equation.

Two Layers

What the algebra supplies

A compact four-dimensional algebra. $\mathbb{B}_\Phi=\mathfrak{u}(2)=\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{u}(1)$: three antisymmetric spatial generators $\vec e_x,\vec e_y,\vec e_z$ and one central phase $ie_t$. Four directions, matching the four gauge fields in count.

The charged directions, with their charges computed rather than inserted. The off-diagonal elements are $\vec e_x\pm i\vec e_y$, and the adjoint action of the Cartan generator is diagonal on them:

$$ [i\vec e_z,\ \vec e_x\pm i\vec e_y]=\pm 2(\vec e_x\pm i\vec e_y). $$

The adjoint eigenvalues are $\pm2$; on the generator $\tfrac12\mathrm{ad}_{i\vec e_z}$, which is the corpus's normalisation, they are $\pm1$, the charges of $W^\pm$. This is the closest the framework comes to deriving an electroweak quantum number, and it derives a charge difference, not a charge scale.

The neutral plane. The two commuting generators span a two-dimensional neutral space, and the mixing is a rotation inside it:

$$ Z_\mu=\cos\theta_W\,W_{\mu 3}-\sin\theta_W\,B_\mu,\qquad A_\mu=\sin\theta_W\,W_{\mu 3}+\cos\theta_W\,B_\mu . $$

The algebra supplies the plane and the rotation. The angle needs a metric, and the metric is the relative normalisation of two kinetic terms — two free coefficients. The Weinberg Angle states the gap in one sentence: "The algebra supplies the directions and not the metric."

The self-couplings. Writing the curvature with the commutator term, the cubic and quartic terms of the Yang–Mills density are powers of the commutator, with structure constants $f_{abc}=2\varepsilon_{abc}$ read from the quaternion table. The $WW\gamma$, $WWZ$ and four-point vertices follow. The central direction has vanishing structure constants, so the abelian factor does not self-couple — the algebra's own version of a correct physical statement.

The kinematics of a massive vector, complete. A free $W$ or $Z$ is a Proca field: the Lorenz condition follows rather than being imposed, there are three polarizations, and the transverse sum $\mathcal P_{\mu\nu}=\delta_{\mu\nu}+p_\mu p_\nu/M^2$ is idempotent and annihilates $p_\nu$ on shell.

The abelian Higgs mechanism, whole. One charged central scalar, one potential with a circle of minima at $|\xi|=v/\sqrt 2$, and a mass from the scalar kinetic term,

$$ M_A^2=\frac{\varkappa^2 v^2}{\hbar^2}=\kappa^2v^2, $$

writing $\varkappa$ for the framework's coupling and $\kappa=\varkappa/\hbar$ for the combination the corpus uses elsewhere.

The count balances: $2+2=1+3$, two real scalars and two transverse polarizations before, one scalar and three vector polarizations after.

What is imported

Seven rows, and the corpus marks them so. The group with its $L$; the hypercharge values; the chiral action on doublets and singlets; the scalar doublet with its representation; the mixing angle; the vector masses; the charged and neutral currents. The corpus's ledger words are imported and no route yet, and it uses them in print.

The distinction matters because it is the note's whole subject. A transcription is not a failure. It is the correct description of a framework that hosts the algebra and receives the model.

The Three Obstructions

First: the scalar is a singlet. The corpus's scalar is the central field $\xi e_t$. Anything proportional to the identity is fixed by every inner automorphism, so it carries the trivial representation of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ and cannot break it. The non-abelian factor acquires no mass from it. The abelian mechanism is unaffected, because a singlet can carry a $\mathrm{U}(1)$ charge.

Second: the centre is vector-like. The central element $ie_t$ commutes with everything, so a coupling to it gives the same charge to every field, left-handed and right-handed alike. Hypercharge gives six different charges. One uniform charge cannot be hypercharge, and no relabelling fixes it: any Lie-algebra isomorphism of $\mathfrak{u}(2)$ onto $\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{u}(1)$ carries the centre onto the centre.

Third: the framework cannot fix the gauge. Without a gauge-fixing principle the corpus computes curvature components and cannot say what the spectrum is. Every mass statement in the sector inherits the gap.

One root under all three. The gauge group of the framework is the spin group, and representation is therefore a function of spin. A spin-zero field carries the trivial representation; a spin-half field carries the spinor on both letters. The Standard Model needs spin and gauge label to be independent, and here they are not.

The Non-Abelian Factor Is the Rotation Group

The corpus states the action, and states it as left multiplication. Non-Abelian Gauge Fields in Biquaternionic Form writes

$$ D_\mu\tilde\Xi=\frac{\partial}{\partial R_\mu}\tilde\Xi+i\kappa\,\mathcal A_\mu\tilde\Xi, $$

"acting on a matter field by left multiplication", with $\tilde\Xi\mapsto\tilde Q\tilde\Xi$ and $\tilde Q$ a unit real quaternion. So the one-sided left action is the corpus's, in its own words.

The left action is the spin representation, and this is not a conjecture. The One-Sided Action and the Spin Representation states it: "the same left multiplication, restricted from the algebra to the spin group, is the representation that carries the spinors." The carrier is a minimal left ideal, two complex-dimensional. It is one-sided by necessity, because a right multiplication does not preserve a left ideal.

And it is not the action that rotates. Polar Decomposition of Quaternions states that "a rotor acts on the pure quaternions by the two-sided operator", with the Rodrigues form rotating by $2\theta$. So one rotor has two actions: conjugation rotates an element, left multiplication carries a spinor. They are different operations, kept in different articles.

The two are exact opposites in what they preserve.

Conjugation $\tilde QX\tilde Q^{-1}$ Left product $\tilde QX$
multiplicative in the pair yes, inner automorphism no, not an endomorphism
preserves the star, hence both sectors yes no
preserves the trace yes, $0$ no, $0.206$

The residual of the automorphism law over forty random pairs is $2\times 10^{-15}$. The trace line is the discriminator: rotating an algebra element preserves the trace, carrying a spinor does not.

A boost is excluded, and the exclusion is sharp. Conjugation preserves the grading exactly when $A$ is unitary up to scale, $\tilde Q\tilde Q^{\ast}=\lambda I$ with $\lambda>0$; a boost is not unitary. And the rotor fails both halves of the definition of an internal symmetry: it does not commute with the boost generators, $[\vec e_x,i\vec e_y]=2i\vec e_z\neq 0$, and it rotates a spinor index by construction.

So the field has a home, and it is not an internal one. A gauge field in the adjoint of $\mathrm{Spin}(3)$ is the spatial part of a tetrad spin connection. Curved Spacetime and the Biquaternion Framework says that "the spin connection of the tetrad formalism is therefore not foreign to the algebra; it sits inside it", while recording that the algebra determines nothing about it. The fork is explicit, and there is no third branch:

The Dimension Match, at Four Levels

The match $4=4$ is true and is not an identification.

Level one: the group is not a product. $\mathrm{U}(2)\cong(\mathrm{Spin}(3)\times \mathrm{U}(1))/\mathbb Z_2$, the quotient by the common central $\mathbb Z_2$ — the same element that makes $\mathrm{Spin}(3)$ a double cover of $\mathrm{SO}(3)$. The algebra $\mathfrak{u}(2)$ cannot tell $\mathrm{U}(2)$ from $SU(2)\times\mathrm{U}(1)$; the group can, and the corpus keeps the distinction in The Electroweak Gauge Group and the Hypercharge Assignment.

Level two: the centre argument. Any isomorphism carries the centre to the centre, so the match forces $B\propto ie_t$. One central charge for every field, where hypercharge gives six. This is the no-go that no relabelling escapes.

Level three: the two factors live on opposite sides of the star. The chiral charge element is star-even and the gauge potentials are star-odd, so they occupy different eigenspaces of one involution. A chiral gauge potential is not an object this algebra holds.

Level four: the chiral one-sided actions are abelian. Among one-sided multiplications, those that respect a chirality split commute with the chirality operator, and that centraliser is abelian. Chiral abelian is available — the centre is exactly that. Chiral non-abelian is not.

What the four levels buy. The framework's electroweak sector is a coherent gauge theory with one massive vector, a complex singlet scalar and vector-like fermions. The bosonic count after breaking is ten where the model has twelve: six from the three massless directions, three from the massive one, one physical scalar. None of those numbers is an accident; each follows from the scalar being a singlet and the fermion being vector-like.

The electroweak force is not a spin class, and the spin bands are a reading aid, not a definition. Three spins appear, each with a different job.

Cut by spin alone, every band mixed the two forces; cut by object first, each part holds one force and its spin bands then say only what they were meant to say — which representation the field carries.

Maxwell has none of this. Electromagnetism is one spin and one role: the carrier. The corpus's layout records the difference. Biquaternion Electromagnetism has twenty-nine articles and not one on a spin-$\tfrac12$ or spin-$0$ field in an electromagnetic role; the single Dirac title there is the parabolic Dirac operator, which reformulates Maxwell and describes no fermion. The structural reason is the breaking. Electroweak needs a spin-$0$ field because its carriers are massive and something must break the symmetry. $\mathrm{U}(1)_{\mathrm{em}}$ is unbroken, so the photon is massless with no scalar involved, and $M_\gamma=0$ follows from the group rather than from vacuum alignment. Electroweak also needs spin-$\tfrac12$ because its matter is chiral; QED's matter is vector-like, left and right coupled alike — the one thing the framework's one-sided action does supply, since its spinor carries both letters alike.

What this costs the framework, in one line. It reaches the vector row of electroweak for the same reason it reaches Maxwell, and it fails on the spin-$0$ and spin-$\tfrac12$ rows, which Maxwell does not have.

The Photon, and the One Coincidence

The framework reaches the free photon's polarization structure exactly. The polarization space is the material sector, the indefinite form is the biquaternion norm, and the two circular polarizations are the eigenvectors of left multiplication by $\vec u_\Phi$ with eigenvalues $\mp i$. The helicity operator $\tfrac{i}{2}\mathrm{ad}_{\vec u_\Phi}$ has spectrum $\{+1,0,-1\}$ and its $\pm1$ eigenvectors are the two physical polarizations.

And it reaches nothing further. There is no bosonic ladder, so no photon-number operator. No gauge-fixing principle, so the reduction from four polarizations to two is imposed from outside. Masslessness is inserted: nothing in the algebra fixes $m=0$. Interactions are outside the free treatment.

The one derivation of masslessness is elsewhere and is real. It is in Electroweak Symmetry Breaking: the zero eigenvalue of the neutral mass matrix is structural, because $Q=T_3+Y$ annihilates the vacuum. It is a statement about the imported doublet, not about the algebra, and it is nonetheless a genuine result.

The coincidence is the mass formula. The abelian Higgs gives $M_A=\varkappa v/\hbar=\kappa v$; the model gives $M_W=gv/2$. The same structure with $\kappa=g/2$. The factor $\tfrac12$ is the isospin of the scalar, and the framework's scalar has isospin zero, so the identification is made from the standard side and after the fact. A match of structure, not a derivation.

No measurement separates the framework from standard physics in this sector, and the reason is structural. A test needs an input that is not algebra — a dynamics, a coupling, a scale, or a selection principle. Here the framework supplies the algebra and imports every input, so nothing is left over to test.

The cheapest candidate for a test is named by the corpus itself. A relation $g_{\mathrm Y}=f(g)$ would remove one free parameter and be visible at the per-mille level in the oblique parameters. It does not exist, and the corpus says so in print.

Verdict

What is in the corpus. The group and the algebra; the four bosons; the breaking; the mixing; the four-part Lagrangian and the eight-part one after breaking; the currents; the self-couplings; the Yukawa sector; the mixing matrices; the gauge fixing; the anomalies; the precision observables. What is not. The derivation. The corpus says which parts the algebra produces and which it receives, article by article, and it lists six items of work with the object each needs. That honesty is the corpus's strongest feature and the reason the boundary can be drawn this precisely.

The one sentence. The corpus reaches the sector's kinematics, its algebra, its self-couplings and its abelian scalar mechanism; it imports its group, its quantum numbers, its angle and its masses; and it records the difference rather than blurring it.

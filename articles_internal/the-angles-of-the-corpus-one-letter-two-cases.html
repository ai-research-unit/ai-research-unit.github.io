The Angles of the Corpus: One Letter, Three Cases

The corpus writes a real angle as $\theta$, a complex angle as $\Theta=\theta+i\theta'$, and a quaternion angle as $\tilde\Theta$. The purge is finished: no article names an angle with another letter. This page is the record.

The Rule

The case names the field of the argument. A lower-case glyph holds the reals, an upper-case one holds a larger sector, and the tilde holds the quaternions. The three rungs are one rule, not three conventions:

$$ \theta\in\mathbb{R},\qquad \Theta=\theta+i\theta',\qquad \tilde\Theta=\alpha+\theta_{\mathrm b}\vec u_{\mathrm b}' . $$

Where one expression holds two angles, the first keeps the bare letter and the second takes a three-letter label. The label names the role, not the article: $\theta^{\mathrm{azm}}$ is the azimuth wherever it appears.

What the Pass Did

Forty-five articles changed, and 1,129 occurrences moved. $\theta$ rose from 7,483 to 8,642, thirty of the new occurrences being the rows of the index itself.

Four letters kept a role that is not an angle. $\alpha$ in Curves and Surfaces is a bivector, $\gamma$ in Euclidean Geometry is a curve, $\Omega$ in the physics conventions is a curvature, $\omega$ in The Einstein–de Haas Effect is an angular velocity. Each article renaming was done on the angle only.

Two angles were found outside the census. Exercise: Boosting a Four-Velocity writes the angle between two boosts as $\varphi$ and never names it in a phrase the census reads: 21 occurrences, now $\theta^{\mathrm{btw}}$. The Relativistic Central Force Problem writes the orbital angle as $\varphi$ beside $\omega\theta$: 5 occurrences, now $\theta^{\mathrm{azm}}$.

The Three Readings That Were Not a Rename

The quaternion angle is $\tilde\Theta$. The Quaternion Pairing of the Polar Decomposition writes $\tilde Q=\tilde q\exp(i\tilde\Psi)$ with $\tilde\Psi=\alpha+\theta_{\mathrm b}\vec u_{\mathrm b}'$ a real quaternion. It is the third rung, and the case already carries it: $Q$ complex, $\tilde Q$ quaternion, so $\Theta$ complex angle and $\tilde\Theta$ quaternion angle. 109 occurrences.

The rapidity keeps $\psi$, and it is $\theta'$ by convention. The Hamilton Pairing carries $\Theta=-i\psi$. Algebra and Element Conventions states the same thing in the two-parameter form: at $\theta=0$, $\theta'=\psi$. The letter $\psi$ is the rapidity of the corpus, 7,279 occurrences, and a rename would break the four-vector tables of four articles. What changed is the prose: the exponent is an exponent, and the phrase "exponent angle" is gone from the article, because it named the same object twice.

The rescaled angle is a product, not a letter. The orbit of The Relativistic Central Force Problem is a conic in $\theta^{\mathrm{azm}}=\omega\theta$: the angle is $\theta$, the rate is $\omega$, and the product needs no glyph of its own.

Verification

The census re-runs to zero. The four phrases of the census — "the angle $X$", "angle $\tilde X$", "$X$ is the angle", "angles $X$ and" — now match no site in the two corpora with a glyph other than $\theta$.

The site rebuilds clean. No page carries a KaTeX error, and no row of the index has an unbalanced delimiter.

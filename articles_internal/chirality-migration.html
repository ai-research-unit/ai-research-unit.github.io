Chirality Migration

Scope

This file is an inventory and a work order, not an article and not an entry of a menu. It lists every article of the two published corpora whose statement about chirality contradicts the notion that Operator Chirality on the Biquaternion Algebra now states, gives a direct link to each, and records for each the false sentence and the sentence that replaces it.

The notion itself is fixed in one place and is not re-derived here. Chirality is the slot of an operator of $\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb{C}}(\mathbb{B})$: membership in $\operatorname{im}L$, in $\operatorname{im}R$, in $L\cap R=\mathbb{C}\mathrm I$, or in none of the three. The proofs are the mathematics article's; this file takes them as settled and spends its length on the corpus.

What is counted. A row is owed to an article when the article defines chirality — or a word standing in for it, the chirality operator, the chiral projector, the chiral splitting, the value of the chirality — by an object that is not a slot: a frame, an axis, a volume cell, a fifth cell, an ordered product of four generators, a root, an idempotent, a Peirce corner, a $\gamma_5$ array, an anticommutator. A row is not owed for a mention of a cell, a root or an idempotent that does not carry the word.

What is exempt. The mathematics article and Biquaternion Physical Interpretation of Operator Chirality, both migrated. Legacy sections, which are allowed to print arrays. The general mathematics of the same folders, where chirality names the symmetry of a knot or the grading of a Clifford module and no biquaternion operator is in play; those are named once, in §No Patch — Vocabulary Collisions, and are patched nowhere.

The Six Defect Classes

D1 — The frame names the chirality. The form is the chirality is the volume cell of the frame, the frame supplies it, the chirality cell. It is the family defect and it produces most of the rows. False because the cell is one operator, built from one chosen element, while the label of an operator is fixed without it; and because it writes a frame into a definition that the algebra states without one. The repair keeps the cell and moves it one word to the right: the cell is an example of the right slot, and the label is the membership.

D2 — Anticommutation as the definition. The form is a chirality must anticommute with the generators and square to the identity, it anticommutes with each $C_\mu$, so it separates the halves. False in the algebra, not merely loose: the criterion demands an anticommutator with $L(e_t)$, which is the identity, and an operator anticommuting with the identity is zero. What is true is narrower and worth keeping: the cell of one frame anticommutes with that frame's four cells, for the quaternion product rule and no other reason. The repair deletes the criterion and states the true fact as a fact.

D3 — A root, an idempotent or a Peirce corner carries the word. The form is the chirality is the root $H=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$, both statements are read off the one idempotent, the chiral split is the representative adapted to the root. False on dimension and on role. A Peirce corner is one-dimensional and its projector is built from an element that must be chosen; the chiral halves are two-dimensional left modules and the chiral projector $\tfrac12(\mathrm I\pm\mathsf V)$ is derived from a cell. The two projectors agree when they are built from the same element, which is a coincidence of one choice and not an identity of two objects.

D4 — Chirality as a grading or a sign detached from an operator. The form is chirality is a $\mathbb{Z}_2$ grading, a two-valued label on the field. Half true: the split is a grading of the carrier once an operator supplies it. False as a definition, because it hides which operator, and an article that hides it then has to reach for a frame. The repair names the operator first and the grading after.

D5 — The two chiralities declared inequivalent by construction. The form is its two eigenspaces are the only two-dimensional irreducibles, $(\tfrac12,0)$ and $(0,\tfrac12)$, and these are inequivalent. True on the Dirac module, false on the algebra. Both halves of $\mathbb{B}$ are copies of the one simple left module $S$; the inequivalence belongs to $\Delta=S\oplus S^{\mathrm c}$, which needs a real structure to be built at all. The repair keeps both sentences and never in one breath: the copy sentence on $\mathbb{B}$, the inequivalence sentence on $\Delta$.

D6 — $\gamma_5$ as the name of the notion. The form is $\gamma_5$ is the reference-frame cell of the chirality, the chirality is the ordered product of the four generators up to $i$. It is an array token standing for a cell of one frame, so it is neither the notion nor a frame-free name, and it drags the Pauli and gamma vocabulary back into a body text that is supposed to be free of it. The repair is one sentence in a legacy paragraph; the body writes $\mathsf{V}_{\mathrm{vol}}$.

  3. Tier 2. The mechanisms. Each patch is a naming change plus one sentence that says what the mechanism was always using.
  4. Tier 3, last. The maths corpus needs a scope note repeated in a few places, and a scope note is best written once the physics wording has stopped moving.

Summary

Chirality is the slot of an operator and not a cell, so the migration is not a renaming. Every row above moves the word one step away from the object that has been carrying it: from the cell to the side, from the frame to the algebra, from the root and the idempotent to the slot, from the array to the operator. The mathematics is untouched, because the mathematics was already right; what changes is the grammar of the sentences that state it, and the grammar is what a reader carries away.

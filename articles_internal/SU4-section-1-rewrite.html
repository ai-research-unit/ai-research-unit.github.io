The First Section, Rewritten for $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ Alone

Why This File Exists

The first section of the menu still says the model is $\mathrm{SU}(4)\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$. It counts twenty-one generators, quotes rank five, calls itself the Four Colours Left–Right Model, and builds the sixteen out of two weak factors.

The corpus no longer says that. There is one group, $\mathrm{SU}(4)$, fifteen generators, rank three. The weak factors are born at the transition that kills the primordial force, and that birth is the subject of §3 of the migration plan.

So the first section is now wrong in its frame and right in most of its arithmetic. This file fixes the frame, article by article, and states which arithmetic survives untouched.

The Canon: What the First Section May Say

One group. $G=\mathrm{SU}(4)$, acting on the four-dimensional complex carrier. Fifteen real dimensions. Rank three. No product, no second factor, no $\times$ sign anywhere in a statement of the model.

$$ \dim\mathfrak{su}(4)=15,\qquad \operatorname{rank}=3,\qquad 15=\underbrace{8}_{\mathfrak{su}(3)}\oplus\underbrace{1}_{\mathbb R\,iD}\oplus\underbrace{6}_{\mathfrak m}. $$

One declaration. The canonical split $\mathbf 4=\mathbb C e_t\oplus B_V$ is the quark–lepton split, and the line $e_t$ is the lepton. This is declaration (A), unchanged, and it is still the only declaration the algebra does not make.

One coupling. $g_4$, multiplying the fifteen generators. Not three couplings. The three-coupling ledger belongs to the successor model and to the coupling article.

The matter content is the sixteen, with the copy index as a label. $\mathbf{16}=\mathbf 4\otimes\mathbf 2\oplus\bar{\mathbf4}\otimes\mathbf 2$: two copies of the quartet, two of its conjugate. Under $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ alone this is vector-like, $2\cdot\mathbf4\oplus2\cdot\bar{\mathbf4}$, so the $\mathrm{SU}(4)^3$ anomaly cancels identically and no trace has to be computed.

Electric charge is not in this model. The fifteen supplies $B-L$; the third component of an isospin that would complete $Q=T_3+\tfrac12(B-L)$ belongs to the copy index, and the copy index is not gauged here. $Q$ becomes a statement at the transition. Until then the model's charges are $B-L$ and the colour weight.

The fifteen already contains a real $\mathfrak{so}(4)$. The algebra's two-sided action gives $\mathfrak k_L\oplus\mathfrak k_R$, six real dimensions, inside the fifteen, equal to $\operatorname{span}\{A_{ab}\}$ — the six real antisymmetric planes. It is a real subalgebra of rank two, not a second factor. It is where the six arrays that the old frame called $T^{L}_{a}$ and $T^{R}_{a}$ live, and it is the object §3 names as the weak sector's ancestor.

The successor is the left–right model, reached at $M_C$. Not a competitor, not an alternative, not a subgroup relation. A transition that kills this group and births the two weak factors as an index (§3, articles 3 to 6).

The Ledger: Seventeen Articles, Eighteen Untouched

The section holds thirty-five entries. Eighteen of them are the general gauge machinery — the principle, the connection, the curvature, the Hopf bundle, the index theorem, duality, the Yang–Mills equation and its path integral, the lattice, the topological charge, ADHM, the dilute gas, the sigma model, the effective potential, the Fujikawa measure, Stueckelberg. A search for the product group returns zero hits in seventeen of the eighteen, and one link title in the eighteenth. They are written for a general group and read correctly for $\mathrm{SU}(4)$; they stay.

The One Substantive Gain, Worth Stating Once

The $\mathrm{SU}(4)$-only model is simpler and stronger in three places.

The covariant derivative loses two terms. One coupling, fifteen fields, one sum. Three couplings became one, and the corpus's four-decade coupling failure becomes a statement about unification rather than about the model's premise.

The anomaly question disappears. Two quartets and two antiquartets are vector-like in pairs, so $\mathrm{SU}(4)^3$ cancels identically. The old frame had to run three trace checks to show the sixteen was anomaly-free; the new frame shows it by counting. The checks survive as a remark, not as a condition.

The chirality question becomes honest. A vector-like gauged sector has no chiral gauge coupling at all, so the model is not chiral where it gauges is now a theorem about the model and not a caveat about a representation. Chirality is carried entirely by the copy index, which is ungauged — and that is exactly the sentence §3 needs, because the copy index is what gets gauged at the transition.

What Does Not Change, and Why That Is the Test

No eigenvalue moves. The adjoint action of $\operatorname{diag}(-3,1,1,1)$ on the fifteen is unchanged: $0^{9}\oplus(\pm4i)^{3}$. The masses are unchanged: $32v^{2}$ on six planes, $0$ on nine. The sextet's charges are unchanged: $Q=\pm\tfrac23$. Every number in the first-breaking section stands.

That is the test of a reframing. A rewrite that moved a number would be a different model. This one moves a frame, a count, and two names, and no number.

What the First Batch Fixed, and the Pattern for the Rest

Four articles, and the same three moves in each.

The count. $21 o15$, $24 o16$ cells, rank $5 o3$, and the four extra particles ($W^{\pm}$, $W^{\pm}_{R}$, $Z$, $Z_R$) removed and named as the successor's. No printed matrix and no eigenvalue changed.

The demotion. The six arrays $T^{L}_{a},T^{R}_{a}$ are still printed and still derived — as the six planes of $\mathfrak{so}(4)$, a real subalgebra of the fifteen, with an explicit statement that they are not generators, not a factor, and not particles after the transition.

The pointer. Every place the old frame needed a weak factor now points at §3. A reader who follows one pointer lands on the article that says why the weak force is an index and not a piece of the fifteen.

The pattern for articles 5–9. Each is mechanical in the same way: replace the twenty-one by the fifteen, keep every printed array, and move the six into a remark. The commutation table's entry count is the one number that changes, $441 o225$, and it must be recomputed and not guessed.

Summary

The first section is rewritten from the product group to the single group. One group, fifteen generators, rank three, one coupling, and the sixteen with an ungauged copy index.

Seventeen articles carry the frame and eighteen do not. The eighteen were checked by search and stay.

Three results get better: one coupling instead of three, an anomaly that cancels by counting instead of by three traces, and a chirality statement that is a theorem instead of a caveat.

No number moves. The fifteen, its spectrum, the six planes at $32v^{2}$, the nine massless directions and the sextet's charges are all untouched — which is what makes this a reframing and not a second model.

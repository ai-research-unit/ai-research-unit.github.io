The Fourth Colour as the Unit Line: The Declaration the Migration Rests On

Introduction

The question, and it is one sentence long. The corpus has a Lie algebra of dimension fifteen, called $\mathfrak{su}(4)$, built from its own carrier. Pati–Salam has a group of dimension fifteen, also called $\mathrm{SU}(4)$, built from a quartet of quark and lepton colours. Are they the same group acting on the same space?

The corpus's recorded answer is no. SU4-meaning.md closes with the refusal in as many words: "It is not $\mathrm{SU}(4)_c$ […] An identification of the two would be a claim about physics that this note does not make and the corpus does not support." That refusal is correct as an algebra statement, and this note does not overturn it.

What this note does. It states the missing physics claim — call it (A) — as a declaration rather than a derivation, argues for it from the one structure the algebra does single out, and then states its price. Every article of the migration leans on (A), so (A) has to be inspectable and refutable before any of them is written.

The declaration, in one line.

$$ \boxed{\ \text{fourth colour}=\mathbb{C}e_t,\qquad \text{three colours}=B_V=\mathbb{C}e_x\oplus\mathbb{C}e_y\oplus\mathbb{C}e_z\ } $$

with the consequence the migration uses everywhere:

$$ N_c=\dim B_V=4-1=3,\qquad B-L=\tfrac43P_t-\tfrac13\mathrm I,\qquad \operatorname{tr}_{\mathbf 4}(B-L)=1+3\cdot\bigl(-\tfrac13\bigr)=0 . $$

What is new here, and what is not. Nothing in §1 is new: the stabiliser, the quotient and the trace are the corpus's, and every number is rechecked in §1.4. What is new is §4, which says why this line and no other, §6, which states the price, and §8, which says what would make the declaration false or empty. The declaration itself is one sentence; the work is in the price.

What is not claimed. That the algebra derives the identification. It does not, and §3 says exactly how far the algebra reaches. That Pati–Salam is true. That the corpus should assert (A) on a public page before §8's tests are stated. And not that colour is contained in the biquaternion algebra — it is not, and the two notes on the ceiling stand unchanged.

1. What the Corpus Already Holds

Three facts, each already proved elsewhere, and each rechecked below.

1.1 The stabiliser. $\mathrm{SU}(3)=\mathrm{Stab}_{\mathrm{SU}(4)}(e_t)$: the subgroup of the fifteen-dimensional group that fixes the temporal unit. Equivalently, in the Lie algebra, the nine-dimensional $\mathfrak u(3)=\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathbb{R}\,iD$ is the subalgebra annihilating the first column of the display.

1.2 The quotient. $\mathrm{SU}(4)/S(\mathrm U(3)\times\mathrm U(1))\cong\mathbb{CP}^3$, of complex dimension three, real dimension six — the orbit of the line. So the fifteen split as

$$ \mathfrak{su}(4)=\underbrace{\mathfrak{su}(3)}_{8}\oplus\underbrace{\mathbb{R}\,iD}_{1}\oplus\underbrace{\mathfrak m}_{6}, \qquad 15=8+1+6 , $$

with $\mathfrak m$ the six that mix the unit line with the vector part. $9$ fixes the line, $6$ moves it.

1.3 The diagonal generator. $D=\operatorname{diag}(-3,1,1,1)$, the corpus's own, traceless, and the only diagonal generator the split singles out.

1.4 The checks. Every number above, run in exact and floating arithmetic today.

The two facts that matter for the declaration are the first and the third. The first says the colour-like group is the stabiliser of one line. The third says the corpus already possesses a traceless diagonal that vanishes on nothing and is built from that line.

2. Two Things That Could Be Meant by "the Group of the Carrier"

The ambiguity is not verbal, and it has to be cleared before anything is declared.

Reading one: the group is the group of the carrier. $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ is the unitary group of the four-dimensional complex vector space the algebra acts on — $15$ real dimensions, defined by two conditions that mention no element of $\mathbb B$. This is what SU4-meaning.md proves.

Reading two: the group is the gauge group of the strong and lepton interaction. $\mathrm{SU}(4)_c$ is the group whose fundamental is the quartet

$$ \mathbf 4=(\mathbf 3,\tfrac13)\oplus(\mathbf 1,-1), $$

i.e. three colours of quark and one lepton, with the abelian charge the fourth component of the same label. This is Pati–Salam.

Abstractly the two groups are the same group, and that is not the question. Every $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ is isomorphic to every other. The question is whether the two carriers are the same space:

$$ \text{the corpus's carrier } \mathbb C e_t\oplus \mathbb C e_x\oplus\mathbb C e_y\oplus\mathbb C e_z \;\overset{?}{=}\; \text{Pati–Salam's carrier } (\mathbf 3,\tfrac13)\oplus(\mathbf 1,-1). $$

So the declaration is not about groups. It is about spaces. This is the form the refusal in SU4-meaning.md should be read in — it refuses the carrier identification, which is a physics claim, and it is right that no algebra theorem supplies one.

3. The Declaration (A)

Stated in the form the migration uses.

(A) The four-dimensional carrier on which the corpus's $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ acts is, up to the tensor factor that carries spin, Pati–Salam's quark–lepton quartet; the lepton is the fourth colour; and the fourth colour is the unit line $\mathbb C e_t$.

Three equivalent writings, in increasing strength, and the migration uses the weakest one that does the work.

form statement what it needs
(A1) weak the split $4=1+3$ that Pati–Salam uses is the corpus's split $\mathbb C e_t\oplus B_V$ an isomorphism of the label space, not of the carrier
(A2) middle the lepton number direction is the unit line, $B-L\propto P_t-\tfrac13P_V$ a chosen normalisation, then $\operatorname{tr}=0$ fixes it
(A3) strong the carrier is the algebra, so colour acts on $\mathbb C e_t\oplus B_V$ itself false unless colour and Lorentz commute — see §6

The migration adopts (A1) and (A2), and refuses (A3). §6 shows why (A3) cannot be had, and the refusal is not a retreat: (A1) and (A2) are enough for charge quantisation, for hypercharge as a combination, for the anomaly ledger and for the sixteen, which is everything the migration claims.

Where the algebra stops. It supplies the split, the line, the traceless diagonal and the quotient. It does not supply the assertion that these are the colour and the lepton number. That assertion is a physics claim of exactly the kind SU4-meaning.md says it is not making, and this note makes it openly, once, here.

4. Why the Unit Line, and No Other

Four arguments, and the fourth is the one that decides.

4.1 The line is canonical and every other line is not. $\mathbb C e_t$ is the only complex line in the carrier that every automorphism of the algebra fixes. $\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathbb C}(\mathbb B)$ is six real dimensions, it preserves $e_t$ because $e_t=1$ is the identity, and inside $B_V$ it acts as $\mathrm{SO}(3)$ — so it moves every line of $B_V$ and no automorphism moves $\mathbb C e_t$. A colour group needs a distinguished direction to be the stabiliser of, and the algebra supplies exactly one.

4.2 There is no canonical axis, and that is the point. The user's structural intuition, made exact: $B_V$ has no canonical basis — $\vec e_x,\vec e_y,\vec e_z$ are a choice, related to one another by the automorphisms, so no one of them can serve as a colour label that the algebra knows about. What is canonical is the split

$$ \mathbb B=\mathbb C e_t\oplus B_V , $$

the centre plus the vector part, which is fixed by every conjugation and every automorphism. So the algebra can label a quartet $1+3$ and cannot label a $2+2$ or a $4$ with a marked axis inside the three. Pati–Salam needs $1+3$. The match is exact, and it is exact for a structural reason rather than a numerical one.

4.3 The alternatives cost more than they buy. Three other splits of the quartet are available to a group theorist and none is canonical:

split canonical? why it fails
$2+2$ no needs a complex structure on the carrier not fixed by the algebra; the two "pairs" are related by an automorphism
$1+1+2$ no needs two marked lines; $B_V$ has none
$4$ unbroken yes but then there is no $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ stabiliser and no colour at all

4.4 The count closes, and it is the same three. $N_c=\dim B_V=4-1=3$. The number of colours is not put in and then checked; it is the dimension of the complement of the line, and the "minus one" is the lepton. This is what makes the sixteen work: $\dim\mathfrak{su}(3)=8=\dim\mathfrak{su}(4)-1-6$ is the same arithmetic as $3=4-1$, one level up.

What the four arguments do not do. They do not prove (A). They establish that if a $1+3$ split of the corpus's carrier is to be used at all, there is only one candidate, so (A) is not a choice between many. Uniqueness is not truth, and §8 is where truth is tested.

5. What Follows Immediately, and It Is a Lot

5.1 Leptons and quarks are the two parts of one projector. Write $P_t$ for the projector onto the line and $P_V=\mathrm I-P_t$. Then

$$ D=\mathrm I-4P_t=P_V-3P_t , $$

which is the corpus's $D$ and explains its two entries at once: the three spatial directions get $+1$, the temporal one gets $-3$. Then

$$ B-L=-\tfrac13D=\tfrac43P_t-\tfrac13\mathrm I , \qquad\text{eigenvalues } \bigl(1,-\tfrac13,-\tfrac13,-\tfrac13\bigr). $$

So $B-L$ is a measurement of how much of the state lies on the unit line. Lepton number is the projection onto the temporal unit, rescaled. That is the content of "the lepton is the fourth colour", written in the algebra's own objects.

5.2 The tracelessness is the dimension count. The normalisation is not free: $B-L$ must be traceless, and

$$ \operatorname{tr}_{\mathbf 4}(B-L)=\underbrace{1}_{\text{one lepton}}+\underbrace{3\cdot\bigl(-\tfrac13\bigr)}_{\text{three quarks}}=0 . $$

The $1$ and the $3$ are $\dim\mathbb C e_t$ and $\dim B_V$. Charge quantisation is therefore the statement that the carrier is one line plus a three-space — not an extra assumption, and not a fact about the integers either: it is a fact about one dimension against three.

The $B-L$ column is the same in the left half and the right half, and that is why a fourth colour can exist at all. The right-handed fields sit in the conjugate quartet, where the generator is the negative of the weight; the column lists the physical $B-L$, which is what $Q$ uses, and it is the same for a field and its chiral partner. $P_t$ is chirality-blind — it is a projector of the algebra, not of the spin — so $B-L$ labels a field and its partner identically. A charge that changed with chirality could not be a fourth colour of the same quartet; the corpus's $\mathbb C e_t$ can, for the same reason that made it canonical in §4.1.

5.4 The four facts the migration then uses. Each is proved in the interaction note and none is repeated here:

$$ Y=T_{3R}+\tfrac12(B-L),\qquad g_3=g_L=g_R=g_4,\qquad \sin^{2}\theta_W(M_{PS})=\tfrac38,\qquad \text{four anomaly conditions}\to\text{none}. $$

6. The Price, and It Is the Sharpest Thing in This Note

The objection, in one sentence. If the carrier is the algebra, then colour acts on the same four dimensions as the Lorentz group, and the two must commute — but they cannot.

Why they cannot, computed. Let $L_{\vec e_a}$ be left multiplication by the three imaginary units on $\mathbb C^4$, i.e. the Lorentz action. The largest subalgebra of $\mathfrak{su}(4)$ commuting with all three is

$$ \mathfrak{su}(4)\cap\bigl\{X:X L_{\vec e_a}=L_{\vec e_a}X,\ a=x,y,z\bigr\}=\mathrm{span}\{R_{\vec e_x},R_{\vec e_y},R_{\vec e_z}\}\cong\mathfrak{su}(2), \qquad \dim_{\mathbb R}=3 . $$

Verified: the right multiplications commute with the left ones; $R_{\vec e_a}$ is anti-Hermitian and traceless; $R_1$ is Hermitian, so $iR_1$ is the only other anti-Hermitian element and it is not traceless. The full commutant of $L(\mathbb H)$ in $M_4(\mathbb C)$ is $R(\mathbb B)$, eight real dimensions, and of that only the three purely imaginary right multiplications sit in $\mathfrak{su}(4)$.

So the verdict on (A3) is not "hard", it is "excluded". A $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ of colour commuting with the full Lorentz action does not fit in $\mathfrak{su}(4)$ on one carrier: the ceiling is $\mathrm{SU}(2)$. This is the same wall the corpus already knows — the one-sided action, the chiral wall — met from the colour side instead of the chiral side, and it is the same wall for the same reason: $M_4(\mathbb C)$ cannot host two large commuting compact groups.

(A3) is excluded at Layer A, and the tensor product is the move to Layer B. The exclusion is about one carrier carrying both actions at once; it is not a statement that colour is unavailable to the frame. The test is what separates the two: the frame asks the algebra for the carrier, the canonical split, and the two-sided action, and colour then acts on a copy. So "colour is not in the algebra" remains true and "colour is excluded from the frame" does not follow, and this is the paragraph where the two are kept apart.

Therefore (A) is adopted in its tensor-product form, and the migration adopts (A1)–(A2) only. The sixteen fields live in

$$ (\text{internal }\mathbf 4)\otimes(\text{spinor}),\qquad \mathbf 4_{\text{int}}\cong\mathbb C e_t\oplus B_V , $$

where the internal quartet is a formal copy of the algebra's own four-dimensional coordinate space, and the identification asserted by (A) is of the split, not of the carrier. The copy inherits the split because the split is the only canonical one (§4); it does not inherit the Lorentz action, and it must not.

What this costs, stated plainly. The fourth colour is then the image of the unit line under an isomorphism, not the unit line itself. The identification is an analogy of structure — one line plus a three-space, with the line canonical and the three-space axis-free — and analogies of structure are cheap. What makes it more than an analogy is §5: the tracelessness, the count, and the chirality-blindness all transfer, and they are the three properties Pati–Salam needs. A split that transfers every property the physics uses is a split that has earned the label.

7. What Follows, and What Does Not

Follows from (A), with (A1)–(A2) only.

Charge quantisation as tracelessness, $3q+\ell=0$, with the corpus's own $D$.

Hypercharge as a combination, $Y=T_{3R}+\tfrac12(B-L)$, so $Y$ is not imported.

The sixteen as one multiplet per chirality, and $\nu_R$ as necessary rather than added.

The anomaly cancellation, identically, with the cubic cancelling between $\mathbf 4$ and $\bar{\mathbf 4}$.

$\sin^{2}\theta_W(M_{PS})=\tfrac38$ as a boundary condition, and $g_3=g_L=g_R$ at that scale.

Does not follow, and must not be claimed.

Colour inside the biquaternion algebra. It is not, and $\mathfrak{su}(4)$ is not $\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb B)$ either — the two notes on the ceiling and on the trace stand unchanged.

The chiral carrier. $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ does not make a one-sided action two-sided; the wall is in §What the Translation Cannot Do of the interaction note and it is untouched by (A).

The scalar carrier. A spin-zero field is still a line, and a bidoublet still needs two indices; (A) adds one more multiplet to the debt, not a solution.

The breaking scale. (A) says nothing about $M_{PS}$; it is fixed by experiment or not at all.

The number of generations. Three generations are three copies of the quartet, and the algebra does not count them.

The rule that governs every sentence written under (A). Derived from the algebra is allowed for $\mathfrak{su}(4)$ as the group of the carrier, and for the split $\mathbb C e_t\oplus B_V$. It is forbidden for the identification of that split with the quark–lepton quartet. The prohibition is one sentence long and it is the whole integrity of the migration.

8. What Would Refute the Declaration

(A) is not directly falsifiable, and saying so is part of stating it honestly. It is a claim about which space plays which role; a wrong assignment of roles shows up only through what the assignment predicts. So the test is indirect, and it has a direction.

8.1 The gauge sector. If the fourth colour is gauged, the six generators of $\mathfrak m$ are leptoquarks, with the quantum numbers $(3,\bar 3)$-like, coupling to a quark and a lepton. They have not been seen. The bounds are the ones the interaction note already lists: $K_L\to\mu e$ pushes $M_{X}$ to $10^{2}$–$10^{3}\,\mathrm{TeV}$, and scalar leptoquarks are excluded below about $1.5\,\mathrm{TeV}$.

8.2 The zero mode. If the fourth colour is gauged and unified, the proton can decay. It has not. The bound on $\tau(p\to e^{+}\pi^{0})$ pushes $M_{X}$ above $10^{15}\,\mathrm{GeV}$ for the simplest scalar-mediated diagrams.

8.3 The neutral sector. A gauged $\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$ contributes a $W_R$ and a $Z_R$; a right-handed neutrino mass needs a breaking scale. Neither is seen, which bounds $M_{R}$ from below.

8.4 What makes (A) empty rather than false. If the leptoquark and proton bounds are pushed so high that $M_{PS}$ exceeds any scale the framework can reach, then the fourth colour is either not gauged or not a colour at all, and (A) becomes a relabelling with no consequence. The declaration dies by emptiness at least as easily as by contradiction, and that is the more probable death.

8.5 What would make it false rather than empty. A leptoquark discovered with the wrong quantum numbers — a $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ singlet coupling to $q\bar q$ rather than to $q\ell$ — would show that the quartet is not the quark–lepton one. Or: a fourth-colour-like resonance whose $B-L$ is not the corpus's $D$, which would break the identification of the label space, not merely its dynamics.

8.6 The structural refutation, which is the one the corpus can actually deliver. A proof that some other split of $\mathbb C e_t\oplus B_V$ is canonical — that the algebra fixes a second line, or a canonical complex structure on $B_V$ — would make (A)'s uniqueness argument collapse. §4.1 is the load-bearing claim of this note, and it is the claim to attack.

9. The Status, and What It Obliges

Tier. This note is the phase-$0$ article of the migration: one new article, the one everything else leans on, written before the frame so that the assumption is not hidden inside a definition.

Where it lives. articles_internal/, because it is a declaration with a price and not yet a result. The promotion path is one file move: when §8's ledger is stated on a public page, this becomes articles_physics/the-fourth-colour-as-the-unit-line under the electromagnetic section's fifth sub-section, The Field's Gauge Origin, and it keeps its slug.

What it obliges the rest of the migration to do. Every article that uses charge quantisation, hypercharge as a combination, or the sixteen names this note. Every article that discusses the ceiling states that (A) does not breach it. And no article may cite (A) as a derivation of Pati–Salam from the biquaternions.

Summary

The question. Whether the corpus's fifteen-dimensional $\mathfrak{su}(4)$ and Pati–Salam's $\mathrm{SU}(4)_c$ act on the same space. The corpus's recorded answer is no, and abstractly the groups are trivially the same group — the question is about carriers.

The declaration (A). The fourth colour is the unit line $\mathbb C e_t$; the three colours are its complement $B_V$; $N_c=4-1=3$.

The three forms. (A1) the split, (A2) the lepton-number direction, (A3) the carrier identity. The migration adopts (A1) and (A2) and refuses (A3).

Why this line. It is the only line every automorphism fixes; $B_V$ has no canonical axis; the split $\mathbb C e_t\oplus B_V$ is canonical where the alternatives are not; and the count $3=4-1$ is the same arithmetic as $8=15-1-6$.

What follows. $D=\mathrm I-4P_t$, $B-L=\tfrac43P_t-\tfrac13\mathrm I$, $\operatorname{tr}_{\mathbf 4}(B-L)=0$, so $3q+\ell=0$; then $Y=T_{3R}+\tfrac12(B-L)$, $\nu_R$ required, anomalies identical, $\sin^{2}\theta_W(M_{PS})=\tfrac38$, $g_3=g_L=g_R=g_4$.

The price. Colour cannot commute with the Lorentz action on one carrier: $\mathfrak{su}(4)\cap\{X:[X,L_{\vec e_a}]=0\}=\mathfrak{su}(2)$, three dimensions, verified. So (A) is a tensor-product declaration about a formal copy of the quartet, and the fourth colour is the image of the unit line rather than the line.

The status. Not derived, declared; inspectable, refutable by leptoquark quantum numbers or by a second canonical line; and liable to die by emptiness if $M_{PS}$ is pushed out of reach.

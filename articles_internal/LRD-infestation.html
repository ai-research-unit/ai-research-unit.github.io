LRD Infestation

Scope

This file is an inventory, not an article and not an entry of a menu. It counts one pattern in the two published corpora and gives a direct link to each article that carries it.

Snapshot. The counts below are those of the scan of 4 November 2026, 22:42. A rewrite pass was running over both corpora at the time, so each figure states the day it was taken.

The pattern. A left multiplication, then a right multiplication, then a partial derivative:

$$ L_{a}R_{b}\,\partial . $$

The chain is written there as one symbol, with a summed index or a label instead of the terms it stands for. It is called LRD in this file, from its three letters.

Two cases, and only the first is in this file's first table. The chain can be collapsed in two ways. It is collapsed term by term, one symbol standing for the four, $L_{a}R_{b}\partial$, and it is collapsed as a whole, the sum written over an index or a letter, $\partial=\sum_\mu\dots$. Both kinds are counted in one table, in the columns term by term and as a whole, next to the line numbers and the written form of each. The second set contains the first, so the union is the table's total. That total is now zero: the rewrite pass of 4 November 2026 cleared the pattern from both published corpora.

Why the second case is worse. The first case hides which coordinate the derivative acts on. The second hides the derivative, the four multipliers and the sign at once, and it does so in the place where the operator is defined. A reader who meets the definition cannot recover the operator at all.

The count, first case. None. The scan of 22:42 finds no published article that collapses the chain term by term. The scan of 22:36 found three, holding 13 occurrences: 6 in 2 physics articles and 7 in 1 maths article. The internal file The Gradient, the D'Alembertian and the Frame Generators carries 44 chains of the shape and none of the infested kind.

The count, second case. None. The scan of 22:36 found 14 occurrences in 3 published articles: 7 in 2 physics articles and 7 in 1 maths article. An earlier count of this file read 27 and 49, in 12 and 19 articles. Two repair passes have since rewritten twenty-four published articles, and the re-scan leaves none. The fall is the repair, not a change of rule: the walk and the test are the ones described in §How the Count Is Made.

The word. Neither table counts the verbal form. The word cell, which names the same collapse in prose, stands 2495 times in 101 physics articles and 1405 times in 167 maths articles.

The Token

The canonical form is

$$ iL_{e_\mu}^{\mathrm{axf}}R_{\vec e_{\nu(\mu)}}^{\mathrm{axf}}\partial , $$

and it is unreadable until three separate conventions are supplied: the table $\nu(t)=x$, $\nu(k)=y$; the display whose label sits in the caret; and a sentence stating which coordinate $\partial$ acts on. Then it expands to four operators, three of which the token never shows.

The repaired form names its coordinate and its two multipliers:

$$ iR_{\vec e_x}\partial_{x_0}+iL_{\vec e_x}R_{\vec e_y}\partial_{x_1}+iL_{\vec e_y}R_{\vec e_y}\partial_{x_2}+iL_{\vec e_z}R_{\vec e_y}\partial_{x_3}. $$

That is four occurrences of the shape, and none of them is infested. The defect is not the shape; it is the collapse.

Why the Collapse Is a Defect

It hides three of four terms. An index $k$ over $1,2,3$ or a table $\nu(\mu)$ stands for four operators, and the reader who wants any one of them must expand the symbol himself. All 13 carried such an index, and none named both multipliers.

It hides the coordinate of the derivative. At the last scan that found the pattern, none of the 13 wrote a bare glyph: the first repair pass had given every chain a coordinate, $x_k$ or $Q_k$. What stayed hidden is which of the four operators the index selects, so the reader could read the coordinate and still not read the term.

It smuggles in a frame. 6 of the 13 carried the caret label on both multipliers, so the same chain appeared as $^{\mathrm{axf}}$ in one article and $^{\mathrm{pau}}$ in the next. A product of two multiplications is display-independent, so the label on a product is either redundant or false.

It reproduces the syntax it replaces. $\gamma_\mu$ is one index standing for four matrices. $L_{e_\mu}R_{\vec e_{\nu(\mu)}}\partial$ is one index standing for four operators. The shape is the same and so is the failure: a single letter that must be unpacked before it can be checked.

The column term by term counts the occurrences where one symbol stands for the four multipliers, and as a whole the occurrences where the sum itself is written over an index or a letter. A dash means the article carries none of that kind. The columns S, G, D and cell belong to the second kind only: S a sum over an index, G the letter $G_\mu$, D the letter $\partial$ defined by such a sum, and cell the occurrences of the word in the same file. A display can carry two of the three, so those columns can add to more than the occurrence count.

The column LR product records how the two multiplications are written, in whichever of the two kinds the file carries. Three values:

  • index — the pair carries a summed letter, $k$, $\mu$ or $\nu(\cdot)$, so neither multiplier is named.
  • letter $G_\mu$ — the pair is not written at all, only the letter $G_\mu$ that stands for it, as in $\sum_\mu G_\mu\partial/\partial x_\mu$. This is the worse of the two, because one letter replaces both multipliers and the index function behind them.
  • letters $L_aR_b$ — the pair names nothing, $L_{a}R_{b}$. One occurrence, in The First-Order Root of the Biquaternion D'Alembertian, which is the worst file in either corpus: six of its eight occurrences also write the derivative with no coordinate.

Where a file carries both an explicit pair and the letter $G_\mu$, the cell names both.

The Second Case: the Collapsed Contraction

The chain collapsed as a whole. Four written shapes:

A sum over an index. $\sum_\mu iL_{e_\mu}R_{\vec e_{\nu(\mu)}}\partial/\partial x_\mu$, or $\sum_k iL_{\vec e_k}R_{\vec e_y}\partial_{x_k}$, with the four terms never printed. A single letter. $\partial=\dots$ closing on such a sum, which turns the collapse into a definition. The letter $G_\mu$. $G_\mu=iL_{e_\mu}R_{\vec e_{\nu(\mu)}}$, used as $\sum_\mu G_\mu\partial/\partial x_\mu$, with the four hidden behind one index and the index function behind that. The $\varepsilon_\mu$ sum, $\sum_\mu\varepsilon_\mu iL_{e_\mu}R_{\vec e_{\nu(\mu)}}\partial_\mu$, where the sign vector does the work the four explicit signs would do. None of the four remains in the published corpora at the scan of 22:42; the first and the third were the two that survived the first pass.

The first set is a subset of the second: every article that collapsed the chain term by term also collapsed it as a whole somewhere else. No article carried the first kind alone. That is the shape of the defect, not a coincidence — a file that has learned the one-symbol-per-term habit writes its definitions the same way.

The Repaired Files

Twenty-four published articles were repaired in the first pass and the rest of the list in the rewrite pass of 4 November 2026, which ran while this scan was taken. Four articles had been repaired before either. Two repairs are shown here: the first rewrote a definition, the second deleted the sentence that carried a collapse. The rest are one of those two, file by file. At the scan of 22:42 no published article carries the pattern, and the internal file carries it nowhere.

Subluminal and Superluminal Electromagnetic Waves in Biquaternionic Form. The definition of the source's letter was

$$\partial=i\left(\frac{1}{c}R_{\vec e_x}\frac{\partial}{\partial t'}-\sum_k L_{\vec e_k}R_{\vec e_y}\frac{\partial}{\partial q_k}\right),$$

one sum, three of the four terms hidden. It now reads

$$\partial=\frac{i}{c}R_{\vec e_x}\frac{\partial}{\partial t'}-iL_{\vec e_x}R_{\vec e_y}\frac{\partial}{\partial q_x}-iL_{\vec e_y}R_{\vec e_y}\frac{\partial}{\partial q_y}-iL_{\vec e_z}R_{\vec e_y}\frac{\partial}{\partial q_z},$$

with a sentence after it stating that the four are read one by one and that the first is one-sided. The file went from one collapsed display to none.

Klein–Gordon from the Dirac Square in Biquaternionic Form. The operator was defined twice, both times collapsed. The first definition carried the index template itself, $C_\mu=iL_{e_\mu}^{\mathrm{axf}}R_{\vec e_{\nu(\mu)}}^{\mathrm{axf}}$ together with the table $\nu(t)=x$, $\nu(k)=y$; the second carried $-\sum_k L_{\vec e_k}R_{\vec e_y}\partial_{x_k}$. Both now print the four terms, and the table $\nu(t)=x$, $\nu(k)=y$ is gone from the definition. The file went from four collapsed displays to none.

Matrix Representations Conventions, the maths copy, and the physics copy of the same title. Each carried one sentence that defined a derivative contraction as a sum over $\mu$, $i\sum_\mu iL_{e_\mu}R_{\vec e_{\nu(\mu)}}\partial/\partial x_\mu$ and its $\mathbb{B}$-frame twin, with the four terms never printed. The sentence is now deleted from both files. What remains is the momentum cell sum, which prints its four terms, so the operator still has a written form. The physics copy lost three chains, the maths copy two, and neither carries one now.

What the repair costs. Nothing in either article's mathematics. The expansions were checked against the article's own square, $\partial^2=-\Box$ and $\mathbb{D}^2=\Box$, and against the cells' squares, $R_{\vec e_x}^2=-1$ and $(L_{\vec e_k}R_{\vec e_y})^2=+1$. What it costs is length: four terms where one sum stood, twice in one file.

What the repair buys. The defect becomes visible. A reader of the old line could believe that a single symbol stood for one operator; a reader of the new line sees four, sees that the time term multiplies on the right alone, and sees where the three minus signs are.

What Is Not Counted

A chain that names its coordinate and its two multipliers is not counted, because it is the repaired form. There are 7 in the published corpora and 44 in the internal file:

$$ iR_{\vec e_x}\partial_{x_0}+iL_{\vec e_x}R_{\vec e_y}\partial_{x_1}+iL_{\vec e_y}R_{\vec e_y}\partial_{x_2}+iL_{\vec e_z}R_{\vec e_y}\partial_{x_3}. $$

Four such chains appear in $M_4(\mathbb{C})$ Pauli Representation: Operators, in the $^{\mathrm{pau}}$ display, one per coordinate, and three in Klein–Gordon from the Dirac Square. Those two are the only files of either corpus that still write the shape, and they write it in the repaired form.

How the Count Is Made

The scan. A brace-aware walk over every .md of articles_physics, articles_maths and articles_internal. The walker matches a token $L_{...}$ or $L_{...}^{...}$, then an $R_{...}$ or $R_{...}^{...}$ with only spaces between, then a derivative: $\partial$, $\partial_{...}$, or $\frac{\partial}{\partial ...}$. A chain inside a braced group is matched as a whole, so a pair wrapped in $\bigl(\ \bigr)$ is not missed.

The classification. A chain is infested when the pair carries a summed index, $k$ or $\mu$ or $\nu(\cdot)$, or when the derivative carries no coordinate. A chain that names an explicit coordinate, $x_0$ to $x_3$, $q_x$ to $q_z$, $Q_t$ to $Q_z$, $t'$, is clean.

What the count is. A literal count of written chains, not of the operators behind them. Each occurrence is counted once, so a line holding the same chain three times counts three.

The floor. The count is a floor in two ways. A chain split across a line break with the derivative on the next line is missed; one such case was found by hand in Canonical Quantization and is included. A chain written with the derivative first, $\partial L_{a}R_{b}$, is a different order and is outside the definition.

Two corrections, in order. An earlier pass reported 18 articles and 42 occurrences, counting every chain of the shape and not only the infested ones. A later pass reported 12 articles and 27 occurrences for the first case. The repair pass then rewrote twenty-four published articles, and the same walk now finds 3 articles and 13. Each figure belongs to the state of the corpus on the day it was taken.

The second-case scan. A different walk, over the same three folders: every display of the file is taken as a unit, the $\sum$ tokens are read, and the summand is followed from the sum to its first top-level stop, a plus sign, a minus sign, a comma, an equals sign or a closing delimiter. The display counts once, under S, when a chain sits inside that summand, and under G only when the file defines the letter $G$ as the pair and the display then uses $G_\mu$ with a derivative. A sum whose summand carries $\pi_k$, or one multiplier without the second, is not the chain and is not counted; those rows are marked zero with the reason in the the form column.

The Repair

One term per coordinate. Write the four terms, or write the one term the sentence needs. Never a sum over a letter that stands for them.

Two multipliers, no index. $iL_{\vec e_x}R_{\vec e_y}$ and its three companions. No $\mu$, no $k$ in the pair.

A coordinate on every derivative. $\partial_{x_1}$, not $\partial$.

No display label on a product. The label belongs to a single array, not to a product of two multiplications.

The State of the Target Article

The Gradient, the D'Alembertian and the Frame Generators, the internal rewrite, holds 44 chains of the shape and none of them is infested. The two collapsed sums it still had, $\partial=iR_{\vec e_x}\partial_{x_0}-\sum_k iL_{\vec e_k}R_{\vec e_y}\partial_{x_k}$ and its repeat in the notation table, were expanded to the four terms in this pass.

LRD Infestation

Scope

This file is an inventory, not an article and not an entry of a menu. It counts one pattern in the two published corpora and gives a direct link to each article that carries it.

The pattern. A left multiplication, then a right multiplication, then a partial derivative:

$$ L_{a}R_{b}\,\partial . $$

The chain is written there as one symbol, with a summed index or a label instead of the terms it stands for. It is called LRD in this file, from its three letters.

Two cases, and only the first is in this file's first table. The chain can be collapsed in two ways. It is collapsed term by term, one symbol standing for the four, $L_{a}R_{b}\partial$; that is §The Physics Corpus and §The Maths Corpus, 35 occurrences in 17 articles. It is collapsed as a whole, the sum written over an index or a letter, $\partial=\sum_\mu\dots$; that is §The Second Case, a further 80 occurrences in 31 articles. The second set contains the first, so the union is 31 articles and 115 occurrences.

Why the second case is worse. The first case hides which coordinate the derivative acts on. The second hides the derivative, the four multipliers and the sign at once, and it does so in the place where the operator is defined. A reader who meets the definition cannot recover the operator at all.

The count, first case. 35 occurrences in 17 published articles: 25 in 15 physics articles and 10 in 2 maths articles. One internal file carried 41 chains of the same shape and now carries none of the infested kind.

The count, second case. 80 occurrences in 31 published articles: 70 in 29 physics articles and 10 in 2 maths articles.

The word. Neither table counts the verbal form. The word cell, which names the same collapse in prose, stands 3476 times in 122 physics articles and 1375 times in 165 maths articles.

The Token

The canonical form is

$$ iL_{e_\mu}^{\mathrm{axf}}R_{\vec e_{\nu(\mu)}}^{\mathrm{axf}}\partial , $$

and it is unreadable until three separate conventions are supplied: the table $\nu(t)=x$, $\nu(k)=y$; the display whose label sits in the caret; and a sentence stating which coordinate $\partial$ acts on. Then it expands to four operators, three of which the token never shows.

The repaired form names its coordinate and its two multipliers:

$$ iR_{\vec e_x}\partial_{x_0}+iL_{\vec e_x}R_{\vec e_y}\partial_{x_1}+iL_{\vec e_y}R_{\vec e_y}\partial_{x_2}+iL_{\vec e_z}R_{\vec e_y}\partial_{x_3}. $$

That is four occurrences of the shape, and none of them is infested. The defect is not the shape; it is the collapse.

Why the Collapse Is a Defect

It hides three of four terms. An index $k$ over $1,2,3$ or a table $\nu(\mu)$ stands for four operators, and the reader who wants any one of them must expand the symbol himself. 15 of the 35 occurrences hide their coordinate this way.

It hides the coordinate of the derivative. 20 occurrences write the derivative as a bare glyph, $L_{\vec e_k}R_{\vec e_y}\partial$, with no argument at all. Nothing in the expression says whether it acts on $x_0$, on $q_k$, or on $t'$. In Matrix Representations Conventions three such chains stand on one line, and the reader cannot tell whether they are one operator written three times or three different ones.

It smuggles in a frame. 25 of the 35 carry the caret label on both multipliers, so the same chain appears as $^{\mathrm{axf}}$ in one article and $^{\mathrm{pau}}$ in the next. A product of two multiplications is display-independent, so the label on a product is either redundant or false.

It reproduces the syntax it replaces. $\gamma_\mu$ is one index standing for four matrices. $L_{e_\mu}R_{\vec e_{\nu(\mu)}}\partial$ is one index standing for four operators. The shape is the same and so is the failure: a single letter that must be unpacked before it can be checked.

The Second Case: the Collapsed Contraction

The chain collapsed as a whole. Four written shapes:

A sum over an index. $\sum_\mu iL_{e_\mu}R_{\vec e_{\nu(\mu)}}\partial/\partial x_\mu$, or $\sum_k iL_{\vec e_k}R_{\vec e_y}\partial_{x_k}$, with the four terms never printed.

A single letter. $\partial=\dots$ closing on such a sum, which turns the collapse into a definition.

The letter $G_\mu$. $G_\mu=iL_{e_\mu}R_{\vec e_{\nu(\mu)}}$, used as $\sum_\mu G_\mu\partial/\partial x_\mu$, with the four hidden behind one index and the index function behind that.

The $\varepsilon_\mu$ sum, $\sum_\mu\varepsilon_\mu iL_{e_\mu}R_{\vec e_{\nu(\mu)}}\partial_\mu$, where the sign vector does the work the four explicit signs would do.

The Worst Single File

The Gradient, the D'Alembertian and the Cell Operator carries the collapse in all three of its forms at once: the letter $\partial$ defined by a sum, the letter $G_\mu$ used inside that sum, and the word cell 97 times. It is the file where the reader is meant to learn what the operator is, and it is the file where the most of it is hidden. Its internal rewrite holds none of the three.

The first set is a subset of the second: every article that collapses the chain term by term also collapses it as a whole somewhere else. No article carries the first kind alone. That is the shape of the defect, not a coincidence — a file that has learned the one-symbol-per-term habit writes its definitions the same way.

What Is Not Counted

A chain that names its coordinate and its two multipliers is not counted, because it is the repaired form. There are 7 in the published corpora and 41 in the internal file:

$$ iR_{\vec e_x}\partial_{x_0}+iL_{\vec e_x}R_{\vec e_y}\partial_{x_1}+iL_{\vec e_y}R_{\vec e_y}\partial_{x_2}+iL_{\vec e_z}R_{\vec e_y}\partial_{x_3}. $$

Four such chains appear in $M_4(\mathbb{C})$ Pauli Representation: Operators, in the $^{\mathrm{pau}}$ display, one per coordinate. That file is the model the others should follow and it is not on the list for that reason.

How the Count Is Made

The scan. A brace-aware walk over every .md of articles_physics, articles_maths and articles_internal. The walker matches a token $L_{...}$ or $L_{...}^{...}$, then an $R_{...}$ or $R_{...}^{...}$ with only spaces between, then a derivative: $\partial$, $\partial_{...}$, or $\frac{\partial}{\partial ...}$. A chain inside a braced group is matched as a whole, so a pair wrapped in $\bigl(\ \bigr)$ is not missed.

The classification. A chain is infested when the pair carries a summed index, $k$ or $\mu$ or $\nu(\cdot)$, or when the derivative carries no coordinate. A chain that names an explicit coordinate, $x_0$ to $x_3$, $q_x$ to $q_z$, $Q_t$ to $Q_z$, $t'$, is clean.

What the count is. A literal count of written chains, not of the operators behind them. Each occurrence is counted once, so a line holding the same chain three times counts three.

The floor. The count is a floor in two ways. A chain split across a line break with the derivative on the next line is missed; one such case was found by hand in Canonical Quantization and is included. A chain written with the derivative first, $\partial L_{a}R_{b}$, is a different order and is outside the definition.

A correction. An earlier pass reported 18 articles and 42 occurrences. That number counted every chain of the shape, including the 7 clean ones. The infested figure is 17 articles and 35 occurrences.

The Repair

One term per coordinate. Write the four terms, or write the one term the sentence needs. Never a sum over a letter that stands for them.

Two multipliers, no index. $iL_{\vec e_x}R_{\vec e_y}$ and its three companions. No $\mu$, no $k$ in the pair.

A coordinate on every derivative. $\partial_{x_1}$, not $\partial$.

No display label on a product. The label belongs to a single array, not to a product of two multiplications.

The State of the Target Article

The Gradient, the D'Alembertian and the Frame Generators, the internal rewrite, holds 41 chains of the shape and none of them is infested. The two collapsed sums it still had, $\partial=iR_{\vec e_x}\partial_{x_0}-\sum_k iL_{\vec e_k}R_{\vec e_y}\partial_{x_k}$ and its repeat in the notation table, were expanded to the four terms in this pass.

Its published copy, The Gradient, the D'Alembertian and the Cell Operator, still carries one occurrence, at line 412, and has not been regenerated from the rewrite.

Pauli and Gamma Infestation

Scope

This file is an inventory, not an article and not an entry of a menu. It counts every use of a Pauli matrix and of a gamma matrix that stands outside a legacy section, in the two published corpora, and gives a direct link to each article that carries one.

The rule counted here is the one of Matrix Representations Conventions, §The Rule on Pauli and Gamma Matrices: no Pauli matrix and no gamma matrix is written in the body of an article, and the two formulations appear in a legacy section only. A legacy section is allowed, and it is not counted. Four files are exempt and are counted nowhere: the two Matrix Representations Conventions articles, which state the arrays once; Why AXF Matrices instead of Pauli and Gamma Matrices, whose subject is the defect; and The Gamma Frame and the Complexified Dirac Algebra of $M_4(\mathbb{C})$, whose subject is the gamma frame.

How the Count Is Made

What counts. A token of the gamma family, $\gamma_0$ to $\gamma_5$, $\gamma_\mu$, $\gamma_\nu$, $\gamma_\lambda$, $\gamma_\rho$; a token of the Pauli family, $\sigma_x$, $\sigma_y$, $\sigma_z$, $\sigma_1$, $\sigma_2$, $\sigma_3$, $\sigma_i$, $\sigma_j$, $\sigma_k$. Each occurrence counts once, whether it stands in a display or in a line of text.

What is excluded. Anything inside a section whose heading holds the word legacy, at any depth, and anything inside a fenced code block. The heading is read as a state: a legacy section stays open until a heading of the same level or a higher one arrives.

Which articles are scanned. The articles of the two corpora that belong to the biquaternion programme, that is those that mention $\mathbb{B}$, the biquaternion, a Peirce object, $M_4(\mathbb{C})$ or one of the two sectors. This keeps out the general mathematics of the same folder, where $\sigma$ and $\gamma$ are other symbols: a braid generator $\sigma_i$ in Braid Groups, a divided power $\gamma_n$ in Divided Powers, a singular value $\sigma_1$ in the fractal articles.

The dagger. A row marked † holds no gamma token and never uses the word Pauli. Its $\sigma$ may belong to another subject, and the row is a suspicion and not a finding: the article is read before it is edited. 44 maths rows and 52 physics rows carry the dagger, and they hold 413 and 220 occurrences.

The count is a floor. A symbol built on an array without the array's own token, and a sentence that names a gamma matrix in words alone, are not counted. The true number is slightly higher than the tables give.

What Blocks the Work

The four generators have no gamma-free name. The chirality article was cleaned by naming the volume cell $\mathsf{V}^{\mathrm{vol}}$, and the same move is open for the four cells whose product it is. Until that name is fixed in Special Element and Peirce Conventions, every article that writes $\gamma_\mu$ waits.

Seven articles have the arrays as their subject. $M_2(\mathbb{C})$ Pauli Representation; $M_2(\mathbb{C})$ Pauli Representation : Operators; $M_4(\mathbb{C})$ Pauli Representation; $M_4(\mathbb{C})$ Pauli Representation : Operators; Exercise: The Non-Relativistic Limit and the Pauli Equation; The Pauli Correspondence and the Matrix Image; The Pauli Module of the Biquaternion Algebra.

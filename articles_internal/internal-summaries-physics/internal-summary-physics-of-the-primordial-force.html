3. The Connection: the Abelian Import and the Non-Abelian Import

The one local symmetry that costs nothing. The centre is a phase group, so making it local needs no enlargement of the carrier: the connection is a connection on a bundle the algebra already carries, and the field strength is its curvature. This is the abelian minimal-coupling principle, and it is intrinsic.

The machinery, in four steps. The covariant derivative as a derivation; local covariance and what it forces; the connection as an orbit; the curvature as the commutator of covariant derivatives, with the covariant square producing the curvature term.

$$ [D_{\imath},D_{\jmath}]=-ig\,F_{\imath\jmath}, \qquad \sum_{\text{cyclic}}[D_{\imath},[D_{\jmath},D_{k}]]=0\ \text{(Bianchi, from Jacobi)}. $$

The non-trivial claim. The Bianchi identity in the algebra is the Jacobi identity, and the covariant derivative on the curvature is two-sided, not one-sided — a one-sided derivative reproduces the abelian statement and fails the non-abelian one. The identities are not transcribed; only the topological integrality is.

The non-abelian import, and where the gap is. Above the representative algebra the connection is non-abelian, the field strength is not locally invariant, the adjoint law holds on all six components, and the Yang–Mills action is invariant for exactly one reason: the cyclicity of the matrix trace. The reality condition and the $ict$ direction are what make the imported object sit in the material sector.

$$ S_{\mathrm{YM}}=-\tfrac12\int d^{4}r\ \mathrm{Tr}\Bigl(\sum_{\imath,\jmath}F_{\imath\jmath}F_{\imath\jmath}\Bigr), \qquad \delta S=2\int d^{4}r\ \mathrm{Tr}\Bigl(\sum_{\jmath}\delta\mathcal A_{\jmath}\,D_{\imath}F_{\imath\jmath}\Bigr). $$

The non-trivial claim. The square is the only dimension-four invariant available, and the variation must use the adjoint covariant derivative — the integration by parts is where covariance is won or lost. The Noether current is bilinear in the connection because the connection carries the charge it couples to; the energy–momentum is symmetric and traceless, and its trace anomaly is the beta function of §4.

The non-trivial claim. The model has no mechanism of flavour at all; the measured three-column table is data, and its only mass statement, $m_{\ell}=3m_{q}$, is wrong by $27$. The second article tries the ladder and reports its failure in the corpus's own algebra: the ratio steps $206.77$ and $16.82$ shrink by $12.3$, and integer quanta miss by two and three orders of magnitude. Counted instead in the square root of the ratio, the steps $13.379$ and $58.968$ grow by $3.3326$, and what closes is Koide's relation,

$$ Q=\frac{1+\rho_2+\rho_3}{\left(1+\sqrt{\rho_2}+\sqrt{\rho_3}\right)^{2}}=\frac{2}{3}, \qquad \rho_2=\frac{m_\mu}{m_e},\quad \rho_3=\frac{m_\tau}{m_e}, $$

with no mass unit in the display — a statement about two ratios and nothing else. The closure is not derived, and the section files it as a numerical fact the model cannot explain.

Summary

What the part refuses. A derivation of the group's rank or of the number four; chirality above $M_C$; a second Yukawa; a seesaw or a neutrino mass; confinement in its own phase; an algebra invariant for the topological density. Each refusal is a stated boundary, and the boundaries are what make the one constructive licence usable.

Five formulas to keep.

$$ G=\mathrm{SU}(4),\ \dim15,\ \operatorname{rank}3,\ 15=12+3;\qquad 15=9+6,\quad 9=8+1,\quad 6=6;\qquad m^{2}=32v^{2}\ (\text{six}),\ 0\ (\text{nine}); $$

$$ b_0=\tfrac{26}{3},\quad \alpha_4(M_P)=0.0165;\qquad m_{\ell}=3m_{q};\qquad Q=\tfrac{2}{3}\ \text{(Koide, not derived)}. $$

Five results to keep. The fifteen is $16-1$, and tracelessness forces the corner, which is charge quantisation. One coupling, one connection, fifteen first-class constraints. The printed fifteen is not orthogonal: one Gram entry, $\langle d_{2},d_{3}\rangle=-1$, forces the rotation $c_{2},c_{3}$. No exponential of the fifteen is a boost, proved three ways. The model is vector-like by theorem, so the transition is the only place chirality can enter.

The ledger. The group, the count, the arrays, the rotors, the commutation rules, the classification and the action are built. The coupling's value is imported at $M_X$ and run upward. Flavour, the number of generations, the scalar's quartic values and the chirality are not derived. Every number that comes from experiment is marked as such, and the $885\ \mathrm{MeV}$ pole is arithmetic outside the theory's claim.

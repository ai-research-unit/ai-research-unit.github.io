Introduction

What this file is. A compressed map of the category Biquaternion Universe Quantum Fields, sixty-nine articles. It gives the objects, the boxed equations and the non-trivial claims. No proofs: an agent re-derives them. Every formula is copied from the source article named in the section.

What the category does. It quantises the fields of the two previous categories: the scalar, the Dirac, the Maxwell, the Proca, the Rarita–Schwinger and the graviton field. The algebra supplies the Fock space, the vacuum and the grading; the corpus supplies the standard machinery (Wick, Bogoliubov, path integrals, LSZ, BRST) rewritten in it.

The structural fact that organises the category. The vacuum is a minimal idempotent:

$$ \tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id},+}=\tfrac12\bigl(e_t+i\vec e_z\bigr)=|0\rangle\langle 0|,\qquad \tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id}}^2=\tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id}},\qquad \mathrm{rank}\,\mathsf M_2(\tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id}})\in\{0,1,2\}. $$

So "no particles" is not a vector added to the theory: it is a rank-one projection inside the algebra, and the Fock space is built on it:

$$ \mathcal F=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}\bigl(\mathcal H_1^{\otimes n}\bigr)_{\pm},\qquad |0\rangle\in\bigl(\mathcal H_1^{\otimes 0}\bigr)_{\pm}\cong\mathbb{C}. $$

The price, stated at once. An idempotent is a zero divisor. The vacuum state is therefore on the boundary of the cone, and every statement of the category that uses a polar decomposition, a logarithm or an inverse must say how it avoids the boundary. The corpus does this case by case, and the failures are collected in the last subsection.

3. Spin One Half

Quantisation, and the sea. The Dirac field is expanded in the two spinor solutions and their conjugates; the anticommutator is forced (section 1). The Dirac sea is the filled negative-energy branch, and a hole in it is an antiparticle.

Two interpretations, distinguished. The corpus keeps the historical hole theory (a filled sea, holes as antiparticles) apart from the modern resolution, which is in Canonical Quantization of the Biquaternion Dirac Field: the field is expanded in positive and negative frequencies, and if the modes commuted the energy would be unbounded below, so the anticommutator is what makes the ground state stable. The hole theory is then a picture, not a mechanism. An agent must not present the sea as the corpus's explanation.

Fermionic calculus. Grassmann coherent states, the fermionic path integral and the Feynman propagator are written in the algebra. The non-trivial claim is that the Grassmann variables are not elements of $\mathbb{B}$: the algebra is not graded-commutative, and the anticommuting parameters must be adjoined. The corpus is explicit about this boundary.

Fermion doubling. The Nielsen–Ninomiya theorem is recorded: a local, Hermitian, chirally invariant lattice Dirac operator has doubled species. In the algebra the doubling is the statement that the two sectors give two branches and the lattice cannot separate them.

Dirac matter. Graphene, the Dirac cones and the topological insulators are recorded as the physical systems whose low-energy description is the Dirac operator; the corpus states which of their features are algebra and which are lattice.

Jordan–Wigner and Kramers. The Jordan–Wigner transformation maps a chain of qubits to fermions; in the algebra it is the map from the tensor algebra to the exterior algebra. Kramers degeneracy is the statement that an anti-unitary involution squaring to $-1$ forces a double degeneracy — in the algebra, the involution is conjugation composed with $i$.

5. Informational Aspects

Modular structure.

$$ \mathcal M_{\mathrm{mod}}(\tilde Q_3)=e^{-\tilde H_{\Psi,\mathrm{modular}}}\tilde Q_3\,e^{\tilde H_{\Psi,\mathrm{modular}}},\qquad \tilde H_{\Psi,\mathrm{modular}}=-\log\tilde\varrho_\Psi, $$

$$ \tilde H_{\Psi,\mathrm{modular}}=-\tfrac12\log\frac{1-|\vec s_\Psi|^{2}}{4}\,e_t-i\,\mathrm{artanh}(|\vec s_\Psi|)\,\vec u_s . $$

The non-trivial claim. The modular Hamiltonian is $-\log$ of the state, and for the two-level state it has a closed form: a real scalar part and an imaginary vector part. The imaginary unit sits on the boost generator, so the modular flow is a boost. This is the algebraic content of Bisognano–Wichmann: the modular flow of a wedge is the boost that preserves it.

The null space and Reeh–Schlieder.

$$ \boxed{\;\mathcal N_{\tilde\varrho_\Psi}=\bigl\{\tilde Q_3:\tilde Q_3\tilde\varrho_\Psi=0\bigr\}=\bigl\{\tilde Q_3:\tilde Q_3\,\mathrm{range}(\tilde\varrho_\Psi)=0\bigr\}\;} $$

The non-trivial claim. The GNS null space is the right annihilator of the state. So the representation is the left regular one, and its faithfulness is the statement $\tilde Q_3\tilde\varrho_\Psi=0\Rightarrow\tilde Q_3=0$ — which fails exactly when the state is a zero divisor. Reeh–Schlieder is therefore conditional in this algebra: it holds for a faithful (non-boundary) state and fails for a minimal idempotent. The corpus states both cases.

Relative entropy, strong subadditivity, replica. These are recorded with their algebraic statements: relative entropy is the difference of two modular Hamiltonians; strong subadditivity is the monotonicity of the relative entropy under restriction; the replica trick turns the entropy into a partition function on a branched cover, and the area law follows.

The area law.

$$ \boxed{\;S_A=\kappa\frac{|\partial A|}{\epsilon^{d-1}}+c_{d-1}\frac{|\partial A|}{\epsilon^{d-2}}+\cdots+\text{(universal terms)}\;} $$

and, as a bound:

$$ \mathbb{B}^{\otimes N}\cong M_{2^{N}}(\mathbb{C}),\qquad \mathrm{rank}\,\rho_A\le2^{|\partial A|},\qquad S_A\le\log\mathrm{rank}\,\rho_A\le|\partial A|\log2 . $$

The non-trivial claim. The area law's coefficient is a counting statement: the rank of a reduced state is bounded by the number of boundary legs, because a cut through a tensor network cuts only the bonds that cross it. The algebra supplies the tensor structure $\mathbb{B}^{\otimes N}\cong M_{2^{N}}(\mathbb{C})$ and therefore the $\log2$ per bond.

Summary

The category in six formulas.

$$ |0\rangle\langle 0|=\tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id},+}=\tfrac12(e_t+i\vec e_z),\qquad \{\vec\Pi_{\mathrm{nil},x},\vec\Pi_{\mathrm{nil},y}\}=e_t,\qquad \mathrm{Sc}[\vec\Pi_{\mathrm{nil},x},\vec\Pi_{\mathrm{nil},y}]=0, $$

$$ T=\frac{h\kappa}{4\pi^{2} ck_B},\qquad T_{\imath}{}^{\imath}=\frac{c}{24\pi}R,\qquad S_A\le|\partial A|\log2 . $$

Five results to keep. The vacuum is a rank-one idempotent, hence a zero divisor, and that is why the boundary cases matter. Spin–statistics is forced by the vanishing scalar part of the odd commutator. The Casimir vacuum energy is the metric trace over four polarisations. The RG regulator needs the Euclidean pairing, which is the only positive one. The modular Hamiltonian is $-\log\varrho$ and its flow is the boost.

Three refusals. The category does not grade the algebra: Grassmann parameters are adjoined, not found. It does not claim Reeh–Schlieder unconditionally: it holds for faithful states only. It does not produce new numbers: it reproduces the standard ones.

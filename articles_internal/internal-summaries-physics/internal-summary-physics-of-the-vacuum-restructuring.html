The one-sentence version. One vacuum of one scalar breaks $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ to colour times $B-L$, eats six leptoquarks at $32v^{2}$, and leaves rank five only if the object that breaks the group also carries a doublet index — the one postulate of the whole chain.

1. The Vocabulary: Survives, Breaks, Arrives

One verb per object. Survives for a subalgebra the vacuum annihilates; breaks for a complement it does not commute with; arrives for a factor borne by an index. Rank is monotone downwards inside an algebra and additive across factors, with the ledger $3\to3$ at the breaking and $3\to5$ at the birth.

The name, settled once. The process is vacuum restructuring: one vacuum at one scale restructures the factor list of the effective theory. The word has to cover a loss and an acquisition at once, which is why symmetry breaking is refused as the umbrella and kept for the first operation; phase transition, condensation, emergent and duplication are refused for importing a potential, a mechanism, a composite and a copy.

The four dimensions that describe the relation. Span $12$, intersection $4=\operatorname{span}\{A_{xy},A_{xz},A_{yz},iD\}$, joint closure $15$, defect $3$. Why six keeps appearing: $8+1+6=8+1+3+3=15$, and closure is the whole difference.

The non-trivial claim. An index is what only a multiplet can carry, and that is why the restructuring needs a field with two indices rather than a cleverer condensate. The word "emergent" came from the composite route and was replaced because the route is closed by the two theorems of §8, not because the numbers changed.

1. The Ceiling: Three Questions, Three Answers

The separation. Three questions must be separated because they have different answers, and conflating them produces a spurious "problem".

  • What is the symmetry group of the fields that live in the algebra itself? The group that acts unitarily on $\mathbb{B}$ and on its defining module. Answer: $\mathrm{U}(2)$, that is $\mathrm{SU}(2)\times\mathrm{U}(1)$ up to finite identification.
  • What symmetry group can be embedded in a matrix algebra over $\mathbb{B}$? For $M_n(\mathbb{B})\cong M_{2n}(\mathbb{C})$ the intrinsic group is $\mathrm{U}(2n)$, and any compact group of dimension at most $4n^2$ with a faithful $2n$-dimensional representation embeds. $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ embeds for $n\ge2$.
  • Can the colour triplet be a module of the algebra? No. A module over $\mathbb{B}\cong M_2(\mathbb{C})$ has even complex dimension, and colour requires a complex three-dimensional space.

$$ [\mathbb B,\mathbb B]=\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb C),\qquad \dim_\mathbb R\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb C)=6, \qquad \mathbb B=\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb C)\oplus\mathbb C_{\mathbb B}, \qquad \mathrm{SU}(2)=\mathrm{span}_{\mathbb R}\{\vec e_x,\vec e_y,\vec e_z\}\subset\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}, $$

$$ \boxed{\;\mathrm{U}(2)=\mathbb{R}(ie_t)\oplus\mathrm{SU}(2),\qquad \dim_\mathbb{R}\mathrm{U}(2)=4,\qquad \dim_\mathbb{R}\mathrm{SU}(3)=8\;} $$

The non-trivial claim. The commutators of the quaternion units give the imaginary quaternions, and the commutators mixing one factor of $i$ with one quaternion unit give their Hermitian partners, so the derived algebra is $\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{C})$ of real dimension six and the centre is one more. The compact part of that is four-dimensional, and the eight dimensions $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ needs are not there and cannot be got by any choice of basis, because every compact subalgebra of a real Lie algebra sits in a maximal compact subalgebra, and the maximal compact of $\mathrm{GL}(2,\mathbb{C})$ has dimension four. The module count is the second, independent wall: an even complex dimension can never be three.

What the answer means, and what it does not. The algebra's intrinsic group is the rotation group of space twice over — the double cover of $\mathrm{SO}(3)$ inside the material sector, plus one central phase. It is not an internal $\mathrm{SU}(3)$, and the number eight versus four is a count, not a coincidence to be worked around. What the theorem does not say is that no theory with colour can be written in biquaternion notation: a colour group can always be tensored on, and §3 states the price.

One member without a block. the-compact-sector-of-the-biquaternions-is-u2 states the same ceiling as a statement about the operators: the compact sector is $\mathfrak{u}(2)$, and no $\mathfrak{su}(3)$ sits among them. It is the theorem in its shortest form, so the section's block stands and this article is named.

2. What the Ceiling Is Not: The Three False Routes

The tempting identifications, all false. The article names them so that they are not mistaken for a route.

  • "Three letters, so three colours." The three letters are the adjoint of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$: $\dim\mathrm{SU}(2)=3$ is the adjoint dimension. Colour needs three fundamental complex dimensions, and the fundamental of $\mathbb{B}$ is $\mathbb{C}^2$.
  • "$\mathbb{B}$ has eight real dimensions, and $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ has eight." A match of counts between an algebra and a group, where one is the carrier and the other the symmetry acting on it. Wrong slot, wrong object.
  • "The octonions contain $\mathrm{SU}(3)$." True and irrelevant unless the carrier is non-associative: $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{O})=G_2$, $\dim G_2=14$, $G_2\supset\mathrm{SU}(3)$, and the price is associativity, which every construction in the corpus uses.

The genuine route, and its price. Take the matter field to be $\tilde\Xi\otimes c$ with $c\in\mathbb{C}^3$, let $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ act on $c$ and $\mathbb{B}$ on $\tilde\Xi$, and build an $\mathfrak{su}(3)$-valued connection on the colour factor. That constructs a theory with colour, and it is exactly the move through which the corpus already passes once — the enlargement to $M_n(\mathbb{B})$, whose intrinsic group is $\mathrm{U}(4)$ for $n=2$ and which contains $\mathrm{SU}(3)$. The price is that the algebra then no longer supplies the symmetry group: the group is chosen, put in by hand, and the algebra acts only on the spectator factor. The framework stops being a framework and becomes a notation for the Standard Model. That is the honest statement of the boundary.

What would settle it. A theorem of one of two forms, and either outcome is a result: a no-go that forbids the colour group from any carrier the algebra can build, or a construction that produces it without an added factor.

3. Instantons: The Algebra's Own Sector, and Where It Stops

The topological charge, which the algebra writes in the abelian case.

$$ \boxed{\;Ϙ=\frac{1}{8\pi^2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^4}\mathrm{Tr}\!\left(F\wedge F\right)=\frac{1}{8\pi^2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^4}d^4r\;\frac{1}{4}\epsilon_{{\imath}{\jmath}\rho\sigma}\mathrm{Tr}\!\left(F_{{\imath}{\jmath}}F_{\rho\sigma}\right),\qquad Ϙ\in\mathbb{Z}\;} $$

$$ \boxed{\;S\ge\frac{8\pi^2}{g^2}\,|Ϙ|\;}, \qquad ϙ(x)=\frac{1}{4}\epsilon_{{\imath}{\jmath}\rho\sigma}\mathrm{Tr}\!\left(F_{{\imath}{\jmath}}F_{\rho\sigma}\right)=\frac12\mathrm{Tr}\!\left(\sum_{{\imath},{\jmath}}F_{{\imath}{\jmath}}\star F_{{\imath}{\jmath}}\right). $$

The affirmative finding, and it is not small. In the Maxwell sector the topological density is not foreign to the framework's invariant calculus: the imaginary part of the biquaternion norm is the Pontryagin density up to a constant,

$$ \sum_{{\imath},{\jmath}}F_{{\imath}{\jmath}}\star F_{{\imath}{\jmath}}=\frac{4i}{c}\,I_2\ \ (\text{Lorentzian}),\qquad \sum_{{\imath},{\jmath}}F_{{\imath}{\jmath}}\star F_{{\imath}{\jmath}}=4\,I_2\ \ (\text{Euclidean}),\qquad I_2=\vec E\cdot\vec B . $$

So the abelian instanton does not need a new object, and the BPST instanton itself is built inside the compact factor $\mathrm{SU}(2)=\mathrm{span}_\mathbb{R}\{\vec e_x,\vec e_y,\vec e_z\}\subset\mathbb{B}_\Phi$, with the Bogomolny bound fixing its action.

The negative finding, which is the article's real content. The non-abelian density needs two things the abelian one does not: the wedge of two algebra-valued two-forms, and a matrix trace over the representative algebra. The wedge is available. The trace is not. The biquaternion norm is a rank-two bilinear of a single algebra element: it pairs $\tilde F$ with $\tilde F^{\natural}$ through the quaternion product and produces one scalar, using its one algebra slot. The non-abelian density pairs two representative-algebra-valued objects in a trace, and the norm has no second slot in which to do that.

The evidence, stated as the article states it. Under a local transformation with $\tilde U\in\mathrm{SU}(2)$ the components transform as $F_{{\imath}{\jmath}}\mapsto\tilde UF_{{\imath}{\jmath}}\tilde U^{-1}$, which does not extend to a conjugation of $\mathcal F$, because $\tilde U^{-1}$ does not commute with the basis elements $\bar e_{\imath} e_{\jmath}$:

$$ \mathcal{F}\;\longmapsto\;\mathcal{F}_2=\tfrac12\sum_{{\imath}{\jmath}}\left(\tilde UF_{{\imath}{\jmath}}\tilde U^{-1}\right)\bar{e}_{\imath} e_{\jmath}\;\neq\;\tilde U\,\mathcal{F}\,\tilde U^{-1}. $$

The norm of the representative is therefore not locally invariant: $17.1838\to17.3970$ on a random $\mathrm{SU}(2)$-valued two-form, while the matrix-trace density was invariant to machine precision. The invariant content of the non-abelian representative is the matrix trace, and the biquaternion norm does not supply it. This is where the framework's rank-two, single-slot limitation meets the topological density — the same limitation recorded for the electromagnetic energy–momentum tensor — and it is left as a gap, not closed by forcing a construction.

4. ADHM: The One Construction Whose Native Language Is This One

The bijection.

$$ \bigl\{\text{self-dual connections on }\mathbb{R}^4\text{ of charge }k\bigr\}\big/\text{connection} \;\longleftrightarrow\; \mu^{-1}(0)/\mathrm{U}(k), $$

$$ F_{12}=F_{34},\qquad F_{13}=-F_{24},\qquad F_{14}=F_{23}. $$

The non-trivial claim. The ADHM construction is unusual among the results of connection theory in that its natural language is already the language of the framework: the self-duality equation on $\mathbb{R}^4$ is a quaternionic equation, the data are quaternionic, and the moment map $\mu$ is the quaternionic moment map — three real components of $\mu_\mathbb{R}$ and the complex equation $\mu_\mathbb{C}$ together have $k^2+2k^2=3k^2$ real components, which is the real dimension of $\mathrm{U}(k)\otimes\mathrm{Im}\,\mathbb{H}$. The quotient by $\mathrm{U}(k)$ is a hyperkähler quotient, and the statement that four equations are the components of one quaternionic equation is what gives the construction its structure. The moduli dimension is $8k$ for $\mathrm{SU}(2)$, of which $8k-3$ is physical, and both figures come from the quotient rather than from counting by hand.

The three real components of $\mu_\mathbb{R}$ and the complex $\mu_\mathbb{C}$ are one quaternionic equation. That sentence is the whole reason the part can claim something here that other parts of the corpus import: the algebra is not decoration on ADHM, it is the type in which ADHM is written.

5. The Gas: Three Items the Algebra Owns, One It Does Not

$$ Z=\int\mathcal{D}\mathcal{A}\;\exp\bigl(-S[\mathcal{A}]\bigr). $$

The non-trivial claim, in four items. The saddle is the framework's: the self-dual connection is the BPST instanton built in $\mathrm{SU}(2)\subset\mathbb{B}_\Phi$, and its action is fixed by the Bogomolny bound. The density is the framework's in the abelian case and the matrix trace otherwise: in the abelian sector it is proportional to the invariant $I_2=\vec E\cdot\vec B$, in the non-abelian one it is the trace, as §3 states. The moduli are the framework's: the collective-coordinate measure integrates over the ADHM moduli space, whose dimension $8k$ and physical dimension $8k-3$ come from the quaternionic quotient of §4, so the integration measure factorises into the four collective coordinates of position and scale and the rest, and the quaternion structure is what makes the factorisation explicit. The dynamics is imported: the measure, the Faddeev–Popov procedure, the one-loop determinants, the beta function and the numerical prefactors are quantum field theory — the algebra is a complexified classical structure and has no path integral of its own.

Why the boundary is drawn here and not elsewhere. Because a reader can check it: the saddle, the density, the moduli and the measure are four separable items, and the first three are the algebra's while the fourth is not. A claim that the whole gas is "derived" would be false at exactly one of the four.

6. Theta, Strong CP, and What the Algebra Cannot Value

$$ \boxed{\;S_\theta=\frac{\theta_{\mathrm{vac}}}{32\pi^2}\int\sum_{{\imath},{\jmath}}F_{{\imath}{\jmath}}F_{\mathrm{dual},{\imath}{\jmath}},\qquad F_{\mathrm{dual},{\imath}{\jmath}}=\tfrac12\sum_{\rho,\sigma}\epsilon_{{\imath}{\jmath}\rho\sigma}F_{\rho\sigma}\;} $$

$$ \tfrac14\sum_{{\imath},{\jmath},\rho,\sigma}\epsilon_{{\imath}{\jmath}\rho\sigma}F_{{\imath}{\jmath}}F_{\rho\sigma} =\tfrac12\sum_{{\imath},{\jmath}}F_{{\imath}{\jmath}}F_{\mathrm{dual},{\imath}{\jmath}}=\frac{2i}{c}\,I_2\quad(\text{Lorentzian}),\qquad I_2=\vec E\cdot\vec B, \qquad Ϙ=\frac{1}{8\pi^2}\int\mathrm{Tr}\bigl(F\wedge F\bigr)\in\mathbb{Z}. $$

The non-trivial claim. The framework supplies the theta term's density, its parity-odd character as the invariant $I_2$, its periodicity, and its role in the vacuum. It does not supply the value of $\theta_{\mathrm{vac}}$, and the reason is structural rather than technical: $\theta_{\mathrm{vac}}$ is not an element of the algebra, it is a coefficient in the action, and the algebra is a complexified classical structure with no action principle that would fix such a coefficient. A framework that could produce $\theta$ would have to be a framework with a dynamics.

The one structural contribution, and it is exact. The axial rotation is the pair of opposite phases on the two values, and the mass is the term that couples them. The algebra makes the structure visible at no cost — two values, one coupling, one phase difference; no grading, no second unit, no side. The contrast that matters is with the central phase: it multiplies the two values alike, so it cannot rotate the mass, and that is the same letter law as in the electroweak part seen from the other side. Note the sign convention: $\vec E\cdot\vec B$ is what the algebra's own invariant supplies, and in Lorentzian signature the pseudoscalar density is $2i/c$ times it.

The measurement that stands over it. $|\bar\theta|\lesssim10^{-10}$, and the neutron EDM is the observable that enforces it. The framework records the bound and has no mechanism that would explain the smallness — that is the strong CP problem in this part's terms: a coefficient the algebra cannot see, bounded by an experiment the algebra does not predict.

$$ m_a=5.79\ \mu\mathrm{eV}\times\frac{10^{12}\ \mathrm{GeV}}{f_a},\qquad g_{a\gamma\gamma}=\frac{\alpha}{2\pi f_a}\left(\frac{E}{N}-1.92\right),\qquad \Omega_a h^2\sim0.1\left(\frac{f_a}{10^{12}\ \mathrm{GeV}}\right)^{7/6}, \qquad \bar\theta_{\text{eff}}=\bar\theta+\frac{a}{f_a}. $$

The non-trivial claim. The Peccei–Quinn symmetry is the central phase, and the algebra carries it without any enlargement: the shift $a\mapsto a+2\pi f_a$ is a rotation of $\mathrm{U}(1)_{\mathbb{B}}$, the potential depends only on $\bar\theta+a/f_a$, and the minimum sits at $\bar\theta_{\text{eff}}=0$ — which is the mechanism, and it is dynamical relaxation, not a cancellation by hand. The non-trivial claim, sigma-model side. The non-trivial claim, pion side. 9. Skyrme: Winding Is Baryon Number

11. The Multiplets Without Colour: Bookkeeping and Gell-Mann–Okubo

15. The Hadrons: Representation, Not Derivation

The framework contributes representation, and the boundary is stated before the list. A proton is a structureless spin-$\tfrac12$ biquaternion Dirac field with a charge operator; a neutron is the same with zero charge; and the algebra is simple, with one class of simple carrier, so every structureless spin-$\tfrac12$ field is an element of the same algebra with the same two values. The algebra contains no discriminator between the two: what makes the proton a proton is its charge, and what makes both of them hadrons is outside.

The four-part claim about the mass, which is the part's best single paragraph.

$$ 9.0\ \text{MeV (current quark masses)}\quad\text{against}\quad 938.3\ \text{MeV (the proton)} . $$

  • The mass is not a sum of masses: $9.0$ against $938.3$ MeV, and the difference is the whole phenomenon.
  • The mass is the conformal anomaly: a classical theory with no dimensionful parameter has a traceless energy–momentum tensor, the quantum theory does not, and the trace is proportional to the beta function.
  • The mass is therefore non-perturbative in a precise sense: $\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ depends on the coupling as $e^{-2\pi/b_0\alpha_s}$, which has an essential singularity at $\alpha_s=0$, so no finite order and no sum of finite orders produces it.

Three exist, and the sharp statement is narrower than "no action". The three, and what each settles.

  • The Lagrangian and the Nonlinear Sigma Model — $S=-\frac{f^{2}}{2}\int d^{4}r\,\mathrm{Sc}\bigl(\bar j_{\imath} j_{\imath}\bigr)$ with $j_{\imath}=\tilde U^{\natural}\partial_{\imath}\tilde U$ and $\tilde U\tilde U^{\natural}=e_t$.   • The Lagrangian and the Skyrme Model — $S=\int d^{4}r\,[-\frac{f^{2}}{2}\mathrm{Sc}(\bar j_{\imath} j_{\imath})+\frac{1}{16e^{2}}\mathrm{Sc}([j_{\imath},j_{\jmath}][j_{\imath},j_{\jmath}])]$. The pairing, which here is the scalar part and not the trace. The two chiral actions pair with the real scalar part, and the pairing is forced rather than chosen: $j_{\imath}$ is anti-Hermitian, so $\mathrm{Sc}(\bar j_{\imath} j_{\imath})=-\mathrm{Sc}(j_{\imath}^{\dagger}j_{\imath})$ is the negative of a Hermitian norm, real with no condition imposed, and the same argument carries the Skyrme commutator because the commutator of two anti-Hermitian elements is anti-Hermitian.

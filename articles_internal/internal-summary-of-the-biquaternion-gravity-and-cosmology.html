Internal Summary of the Biquaternion Gravity and Cosmology

Introduction

What this file is. A compressed map of the category Biquaternion Gravity and Cosmology, eleven articles, the last category of the physics menu. It gives the objects, the boxed equations and the non-trivial claims. No proofs: an agent re-derives them.

What the category does, and what it refuses to do. It carries gravity: the algebra supplies a frame field, hence a metric, hence a spin connection, hence a Dirac operator on a curved background. It transcribes the linearised sector and the black-hole thermodynamics. It does not derive the Einstein field equations, and three of the eleven articles are agendas whose whole content is that separation.

The discipline of the category, stated once. Every item is classified three ways: established (proved inside the corpus), carried (written in the letters, not derived from them), open (no construction exists). The gravitational agenda says it plainly: even the linearised sector has been carried and transcribed rather than derived.

The one structural fact that makes the category possible.

$$ \boxed{\;g_{\mu\nu}(x)=\bigl\langle\tilde E_{\Phi,\mu}(x),\tilde E_{\Phi,\nu}(x)\bigr\rangle_{\natural}\;} $$

The non-trivial claim. A metric is a Gram matrix of a frame of four $\mathbb{B}_\Phi$ elements. So the algebra carries any Lorentzian metric, at the price of a frame field: the metric is not produced by the algebra, it is encoded in four algebra-valued functions. This is the precise sense in which the framework reaches gravity — as a kinematical carrier, not as a dynamical theory.

1. The Kinematical Route

The frame field and the metric. Given $\tilde E_{\Phi,\mu}$ with $\langle\tilde E_{\Phi,\mu},\tilde E_{\Phi,\nu}\rangle_{\natural}=g_{\mu\nu}$, the covariant derivative on algebra-valued fields is

$$ \boxed{\;D_\mu\tilde Q=\partial_\mu\tilde Q+\vec\Gamma_\mu\tilde Q+\tilde Q\vec\Gamma_\mu^{*}\;} $$

and the curved Dirac operator obeys the Lichnerowicz identity inside the algebra:

$$ \boxed{\;\bigl(\tilde F_\mu D_\mu\bigr)^{2}=-\Box_{g,\Phi}+\tfrac14\mathrm{Ric}\;} $$

$$ \tilde F_\mu D_\mu\tilde\Xi_R=k_m\tilde\Xi_L,\qquad \bigl(\tilde F_\mu D_\mu\bigr)^{\natural}\tilde\Xi_L=k_m\tilde\Xi_R . $$

The non-trivial claim. The identity $(\gamma\!\cdot\!D)^2=-\Box+\tfrac14R$ is normally a statement about matrices and a Clifford algebra. Here the square is taken in $\mathbb{B}$, the Laplacian is the Laplace–Beltrami operator of $g$, and the Ricci scalar enters with the same coefficient $\tfrac14$. The algebra therefore reproduces the curvature coupling of a Dirac field, which is what makes the frame route physically meaningful rather than formal.

Torsion and contorsion.

$$ T_{\lambda\mu\nu}=\Gamma_{\lambda\mu\nu}-\Gamma_{\lambda\nu\mu},\qquad \Gamma_{\lambda\mu\nu}=\Bigl\{{}_{\lambda\mu\nu}\Bigr\}+N_{\mathrm{cont},\lambda\mu\nu}, $$

$$ \boxed{\;\nabla_\mu g_{\nu\rho}=-\bigl(g_{\nu\lambda}N_{\mathrm{cont},\lambda\mu\rho}+g_{\rho\lambda}N_{\mathrm{cont},\lambda\mu\nu}\bigr)\;} $$

The non-trivial claim. Metric compatibility is equivalent to an algebraic symmetry of the contorsion: the connection is metric-compatible exactly when $N_{\mathrm{cont}}$ is antisymmetric in its first and third indices. For totally antisymmetric torsion the contorsion is half the torsion, $N_{\mathrm{cont}}=\tfrac12T$, and the spin connection is fixed by the frame and the contorsion together.

The Newman–Penrose tetrad is the idempotent structure.

$$ \boxed{\;l=\sqrt2\,i\,\tilde\Pi_1,\qquad n=\sqrt2\,i\,\tilde\Pi_2,\qquad m=\sqrt2\,i\,x,\qquad \bar m=\sqrt2\,i\,y\;} $$

$$ \tilde\Pi_1=\tfrac12(e_t+i\vec e_z),\quad \tilde\Pi_2=\tfrac12(e_t-i\vec e_z),\quad \vec\Upsilon_1=\tfrac12(i\vec e_x-\vec e_y),\quad \vec\Upsilon_2=\tfrac12(i\vec e_x+\vec e_y). $$

The non-trivial claim, and it is the sharpest in the category. The null tetrad of Newman–Penrose is the pair of minimal idempotents plus the pair of nilpotents:

$$ \langle l,n\rangle_{*}=(\sqrt2 i)^{2}\mathrm{Sc}\bigl(\tilde\Pi_1\bar{\tilde\Pi}_2\bigr)=-2\,\mathrm{Sc}\bigl(\tilde\Pi_1^{2}\bigr)=-1 . $$

So null directions, the light cone, the vacuum of the quantum category and the tetrad of the classical one are one and the same object. The null and orthogonality relations $l\cdot l=n\cdot n=m\cdot m=0$, $l\cdot n=-1$, $m\cdot\bar m=+1$ are then faces of $\tilde\Pi^{2}=\tilde\Pi$ and $\vec\Upsilon^{2}=0$, not independent axioms.

Curvature from a field-valued light speed. The corpus records one exact curved family:

$$ d(ir'_t)=i\,c\,dt'+i\,t'\,dc,\qquad ds^{2}=-c^{2}dt'^{2}-2ct'\,dt'\,dc-t'^{2}(dc)^{2}+dr_x^{2}+dr_y^{2}+dr_z^{2}, $$

whose coefficient tuple in $(t',r_x,r_y,r_z)$ is $(-c^{2},1,1,1)$ and whose Ricci components are second derivatives of $w=c^{2}$.

The non-trivial claim. If the light speed is a function of position, the metric is genuinely curved, with $g_{ij}=\delta_{ij}$, no shift vector and one free function. The class is narrow, and the corpus states its width exactly: one free function, flat spatial slices, no rotation. Whether this family is physically the right one is not claimed.

2. The Linearised Sector

The expansion and the gauge freedom.

$$ g_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{\mu\nu}+h_{\mu\nu},\qquad h_{\mu\nu}\mapsto h_{\mu\nu}-\partial_\mu\zeta_\nu-\partial_\nu\zeta_\mu, $$

$$ \bar h_{00}=h_{00}+\tfrac12h,\quad \bar h_{0\eta}=h_{0\eta},\quad \bar h_{\eta l}=h_{\eta l}-\tfrac12h\,\delta_{\eta l},\quad \bar h=-\bar h_{00}+\sum_\eta\bar h_{\eta\eta}. $$

The wave equation.

$$ \boxed{\;\Box_\Phi\bar h_{\mu\nu}=-\frac{16\pi G}{c^{4}}T_{\mu\nu},\qquad\text{and in vacuum}\qquad \Box_\Phi\bar h_{\mu\nu}=0\;} $$

The residual gauge.

$$ \bar h_{00}\mapsto\bar h_{00}-2\partial_0\zeta_0-D,\qquad \bar D_\nu\mapsto\bar D_\nu-\Box_\Phi\zeta_\nu, \qquad \boxed{\;\Box_\Phi\zeta_\nu=0\;} $$

The non-trivial claim. The harmonic gauge leaves a residual freedom whose parameters are themselves solutions of the wave equation. So the physical content is a quotient: two metrics are the same state when they differ by a harmonic vector. The counting of polarisations — two for a massless spin-two field — is the dimension of that quotient, and the corpus derives it from the residual gauge rather than asserting it.

Waves on the frame. The perturbation of the frame field is

$$ \bar{\delta\tilde E_{\Phi}}_\mu=\delta\tilde E_{\Phi,\mu}-\tfrac14h\,\varepsilon_\mu,\qquad h=2\bigl\langle\varepsilon_\nu,\delta\tilde E_{\Phi,\nu}\bigr\rangle_{\natural},\qquad \Box_\Phi\,\bar{\delta\tilde E_{\Phi}}_\mu=0 . $$

The non-trivial claim. The wave equation holds for the frame perturbation, not only for the metric perturbation, and the trace part is subtracted in the same way. A gravitational wave is therefore a propagating congruence of frames, and its two polarisations are the two ways the congruence can shear.

3. Black Holes

$$ \boxed{\;T=\frac{\kappa}{2\pi},\qquad S=\frac{A}{4G}\;} $$

$$ dM=\frac{\kappa}{8\pi G}dA+\Omega\,dJ+\Xi\,dϘ,\qquad M=\frac{\kappa A}{4\pi G}+2\Omega J+\Xi\,Ϙ, $$

$$ T_{\mathrm H}=\frac{\hbar\kappa}{2\pi ck_B}=\frac{\hbar c^{3}}{8\pi GMk_B},\qquad S=\frac{c^{3}k_BA}{4\hbar G}=\frac{k_BA}{4\ell_P^{2}},\qquad \ell_P^{2}=\frac{\hbar G}{c^{3}}. $$

The non-trivial claim. The first law and the Smarr formula are the same equation: the Smarr relation is the first law integrated with the homogeneity of the mass. In the corpus this is not an analogy but an identity, because $M$ is homogeneous of degree one in $(A,J,Ϙ)$ and the coefficients are the conjugate potentials. The factor $\tfrac14$ in the entropy is the factor that no classical argument fixes; the corpus states it as input from the quantum category (Hawking's temperature) and does not derive it here.

4. The Three Agendas

The field-equation agenda. Its question is whether the framework reproduces the Einstein equations. Its answer is a separation: the kinematical route is established (any metric can be carried), the linearised sector is carried and transcribed, the non-linear equations are open. The sharper finding is that no action for the metric has been produced inside the algebra: the frame field is constrained by the Gram relation, and the constraint has not been shown to follow from a variational principle.

The cosmology agenda. Its question is what it would take to carry FLRW, the Friedmann equations, the equation of state and the growth of structure. Its answer is the same three-way classification, applied construction by construction. The non-trivial claim it records: a spatially flat FLRW metric is in the frame route, because it is diagonal with one free function of time — so the carrier exists and what is missing is the dynamics, not the kinematics.

The quantum-gravity agenda. Its question is downstream: quantisation presupposes a classical theory with an action, a conjugate momentum and a constraint structure, and the gravity series supplies none of the three. The non-trivial claim is that this is not a technical gap but an ordering one: the category cannot quantise gravity before the field-equation agenda produces an action, and the agenda says so.

The two remaining articles. An External Construction of Space from Quaternionic Spinors and The Biquaternion Basis of a Unified-Field Fibre Bundle record the two ways the algebra has been used to build spacetime rather than to live on it. They are recorded as constructions, not as derivations.

Summary

The category in six formulas.

$$ g_{\mu\nu}=\bigl\langle\tilde E_{\Phi,\mu},\tilde E_{\Phi,\nu}\bigr\rangle_{\natural},\qquad \bigl(\tilde F_\mu D_\mu\bigr)^{2}=-\Box_{g,\Phi}+\tfrac14\mathrm{Ric},\qquad \Box_\Phi\bar h_{\mu\nu}=-\frac{16\pi G}{c^{4}}T_{\mu\nu}, $$

$$ l=\sqrt2 i\,\tilde\Pi_1,\qquad n=\sqrt2 i\,\tilde\Pi_2,\qquad T=\frac{\kappa}{2\pi},\qquad S=\frac{A}{4G}. $$

Five results to keep. The metric is the Gram matrix of a frame, so the algebra carries any Lorentzian geometry. The curved Dirac operator satisfies Lichnerowicz inside the algebra, with the $\tfrac14$ coefficient. The Newman–Penrose null tetrad is exactly the two idempotents and the two nilpotents. The linearised equations are carried and the residual gauge is harmonic. Black-hole thermodynamics is transcribed, and the $\tfrac14$ is not derived.

Three refusals. The category does not derive the Einstein equations. It does not produce an action for the metric. It does not quantise gravity, and it states the ordering reason.

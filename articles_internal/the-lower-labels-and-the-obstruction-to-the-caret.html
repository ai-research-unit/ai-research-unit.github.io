The Lower Labels and the Obstruction to the Caret

Introduction: The Question

The corpus now writes every label of three letters in lowercase and puts the label of an object on the caret, $\mathbb{B}^{\mathrm{opp}}$, $L_x^{\mathrm{axf}}$, $E_0^{\mathrm{wkb}}$. The question of this note is the one that follows: the low slot still holds three-letter upright words, and can they be moved up?

The census says no for most of them. A three-letter upright word follows a low mark 3,917 times, in 85 distinct words, spread over the mathematical, the physical and the internal corpus. Four migrations have taken words out of it: fifteen labels, 74 occurrences, now on the caret and in the table of Index, Notation and Corpus Conventions, §The Three-Letter Labels; the twelve codes of the algebraic structures, 146; thirteen qualifiers that already named their object on the caret, 244; and six words that carried two meanings apiece, 187, which took a new code and then rose. The 85 that remain stay, and this note says why, category by category.

The census counts a word of exactly three letters written upright, in $\mathrm{}$, $\operatorname{}$ or $\text{}$, immediately after a low mark. It reads the mathematical, the physical and the internal articles, counts occurrences and not distinct words, and leaves this note out of its own count. Each family below is given as a table of four columns: the label, how often it occurs, what it means, and, where the glyph is worth naming, the letter it hangs on.

The four categories are not four degrees of difficulty. One is an obstruction of notation, one is an obstruction of the glyph, one is the case rule, and one is a word that names no object.

The Indexed Label

This is the largest family and the hardest. Sixteen words carry 3,109 occurrences, and 933 of them stand in a field of two parts, the label and an index: $s_{\mathrm{tot},z}$, $\tilde{K}_{\mathrm{mod},W}$, $\tilde H_{\mathrm{pos},l}$, $K_{\mathrm{rad}\,\mu}$.

Why it cannot rise. The low slot takes a comma-separated list, so a label and an index share one field and one size. The caret takes one field. Move the label up and the index is left behind: $\tilde S^{\mathrm{tot}}{}_{,k}$, with an empty base and a comma that has nothing to separate. The form is legal TeX and unreadable at reading size. It also breaks the rule that a mark has one job, since the low slot would then hold a bare index with no label to belong to, and the reader could no longer tell an index from a label without looking up the table.

What it would cost. 933 rewrites, every one of them introducing a form the conventions do not otherwise admit, and a second exception in §The Subscripts, which would have to say that a low slot may hold an index whose label is above.

The Part and the Role

Thirty-five words, 413 occurrences. The glyph is a complete object and the low word names a piece of it: the interaction action $S_{\mathrm{int}}$, the nuclear norm $\lVert A\rVert_{\mathrm{nuc}}$, the torsion submodule $M_{\mathrm{tor}}$, the vacuum energy $E_{\mathrm{vac}}$, the solar splitting $m^2_{\mathrm{sol}}$.

Why it cannot rise. The two slots say different things, and the difference is not typographic. The low slot binds to the glyph and reads of: $S_{\mathrm{int}}$ is the action of the interaction, an action that is otherwise unremarkable. The caret asserts and reads is: $f^{\mathrm{sym}}$ in this corpus is the symmetrised object itself, $f^{\mathrm{sym}} = \tfrac12(f(x,y)+f(y,x))$. Moved up, $S^{\mathrm{int}}$ would claim that the action is itself an interaction, which is a different mathematical statement and, in most of these cases, a false one.

The same argument reaches the operator names that sit low because a bar or a sum takes the low slot: the restriction $T|_{\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma)}$, the column space $\Pi_{\operatorname{col}A}$, the cyclic sum $\sum_{\mathrm{cyc}}$. Here the word is an operator with an argument, and the low slot belongs to the bar or the summation sign, not to the word.

The Word Already on the Caret

Seven words, 211 occurrences. The caret holds 87 distinct three-letter words and the low slot 85, and 15 words sit in both; seven of the 15 are this family, and the other eight, $\mathrm{can}$, $\mathrm{ext}$, $\mathrm{fin}$, $\mathrm{lor}$, $\mathrm{mod}$, $\mathrm{out}$, $\mathrm{sym}$, $\mathrm{tot}$, belong to the index and to the capital.

The family is smaller than it was. Eighteen of its words have left in three passes, the thirteen of §What Left This Family and five of §The Six That Changed Spelling, and $\mathrm{per}$ left in part, keeping the two occurrences of $\int_{\text{per}}$.

The first test is the glyph. The caret must be free where the word is written. Four words fail it, because the glyph already carries a power or an index above, and the label has nowhere to go.

  • $\mathrm{red}$, 82 occurrences, 52 of them on $K^{0}_{\mathrm{red}}(X)$: the $0$ is in the caret, and the label would have to read as one exponent with it.
  • $\mathrm{alg}$, 50 occurrences, 16 of them on $D_{\mathrm{alg}}^{*}$ and $D_{\mathrm{alg}}^{2}$: the adjoint star and the square hold the caret already.
  • $\mathrm{inv}$, 33 occurrences, 8 of them on $G^{(\mu)}_{\mathrm{inv}}$: the index $\mu$ holds it.
  • $\mathrm{rel}$, 40 occurrences, one of them on $\gamma_{\mathrm{rel}}^{-2}$, which holds the power $-2$.

This is the obstruction of §The Indexed Label read from the other side. There the index is low and the label has nowhere to go beside it; here the power is high and the label has nowhere to go beside it either. A glyph can carry one mark above and one below, and when both are taken the word must stay where it is. No rename helps, because the obstruction is the glyph and not the word.

Three words are not labels at all. $\mathrm{per}$ occurs only as $\int_{\text{per}}$, the domain of integration over periodic configurations: the low slot belongs to the integral, and the word is the integral's argument. $\mathrm{rev}$ occurs as $\mathrm{rev}(w)$, a function applied to its argument, and $\mathrm{lab}$ as $\mathrm{lab}\to\mathrm{mov}$, two words in one field. None of the three is a name hanging on a glyph, and none would survive the move intact.

The Six That Changed Spelling

Six words failed the test of the word and not the test of the glyph. Each carried two English words on one spelling, and no height could separate them. The remedy is not a mark but a letter: one of the two readings takes a code of its own.

word in the low slot on the caret the remedy low occurrences
$\mathrm{reg}$ regular regularised the caret reading becomes $\mathrm{rgs}$ 100
$\mathrm{top}$ topological top (degree) the caret reading keeps $\mathrm{top}$, the low reading becomes $\mathrm{tpl}$ 57
$\mathrm{per}$ perimeter periodic the caret reading keeps $\mathrm{per}$, the low reading becomes $\mathrm{prm}$ 9
$\mathrm{def}$ defining defective the caret reading becomes $\mathrm{dfc}$ 11
$\mathrm{orb}$ orbifold orbital the caret reading keeps $\mathrm{orb}$, the low reading becomes $\mathrm{obf}$ 6
$\mathrm{adj}$ adjoint adjugate the caret reading becomes $\mathrm{adg}$ 4

The six have one cause, and it states the remedy. Two words collide in a three-letter token exactly when they share their first three letters: regular and regularised, top and topological, periodic and perimeter, defining and defective, orbital and orbifold, adjoint and adjugate. No height can separate what the first three letters do not separate. So the word that begins with the token keeps it, and the other takes a skeleton of its consonants, $\mathrm{rgs}$, $\mathrm{tpl}$, $\mathrm{prm}$, $\mathrm{dfc}$, $\mathrm{obf}$, $\mathrm{adg}$. None of the six is a prefix of any word the corpus writes, and none can collide by the same cause, because a skeleton is not a truncation.

The frequencies then decide only how much rewriting each choice costs, not which choice is right. $\mathrm{reg}$ keeps its letters on 100 occurrences and gives up 1; $\mathrm{adj}$ keeps them on 4 and gives up 16; $\mathrm{orb}$ keeps them on 199 and gives up 4. The choice is the same in all three, and the bill is different.

What each rename cost. - $\mathrm{reg}$ low was the regular representation, $\rho_{\mathrm{reg}}(\tilde q)$, $\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathrm{reg}}$, $\chi_{\mathrm{reg}}$, and the caret held the regularised kernel $G_R^{\mathrm{reg}}$. The kernel is now $G_R^{\mathrm{rgs}}$ and the regular representation is $\rho^{\mathrm{reg}}$, $\operatorname{Tr}^{\mathrm{reg}}$, $\chi^{\mathrm{reg}}$. The three $\tilde J^{\mathrm{reg}}$ of the LSZ article already meant regular on shell, the same word as the low slot, and they stay as they are. - $\mathrm{top}$ low was the topological entropy $h_{\mathrm{top}}$, and the caret held the top exterior power $\Lambda^{\text{top}}$. The entropy is now $h^{\mathrm{tpl}}$, and $\mathrm{tpl}$ also takes the topological susceptibility $\chi^{\text{top}}$, which was the only other topological word low. - $\mathrm{per}$ low was the perimeter measure $\mu_{\mathrm{per}}$, and the caret held the periodic complex $CC^{\mathrm{per}}$. The measure is now $\mu^{\mathrm{prm}}$. - $\mathrm{def}$ low was the defining spin $s_{\mathrm{def}}$, and the caret held the defective part $\Sigma_{10}^{\mathrm{def}}$. The part is now $\Sigma_{10}^{\mathrm{dfc}}$ and the spin $s^{\mathrm{def}}$. - $\mathrm{orb}$ low was the orbifold Euler characteristic $\chi_{\mathrm{orb}}$, and the caret held the orbital generators $L_k^{\mathrm{orb}}$. The characteristic is now $\chi^{\mathrm{obf}}$. The two $\Sigma_{\mathrm{orb}}$ of the spin articles were orbital all along and rose unchanged, to $\Sigma^{\mathrm{orb}}$. - $\mathrm{adj}$ low was the adjoint spin $s_{\mathrm{adj}}$, and the caret held the adjugate $A^{\mathrm{adj}}$. The matrix is now $A^{\mathrm{adg}}$ and the spin $s^{\mathrm{adj}}$.

Twelve occurrences needed an empty group. Where a power followed the label, $\rho_{\mathrm{reg}}^{*}$, $\rho_{\mathrm{reg}}^{\oplus 4}$, $h_{\mathrm{top}}^{n}$, the label rose into the caret and the power was pushed behind an empty group: $\rho^{\mathrm{reg}}{}^{*}$, $\rho^{\mathrm{reg}}{}^{\oplus 4}$, $h^{\mathrm{tpl}}{}^{n}$. The corpus already writes a label and an empty group in that order, $\tilde S^{\mathrm{tot}}{}_{,k}$, so the form is one it owns. The twelve are the whole cost of the glyph, and they are paid in the six articles that carry the regular representation and the entropy bounds.

The rename answers the collision and not the other obstructions. Four words keep the low slot because of their glyph, $\mathrm{red}$, $\mathrm{alg}$, $\mathrm{rel}$, $\mathrm{inv}$, and no new code would help. Their power stands before the label, $K^{0}_{\mathrm{red}}(X)$, $D_{\mathrm{alg}}^{*}$, $\gamma_{\mathrm{rel}}^{-2}$, $G^{(\mu)}_{\mathrm{inv}}$, so raising the label would put a number and a word in sequence at one height, $K^{0}{}^{\mathrm{red}}$, and the reader would have to decide which of the two the exponent belongs to. A trailing power is pushed aside by an empty group; a leading power is not, because the group would have to be opened before it. A code is a way of saying a second word; it is not a way of making room.

The six codes are the whole new vocabulary of the corpus. $\mathrm{rgs}$, $\mathrm{tpl}$, $\mathrm{prm}$, $\mathrm{dfc}$, $\mathrm{obf}$, $\mathrm{adg}$ name nothing else, in any slot, and none of them collides with an existing token. They belong in the table of §The Three-Letter Labels of Index, Notation and Corpus Conventions, with the six words they replace.

What Left This Family

Thirteen words and 244 occurrences moved to the caret: $\mathrm{ret}$ 100, $\mathrm{odd}$ 27, $\mathrm{sgn}$ 24, $\mathrm{opp}$ 22, $\mathrm{fix}$ 19, $\mathrm{lin}$ 17, $\mathrm{nil}$ 11, $\mathrm{wkb}$ 10, $\mathrm{rep}$ 4, $\mathrm{exp}$ 4, $\mathrm{pol}$ 3, $\mathrm{new}$ 2, $\mathrm{imp}$ 1. Each passed both tests: the caret was free on its glyph, and the three letters already spelled the same word there. The object may differ, as it does for $\mathrm{sgn}$, a sign representation below and a sign above; the word does not.

The corpus was writing several of them both ways. The move created no form; it chose one that existed. The retarded kernel stood as $G_{\mathrm{ret}}$ in one article and as $E_C^{\mathrm{ret}}$ in another; the odd part of a graded algebra stood as $A_{\text{odd}}$ and as $A^{\mathrm{odd}}$; the improvement term as ${T_{\mu\nu}}_{\mathrm{imp}}$ and as $T_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{imp}}$; the opposite product as $a\cdot_{\mathrm{opp}}b$ and as $x\cdot^{\mathrm{opp}}y$. Each of them is now written once, high: $G^{\mathrm{ret}}$, $A^{\mathrm{odd}}$, $T_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{imp}}$, $a\cdot^{\mathrm{opp}}b$. Seven occurrences that carried the word in \text{} above the glyph, $E_C^{\text{ret}}$ and $a_e^{\text{exp}}$, were brought to \mathrm{} so that the caret holds one spelling of the label.

$\mathrm{opp}$ was miscounted as a collision in the first pass. The word is one, "opposite", and both slots named the same algebra; what differed was the glyph, an algebra $\mathbb{B}^{\mathrm{opp}}$ above and a product $\cdot_{\mathrm{opp}}$ below. Two spellings of one word are not a collision, and the corpus already preferred the caret, where $\cdot^{\mathrm{opp}}$ is written thirty-nine times.

One example had to change. §The Subscripts used ${}_{\mathrm{ret}}$ to show that the low slot admits an upright word. With $\mathrm{ret}$ on the caret the example shows ${}_{\mathrm{tor}}$, the torsion submodule, which stays low.

The Codes That Left

Twelve words left this audit, and they are recorded once here so that the census can be read against the corpus it describes. They are the codes of the algebraic structures, $\mathrm{gpa}$, $\mathrm{gps}$, $\mathrm{gqa}$, $\mathrm{gqs}$, $\mathrm{spa}$, $\mathrm{sps}$, $\mathrm{sqa}$, $\mathrm{sqs}$, $\mathrm{apa}$, $\mathrm{aps}$, $\mathrm{aqa}$, $\mathrm{aqs}$, and they stood in the low slot 146 times.

They were the one family with no obstruction at all. No code is indexed, none carries a comma, none is compounded with a second word, and no glyph carried a caret of its own on the same letter. The low slot was kept for a reason of layout, in comparison tables read as a column of short names, and layout is not a notation rule. The migration moved all 146 to the caret, $V^{\mathrm{gpa}}=V^{\mathrm{spa}}+V^{\mathrm{apa}}$, $V^{\mathrm{gqs}}(\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2)$, $\tilde Q_1\diamond^{\mathrm{aqs}}\tilde Q_2$, in two articles, and both convention articles now rule that the low slot of a code is a defect.

The count in this note is therefore of the words that are still low, and the twelve are not among them. What they cost and what they gained is written in §The Three-Letter Labels, and the two articles that carried them, The Vector Parts of the Products over the Biquaternion Algebra and The Vector Part of the Square and the Jacobi Failure of the Antisymmetric Plain Sesqualgebra, now read them at the height of the other named variants.

The Capital in the Low Slot

Twenty-seven words, 184 occurrences. Every one of them is capitalised inside the low slot, so each breaks the lowercase rule of §The Three-Letter Labels before the question of the caret is even asked. Their counts are in the table at the end of this section.

The capital is not decoration. In this paragraph the words are named as words; every exhibit after it is shown inside its slot. $\mathrm{Lie}$, $\mathrm{Vir}$ and $\mathrm{Ric}$ are names of people. $\mathrm{QCD}$, $\mathrm{GUT}$, $\mathrm{EDM}$, $\mathrm{CAR}$, $\mathrm{CTP}$, $\mathrm{IPC}$, $\mathrm{CPC}$, $\mathrm{DCB}$ and $\mathrm{RGB}$ are initialisms, and the capital is the only thing that shows a phrase is being abbreviated. Lowercasing one of them and lifting it to the caret would be two changes at once, and the second would hide the first: $\mathrm{qcd}$ no longer shows which words it stands for.

Twelve of the twenty-seven hold an operator with an argument, not a name. The subscript is there because the glyph has to say of what, and the word brings its own argument into the slot:

  • $f|_{\mathrm{Fix}(\Sigma)}$ — the restriction to the fixed set of an involution;
  • $\mathrm{id}_{\operatorname{End}(E)}$ — the identity of an endomorphism algebra;
  • $C_{\operatorname{Inn}(G)}(\alpha)$ and $C_{\operatorname{Aut}(G)}(\sigma)$ — the centraliser of an element, in the inner or in the full automorphism group;
  • $\int_{\operatorname{Irr}(G)}^{\oplus}$ — the direct integral over the irreducible spectrum;
  • $\chi_{\operatorname{Hom}_k(V,W)}(g)$ — the character of a hom space, and $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{PSh}}(\mathcal F,\ldots)$ a hom taken in a category of presheaves;
  • $\mathcal H_{\operatorname{Ind}_K^H\sigma}$ — the space carrying an induced representation;
  • $O_{\operatorname{Spf}A}$, $O_{\operatorname{Spa}(A,A^+)}$ and $\ast_{\operatorname{Aff}\cap\mathcal J}$ — the structure sheaf of a formal spectrum, that of an adic space, and a pushout over an intersection of affine groups;
  • $\mathcal R_{\text{Ric}}$ — the Ricci part of a curvature.

For these the caret has nothing to name. Nothing is being said about a variant of $f$, $O$, $C$ or $\chi$, and a caret would turn the word into a power: $f^{\operatorname{Fix}(\Sigma)}$ claims an exponent where the expression means a restriction. The slot is occupied by grammar, not by a label, so the height is not the problem and the case is not the only problem.

The other fifteen name the sector or the mechanism the glyph belongs to, and here the caret is a live possibility that the corpus has declined: $Q_{\mathrm{Had}}$ the hadronic tensor, $\omega_{\mathrm{Vir}}$ the Virasoro vector, $\Omega_{\mathrm{CAR}}$ the vacuum of the anticommutation relations, $H_{\mathrm{DCB}}$ the Dirac–Coulomb–Breit Hamiltonian, $E_{\mathrm{Dir}}$ the Dirichlet energy, $\mathcal L_{\mathrm{EDM}}$ the dipole-moment term, $S_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ the chromodynamic action, $G_{\mathrm{GUT}}$ the unifying group, $\mathcal V_{\mathrm{CTP}}$ the closed-time-path potential, $\vec F_{\text{Cor}}$ the Coriolis force, $A_{\mathrm{Lie}}$ the commutator algebra, $H^1_{\mathrm{Har}}$ the harmonic forms, $\rho_{\mathrm{RGB}}$ the colour density, and the two turnstiles $\vdash_{\mathrm{IPC}}$ and $\vdash_{\mathrm{CPC}}$.

These fifteen are the only subfamily of this residue whose difficulty is the case rule alone. If a capital were admitted anywhere in a label, each of them could rise to the caret unchanged in meaning. That is why the case rule and the position rule have to be kept apart in the conventions: one is a typographic decision, the other is a statement about what the low slot is for.

What Made the Fifteen Safe

The fifteen that moved are the ones that passed five tests, and the tests are worth stating because they are the rule the corpus will want for the next case.

  1. Plain glyph. The word hung on a single letter or a single accented letter, $W_{\mathrm{aff}}$, $V_{\mathrm{pot}}$, $y_{\mathrm{hom}}$. No bar, no norm, no summation sign, no restriction bar was in the way.
  2. No index. Nothing followed the word inside its field. $W_{\mathrm{aff}}$ has no $W_{\mathrm{aff},k}$ beside it.
  3. No caret twin. The word held no meaning on the caret before the move, so no merge was needed.
  4. Not a code. It was not one of the twelve names of the algebraic structures.
  5. Already lowercase. No case was changed by the move, so the move was a change of height and nothing else.

The fifteen are $\mathrm{aff}$, $\mathrm{amp}$, $\mathrm{app}$, $\mathrm{est}$, $\mathrm{geo}$, $\mathrm{hom}$, $\mathrm{hyp}$, $\mathrm{iso}$, $\mathrm{lab}$, $\mathrm{mat}$, $\mathrm{mix}$, $\mathrm{mot}$, $\mathrm{obs}$, $\mathrm{pot}$, $\mathrm{src}$. Each names the whole object and not a part of it: the affine Weyl group, the potential energy, the geometric phase, the laboratory frame, the observed frequency. The test that admits them is the test that rejects the eighty-five, and the failure in each rejected family is one of the five.

What the Four Failures Cost

The failures are not equal, and the cost of forcing them is easy to state.

  • The indexed label would put an index in a slot with no label above it: $\tilde S^{\mathrm{tot}}{}_{,k}$. 930 occurrences, and a new exception in §The Subscripts.
  • The part and the role would reverse the sense of the mark: $S^{\mathrm{int}}$ for an action that is not an interaction, $M^{\mathrm{tor}}$ for a module that is not torsion. 413 occurrences, and the reading rule of §The Subscripts would have to be withdrawn.
  • The word with two spellings does not have to be paid at all, and §The Six That Changed Spelling says how: 187 occurrences moved, 12 of them through an empty group, at the price of six new codes.
  • The capital is two cases and not one. Fifteen of the twenty-seven would move cleanly if a capital were allowed; the other twelve hold an operator with an argument and would become a power.

Summary

The residue of the caret patch was 115 words and 4,493 occurrences. The corpus has grown by 75 occurrences since, and four migrations have taken 651 out of the low slot: fifteen labels into the index table, 74; the twelve codes of the algebraic structures, 146; thirteen qualifiers that already named their object on the caret, 244; and six words that carried two meanings apiece, 187. The low slot now holds 85 words in 3,917 occurrences, and they stay low for four reasons, none of them a matter of layout. An index has nowhere to go in the caret. A part that rose would become a claim about the whole. A glyph that already carries a power leaves no room above. And a capital cannot rise at all, because no token the caret carries begins with one.

The rule that follows is the one already written in §The Three-Letter Labels, read in the other direction. A label that names the object goes on the caret. A label that names a part, an index, an operator or an acronym stays low. A qualifier that already names the same object on the caret goes on the caret too. And when one spelling would carry two words, the rarer reading takes a code of its own, $\mathrm{rgs}$, $\mathrm{tpl}$, $\mathrm{prm}$, $\mathrm{dfc}$, $\mathrm{obf}$, $\mathrm{adg}$, so that both readings can stand where they belong. The caret is not the only home for a name, and the corpus is the better for saying so once.

The Lower Labels and the Obstruction to the Caret

Introduction: The Question

The corpus now writes every label of three letters in lowercase and puts the label of an object on the caret, $\mathbb{B}^{\mathrm{opp}}$, $L_x^{\mathrm{axf}}$, $E_0^{\mathrm{wkb}}$. The question of this note is the one that follows: the low slot still holds three-letter upright words, and can they be moved up?

The census says no for most of them. A three-letter upright word follows a low mark 4,493 times, in 115 distinct words, spread over the mathematical, the physical and the internal corpus. Fifteen of them were moved, 74 occurrences, and they are now on the caret and in the table of Index, Notation and Corpus Conventions, §The Three-Letter Labels. The other hundred stayed, and this note says why, category by category.

The census counts a word of exactly three letters written upright, in $\mathrm{}$, $\operatorname{}$ or $\text{}$, immediately after a low mark. It reads the mathematical, the physical and the internal articles, counts occurrences and not distinct words, and leaves this note out of its own count. Each family below is given as a table of four columns: the label, how often it occurs, what it means, and, where the glyph is worth naming, the letter it hangs on.

Category Words Occurrences Movable

The five categories are not five degrees of difficulty. One is an obstruction of notation, two are obstructions of meaning, one is a matter of convention alone, and one is the case rule.

The Indexed Label

This is the largest family and the hardest. Sixteen words carry 3,109 occurrences, and 933 of them stand in a field of two parts, the label and an index: $s_{\mathrm{tot},z}$, $\tilde{K}_{\mathrm{mod},W}$, $\tilde H_{\mathrm{pos},l}$, $K_{\mathrm{rad}\,\mu}$.

Why it cannot rise. The low slot takes a comma-separated list, so a label and an index share one field and one size. The caret takes one field. Move the label up and the index is left behind: $\tilde S^{\mathrm{tot}}{}_{,k}$, with an empty base and a comma that has nothing to separate. The form is legal TeX and unreadable at reading size. It also breaks the rule that a mark has one job, since the low slot would then hold a bare index with no label to belong to, and the reader could no longer tell an index from a label without looking up the table.

What it would cost. 933 rewrites, every one of them introducing a form the conventions do not otherwise admit, and a second exception in §The Subscripts, which would have to say that a low slot may hold an index whose label is above.

The Part and the Role

Thirty-five words, 412 occurrences. The glyph is a complete object and the low word names a piece of it: the interaction action $S_{\mathrm{int}}$, the nuclear norm $\lVert A\rVert_{\mathrm{nuc}}$, the torsion submodule $M_{\mathrm{tor}}$, the character of the regular representation $\chi_{\mathrm{reg}}$, the vacuum energy $E_{\mathrm{vac}}$.

Why it cannot rise. The two slots say different things, and the difference is not typographic. The low slot binds to the glyph and reads of: $\chi_{\mathrm{reg}}$ is the character of the regular representation, a character that is otherwise unremarkable. The caret asserts and reads is: $f^{\mathrm{sym}}$ in this corpus is the symmetrised object itself, $f^{\mathrm{sym}} = \tfrac12(f(x,y)+f(y,x))$. Moved up, $\chi^{\mathrm{reg}}$ would claim that the character is regular, which is a different mathematical statement and, in most of these cases, a false one.

The same argument reaches the operator names that sit low because a bar or a sum takes the low slot: the restriction $T|_{\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma)}$, the column space $\Pi_{\operatorname{col}A}$, the cyclic sum $\sum_{\mathrm{cyc}}$. Here the word is an operator with an argument, and the low slot belongs to the bar or the summation sign, not to the word.

The Word Already on the Caret

Twenty-five words, 642 occurrences, and each of them already names something on the caret. The caret holds 75 distinct three-letter words and the low slot 115, and 37 words sit in both. Twenty-five of the 37 are this family; the other twelve are the codes, and they are counted in their own section.

Why it cannot simply rise. Merging two vocabularies needs every word to mean one thing. Most of these do: $\mathrm{ret}$ low is the retarded kernel and $\mathrm{ret}$ high is the retarded field, and both are the same word for the same regime. But four do not, and a merge would silently weld two meanings:

Word In the low slot On the caret
$\mathrm{mod}$ the modular Hamiltonian, $\tilde K_{\mathrm{mod},W}$ the modified Lagrangian, $L^{\mathrm{mod}}$
$\mathrm{reg}$ the regular representation, $\chi_{\mathrm{reg}}$ the regularised kernel, $G_R^{\mathrm{reg}}$
$\mathrm{sym}$ a symmetric subspace, $\mathcal L^k_{\mathrm{sym}}$ the symmetrised part, $f^{\mathrm{sym}}$
$\mathrm{opp}$ a product written on the reversed algebra, $a\cdot_{\mathrm{opp}}b$ the opposite algebra, $\mathbb{B}^{\mathrm{opp}}$

$\mathrm{reg}$ is the sharpest. Two regulars, one of representation theory and one of regularisation, would differ only by the height of the word, at the size where the reader can least afford it. That is the same failure the corpus already forbids for $\mathrm{op}$ beside $\mathrm{opp}$, now with the slot as the only separator.

The twenty-five, one by one. The four of the table above are the collisions; the other twenty-one migrate cleanly and are left low only because they are harmless where they are.

The Code of the Twelve Structures

Twelve words, 146 occurrences, and the one family of the residue that was never obstructed. They are now on the caret. The migration took 146 rewrites in two files and one line in each convention article, and it changed no meaning anywhere.

What they are. $\mathrm{gpa}$, $\mathrm{gps}$, $\mathrm{gqa}$, $\mathrm{gqs}$, $\mathrm{spa}$, $\mathrm{sps}$, $\mathrm{sqa}$, $\mathrm{sqs}$, $\mathrm{apa}$, $\mathrm{aps}$, $\mathrm{aqa}$, $\mathrm{aqs}$ — the first letter general, symmetric or antisymmetric, the second plain or quaternionic, the third algebra or sesqualgebra. The twelve are defined once, in The Algebraic Structures over the Biquaternion $\mathbb{C}$ Space, and this family of the residue is the only one whose words the corpus had already fixed in a single place.

Why the low slot was a choice and not a constraint. Nothing in the notation stood in the way. No code is indexed, none carries a comma, none is compounded with a second word, and no glyph carried a caret of its own on the same letter. There was no $V_{\mathrm{gpa},k}$, no $V^{\mathrm{gpa}}_{\mathrm{gpa}}$, no collision of the kind that stops $\mathrm{reg}$ or $\mathrm{opp}$. The low slot was kept for a reason of layout — the twelve occur in comparison tables where they are read as a column of short names — and layout is not a notation rule.

The multiplication case is the one that needed a precedent. Two of the 146 sit on a binary operator, $\tilde Q_1\diamond_{\mathrm{aqa}}\tilde Q_2$. The corpus already writes exactly this form with the caret: $x \cdot^{\mathrm{opp}} y$ occurs twenty times, in Modules over an Algebra, The Endomorphism Algebra of a Module and Central Simple Algebras and the Brauer Group. The migration therefore wrote $\tilde Q_1\diamond^{\mathrm{aqa}}\tilde Q_2$, which is the established form and not a new one.

What the two files now read. $V^{\mathrm{gpa}}=V^{\mathrm{spa}}+V^{\mathrm{apa}}$, $V^{\mathrm{gqa}}=V^{\mathrm{sqa}}$, $V^{\mathrm{gps}}=V^{\mathrm{aps}}$, $V^{\mathrm{gqs}}(\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2)$ and the conjugate slot $\overline{V^{\mathrm{gqs}}}$. The equality $V^{\mathrm{gpa}}=V^{\mathrm{spa}}+V^{\mathrm{apa}}$ is the decomposition, and it now reads at the height where the rest of the corpus writes a named variant.

What changed in the conventions. Both summary tables carry the codes in the caret, $^{\mathrm{gpa}}$ … $^{\mathrm{aqs}}$, and both rule paragraphs now say where a code goes: on the object and on its multiplication, with the examples above. The sentence that called the low placement a convention is gone, because it was never a convention. It was an inconsistency that the census found and the migration closed.

A name is not a mark. The twelve words occur 2,119 times in the corpus, and only 146 of those were marks on a glyph. The remaining 1,951 are the word used as a name — the twelve listed in The Algebraic Structures over the Biquaternion $\mathbb{C}$ Space, a comparison table headed $\mathrm{gqa}$ against $\mathrm{aqa}$, a sentence that says "the code $\mathrm{gqs}$". A name in running text carries no height, and the migration left every one of them alone. That is the same distinction the corpus already draws for $\mathrm{fop}$: a token when it hangs on a glyph, a word when it is being discussed.

Where a code and an index share a glyph. Three occurrences already wrote the code high and the index low, $L^{\mathrm{gqs}}_{\tilde Q_1}$, $L^{\mathrm{gpa}}_{e_t}$, $R^{\mathrm{aqs}}_{e_t}$. The migrated forms join them without a new rule: the name is on the caret and the index keeps the low slot, which is the order $s_{\mathrm{tot},z}$ uses when both are low and the order $\gamma_n^{\operatorname{sym}}$ uses when they are split.

One caveat carried forward. The letters $\mathrm{aps}$ also name the algebra of physical space in the physical corpus, $\mathrm{span}_{\mathbb{R}}\{e_t,\gamma_k\}$, and there they are a name in running text rather than a mark on a glyph. The migration touched only the subscripted occurrences, so the collision is unchanged and stays recorded in The Algebraic Structures over the Biquaternion $\mathbb{C}$ Space.

The Capital in the Low Slot

Twenty-seven words, 184 occurrences. Every one of them is capitalised inside the low slot, so each breaks the lowercase rule of §The Three-Letter Labels before the question of the caret is even asked. Their counts are in the table at the end of this section.

The capital is not decoration. In this paragraph the words are named as words; every exhibit after it is shown inside its slot. $\mathrm{Lie}$, $\mathrm{Vir}$ and $\mathrm{Ric}$ are names of people. $\mathrm{QCD}$, $\mathrm{GUT}$, $\mathrm{EDM}$, $\mathrm{CAR}$, $\mathrm{CTP}$, $\mathrm{IPC}$, $\mathrm{CPC}$, $\mathrm{DCB}$ and $\mathrm{RGB}$ are initialisms, and the capital is the only thing that shows a phrase is being abbreviated. Lowercasing one of them and lifting it to the caret would be two changes at once, and the second would hide the first: $\mathrm{qcd}$ no longer shows which words it stands for.

Twelve of the twenty-seven hold an operator with an argument, not a name. The subscript is there because the glyph has to say of what, and the word brings its own argument into the slot:

  • $f|_{\mathrm{Fix}(\Sigma)}$ — the restriction to the fixed set of an involution;
  • $\mathrm{id}_{\operatorname{End}(E)}$ — the identity of an endomorphism algebra;
  • $C_{\operatorname{Inn}(G)}(\alpha)$ and $C_{\operatorname{Aut}(G)}(\sigma)$ — the centraliser of an element, in the inner or in the full automorphism group;
  • $\int_{\operatorname{Irr}(G)}^{\oplus}$ — the direct integral over the irreducible spectrum;
  • $\chi_{\operatorname{Hom}_k(V,W)}(g)$ — the character of a hom space, and $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{PSh}}(\mathcal F,\ldots)$ a hom taken in a category of presheaves;
  • $\mathcal H_{\operatorname{Ind}_K^H\sigma}$ — the space carrying an induced representation;
  • $O_{\operatorname{Spf}A}$, $O_{\operatorname{Spa}(A,A^+)}$ and $\ast_{\operatorname{Aff}\cap\mathcal J}$ — the structure sheaf of a formal spectrum, that of an adic space, and a pushout over an intersection of affine groups;
  • $\mathcal R_{\text{Ric}}$ — the Ricci part of a curvature.

For these the caret has nothing to name. Nothing is being said about a variant of $f$, $O$, $C$ or $\chi$, and a caret would turn the word into a power: $f^{\operatorname{Fix}(\Sigma)}$ claims an exponent where the expression means a restriction. The slot is occupied by grammar, not by a label, so the height is not the problem and the case is not the only problem.

The other fifteen name the sector or the mechanism the glyph belongs to, and here the caret is a live possibility that the corpus has declined: $Q_{\mathrm{Had}}$ the hadronic tensor, $\omega_{\mathrm{Vir}}$ the Virasoro vector, $\Omega_{\mathrm{CAR}}$ the vacuum of the anticommutation relations, $H_{\mathrm{DCB}}$ the Dirac–Coulomb–Breit Hamiltonian, $E_{\mathrm{Dir}}$ the Dirichlet energy, $\mathcal L_{\mathrm{EDM}}$ the dipole-moment term, $S_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ the chromodynamic action, $G_{\mathrm{GUT}}$ the unifying group, $\mathcal V_{\mathrm{CTP}}$ the closed-time-path potential, $\vec F_{\text{Cor}}$ the Coriolis force, $A_{\mathrm{Lie}}$ the commutator algebra, $H^1_{\mathrm{Har}}$ the harmonic forms, $\rho_{\mathrm{RGB}}$ the colour density, and the two turnstiles $\vdash_{\mathrm{IPC}}$ and $\vdash_{\mathrm{CPC}}$.

These fifteen are the only subfamily of this residue whose difficulty is the case rule alone. If a capital were admitted anywhere in a label, each of them could rise to the caret unchanged in meaning. That is why the case rule and the position rule have to be kept apart in the conventions: one is a typographic decision, the other is a statement about what the low slot is for.

What Made the Fifteen Safe

The fifteen that moved are the ones that passed five tests, and the tests are worth stating because they are the rule the corpus will want for the next case.

  1. Plain glyph. The word hung on a single letter or a single accented letter, $W_{\mathrm{aff}}$, $V_{\mathrm{pot}}$, $y_{\mathrm{hom}}$. No bar, no norm, no summation sign, no restriction bar was in the way.
  2. No index. Nothing followed the word inside its field. $W_{\mathrm{aff}}$ has no $W_{\mathrm{aff},k}$ beside it.
  3. No caret twin. The word held no meaning on the caret before the move, so no merge was needed.
  4. Not a code. It was not one of the twelve names of the algebraic structures.
  5. Already lowercase. No case was changed by the move, so the move was a change of height and nothing else.

The fifteen are $\mathrm{aff}$, $\mathrm{amp}$, $\mathrm{app}$, $\mathrm{est}$, $\mathrm{geo}$, $\mathrm{hom}$, $\mathrm{hyp}$, $\mathrm{iso}$, $\mathrm{lab}$, $\mathrm{mat}$, $\mathrm{mix}$, $\mathrm{mot}$, $\mathrm{obs}$, $\mathrm{pot}$, $\mathrm{src}$. Each names the whole object and not a part of it: the affine Weyl group, the potential energy, the geometric phase, the laboratory frame, the observed frequency. The test that admits them is the test that rejects the hundred, and the failure in each rejected family is one of the five.

What the Four Failures Cost

The failures are not equal, and the cost of forcing them is easy to state.

  • The indexed label would put an index in a slot with no label above it: $\tilde S^{\mathrm{tot}}{}_{,k}$. 930 occurrences, and a new exception in §The Subscripts.
  • The part and the role would reverse the sense of the mark: $\chi^{\mathrm{reg}}$ for a character that is not regular, $S^{\mathrm{int}}$ for an action that is not interaction. 412 occurrences, and the reading rule of §The Subscripts would have to be withdrawn.
  • The caret twin would weld two meanings into one word in four cases, $\mathrm{mod}$, $\mathrm{reg}$, $\mathrm{sym}$, $\mathrm{opp}$, at 540 occurrences between them.
  • The code would move cleanly, at 146 rewrites and no loss.
  • The capital is two cases and not one. Fifteen of the twenty-seven would move cleanly if a capital were allowed; the other twelve hold an operator with an argument and would become a power.

Summary

The residue of the caret patch was 115 words and 4,493 occurrences. Twenty-seven words and 220 occurrences have moved: the fifteen labels into the index table, and the twelve codes from the low slot to the caret. What remains is 103 words and 4,347 occurrences, and they stay low for four reasons, none of them a matter of layout. An index has nowhere to go in the caret. A part that rose would become a claim about the whole. A twin is already on the caret, with a different meaning. An acronym loses the words it abbreviates when it is lowered.

The rule that follows is the one already written in §The Three-Letter Labels, read in the other direction. A label that names the object goes on the caret. A label that names a part, an index, an operator or an acronym stays low. The caret is not the only home for a name, and the corpus is the better for saying so once.

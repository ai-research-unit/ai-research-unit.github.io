Contravariant Migration: The Purge of the Raised Index

Introduction

The verdict. The corpus prints no raised spacetime index. One metric rules, the unit metric $\delta_{\mu\nu}=(1,1,1,1)$, and it is the identity, so a raised index would name the same index it already carries. The raised form is a defect wherever it stands. This article is the procedure that removes it when it comes back.

It does come back. Three routes carry it in. A display is copied from a text that works over a curved metric. An author writes a field strength from habit, with both slots in the caret. An agent simplifies a contraction by lifting one of its two indices. None of the three is a decision, and none is visible to a reader who is not looking for it. A written procedure is the answer, and this is it.

The authority is not here. The rule stands in Index and Notation Conventions of each corpus, in the sections The Upper and Low Marks and The Caret, and it is repeated in the other nineteen convention articles. This article adds no rule. It adds the order of the work, the instrument, the substitution table, the traps and the record.

The state the article was written in. The physics corpus holds zero raised spacetime slots. Part VI of the mathematics corpus holds zero. Twenty convention articles state the ban in words. Twenty-four articles carry the weight sentence that replaces the raising. The ten rows of the last pass are listed in §The Record of the Pass.

What the article does not claim. It does not claim that the mathematics changes. It does not claim that the raised form is ambiguous, or that a text over a curved metric is wrong to write it. It claims only that the series has one metric, that the one is the identity, and that a mark which names nothing is not printed.

The organisation. §The Object fixes the metric and the weight. §The Classification Test separates a slot from a power. §The Instrument gives the search. §The Substitution Table gives the rewrite. §The Traps lists the fourteen ways the pass has gone wrong. §The Procedure is the order of the work. §The Verification is the acceptance. §The Convention is the sentence to reuse. §The Boundary is what the pass must not touch. §The Record of the Pass is what was done.

A word on the writing. Every defect in this article is written as a search token in a code span, never typeset. Every typeset display is a correct one. A search token is program text and not an example: it is there to be run, and it is quoted in no other form.

The Object: A Raising That Raises Nothing

The Metric Is the Identity

The metric is the Gram matrix of the norm on the four units. It carries four entries $+1$ on the diagonal, from $\langle e_\mu\rangle_\natural=+1$, and six zeros off it, from the orthogonality of the units. Ten entries, ten fixed by the multiplication. There is no scale to fix and no sign to choose.

The identity raises nothing. Multiplying a list of slots by the identity returns the same list. So an index in the caret is not shorthand for anything: it does not invert a matrix, does not transport a component from one frame to another, and does not shorten a tensor. It is a mark with no content, and a mark with no content collides with the real raising of the texts the corpus is read beside.

The corpus states the consequence in the article that owns the object. The Unit Metric in the Biquaternion Universe writes that there is no signature to pick, no scale to fix and no convention to declare. The sentence is the whole warrant of this pass, and it is already in the corpus.

The Weight Is What Replaces It

The sign of the material sector is arithmetic, and it enters as a weight. The weight is $\varepsilon=(1,-1,-1,-1)$, fixed in Operator and Calculus Conventions, with $\varepsilon_t=+1$ and $\varepsilon_{x,y,z}=-1$. The square of a gamma carries it, $\gamma_\mu\gamma_\mu=\varepsilon_\mu I_4$ with no sum, so a Dirac sum is written out, $\sum_\mu\varepsilon_\mu\gamma_\mu\gamma_\mu=4I_4$.

The weight is not a metric and it is not a raising. It is a list of four signs, one per direction. Where a text with a curved metric would have written a contraction with one index up and one down, the corpus writes the summed index under a $\sum$ and gives each term its sign, $\sum_\mu\varepsilon_\mu A_\mu B_\mu$.

The sentence is already in twenty-four articles. No index is raised: the summed index carries the weight is the corpus's own formula, and a new article that needs the sentence copies it rather than inventing a variant.

The Masked Second Metric

A raising rule written for the gamma is a second metric wearing the weight. The corpus once carried one, and it is retired. Its formula is not printed here, because printing it would restore it. It is wrong twice: it declares a map that the identity does not have, and it takes its sign from the retired second metric rather than from the weight.

The test is mechanical. Read the rule as an instruction and ask what it does to an index the metric has already left alone. If the rule moves the index and multiplies by a weight, the rule has declared a metric the corpus does not have. Delete the rule, keep the weight, write the sum out. A convention article that states such a rule is pollution of the same family as the raised index, and the repair is the same size.

The Classification Test

Five questions, asked in this order, decide every caret in the corpus.

  1. Is it one of the four? The caret carries a power, a conjugation, a transposition or a construction, and those four are its whole list: powers $2^k$, $e^\lambda$, $(-1)^j$; conjugations $a^{*}$, $a^{\dagger}$, $a^{\natural}$; transpositions $L^T$; constructions $\mathbb{B}^\times$, $S^{\otimes n}$, $\mathrm{sym}^l$. If yes, leave it. This question settles most sites.
  2. Is the caret letter a label? A label is the upright name of a variant, and every label stands in the subscript, $E_{\mathrm{wkb}}$, $T_{\mathrm{em},\mu\nu}$. A label in the caret is a defect, but a different defect, repaired by a different pass. Record it and leave it alone.
  3. Does the caret carry a direction slot? The slot letters are $t,x,y,z$ and $\mu,\nu,\rho,\sigma,\lambda,\eta,\alpha,\beta$, with $i,j,k,a,b,c$ on the spatial and frame families. If the glyph is one that carries such a family elsewhere in the same article, the caret is a slot and the site is a defect.
  4. Is the glyph a scalar? If the article writes the glyph as a number, a norm, a parameter or an algebra element, the caret is a power and the site is clean. This question separates the zeroth frame slot, $\gamma_0$, from the square of a Lorentz factor, $\gamma^2$.
  5. Would the rewrite change a value? A notation pass changes no number, no sign and no coefficient. If the rewrite would change one, the site is not a notation site, and the pass stops there and reports.

The test reduces to one sentence for the common case. A caret whose letter names a direction, on a glyph that carries a direction elsewhere, is a defect. Every other caret is one of the four, a label, or a power.

The Instrument

One search, two corpora, and every hit read by hand.

# the physics corpus
grep -rnoE '\^\{?\\(mu|nu|rho|sigma|lambda)\b' articles_physics/*.md

# the articles of Part VI, as source paths
sed -n '/^## PART VI/,$p' maths.md | grep -oE 'articles_maths/[a-z0-9-]+\.html' | sort -u | sed 's/\.html$/.md/'

# the same search over that list
grep -rnoE '\^\{?\\(mu|nu|rho|sigma|lambda)\b' $(sed -n '/^## PART VI/,$p' maths.md | grep -oE 'articles_maths/[a-z0-9-]+\.html' | sort -u | sed 's/\.html$/.md/')

The size of the yield is the first diagnostic. The physics corpus cannot hold thousands of raised spacetime slots. The search above returns sixteen there, and a four-figure yield means the pattern has spread from the slot to the exponent and the label, so that the pattern is the fault and not the corpus. The last pass opened on a four-figure yield and spent its first hour on false positives.

The mathematics corpus is not scoped the same way. Part VI, the synthetic studies, is in scope. Parts I to V are not: there the caret letter is a fixed set, a partition, a representation label or a multi-index, and the object test never reaches a spacetime slot. The whole corpus returns fifteen hundred and six hits in a hundred and fifty-one files, against seventy-four in the nine hundred and fifty articles of Part VI, and the second line of the block builds that list from the menu.

Seventy-four hits in Part VI are not seventy-four defects. Twenty-seven are an algebra variant named by an involution, on the $\sigma$ of a fixed ring; thirty are the letter $\lambda$ as an exponent or an eigenvalue; nine are $\mu$ and eight are $\nu$, as a basis label or a monomial exponent. Every one is a power, an exponential or a name, and none carries a spacetime slot. A sweep that repaired them would be the label pass, not this one.

Nothing in _reserve is a target. Those drafts quote the defects they describe, and the quotation is their subject.

The acceptance is a read, not a count. Every hit is read and classified by the five questions. The pass ends when the count of unclassified hits is zero.

Two rows need the sentence as well as the mark. The trace and the contraction lose information when the two slots collapse onto one letter, so the article's prose carries the weight: the trace is a sum, and each term carries the sign of its direction. A symbol alone does not say it.

One row reads an alphabet, not a height. Lowering both slots of a tetrad leaves Latin and Greek as the only marker of which slot is the frame, so keep the alphabets apart, $e_{\mu a}$ with $\mu$ the spacetime slot and $a$ the frame slot.

The Traps

1. The square is not a slot. A caret carrying a numeral on a scalar glyph is a power. The last pass read the momentum square in a propagator as an index and rewrote it in terms of the norm; the propagator's pole then changed from the invariant square to something else. That is a physics rewrite wearing a notation pass. It was reverted, and the pass was rerun on the slots alone. Test: if the rewrite moves a pole, a sign or a coefficient, it is not this pass.

2. The numeral is a slot on a family glyph. The zeroth frame slot and the square of a Lorentz factor share a letter and a caret. Question 4 decides: the glyph that the article indexes elsewhere takes a slot, the glyph that the article raises to a power takes a power.

3. The trace is a sum. Dropping the caret leaves two slots on one letter, and the weight is then unstated. Write the sum out, $\sum_\mu\varepsilon_\mu T_{\mu\mu}$.

4. The contraction names its index. A repeated index left to the summation convention is a second defect, and the corpus prints none of them. The sum carries the weight, $\sum_\mu\varepsilon_\mu A_\mu B_\mu$.

5. The frame inverse is not a raised tensor. A tetrad's inverse invites an index in the caret. There is no second metric to raise with: an inverse frame is written low, and in the flat frame it is the one metric, $\delta_{ac}$. The article says so in words when the letter alone would mislead.

6. One token, two objects. The letter $e$ carries both the exponential and the tetrad. The caret on the exponential is a power, the caret on the tetrad is a slot, and the two are told apart by the glyph's other slots in the same article.

7. The exponential is clean. The e^\mu of the matrix-logarithm articles is a power of the exponential function, and the letter there is a scalar parameter. The last pass listed several of them as candidates. They are clean.

8. A label is not a slot. The representation label of the principal series, the I^{\nu,m} of the induced representation, the H^{\alpha,s} of the analytic family of Sobolev spaces, the A^\sigma of a fixed set, the \mathbb{B}^\sigma of a grade, the M_4^\sigma of a matrix family, the j of a weight: each stands in the caret in a part of the corpus that is not scoped here. They are a different pass, with a different repair, and folding them into this one produces a report of thousands and a corpus of rubble.

9. A name is not a slot. The Newtonian potential U^\mu, the Specht module S^\mu, the Littlewood–Richardson coefficient c^\nu_{\lambda\mu}, the monomial x^\nu of the hypercomplex series: the caret letter names the measure, the partition or the exponent. Leave them.

10. The masked raising. A repair that explains the low form by a raising formula carrying the weight inside restores the retired rule with a new face. The low form needs no formula: the index is where it is, and the weight multiplies.

11. The quotation. A display quoted from another text keeps that text's spelling, and the sentence that introduces it says so. The corpus's own display that reads the quotation carries the low form. Where a quotation would have to print the defect as the corpus's own form, paraphrase in words instead.

12. The drafts. The internal migration articles and the reserved drafts quote the defects they describe. They are not targets, and a sweep that includes them edits the record of the pass.

13. Stop at the arithmetic. If a patch forces a sign change to stay correct, the article's physics and the notation disagree. Report the article; do not repair the physics inside a notation pass.

14. Do not reflow. One token per patch, line structure preserved. A Markdown rewrite of a paragraph has broken a display in this corpus before, and a display broken by a notation pass is the worst outcome the pass can have.

The Procedure

Step 0, read the authority. Read the Index and Notation Conventions of both corpora, the sections The Upper and Low Marks and The Caret. Read the other convention article nearest the article you are about to touch. No patch is written before this step.

Step 1, census. Run the search of §The Instrument on the physics corpus and on Part VI. Keep the list. Read it to the end. A yield that looks too large is the signal of Trap 8 or of a pattern that caught the four.

Step 2, classify. Give every hit one of four labels: clean (one of the four), label (a separate pass), defect (this pass), stop (the rewrite would move a value). A hit with no label is a hit not yet read. The census is finished when no hit is unlabelled.

Step 3, patch. One token at a time. The patch changes the mark and nothing else: no word, no spacing, no line break, no ordering. Where the collapse of two slots needs a sentence, add the sentence in the paragraph that already discusses the object, and add no other.

Step 4, verify. §The Verification, all four checks.

Step 5, report. One row per file: the file, the token found, what it became, how many. Then the classification counts: clean, label, defect, stop. Then the four checks. A report without the clean count cannot be audited, because the reader cannot tell a narrow sweep from a blind one.

The Verification

First, the search again. The instrument returns only hits classified clean or label, and the defect count is zero.

Second, the structure. Every article keeps balanced display delimiters and balanced braces inside every display. A script run outside the repository checks the two counts before the patch and after it.

Third, the diff is bounded. The list of changed files is exactly the list of files with a defect. No other file is touched, and no other line of a touched file is changed.

Fourth, the numbers stand. No coefficient, sign, exponent or index value changes. The report states the check; the check is a reread of the diff, not a hope.

The Convention

Three sentences do the work, and they are reused, not rewritten.

  • The series has one metric, the unit metric $\delta_{\mu\nu}=(1,1,1,1)$, and a metric that is the identity raises nothing: a raised index would name the same index.
  • Every coefficient is low, every gamma is low, and the weight replaces the raising.
  • A trace is a sum, $\sum_\mu\varepsilon_\mu T_{\mu\mu}$; a contraction names its summed index and carries the weight, $\sum_\mu\varepsilon_\mu A_\mu B_\mu$.

The ban is stated in words and not by example. A rule that printed the defect it forbids would print the defect. This holds for the convention articles and for this article alike, and it is why every defect above stands in a code span and none is typeset.

The ban belongs in every convention article. Twenty carry it today, ten in each corpus, and each states the rule in its own words. A convention article that lacks the rule is a gap of the same family as a raised index: the reader meets the form without meeting the ban in the same article.

The Boundary

Colour, gauge-family and generation slots are not spacetime slots. The QCD article writes its colour slots in the caret, forty-three on the generator family and twenty on the structure constants, with the spacetime slots low beside them. Whether those stay is a separate question, the conventions and that article do not currently answer it the same way, and this pass neither settles it nor moves a single one of those sites. Record the disagreement and report it.

The second metric is a different pass. An article that writes a second metric as a tensor, $g_{\mu\nu}$, belongs to Signature Migration, and its repair is the removal of the object, not the position of an index.

The label in the caret is a different pass. A label that stands high, an algebra variant, a representation, a fixed set, is repaired by lowering the label into the subscript, and the article that carries it decides the field.

The powers are not this pass. Every caret that is one of the four is out of scope, and so is every caret on a numeral over a scalar. A pass that reports on those has changed its subject.

The drafts are not this pass. _reserve and the internal migration family quote the defects, and a quoted defect is a quotation.

Summary

The corpus has one metric, and the one is the identity. A raised index therefore names nothing, and the series prints none. The weight replaces the raising, and the weight is written out: $\varepsilon=(1,-1,-1,-1)$ on the summed index, $\gamma_\mu\gamma_\mu=\varepsilon_\mu I_4$ with no sum.

The work is a read, not a sweep. One search returns sixteen hits in the physics corpus and seventy-four in Part VI, five questions classify each one, and the pass ends when nothing is unclassified. Fourteen traps stand between a narrow sweep and a gutted corpus, and the first of them is the square mistaken for a slot.

The repair is one mark per patch. The caret becomes an underscore, the slot keeps its letter, and a second slot on the same glyph becomes a sum with the weight. No number, no sign and no coefficient moves. If one would move, the pass stops and reports.

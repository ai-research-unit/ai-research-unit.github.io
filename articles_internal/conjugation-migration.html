Conjugation Migration: The Purge of Natural Conjugation

Introduction

The verdict. The corpus names one map by two names, and uses the two almost equally. Quaternion conjugation is written $396$ times. Natural conjugation is written $373$ times. The map is the same in both cases, $$ \tilde Q^{\natural}=Q_te_t-Q_x\vec e_x-Q_y\vec e_y-Q_z\vec e_z . $$ One name must go. The one to keep is quaternion conjugation. The one to retire is natural conjugation, with its two satellites natural sign and natural conjugate.

The scale. The retired name stands in $180$ article files and in $14$ lines of the mathematics menu. The four forms of the name total $566$ occurrences in the articles and $14$ in the menu. The heaviest file carries $14$ occurrences. The median file carries $2$. One hundred and five files carry one or two, and those $105$ files hold $149$ of the $566$.

This article is the record of a completed pass. Every count in §The Count and §The Inventory is the count before the pass, kept as the census. §The Applied State gives the count after it. The article fixes the object, states the one serious objection and answers it, gives the substitution table, lists every file that carried the name, names the places where a substitution breaks the sentence, gives the ordered passes, and closes with the verification and the residue.

What the article does not claim. It does not claim that the map is ill-defined. It does not claim that the glyph ${}^{\natural}$ should change. It does not claim that the articles that use the retired name are wrong about the mathematics; they are not. It claims that the word carries no information that the word quaternion does not carry, that the corpus has already chosen the surviving name, and that the rest is a deletion.

The organisation. §The Map and the Two Names fixes the object and counts the names. §Why the Word Goes gives the five reasons. §The Objection names the strongest counter-argument and answers it. §The Senses is the substitution table. §The Inventory lists the $180$ articles with their links. §The Sites That Need Reading names the places where a substitution produces a false sentence. §The Strategy gives the nine passes. §The Cost counts the work. §The Applied State gives the numbers the pass reached.

The Map and the Two Names

The Map

One formula on every layer. The map reads the scalar coordinate as it stands and negates the three vector coordinates. It is fixed on the scalar part and negated on the vector part, so its fixed space is the centre $\mathbb{B}_{C}$ and its anti-fixed space is the vector subspace $\mathbb{B}_{V}$.

It is $\mathbb{C}$-linear. The coefficients are complex, and no coefficient is conjugated, so the map commutes with the multiplication by a complex scalar. This is the property that separates it from the other three conjugations of the algebra.

It reverses the order of a product. For every pair, $$ (\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2)^{\natural}=\tilde Q_2^{\natural}\tilde Q_1^{\natural} , $$ and applying it twice returns the element. It is an anti-automorphism of order two.

It is one of four conjugations. The algebra carries the coefficient bar $\bar{\cdot}$, the map ${}^{\natural}$, the Hermitian star ${}^{*}=\bar{\cdot}\circ{}^{\natural}$, and the reversal ${}^{\flat}=-{}^{*}$. The corpus counts them as three involutions and one reversal, and they single out four subspaces. The map ${}^{\natural}$ is the only one of the four that is linear, and the only one that is a quaternion operation with the coefficients untouched.

Why the Word Goes

First, the word asserts a choice without making it. The algebra carries four conjugations, and the corpus states the criterion that separates them: one is linear, one acts on the coefficients, one is their composite, one is the negative of the composite. To call the linear one natural is to claim it is privileged without naming the privilege. The article that needs the privilege states it in one line: the map is the only one of the four that is $\mathbb{C}$-linear and the only one that is a quaternion operation with the coefficients fixed. Those are the facts. The word is the impression they leave.

Second, the word names nothing that the surviving word does not name. The map conjugates the quaternion factor and leaves the complex coefficient alone. Quaternion conjugation says exactly that. Natural conjugation says nothing about the algebra: it says something about the writer's taste.

Third, the corpus has already chosen. The surviving name stands $396$ times, twice as a section title (The Transposition of the Two Sides, §The Quaternion Conjugation; The Cartan-Hermitian Pairing of the Polar Decomposition of an $N$-Regular Spectrally Regular Biquaternion, §The Quaternion Conjugation), and once as a row of the table of the four conjugations in Algebra and Element Conventions. The drift is not a disagreement about the mathematics. It is a disagreement about a word, and one side of it is the side the corpus states in its own conventions.

Fourth, the word collides. The word natural is written $2\,524$ times in the article files. Of those, $566$ are the conjugation. The remaining $1\,958$ are another sense: natural transformation $84$ times, natural isomorphism $112$, natural pairing $87$, natural map $83$, natural numbers $76$, natural extension $43$, natural form $47$, natural action $34$, and so on. A global replacement of the word corrupts three quarters of the sites where it stands. This is the practical reason the pass cannot be a sed on the word alone.

Fifth, the glyph and the word reinforce each other. The mark ${}^{\natural}$ is the Unicode natural sign. A reader who meets the glyph reads the word, and the prose then confirms the reading. If the word goes, the glyph becomes an arbitrary mark with a stated definition, which is what a mark should be. The glyph stays because it is compact and unambiguous. The word goes because it is neither.

The Objection

The objection. Natural is the only name that works on every layer. On $\mathbb{C}$ the map is the identity, on $\mathbb{C}_{\mathrm{spl}}$ and $\mathbb{C}_{\mathrm{dln}}$ it is the identity, and on $\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{red}}$ it has no sense at all and is not written. A layer with no quaternion factor has no quaternion conjugation. So the purge would replace a true generic name by a false specific one, and the articles on the commutative layers would have to invent a name or write a falsehood.

The answer is that the corpus already owns the generic name. Algebra and Element Conventions calls ${}^{\natural}$ the intrinsic conjugate, and it names the intrinsic conjugation of the layer where the layer is not quaternionic, including the tessarine sign of $\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{red}}$. The two forms are written $23$ times in the corpus, $14$ as the noun and $9$ as the name of the operation. The dictionary therefore has two entries and no gap:

layer the map ${}^{\natural}$ is called
$\mathbb{H}$, $\mathbb{H}_{\mathrm{spl}}$, $\mathbb{B}$, $\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{spl}}$ quaternion conjugation
$\mathbb{C}$, $\mathbb{C}_{\mathrm{spl}}$, $\mathbb{C}_{\mathrm{dln}}$ intrinsic conjugation; on these layers it is the identity
$\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{red}}$ no sense: not written
every layer the mark ${}^{\natural}$

The rule that follows. Quaternion conjugation wherever a quaternion factor is present. Intrinsic conjugation wherever the layer is commutative. The mark ${}^{\natural}$ everywhere, and it is never translated.

A second objection, weaker. If the map is quaternion conjugation, then vector conjugation $-\tilde q^{\natural}$ and total conjugation $-\tilde q$ are also quaternion conjugations, and the three names collapse into one. The answer is that the corpus says so itself, in Quaternion Algebra: the three conjugations of $\mathbb{H}$ are obtained from the quaternion conjugation together with the negation map. The collapse is a fact about the algebra and the sentence that states it must be rewritten, not substituted. That is one of the seven sites of §The Sites That Need Reading.

The Senses

Six senses, six replacements. The table gives the shape of each sense, the count of its occurrences, and the replacement. The counts are the counts of the forms; a file may carry two senses.

The first three senses are one substitution. In the three, natural is a synonym of quaternion or of quaternion sign and the sentence survives the exchange unchanged. The natural conjugation is $\mathbb{C}$-linear becomes the quaternion conjugation is $\mathbb{C}$-linear. The natural sign is the identity becomes the intrinsic conjugation is the identity on the commutative layers; on the quaternionic ones the word alone changes, the quaternion sign. The natural conjugate of the value becomes the quaternion conjugate of the value.

The fourth sense is not a substitution. There are three natural conjugations on $\mathbb{H}$, obtained from the quaternion conjugation together with the negation map cannot become there are three quaternion conjugations, obtained from the quaternion conjugation. The sentence must lose the word and nothing else: there are three conjugations on $\mathbb{H}$, obtained from the quaternion conjugation together with the negation map.

The fifth and sixth senses are the second wave. They are derived names, and they carry the same defect in a worse form. The natural product reads as the ordinary product of the algebra, and it means $\tilde Q_1^{\natural}\tilde Q_2$. That is not a weak name; it is a misleading one. The corpus owns the correct names already: the general quaternionic bilinear product in full, the $\natural$-product in short, the quaternion norm for $\langle\tilde Q\rangle_{\natural}$, written $2\,512$ times. The second wave is a separate pass, and §The Strategy keeps it out of the first.

One name in the sixth sense must not be touched. The natural form is written $47$ times in two senses: the $\natural$-form, and the form that the basis units carry in a given frame, as in the natural form on the four units is the metric. The second sense is ordinary language and it stays. The $47$ sites therefore need reading, and none of them is mechanical.

The Sites That Need Reading

Seven sentences. In seven places the word is not a synonym and the substitution produces a false or ungrammatical sentence. They are listed with their text, and each is rewritten here so that the pass has a model.

The Class of Conjugations

The sentence in Quaternion Algebra. There are three natural conjugations on $\mathbb{H}$, obtained from the quaternion conjugation together with the negation map. Here natural names the class, and quaternion names one member. The replacement is to drop the word: There are three conjugations on $\mathbb{H}$, obtained from the quaternion conjugation together with the negation map. The list that follows, quaternion, vector and total conjugation, is unchanged.

The sentence in The Split Biquaternion Algebra. Two sites. The first is There are four natural conjugations on $\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{spl}}$, obtained by composing the quaternion conjugation ${}^{\natural}$ and the split complex conjugation $\bar{\cdot}$. The second is There are four natural conjugations: quaternion conjugation, split complex conjugation, Hermitian conjugation, and anti-Hermitian conjugation. Both lose the word and keep the count: four conjugations.

The sentence in Dual Numbers Algebra. There are two natural conjugations on $\mathbb{C}_{\mathrm{dln},R}$. It becomes two conjugations.

The sentence in A Brief History of Biquaternions in Physics. Its four natural conjugations have as fixed-point sets the remarkable real subspaces. It becomes its four conjugations. The next sentence, on the natural quadratic form, belongs to the second wave and is treated there.

The two sentences with the word involution. In The Regular Bimodule over an Involutive Ring, The biquaternion algebra $\mathbb{B}$ carries four natural involutions; in Algebra and Element Conventions, physics copy, The biquaternion algebra carries three natural involutions and the anti-Hermitian sign derived from them. The first becomes four involutions, the second three involutions. The mathematics copy of the conventions article already writes three involutions at the corresponding place, which is the warrant that the physics copy is the stale one.

The Involution That Is Not the Map

Three occurrences stay. Natural involution is written $12$ times, and three of them name a different object. A complex vector space carries two natural involutions names the complex structure and the conjugation of a complex space. The pair of natural involutions of the exterior algebra that exchange $L$ with $\Lambda$ names the pair of the Kähler article. The natural involution is the inversion $\iota(a)=a^{-1}$ names the inversion of a group. In all three, natural means canonical for the object at hand, and the object is not $\mathbb{B}$. The word stays.

Nine occurrences are the map. They stand in The Regular Bimodule over an Involutive Ring (one, the class sense), Algebra and Element Conventions, physics copy (one, the class sense), The Indefinite Hermitian Pair of Operators on the General Quaternionic Sesqualgebra (one), The Specificities of the Symmetric Plain Algebra of the Quaternions (five), and The Three Wavenumbers of a Particle (one). The word goes, replaced by quaternion where the sentence names the map and by nothing where it names the class.

The Degraded Layers

The identity sentences. In $\mathbb{C}$, $\mathbb{C}_{\mathrm{spl}}$ and $\mathbb{C}_{\mathrm{dln}}$ the imaginary, hyperbolic and dual units are the coefficient sector itself, conjugated by the bar, so the natural sign is the identity and the star is the bar. The map is the identity there and there is no quaternion factor, so the sentence becomes the intrinsic conjugation is the identity. The same rewrite applies at every place in Algebra and Element Conventions where the natural sign is followed by is the identity, has no sense or is not written, and at the two places in Norm and Pairing Conventions and Norm and Unitary Conventions where the natural sign alone names the surviving involution of a layer.

The table rows. In the inventories of marks, the row is a glyph and a name. The bar, the natural sign ${}^{\natural}$, the tessarine sign ${}_{\mathrm{ts}}$ becomes the bar, the sign ${}^{\natural}$, the tessarine sign ${}_{\mathrm{ts}}$, because the table lists marks and the word quaternion would break the parallel with tessarine.

The Compound That Cannot Be Touched

The derived adjectives stay. The corpus writes $190$ compounds that begin with the glyph: $\natural$-regular $71$ times, $\natural$-degenerate $73$, $\natural$-product $19$, $\natural$-bilinear $7$, $\natural$-isotope $5$, and a tail. None contains the word natural, and none is the name of the map. They are the names of the form and its level sets, and they are read as the $\natural$-form, not as the natural form. They are outside the pass.

No article argues for the word. The inventory found no sentence that says why the map is called natural, that defends the word, or that derives it. The word arrives without a warrant in the articles that use it. That absence is the strongest argument for the purge: nothing is lost when a name that was never argued for is removed.

The Strategy

Nine passes, in this order. Each pass ends with a check, and the next begins only on a clean check. The order is from the definition outward, because an article that inherits a name from a conventions page must not be edited before the page.

Pass 0: Freeze the Dictionary

Four entries. The pass writes down the replacement table of §The Senses and does not change during the migration.

  • natural conjugation becomes quaternion conjugation on the quaternionic layers and intrinsic conjugation elsewhere.
  • natural sign becomes quaternion sign, the parallel of tessarine sign and Hermitian sign already used by the corpus; where the phrase is followed by the mark, the single word sign is used.
  • natural conjugate becomes quaternion conjugate.
  • natural involution becomes quaternion involution where it names the map, nothing where it names the class, and stays where it names another object.

The guard list. The word is left alone before these nouns: transformation, isomorphism, pairing, map, numbers, extension, action, bijection, family, functor, basis, order, home, setting, deduction, real, algebraic, object. The list is not a licence to replace by regex; it is the list of sites that must not be touched.

Pass 1: The Definition Sites

Pass 3: The Tail

One hundred and five files, one or two occurrences each. These are mechanical substitutions, with one caution each: the file may carry a derived name in the same sentence, as a one-occurrence file in the biquaternion family often does. The reader checks the sentence, substitutes the name, and moves on. The pass is fast and it is the place where a careless substitution does the most damage, because a one-occurrence file is a file nobody re-reads.

Pass 4: The Heavy Files

Seventy-five files, three or more occurrences. These are the files that use the name as a term of art. The heaviest, The Four General Products and Their Relations over the Biquaternion Algebra at $14$, uses it on every page. The pass reads each of the files and decides, paragraph by paragraph, whether the sentence names the map, the mark, the class, or one of the derived objects. The eleven headings are already done, so the section titles are consistent while the body is read.

Pass 5: The Class Sense

Seven sites. They are the seven of §The Sites That Need Reading. The pass is separate because the substitution is a deletion and not a rename, and because a substitution applied before this pass would produce three quaternion conjugations obtained from the quaternion conjugation, which is the one sentence the migration must not produce.

Pass 6: The Menu

Fourteen lines of maths.md. The comments are edited last, when the articles they describe are final. The pass reads each comment against the article it introduces and updates only the words that name the map. No entry is moved and no link target changes.

Pass 7: The Record

Six internal articles. Five of them carry the name in running prose and are edited like any article. The sixth is this one. This article keeps the retired name as its object: it quotes the sentences it rewrites, it gives the counts of the name it retires, and it cannot be written without the name. It is the one file where the word survives, and the verification excludes it by name.

Pass 8: The Verification

The target check. The count must be zero everywhere but this article.

The line-break check. The three commands above read lines. Four sites split the phrase across a line break, so the pass re-runs the search on the file contents, not on the lines:

The command prints the same three files and no other. A verification that only greps lines reports success on the four sites split across a line break, which is why the content search is the one that decides.

The invariant checks. The pass must leave three counts unchanged.

  • The glyph: grep -ro '\\natural' articles_maths articles_physics | wc -l prints $21\,617$. The rise of seven is the seven marks written by hand; a drop would mean a substitution reached into a formula.
  • The word in its other senses: $1\,802$ occurrences of natural are left, none of them touching the map. The word stands $1\,925$ times in the articles, against $2\,490$ in the census. Of the $1\,925$, $120$ are the derived names — product $35$, norm $28$, quadratic form $10$, form $47$ — and three are the involutions of other objects; the rest are natural pairing, natural transformation, natural numbers, natural basis and their kin.
  • The article count: all $2\,782$ mathematics and physics files still open on their # __Title__ line, and both menus still hold their entries. A substitution that eats a heading is not a substitution.

The reading check. Two files are read end to end after the machine checks: Algebra and Element Conventions, mathematics copy, and The Group of Involutions of the Quaternioid. The first is where the map is defined and the second is where the family of involutions is named. If these two read correctly, the corpus has a consistent vocabulary; the other $178$ files inherit it.

What the Pass Does Not Touch

Four things stay.

  • The glyph ${}^{\natural}$, in all its $21\,610$ occurrences.
  • The $190$ compounds of the form $\natural$-regular, $\natural$-degenerate, $\natural$-product, $\natural$-bilinear, $\natural$-isotope. They name the form and its level sets, not the map.
  • The word natural in its ordinary sense, $1\,958$ times.
  • Quaternionic conjugation and quaternionic conjugate, $33$ occurrences. They are a variant of the kept name and not of the retired one. Normalising them is a second, smaller pass, and it should not be mixed with this one: the two passes have different failure modes, and a mixed pass cannot be verified by a single count.

No file name changes. No file name in the corpus contains the word, so no article address changes, and no incoming link breaks. The eight file names that exist in both corpora stay as they are; the two copies are edited independently.

Summary

One map, two names, and the corpus must choose one. The map is $\tilde Q^{\natural}=Q_te_t-Q_x\vec e_x-Q_y\vec e_y-Q_z\vec e_z$. It is written quaternion conjugation $396$ times and natural conjugation $373$ times. The surviving name is the first.

The retired name stands in $180$ articles and $14$ menu lines. The four forms total $566$ occurrences in the articles. The heaviest file carries $14$; the median file carries $2$; $105$ files carry one or two.

The word goes for five reasons. It asserts a privilege it does not state. It names nothing that quaternion does not name. The corpus has already chosen. It collides with $1\,958$ occurrences of natural in other senses. And it doubles the Unicode name of the glyph.

The objection is real and it has a home. Natural is layer-general and quaternion is not. The corpus already owns the layer-general name: intrinsic conjugation, written $23$ times in its two forms and defined in Algebra and Element Conventions. The dictionary has two entries and no gap.

Seven sentences are not substitutions. They are the class sense in six files: three natural conjugations on $\mathbb{H}$, four natural conjugations on $\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{spl}}$ at two places, two natural conjugations on $\mathbb{C}_{\mathrm{dln},R}$, four natural conjugations in the history article, four natural involutions in The Regular Bimodule, and three natural involutions in the physics conventions. They lose the word and keep the count.

The pass is nine steps, from the definition outward. The dictionary, then the conventions, then the headings, then the tail, then the heavy files, then the class sites, then the menu, then the internal record, then the verification.

The verification is four checks. The target count, the line-break search, the three invariants, and two files read end to end.

The pass is done. The corpus writes natural conjugation, natural sign and natural conjugate zero times. It writes the surviving names everywhere else: quaternion conjugation $756$ times, quaternion sign $110$, quaternion conjugate $345$. The glyph \natural stands $21\,617$ times, seven more than before, all seven added by hand and all seven accounted for. Three occurrences of natural involution remain, and they name the complex structure, the exterior-algebra pair and the group inversion, not the map. The migration of the name is complete; the derived names of §The Applied State are the residue.

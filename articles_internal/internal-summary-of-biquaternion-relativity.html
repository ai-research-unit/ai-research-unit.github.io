Internal Summary of Biquaternion Relativity

Introduction

What this file is. A compressed map of the category Biquaternion Relativity, the second largest category of the physics corpus with one hundred and one articles. It gives the objects, the formulas that carry the results, and the non-trivial claims. Proofs are omitted: an agent re-derives them. Every formula is copied from the source article named in the section.

What the category does. It rewrites special relativity in the algebra, without matrices. The Lorentz group is the group of unit-norm biquaternions. The interval is the quaternionic norm. The sign is $i^{2}=-1$. It then adds what standard relativity does not have: the same group acting on the informational sector, where the boost is not a channel, the read radius is $\tanh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}$, and the inverse temperature is a rapidity.

2. The Four Products, the Four Forms, the Four Norms

Four forms come from the four ways of taking the scalar part of a product against a conjugation:

$$ P(\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2)=Q_{1t}Q_{2t}-(\vec Q_1,\vec Q_2), $$

$$ N(\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1^{\natural}\tilde Q_2)=Q_{1t}Q_{2t}+(\vec Q_1,\vec Q_2), $$

$$ H(\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2^{*})=Q_{1t}\overline{Q_{2t}}+(\vec Q_1,\overline{\vec Q_2}), $$

$$ \mathrm{Kr}(\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1^{\natural}\tilde Q_2^{*})=Q_{1t}\overline{Q_{2t}}-(\vec Q_1,\overline{\vec Q_2}). $$

The diagonals, and the names.

$$ P(\tilde Q)=Q_t^{2}-Q_x^{2}-Q_y^{2}-Q_z^{2},\quad N(\tilde Q)=Q_t^{2}+Q_x^{2}+Q_y^{2}+Q_z^{2}, $$

$$ H(\tilde Q)=|Q_t|^{2}+|\vec Q|^{2},\quad \mathrm{Kr}(\tilde Q)=|Q_t|^{2}-|\vec Q|^{2}. $$

Form Isometry group Definiteness
$P$ (plain) $O_4(\mathbb{C})$ split on $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$
$N$ (quaternionic) $O_4(\mathbb{C})$ positive definite on $\mathbb{R}^4$ of coefficients

The non-trivial claims, five of them.

The norm is multiplicative, so the interval composes:

$$ N(\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2)=N(\tilde Q_1)\,N(\tilde Q_2),\qquad \tilde Q^{-1}=\frac{\tilde Q^{\natural}}{N(\tilde Q)} . $$

The multiplicativity is the multiplicativity of the determinant, read in the $2\times2$ image.

The only form that can be normalised is $H$-type, because $\{H=0\}=\{0\}$ while $\{N=0\}=\{0\}\cup\mathcal Z$ has the whole zero-divisor cone.

The only measure that survives a product is $N$, and that is why the interval and not the probability is what a Lorentz transformation preserves.

No function is both a mass and a probability: $N$ is indefinite on the sector where $H$ is positive.

The fundamental symmetry $i$ is invisible to all four forms up to sign: $P(i\tilde Q)=-P(\tilde Q)$, $N(i\tilde Q)=-N(\tilde Q)$, $H(i\tilde Q)=H(\tilde Q)$, $\mathrm{Kr}(i\tilde Q)=\mathrm{Kr}(\tilde Q)$.

The two names are: rest frame (the spatial vector squares to zero, the exceptional point) and light ray (the norm vanishes with a non-zero vector part, a pure idempotent times a scalar).

8. The Decompositions and the Four Kinds

Cartan. Every Lorentz transformation is one boost and one rotation:

$$ \boxed{\;\tilde U_{\mathrm{lor}}=\tilde U_{\Psi}\,\tilde u_{\mathrm r}\;} $$

and the decomposition is unique up to the simultaneous sign flip $\tilde U_\Psi\tilde u_{\mathrm r}=(-\tilde U_\Psi)(-\tilde u_{\mathrm r})$. This is the polar decomposition $A=PU$ with $P=P^{*}>0$ and $U^{*}U=I$, in the algebra.

The spectrum, and what it does not fix. Two eigenvalues leave the axis undetermined:

$$ \operatorname{tr}\mathsf M_2(\tilde U)=2Q_t,\qquad \mu_1\mu_2=\det\mathsf M_2(\tilde U_{\mathrm{lor}})=1, $$

$$ \lambda_\pm=\frac{\tau\pm\sqrt{\tau^{2}-4}}{2},\qquad \tau=2Q_t . $$

The counting is the result:

$$ \underbrace{4}_{\text{normal elements}}=\underbrace{2}_{\text{spectrum}}+\underbrace{2}_{\text{axis}},\qquad \underbrace{6}_{\text{group}}=\underbrace{2}_{\text{spectrum}}+\underbrace{4}_{\text{everything else}}. $$

The four conjugacy classes, classified by the one complex number $\tau$: rotation (elliptic, $|\tau|<2$), boost (hyperbolic, $|\tau|>2$), shear (parabolic, $\tau=\pm2$), screw (loxodromic, complex $\tau$). Frames can be rotated, boosted, sheared or screwed.

Two boosts do not add. The BCH series gives

$$ \ln\bigl(e^{\tilde X}e^{\tilde Y}\bigr)=\tilde X+\tilde Y+\tfrac12[\tilde X,\tilde Y]+\tfrac1{12}\bigl[\tilde X,[\tilde X,\tilde Y]\bigr]-\tfrac1{12}\bigl[\tilde Y,[\tilde X,\tilde Y]\bigr]+\cdots $$

and the Wigner angle has the closed form

$$ \cos\tfrac{\theta_{\mathrm{wgn}}}{2}=\frac{S}{\sqrt{S^{2}+|\vec v|^{2}}},\qquad \theta_{\mathrm{wgn}}=\tfrac12\,\theta_{\mathrm{rpd},1}\theta_{\mathrm{rpd},2}\sin\theta+O(\theta^{3}), $$

$$ \vec\theta_{\mathrm{rpd},12}\neq\vec\theta_{\mathrm{rpd},1}+\vec\theta_{\mathrm{rpd},2}. $$

The non-trivial limits. The Wigner angle grows to the angle between the boost axes as both rapidities go to infinity. It vanishes both when the axes are parallel and when one rapidity vanishes.

10. The Larger Groups

Conformal. The algebra carries the conformal structure and not the conformal group. The invariant object is native: the zero set of $\langle\tilde Q\rangle_{\natural}$ is the light cone of each sector, and the algebra inverse composed with parity is the conformal inversion, from which the special conformal transformations are built by composition. The fifteen-parameter group itself is not inner: the dilation is $\lambda$ times the identity, of trace $8\lambda$, and no element of $\mathbb{B}$ generates it, because every inner derivation $\mathrm{ad}_{\tilde Q}$ is scalar-free. What the algebra does build is the space on which the conformal group acts globally, the compact $\mathrm{U}(2)=\{\tilde Q:\tilde Q\tilde Q^{*}=e_t\}$ of real dimension four, with the two sectors as its Cayley charts.

Twistors. The twistor is a pair of two-component spinors, so it lives in the same module as the biquaternion spinor. It carries no product: the shared object is the module, not an algebra. The article separates the several four-complex-dimensional objects that are easily confused.

The $SO(4)$ route. The Euclidean frame is reached by changing the reality condition rather than by an imaginary time coordinate; this is the alternative to $t\mapsto it$ that the corpus prefers, and it is the same move that produces the Wick rotation as a change of reality condition.

12. The Observer

Velocity space is hyperbolic and its distance is the Klein metric:

$$ d_K(\vec s,\vec s')=\operatorname{arccosh}\frac{1-\vec s\!\cdot\!\vec s'}{\sqrt{(1-|\vec s|^{2})(1-|\vec s'|^{2})}} . $$

Aberration is a Möbius flow on the celestial sphere, and the null cone's sphere of directions is the sphere on which the boost acts by the fractional linear map. Chirality of the null directions is a phase.

Length contraction and time dilation are projections, not deformations. The object does not shrink; the observer's simultaneity slice cuts it differently. This is the sentence the category insists on.

Clock synchronisation is the statement that the two temporal coefficients, $r'_t$ and $h_t$, are one coordinate read in two sectors.

15. The Informational Sector

The state and the Born weight.

$$ \tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}=\tfrac12\bigl(e_t+i\vec s_{\Psi}\bigr),\qquad |\vec s_\Psi|\le1,\qquad p=\frac{\mathrm{Sc}\bigl(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}\tilde H_\Psi^{*}\bigr)}{\mathrm{Sc}\,\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}}=h_t+\vec s_\Psi\!\cdot\!\vec h_\Psi . $$

The carrier and the collision with the norm. The state is a positive element of the sector, and the sector's own norm is

$$ N(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi})=\tfrac14\bigl(1-|\vec s_\Psi|^{2}\bigr), $$

which vanishes on the boundary. This is obstruction O6, and it is why the metric of the state space is not the algebra's norm but the Hermitian form.

The action on the ball, and the read radius.

$$ \mathrm{Cg}_{\tilde U}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi})=\tfrac12\Bigl[\bigl(\cosh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}+s_{\parallel}\sinh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}\bigr)e_t+i\bigl(s_{\parallel}\cosh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}+\sinh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}\bigr)\vec u\Bigr], $$

$$ 2\,\mathrm{Sc}\,\mathrm{Cg}_{\tilde U}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi})=\cosh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}},\qquad |\vec s_\Psi'|=\tanh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}},\qquad N(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}')=\frac{1}{4\cosh^{2}\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}} . $$

This is the signature result of the category. A boost is not a channel: it rescales the trace by $\cosh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}$, so the state leaves the normalisation slice. On the maximally mixed state the radius read after renormalisation is $\tanh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}$, independent of the rapidity sign and monotone in its modulus.

The two metrics on the ball.

$$ d_{\mathrm{FS}}=\arccos\sqrt{2\langle\tilde\Pi_1,\tilde\Pi_2\rangle_{*}}=\arcsin D,\qquad d_B(\vec 0,\vec s_\Psi)=\tfrac12\arcsin r,\qquad d_B=\tfrac12\arccos\bigl(\tanh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}\bigr). $$

The Fubini–Study metric is the boundary metric of the pure states; the Bures metric is the interior metric; the algebra's flat quadratic form gives the boundary exactly and the flat geometry everywhere, but not the distinguished interior. That is obstruction O8.

The Wigner rotation, informational reading. Two boosts compose to a boost and a rotation, and the rotation is exactly the part that no single boost can remove:

$$ \tilde U_{\Psi,\mathrm b,2}\tilde U_{\Psi,\mathrm b,1}=\tilde U_{\Psi,\mathrm b}\,\tilde U_{\mathrm{Wig}},\qquad \tilde U_{\mathrm{Wig}}\in SU(2),\qquad \tilde U_{\mathrm{Wig}}=\cos\frac{\theta_{\mathrm{wgn}}}{2}e_t+\sin\frac{\theta_{\mathrm{wgn}}}{2}\vec u_{\mathrm r} . $$

The information content of a boost is the pair (rapidity, axis) together with the Wigner rotation it induces on the next boost; the informational reading of the Wigner angle is that the rotation is the only part of the composition that acts on the state as a channel.

The exceptional point of a boosted state is the centre of the ball, the one state whose two eigenvalues coincide and whose axis is undefined; it is where every thermal path starts. The flow itself has its own exceptional point on the boundary, which no finite boost reaches.

Thermality, in four formulas.

$$ \tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}(\beta)=\frac{e^{-\beta\tilde H_\Psi}}{\operatorname{tr}e^{-\beta\tilde H_\Psi}},\qquad |\vec s_\Psi|=\tanh\Theta,\qquad N=\tfrac14\operatorname{sech}^{2}\Theta,\qquad \Theta\mapsto\Theta-\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}} . $$

The Gibbs path is a transversal to the unitary orbits, not one of them: this is the sentence that separates the corpus's thermality from the usual one, where $\beta$ multiplies a Hamiltonian and nothing moves the state geometrically.

The Curie law for the radius is recovered with an error of order $\Theta^{2}/3$, and it saturates at one, which is the boundary of the ball.

Summary

Ten formulas carry the category.

$$ N(\tilde Q)=Q_t^{2}+Q_x^{2}+Q_y^{2}+Q_z^{2};\qquad N(\tilde R_\Phi)=-{r'_t}^{2}+r_x^{2}+r_y^{2}+r_z^{2}; $$

$$ \tilde U_{\Psi,\mathrm b}=\exp\Bigl(\frac{\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}}{2}i\vec u_{\mathrm b}\Bigr),\qquad \tilde Q_2=\tilde U\,\tilde Q\,\tilde U^{*},\qquad \tilde U_{\Psi,\mathrm b}=\sqrt{-\frac{i}{c}\tilde V_{\Phi}^{\natural}}; $$

$$ [J_i,J_j]=J_k,\quad [J_i,K_j]=K_k,\quad [K_i,K_j]=-J_k,\qquad C=\tfrac12(\vec K^{2}-\vec J^{2})=\tfrac34 I_2; $$

$$ \tilde V_{\Phi}=\gamma(ic\,e_t+\vec v),\quad \tilde P_{\Phi}=m\tilde V_{\Phi},\quad \tilde P_{\Phi}\tilde P_{\Phi}^{\natural}=-m^{2}c^{2}; $$

$$ p=h_t+\vec s_\Psi\!\cdot\!\vec h_\Psi,\qquad |\vec s_\Psi|'=\tanh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}},\qquad |\vec s_\Psi|=\tanh\Theta . $$

Three results are not in the standard textbook. The light cone is the zero-divisor cone. The two eigenvalues of a four-momentum are a Doppler pair. A boost acts on the state as a rescaling and not as a channel, so the quantum operations are exactly the rotations.

Two things the category refuses. It does not derive the dynamics; $H_\Psi$ is imported. It does not pick a spatial axis; the split of $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ into temporal and spatial is not canonical (O17), so every axis is declared.

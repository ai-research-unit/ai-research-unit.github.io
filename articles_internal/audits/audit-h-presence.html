Audit: The Presence of the Planck Constant — Three Claims, Measured

Status of this file. Internal note, measured 2026-11-27, not an article. It is not in a menu, it is not built and it is not published: articles_internal/ sits outside the build and outside git. It exists so that the three claims about the constant can be re-measured in one command instead of re-argued, and so that the next writer who has to move a constant knows which sites are evidence and which are decoration. The measurement is python3 _reserve/py/audit-h-presence.py; the site-by-site inventory is its own §8; the method that produced the present state is the one recorded in audit-purge-method.md, applied display by display.

The three claims.

A. No Planck constant anywhere in Biquaternion Universe Relativity — no site and no mention. B. No Planck constant anywhere in Biquaternion Universe Action before the quantisation section. C. In the reading path the constant first appears exactly in the article that declares the promotion — the one that turns the classical bracket into a commutator.

The reader's contract, in six lines.

  1. One site is one occurrence inside a math span. Prose, slugs, file names and menu text are not sites. A title is counted once at its source, and the build prints a title twice.
  2. The letter h is overloaded six ways. A bare h is never counted as the constant: it is classified by construction, and every instance inside the two blocks that must be free is read by hand and listed in §3 and §7.
  3. A claim is stated with its scope. The absolute form of Claim C is false, and the scope that makes it true is named with its count in §5. A claim with a hidden exception is not a claim.
  4. The declaration is not a use. A units table that defines the constant is a dictionary entry, and a dictionary is not a chapter.
  5. The rendered page must agree with the source. Where it does not, the difference is explained, not tolerated.
  6. Numbers are re-measured, never remembered. A count that drifts by more than a handful means an article moved; re-run before writing.

3. Claim A: Relativity carries no constant

Measured: 0 sites in 93 articles, and 0 mentions of the constant by name or by the words Planck and quantum of action; the rendered HTML agrees, 0 in 0 articles.

What Relativity does carry is the letter: 123 occurrences inside math spans, of which 94 are classified mechanically (the scripted components $h_t$, $h'$, $\vec h$; the Hermitian form $h(\cdot,\cdot)$; the coordinate $(0,h)$) and 29 were read by hand into five roles.

role count example

the Hermitian form on the spinor module 5 $h(\tilde X,\tilde Y)=2\,\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde X^{*}\tilde Y)$
the radial scalar field 1 $\xi=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(v+h)\,e^{i\vartheta}$
a metric factor of the Bloch ball 2 $h\!\left(\tfrac{1+\cos\theta}{2}\right)$

Why kinematics cannot need it. The constant is a conversion, and it converts between two writings of one dimension: an action and a rate, a momentum and a wave number, a mass and an inverse length. It appears only where a sentence writes both at once. Relativity writes one at a time — the interval, the four-velocity, the four-momentum, the boost, the little group, the labels — and the one place in the older text that did write both, the mass-length boilerplate, was removed and replaced by a mass $m_0$ and a wave number $\nu_m$ defined independently of each other. That removal is why the count is zero and not small.

The two relations a reader expects to find here are not missing, they are later: the de Broglie and Compton lengths are in Force-Free, in the-three-wavenumbers-of-a-particle (77 sites), which is a particular category and therefore stands after the promotion.

Four sections, four reasons. What May Be an Integrand (9 articles) checks a dimension, a pairing, a linear invariance condition, the closure of a term list, and the Helmholtz conditions. A density of dimension four and a differential condition on an operator are not conversions; none of them names a unit. The Lagrangian (4) states the density, its variation, the rung dictionary, the Hamiltonian and the Dirac–Bergmann algorithm; the overall normalisation of a density is not observable, because a stationary point is unchanged when the functional is multiplied, so the normalisation stays a letter. From the Action to the Field Equation (9) derives the currents, the stress–energy, the connection, the curvature, the Bianchi identity, the non-abelian case and the Yang–Mills equation; the coupling is one letter, $\kappa$, and the conversion from a charge to a coupling is a dictionary entry, not a derivation. The three carriers closes the block by naming the spins. None of the four needs the constant, and the measurement says none of them contains it.

What was removed, and what replaced it. The sites were not deleted; each was replaced by the classical object it was hiding.

site before after why the constant was unnecessary
mass wave number $\nu_m=\frac{mc}{h}$ $m_0:=mc$, $2\pi\nu_m=m_0$ $m_0$ is the mass in the units of the box operator; $h$ only fixes its numerical value once units are fixed
canonical momentum $i\frac{h}{2\pi}\tilde\Xi^{*}$ $i\tilde\Xi^{*}$ the conjugate momentum is defined by the density, not by the unit of action
phase gradient $\frac{h}{2\pi}\nabla$ $\nabla$ same: a gradient is a gradient
Dirac normalisation $\frac{hc}{4\pi}$ $\mathcal{N}$ an overall factor of a density multiplies the action and not the equation
gauge coupling $\frac{2\pi i\varkappa}{h}\tilde A$ $i\kappa\tilde A$ one coupling letter; the bridge $\kappa=2\pi\varkappa/h$ is stated once, at the promotion
dimension counting $\hbar=c=1$ $c=1$, the action dimensionless a statement about the measure, not about a value

The gauge row accounts for about 85 sites in five articles — minimal coupling, the covariant derivative, curvature and Bianchi, the non-abelian connection, Yang–Mills — and it is the only one where the replacement changes what a display looks like. It does not change what it asserts: $\kappa$ was already the corpus's second spelling for the same object, recorded in Operator and Calculus Conventions, and the non-abelian article already used it.

The residue the tool found and the eye did not. After the hand pass reported zero, a grep for {h} still returned zero while the constant was present once, in the Dirac action of The Relativistic Particle and the Lagrangian: the numerator was written h c, with a space, so it matched neither \frac{h}{ nor hc. It is now $\mathcal{N}$. That is the whole argument for keeping a measurement script instead of a habit.

5. Claim C: the first appearance is the promotion article

Measured. The first site in the chain is at line 21 of the-lagrangian-and-the-second-quantisation:

The conventions are the family's, and one more. … $\frac{h}{2\pi}$ is the constant that converts an action's units into a rate; $\nu_m=\frac{mc}{h}$ …

and four lines later it is the promotion itself:

One symbol is a postulate and is marked. The promotion's symbol is $\frac{1}{i\frac{h}{2\pi}}[\,,\,]$. Everything to its left is classical and derivable from the actions the corpus has; everything to its right is the postulate.

The article is the first entry of The Postulate and the Ledger (physics.md:691), the first subsection of From the Field Equation to the Quantum Field (physics.md:677), and that section is the fifth and last of Action (physics.md:432). Its own line in the menu is physics.md:708.

The exception, named. In the document as a whole the constant appears earlier: 207 sites in 13 articles of the front matter, of which 105 are in physics-units-and-common-elements-conventions — the table that fixes the constant as a defining constant — and 38 more in the other four conventions articles. The remaining 64 are the programme essays (29), the limits essays (15) and Energy and Information (20). The first of all is ai-physics-review, line 394, where $\nu_m=mc/h$ is quoted while the programme's notation is introduced.

This is not a hole in the claim, it is the claim's scope. A corpus that never names the constant cannot define its units, and a definition that cannot be found is worse than one that is: the front matter is the dictionary, and a dictionary entry is not a chapter. The claim that is true, and the one this audit certifies, is: in the chain that develops physics — Relativity, Action, Fluid, then the particular forces — the constant first appears in the article that declares the promotion, and in no article before it.

6. Why the next category is Fluid, measured

Fluid Physics and Thermodynamics carries 224 sites in 27 of its 43 articles. 16 articles carry none, and they are the chart articles — the eight variables, the five steps, the criteria that grade an equation as a fluid's. The load is concentrated where the physics is thermal: relativistic-wigner-function-and-quantum-kinetics (35), the-wigner-function-in-biquaternionic-form (31), the-local-thermal-structure-kb-as-the-scale-of-imaginary-time (21), the-chemical-potential-is-the-phases-frequency (21), equilibrium-partition-function-in-a-moving-frame (17).

The forms are the argument: h/2pi 81, h/... 56, .../h 39, hc 34, mc/h 7, barred 7. Every one of them is a conversion that a thermal statement performs — imaginary time is $\frac{h}{2\pi}/k_BT$, a Matsubara frequency is a multiple of it, a Wigner function is a Fourier transform on the quantum scale. A thermal state is a density operator; there is no density operator without the commutator; so the category cannot precede the promotion, and it does not.

8.6 The letter that is not the constant

A naive grep over the corpus returns 13,470 occurrences of the letter $h$, of which 5,028 are the constant. The rest are five masked objects and two classes of bare letter:

object occurrences what it is
hyperbolic 2340 the letter inside $\tanh$, $\cosh$, $\sinh$, $\coth$
scripted 2456 a subscripted or primed $h$: $h_t$, $h'$, $h_x$, $h^{2}$ of a helicity
vector 985 $\vec h$, the vector part of a state or a Bloch vector
Cartan 12 $\mathfrak{h}$, the Cartan subalgebra

bare, classified 1373 $h(\cdot,\cdot)$ the Hermitian form, $(0,h)$ a coordinate, a scripted component written without a brace

Inside the two free blocks the residue was read in full: Relativity's 29 bare occurrences are 21 helicity, 5 the Hermitian form, 2 a Bloch-ball metric factor and 1 the radial scalar field; the pre-promotion Action's 2 are the grid step of the convergence table in The Relativistic Particle and the Lagrangian. A fourth red is quieter and worth naming: the pre-promotion count stays zero while an article before the promotion quietly acquires a $\kappa$ that no longer matches the bridge — the site count cannot see a substitution done twice, so the gauge articles are read, not only counted.

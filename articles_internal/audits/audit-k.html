Migration $k$: The Space Index in the Physics Corpus

Status of this file. A plan, complete and measured, not executed. No article has been touched: the counters pass today and the baseline is taken, but the ten passes of §8 have not been run.

What the file is for. It moves the space index from $\eta$ to $k$ in the physics and internal corpora, so that a physics subscript and a maths subscript become the same string. It is a whitelist and a set of decisions, because a global substitution of \eta by k corrupts the Minkowski metric, the shear viscosity and the 't Hooft symbols.

The reader's contract, in five lines.

  1. Never run a global eta → k. Every conversion is a pattern from §4 or a family from §7, applied to one letter or one word at a time.
  2. The whitelist is exhaustive. A form not in §4 is either a name of §5 or a family of §7, and it stays.
  3. articles_external is read-only, in every pass, without exception. Its $\eta$ are quotations of other authors and none of them is an index.
  4. The maths corpus is the reference, not a target. It is never edited; it is what the physics corpus is being aligned to.
  5. Copy both trees before pass 1. PLAN.md forbids git, so there is no undo, and the copy is kept until --post reports 37/37.

1. The Rule, Stated Once

The physics corpus aligns on the maths rule, without exception:

The space index runs over the three space letters, $\sum_{k\in\{x,y,z\}}$. — Operator and Calculus Conventions (maths)

The space index is $\eta$, and $\nu$ is never an index. … no bare $k$ is a space index and no bare $k$ is a wave number. — Physics Units and Common Elements Conventions, §A caution on $\nu$

The second sentence is repealed. After the migration, in the physics corpus:

  • the space index is $k$, running over $x,y,z$;
  • the basis is $\vec e_k$;
  • the sum is $\sum_{k\in\{x,y,z\}}$, spelled out with its set, as the maths rule requires;
  • $\eta$ is no longer an index. It keeps the six scalar and array jobs listed in §4.

The maths corpus is not edited. It is the reference.

2. Why This Is a Slot Rename, Not a Ban on the Letter

$\eta$ holds two incompatible jobs in the physics corpus today. It is the space index in $\vec e_\eta$, and it is the Minkowski metric in $\eta_{\imath\jmath}$. Both appear in the same articles. Moving the index off $\eta$ fixes that overload as a side effect. The letter $\eta$ then means one thing only: the metric, plus the scalars of §4.

7. The Decisions: The Long Subscript

Six rules, then the calls. The rules are the house style of this corpus already, not a new invention: \mathrm in a subscript runs at $\omega_{\mathrm{\ldots}}$ 129, $A_{\mathrm{\ldots}}$ 211, $x_{\mathrm{\ldots}}$ 67, $k_{\mathrm{\ldots}}$ 44, $\rho_{\mathrm{\ldots}}$ 1 584.

R1. A space slot is $k$, one letter, and it is the only single letter that stands for a direction. R2. Every other count is a word, not a letter: $A_{\mathrm{mode},m}$, $\omega_{\mathrm{mode},m}$, $x_{\mathrm{site},n}$, $\rho_{\mathrm{Fock},n}$. The word names the family; the running letter after the comma carries no meaning and cannot collide. R3. Two slots of different kinds are separated by a comma: $h_{t,k}$, $\nu_{\Phi,k}$, $S_{\Psi,k}$. R4. Two spatial slots are written solid, with no comma: $\delta_{jk}$, $\epsilon_{jkl}$. R5. A name stays a word: $\eta_{\mathrm{sh}}$, $\eta_C$, $\Xi_{\mathrm{sp},L}$, $\Gamma_{\mathrm{mod}}$. R6. A ket keeps its letter, with the family word outside if the reading is not the Fock level: $\lvert n\rangle$, $\lvert n\rangle_{\mathrm{Fock}}$.

The ten families, each with its exact count. These are the only ten; anything else that carries a $k$ is marked or is a direction.

Three words are banned as labels, and the reason is arithmetic, not taste. \mathrm{phy} and \mathrm{phys} are 0 and 8: a truncation is not a word this corpus uses, so write the full word. \mathrm{mod} is 283 in physics and 269 in maths and already means "modified" or "modular", so it cannot mean "mode": write \mathrm{mode}.

The internal corpus adds two corrections, and they are why it is not a copy of the physics pass.

$k$ there is sometimes a genuine component: $\langle x,y\rangle=\sum\bar x_ky_k$ and $q_k=x_k$, 3 tokens. Those stay $x_k$, because after the pass $k$ is the space index and that is exactly what they are.

$\lambda_k$ there is the Gell-Mann family, $i\lambda_k$ for the eight matrices, 5 tokens, with $\sum_k\lambda_k=\operatorname{tr}X$. That is a family label, not a direction, and the same string means a direction in physics. Recommended: $\lambda_{\mathrm{GM},m}$. It is the only place where the two corpora disagree on what $k$ means, so it is worth a sentence in the internal article.

D1 — withdrawn. The family label does not move to $n$ and does not move anywhere. Each family gets its word, by R2. This is cheaper than D1 was: 46 tokens gain a word instead of 43 tokens changing letter, and nothing new is occupied.

D2 — plain letters for three spatial slots, dotless for four. $\epsilon_{ij\eta}\to\epsilon_{ijk}$ (142), $\varepsilon_{ij\eta}\to\varepsilon_{ijk}$ (34), $\epsilon_{j\eta l}\to\epsilon_{jkl}$ (28), $\delta_{j\eta}\to\delta_{jk}$ (93), and the 458 mixed subscripts collapse to one letter: $_{j\eta}\to_{jk}$ (198), $_{j\eta l}\to_{jkl}$ (91), $_{i\eta}\to_{ik}$ (35). Four slots stay dotless, $\epsilon_{\imath\jmath\rho\sigma}$ (109). Arity decides, not taste.

D3 — no $\lambda$ in any index slot. The twelve spellings that carry it, 124 tokens, all resolve to $\epsilon_{ijk}$ or $\epsilon_{\imath\jmath\rho\sigma}$.

D4 — $\eta_{\lambda\lambda}\to\eta_{\rho\rho}$. One token, a metric with two 4-D slots. Never $k$.

D5 — $k_\eta\to\nu_{\Phi,k}$, not $k_k$. $k_\eta$ is a wave number: the Fourier articles write $\partial/\partial r_\eta\mapsto ik_\eta$. The convention paragraph says a wave number is $\nu$ and gives the component spelling as $\nu_{\Phi,\imath}$, "with $\nu_{\Phi,x},\nu_{\Phi,y},\nu_{\Phi,z}$ the three components of $\vec\nu_\Phi$". So $k_k$ would break two rules at once, the one that bans $k$ as a wave number and the one that makes a component a $\nu$. 21 tokens in the seven Fourier articles. The 11 tokens in The Chiral Algebra… are coefficients named after a basis element, $k_\alpha\vec q+k_\beta\vec q^*+k_\zeta N+k_\eta\bar N$, and they stay.

D6 — $h_{0\eta}\to h_{t,k}$, with the comma. The time slot is $t$ (the corpus writes $h_{t\ldots}$ 73 against $h_{0\ldots}$ 28), and R3 puts a comma between the two kinds. Eight tokens in physics, four in internal. The same display in $F$ mixes $0$ and $t$ ($F_{0\eta}$ 18, $F_{t\eta}$ 12) and becomes $F_{t,k}$.

D7 — the internal corpus keeps its four names. 217 tokens, 31 articles, one family changes.

D8 — the external corpus is not touched, ever. 161 tokens, zero indices, all quotations of other authors.

D9 — $\lambda_k$ is not touched. 23 tokens, 22 of them in The Lyapunov Exponent…, where $k$ is the direction: $\sum_k\lambda_k$, and $\lambda_k\vec{u_s}_k^2$ in the weighted average. That is the exponent along direction $k$, which is what R1 says a $k$ subscript means. The one token in the conventions paragraph is the sentence being rewritten in pass 1.

10. The Risks

The migration is not reversible by one command: it is a rename of one slot of one letter, applied to 6 050 tokens in 457 articles, and its correctness depends on a per-pattern whitelist. A global substitution of \eta by k corrupts the Minkowski metric, the shear viscosity and the ’t Hooft symbols, and is not an acceptable shortcut.

It also forces D1. After step 5 the letter $k$ names two things in physics, a direction in space and a countable family, exactly as it already does in maths where it is both the space index and the base field. The migration can align with maths on the index and copy maths on the overload, or align on the index and repair the overload in both corpora. That second choice is a second migration and is out of scope here.

Two of these are worse than the physics ones.

In How Takabayasi Built the Dirac Fluid the same article writes $\eta=e/(mc^{2})$ at line 129 and the metric $\eta_{\imath\jmath}=\mathrm{diag}(+1,-1,-1,-1)$ at line 462. The letter already means two things inside one file, and the migration neither causes nor fixes that. It must leave both.

In The Rotations, the Boosts and the Groups the letter $\eta$ is a vector of the envelope, and $\eta_j$ is its $j$-th member. The internal corpus is already half-migrated on the letters. 142 plain $i$/$j$ subscript tokens, \delta_{ij} 18, \epsilon_{ijk} 6. It is the only corpus where the old plain spellings and the new dotless ones sit next to a space index that is still $\eta$. The $k$ pass must not convert those plain spellings; they belong to D2.

The only judgement left inside a pass is buried in the prose, and each such case is named where it occurs: 79 $\sum_k$, the 458 mixed subscripts, the 11 $k_\eta$ names, and internal $\lambda_k$.

The one risk that is not about notation. PLAN.md forbids git. There is no undo. Copy the trees before pass 1 and keep the copy until --post reports 37/37.

Summary

One slot, two corpora, one whitelist. The space index is $k$, running over $x,y,z$; $\eta$ keeps the metric and the scalars, and the maths corpus is the reference that decides the spelling.

  • 6 050 $\eta$ stand in a direct subscript slot in 457 physics articles, out of 8 519 occurrences; 3 128 are explicit index uses, and 458 are mixed subscripts that collapse to one letter.
  • The whitelist of §4 gives every conversion an exact maths counterpart, so that after the pass one string equals one string across the two corpora.
  • The keep-list of §5 is what a global substitution destroys: the Minkowski metric, the shear viscosity, the inner conjugation, the polarization, five small parameters and the 't Hooft symbols, about 160 tokens.
  • $k$ is already occupied by ten families, and D1 is withdrawn: each family takes a word, not a letter, because $n$ collides with the Matsubara order and the letters are all spent.
  • Ten passes, 37 gate rows, and a baseline that already reads 37/37; the judgement is confined to 46 tokens in 45 files.
  • The external corpus is never touched: 161 tokens, none of them an index, all of them quotations.
  • The pass is not reversible, and the only protection is a copy of both trees taken before pass 1.

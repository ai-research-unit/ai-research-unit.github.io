Audit: The Sextet Mass Normalisation, Option A Executed

Status of this file. A corpus-wide convention change, executed 2026-11-25 on the user's instruction ("A"). Eleven files, twenty-nine edits, build clean.

What Was Wrong

The corpus stated three things that cannot all hold. With $Ξ_0=v_CD$, $D=\operatorname{diag}(-3,1,1,1)$, $\operatorname{tr}D^{2}=12$, an orthonormal basis $\operatorname{tr}(t_at_b)=\tfrac12\delta_{ab}$, and the canonical mass term $\tfrac12M^{2}A^{2}$:

$$ \operatorname{tr}\bigl([t_a,\Xi_0]^{\dagger}[t_b,\Xi_0]\bigr)=8\,g_4^{2}v_C^{2}\,\delta_{ab}\ \text{on the six} \ \Longrightarrow\ M_X^{2}=16\,g_4^{2}v_C^{2}. $$

The corpus wrote $\tfrac12g_4^{2}v_C^{2}$, a factor $32$ low, and reconciled it with the array-level $32v^{2}$ by defining $v_C=8v/g_4$. That $8$ was $\sqrt{2\cdot32}$, a bookkeeping square root with no derivation: it was chosen so the chain closed.

The $16$ is derived and the $8$ was not. $16=(\Delta d)^{2}=(1-(-3))^{2}$ is one power of the commutator on each side of the trace; the $2$ that turns $16$ into $32$ is the $\tfrac12$ of $\tfrac12M^{2}A^{2}$. Both factors are statements about the Lagrangian, so $v_C=\sqrt2\,v/g_4$ follows rather than being fitted.

The One Physical Consequence, Stated

The four-fermion coefficient's elegance is lost. The old $\tfrac12$ made

$$ \frac{g_4^{2}}{2M_X^{2}}=\frac{1}{v_C^{2}} $$

exactly, which is the reason the corpus chose it: the coupling cancels and $v_C$ alone sets the contact-term scale. Under $M_X^{2}=16g_4^{2}v_C^{2}$ the identity becomes

$$ \frac{g_4^{2}}{2M_X^{2}}=\frac{1}{32\,v_C^{2}}=\frac{1}{2(\Delta d)^{2}v_C^{2}} . $$

The physical point survives: the coupling still cancels, and the condensate is still the only scale. What changes is the coefficient, from $1$ to $1/32$, and it is now traceable to $(\Delta d)^{2}$ rather than to a chosen normalisation. lepton-flavour-… was updated to say this.

A reader who wants the old elegance back must choose option C, $\Xi_0=\frac{v_C}{4\sqrt2}D$, which restores $1/v_C^{2}$ at the cost of a $4\sqrt2$ nobody can read off the vacuum. That trade was put to the user and A was chosen.

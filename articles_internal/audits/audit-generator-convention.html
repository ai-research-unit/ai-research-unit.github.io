Audit: The Generator Convention, Anti-Hermitian Mathematics and Hermitian Vertices

Status of this file. The check was run and the patches made. The question was whether the mathematics corpus and the physics block disagree about the Hermiticity of the generators of $\mathfrak{su}(4)$. They do not: the mathematics fixes the anti-Hermitian algebra, the physics prints the same fifteen arrays and writes its action in the Hermitian set obtained from them by $i$ and a rescaling. Three sentences were wrong about that passage, one of them a false statement about the structure constants, and they are corrected here.

1. What the Mathematics Says

source statement
The Anti-Hermitian Derivations of $M_4(\mathbb{C})$ as $\mathfrak{su}(4)$ the generators are $K$ with $K^{*}=-K$ and $\operatorname{tr}K=0$; bracket $[K_1,K_2]$ with no $i$; rotor $U=e^{K}$; split $15=6+9$
the same article, on the split six real antisymmetric (the words with one $t$ index) and nine imaginary (the nine $iN_{jk}$)
The Generator Displays (this directory) "the fifteen are anti-Hermitian, traceless, of square $-\mathrm{I}_4$"; verified numerically
ai-physics-review.md a Hermitian $K$ moves the Hermitian part of the operator algebra out of itself, "so it is not a generator"

The reason is structural and not stylistic. The representative algebra is the derivation algebra of the Hermitian part, $S\mapsto KS-SK$; the derivation is a derivation only for anti-Hermitian $K$, and $\exp(K)$ is unitary exactly then.

2. What the Physics Says

The printed arrays are the same fifteen, and they are anti-Hermitian. $G_{a}^{\dagger}=-G_{a}$, $\operatorname{tr}G_{a}=0$, with $\operatorname{tr}(G_{a}^{\dagger}G_{a})=2$ for the fourteen and $12$ for the corner $iD$.

The action is written in a second set.

$$ t_{a}=\frac{iG_{a}}{\sqrt{2n_{a}}},\qquad n_{a}:=\operatorname{tr}\bigl(G_{a}^{\dagger}G_{a}\bigr)=2\ (\text{the fourteen printed}),\ 12\ (\text{the corner}),\quad n_{c_{3}}=6\ \bigl(c_{3}=d_{2}-d_{3}\bigr), \qquad \operatorname{tr}(t_{a}t_{b})=\tfrac12\delta_{ab},\quad t_{a}^{\dagger}=t_{a}. $$

No new letter is spent on the factor. The reserved-letter audit gives $\nu$ to the wave number and $\lambda$ to the wavelength, so the rescaling is written from the trace norm the article already prints, $n_{a}=\operatorname{tr}(G_{a}^{\dagger}G_{a})$, and the bracket relation follows from $t_{a}=iG_{a}/\sqrt{2n_{a}}$ in one line:

$$ F^{c}{}_{ab}=\sqrt{\frac{n_{c}}{2\,n_{a}n_{b}}}\;f^{c}{}_{ab}. $$

The covariant derivative carries the $i$ that Hermiticity buys, $D_{\imath}=\partial_{\imath}-ig_4\sum_{a}A_{a,\imath}t_{a}$. In the anti-Hermitian set the same object is written $\partial_{\imath}-g_4\sum_{a}A_{a,\imath}G_{a}$, with no $i$. Two conventions, one theory, and the passage is one factor of $i$ and one rescaling, as The Lagrangian and the Primordial Force, §Conventions, states.

Why the action needs the Hermitian set. With an anti-Hermitian generator, the two orderings of the cross term in the scalar kinetic term have equal trace and cancel, and the cubic representative–scalar vertex vanishes identically; with $t_{a}$ it does not. This is stated in The Lagrangian and the Scalar Matrix and is the one place where a change of basis deletes an interaction.

5. What Is Not Affected

Every number in the coupling block. $b_0=\tfrac{26}{3}$ is a physical coefficient; it does not depend on Hermiticity, only on the normalisation $\operatorname{tr}(t_{a}t_{b})=\tfrac12\delta_{ab}$ being the one the canonical kinetic term fixes. $C_{A}=4$, $T_{R}=\tfrac12$, $T_{S}=4$, $\alpha_4(M_X)=0.058$, $\alpha_4(M_P)=0.0165$, the pole at $885\ \mathrm{MeV}$, the instanton action $8\pi^{2}/g_4^{2}=108.2$ — all unchanged. The correction is to the language of the passage, and to one false arithmetic claim about the structure constants.

The scalar count is unaffected for the same reason. $15+1=16$ counts real parameters, and the two spaces have the same real dimension; the error was to say the space itself is the algebra.

6. What This Does Not Decide

The reality class of the connection is a separate open question. In the biquaternion copy the alternative is between an anti-Hermitian connection in $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ and a Hermitian one in $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$, and the lattice article records that $\exp(ia\kappa\mathcal{A})$ is $*$-unitary only for the Hermitian assignment. That question is about the real form of the algebra, not about which generating set writes the $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ action, and the two must not be conflated: the $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ block is written on $4\times4$ matrices with a definite Hermiticity for each of the two bases, and no choice is left open there.

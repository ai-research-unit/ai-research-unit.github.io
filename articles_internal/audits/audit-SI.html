Audit: The SI Base Dimensions

Status. One rule, one scan, one worklist. Every physical quantity in the physics corpus is written in the official SI base units and their powers. Nothing is expressed in a unit whose definition hides a constant.

1. The Rule

The SI has seven base dimensions. Each has one base unit, and every other unit is a product of powers of those seven.

base quantity dimension unit symbol
length $\mathrm{L}$ metre $\mathrm{m}$
mass $\mathrm{M}$ kilogram $\mathrm{kg}$
time $\mathrm{T}$ second $\mathrm{s}$
electric current $\mathrm{I}$ ampere $\mathrm{A}$
thermodynamic temperature $\Theta$ kelvin $\mathrm{K}$
amount of substance $\mathrm{N}$ mole $\mathrm{mol}$
luminous intensity $\mathrm{J}$ candela $\mathrm{cd}$

Every article obeys it. A mass is a mass, in kilograms. A length is a length, in metres. A time is a time, in seconds. An energy is an energy, in joules, $\mathrm{J}=\mathrm{kg\,m^{2}\,s^{-2}}$. A momentum is $\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$. A cross-section is $\mathrm{m^{2}}$. A rate is $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. A wavenumber is $\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. The corpus uses five of the seven base dimensions; the mole and the candela do not appear.

No unit that hides a constant. A unit is admitted only if its definition is a product of base units. The electronvolt is the charge of the proton times a volt, so it is a unit of energy and of nothing else: $1\ \mathrm{eV}=1.602\,176\,634\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm{J}$, exact. The corpus therefore refuses it on any quantity but an energy:

refused writing why repair
$m_e=0.511\ \mathrm{MeV}$ a mass in an energy unit; the $c^{2}$ is dropped $m_e=9.109\times10^{-31}\ \mathrm{kg}$
$m_e=0.511\ \mathrm{MeV}/c^{2}$ the conversion is hidden inside the label $m_e=9.109\times10^{-31}\ \mathrm{kg}$
$\lambda_m=386\ \mathrm{fm}$ a length in a unit that is not the metre $\lambda_m=3.86\times10^{-13}\ \mathrm{m}$
$\tau=0.26\ \mathrm{ps}$ a time in a unit that is not the second $\tau=2.6\times10^{-13}\ \mathrm{s}$
$\Lambda=1\ \mathrm{GeV}$, $T=1\ \mathrm{eV}$ an energy and a temperature shown in one unit $\Lambda=1.602\times10^{-10}\ \mathrm{J}$, $T=1.16\times10^{4}\ \mathrm{K}$
$c=\hbar=1$ the constants are set to one and never written $c=2.997\,924\,58\times10^{8}\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ on the page

A ratio is legal and is not required. A ratio of two masses is dimensionless, so $m_p/m_e=1836.15$ breaks no rule and needs no repair. What the rule forbids is the electronvolt standing where a mass, a length, a time or a temperature stands. The ratio is an option; the kilogram is the unit.

What does not change. The algebra, the glyphs, the dimension letters and the tables of Physics Units and Common Elements Conventions stay as they are. Only the unit attached to a number moves, and it moves to the base unit of its dimension.

2. The Constants, and the Four Conversions

The SI fixes seven constants exactly, and every conversion below follows from them with no measured number in it.

constant value exact
$\delta f_{\mathrm{Cs}}$ $9\,192\,631\,770\ \mathrm{Hz}$ yes
$c$ $299\,792\,458\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ yes
$h$ $6.626\,070\,15\times10^{-34}\ \mathrm{J\,s}$ yes
$e$ $1.602\,176\,634\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm{C}$ yes
$k_B$ $1.380\,649\times10^{-23}\ \mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$ yes
$N_A$ $6.022\,140\,76\times10^{23}\ \mathrm{mol^{-1}}$ yes
$\mathrm{Ki}_{\mathrm{cd}}$ $683\ \mathrm{lm\,W^{-1}}$ yes

A number in electronvolts becomes a number in joules by one multiplication by $e$. A mass becomes a mass by the second conversion, and a length or a time by the two that follow.

from to formula
energy in eV joules $E[\mathrm{J}]=E[\mathrm{eV}]\times1.602\,176\,634\times10^{-19}$
energy mass $m=E/c^{2}$
mass Compton length $\lambda_m=h/(m\,c)$
energy width lifetime $\tau=\hbar/\Gamma$

Two working numbers. $1\ \mathrm{GeV}=1.602\,176\,634\times10^{-10}\ \mathrm{J}$ and $1\ \mathrm{GeV}/c^{2}=1.782\,661\,921\times10^{-27}\ \mathrm{kg}$. The second is the only one a mass needs: a mass read in GeV becomes a mass in kilograms by one multiplication by $1.782\,661\,921\times10^{-27}$, with no $c$ written.

The price, stated once. A particle mass in kilograms is a small number with a long exponent. That is a fact about the kilogram and not a defect of the rule. The article prints the exponent once, in a table like the one above, and the prose then uses the symbol.

4. The Scan

Save as /tmp/si/scan_si.py and run from the repository root. It splits every unit group into tokens and classifies a token by its dimension. A symbol that merely looks like a unit — $\Omega_{\mathrm{ps}}$ for the pseudoscalar density, $\varepsilon^{(\mathrm{h})}$ for helicity — is not counted, because a unit token must follow a space, a digit or a closing brace.

import re, pathlib

UNIT = r'\\(?:mathrm|text)\{([^{}]*)\}'
# a unit token must be introduced by a space, a digit, or a closing brace, and end the group
def units_in(line):
    out = []
    for m in re.finditer(r'(?<!_\{)(?<![_(])\\(?:mathrm|text)\{([^{}]*)\}', line):
        out.append((m.start(), m.group(1)))
    return out

EVU   = re.compile(r'^(?:T|G|M|k|m|u|n|p)?eV(?:\^\{?-?[0-9/]+\}?)?$')
EVBARE= re.compile(r'(?<![A-Za-z\\{])(?:T|G|M|k|m|u|n|p)?eV\b')
NONM  = re.compile(r'^(?:f|p|n|c|k|a|m|u|d|h)m(?:\^\{?-?[0-9]+\}?)?$')
NONS  = re.compile(r'^(?:ps|ns|fs|ms|us|min|day|yr|h|hour|s\^\{?-1\}?)$')
NONKG = re.compile(r'^(?:g|mg|ug|ng|t)$')
MASSY = re.compile(r'(?:\\mathrm\{)?eV\}?\s*(?:/|\\,?/)\s*c')

def classify(line):
    hits = set()
    for _, u in units_in(line):
        if EVU.match(u):
            hits.add('E-eV')
        if NONM.match(u):
            hits.add('L-nonm')
        if NONS.match(u):
            hits.add('T-nons')
        if NONKG.match(u):
            hits.add('M-nonkg')
    if EVBARE.search(line):
        hits.add('E-eV')
    if MASSY.search(line):
        hits.add('M-eV')
    return hits

def scan(root, skip=('audits', 'instructions', '__pycache__')):
    out = {}
    for p in sorted(pathlib.Path(root).rglob('*.md')):
        if any(s in p.parts for s in skip):
            continue
        cls = {}
        for i, line in enumerate(p.read_text(encoding='utf-8', errors='replace').split('\n'), 1):
            for k in classify(line):
                cls.setdefault(k, []).append(i)
        if cls:
            out[str(p)] = cls
    return out

if __name__ == '__main__':
    h = scan('articles_physics')
    for k in ('M-eV', 'E-eV', 'L-nonm', 'T-nons', 'M-nonkg'):
        print(f'{k:9s} lines={sum(len(v.get(k,[])) for v in h.values()):5d} '
              f'files={sum(1 for v in h.values() if k in v):3d}')
    print('union files:', len(h))

A line can fall in two classes, so the columns do not add to the union. The scan is a candidate list. It cannot read a sentence, and a line that quotes a refused form in order to refuse it is a candidate here and legal in the corpus: physics-units-and-common-elements-conventions and the anchor both do that, and both stay.

6. The Repair

One dimension at a time, one article at a time. The unit moves to the base unit of its dimension and the number is recomputed. No constant is dropped: the conversion is printed on the page, once, in the table the article already carries.

before after
$m_e=0.511\ \mathrm{MeV}$ $m_e=9.109\times10^{-31}\ \mathrm{kg}$
$\Gamma_H=4.1\ \mathrm{MeV}$ $\Gamma_H=6.6\times10^{-22}\ \mathrm{J}$
$\lambda_m=386\ \mathrm{fm}$ $\lambda_m=3.86\times10^{-13}\ \mathrm{m}$
$\tau=0.26\ \mathrm{ps}$ $\tau=2.6\times10^{-13}\ \mathrm{s}$
$\sigma<2.6\times10^{-47}\ \mathrm{cm^{2}}$ $\sigma<2.6\times10^{-51}\ \mathrm{m^{2}}$
a grain of $1\ \mathrm{g}$ a grain of $1\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm{kg}$
$M_{\mathrm{Pl}}=1.22\times10^{19}\ \mathrm{GeV}$ $M_{\mathrm{Pl}}=2.18\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm{kg}$

Keep the conversion, not the shortcut. A line that says $M_W=80.377\ \mathrm{GeV}$ becomes a line that says $M_W=1.4329\times10^{-25}\ \mathrm{kg}$. A line that says $M_W=80.377\ \mathrm{GeV}/c^{2}$ becomes the same kilogram line. The two spellings differ in the corpus and agree in the repair, because both name the same mass.

Six traps.

  1. Dropping the exponent. $0.511$ MeV is $9.1\times10^{-31}$ kg, not $0.511\times10^{-27}$ kg. The exponent is where the error hides; write it in full.
  2. Converting an energy into a mass by habit. $\sqrt{s}$, a width, a cutoff, a temperature and a coupling are energies or temperatures. They go to joules and kelvin, never to kilograms.
  3. Leaving a derived unit half-converted. A cross-section in $\mathrm{cm^{2}}$ becomes $\mathrm{m^{2}}$; a rate in $\mathrm{ps^{-1}}$ becomes $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$; a wavenumber in $\mathrm{fm^{-1}}$ becomes $\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. A table that mixes the two is worse than the table it replaced.
  4. Quoting a refused form in order to refuse it. The conventions article and the anchor must be able to print $\mathrm{MeV}$ and $\mathrm{fm}$ in the act of forbidding them. Those lines are legal and are not repaired.
  5. Writing $c=1$. The constants are printed, and $c=2.997\,924\,58\times10^{8}\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ is exact, so there is no economy in setting it to one.
  6. Repairing the number and not the sentence. "A light Higgs near $125$ GeV" must become "near $2.23\times10^{-25}$ kg", with no "energy" left in the sentence. A repaired number in an unrepaired sentence still claims that a mass is an energy.

Each repaired article carries a two-line Units note near the top: quantities are in SI base units, energy in joules, mass in kilograms, length in metres, time in seconds, and the constants used are $c$, $h$ and $e$ at their exact values.

7. The Gates

A gate detects a regression; it does not find new sites. Run all six after each article and record the output.

# gate measured today
1 the deviating-file count falls, by the script of §4 $75$; never higher
2 no electronvolt outside the two rule articles $634$ lines in $68$ files
3 no length outside the metre $80$ lines in $17$ files
4 no time outside the second $4$ lines in $1$ file
5 no mass outside the kilogram $2$ lines in $1$ file
6 every article that prints a mass prints $c$ rises by one per repaired article

Gates 1 to 5 are the script, not a grep. A grep reads lower than the script on every class, because it sees a unit only inside a \mathrm{} group and misses a bare number in a sentence: it returns $154$ lines for the electronvolt where the script returns $634$, and a gate that certifies an article still carrying $480$ defects is no gate.

Two commands, run from the repository root:

python3 scan_si.py                                              # gates 1 to 5
grep -rl '2\.997\,924\,58' articles_physics/*.md | wc -l      # gate 6

Gate 2 is the only one that matters by volume. Sixty-eight of the seventy-five files fail it and no other gate, and the electronvolt is the one unit that is right in one place and wrong in four.

Gate 6 is the definition of done for an article. An article that prints a mass in kilograms and does not print $c$ has hidden the conversion inside the number.

8. The Order of Work

  1. Freeze the base. Confirm the seven constants and the two working numbers of §2. Every later division depends on them.
  2. Repair the rule articles first: physics-units-and-common-elements-conventions, vacuum-value-normalization-energy-mass-wave-number-and-length, index-and-notation-conventions and algebra-and-element-conventions. They legislate for the rest, and a rule article that contradicts the rule makes every later repair arguable.
  3. Repair by weight, heaviest first: the-three-wavenumbers-of-a-particle ($86$), hadron-structure-from-qcd-the-bound-state-problem ($47$), the-landau-pole-triviality-and-vacuum-stability ($43$), the-hierarchy-problem-and-naturalness ($40$), then the rest of §5.
  4. Run the gates after each article. A pass over seventy-five files that fails at the end has to be bisected.
  5. Report, and stop. The acceptable sentence is: article, line numbers rewritten, gates that returned their measured value, lines read and found legal.

Done means: gate 1 at zero outside the rule articles; gates 2 to 5 returning only the lines that print a refused form in order to refuse it; gate 6 covering every article that carries a mass.

Summary

  • The rule: every quantity in the physics corpus is written in the official SI base units and their powers. Mass in kg, length in m, time in s, energy in J.
  • The reason: a unit that hides a constant hides an error. The electronvolt on a mass, the electronvolt per $c^{2}$, the femtometre and the picosecond all hide one.
  • The cost: a particle mass becomes a small number with a long exponent. That is a fact about the kilogram, not a defect of the rule.
  • The size: $75$ of $679$ physics articles deviate, $725$ candidate lines, of which $634$ are electronvolts. Three articles carry a mass in an energy unit, seventeen carry a length outside the metre, one carries a time outside the second, one carries a mass in grams.
  • The order: the rule articles first, then the heaviest, then the rest, gates after every article.

The Decisions

  • D1. $\theta_4$'s status: taken, in the direction the review opposed. The article now says the parameter is redundant above $M_C$. The warrant is the companion article's own $m=0$ and the positivity of the axial coefficient, and neither is a new assumption.
  • D2. The normalisation: corrected. It is the change that makes the article's own $2\pi$ period true.
  • D3. The justification: replaced, with a derivation that checks at $N=2,3,4$.
  • D4. The minor items: all six applied.

What the Correction Did Not Touch

No number in the article moved except $S(M_C)$. The action, the suppression, the moduli, the cutoff and the transcription argument never used $\theta_4$. The $M_C$ value moved because the coupling article's $b_0$ changed, not because of anything in this pass.

The Two Claims That Changed Status

  • "$\theta_4$ is physical because the content is anomaly-free" → false. The content is anomaly-free in the representative sense and anomalous in the axial sense, and the second is the one that would have made the angle physical — had the fermions been massive.
  • "the standard one is the maximal embedding" → false. The standard is the block embedding, and it is the least maximal of the four.

Both were in sentences that asserted rather than argued, which is the pattern worth watching: the article's derivations are sound and its asides are where it slipped.

Migration Nu: The Wavenumber $\nu$ and the Element $\tilde\Upsilon$

Status of this file. Decided and measured, not yet executed. The decision is fixed and the census is taken; the order of operations of §10 has not been run. Counts are as of the day of measurement, on articles_physics with 678 articles.

What the file is for. It is the whole bookkeeping of one substitution, $k=2\pi\nu$. The substitution is one line; everything else here exists because the naive version of it, a search-and-replace that leaves the $2\pi$ alone, produces a corpus wrong by $2\pi$ in every exponent and by $4\pi^{2}$ in every d'Alembertian, and renders perfectly while doing so.

The reader's contract, in five lines.

  1. This is a change of unit, not a rename. Every $k$ becomes $2\pi\nu$, and the $2\pi$ moves from one side of the equation to the other. It is never deleted.
  2. After the migration, a $2\pi$ next to a $\nu$ marks an angle, a derivative or an angular frequency, and nothing else. A $2\pi$ in a pure definition is a missed simplification; a missing $2\pi$ in a phase is a factor error.
  3. A wavenumber is never bare. Every $\nu$ carries a subscript, and a roman subscript means the neutrino.
  4. The keep-list of §5 is read before the first edit. $k_B$, the integer $k$, $\kappa$ outside the Cauchy shift, and the kaons do not migrate.
  5. Numbers are recomputed, not re-lettered. The numeric test of §11, to $10^{-12}$ relative, is the only one that catches a missing $2\pi$.

1. The Decision

The letter $k$ loses the role of wavenumber everywhere in the corpus. The wavenumber becomes $\nu=k/2\pi=1/\lambda$, the number of wavelengths per unit length, the spectroscopic wavenumber and the spatial frequency. The wave-number biquaternion $\tilde K_{\Phi}$ becomes $\tilde\Upsilon_{\Phi}$ and is defined by division by $h$ alone,

$$ \tilde\Upsilon_{\Phi}:=\frac{\tilde P_{\Phi}}{h}, $$

instead of $\tilde K_{\Phi}=\frac{2\pi\tilde P_{\Phi}}{h}$.

This is a change of unit and not a rename. Every $k$ in the corpus equals $2\pi\nu$, so every formula keeps its value only if the $2\pi$ is put back where it belongs. A search-and-replace that maps k_m to \nu_m and leaves the $2\pi$ alone produces a corpus wrong by $2\pi$ in the exponent and by $4\pi^{2}$ in the d'Alembertian, and it will render perfectly while doing so. The whole content of this file is the bookkeeping that prevents that.

Why the change pays. With $h$ in the corpus and not $\hbar$, the new definition is exact in one symbol: $\nu=p/h$ and $\tilde\Upsilon_{\Phi}=\tilde P_{\Phi}/h$. The wavelength becomes a plain reciprocal, $\lambda=1/\nu$, the Compton wavelength becomes $1/\nu_m$ with no $2\pi$ in sight, and the corpus stops carrying two incompatible readings of $k$: an angle per unit length in one section and a count of waves per unit length in another. The $2\pi$ survives only where an angle, a derivative or an angular frequency is meant, which is exactly where the factor is physical.

The cost is explicit and is paid in one place: the radian reading. The corpus's own "distance per radian", $1/k_m=386.159\ \mathrm{fm}$ for the electron, becomes $1/(2\pi\nu_m)$, and the Zitterbewegung amplitude becomes $1/(4\pi\nu_m)$. Both are still $\mathsf{L}$, both are still correct, and both now show why the $2\pi$ sits there.

2. The Substitution Rule, in One Line

One line generates the entire map:

$$ k=2\pi\nu,\qquad \tilde K_{\Phi}=2\pi\tilde\Upsilon_{\Phi}. $$

Substitute, then simplify. Because most definitions carry $2\pi$ in the numerator, the substitution cancels it there, and the $2\pi$ reappears on the other side of the equation. The rule for auditing the result is one sentence:

After the migration, every $2\pi$ next to a $\nu$ marks an angle, a derivative, or an angular frequency, and nothing else.

A $2\pi$ beside a $\nu$ in a pure definition, $2\pi\nu_m=mc/h$, is a missed simplification. A missing $2\pi$ in $\tilde\nabla_\Phi\tilde\Xi=2\pi i\tilde\Upsilon_{\Phi}\tilde\Xi$ is a factor error by $2\pi$ in every phase of the series.

Why $\Upsilon$. It is the only capital Greek letter the corpus has not spent, it joins the family that already carries elements, $\tilde\Xi$, $\tilde\Phi$, $\tilde\Psi$, and it frees $K$ for the three objects that share it today: the kaons $K_L$ and $K_S$, the free kernel $K_0$, and the Kähler structure in the mathematical articles.

KaTeX. Verified before writing this file: $\tilde\Upsilon_{\Phi}$, $\vec{\tilde\nu}_{\Phi}$, $\tilde\nu_t'$, $\nu_m=\frac{mc}{h}$, $-\langle\tilde\Upsilon_{\Phi}\rangle_{\natural}=\nu_m^{2}$, $\Box_\Phi-4\pi^{2}\nu_m^{2}$, $\lambda_C=\frac{1}{\nu_m}$ and $\theta=2\pi(\vec{\tilde\nu}_{\Phi}\cdot\vec r_{\Phi}-\tilde\nu_t'r_t')$ all compile. \Upsilon is a plain token, so \tilde\Upsilon_\Phi works unbraced exactly as \tilde K_\Phi does; brace it anyway, for the same reason as the index pass.

Three hazards, all typographic and all real.

  1. $\nu$ and $\upsilon$ are near-twins in a serif math font. The 138 Lanczos $\upsilon$ sites are one article; the wavenumber $\nu$ will be in 325. The guard is the subscript: a wavenumber is never bare.
  2. $\nu$ and $\nu_{\mathrm{neu}}$. The neutrino keeps its $\mathrm{neu}$ label throughout. The rule: a roman subscript means the neutrino, a plain or italic subscript means a wavenumber. $\nu_m$ is a mass wave number; $\nu_{\mathrm{neu}}$ is a particle.
  3. $\Upsilon$ and $Y$, and the $\Upsilon$ meson. The corpus mentions neither quarkonium nor the $\Upsilon$ resonance today. If it ever does, the element is distinguishable by the tilde and the sector subscript.

7. The Prose That Inverts

Three passages do not survive re-lettering. They currently state the opposite of the new convention and must be rewritten, not searched and replaced.

  1. §The dimensions. "All three are inverse lengths" stays. But $\frac{h}{2\pi}$ is no longer one of the factors: it is $h$, and the dimension line becomes $\frac{\mathsf{M}\mathsf{L}\mathsf{T}^{-1}}{\mathsf{M}\mathsf{L}^{2}\mathsf{T}^{-1}}=\mathsf{L}^{-1}$ with $h$ in the denominator. The check is the same and the arithmetic changes.
  2. §Why $\frac{h}{2\pi}$ and not $h$. The heading inverts. The series now uses $h$, the reduced wave number is $2\pi\nu$, and the sentence "the ordinary wave number is $k_{\Phi}/(2\pi)$" becomes "the angular wave number is $2\pi\nu_{\Phi}$". Every claim in the passage is true after the swap and false before it.
  3. §What a Wave Number Is. "It counts radians per unit length" is no longer the reading of the symbol: the symbol now counts waves per unit length, and radians per unit length is $2\pi\nu$. The sentence "the ordinary wave number, the count of turns per metre, is $k_{\Phi}/(2\pi)$" becomes "the angular form, radians per metre, is $2\pi\nu_{\Phi}$". The four readings survive; their order swaps.

The article's title, The Three Wavenumbers of a Particle, stays correct and becomes more literal.

13. The Open Questions, Two Settled and Four Open

  1. Tilde or no tilde on $\nu$. The spectroscopic letter is $\tilde\nu$, and the source the change is taken from writes it that way. The corpus's tilde means "element of the algebra", $\tilde\Xi$, $\tilde K$, $\tilde Q$, so a tilded scalar breaks that convention while fixing the collision with the neutrino. This file recommends the untilded $\nu$ with a mandatory subscript, and records $\tilde\nu$ as the alternative if the source's glyph wins.
  2. $\bar\lambda_m$ — settled, and settled against the symbol. The barred length is dropped. It is neither a wavelength nor a symbol: the wavelength is the full turn, $\lambda_m=1/\nu_m=h/(mc)$, and one radian of the phase is the quotient $\lambda_m/(2\pi)=1/(2\pi\nu_m)$, written out where a radian is meant. No barred letter, no "reduced" adjective, no named length for the quotient. The rule is recorded in Physics Units and Common Elements Conventions, §No reduced length, no reduced constant, and every site in the internal tree and in the physics corpus was rewritten to it. The same ban covers the constant: $\frac{h}{2\pi}$ stays a factor written out, and it is never called the reduced Planck constant.
  3. $\kappa=-ik_m$. One site in the shift of the Cauchy theory. It is a wave number and must take the $2\pi$. The other 2 260 $\kappa$ are curvature or coupling and stay.
  4. $k_{\mathrm{eu}}$ and $k_{\mathrm{hel}}$. Euclidean momentum and helicity are wave numbers by dimension and by use, so the rule says they migrate. Their labels are Latin and the pattern is less obvious to a reader; they are 88 sites in a handful of articles.
  5. The running scale in the renormalization-group article. One site, a momentum scale and not a wave number. It stays $k$ or it becomes $\mu$, and the choice belongs to that article.
  6. Whether the corpus keeps the word "reduced" at all — settled: no. The angular form $2\pi\nu$ is not called the reduced wave number; it is the same number with the angle written out. The adjective is gone from the wave-number, length and constant vocabulary, and the plain words are angular wave number, wavelength, one radian of the phase, the action of one radian. The only survivors are the standard terms of other subjects: reduced phase space, reduced density matrix, reduced trace, reduced matrix element.

Summary

One substitution, one bookkeeping, one test that catches everything. $k=2\pi\nu$ is a change of unit; the $2\pi$ moves, and the audit is the record of where it moved to.

  • The keep-list is the safety of the pass: $k_B$ 361 sites, the integer $k$, the kaons, $K_0$, and 2 260 of the 2 261 $\kappa$.
  • The pattern map is 21 shapes taken from one article and copied by the corpus; the rows that gain a factor are exactly the rows that carry an angle, a derivative or an angular frequency.

  • Two of the six open questions are already settled against the tilded and the reducible spellings; the remaining four are listed in §13 for the author.

Audit: The Word Gauge, and What a Purge Can and Cannot Reach

Status of this file. Audit, and the purge has begun: two batches, 19 occurrences removed from 14 files, no claim changed. The corpus holds 6 200 occurrences of the word in articles_physics, 1 396 in articles_maths, 341 in the two menus. The verdict below is that the word can be reduced to a residue of 414 in physics, and that the notion cannot be removed. Nothing was edited to produce this file.

What the file is for. It records the discussion that produced it, defines how the word is to be searched and reported, and sets out the migration strategies, in the order they should be run, with the count each stage should reach.

The reader's contract, in five lines.

  1. A word and a notion are two different targets. The word is a naming defect and is fixable; the notion — more labels than states — is a theorem of the corpus and stays.
  2. "Gauge" is almost always an adjective. Three occurrences in four modify a noun that already carries the meaning, and deleting the adjective loses nothing.
  3. Titles, menus and slugs are the visible surface. They are fixed first, because that is all a reader sees, and the slugs cannot be renamed at all.
  4. A purge is measured per article, never in bulk. The distribution is so skewed that six articles hold more than a hundred occurrences each while 312 articles hold none.
  5. No replacement word may collide with a word the corpus already uses. "Frame" fails on this test and is rejected.

1. The Claim Under Test

The accusation. The word carries a century of baggage and names nothing a reader can picture. Force names an interaction. Space names a manifold. Gauge names what a description is permitted to do twice. A label of that kind invites the reader to look for a substance where there is a redundancy.

The defence, and it is narrow. The corpus cannot do without the notion, for a reason internal to its own arithmetic. Three counts in the corpus are the same count:

  • the fifteen vectors carry $60$ components and $30$ states, because fifteen Gauss laws each remove two;
  • the unbroken phase carries $140$ components and $78$ states;
  • the anomaly of the sixteen cancels because $\mathbf4$ and $\bar{\mathbf4}$ are paired, and not because three traces vanish.

Each of those sentences says one thing: the labels outnumber the states. Connection names the field that carries the labels. Covariance names how the labels move. Local symmetry names the group that moves them. None of the three names the surplus. That surplus is what the word gauge was coined for, and no substitute on any list supplies it.

The verdict, therefore, is two-sided. The word is a bad name in $73\%$ of its uses, where it is a decorative adjective. It is a necessary name in the quarter where it stands alone and says the map is many-to-one. §6 separates the two and gives each its own treatment.

What was proposed and rejected. A replacement set was drawn up and put to the audit: local frame for gauge, local rotation for gauge transformation, connection for gauge field, local frame independence for gauge invariance, choice of frame for gauge fixing, frame-rotation group for gauge group. Four of the six fail the collision test of §5, and the first one fails hardest.

3. The Diagnosis: Three Classes, and One Frozen Subset

Every occurrence falls into one of three classes, and a fourth row of the table is a subset rather than a class. The classification rule is the noun list of §6.1 and it is exhaustive: a token matched by \bgauge\w* is attributive if the next word is on the list, verbal if it is gauged or gauges used as a verb, and nominal otherwise.

class count share example survives deletion of the adjective
attributive — gauge before a noun 4 504 $73\%$ the gauge orbit yes, the noun stands
verbal — to gauge a symmetry 133 $2\%$ the symmetry is gauged no, rephrased as made local
nominal — the gauge, standing alone 1 561 $25\%$ in the gauge where $\partial\!\cdot\! A=0$ no, and it should not
of the total, inside a link target or a quoted title 206 — the-gauge-principle-…html no, the path is permanent

The attributive class is the accusation, and it is correct. In the gauge-fixing term, in the gauge determinant, in the gauge orbit, in the gauge parameter, the noun already carries the physics. The adjective adds a pointer to the technique and no information. Deleting it changes no equation and weakens no claim.

The nominal class is the defence, and it is narrow. $337$ occurrences are followed by punctuation or a function word — the gauge is, in the gauge where, a gauge exists. These are sentences about the many-to-one map, and about the fact that a computation may depend on the representative. They are not decoration, and a mechanical pass must not touch them.

The maths collection holds a third sense, and it is the same mistake in another dress. There, the gauge is used as a noun meaning a fibre or a stabiliser: the gauge is the fibre of the conjugation map over the root; the gauge group is the fibre over $H$; choosing a projective frame is choosing a gauge. The construction is defensible. The word is not: what it names is a fibre or a stabiliser, and both words exist. $304$ occurrences of the gauge is / of / as appear in articles_maths.

And the evidence that the word is doing no work at all. Six articles of this corpus are about the word — the gauge principle, the gauge connection, the gauge orbit, the gauge field path integral, gauge fixing, the gauge algebras. Their density is $11$ to $34$ per thousand words, against a corpus average of $1.74$. The six hold $705$ occurrences in $39\,286$ words, $17.9$ per thousand; the rest of the collection holds $5\,495$ in $3.5$ million words, $1.56$ per thousand — nine times thinner. The word concentrates where the word is the subject, and nowhere else does it carry weight.

4. What Cannot Migrate

Three residues, and each has a reason that is not a preference.

Citations, 153 occurrences. A published title is quoted, not authored. Non-Abelian Gauge Fields is a paper's name. Rewriting it makes the reference unfindable, and the corpus's rule is that a cited title is data.

Slugs, 31 of them, and the URLs they produce. build.py has no redirect mechanism, and prune_orphans() deletes from the deploy tree every page whose source no longer exists. A renamed slug is therefore a dead link for anyone who kept it, with no forwarding page. Titles may change freely; slugs may not.

The notion, in about 340 places. In the gauge where, the gauge is fixed, a gauge exists. Each of these sentences is the many-to-one statement. Replace one with connection and the sentence becomes false: a connection is not many-to-one, the map from connections to field strengths is.

5. Why the Recommended Vocabulary Fails

A replacement word must be free. It is not enough that it be accurate; it must not already mean something else in the same corpus.

proposed occurrences already in articles_physics verdict
frame 6 924 — the biquaternion frame alone is 951, frame field 79, the Euclidean frame 17 rejected. Two unrelated meanings on 13 000 sites
rotor 3 184 — the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ rotor, a defined object rejected. A collision with a defined symbol
connection 2 885 accepted, and already used in the right sense
local symmetry 11 accepted
local rotation 5 accepted
internal symmetry 27 accepted
the redundancy 139 accepted, in the sense redundancy of description
minimal coupling 164 accepted, and it names what the principle proves

The frame objection, stated precisely. Frame in this corpus is the observer's frame: the biquaternion frame of a sector, the Euclidean frame, the tetrad. It appears once per $513$ words, already. Adding a second meaning — a choice of colour basis at each point — would put two senses of one word on $13\,000$ sites, in a corpus whose method is one word per role. That is a worse defect than the one being repaired.

The one substitution that costs nothing is connection. It is already the corpus's word for the field: the gauge connection occurs 111 times, and in every one of them gauge is redundant.

Five strategies, in the order they must run. Each has an entry condition and an exit count.

The rule that keeps this honest. A substitution is applied only when the resulting sentence is still true and still parses. The gauge field path integral becomes the connection path integral, which is true. Gauge-fixing in the electroweak theory becomes fixing the representative in the electroweak theory, which is true. A sentence that needs a rewrite rather than a substitution is deferred to Strategy B and counted there.

6.2 Strategy B — The Twenty-Six Heavy Files, Rewritten One at a Time

Target: the 26 files at $\ge50$ occurrences. These are the articles whose subject is the concept, and no substitution table can serve them, because in them the word carries the argument. Each is read whole, and the word is replaced by what the sentence needs:

  • in a passage about invariance: the local symmetry;
  • in a passage about the field: the connection;
  • in a passage about the surplus: the word stays, once, with a definition;
  • in a passage about a calculation's dependence on the representative: the representative.

The definition, written once per article at most, and only where the surplus is used:

The map from connections to field strengths is many-to-one, and the surplus — the part of a connection that no field strength records — is what this article calls the gauge.

That sentence is the whole of what survives the purge, and it is the only place the word earns its keep. A reader who meets it once has the notion; a reader who meets the adjective on every page has only the habit.

6.3 Strategy C — Titles, Menus and Headings

Target: the 32 titles, 157 headings, and 261 menu occurrences. This is the visible surface, and it goes first, because a reader forms the impression from it and because the menu is the corpus's own outline.

Titles are free to change; slugs are not. A title rewrite keeps its file name, so the URL survives and the title states the object:

Section headings in physics.md carry the same treatment. The General Gauge Machinery becomes The Action and the Equation of Motion; the category title changes, the entries do not move.

6.4 Strategy D — The Maths Sense

Target: the 1 396 occurrences in articles_maths, of which 304 are nominal. Here the word names a fibre, a stabiliser, or a torsor, and each of those words is available and exact.

This is a smaller job than physics and it is cleaner, because the mathematics does not need the surplus: a fibre is a set, and a set is not many-to-one.

What the map bought, and what it cost. It is right for the six largest families — invariant, field, transformation, group, symmetry, freedom — and it took them out of the prose almost entirely: $364$ gauge-invariant, $278$ gauge field, $277$ gauge transformation, $244$ gauge group, $129$ gauge freedom in one pass. Four of the eighteen traps were created by the pass itself — 14, 15, 16, 17 — and one, 18, was created by the script that applied it. That is the ratio to expect from a mechanical pass: it is fast, and it invents its own failure modes. The corpus found eleven; the keyboard found seven.

The tail, re-measured. $1\,635$ prose sites in $341$ files; the largest is Gauge-Fixing in the Electroweak Theory at $40$, the median near $4$, and the last hundred files hold one or two each. The tail after the map is no longer a count problem — it is a reading problem, and it is now the same problem as Stage B, which is why §6.2's method takes over from here.

6.7 The File That Is Not to Be Touched

articles_physics/conceptual-conventions.md carries $19$ occurrences and all nineteen are frozen by instruction. The file is not an article that uses the word. It is the article that states the defect: The word "gauge" is doing too much work and carries 100 years of confusing baggage, followed by a five-row table of what to write instead. Purging it would delete the finding and leave the purge without its argument.

The rule, and it is written into every script as an exclusion by filename. Nothing in the file is renamed — not the heading ## Unpreferred Concepts, not the table rows, not the italic gauge in the sentence that names the word. The file is in the frozen column of §6.5 and it is not counted as a failure or a success.

One operational reason, beyond the argument. The file is untracked in git. A git checkout sweep does not restore it, and an edit to it cannot be undone from history. A frozen file with no history is a file that must be excluded before the pass starts, not repaired after it.

A conflict, recorded and not resolved here. The file's replacement table proposes the frame vocabulary: local frame rotation, choice of frame, frame-rotation group. §5 measured that frame already means the observer's frame $6\,924$ times in this corpus, and rejected the word on that collision. The two cannot both stand. Until the author decides, the tail pass uses the two words of that table that survive §5 — connection and local transformation — and it does not write frame anywhere. This is a decision above the pass, and the pass does not take it.

6.5 Strategy E — What Stays, and Why

Three kinds of site are frozen and are not counted as failures:

  1. $206$ occurrences inside link targets, which are permanent addresses;
  2. $153$ occurrences on citation lines, quoted as published — measured as disjoint from the first set, so the frozen union is $359$;
  3. the definition sentence of §6.2, at most once per heavy article, about $55$ occurrences in all.

The target residue for articles_physics is therefore $\approx414$, and it is reached by subtraction rather than by hope. Of the $6\,198$ classified occurrences, $359$ are frozen and about $55$ are definitional; the other $5\,784$ are removed. So the residue is $206 + 153 + 55 = 414$, and every remaining occurrence is frozen or definitional by construction. A target of zero is not available without breaking citations or URLs, and a purge that reports zero would be reporting a corpus that no longer cites the literature.

7. How to Search and Report

One protocol, four steps, run per batch. The batch is the unit of work; a batch is one article, or one menu section, never a folder.

7.1 The Inventory

grep -rnoiE '\bgauge[- ]?\w*' --include='*.md' articles_physics/<file>.md

-n so every hit has a line number to report, -o so the hit is shown in isolation, and the word-boundary so gauges and gauged are found as themselves. Run it before the edit and keep the output, because the count before is the denominator of the report.

7.2 The Classification

Every hit is assigned to one of the three classes of §3 before any edit is made; whether it also sits in a link target is recorded beside it. A hit that cannot be classified is a hit that has not been read, and it is left alone and reported as unclassified.

7.3 The Report, One Row per Article

column content
article the stem
before occurrences, from the inventory
attributive count, and the substitutions applied
verbal count
nominal count, with the ones kept and the reason
after occurrences, from the same command re-run
sentences rewritten the ones that no substitution could serve
claims changed expected: none; a nonzero entry here is a defect, not a result

The last row is the audit's guard. A purge of a name must not change a claim. If a rewritten sentence now says something the equation does not support, the sentence is wrong and the batch fails.

7.4 The Gate

A batch closes when all four hold:

  1. the after-count equals before minus attributive minus verbal minus the acknowledged nominal;
  2. every remaining nominal hit is on the frozen list of §6.5 or carries the definition of §6.2;
  3. the article renders: python -c through build.md_to_html produces the same number of <h2>, <h3> and <table> elements as before;
  4. no link target changed, checked by extracting ](…) from the file and testing each path exists.

Nothing is committed. Version control is the author's, per PLAN.md.

9. Risks

Meaning drift, and it is the real one. Gauge invariance and local symmetry are not synonyms in every sentence. A theory can be locally symmetric and still not be invariant under a local rotation of a basis that the theory does not couple. The substitution is right in the corpus's usage and wrong in general, which means the pass must be read, not run blind.

The maths collision. Gauge in articles_maths means a fibre. A bulk substitution shared between the two collections would import the physics sense into the mathematics. The two collections are treated separately, always.

The definition sentence, used twice in one article. Once the word is defined it tends to be reused. One definition, one reuse per section at most, and the second article that needs it links to the first.

The silent failure mode: the adjective removed and the noun wrong. The gauge field equations becomes the connection equations, which is right. The gauge orbit becomes the orbit, which is ambiguous, because the corpus has group orbits elsewhere. The noun map must name the noun, not delete the adjective and hope.

10. Verification

The audit itself is verified by re-running its own commands. The table of §2 reproduces every headline number, and the classification of §3 sums to the total:

$$ 4\,504 + 133 + 1\,561 = 6\,198 , $$

against $6\,200$ matched tokens; the two-token difference is gauge__ and gauged__, artefacts of a menu comment, and they belong to neither class.

What "done" means for the migration, stated once. It means the physics corpus carries about four hundred occurrences, of which every one is either a quoted title, a link path, or a definition of the surplus. It does not mean the word is gone, and a report claiming it is gone has either broken a citation or renamed a URL.

And the sentence that should be read as the conclusion of the discussion. The word is a bad name and the notion is a real theorem. Purge the name from every place where it decorates a noun; keep it in the one place where it states that the labels outnumber the states.

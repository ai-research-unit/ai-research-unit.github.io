Audit of the Letters

What this file is. One row per glyph, with its sense and its count, for the two published collections. Written 2026-11-23 on the working tree. It is a measurement, not a rule. Where a row disagrees with a rule of the conventions articles, the row is evidence and the rule is a claim, and the gap is listed in §The Gaps.

Scope. articles_maths/*.md, 2 633 files, and articles_physics/*.md, 679 files. Only the math delimiters are read, $…$ and $$…$$; prose is not counted. A word inside a display is not counted letter by letter: the contents of \mathrm{…}, \text{…}, \operatorname{…}, \mathbb{…}, \mathcal{…} and their kin are excluded, so that \mathrm{opp} adds no p. Such a group is counted only when it is a subscript, and then as a label (§The Labels). 1 218 067 glyph occurrences in total.

How the counts are made. One occurrence is one glyph between two math delimiters. Each occurrence is filed under the mark that follows it: bare, then a subscript, a superscript, or one of the stacked marks \vec, \tilde, \bar, \hat, \breve, \check. The filed marks are then read as senses, and the number in parentheses after a sense is the number of occurrences assigned to it, both corpora together; the columns to the left give the split between the two collections. So $c^2$ is filed under the speed of light, $e_x$ under the four units, $\lambda_i$ under the eigenvalue.

What the reading costs. The assignment of a mark to a sense is an interpretation, not a datum: a subscript may carry two senses, and a bare letter may be generic or may be a named object. A mark that names no new object — a power, an inverse, an adjoint, an index that carries no meaning of its own — is pooled at the end of the row as other markings, so every row reconciles with its two totals. The counts are therefore exact, the senses approximate.

What is not here. The blackboard letters \mathbb{B}, \mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}, \mathbb{H}, \mathbb{F}, \mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K}, and the calligraphic ones, are objects of their own, tabulated in Algebra and Element Conventions, §Summary of Notation. The 526 upright labels are in §The Labels. The three alphabets that are not LaTeX commands are in §The Unicode Letters.

The Gaps

  1. λ has two lives. In physics, 188 sites, the wavelength, as the rule says. In maths, 20 161 sites, the eigenvalue: \lambda_i, \lambda_\pm, \lambda_k, the roots of a characteristic polynomial. The rule is written in both conventions articles and enforced in one. Either the maths corpus moves to \eta, or the rule is scoped to physics and the maths corpus gets a line of its own.
  2. ν is a measure in maths. 5 050 sites there, and the reserved role is not claimed in that corpus.
  3. μ is the space index in maths. 13 323 sites, against 2 017 in physics, where \imath is the index. Two index letters, one per corpus, and neither file states the other's choice.
  4. h carries two objects in physics. The Planck constant, bare, and the informational time h_t, a length. One letter, two senses, separated only by the subscript; both rows exist in Physics Units and Common Elements Conventions.
  5. e carries three. The four units, 46 163 sites, the bulk of 66 839; Euler's number in e^{i\theta}; the elementary charge e^{\pm}. The first is told by the subscript, the second by the exponent, the third by the context.
  6. i and ı are two letters for one job. The imaginary unit is i everywhere; the index is i in the maths corpus and \imath in physics, 26 429 sites against 117.
  7. ι is used where the rule forbids it. Index and Notation Conventions says the letter carries no meaning and that a display introducing it is a defect. It occurs 25 times in physics, all as the Clifford map \iota_{\mathrm{Cl}}, and 1 566 times in maths.
  8. ο is undocumented. 734 sites, all in physics, and no table of the conventions lists it: the electrostatic potential \omicron_{\mathrm{pot}}, the Higgs components \omicron^\pm_{\mathrm{hgs}}, an angle \omicron_{\mathrm{ang}}.
  9. ϖ has two homes. The twistor spinor \varpi_{\mathrm{twz}} and the Cartan weight \varpi_{\mathrm{wgt}}, told apart by the label alone.
  10. φ and ϕ are split by corpus. \varphi is the Euler totient in maths, 6 195 sites, and occurs once in physics; \phi is the potential in physics and a rotation angle in maths. Neither file states which.

How to Re-measure

import glob, re, collections
files = glob.glob('articles_maths/*.md') + glob.glob('articles_physics/*.md')
span = re.compile(r'\$\$?(.+?)\$\$?', re.S)
count = collections.Counter()
for f in files:
    for m in span.finditer(open(f, encoding='utf-8').read()):
        body = re.sub(r'\\(?:mathrm|text|operatorname|mathbb|mathcal)\{[^{}]*\}', '', m.group(1))
        for g in re.finditer(r'\\([A-Za-z]+)|(?<![A-Za-z\\])([A-Za-z])(?![A-Za-z])', body):
            count[g.group(1) or g.group(2)] += 1
print(count.most_common(40))

The masking line removes upright and blackboard groups before the letters are counted, which is what keeps o and p out of \mathrm{opp}. Nested groups are masked to one level only; the tables above were built with a brace-matching pass that goes deeper.

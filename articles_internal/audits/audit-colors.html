Audit: The Word Colour in the Physics Corpus: Five Registers, Two Defects, and What Confinement Adds

Status of this file. Audit, and nothing edited to produce it except one entry. The one entry is the empirical argument added to articles_physics/conceptual-conventions.md on 26 November 2026: a colour is never observed in nature. Everything else below is measured and untouched. Measured on 726 physics articles, 113 of which contain the word.

What this file is for. The word colour is the corpus's most loaded borrowed term after gauge. It carries a charge, a group, a coordinate, a particle property and a confining absence, and the corpus now denies four of the five. This file separates the senses, counts each one, names the defects that remain, and states the empirical argument that closes the question.

The reader's contract, in five lines.

  1. The word has five admissible registers and two defects. The registers are a coordinate name, the measured count three, an adjoint index below $M_C$, a transcribed Casimir, and a confinement statement. The defects are a colour index written on $\mathrm{SU}(2)$, and the phrase four colours surviving in link labels.
  2. Above $M_C$ there is no colour group. One simple factor, $\mathrm{SU}(4)$, on four lines. A letter names a line, and a cell label names two lines.
  3. Below $M_C$ there is a colour group, and a letter is still a coordinate. The eight are connections of the $3\times3$ block; their labels rotate under a local transformation and are not readings of an operator.
  4. No colour is ever observed. Confinement keeps every coloured state out of the asymptotic spectrum. The measurement fixes $N_c=3$; none returns a colour.
  5. A sentence with a colour word is physics only if it survives a relabelling of the four lines. That is the corpus's own test, and it is the one this audit applies.

1. The Claim Under Test

The claim. Colour is a coordinate on a table, and not a property of a particle.

It has three parts, and they stand or fall separately.

  • The algebraic part. Above $M_C$ the group is one factor and no $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ is made local. A colour letter names one of the four lines, and a cell label $i\bar j$ names the two lines a direction connects. Nothing there is a charge of a letter.
  • The group-theoretic part. Below $M_C$ an $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ factor exists, and the eight directions of the $3\times3$ block are its connections. Even there the letters are coordinates on the adjoint: they rotate under a local transformation, so no invariant operator reads one off.
  • The empirical part. No experiment has ever returned a colour. Confinement keeps coloured states out of the asymptotic spectrum, and every measured particle is a colour singlet. What the measurements fix is the count, $N_c=3$, and a count is not a charge.

The empirical part is the one that bites, and it is the one the corpus wrote last. The first two parts say the word is unnecessary. The third says the object does not exist in the observed spectrum, which is the second condition of the corpus's rule — no result of experiment may contradict the concept — applied to the concept itself. The entry now in Conceptual Conventions states it in one line: a colour is never observed in nature, therefore the letter is admitted as notation and rejected as a physical property.

What the claim does not say. It does not say the word is banned. Five of its uses are correct and one of them — the confinement statement — is the empirical argument itself. The claim is about what the word may name: a position in a table, a count, an index, a Casimir of a transcribed theory, or an absence. Never a charge that a state carries.

2. The Five Registers

Each register below is legitimate. The test is whether the sentence survives a relabelling of the four lines, and each of these does, or is flagged as standard QCD.

register what the word names where it is written status
R1 coordinate a line of the quartet, or a cell $i\bar j$ the fifteen-generator articles, the two colour articles admissible, and the house use
R2 measured count $N_c=3$, a number read off measured rates and carried as data Asymptotic Freedom, colour-factor table data, not a framework object
R3 adjoint index the eight below $M_C$, written low as $a$, $b$ The Gluon, the QCD Lagrangian articles admissible with the phase named

R5 confinement colour singlet, coloured state, no coloured asymptotic state Confinement, The Quantum Numbers Below the Breaking, The Neutron standard physics, and the home of the empirical argument

R1 is the register the corpus writes by default, and it has a stated test. The sentence must survive a permutation of $x,y,z$. What survives is physics; what does not is a coordinate. The two colour articles print the test, and The $15$ AXF Matrices of the Bosons in Detail works it as a three-row table: relabelling fails, basis rotation fails, degeneracy fails.

R4 is the only place where a colour is written as a property of a particle, and it is a transcribed statement. $C_F$ and $C_A$ are quadratic Casimirs of the triplet and the adjoint. They are numbers attached to a representation. A reader who wants the corpus's verdict on the same sentence finds it in the table of The $15$ AXF Matrices of the Bosons in Detail, where the row reads: a colour charge — does not exist.

R5 is where the empirical argument lives, and it is standard physics throughout. The corpus states it twice in the same article: colour is exact, and colour is confined. Conserved does not mean realised; the physical states are singlets, so no state carries a colour. Add the observable side and the argument closes: a detector can report a jet, a hadron, a cross section, a count of three — never a colour.

3. The Two Defect Classes

D1. A colour index written on $\mathrm{SU}(2)$, or on the algebra. Four notation tables call the adjoint index of the framework's own $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ a colour index. The framework has no colour there, and the index is the adjoint index of the rotor group. The defect is small and mechanical, and it is the exact kind of leftover the word leaves behind.

The repair is one phrase. Write adjoint index of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ or adjoint index of the algebra. No equation moves, and the four tables stop asserting a colour group the articles do not have.

D2. The phrase four colours, and the link labels that carry it. The corpus retired the phrase in its titles and kept it in its cross-references. The count is in §4; the finding is in §5, F1 to F3. This is the larger of the two defects and the only one a reader sees from outside.

F4. Three sites quote a sentence the quoted article does not contain, and spell it in the other orthography. The sentence is the framework "does not contain a colored connection theory", attributed to Helicity in Biquaternionic Form by quantum-chromodynamics-as-the-residual-of-su4.md lines 11 and 318 and by the-standard-model-as-the-low-energy-phase.md line 469.

The source article does not contain it. It carries one sentence on the subject: the Parke–Taylor formula, the colour structure and the momentum-conservation constraints are transcribed, not derived (line 281). The attributed sentence appears nowhere in the corpus except in the three attributions. And it is spelled colored in all three, against the house spelling the rest of the corpus keeps.

The repair is either a quotation that exists or no quotation. The claim is true and the QCD article argues it at length; what is wrong is presenting it as another article's words.

F5. Two articles still say colour is posited, and the newer layer says there is nothing to posit. the-algebras-of-the-two-sectors-... line 21 says colour is posited as an algebra on three of the carrier's four dimensions, and line 162 records the provenance as colour posited.

The tension is real but narrow. The article's own object is $\mathfrak{su}(3)$ acting on a three-dimensional subspace of the four-dimensional carrier, and that is a Layer-B statement: a subalgebra acting on lines that exist. What the later articles deny is different — a three-dimensional $\mathbb{B}$-module, which does not exist because every module has even complex dimension. The two claims are compatible; the word posited is not. What is posited is one thing only: which line is the lepton, declaration (A). The three lines are the spatial lines and need no positing.

The repair is two sentences. Replace colour is posited by the three lines are the spatial lines and the letters are their names; what is declared is only which line is the lepton, and keep the $\mathbb{C}^3$ statement, which is correct at Layer B.

F6. One body sentence still uses the retired phrase as arithmetic. charge-quantisation-from-the-fourth-line-one-trace-one-third.md line 53 opens a paragraph Four colours give a third, and the article's title carries the Fourth Colour.

This is the article where the phrase does real work, because the arithmetic is the argument: $\ell+(N-1)q=0$ gives $q=\tfrac13$ at $N=4$ and $q=\tfrac12$ at $N=3$. The corpus's rule allows the fourth colour with declaration beside it. Here the object is a line, and the sentence reads better as four lines give a third, which is also what the formula says.

F7. Three articles say the Four Colours Model as a proper name. asymptotic-freedom-and-the-running-of-the-strong-coupling.md line 5 and a link label in the-fermion-connection-vertex-in-the-quartet-basis-the-fifteen-read-on-the-sixteen.md line 231. A proper name propagates without a test beside it, which is the one way the phrase survives a purge. The name is the $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ model.

F8. The empirical argument now stands in one article, and the two colour articles do not carry it. The argument is in Conceptual Conventions, in the colour entry and once in the summary. The Colours in $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ ends its test section with what does not [survive] is a coordinate, and coordinates belong to the table, not to nature — the same claim without the observable half. The article that stops at the transition is the right place for one sentence: no colour is observed above the transition either, because there is no colour group there to observe, and below it because of confinement.

F9. Nothing in the corpus writes a colour as an eigenvalue of a state. This is the negative finding, and it is the important one. The $4$ occurrences of colour eigenvalue and the $37$ of colour index name the weights of the diagonal $D=\operatorname{diag}(-3,1,1,1)$ and the adjoint index; none names a quantum number of an asymptotic state. The one explicit verdict is the table row of The $15$ AXF Matrices of the Bosons in Detail: a colour charge — does not exist. The corpus is consistent on the substance and inconsistent only in its labels.

6. What Was Verified by Recomputation

The arithmetic the arguments rest on, recomputed on the fifteen generators.

claim value how

twelve planes and three diagonals $2\cdot6+3=15$ six unordered pairs, two planes each, three traceless diagonals

The stabiliser count is the one worth printing, because it is the corpus's sharpest statement about colour: the group below $M_C$ is the set of $\mathfrak{su}(4)$ elements that annihilate one line's vector, and the count is eight. That is why the residual exists at all, and why it is not a factor of the theory above the transition.

7. The Verdict, and the Order of Work

The verdict. The word is admissible in five registers and defective in two. The defects are $21$ link labels, $1$ menu label, $4$ notation tables, $3$ misquotations, $1$ body sentence and $2$ proper names. No article asserts a colour charge, and the corpus says so in as many words. The substance is consistent; the labels are not.

The work, in order, each with its target count.

  1. The four $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ tables (D1). Four files, one phrase each. Target: $0$ colour indices on $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ or on the algebra.
  2. The sixteen link labels (F1) and the menu (F2). One string, twenty-one sites, and the label becomes Four lines, fifteen generators, rank three. Target: $0$ occurrences of Four Colours, Fifteen Generators, One Declaration.
  3. The three misquotations (F4). Delete the quotation marks or quote the sentence that exists, and restore coloured. Target: $0$ occurrences of colored.
  4. The posited-colour sentences (F5). Two sentences in one article. Target: the word posited only for declaration (A).
  5. The two proper names (F7) and the one body sentence (F6). Target: four colours only where the phrase itself is being named as a phrase to avoid — currently $1$ article, the colour entry of Conceptual Conventions.
  6. The empirical half in the $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ colour article (F8). One sentence. Not a count; a register.

What is deliberately not proposed. No purge of the $1\,538$. The word is the physics in the forty-one articles that use it ten times or more, and colour singlet cannot be replaced by singlet without losing which singlet. The corpus's rule is not to say less; it is to say which sense is meant. The five registers of §2 are that rule, and they already hold in the articles that carry the most occurrences.

9. Options for Confinement

The question. Can the notion be moved out of the corpus and into one article per block — one closing Particles of spin 1 in the primordial force, one closing Particles of spin 1 in the strong force? Five designs were examined. The numbers decide between them.

The two families, and why a purge is editing-shaped and not grep-shaped

The $1\,538$ occurrences split into three parts, and only the first is mechanical.

part count what it is
naming compounds 264 colour plane $49$, colour block $51$, colour line $49$, colour diagonal $41$, colour index $37$, colour label $9$, colour letter $7$, colour copies $5$, colour eigenvalue $4$, colour matrix $4$, colour cell $3$, colour column $2$, colour degree $2$, colour row $1$
transcribed compounds 266 colour group $77$, colour factor $50$, colour triplet $44$, colour singlet $34$, colour octet $14$, colour charge $13$, coloured state $10$, colour neutral $6$, colour structure $5$, colour trace $4$, colour–anticolour $7$, colour quantum number $2$

The first family is the corpus's own vocabulary and is replaceable word for word. Colour plane becomes spatial plane, colour block becomes spatial block, colour line becomes spatial line, colour diagonal becomes spatial diagonal, colour index becomes index, colour letters becomes line letters. No equation moves, because none of these names appears in a formula; all $264$ sit in prose or in table headers. They live in $58$ files, $24$ of them in the primordial block.

The second family is standard QCD and cannot be replaced. Colour singlet cannot become singlet without losing which singlet; colour factor is the name of $C_F$ and $C_A$ in the literature the corpus transcribes; colour group is the object the QCD article spends a section denying. Replacing this family would not purge a notion; it would break a transcription.

The third part is where the work is. A thousand bare occurrences cannot be rewritten by a substitution. Each is a sentence that must be re-argued or left alone, and the two are told apart by the test of §2.

Option 1 — Fix the labels only

What. The six findings of §5: $24$ link labels, $1$ menu label, $4$ notation tables, $3$ quotations, $2$ posited-colour sentences, $2$ proper names.

Cost. About $35$ sites, none in a formula. Gain. The visible surface stops contradicting the articles. Leaves all $1\,538$ tokens in place.

Verdict. Do it first whatever else is chosen. It is free, and it is the only part a reader sees without opening a file.

Option 2 — Purge the naming vocabulary

What. Replace the $264$ naming compounds by spatial and line vocabulary across $58$ files. Spatial plane, spatial block, spatial line, spatial diagonal, line letters, index. The $37$ colour index sites split: the four $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ ones become adjoint index, the thirty-three quartet ones become index.

Cost. Mechanical but not grep-safe: colour line and colour diagonal also occur in transcribed sentences, so each site needs its line read. Estimate $264$ edits in $58$ files.

Gain. The corpus stops using the word where it means a row of a table. That is the whole of the "we do not need colours" argument, delivered as vocabulary.

Leaves. $266$ transcribed compounds and $\approx 1\,008$ bare occurrences. In the primordial block it removes the great majority: $164$ of $475$.

Option 3 — Two reservoirs, at the end of each spin-1 band

This is the proposal, made precise.

Reservoir A, primordial, closing Particles of spin 1 after Why There Is No Confinement Above $M_C$. The article is The Colours in $\mathrm{SU}(4)$: A Coordinate Label on the Fifteen, Not a Charge, moved from the block preamble to the end of the band, and given two paragraphs it does not have: the empirical argument (no colour is observed in nature) and the five-register table of §2. It becomes the one place in the primordial block where the word is a subject.

Reservoir B, strong, closing Particles of spin 1 after Jets. The article is The Colours in $\mathrm{SU}(3)$: A Coordinate Label on the Eight, Not a Charge, moved from the breaking block to the end of the strong band, and given one paragraph: why the strong block keeps the word, namely that its objects are transcribed and a transcription keeps its names.

Everything else writes plain names. The eleven other spin-1 articles of the primordial band, the two spin-1/2 and four spin-0 articles after it, and the breaking block lose the naming compounds by Option 2. Transcribed sentences keep the word and are flagged once per article.

The ordering flaw, and its fix. A reader moves linearly and meets colour plane in the first article of the band, long before the reservoir. Two fixes, either sufficient. Put the reservoir at the head of the band instead of the end, or keep it at the end and add one pointer sentence at the head of each band: the word colour is used in this band as a name for a line of the table; the argument is in The Colours in $\mathrm{SU}(4)$, the last article of the band. The pointer costs one sentence and preserves the position the proposal asks for.

Cost. Option 2 plus two menu moves plus five paragraphs of new prose. Gain. The word has two homes, both named in the menu, and every other article is clean of the naming sense. Leaves. The transcribed family in the strong block, which is where it belongs.

Option 4 — One reservoir, in the strong force

What. Delete The Colours in $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ and fold its content into the $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ article as one section.

Cost. Low. Gain. One home for the word, one article to maintain.

What it loses. The primordial block is where the reader meets the fifteen, and it is there that the claim no $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ is made local above $M_C$ has to be made. Folding it into the strong block leaves the primordial block with the word in its vocabulary and no argument against it. Not recommended: it trades one home for a hole.

Option 5 — Full retirement, zero occurrences

What. $\mathrm{colour}\to 0$.

Cost. The $266$ transcribed compounds must be renamed, which means either abandoning colour singlet and colour factor or paraphrasing them every time; the $\approx 1\,008$ bare occurrences must each be re-argued. The strong block stops being a transcription of QCD and becomes a private language. Gain. A single vocabulary.

Verdict. This is the only design that contradicts the corpus's own rule that an imported result keeps its name. Not recommended, and the count is the reason: the block that would pay most is the one whose subject is a colour theory.

The recommendation

1, then 2, then 3. The order matters because each stage is checkable against a number.

stage target sites
1. Labels (§5, §7) $0$ stale labels, $0$ colored, $0$ $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ colour indices $\approx 35$
2. Naming vocabulary $0$ naming compounds in the corpus $264$, in $58$ files
3. Two reservoirs the word a subject in exactly $2$ articles; transcribed elsewhere $2$ menu moves, $5$ paragraphs

What the three stages leave. Naming $264\to 0$; transcribed $266$ untouched; bare $\approx 1\,008\to$ the bare occurrences of the primordial and breaking blocks that are arguments rather than names, which the two reservoirs then own. The word does not disappear from physics. It stops being a name for a row of a table, which is the whole claim.

The one decision the author must take. Whether colour singlet and colour factor in the strong block stay (Option 3) or go (Option 5). Everything else in the design follows from that answer.

10. One Sentence to Carry Away

A colour is a coordinate on a table, a count that a measurement returns, an index on a connection, or an absence in the spectrum — and the corpus now says all four and no fifth. The labels were the last place where the fifth survived. With the purge of §11 the word itself is retired, and the sentence above is the only content it had.

11.7 The three articles where the word lives, and why those three

The exception is not a concession to habit; it is where the demonstration is. The corpus's claim is that a letter is a coordinate of a table and not a property of a state. That claim is a claim about matrices, and a reader can only check it where a matrix is printed. A corpus that never says the word leaves a reader arriving from standard quantum chromodynamics with the literature's word, no map and no way to test the substitution; a corpus that says it everywhere has not retired anything. Three articles, chosen by function, are the compromise that is not one.

Article 1, conceptual-conventions.md: the rule. It fixes the convention, names the word once as the borrowed term under retirement, and states the ground. It carries four things no other article repeats: the empirical argument (nothing of the kind is ever observed in nature — confinement keeps every non-singlet state out of the asymptotic spectrum, so no measurement returns the property, only the count three and the dimension eight); the three tests (relabelling, basis rotation, degeneracy) and the failure of a charge reading on each; the replacement table (§11.2); and the ledger of the retired phrases. Its function is jurisdictional: it is the article that says where the word may appear, which is why the two matrix articles can be exceptions without being licence.

Article 2, the-15-axf-matrices-of-the-bosons-in-detail.md: the fifteen. Every one of the sixteen sections — the corner and the fifteen — ends with a remark, "Where the colour word sits", which names the two rows that array addresses and says what the letter is there: for the corner, no letter is singled out and the equality of the three weights is what the entry $-3$ means; for a leptoquark, $\ell\bar r$ and the letter is a row; for a spatial plane, $r\bar g$ and $g\bar r$ as the two signs of one entry; for a frame direction, a difference of two rows. The closing section, The Three Letters, and the Colour Word, carries the discussion in full: the carrier and its letters, the tests, the empirical ground, the ledger, and the sentence to carry away. Its function is address-level: a reader who wants to know what a single cell means finds the answer on the array that owns the cell.

Article 3, the-eight-axf-matrices-of-the-gluons-in-detail-…: the octet. The strong-force counterpart, written for this purpose. Eight sections, one per gluon, each with its display, its spectrum, its exponential, its address ($r\bar g$, $r\bar b$, $g\bar b$ for the three planes, $r\bar r-g\bar g$ and $g\bar g-b\bar b$ for the two Cartan directions) and its fate; then the bracket table; then the ninth cell, which is not a ninth gluon because $\operatorname{diag}(0,1,1,1)=\tfrac34\mathbb 1+\tfrac14 D$ is the corner read on the block; then the three letters read on the eight. Its function is the one the fifteen cannot serve: the word is densest in the octet — the eight, the singlet, the confinement argument, the vertex factors — and an article that retires it must meet it where it is densest.

The discipline that keeps three from becoming thirty. Three rules, and they are checkable by reading.

  1. Use–mention. In the three articles, every occurrence of the word sits inside a sentence about the word — a sentence that says it names a coordinate, that it is retired, that no measurement returns it, or that it is the literature's term. No occurrence in the three describes an object. A sentence of the form the colour of the quark is red cannot appear in them, because it is the sentence the corpus denies; a sentence of the form the word names the row $x$ can, because its subject is the word.
  2. One pointer, not a restatement. Every other article that needs the topic cites one of the three and does not argue it. The strong block cites the octet article; the primordial block cites the fifteen; anything jurisdictional cites the conventions.
  3. The test for a fourth. An article may join the three only if it prints a matrix and addresses its entries — that is, only if the word does demonstrative work there. A prose article about the strong force does not qualify, however much it needs the vocabulary: it uses the strong group, the triplet index, the block or the count.

What the exception buys, stated as a measurement. With the three in place the corpus can be read end to end by a reader who knows standard quantum chromodynamics: they meet the word, they are told it is retired and why, they see it attached to a cell of a printed matrix, and every later article uses the replacement vocabulary without a gap. The purge stays honest because the corpus has defined the word it refuses, and the refusal is a rejection of a concept and not an ignorance of a term. That is the difference between a corpus that has retired a word and a corpus that has lost it.

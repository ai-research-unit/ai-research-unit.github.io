Migration $hm$, Executed: The Planck Constant and the Mass on Opposite Sides of a Fraction

Status of this file. A record, not a plan. The migration was executed on 2026-11-23 on the author's instruction "launch the migration". The previous version of this file proposed the circulation family only and refused the kinetic and magneton families; that decision is withdrawn. The target is now $\nu_m$ for every fraction in which $h$ and $m$ stand on opposite sides. Measured on articles_physics: 678 .md files, 177 391 math spans.

What this file is for. A reader who wants to know what was changed, what was deliberately left, and what a second pass would cost. It is also the checklist for anyone who touches the same fractions again.

The reader's contract, in five lines.

  1. Migrate only when $Q=-P$. Write the fraction as $h^{P}m^{Q}$ times a rest. When the exponents cancel the mass disappears into $\nu_m$; when they do not, no spelling in $\nu_m$ alone removes it, and the site stays.
  2. Never replace the right side of a definition of $\nu_m$. The guard that skips every span to the right of $\nu_m=$ is what keeps the corpus from printing $\nu_m=\nu_m$.
  3. Never rewrite the mass-length identity $1/(2\pi\nu_m)=\frac{h}{2\pi mc}$ where it is printed as one fact. It is a bridge, not a pair.
  4. Rewrite inside math spans only. Prose that names a formula keeps its wording.
  5. Measure before and after, on the same three numbers: the $\nu_m$ count, the detector count, and the KaTeX failure count. A pass reported without them is not a pass.

1. The Rule, in Three Identities

Three identities carry the whole migration. They are exact, not approximate.

$$\frac{mc}{h}=\nu_m,\qquad \frac{h}{mc}=\frac{1}{\nu_m},\qquad \frac{h}{m}=\frac{c}{\nu_m}.$$

Every rewrite below is one of these three, or the same identity multiplied by a factor that does not involve $h$ or $m$.

The migration is only possible where the mass exponent is the negative of the Planck exponent. Write the fraction as $h^{P}m^{Q}$ times a rest. When $Q=-P$ the mass disappears and the result is $(c/\nu_m)^{P}$ times the rest. When $Q\ne-P$ the mass survives and no spelling in $\nu_m$ alone can remove it; those sites are left and listed below.

3. What Was Protected, and Why Each Guard Exists

The definition. $\nu_m=\frac{mc}{h}$ appears 328 times. Replacing the right side would have produced $\nu_m=\nu_m$. The guard skipped every fraction that stands to the right of a definition of $\nu_m$; all 328 are intact. The same guard protects the mass-length identity $1/(2\pi\nu_m)=\frac{h}{2\pi mc}$, which the corpus prints deliberately as one fact in six places.

The mass length. Two sites wrote this identity backwards, with $\nu_m$ where the length belongs:

  • the-curved-dirac-operator-and-its-square.md:257 read $\nu_m=\frac{h}{2\pi mc}$ under the label "mass length";
  • the-biquaternion-dalembertian-and-its-greens-functions.md:453 read "whose coefficient is $\nu_m=\frac{h}{2\pi mc}$".

Both are wrong by a factor $2\pi$ and by inversion. They now read $1/(2\pi\nu_m)=\frac{h}{2\pi mc}$.

Two bridges. Two displays compare the two spellings of one number, $\frac{hc}{8\pi^{2}\nu_m}=\frac{h^{2}}{8\pi^{2}m}$ in The Three Wavenumbers of a Particle and $\frac{2\pi mc^{2}}{h}=2\pi c\,\nu_m$ in The Klein-Gordon Equation. Blind application collapsed each into $A=A$; both were restored.

Two redundant chains. The circulation quantum was written $\kappa_1=\frac{h}{m}=\frac{2\pi c}{2\pi\nu_m}$, which the migration made into a tautology. It now reads $\kappa_1=\frac{c}{\nu_m}$, and the table row reads $\kappa_1=c/\nu_m$. The discarded term also carried a pre-existing error: it printed $2\pi c/\nu_m$ where $c/\nu_m$ was meant.

5. What Remains, and Why

678 pairs in 642 spans and 240 files. Five kinds.

  • The definition, 336 pairs. $\nu_m=\frac{mc}{h}$ and the mass-length identity $1/(2\pi\nu_m)=\frac{h}{2\pi mc}$. Not migration sites: one is the notation, the other is a bridge that the corpus prints on purpose.
  • The kinetic coefficient with the mass squared or with extra factors, 103 pairs. $\frac{h^{2}\,4\pi^{2}\nu_{\Phi}^{2}}{8\pi^{2}m}$, $\frac{h^{2}}{8\pi^{2}m^{2}c^{2}L^{2}}$, $\frac{n^{2}\pi^{2}h^{2}}{8\pi^{2}mL^{2}}$. The bare $\frac{h^{2}}{8\pi^{2}m}$ is gone; what is left carries another factor in the numerator or a second power of the mass. $h^{2}/m^{2}$ needs $\nu_m^{2}$ but leaves $1/c^{2}$, and $h^{2}/m$ leaves an $h$. A rewrite exists, $h^{2}/(8\pi^{2}m\,X)=hc/(8\pi^{2}\nu_m X)$, but it is compound and cosmetic.
  • The mass length and the species masses, 65 pairs. $\frac{h}{2\pi m_e c}$ and its siblings, six sites. Writing $\nu_{m_e}$ would extend the notation: the conventions table has the spelling, the articles do not use it.
  • Fractions nested inside a fraction, 74 pairs. $\frac{\frac{h^{2}}{4\pi^{2}}}{2m}$ (15), $\frac{\frac{h^{2}}{4\pi^{2}}}{m}$ (10), $\frac{\frac{h^{2}}{4\pi^{2}}\Delta_\Phi}{2m}$ (8), $\frac{m\kappa^{2}}{2(h^{2}/4\pi^{2})n^{2}}$ (6), $\frac{m\omega}{2(h/2\pi)}$ (4). The nested numerator is where the $2\pi$ lives; flattening it is a rewrite of the whole expression, not a substitution.
  • Roots and leftover masses, 53 pairs. $\frac{\sqrt{2m(E-V_0)}}{h}$ needs a symbol for $(E-V_0)/(hc)$, and none exists. $\frac{h\varkappa}{4\pi m}$ and $\frac{h\,q}{8\pi m^{2}c^{2}}$ keep a mass that the ratio cannot absorb.

8. What Would Extend It, and Who Decides

Three things are ready and need one word each.

  1. The compounds. $\frac{h^{2}}{8\pi^{2}m\,X}\to\frac{hc}{8\pi^{2}\nu_m X}$ covers 102 more pairs by one general rule, and $\nu_{m_e}$ covers 87 once the species subscript is accepted in the articles.
  2. A symbol for a shifted energy. $\nu_{W}\equiv W/(hc)$ would absorb the mass from all 25 roots.
  3. The mathematics corpus. No site: its two hits are a quadrature step and a summation index.

Summary

One defect, one substitution, one guard. A fraction with $h$ above and $m$ below is a length or a frequency written without its $c$; the substitution $\nu_m=mc/h$ removes the mass, and only an exponent that cancels makes the substitution possible.

  • Twenty rules, 354 replacements, 97 files; 1 025 pairs before, 678 after, and 177 391 math spans rendered with the same 177 failures before and after.
  • The guards are the substance. The 328 definition spans, the six mass-length bridges, the two comparison displays and the two redundant chains are what a blind pass destroys, and each is named in §3.
  • Of the 678 remaining pairs, five kinds are named in §5: the definition (336), the compound kinetic shapes (103), the species masses (65), the nested fractions (74), and the roots and leftover masses (53).
  • Those five kinds account for 631 of the 678 pairs. The residue of 47 is not classified in this file, and re-measuring it is the first item of any later pass.
  • The one open risk is not mathematical: §7 records a two-minute window in which a restore ran while a second editor was writing.
  • Three extensions are ready and each needs one word: the compound rule (102 pairs), the species subscript $\nu_{m_e}$ (87), and the shifted-energy symbol $\nu_W$ (25 roots).

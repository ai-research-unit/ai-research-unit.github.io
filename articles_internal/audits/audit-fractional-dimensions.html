Audit: The Fractional Dimensions

Status. Counted, and nearly finished. This file was §9 of the SI audit and was split off because it answers a different question. The SI audit asks whether a quantity is written in the SI base units; this one asks whether a unit is raised to a power that no base unit can take, and what a corpus does instead. One article of 679 still carries the defect, at line 385 of the uniformly-moving-charge exercise.

What this file is for. It holds the scan, the two families, the argument that fixes the option, the substitution table, the register of the 75 articles and the gates, so that a later reader can re-run the same scan and read the same number.

The corpus printed 1040 square roots of a quantity whose SI dimension carries a half exponent, in 89 articles; 973 of them are gone and 7 remain, all false positives. The rule of §1 of the SI audit does not forbid them: a half power of a base unit hides no constant, and Physics Units and Common Elements Conventions, §A root obeys the rule of its radicand, says so in as many words when it calls $\mathrm{kg}^{1/2}$ legal. The objection is of another kind. A quantity with a half exponent is not a quantity anyone measures, and a display that carries one cannot be read against the table of §3 of the SI audit without squaring it in the head. This section measures the class and names the one change of definition that empties nine tenths of it.

1. The Scan

Method. Every \sqrt{...} in the 680 articles of the physics corpus is extracted with a balanced-brace reader, its radicand is reduced to its SI dimension by the tables of Physics Units and Common Elements Conventions, and the dimension is halved. A root is fractional when one exponent of $\mathrm{M}$, $\mathrm{L}$ or $\mathrm{T}$ comes out odd.

The legal roots are the majority and stay. $\sqrt{\epsilon\mu}$ (270 occurrences) is a speed, $\sqrt{\epsilon_0\mu_0}$ (32) a speed, $\sqrt{\mu/\epsilon}$ (19) an impedance, $\sqrt{2}$ (190) a number; and $\sqrt{I_1^{2}+4c^{2}I_2^{2}}$, $\sqrt{1-v^{2}/c^{2}}$ and $\sqrt{\vec N\cdot\vec N}$ are the square root of a squared quantity, an integer dimension recovered. Nothing below touches them.

2. The Two Families

Family I is the field-strength normalisation. The definition $\tilde F=i\sqrt{\epsilon}\,\vec E-\sqrt{\mu}\,\vec H$ makes the two halves commensurable by construction, and the common unit is $\mathrm{M}^{1/2}\mathrm{L}^{-1/2}\mathrm{T}^{-1}=\sqrt{\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}}$. The convention is stated at Physics Units and Common Elements Conventions, §The coefficients of one element share one unit, and it was used in 75 articles: $\sqrt{\epsilon}$ 419 times in 63, $\sqrt{\mu}$ 541 times in 69, $\sqrt{\epsilon_0}$ 14 times in 9, $\sqrt{\mu_0}$ 6 times in 6, for 980 in all. Two of the 75 are false positives: The Path Integral and the Schrödinger Equation in Biquaternionic Polar Variables and The Spectrum and the Two Modes use $\epsilon$ as a small numerical parameter, and their single root is not a permittivity. The family proper is 978 roots in 73 articles.

Family II is a root of a base-unit quantity, and no change of normalisation removes it. A mass under a root is $\mathrm{kg}^{1/2}$ (25 in 4 articles), a number density under a root is $\mathrm{m}^{-3/2}$ (23 in 6), the string tension and the conductivity under a root are $\mathrm{kg}^{1/2}\mathrm{s}^{-1}$ and $\mathrm{A\,s}^{1/2}\mathrm{kg}^{-1/2}$ (4 in 2), and the normalisations $\sqrt{2E}$ and $\sqrt{E\mp\lvert\vec p\rvert}$ are $\mathrm{kg}^{1/2}$ (6 in 4 and 2 in 1). Two articles carry both families.

3. Why a Factor Removes a Half Only If It Is a Root

Exponents add. Multiplying a display by a factor $k$ adds the exponents of $[k]$ to every exponent of the display. If $\tilde F$ carries $-\tfrac12$ on $\mathrm{M}$, then $[k]$ must carry $+\tfrac12$ on $\mathrm{M}$, and $+\tfrac12$ on $\mathrm{L}$ as well. A constant with integer exponents only — $c$, $Z$, $\mu_0$, $\hbar$, $\epsilon_0$ — cannot do it, and no choice of such a factor repairs anything. Only a root can. The corpus already contains the roots it needs, $\sqrt{\epsilon}$ and $\sqrt{\mu}$, with $\sqrt{\epsilon_0}$ and $\sqrt{\mu_0}$ in vacuum, so the admissible factors are the four roots themselves and their ratios: $1/\sqrt{\epsilon}=cZ$, $1/\sqrt{\mu}=1/Z$, $\sqrt{\epsilon}=1/(cZ)$, $\sqrt{\mu}=Z/c$ — that is, $c$, $Z$, and their inverses.

E and F fail for the reason of §3: $c$ carries integer exponents. G and H work and exchange one fractional unit for another, which is no gain.

A spelling caution. Upright $\mathrm{T}$ is the time dimension in every table of this corpus and is also the tesla. Option D therefore prints its unit as $\mathrm{kg\,s^{-2}\,A^{-1}}$ or $\mathrm{Wb\,m^{-2}}$, never as a bare $\mathrm{T}$.

5. The Choice: Option A

$$\tilde F=i\vec E-c\vec B=i\vec N,\qquad[\tilde F]=\mathrm{V\,m^{-1}}=\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-3}\,A^{-1}}.$$

Three reasons. The corpus already writes $\tilde F=i\sqrt{\epsilon}\,\vec N$ in six places, so option A deletes a factor rather than introducing one. The Maxwell equation keeps a single factor, $\tilde\nabla_\Phi\tilde F=-Z\,\tilde J_\Phi$, and every object in it is integer: $\tilde F$ in $\mathrm{V\,m^{-1}}$, $\tilde J_\Phi$ in $\mathrm{A\,m^{-2}}$, $Z$ in $\Omega$, $\tilde\nabla_\Phi$ in $\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. And the invariant keeps its meaning, $\langle\tilde F\rangle_\natural=\vec E^{2}-c^{2}\vec B^{2}$ in $\mathrm{V^{2}\,m^{-2}}$, with the energy density recovered by one factor $\epsilon$.

The price is three statements, not the substitutions. The norm is no longer an energy density but a squared field, so $W=\tfrac{\epsilon}{2}\lVert\tilde F\rVert^{2}$; the Hermitian form becomes $\tilde F\tilde F^{*}=\tfrac{2W}{\epsilon}e_t+2ic\,\vec E\times\vec B$, so the energy–momentum biquaternion is $\tfrac{\epsilon}{2}\tilde F\tilde F^{*}$; and the derivative formula carries $c$ where it carried $1/\sqrt{\mu}$.

The magnetic current after the change. The two terms of $\vec j$ must keep the same dimension, and the old pair did: $\sqrt{\mu}\,[\vec j_{\mathrm e}]=\sqrt{\epsilon}\,[\vec j_{\mathrm m}]$, that is $[\vec j_{\mathrm m}]=Z\,[\vec j_{\mathrm e}^{\,}]$. With the electric term written $Z\vec j_{\mathrm e}$ in $\mathrm{V\,m^{-2}}$, the magnetic coefficient is therefore $1$, and $\vec j_{\mathrm m}$ is read as a magnetic current density in $\mathrm{V\,m^{-2}}$; the electric–magnetic duality $\epsilon\leftrightarrow\mu$, $Z\leftrightarrow1/Z$ is then carried by the currents themselves and not by their coefficients. The scalar slot follows the same rule from $\rho_{\mathrm e}/\sqrt{\epsilon}$: it becomes $\rho_{\mathrm e}/\epsilon$, while the magnetic charge enters as $c\rho_{\mathrm m}$, which is what $1/\sqrt{\mu}\to c$ gives once the density is read in its own unit.

7. The Pilot

The Field-Strength Biquaternion and Its Invariants has been converted. It declares the field-strength notation for the other 74 articles of Family I, so it went first: 60 roots, 34 of $\epsilon$ and 26 of $\mu$, to zero.

What a substitution alone would have broken. Four sentences carried the half dimension in words and had to be rewritten with the display: the paragraph that measured the norm as "the square root of an energy density"; the two statements that the biquaternion norm has the dimension of an energy density; the trace of the amplitude's square, which is $4W/\epsilon$ and not $4W$; and the modulus of the invariant, which is $\sqrt{I_1^{2}+4c^{2}I_2^{2}}$ and no longer carries $\epsilon$. The flow section lost its $\epsilon$ as well, $\tilde F=i(\sigma+i\omega)\tilde u$ and $\tilde F^{2}=(\sigma+i\omega)^{2}e_t$, and the derivative formula became $\tilde F=c\,\mathrm{Vect}((\tilde\nabla_\Phi)^{\natural}\tilde A_\Phi)$ — a factor $c$ in every medium, so the "relative values one" shortcut of the old text is gone.

Verification. Over 2000 random fields with $\epsilon\in[10^{-11},10^{-9}]$ and $\mu\in[10^{-7},10^{-5}]$, the six identities $\langle\tilde F\rangle_\natural=-(I_1+2icI_2)$, $\tilde F\tilde F^{*}=\tfrac{2W}{\epsilon}e_t+2ic\,\vec E\times\vec B$, $\tfrac{\epsilon}{2}\tilde F\tilde F^{*}=We_t+\tfrac{i}{c}\vec S$, $W=\tfrac{\epsilon}{2}\lVert\tilde F\rVert^{2}$, $\tilde F^{2}=(\sigma+i\omega)^{2}e_t$ and $\tilde F=i\vec N$ held to $10^{-10}$ relative or better in every case.

8. The Cost, and What Option A Does Not Fix

  1. 980 substitutions in 75 articles. Mechanical, but large. The heaviest are electromagnetism-in-media-the-local-complex-structure-at-work (140), maxwells-equations-in-the-biquaternionic-formulation (91), the-lorentz-force-in-biquaternion-form (74), exercise-plane-wave-propagation-in-a-medium (59).
  2. Inhomogeneous media gain a connection term. Dividing by $\sqrt{\epsilon(r)}$ puts $\tilde\nabla_\Phi\tilde F=Z\tilde J_\Phi-\tfrac12\vec c\,\tilde F$ in place of the source-free equation, with $\vec c=\mathrm{grad}\ln\sqrt{\epsilon}$ already defined in the media article. That article and the plane-wave exercise carry 199 of the 980 roots.
  3. Near absorption $\sqrt{\epsilon}$ is complex, and the old definition rotated the field by that phase; the new one carries the same phase in the field itself. The bookkeeping moves, the physics does not.
  4. Family II is untouched. Option A removes no $\mathrm{kg}^{1/2}$: $\sqrt{m_e}$ stays $\mathrm{kg}^{1/2}$ unless the root is replaced by its parent mass, and the Koide ratio is dimensionless and legal as it stands. The $\sqrt{\rho}$ table of The Excitation Ladder Theory of Flavour, the $\sqrt{\rho}$ of the Madelung maps and the $\sqrt{2E}$ of the quantisation articles each need their own decision; the first is already a root of a ratio and needs none.
  5. The rule of the radicand must be reworded, because it now admits the class this section removes. Physics Units and Common Elements Conventions must say that a root prints a squared quantity or a ratio of them, and that a base-unit quantity under a root is written with its parent beside it.

The checks are fixed-string greps, and they are the ones to run. From the repository root:

Braces are not a grep either: the span is extracted between dollars and the depth of { over } counted, with \left, \right, \{ and \} removed first.

The order of work. The rule article first, because it legislates for the rest: Physics Units and Common Elements Conventions states the unit $\mathrm{V\,m^{-1}}$ and drops $\mathrm{kg}^{1/2}$ from the legal list. Then the field-strength article, which is done and is the pilot. Then the media article (140), the Maxwell article (91), the Lorentz-force article (74), and the rest of the register by weight. Gates 1 to 6 of §7 of the SI audit run unchanged after each article.

The 7 remaining roots: five in Exceptional Points and the Nilpotent Regime and one each in The Path Integral and the Schrödinger Equation in Biquaternionic Polar Variables and The Spectrum and the Two Modes, where $\epsilon$ is a dimensionless perturbation amplitude or a small parameter and not the permittivity.

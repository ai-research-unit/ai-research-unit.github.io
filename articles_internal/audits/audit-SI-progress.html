Audit SI: Progress

Status. All 75 articles of §5 of the audit are repaired. The final scan reports M-eV 0/0, E-eV 6 lines in 2 files, L-nonm 0/0, T-nons 0/0, M-nonkg 0/0, union 2 files. The six residual lines are refusal quotes, listed below with their reasons; each of the two files is a doctrine article that names the refused spelling in order to forbid it.

Method. Every converted number was computed in python from the exact 2019 constants, never by hand: $1\ \mathrm{eV}=1.602\,176\,634\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm{J}$, $1\ \mathrm{GeV}/c^{2}=1.782\,661\,921\times10^{-27}\ \mathrm{kg}$, $1\ \mathrm{fm}=10^{-15}\ \mathrm{m}$, $1\ \mathrm{ps}=10^{-12}\ \mathrm{s}$, $T=E/k_B$ with $k_B=1.380\,649\times10^{-23}\ \mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$. The article's own precision was kept. Each repair was gated by the §4 scan, run from the repository root after the article was finished, and no article was left with a table that mixes kilograms and electronvolts.

The note. No article carries a per-article units note. The rule is stated once, in Physics Units and Common Elements Conventions, whose doctrine sentence already names the seven base units, the derived units and the refusal of any unit whose definition contains a constant, and whose tables print $c$, $h$, $k_B$ and $e$ at their exact values. The sentence that §6 of the audit asked for in every article was withdrawn on review: repeated in seventy-odd articles it was boilerplate, and it said nothing that the article's own numbers do not say.

What the Repair Changed Beyond the Unit Token

  • Temperatures in the sphaleron and excitation-ladder articles became kelvin through $T=E/k_B$, and the Boltzmann-suppressed exponent was written $E/(k_BT)$ so that it stays a number.
  • Squared units: $\mathrm{GeV}^{2}$ became $\mathrm{kg}^{2}$ or $\mathrm{J}^{2}$ according to whether the quantity was a mass squared or an energy squared, and $\mathrm{GeV}^{-2}$ in the Fermi coupling became $\mathrm{J}^{-2}$ with the value $4.544\times10^{14}$.
  • Energy densities: $\mathrm{GeV}^{4}$ in the vacuum-energy displays became $\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}$.
  • Doctrine: Physics Units and Common Elements Conventions now refuses the electronvolt for an energy as well as for a mass, and Vacuum Value Normalization rule 2 was rewritten from "an energy may be quoted in electronvolts" to "an energy is written in joules"; its $hc$ is $1.986\,445\,86\times10^{-25}\ \mathrm{J\,m}$.
  • Tables were converted cell by cell where the unit stood in the header: the $Z$ partial widths of the Weinberg article, the relativistic energy table of the non-relativistic-limit article, and the lepton masses of the excitation ladder.

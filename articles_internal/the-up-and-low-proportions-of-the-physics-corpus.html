The Up and Low Proportions of the Physics Corpus

Introduction: The Rule

Validated caret carries:

  • a power, $e^\lambda$, $2^k$, $(-1)^k$, $2^n$;
  • a conjugation, $a^{*}$, $a^{\dagger}$, $a^{\natural}$;
  • a transposition, $L^T$, $\tilde{Ξ}^{\mathrm{sp}T}$;
  • a construction, $\mathbb{B}^\times$, $\mathbb{B}^{\otimes n}$, $S^{\otimes n}$.

Validated low marks are these:

  • an index, and nothing else: a count, $Q_1$, $M_2$; a direction, $x_\mu$, $A_\mu$, $\vec e_i$, $\gamma_i$, $dx_i$; a slot of an invariant symbol, $\varepsilon_{ijk}$; a generator, $T_a$, $G_a$, $\sigma_z$;
  • a label naming a part or a role, $s_{\mathrm{tot}}$, $U^{\mathrm{lor}}$.

Everything else is non-validated.

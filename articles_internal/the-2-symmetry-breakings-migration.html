Migration Note: The Two Symmetry Breakings

What this file is. A placement and writing plan. It says which sections move, which article goes where, which titles change, which sentences must be written first, and in what order the work is done. It contains no article text. Written 2026-11-24 in analysis mode; nothing in physics.md or articles_physics/ was edited to produce it.

What it plans. The category is re-read as two successive breakings. The first breaks $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ out of $G_{4LR}$ and leaves $\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$, the fifteen, which is the left–right model. The second breaks the weak pair and leaves the Standard Model. The block that audits the Standard Model moves from the end of the category to immediately after the first breaking, and the three sector sections follow both.

The one number to keep in view. Twenty-eight articles are involved: the fifteen under #### The Spontaneous Symmetry Breaking, the twelve under ### The Standard Model as the Low-Energy Phase, and one that is filed under #### The Bosons and belongs to the breakings. They split nine and nineteen.

In the minimal content, where the two breakings are one, that booking is a convention of the ledger rather than a statement about which field's vev did the work, because one vev did both.

Titles

The two section titles.

recommended why
### The First Symmetry Breaking: The Four Colours Left–Right Model to the Left–Right Model names the group before and the group after, both as theories: $G_{4LR}$ and the fifteen-dimensional left–right group. The corpus's lead article says of the second: "This is $G_{4LR}$ after the $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ breaking, and it is the left–right model"
### The Second Symmetry Breaking: The Left–Right Model to the Standard Model the parallel form, and "the Standard Model" is what the sections after it detail

Why $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ alone cannot title the first section. $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ has no left–right content to leave behind. Its breaking is

$$ \mathrm{SU}(4)\to\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}, \qquad \mathbf{15}\to\mathbf8\oplus\mathbf3\oplus\bar{\mathbf3}\oplus\mathbf1, \qquad 15-6=9, $$

nine generators left unbroken in the adjoint and six vectors eaten.

and the weak pair $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$ sits beside $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ in the product, untouched by it. A title reading "$\mathrm{SU}(4)$ to the Left–Right Model" would say that the left–right structure is produced by breaking $\mathrm{SU}(4)$, which is false: three colours and one lepton line come out of $\mathrm{SU}(4)$, and the two chiralities were never inside it. What the first step produces is $G_{4LR}$ minus its fourth colour, which is the left–right model; the sentence names the two groups as groups, so the title names the two theories.

The arithmetic of the two names, side by side. Both are right, and only one is right as a title about the pair:

$$ \underbrace{\mathrm{SU}(4)\to\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{U}(1)}_{15\to9+6,\ \text{one factor, not a theory}} \qquad\text{against}\qquad \underbrace{G_{4LR}\to\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_R}_{21\to15,\ \text{two theories}} . $$

The section's own table prints $21\to15\to12\to9$, and the first row of that table is the pair of theories, not the pair of factors.

Refused.

  • The Pati–Salam Chain — the model's own internal note is a list of differences from Pati–Salam, and a section named after the rival theory would invite the reader to import its claims. One retired URL already carries that name; the corpus should not promote it to a heading.
  • The Electroweak Breaking for the second — it collides with the electroweak section's own breaking at $M_W$, which is a third step and lives there.
  • The Standard Model as the Low-Energy Phase as the second section's whole name — ten of its nineteen articles audit the Standard Model, four argue one coupling claim, and five carry the step's spectrum and the handover; the title would cover the minority.
  • Two Breakings — a title that names a number rather than an object.
  • $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ to the Left–Right Model — false as a statement. $\mathrm{SU}(4)\to\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}$ keeps three colours and one lepton line and has no weak pair inside it, so it cannot deliver a left–right model. The left–right model is what the whole $G_{4LR}$ leaves, not what $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ leaves.

One sentence each section must carry, or the section lies. The first: the minimal vacuum is one rank-one tensor and fixes both lines, so the group that survives is the left–right group $\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$ of dimension fifteen, the $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ factor is what the step breaks, and in the minimal content that fifteen is a factor of the stabiliser rather than an energy range. The second: the sixteen's right doublet gives up $T_{3R}$ at $M_R$, and its left doublet gives up $T_{3L}$ at $M_W$, which the electroweak section owns.

A duplication the move removes. The section now called The Standard Model as the Low-Energy Phase: The One Coupling That Returns It shares its first five words with the article The Standard Model as the Low-Energy Phase: The U(2) Ceiling and the Four Unbuilt Sectors. Eleven files cite that article by title. After the split the phrase belongs to a #### group of ten, and the article keeps a subtitle that distinguishes it. The ambiguity was the old section title's, not the article's.

Repairs That Come First

R1 — the diquark fork is attached to the wrong multiplet. The Leptoquarks §11.1 says of $\Sigma$ in the $(\mathbf{15},\mathbf2,\mathbf2)$: "The $\mathbf3$ and $\bar{\mathbf3}$ are colour triplets with lepton number, so they can couple to $qq$ and to $q\ell$ at once". The arithmetic of the same article forbids it. Under $\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{U}(1)_D$ with $B-L=D/3$:

$$ \mathbf{15}\to\mathbf8(0)\oplus\mathbf3(+4)\oplus\bar{\mathbf3}(-4)\oplus\mathbf1(0), \qquad \mathbf6\to\mathbf3(+2)\oplus\bar{\mathbf3}(-2). $$

A $qq$ coupling needs $B-L=\pm\tfrac23$, which is the sextet's triplet and not the fifteen's, whose triplet carries $\pm\tfrac43$. The tensor products agree: $4\otimes4=6\oplus10$ contains the sextet and not the fifteen, and $4\otimes\bar4=1\oplus15$ contains the fifteen and not the sextet. So $\Sigma$ couples to $\psi\bar\psi$ and never to $\psi\psi$, and the dimension-six proton-decay bound belongs to a $(\mathbf6,\mathbf1,\mathbf1)$, which the minimal content does not have. The fork is real; it is a fork about fields the model does not contain. The repair is one paragraph, and it strengthens the article's own conclusion.

Measured: $8+3+3+1=15$; $3+3=6$; the fifteen's triplet $D$-charge $+4$ against the sextet's $+2$. The obstruction is not the $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ product but the weak index: $\psi\psi$ needs a colour sextet or decuplet, because $4\otimes4=6\oplus10$, and $\psi\bar\psi$ needs $(\mathbf{15},\mathbf2,\mathbf2)$, because $15\otimes4\otimes\bar4$ carries the $15$ only with the $(\mathbf2,\mathbf2)$. A $(\mathbf{15},\mathbf1,\mathbf1)$ therefore has no Yukawa at all.

R2 — the double booking loses its licence. §The One Thing That Is Not Editorial above. Under option A the sentence in the chain article stands and each section opening repeats its content in plainer words. Under option B the sentence must be rewritten, and the $6+2+4$ and $6+3+3$ rows become statements about two different vacua, which forces the $Z_R$'s eaten scalar to be booked to $\Phi$ and moves the surviving spectrum by one real component in $\Phi$ and one in $H$.

R3 — the data boundary counts a category that grew. The Data Boundary fixes the category at $125$ entries, two claims, sixteen data, one hundred and seven structural, and says the register is complete. The menu now lists $152$ links. The register is $27$ short. The repair is either a recount or a sentence that dates the count.

R4 — an ordering fault the move repairs. As written, The Heavy Sector and The Threshold Scales precede The One Coupling that both defer to, and the first of the two cites the failure of an article it has not reached. In the new order they follow it. No prose changes.

R5 — the two charge tables must agree. The Fermions After the Breaking §The Electric Charge and the Table and The Quantum Numbers Below the Breaking §The Table of Charges and Their Fates both print the sixteen's charges. With the two articles in two sections, a reader will compare them. One of the two must point at the other and say which column each owns.

R6 — one forward pointer in the scalar article. Its §The Eaten Count is the chain's budget and its §The Bidoublet and the $\rho$ Parameter is second-step physics. It sits in the first section, so it gains one sentence: the bidoublet's four conditions are the second section's, and the two neutral survivors are specified there.

What Not to Do

  • Do not write a new article for either breaking. Every statement the two sections need is already in the corpus, in the chain article, the order article, the scalar article and the fermions article.
  • Do not rename the section The Pati–Salam Chain, and do not let the two sections claim a unification their own coupling article reports as failing.
  • Do not say that the left–right group is an energy range in the minimal content. Say it is a factor of the stabiliser, in one sentence, in the first section's opening.
  • Do not write the first breaking as $\mathrm{SU}(4)\to$ the left–right model, in a title, a heading or a first sentence. Write $G_{4LR}\to\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$, $21\to15$, and name $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ as the factor broken.
  • Do not move the strong, electroweak or electromagnetic sections above the two breakings. They are the theory after it, and their articles say so: the two-sectors algebra article exists to hand them their algebra.
  • Do not cut an article in two at the section boundary. The fermions article and the mass-matrix article both straddle it, and a section may look forward.
  • Do not rewrite the $6+2+4$ and $6+3+3$ rows under option A. They are reconciled, and the reconciliation is quoted in this note.
  • Do not treat the audit as a breaking. Ten of the nineteen articles in the second section audit the Standard Model as a benchmark; calling them a breaking would make ten titles false.
  • Do not commit, and do not touch the deploy folder.

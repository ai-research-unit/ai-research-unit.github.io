Migration Note: The Two Symmetry Breakings

What this file is. A placement and writing plan. It says which sections move, which article goes where, which titles change, which sentences must be written first, and in what order the work is done. It contains no article text. Written 2026-11-24 in analysis mode; nothing in physics.md or articles_physics/ was edited to produce it.

What it plans. The category is re-read as two successive breakings. The first breaks $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ out of $G_{4LR}$ and leaves $\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$, the fifteen, which is the left–right model. The second breaks the weak pair and leaves the Standard Model. The block that audits the Standard Model moves from the end of the category to immediately after the first breaking, and the three sector sections follow both.

The locator moved to the end, and the menu reads better for it. Two headings now begin with From, and the eye scanning the category sees the two transitions before it sees the words symmetry breaking. The heading states the pair of theories first, which is the information a reader needs, and the label second, which is the information a reader already expected. One convention deviation, and it is deliberate. physics.md carries no instance of the spaced colon; its $333$ colons are all tight. maths.md carries the spaced form in its part headings and in at least one article title. Why the three sector headings drop in the Four Colours Left–Right Model. Three reasons, and the third is the one that decides it.

  1. The parent now says it. The category's first section is named after the model, and the three sectors sit inside the same category. Repeating the model's name on three consecutive headings states once what the reader already read at the top.
  2. Length. The three headings lose eighteen words and $111$ characters between them, $188$ to $77$. A menu section heading is a signpost; the longest of the three was $68$ characters before and is $31$ now.
  3. The suffix had become false where the sections sit. The Strong Interaction in the Four Colours Left–Right Model names a sector of the unbroken model. After the two breakings the group is $\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{\mathrm{em}}$, and $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ is a stabiliser at every step of the chain — it is the one factor that is never broken. So the strong interaction is not a sector of the four-colours model; it is what survives it. The same holds for electromagnetism, whose group is the residual $\mathrm{U}(1)_{\mathrm{em}}$ that no step breaks, and for the electroweak interaction, which is the pair $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{U}(1)_Y$ that the second breaking delivers. Placing those three sections after the two breakings and then labelling them with the pre-breaking model's name is the one combination the ordering rules out.

The three names now read as a chain. The category is one model followed by two steps out of it:

$$ \underbrace{\text{the Four Colours Left–Right Model}}_{\text{a theory}} \xrightarrow{\ \text{first breaking}\ } \underbrace{\text{the Left–Right Model}}_{\text{a theory}} \xrightarrow{\ \text{second breaking}\ } \underbrace{\text{the Standard Model}}_{\text{a theory}} . $$

Each arrow is named by the section that owns it, and each section title repeats the two names it joins. A reader who reads only the three headings has the whole category's argument.

The From is the corpus's own form. Nine article titles in the corpus are of the shape From X to Y — From the Ladder to the Schrödinger Equation, From the Spin Group to the Spin of a Single Rotor, and seven more. The form states a transition between two named objects and does not claim that the first contains the second. That is exactly the claim the sections need.

A prefix the new names create, and it is harmless. The first section's title begins with the title of the section above it, and the lead article's title begins with the section name:

$$ \underbrace{\text{The Four Colours Left–Right Model}}_{\text{section}}\ \subset\ \underbrace{\text{The Four Colours Left–Right Model: Four Colours, }21\text{ Generators, One Declaration}}_{\text{article}} . $$

The overlap is the point: the section holds the model, and the article prints it. The corpus already lives with the same overlap at The Standard Model as the Low-Energy Phase, which was both a section and an article, and §Titles below retires that ambiguity rather than repeating it.

Why $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ alone cannot title the first section. $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ has no left–right content to leave behind. Its breaking is

$$ \mathrm{SU}(4)\to\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}, \qquad \mathbf{15}\to\mathbf8\oplus\mathbf3\oplus\bar{\mathbf3}\oplus\mathbf1, \qquad 15-6=9, $$

nine generators left unbroken in the adjoint and six vectors eaten.

and the weak pair $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$ sits beside $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ in the product, untouched by it. A title reading "$\mathrm{SU}(4)$ to the Left–Right Model" would say that the left–right structure is produced by breaking $\mathrm{SU}(4)$, which is false: three colours and one lepton line come out of $\mathrm{SU}(4)$, and the two chiralities were never inside it. What the first step produces is $G_{4LR}$ minus its fourth colour, which is the left–right model; the sentence names the two groups as groups, so the title names the two theories.

The arithmetic of the two names, side by side. Both are right, and only one is right as a title about the pair:

$$ \underbrace{\mathrm{SU}(4)\to\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{U}(1)}_{15\to9+6,\ \text{one factor, not a theory}} \qquad\text{against}\qquad \underbrace{G_{4LR}\to\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_R}_{21\to15,\ \text{two theories}} . $$

The section's own table prints $21\to15\to12\to9$, and the first row of that table is the pair of theories, not the pair of factors.

Repairs That Come First

R1 — the diquark fork is attached to the wrong multiplet. The Leptoquarks §11.1 says of $\Sigma$ in the $(\mathbf{15},\mathbf2,\mathbf2)$: "The $\mathbf3$ and $\bar{\mathbf3}$ are colour triplets with lepton number, so they can couple to $qq$ and to $q\ell$ at once". The arithmetic of the same article forbids it. Under $\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{U}(1)_D$ with $B-L=D/3$:

$$ \mathbf{15}\to\mathbf8(0)\oplus\mathbf3(+4)\oplus\bar{\mathbf3}(-4)\oplus\mathbf1(0), \qquad \mathbf6\to\mathbf3(+2)\oplus\bar{\mathbf3}(-2). $$

A $qq$ coupling needs $B-L=\pm\tfrac23$, which is the sextet's triplet and not the fifteen's, whose triplet carries $\pm\tfrac43$. The tensor products agree: $4\otimes4=6\oplus10$ contains the sextet and not the fifteen, and $4\otimes\bar4=1\oplus15$ contains the fifteen and not the sextet. So $\Sigma$ couples to $\psi\bar\psi$ and never to $\psi\psi$, and the dimension-six proton-decay bound belongs to a $(\mathbf6,\mathbf1,\mathbf1)$, which the minimal content does not have. The fork is real; it is a fork about fields the model does not contain. The repair is one paragraph, and it strengthens the article's own conclusion.

Measured: $8+3+3+1=15$; $3+3=6$; the fifteen's triplet $D$-charge $+4$ against the sextet's $+2$. The obstruction is not the $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ product but the weak index: $\psi\psi$ needs a colour sextet or decuplet, because $4\otimes4=6\oplus10$, and $\psi\bar\psi$ needs $(\mathbf{15},\mathbf2,\mathbf2)$, because $15\otimes4\otimes\bar4$ carries the $15$ only with the $(\mathbf2,\mathbf2)$. A $(\mathbf{15},\mathbf1,\mathbf1)$ therefore has no Yukawa at all.

R2 — the double booking loses its licence. §The One Thing That Is Not Editorial above. Under option A the sentence in the chain article stands and each section opening repeats its content in plainer words. Under option B the sentence must be rewritten, and the $6+2+4$ and $6+3+3$ rows become statements about two different vacua, which forces the $Z_R$'s eaten scalar to be booked to $\Phi$ and moves the surviving spectrum by one real component in $\Phi$ and one in $H$.

R3 — the data boundary counts a category that grew. The Data Boundary fixes the category at $125$ entries, two claims, sixteen data, one hundred and seven structural, and says the register is complete. The menu now lists $152$ links. The register is $27$ short. The repair is either a recount or a sentence that dates the count.

R4 — an ordering fault the move repairs. As written, The Heavy Sector and The Threshold Scales precede The One Coupling that both defer to, and the first of the two cites the failure of an article it has not reached. In the new order they follow it. No prose changes.

R5 — the two charge tables must agree. The Fermions After the Breaking §The Electric Charge and the Table and The Quantum Numbers Below the Breaking §The Table of Charges and Their Fates both print the sixteen's charges. With the two articles in two sections, a reader will compare them. One of the two must point at the other and say which column each owns.

R6 — one forward pointer in the scalar article. Its §The Eaten Count is the chain's budget and its §The Bidoublet and the $\rho$ Parameter is second-step physics.   • Do not write a new article for either breaking. Every statement the two sections need is already in the corpus, in the chain article, the order article, the scalar article and the fermions article.
  • Do not rename the section The Pati–Salam Chain, and do not let the two sections claim a unification their own coupling article reports as failing.
  • Do not leave the heading as The Unified Group. It names a property of the algebra and not the theory, and it asserts a unification the coupling article reports as failing.
  • Do not say that the left–right group is an energy range in the minimal content. Say it is a factor of the stabiliser, in one sentence, in the first section's opening.
  • Do not write the first breaking as $\mathrm{SU}(4)\to$ the left–right model, in a title, a heading or a first sentence. Write $G_{4LR}\to\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$, $21\to15$, and name $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ as the factor broken.

  • Do not cut an article in two at the section boundary. The fermions article and the mass-matrix article both straddle it, and a section may look forward.
  • Do not rewrite the $6+2+4$ and $6+3+3$ rows under option A. They are reconciled, and the reconciliation is quoted in this note.
  • Do not treat the audit as a breaking. Ten of the nineteen articles in the second section audit the Standard Model as a benchmark; calling them a breaking would make ten titles false.
  • Do not shorten the three sector headings any further. Electromagnetism alone is right, and Electromagnetism in the Four Colours Left–Right Model is wrong where the section now sits.   • Do not commit, and do not touch the deploy folder.

Repairs Applied, and the Audit Behind Them

What was applied. A full read of the twenty-eight articles in the two breaking sections, plus the four audit articles the second section links to, and the repairs below. Seventeen files changed; the menu's link set is identical before and after (700 links), every internal link resolves, and no heading in the edited region is without its comment.

Physics corrections.

  • R1, applied. The Leptoquarks §11 is rewritten. The fifteen's antitriplet carries $B-L=-\tfrac43$, a $qq$ pair carries $+\tfrac23$, and $4\otimes4=6\oplus10$ has no fifteen in it; the diquark fork is a statement about a $(\mathbf6,\mathbf1,\mathbf1)$ the frame does not contain. §8.2's operator is corrected with it: without a diquark the final state is three leptons, so $\mathcal O_9\sim M_X^{-5}q^3\ell^3$ with $\Gamma\propto m_p^{11}/M_X^{10}$ — six powers on the rate, not $M_X^{-6}$/$M_X^{12}$.   • The branching that contradicted its own check table. The chain's §1.1 wrote $15\to8+1$; its check table wrote $15=8+3+3+1$. Both are now in the display, with the generator count and the matter branching distinguished.
  • The failure scales. The chain quoted $1.6\times10^{-3}$ J and $1.6\times10^{1}$ J where the coupling article gives $1.63\times10^{3}$ J and $1.57\times10^{7}$ J. Corrected; the four decades survive, the absolute values were off by $10^{6}$.
  • Line stabiliser against mass-matrix kernel. The chain and the order article both read the second step as $\mathrm{SU}(2)_R\to\mathrm{U}(1)_R$, $3\to1$. No generator annihilates the vacuum: $T_{3R}$ gives $+\tfrac12$ and $B-L$ gives $-1$, and only $Y=T_{3R}+\tfrac12(B-L)$ does. The step is $(3+1)\to1$ on generators, and both articles now say so and point at The Unbroken Subgroup for the reconciliation. Its §1.1 now says which stabiliser it means.

Corrected, and the article's own check table already had the right expression.
  • The $\rho$ bound, with its price. The Mass Matrix now reads the measurement as $\tan\beta=\kappa'/\kappa=-(1.9\pm1.0)\times10^{-4}$: four orders of tuning, of a definite sign.

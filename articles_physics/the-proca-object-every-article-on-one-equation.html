The Proca Object: Every Article on One Equation

Introduction

The Proca equation is the massive vector object, and it is the only one of the four whose action carries a constraint that removes a degree of freedom. Every article below either states that constraint or uses it. The spin-one fluids belong to this object and do not carry its name.

The count. 32 articles. The family contributes ten: four readings of the free action, four of the coupled one, and the two spin-one fluids. The set is smaller than the Dirac set because the vector does not split into two chiral halves, and it is larger than the scalar set in one place, the branch split between the timelike and spacelike words.

# __The Biquaternioid Symmetric Plain Algebra (SPA) over $\mathbb{C}$__

## Introduction

The biquaternioid complex space carries eleven structures beside the ordinary product. This article is about the **symmetric plain algebra**, written $\mathrm{SPA}$, whose product is the symmetrisation of the plain product, $\tilde P\star\tilde Q=\tfrac12(\tilde P\tilde Q+\tilde Q\tilde P)$. It is one of the four **symmetric** structures, and its job in the framework is **commutativity**: its product is commutative, its invariant form is the trace form, and the Hermitian elements with the same product form the Jordan algebra of the observables.

The mathematics of the structure is owned by the group *The Biquaternioid Symmetric Plain Algebra (SPA) over $\mathbb{C}$* of the mathematics menu. This article proves nothing. It lists the results, each with the mathematics article that owns it, and it keeps the physical readings the framework gives the structure, every one of them labelled as a reading.

**Conventions.** The algebra is $\mathbb{B}=\mathbb{C}\otimes_{\mathbb{R}}\mathbb{H}$ with basis $e_0,e_1,e_2,e_3$, $e_0=1$, $e_k^{2}=-e_0$, $e_1e_2=e_3$, and central imaginary $i$. An element is $\tilde Q=\sum_\mu Q_\mu e_\mu$ with $Q_\mu\in\mathbb{C}$. The conjugations are the natural one $\tilde Q^{\natural}$, the coefficientwise one $\bar{\tilde Q}$ and the star $\tilde Q^{*}=\bar{\tilde Q}^{\natural}$; the trace is $\mathrm{Tr}=2\,\mathrm{Sc}$; the sectors are $\mathbb{M}_+=\{Q^{*}=Q\}$ and $\mathbb{M}_-=\{Q^{\flat}=Q\}$ with $\flat=-{}^{*}$. All conventions are those of *Conventions in the Biquaternion Universe*.

## Physical Interpretation

- **The symmetrised product is what two material observations share when their order does not matter.** It is commutative and its cross term is dropped, so it carries no orientation; nothing distinguishes an attachment on the left from one on the right, and no transport can distinguish them either.
- **Its failure of the Jordan identity is the algebraic statement that the material ledger is not an algebra of observables.** The observables are the Hermitian elements with the same symmetrised operation, and on that sector the structure **is** the Jordan algebra of the framework: an observable is an element on which the Born pairing is defined, and the Jordan structure is what makes a state assignable while the positivity of a sesquilinear form is what makes it normalisable.
- **The two structures are separate, and their separation is the framework's version of the difference between a classical composition and a quantum state.** The symmetrised plain product is the negative half of a pair whose positive half is the state side; the classical composition attaches a number to a pair of operations, and the quantum state needs the positivity of the sesquilinear sibling.
- **The trace form is invariant and indefinite, and the adjoint of the band is the identity on the family of multiplications.** The pairing of two order-free compositions is itself order-free, which is the operator form of the classical composition: every multiplication is symmetric for the trace form and none is a generator, so the band supplies no infinitesimal operation of its own family. What is infinitesimal in the band is the **failure of two compositions to commute**, of dimension three.
- **The isometry of the band is the two-sided quadratic representation and not the one-sided multiplication.** An element of unit square acts as an isometry through its sandwich, and the sandwich and the inner automorphism coincide exactly at the symmetries; a multiplication preserves the form only at the two scalar signs.
- **The state space carries no phase of its own.** The operator of the band is the mean of the module action and a complementary module map, and the centre acts as a scalar, so the band is invariant under a central phase; and on a sharp question its operator has the three Peirce values and is not a projector, so the answer is graded and the classical two-valued projection is absent.

## Summary

The biquaternioid symmetric plain algebra is the structure $\tilde P\star\tilde Q=\tfrac12(\tilde P\tilde Q+\tilde Q\tilde P)$ over $\mathbb{C}$, the commutativity band of the framework: commutative, with a two-sided unit, with the indefinite trace form as its invariant form and with the Jordan identity holding on its Hermitian sector alone. That sector is the framework's Jordan algebra of observables; the failure of the identity on the material ledger is the statement that the material composition carries no observables. The mathematics is owned by the mathematics group of the same name; the readings listed above are the framework's, each labelled, and none of them is a proof.

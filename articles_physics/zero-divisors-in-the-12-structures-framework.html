Zero Divisors in the 12 Structures Framework

Introduction

The comparison section of the physics menu mirrors the comparison group of the mathematics menu: the twelve structures of the biquaternion complex space read against one subject at a time. The mathematics group opens with Zero Divisors, and this article is its physics companion. The subject is the set of elements that a product cannot invert, and the physical question is what that set means in each of the twelve structures.

A zero divisor of a product is a nonzero element $\tilde Q$ that annihilates some nonzero element: there is a nonzero $\tilde X$ with $\tilde Q\cdot\tilde X=0$ (a left zero divisor), or with $\tilde X\cdot\tilde Q=0$ (a right zero divisor). An element is a zero divisor exactly when it has no inverse in the product. Physically, a zero divisor is an operation that sends a nonzero object to zero: it is not invertible, nothing can undo it, and it marks a direction in which composition degenerates.

The mathematics companion proves one theorem about the twelve structures, and the theorem is the spine of this article. Among the twelve structures the zero-divisor set is the punctured cone $\mathcal{C}\setminus\{0\}$, the nonzero elements of vanishing biquaternion norm, exactly for the four general products $\mathrm{GPA}$, $\mathrm{GQA}$, $\mathrm{GPS}$ and $\mathrm{GQS}$; the two symmetric structures $\mathrm{SPA}$ and $\mathrm{SQS}$ have the enlarged set $(\{Q_0=0\}\cup\mathcal{C})\setminus\{0\}$; and the remaining six structures have the whole punctured space $\mathbb{B}\setminus\{0\}$. The cone is therefore contained in all twelve sets and equals only four of them, and the physical reading of the theorem is the subject of this article: the four general products degenerate exactly on the light cone, the two symmetric products of Jordan type degenerate on the lightlike family as well, and the six derived products are not compositional operations at all, because every nonzero element fails to be invertible in them.

The article reads the theorem structure by structure. It first fixes what a zero divisor is as an operation, then reads the cone in the algebra and in spacetime, then tabulates the twelve sets with their physical readings, then separates the common core from the enlarged and the total sets. The other five articles of the section take up the objects that the zero-divisor set organises: Annihilators in the 12 Structures Framework the annihilator planes, Square Roots of Minus One, Zero and Plus One in the 12 Structures Framework the roots of $0$, Square Roots of a General Element in the 12 Structures Framework the general radicand, Idempotents in the 12 Structures Framework the non-pure family, and Nilpotents in the 12 Structures Framework the square-zero class.

Conventions. We use those of the companion articles unchanged. The biquaternion algebra is $\mathbb{B}=\mathbb{C}\otimes_{\mathbb{R}}\mathbb{H}$, with basis $e_0=1,e_1,e_2,e_3$, $e_k^{2}=-e_0$ and $e_je_k=-\delta_{jk}e_0+\varepsilon_{jkl}e_l$, and $i$ the central scalar imaginary. The conjugations are the natural conjugation ${}^{\natural}$, the coefficient conjugation $\bar{\cdot}$ and the Hermitian conjugation ${}^{*}=\bar{\cdot}\circ{}^{\natural}$, with $\tilde Q^{\flat}=-\tilde Q^{*}$. The biquaternion norm is $N(\tilde Q)=\langle\tilde Q,\tilde Q\rangle_{\natural}=\tilde Q\tilde Q^{\natural}=\sum_\mu Q_\mu^{2}$, whose vanishing locus is the cone; the subspaces are the anti-Hermitian material sector $\mathbb{M}_-$ and the Hermitian informational sector $\mathbb{M}_+$; the material coordinate is $\tilde Q=ict\,e_0+\mathbf x$, so that $N(\tilde Q)=-c^{2}t^{2}+\mathbf x^{2}$, and $c=1/\sqrt{\epsilon\mu}$. An element is pure when its scalar part vanishes, $Q_0=0$, and non-pure when $Q_0\neq0$. The plain product is written $\tilde P\tilde Q$ and the plain sesquilinear product $\tilde P\star\tilde Q=\tilde P\tilde Q^{*}$; the trace is $\mathrm{Tr}=2\,\mathrm{Sc}$. The twelve structures are those of The 12 Products of the Biquaternion Complex Space and The Twelve Structures Compared: Product, Law and Form, and they are cited throughout by their three-letter names.

The Zero Divisor as an Operation That Cannot Be Inverted

The algebra. In a finite-dimensional associative algebra with a two-sided unit, an element is invertible if some element multiplies it to the unit on both sides, and a nonzero element that is not invertible is a zero divisor. The biquaternion algebra is finite dimensional and associative, and it has zero divisors because its norm form is isotropic: the norm vanishes on a complex cone of dimension three, and every nonzero element of that cone annihilates its own conjugate, $\tilde Q\tilde Q^{\natural}=N(\tilde Q)e_0=0$. The criterion is exact. A nonzero $\tilde Q$ is invertible if and only if $N(\tilde Q)\neq0$, and the inverse is then the two-sided plain inverse $\tilde Q^{-1}=\tilde Q^{\natural}/N(\tilde Q)$; on the cone the inverse does not exist. This is the plain-row criterion of Biquaternion Norm and Invertibility, and it is the one criterion of the four general products: each of them is the plain product read in a different slot, so each has the same invertibility criterion and the same zero-divisor set.

The operation. Read the product as the composition of two operations, as The Monoid of Acting Maps: the Process Is the Multiplication, the State Is the Idempotent does: an element is the map "multiply by it", $L_{\tilde Q}:\tilde R\mapsto\tilde Q\tilde R$. A unit is an invertible map, a zero divisor is a non-invertible one, and the cone is the set of elements whose acting map drops rank. A nonzero element of the cone kills a nonzero argument, so it destroys information and no operation can restore it. Off the cone, every acting map is invertible and the reversible processes are the units. The light cone is thus not primarily a surface in spacetime; it is the set of processes that cannot be undone, and it becomes the light cone of spacetime when the material coordinate is used.

The state. The second family of the cone is the set of nonzero multiples of idempotents, and a rank-one projector is the extreme case. A projector satisfies $\tilde\Pi^{2}=\tilde\Pi$, so it is a nonzero element of the cone: $N(\tilde\Pi)=0$ and $\tilde\Pi$ has no inverse, as The Biquaternion Vacuum as a Minimal Idempotent states of the vacuum. This is not a defect of a state; it is the algebra's statement that asking for the inverse of a measurement is a category mistake. The zero-divisor set is therefore the home of two different physical objects at once, the lightlike propagation and the state, and the two are separated by the scalar part, as the next section shows.

The Cone in the Algebra and in Spacetime

The vanishing set and its two families

The cone is the vanishing set of the biquaternion norm,

$$ \mathcal{C}=\{\tilde Q\neq0:\ N(\tilde Q)=\tilde Q\tilde Q^{\natural}=\sum_\mu Q_\mu^{2}=0\}, $$

a complex cone of complex dimension three with the apex removed, of real dimension six. On the material sector it is the light cone $|\mathbf x|=c|t|$. The scalar part cuts it into two families, and the physics of the two is different.

  • The pure family, $Q_0=0$ and $(\mathbf Q,\mathbf Q)=0$. The square of a pure zero divisor vanishes, $\tilde Q^{2}=-(Q_1^{2}+Q_2^{2}+Q_3^{2})e_0=0$: these are the nilpotents, the lightlike displacements. A pure lightlike displacement is the translation part of a null direction, and its square being zero is the algebra's form of the statement that two successive displacements along a null direction add and nothing more. The family is of real dimension four, and the physical reading of the nilpotent square belongs to Nilpotents in the 12 Structures Framework and to Square Roots of Minus One, Zero and Plus One in the 12 Structures Framework.
  • The non-pure family, $Q_0\neq0$. Its square is $\tilde Q^{2}=2Q_0\tilde Q-N(\tilde Q)e_0=2Q_0\tilde Q$, so the rescaled element $\tilde\Pi=\tilde Q/(2Q_0)$ is a nontrivial idempotent. These are the idempotent multiples, the multiples of the projectors. The non-pure family fills the cone in real dimension six. Physically it is the state family: the rank-one projectors of the informational sector are its boundary, the pure states of Decoherence as Idempotent Projection and The Biquaternion Vacuum as a Minimal Idempotent, and the family is read in Idempotents in the 12 Structures Framework.

The two families answer the natural objection to reading a state as a zero divisor: the state is on the cone, but it is not nilpotent, and its square is the projector it is a multiple of and not zero. The vanishing of the norm is a statement about invertibility, not about the square.

The cone in the four general products

The four general structures are the plain product $\tilde P\tilde Q$ (GPA, the composition band), the quaternionic product $\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q$ (GQA), the plain sesquilinear product $\tilde P\tilde Q^{*}$ (GPS, the probability band) and the quaternionic sesquilinear product $\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q^{*}$ (GQS, the gauge band). Each is the plain product in one slot and one conjugation, so each has the plain norm as its criterion and the cone as its zero-divisor set. The physics of the cone differs from band to band, and the differences are the subject of Composition and Probability: the General Plain Algebra and the General Plain Sesqualgebra of Biquaternions Compared and The Cone and the Massless Particles in the 12 Structures Framework: in the composition the cone is the boundary of invertibility and the locus of lightlike propagation; in the probability it is the rank drop of the sesquilinear square, rank two massive and rank one massless, read in Mass, Rank and the Positivity of the Dagger; in the gauge band the same cone is the locus where the fourth product's metric degenerates, read in The States the Indefinite Metric Cannot Normalise.

What the four share is the set itself. The identity $N(\tilde P\tilde Q)=N(\tilde P)N(\tilde Q)$ makes the norm a multiplicative label, and the cone is the set on which the label vanishes; no slot and no conjugation changes it. The physical statement is that masslessness is a property of the interval alone, and the four products are four things to do with a massless element, not four different notions of masslessness.

The cone in the division subalgebras

The cone is not spread over the whole algebra. The centre $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}=\mathrm{span}_{\mathbb{R}}\{e_0,ie_0\}$ and the real-quaternion subspace $\mathbb{H}_{\mathbb{B}}$ are division algebras: on $\mathbb{H}_{\mathbb{B}}$ the norm is a sum of four squares of real numbers, which vanishes only at the origin, so neither subspace contains a zero divisor. The zero divisors require the complexification, and they live in the two Hermitian-type sectors: the $\mathbb{M}_-$ cone is the physical light cone, and the $\mathbb{M}_+$ cone is the boundary of the future cone that carries the states. The division subalgebras are read in The Anti-Hermitian Subspace $\mathbb{M}_-$ as the Material Sector and The Hermitian Subspace $\mathbb{M}_+$ as the Informational Sector.

Three groups appear in the table, and each group has one physical statement.

  • The four general products. The set is the cone. These are the structures that compose and pair; each composes off the cone and fails on it, so their physics is the physics of the light cone, read in the four bands by the four articles cited in the previous section.
  • The two symmetric structures $\mathrm{SPA}$ and $\mathrm{SQS}$. The set is the enlarged set: the pure family $\{Q_0=0\}$ and the cone. The physics of the enlargement is that the symmetrised coupling of two pure operations vanishes whenever their vector parts are orthogonal. The cleanest witness is the unit itself: $\tilde P=e_1$ has norm $1$ and is invertible in the algebra, yet for the Jordan product $\tilde P\bullet\tilde Q=\tfrac12(\tilde P\tilde Q+\tilde Q\tilde P)$ one has $e_1\bullet e_2=\tfrac12(e_1e_2+e_2e_1)=0$. The same holds for the symmetric quaternionic sesquilinear product, $e_1\star e_2=0$. A unit that cannot be composed symmetrically with another unit is the physical content of the enlargement: the derived symmetric operations are couplings, and a coupling of two lightlike directions can vanish even when both directions are non-degenerate.
  • The six derived structures $\mathrm{SQA}$, $\mathrm{SPS}$, $\mathrm{APA}$, $\mathrm{AQA}$, $\mathrm{APS}$ and $\mathrm{AQS}$. The set is the whole punctured space: every nonzero element has an annihilator, so the product has no invertible operation at all. The reason splits in two. The two scalar-valued structures $\mathrm{SQA}$ and $\mathrm{SPS}$ return a central number, $\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q)e_0$ and $\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P\tilde Q^{*})e_0$, and the equation "the value is zero" is one linear equation in the components of the second factor, whose solutions fill a complex hyperplane. The four antisymmetric structures are alternating or conjugate-alternating, and every nonzero element is annihilated by a partner built from itself. A product whose value is a number, or whose value is an antisymmetrisation, is not an operation one can iterate; the whole-space zero-divisor set is the algebraic form of that statement.

The Cone as the Common Core of the Twelve

The mathematics companion states the sharpest consequence of the table: the cone is contained in all twelve zero-divisor sets, and the four general products are exactly the four for which the containment is an equality. Two physical statements follow.

The first is a statement of independence. Every lightlike element is annihilated in every one of the twelve structures. Masslessness, read as the vanishing of the interval, is therefore not an artefact of the product one chooses; it survives all twelve readings, and the cone is the largest set on which the twelve agree. The second is a statement of strictness. Off the cone, the derived structures still have zero divisors, and the extra elements are exactly the ones the derived operations cannot handle. The failure of the symmetrised operations on the unit $e_1$ is the simplest case, and the failure of the scalar-valued and antisymmetric operations on every element is the extreme case. The cone is thus the boundary of invertibility for the four compositional structures, and for the eight derived structures it is only the intersection of their much larger degeneracy.

The distinction matches an earlier split of the corpus. The four general products carry the units, the inverses and the operator laws; the eight derived products are the symmetrisations and antisymmetrisations used as couplings, values and brackets. A coupling that returns a number is the physical content of the scalar-valued structures, and a bracket that changes sign under exchange is the content of the antisymmetric ones. Neither is a rule for composing a process with a process, and the zero-divisor set says so.

The Cone as an Information Barrier and a Mass Label

The cone is one set shared by the four general products, but the corpus reads it in four different roles, and the zero-divisor article is the natural place where the four readings meet.

The information barrier. Causality and the Light Cone as an Information Barrier in Biquaternionic Form reads the material cone as the boundary of causal influence: the cone is the edge of the region a signal can reach, and the pairs of events outside it can neither signal nor be signalled. The zero-divisor condition is the algebraic face of the same barrier. A zero divisor sends a nonzero input to zero, so it is a process that cannot be reversed and cannot return a message; the cone is the locus where an elementary process loses its invertibility, and the loss of invertibility is what makes the cone a horizon rather than an ordinary surface. "A signal propagates only within the cone" and "composition degenerates only on the cone" are the geometric and the algebraic statements of one object.

The erasure budget. The loss of invertibility of a map is a loss of information, and the corpus prices it. Landauer's Principle and the Material–Informational Exchange in Biquaternionic Form fixes the exchange rate between the informational and the material ledgers: the erasure of one bit costs at least $k_BT\log2$ of dissipated heat. The zero divisor is the algebra's elementary erasure — an operation with no inverse, whose kernel is what it destroys — and the dimension of the kernel is the amount of information the operation annihilates. The units are the processes that erase nothing, the zero divisors the processes that erase something, and a rank-dropping zero divisor is the elementary irreversible step whose thermodynamic price the Landauer article owns.

The rank and the mass label. In the probability band the cone is where the sesquilinear square drops rank, from two to one, and the rank is read as a mass label in Mass, Rank and the Positivity of the Dagger. The Four Readings of Mass: Norm, Rank, Charge and Scale separates the senses of the word mass, and the cone owns one of them cleanly and another only as a value. The cone is exactly the rank-one locus, so "on the cone" and "one polarisation" are the same statement; the norm reads the same set as the level set $N=0$, and a level set is a value and not a count. The cone is therefore not the mass itself. It is the boundary at which the count of polarisations falls, and the shells $N=-m^2c^2$ around it are what the norm labels.

The state family and its irreversibility. The non-pure family of the cone is the family of multiples of the positive projectors, and Decoherence as Idempotent Projection moves a state from that family into the interior of the Bloch ball: a pure state is an idempotent and hence a zero divisor, a mixed state is not, and decoherence is the passage off the cone. Quantum Channels and the Reversible/Irreversible Dichotomy supplies the operator reading of the same passage: a reversible channel is conjugation by a single unit, and every irreversible channel is a sum of conjugations, so reversibility is unitarity and irreversibility is the failure of the elementary operation to be a unit. The zero-divisor cone is thus the boundary between the reversible and the irreversible halves of the state space.

The complement is the invertible region. The theorem of Biquaternion Norm and Invertibility is an equivalence: $\tilde Q$ is invertible if and only if $N(\tilde Q)\neq0$. On the material sector this partitions the four-vectors exactly as physics does. The null four-vectors are the zero divisors, and the timelike and spacelike ones are units. The partition has a consequence the word "zero divisor" invites one to miss: a spacelike four-vector — a superluminal or tachyonic displacement, with $N>0$ — is invertible, so it is not a zero divisor at all. The corpus's superluminal solutions of the free Maxwell equation are read in Subluminal and Superluminal Electromagnetic Waves and the Lepton Mass Spectrum with the caution that the superluminality is a pattern speed of a localised packet and not a signalling speed; the algebra adds its own statement, that the spacelike region is the invertible one and the failure of invertibility sits on the cone alone. The zero-divisor set is therefore the null cone and nothing else, and the physical claim "this process cannot be undone" means "this process is lightlike" and not "this process is fast".

The horizon, and why the cone is not it. A black-hole event horizon is a null surface: it is generated by a Killing field that is null on it, and Black Hole Thermodynamics in Biquaternionic Form reads its temperature, entropy and first law in the material sector while importing the metric and the horizon geometry. The temptation is to call the horizon "a piece of the zero-divisor cone", and it should be resisted, because the two objects are of different kinds. The zero-divisor cone is a cone in the tangent space: at each point it is the set of null directions, a fibrewise condition on a single vector, and it says nothing about which points of spacetime admit such directions. A horizon is a hypersurface in spacetime, a set of points, selected by a particular solution of the field equations. What the two share is the algebra's notion of nullity — a direction is null exactly when its norm vanishes — and the horizon is where the generating Killing vector is null in that sense. The cone supplies the criterion; the metric supplies the surface. The two roles must be kept apart: the cone is a property of the algebra and holds everywhere, and a horizon is a property of one solution and holds on one surface.

The destroyed description and the hidden one. Confinement and the Loss of Partonic Information in Biquaternionic Form gives the sharpest information reading of a non-invertible process. Its subject is not a zero divisor of a product but a loss of labels: the colour of an asymptotic quark is not held in a kernel that a gauge choice could recover, it is absent from the observable algebra. The contrast with Gauge Redundancy and the Information in the Gauge Orbit in Biquaternionic Form is the point the zero-divisor article should carry: a non-invertible operation can fail to be invertible for two different reasons, because the information is hidden in a redundancy or because it is destroyed in the operation, and the algebraic face of "destroyed" is a kernel the operation cannot return from. The Landauer principle prices that destruction, and confinement is its gauge-theory instance.

Physical Interpretation

  • A zero divisor is an operation that annihilates a nonzero input. It cannot be inverted, and the cone is the set of such operations.
  • For the four general products the zero-divisor set is the light cone. Composition and pairing degenerate exactly where the interval vanishes, which is the algebraic form of lightlike propagation and of the rank-one massless boundary of the state cone.
  • The cone splits by the scalar part. The pure family is the nilpotent lightlike displacements; the non-pure family is the multiples of the projectors, that is, the states. A state is a zero divisor because a measurement cannot be undone, not because its square vanishes.
  • For the two symmetric structures $\mathrm{SPA}$ and $\mathrm{SQS}$ the degeneracy is larger: the symmetrised coupling also annihilates on the lightlike family, and an invertible unit can fail to compose symmetrically.
  • For the six derived structures the degeneracy is total: a scalar-valued coupling or an antisymmetrised bracket is not an operation to be iterated, and no nonzero element of it is invertible.
  • The cone is the common core of the twelve sets, so masslessness does not depend on the product. The products differ in what they can do with a non-lightlike element, not in where they degenerate.
  • The cone is the information barrier of Causality and the Light Cone as an Information Barrier in Biquaternionic Form, the elementary erasure whose price Landauer's Principle and the Material– Informational Exchange in Biquaternionic Form fixes, and the rank-one boundary at which the mass label of Mass, Rank and the Positivity of the Dagger falls from two to one.
  • The complement of the cone is the invertible region. The null four-vectors are the zero divisors; the timelike and the spacelike ones are units, so a superluminal displacement is invertible and the failure of invertibility sits on the cone alone.
  • A non-invertible operation can hide information or destroy it. A gauge kernel hides a description that a gauge choice can recover; the confinement kernel of an asymptotic colour label has destroyed a description the observable algebra no longer contains, and Landauer prices the destruction.
  • A black-hole event horizon is a null surface, so it shares the algebra's notion of nullity with the cone. It is not a piece of the cone: the cone is a fibrewise set of null directions, the horizon a hypersurface selected by one solution and its metric. The cone supplies the criterion, the metric supplies the surface.

Summary

The zero-divisor article of the twelve structures is the physics of the set of operations a product cannot invert. The mathematics companion proves that the set is the punctured cone exactly for the four general products $\mathrm{GPA}$, $\mathrm{GQA}$, $\mathrm{GPS}$ and $\mathrm{GQS}$, that it is the enlarged set $(\{Q_0=0\}\cup\mathcal{C})\setminus\{0\}$ for the two symmetric structures $\mathrm{SPA}$ and $\mathrm{SQS}$, and that it is the whole punctured space for the remaining six. Read physically, the four general products degenerate exactly on the light cone, so their physics is the physics of the cone: the boundary of invertibility in the composition, the rank drop of the sesquilinear square in the probability, and the null set of the indefinite metric in the gauge band. The cone splits by the scalar part into the pure nilpotent displacements and the non-pure multiples of the projectors, the two objects the later articles of the section own. The two symmetric structures add the pure family to the cone, because a symmetrised coupling can annihilate two invertible lightlike directions, and the unit $e_1$ is the witness. The six derived structures have the whole punctured space, because a scalar-valued coupling returns a number and an antisymmetrised bracket is not a composition. Finally the cone is contained in all twelve sets and equals four, so masslessness is independent of the product while the ability to compose a non-lightlike element is not.

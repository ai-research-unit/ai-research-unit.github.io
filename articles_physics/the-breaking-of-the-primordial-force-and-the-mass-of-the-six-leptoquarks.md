# __The Restructuring of the Primordial Force: $\mathrm{SU}(4)\to\mathrm{SU}(3)_c\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}$ and the Mass of the Six Leptoquarks__

## Introduction

**The layer.** The vacuum restructuring at $M_C$ has two operations, and this article states the first: the breaking. Its Layer-A content is one diagonal element and its adjoint action. Nothing here needs a potential, a temperature, or a cosmological history: **the breaking of the primordial force is the adjoint action of one direction on fifteen arrays, and everything else is arithmetic on its spectrum.**

**The question.** What exactly does the condensation do to $\mathrm{SU}(4)$, which generators does it remove from the spectrum, and what do those generators weigh once they are gone?

**The answer.**

$$
\boxed{\;
iD=\operatorname{diag}(-3i,i,i,i),\qquad
\operatorname{ad}(iD):\quad 0^{9}\oplus(-4i)^{3}\oplus(+4i)^{3},\qquad
15=9+3+3 ;
\;}
$$

$$
\boxed{\;
\ker\operatorname{ad}(iD)=\mathfrak{u}(3)=\mathfrak{su}(3)_c\oplus\mathfrak{u}(1)_{B-L},\qquad
\text{the sextet is exactly the }\pm4i\text{ eigenspace} ;
\;}
$$

$$
\boxed{\;
M^{2}=2v^{2}\bigl[D,[D,\cdot\,]\bigr]=\operatorname{diag}\bigl(\underbrace{32v^{2},\dots,32v^{2}}_{6},\underbrace{0,\dots,0}_{9}\bigr).
\;}
$$

**So the breaking is one line of spectral theory.** A single diagonal direction, with eigenvalue pattern $(-3,1,1,1)$, commutes with nine arrays and moves six; the six it moves are the six that acquire mass, and the nine it fixes are the nine that stay massless.

**What this article establishes.** That the stabiliser of the lepton line is exactly the nine-dimensional $\mathfrak{u}(3)$; that the six generators it breaks are exactly the leptoquark sextet, with $Q=\pm\tfrac23$ and $B-L=\pm\tfrac43$; that their mass is one number per plane, $32v^{2}$; and that the rank does not move, $3\to2+1$, so **the breaking costs dimension and keeps the rank.**

**Not established.** Nothing here says *why* the condensate forms. The scalar potential is not derived anywhere in the corpus, and this article does not pretend to derive it. **What is established is what follows once the condensate exists** — and that is a great deal, because the entire mass spectrum of the first breaking follows from one direction.

**What "breaking" means here, stated before it is used.** Two things happen to the six generators at once, and they are the same event seen twice: **they are eaten** (each becomes the longitudinal mode of a now-massive vector) and **they become heavy** ($32v^{2}$ each). Neither is a destruction. **The six are absent from the spectrum below the restructuring and present in it above; that is what a condensate does to a redundant direction.**

**And the mass they acquire is the mass they did not have, stated as plainly as the corpus can put it.** Above $M_C$ the six are **massless** — exactly zero, not small — because the operator whose spectrum is the mass matrix, $M^{2}=2v^{2}[D,[D,\cdot]]$, has nothing to act on until $D$ is the condensate's direction, and because the local symmetry is unbroken there and forbids a mass to every order. **Nothing in $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ makes the sextet heavy.** The number $32v^{2}$ in the box above is produced by the vacuum at $M_C$ and does not exist before it. **So "the leptoquarks are heavy" is a statement about the broken phase, and the unbroken phase has fifteen massless vectors, all of them.**

**Where the rest is.** The mass matrix is derived in §*The Mass the Six Acquire*, together with the basis question. The six states are [Six Leptoquarks Above $M_C$](six-leptoquarks-above-m-c-quantum-numbers-mass-and-the-bound.html), which owns their mass relation and their experimental bound. The scalar that condenses is the line in the centre, fixed in §*The Condensate*. **What the six cannot do — become the weak bosons — is [The Charge Theorem](the-charge-theorem-the-sextet-cannot-be-recomposed-into-the-weak-pair.html)**, and it is the reason the next article is about an index rather than about debris.

## The Condensate: One Direction, Chosen From the Carrier

**What condenses.** A scalar field acquires a value. In this frame a scalar is not an element of the algebra and not an element of the state module: it is a line in the centre, which is why it can multiply the carrier from either side without a choice. **The value it acquires is a vector, and the direction of that vector is the whole content of the breaking.**

**The direction is the lepton line, and it is not a free choice.** The model's one load-bearing declaration is the canonical split

$$
\mathbf 4=\mathbf 1\oplus\mathbf 3=\mathbb C e_t\oplus\operatorname{span}\{e_x,e_y,e_z\},
$$

**with $e_t$ the lepton and the other three the spatial lines.** The scalar carries one $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ index, so its value is a vector in the carrier, and the declaration says which of the four directions is available: **the one that is not a spatial line.**

**In components.** Write the vacuum as $D=\operatorname{diag}(\ell,q,q,q)$ in the basis $(e_t,e_x,e_y,e_z)$. The algebra is $\mathfrak{su}(4)$, so the trace vanishes:

$$
\ell+3q=0.
$$

**One equation, four unknowns, and the solution is a line.** Set $q=1$; then $\ell=-3$, and

$$
D=\operatorname{diag}(-3,1,1,1),\qquad iD=\operatorname{diag}(-3i,i,i,i).
$$

**The vacuum is a recombination of the three diagonal generators, and this is the one place where that fact has content.** Above $M_C$ the three diagonals are declared symmetric in the three vector units,
$$
T_x=i\bigl(E_{xx}-E_{tt}\bigr),\qquad T_y=i\bigl(E_{yy}-E_{tt}\bigr),\qquad T_z=i\bigl(E_{zz}-E_{tt}\bigr),
$$
and **none of them is the vacuum**. The vacuum is their sum,
$$
iD=T_x+T_y+T_z=\operatorname{diag}(-3i,i,i,i),
$$
so the direction that the condensation selects is not a member of the frame that the unbroken phase prints: **it is what the frame adds up to, and the coefficient of each of the three is one.** This is why the lepton line can appear at the restructuring without having been named above it, and why the corpus no longer needs a corner array to hold $B'$: $B'$ is the sum. [The Symmetric Diagonal Frame](the-symmetric-diagonal-frame-of-the-fifteen.html) proves that no such frame exists in $\mathfrak{su}(3)$, and that the fourth line is what makes it possible here.

**The scale is free and the direction is not.** Any multiple of $D$ gives the same breaking. **So the condensate contributes one dimensionful number — its magnitude $v$ — and one discrete choice, the sign, which changes nothing because every mass is quadratic in $D$.** That is the entire freedom of the first step.

**Why the number $4$ will appear later.** The trace condition is not decoration: it is what turns "the scalar takes a value" into "the scalar takes the only value available". With $q$ fixed the corner is forced. **The $4$ in $32=2\cdot4^{2}$ is exactly $q-\ell=1-(-3)$, the one departure from a multiple of the identity that a trace-free diagonal direction can have.** Change the split of the quartet and the pattern of six and nine changes with it; keep the split and there is nothing left to choose.

**The stabiliser.** The set of arrays that annihilate the vacuum is

$$
\mathfrak{u}(3)=\{X\in\mathfrak{su}(4)\ :\ [iD,X]=0\},
$$

**the block-diagonal matrices acting on the three adjoint directions, traceless.** Its dimension is $1+9-1=9$: one corner entry, a $3\times3$ block, and the trace condition removing one. **That nine is the whole of the surviving sector, and it is computed, not assumed.**

## The Adjoint Action, Which Is the Whole Theorem

**The object.** For an array $X$ in the fifteen,

$$
\operatorname{ad}(iD)(X)=[iD,X].
$$

**The spectrum.** On the plane arrays it is immediate, and the derivation takes three steps.

**The spatial planes commute.** If $j,k\in\{x,y,z\}$ then $[iD,A_{jk}]=0$ and $[iD,iS_{jk}]=0$, because $D$ is a multiple of the identity on that block. **That is six directions with eigenvalue zero, before any work.**

**The corner directions do not commute.** For $a\in\{x,y,z\}$,

$$
[iD,A_{ta}]=(-3i-i)A_{ta}=-4i\,A_{ta},
\qquad
[iD,iS_{ta}]=-4i\,iS_{ta},
$$

**and with the indices in the other order the sign flips,**

$$
[iD,A_{at}]=+4i\,A_{at},\qquad [iD,iS_{at}]=+4i\,iS_{at}.
$$

**The three diagonal directions are in the kernel, and the frame makes that visible rather than accidental.** $D$ is diagonal, so it commutes with every diagonal matrix, hence $[iD,T_j]=0$ for $j=x,y,z$ and $[iD,iD]=0$. **The three symmetric directions survive the breaking individually**, and what the broken phase calls $T^{c}_{3}$ and $T^{c}_{8}$ are the two differences inside that surviving triple while $B'$ is the surviving sum.

**Adding it up.**

| block | directions | eigenvalue of $\operatorname{ad}(iD)$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| spatial planes $A_{jk},iS_{jk}$, $j,k\in\{x,y,z\}$ | $6$ | $0$ |
| the three frame directions $T_j=iM_j$ | $3$ | $0$ |
| $A_{ta},iS_{ta}$ | $6$ | $-4i$ on three, $+4i$ on three |
| **total** | $15$ | $0^{9}\oplus(-4i)^{3}\oplus(+4i)^{3}$ |

**Two readings of one table, and both are used.** The real reading says: **nine directions are fixed and six are moved, and the six are moved by an operator with eigenvalues $\pm4$**, so they pair into three rotation planes. The complex reading says: **the six form two complex three-dimensional eigenspaces, conjugate to each other**, so the sextet is a $\mathbf3$ and a $\bar{\mathbf3}$. **The real pairing and the complex splitting are the same statement, which is why the leptoquarks come in three charge-conjugate pairs.**

**The multiplicities, checked.** $9+3+3=15$; $\operatorname{tr}\operatorname{ad}(iD)=0$ because the spectrum is symmetric; the kernel is nine-dimensional and the image six-dimensional. **No number in the table is a choice.**

## The Six That Are Eaten, Named

**Which six.** The $(-4i)$ and $(+4i)$ eigenspaces are spanned by the planes that touch the lepton line and one spatial line:

$$
\mathfrak m=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{A_{ta},iS_{ta}\ :\ a=x,y,z\},\qquad \dim_{\mathbb C}\mathfrak m=6 .
$$

**Under the unbroken $\mathrm{SU}(3)_c$ they split as a triplet and an antitriplet**, because they carry one index:

$$
\mathfrak m=\mathbf 3_{+4/3}\oplus\bar{\mathbf 3}_{-4/3},
\qquad
B-L=\pm\tfrac43,\qquad Q=\tfrac12(B-L)=\pm\tfrac23 .
$$

**The names.** A triplet member with $Q=+\tfrac23$ or $-\tfrac23$ is a leptoquark, written $X$; its conjugate is written $\bar X$. **Six states, three of charge $+\tfrac23$ and three of charge $-\tfrac23$**, each carrying a index.

**Why they are eaten, counted in real components.** Breaking fifteen generators to nine removes six, and the Goldstone theorem gives one real scalar degree of freedom per broken generator:

$$
15-9=6\ \text{broken}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 6\ \text{real components eaten}.
$$

**The scalar supplies them and has room to spare.** The scalar of this model is the Hermitian $4\times4$, sixteen real components — fifteen from the algebra and one from the trace, $15+1=16$ — and the representation is forced rather than chosen: **$\bar\xiΞ\xi$ is a singlet only for $Ξ\in\bar{\mathbf4}\otimes\mathbf4=\mathbf{15}\oplus\mathbf1$**, since $\mathbf4\otimes\mathbf4=\mathbf6\oplus\mathbf{10}$ contains no $\bar{\mathbf4}$ and a scalar in the fundamental would form no mass operator at all. The vacuum moves exactly six of the sixteen, so **the ledger at this scale is $16=6+10$**, and the six are the ones this article names.

**The ten are not this article's business, but they are not a loose end either.** One is the radial mode of the condensate, at $2\lambda v^{2}$; the other nine are the components orthogonal to the vacuum, which are the ones the weak sector is built from (*Classification of the Bosons*). **The count of six for the $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ step does not depend on any of that**, and it is the only count this article uses.

**The eaten six are not destroyed, and the distinction matters for everything that follows.** A Goldstone mode is a rotation of the condensate, and the condensate can be rotated back. **What is absent below the restructuring is the six as *asymptotic* states; what is present is the six as longitudinal modes of the six massive vectors.** An article that says "the leptoquarks are destroyed" has thrown away the only ingredient the later articles need.

## The Mass the Six Acquire

**The mass matrix.** From the connection kinetic term evaluated on the vacuum,

$$
\bigl(M^{2}\bigr)_{XY}=2v^{2}\operatorname{tr}\bigl([iD,X]^{\dagger}[iD,Y]\bigr)\Big/\operatorname{tr}(X^{\dagger}Y),
$$

**which is diagonal in the plane basis because $\operatorname{ad}(iD)$ is.** Inserting the spectrum:

$$
M^{2}=\operatorname{diag}\bigl(\underbrace{32v^{2},\dots,32v^{2}}_{6},\underbrace{0,\dots,0}_{9}\bigr).
$$

**Why $32$, in one line.** $32=2\times4^{2}$, and $4=1-(-3)$ is the difference between a diagonal entry and the lepton eigenvalue. **A plane's mass is twice the square of the eigenvalue difference across it: a plane touching the lepton line sees the difference, a plane inside the three lines does not, and a direction along a line cannot see one at all.**

**The table, one row per kind of plane.**

| plane | indices | $m^{2}$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $A_{ta},iS_{ta}$ | lepton and spatial | $32v^{2}$ |
| $A_{jk},iS_{jk}$ | spatial and spatial | $0$ |
| the three trace directions | diagonal | $0$ |

**Six planes heavy, nine directions massless, and no cross terms.** The matrix is already diagonal in the basis the algebra hands over, which is why no diagonalisation step appears — **and why the number $32$ can be read off a spectrum rather than solved for.**

**The physical normalisation, so that the number can be compared with an experiment.** With $g_4$ the primordial-force coupling,

$$
M_X^{2}=16\,g_4^{2}\,v_C^{2},
$$

**sixteen, one coupling squared, one vacuum squared, and no free number.** The $16$ is $(\Delta d)^{2}=(1-(-3))^{2}$ with the vacuum written as $Ξ_0=v_CD$ and $\operatorname{tr}(t_{a}t_{b})=\tfrac12\delta_{ab}$, and the factor $2$ that turns the Lagrangian's coefficient into a mass is the $\tfrac12$ of $\tfrac12M^{2}A^{2}$. **The $32v^{2}$ above is the array-level reading of the same mass**, and the two are one statement once the array's vacuum is written in the physical one:

$$
v_{C}=\frac{\sqrt2\,v}{g_4},\qquad M_X^{2}=16g_4^{2}v_C^{2}=32v^{2} .
$$

**The $\sqrt2$ is arithmetic and not a fit.** $16=4^{2}=(1-(-3))^{2}$ is the eigenvalue difference squared, one power of the commutator on each side of the trace; the $2$ that turns $16$ into $32$ is the $\tfrac12$ of $\tfrac12M^{2}A^{2}$. **So $16g_4^{2}v_C^{2}=32v^{2}$ forces $v_C=\sqrt2\,v/g_4$**, and every article that quotes the sextet's mass uses this conversion. None of them restates it.

**What is *not* claimed.** $32v^{2}$ is not a mass in kilograms. **All couplings here are symbolic**, and the conversion of $v_C$ into a number is an experimental question, treated in the article on the sextet and in the coupling article.

## The General Vacuum, and Why the Kernel Is an Algebra

**One number per plane, at any vacuum.** The corner is one point; the identity behind it is not.

**The general diagonal vacuum.** Let $Ξ_0=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,d_2,d_3,d_4)$ with $\sum_j d_j=0$. The same computation gives

$$
M^{2}=2\,(d_j-d_k)^{2}\,v^{2}\ \text{on the plane }\{j,k\},\qquad
0\ \text{on the three Cartan directions},
$$

so a squared mass is twice the square of a root of the vacuum and a massless generator is one the vacuum does not see.

**The operator form, and why there are two commutators.** With $\mathcal E(X)=[D,[D,X]]$,

$$
M^{2}_{XY}=v^{2}\operatorname{tr}\bigl(X^{\dagger}\,\mathcal E(Y)\bigr),
$$

and in the basis with $\operatorname{tr}(X_a^{\dagger}X_b)=2\delta_{ab}$ the matrix is $2v^{2}$ times the matrix of $\mathcal E$. **One commutator does not preserve the fifteen**: $[A_{jk},D]=(\lambda_k-\lambda_j)S_{jk}$ is real symmetric, outside $\mathfrak{su}(4)$, and the second commutator returns $(\lambda_j-\lambda_k)^{2}A_{jk}$. The mass operator is defined on the fifteen; its square root is not.

**The corner is the degenerate case.** At $D=\operatorname{diag}(-3,1,1,1)$ the three spatial entries are equal, so the six roots inside the three lines vanish. The twelve differences become $\pm4$ three times each and $0$ six times; the square sends the two signs to the single number $32v^{2}$. **The nine-and-six display is a statement about how degenerate the vacuum is**, which is the same statement as "the strong group is unbroken".

**A regular vacuum, as the check.** At $d=(3,1,-1,-3)v$ the pairwise differences are $2,4,6$, so

$$
M^{2}=2v^{2}\cdot\{\underbrace{4,\dots,4}_{6},\underbrace{16,\dots,16}_{4},\underbrace{36,36}_{2}\},\qquad 0,0,0,
$$

that is $8v^{2}$ six times, $32v^{2}$ four times, $72v^{2}$ twice and three zeros. $6+6+3=15$: twelve roots and three Cartan directions.

**The kernel is a centraliser, hence an algebra.** $\ker M^{2}=\{X:[D,X]=0\}$, and if $[D,X]=[D,Y]=0$ then $[D,[X,Y]]=0$ by the Jacobi identity. **The nine massless generators are therefore closed under the bracket by construction, not by a check of every bracket among them.** A mass matrix whose kernel is a subalgebra is the matrix statement that the breaking leaves a group standing.

## The Nine That Survive, and Why They Are Exactly Nine

**The list.**

| surviving direction | count | name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $A_{jk},iS_{jk}$ for $j,k\in\{x,y,z\}$ | $8$ | gluons |
| $iD=T_x+T_y+T_z$ | $1$ | $B'$, the sum of the surviving frame |
| the three frame directions $T_j$ | $3$, of which two are in the eight | $B'$ is the sum; $\lambda_3,\lambda_8$ are the two differences |

**Eight gluons and one abelian vector.** $\mathfrak{u}(3)=\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{u}(1)$ is $8+1=9$, and the corpus's $B'$ is the abelian one — **the fourth-adjoint direction that survives because the strong group is a stabiliser and is therefore not broken.**

**The rank does not move, and this is the structural fact to carry forward.**

$$
\operatorname{rank}\mathfrak{su}(4)=3\ \longrightarrow\ \operatorname{rank}\bigl(\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{u}(1)\bigr)=2+1=3 .
$$

**The breaking costs dimension and not rank.** A condensate chooses a direction inside the maximal torus, so the torus survives; **that is why the strong group plus one abelian factor is available as an endpoint and the block plus two weak factors is not.** The second is a rank-five statement, and rank five is not reachable from rank three by any breaking at all — **which is the content of the next two articles, and the reason the weak force cannot be a fragment of what breaks here.**

## What Breaks and What Does Not

**Four rows, because four different things are true at once.**

| the group | $\mathrm{SU}(4)$, dimension $15$ | $\mathrm{SU}(3)_c\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}$, dimension $9$ | it breaks |

| the rank | $3$ | $3$ | it does not move |

**The sentence to keep.** **The primordial force breaks into the strong group and one abelian factor, and the price of the breaking is six vectors at $32v^{2}$ and six real scalars absorbed.** Everything else — the rank, the spatial block, the abelian direction, the vacuum — is unchanged.

**And the sentence that must not be written.** *The leptoquarks are destroyed and their debris becomes the weak force.* The charge theorem of [the companion article](the-charge-theorem-the-sextet-cannot-be-recomposed-into-the-weak-pair.html) closes that reading: every member of the sextet has $Q=\pm\tfrac23$, so no combination of them reaches $Q=\pm1$. **The breaking produces the strong group, one abelian factor, and six heavy vectors. It does not produce a weak doublet.**

## The Restructuring Is a Statement About the Vacuum, Not About the Theory

**Nothing in this article removes a generator from the model.** The fifteen arrays are the same fifteen above and below the restructuring; what changes is which of them the ground state commutes with. **A condensate is a property of the vacuum, so the restructuring is a property of one phase of the theory and not of its Lagrangian**, and the Lagrangian is one $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ theory at every scale.

**So the primordial force is not gone, and the word for what happens to it is restructuring: a breaking of the group into nine plus six, and an arrival of the weak factors beside it.** Above $M_C$ the condensate is absent, the fifteen directions are all massless redundant directions, and the group is one simple factor. Below $M_C$ the same fifteen are read through a vacuum that commutes with nine of them and not with six. **The two descriptions are one theory in two phases**, and the passage between them is the vanishing of one scalar expectation value.

**What would restore it, stated as a reading and not as a result.** A condensate that vanishes restores the symmetry. Three routes do that: a temperature of order $M_C$; a field excursion that carries the scalar to the origin; or a region in which the scalar never condensed. **The corpus derives none of the three**, because the scalar potential is not in it. What the corpus establishes is the spectrum on either side of the restructuring, and that is enough to say what restoration would look like and what it would cost.

**The observable is the sextet.** No generator of $\mathrm{SU}(3)_c\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}$ carries a quark into a lepton: triality and $B-L$ are both conserved by the residual group, and the sextet is the only place in the fifteen where the two are traded. **So a leptoquark seen is not a particle added to the model; it is one of the fifteen acting**, and its rate is set by the coupling the model already has. The null searches at [the threshold article](the-threshold-scales.html) bound $M_C$ from below, and **that bound is the statement that the unbroken phase has not yet been reached.**

## Distinctions That Are Easy to Lose

A condensate is not a choice of basis. The direction is chosen by the scalar's value; the basis is chosen by the writer, and the spectrum does not care (*The Condensate*).

Eaten is not destroyed. The six leave the asymptotic spectrum and enter it as longitudinal modes; calling them destroyed removes the only object the later articles use (*The Six That Are Eaten*).

$32v^{2}$ is not a mass. It is the coefficient of $v^{2}$; the conversion is a coupling statement and lives elsewhere (*The Mass the Six Acquire*).

The nine are not "the rest". They are exactly the centraliser of the vacuum, and there is a theorem behind the word exactly (*The Nine That Survive*).

The rank is not reduced. Six dimensions leave and the torus stays; that is why the endpoint is $\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{U}(1)$ and not something with fewer abelian directions (*What Breaks and What Does Not*).

**The six halves are not bosons of the model.** The corpus states it in the mass-matrix article and it is worth repeating: on the carrier each is one spatial plane plus or minus one leptoquark plane, so it is a coherence of a massless direction and a heavy one, not a particle. **That sentence is the seed of the whole migration**: what breaks here is the parent of the strong group, the weak vectors arrive with the doublet index, and their masses come from a later breaking through a different vacuum.

## Summary

**The restructuring turns on one direction.** $iD=\operatorname{diag}(-3i,i,i,i)$, that is $D=\operatorname{diag}(-3,1,1,1)$ times $i$, forced by the lepton-line declaration and by the trace condition up to scale. **The $i$ is what puts $\pm4i$ in the adjoint's spectrum**: on the real matrix $\operatorname{diag}(-3,1,1,1)$ the eigenvalues are the real $\pm4$.

**Its adjoint action is the whole theorem.** $\operatorname{ad}(iD)$ has spectrum $0^{9}\oplus(\pm4i)^{3}$; the kernel is $\mathfrak{u}(3)=\mathfrak{su}(3)_c\oplus\mathfrak{u}(1)_{B-L}$, dimension nine; the $\pm4i$ eigenspace is the leptoquark sextet, $\mathbf3_{+4/3}\oplus\bar{\mathbf3}_{-4/3}$, $Q=\pm\tfrac23$.

**The six are eaten and heavy at once.** Six real scalar components become longitudinal modes, and the six vectors acquire $M^{2}=32v^{2}=2(1-(-3))^{2}v^{2}$ each, equivalently $M_X^{2}=16g_4^{2}v_C^{2}$.

**The nine survive exactly.** Eight gluons and $B'$, the centraliser of the vacuum, massless and untouched.

**The rank does not move.** $3\to2+1=3$: the breaking costs dimension and not rank. **That single line is why the weak force cannot come from here**, and it is where the next article begins.

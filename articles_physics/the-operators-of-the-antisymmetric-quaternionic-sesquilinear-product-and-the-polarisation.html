The Operators of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Sesquilinear Product and the Polarisation

Introduction

This article is the operator article of the fourth band's antisymmetric half, the operation $\mathrm{AQS}$ of The 12 Algebraic Structures over the Biquaternion $\mathbb{C}$ Space. Its product is the antisymmetric quaternionic sesquilinear product

$$ \tilde P\diamond\tilde Q=\tfrac12\bigl(\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q^{*}-\tilde Q^{*}\tilde P^{\natural}\bigr) =\vec P\times\overline{\vec Q}, $$

and its operators attach an element to an argument:

$$ L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}(\tilde X)=\tilde A\diamond\tilde X=\vec A\times\overline{\vec X},\qquad R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}(\tilde X)=\tilde X\diamond\tilde A=\vec X\times\overline{\vec A} . $$

The left family is conjugate-linear in its argument, the right family is $\mathbb{C}$-linear, and the band's conjugate-alternation links the two, $L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}=-C\circ R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$ with $C$ the coefficientwise conjugation. The composite of two left multiplications is again $\mathbb{C}$-linear and is not a left multiplication, so the family is not closed and carries no Lie algebra; the closure that exists is the closure of the underlying cross product, and it is invisible on the real quaternion forms.

The answer in one sentence is this. The operators of the band are adjoint for the indefinite Krein form $K$ of the fourth product, and the adjoint of the left multiplication is its negative, $L^{\diamond\dagger}_{\tilde A}=-L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$: it reverses the parameter and keeps the side, a third behaviour on the one algebra beside the composition band's side-swapping transpose and the probability band's parameter-conjugating Hermitian adjoint. The band's sandwich is consequently self-adjoint for $K$ for every parameter, and the type criteria, the symmetries and the interpretation follow from that sign.

The operation is Introduction to the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Sesqualgebra of Biquaternions; the operator content is The Adjoint Operators of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Sesqualgebra, which owns the left multiplication, its invariants, its adjoint and its structure group; the form is The Krein Gram Matrix and the Restrictions of the Form; the indefinite adjoint and its type criteria are J-Self-Adjoint and J-Unitary Operators on the General Quaternionic Sesqualgebra of Biquaternions; and the algebraic norm, the Jacobi failure and the higher structure are The Cross Product of a Vector with Its Conjugate, A Bracket Invisible on the Real Forms and A Higher Structure for the Jacobi Failure, cited and not repeated.

Conventions. As in the block: $\mathbb{B}=\mathbb{C}\otimes_{\mathbb{R}}\mathbb{H}$ with basis $e_0=1,e_1,e_2,e_3$, $e_k^{2}=-e_0$, $e_1e_2=e_3$, central $i$; $\tilde Q=\sum_\mu Q_\mu e_\mu$ with vector part $\vec Q$, natural conjugation $\tilde Q^{\natural}=Q_0e_0-\vec Q$ and star $\tilde Q^{*}=\overline{\tilde Q^{\natural}}$; $(\cdot,\cdot)$ and $\times$ are the bilinear dot and cross products; the form is $K(\tilde P,\tilde Q)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q^{*})=\sum_\mu\varepsilon_\mu P_\mu\overline{Q_\mu}$ with $\varepsilon=(1,-1,-1,-1)$ and fundamental symmetry $J={}^{\natural}$. The symbol $\diamond$ is the mathematics articles' name for the operation; the physics menu comment renders it $\wedge$, which the mathematics avoids because $\wedge$ names the plain cross product.

The Two Multiplication Operators and Their Composition Law

The band attaches one operator to each slot, and the two are not of the same kind: the first slot is the linear one, the second the conjugate-linear one.

Definition (the multiplications of the product). For $\tilde A\in\mathbb{B}$ the left multiplication and the right multiplication of the product $\diamond$ are

$$ L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}(\tilde X)=\tilde A\diamond\tilde X=\vec A\times\overline{\vec X},\qquad R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}(\tilde X)=\tilde X\diamond\tilde A=\vec X\times\overline{\vec A} . $$

Their determination is The Adjoint Operators of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Sesqualgebra, which owns the left family, its matrix, its rank and its adjoint.

The Parities and the Link Between the Families

Proposition (the parities). For every $\lambda\in\mathbb{C}$ and every $\tilde X\in\mathbb{B}$,

$$ L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}(\lambda\tilde X)=\overline{\lambda}\,L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}(\tilde X),\qquad R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}(\lambda\tilde X)=\lambda\,R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}(\tilde X), $$

and in the parameter $L^{\diamond}_{\lambda\tilde A}=\lambda L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$, $R^{\diamond}_{\lambda\tilde A}=\overline{\lambda}R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$. The left multiplication depends on $\tilde A$ through $\vec A$ and is $\mathbb{C}$-linear in the parameter; the right multiplication depends on $\tilde A$ through $\overline{\vec A}$ and is conjugate-linear in the parameter.

Proof. The cross product is conjugate-linear in its second factor and linear in its first; the parameter laws are scalar multiplication of the vector part. Verified on sixty random pairs for the variable laws and sixty for the parameter laws. The sibling mathematics article states the opposite of the parameter parity of $L$ ("conjugate-linear in $\tilde A$"); it is corrected there, since the first slot is the linear one and $L^{\diamond}_{\lambda\tilde A}=\lambda L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$.

Read as a physical statement. The two slots have opposite polarity and the attachments inherit it: attaching on the left conjugates the variable, attaching on the right does not, and the parameter follows its slot. This is an interpretation that names the parity of a sesquilinear product and calls it the two signs of a gauge pairing.

Proposition (the antisymmetry links the two families). The conjugate-alternation reads on the operators as

$$ L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}=-C\circ R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A},\qquad R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}=-C\circ L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}, $$

that is $L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\tilde X=-\overline{R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\tilde X}$ for every $\tilde X$, where the bar is the coefficientwise conjugation of the value.

Proof. The product's conjugate-alternation is $\tilde X\diamond\tilde A=-\overline{\tilde A\diamond\tilde X}$; the identity is that equality read for a fixed $\tilde A$. Verified on fifty random pairs.

Read as a physical statement. The band has one operator family, not two independent ones: the right multiplication is the conjugate of the left, and the conjugation of the second slot is the tie, which is why the band never carries a full matrix algebra of one kind. This is an interpretation.

The Composition Law

Theorem (the composition laws). For all $\tilde A,\tilde B,\tilde X\in\mathbb{B}$,

$$ L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}L^{\diamond}_{\tilde B}\tilde X=\vec A\times\bigl(\overline{\vec B}\times\vec X\bigr) =\overline{\vec B}\,(\vec A,\vec X)-(\vec A,\overline{\vec B})\,\vec X, $$

$$ R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}R^{\diamond}_{\tilde B}\tilde X=\bigl(\vec X\times\overline{\vec B}\bigr)\times\overline{\vec A},\qquad L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}R^{\diamond}_{\tilde B}\tilde X=\vec A\times\bigl(\overline{\vec X}\times\vec B\bigr),\qquad R^{\diamond}_{\tilde B}L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\tilde X=\bigl(\vec A\times\overline{\vec X}\bigr)\times\overline{\vec B} . $$

The first law is the composition law adopted by this article; the second form is the vector triple-product identity $\vec A\times(\vec B\times\vec C)=\vec B(\vec A,\vec C)-\vec C(\vec A,\vec B)$.

Proof. Each law substitutes one definition into the other and cancels the two conjugations in the inner slot. The four laws were verified on sixty random triples each to machine precision. The left composition is The Adjoint Operators of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Sesqualgebra, §The Composition and the Conjugate Invariants.

Corollary (the square). Setting $\tilde B=\tilde A$, the square is $\bigl(L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\bigr)^{2}\tilde X=\overline{\vec A}(\vec A,\vec X)-(\vec A,\overline{\vec A})\vec X$, a $\mathbb{C}$-linear map of the vector part, in general nonzero and vanishing on the centre. Verified on fifty random pairs.

Read as a physical statement. Two attachments on the left compose into a linear operation of the ordinary kind, with a rank-one part and a scalar part proportional to $(\vec A,\overline{\vec A})$: the band composes its operations into objects that are not operations of the band. This is an interpretation.

The Failure of Closure

Theorem (no Lie algebra of the left multiplications). The commutator of two left multiplications is

$$ \bigl[L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A},L^{\diamond}_{\tilde B}\bigr]\tilde X =\overline{\vec B}\,(\vec A,\vec X)-\overline{\vec A}\,(\vec B,\vec X) -2i\,\mathrm{Im}\bigl(\vec A,\overline{\vec B}\bigr)\,\vec X, $$

a $\mathbb{C}$-linear operator. On the real quaternion subspace the last term vanishes and

$$ \bigl[L^{\diamond}_{\vec a},L^{\diamond}_{\vec b}\bigr]\tilde X=(\vec a\times\vec b)\times\vec X =R^{\diamond}_{-(\vec a\times\vec b)}\tilde X, $$

while on the real subspace of the arguments it is the left multiplication of the cross product, $[L^{\diamond}_{\vec a},L^{\diamond}_{\vec b}]=L^{\diamond}_{\vec a\times\vec b}$. Off the real forms the last term is a nonzero multiple of the identity whenever $\mathrm{Im}(\vec A,\overline{\vec B})\neq0$, and no multiplication of the band has such a term: the commutator is then not a multiplication of the band, and the family is not closed.

Proof. The commutator is the difference of the two composites of the composition law, and the triple-product identity gives the displayed form. For real parameters the last term vanishes because $(\vec A,\overline{\vec B})$ is real, and the first two give $(\vec A\times\vec B)\times\vec X$; a multiplication of the band has no term proportional to $\vec X$, so the last term cannot be absorbed. Verified on sixty complex triples for the general formula, sixty real pairs for the real-form law, and sixty further triples for the failure count.

A complex witness. At $(\tilde A,\tilde B,\tilde X)=(e_1,\,ie_1,\,e_2)$,

$$ \bigl[L^{\diamond}_{e_1},L^{\diamond}_{ie_1}\bigr]e_2=2i\,e_2, \qquad\text{while}\qquad L^{\diamond}_{e_1\diamond ie_1}e_2=L^{\diamond}_{0}e_2=0, $$

because $e_1\diamond ie_1=e_1\times\overline{ie_1}=e_1\times(-ie_1)=0$. The witness uses $ie_1$, an element off the real basis; the failure count is sixty of sixty complex triples against zero of sixty real ones.

Read as a physical statement. A family of operators that is to be a Lie algebra must close under the commutator; here the commutator is an operator of a kind that no multiplication of the band supplies, so the family closes into nothing. The obstruction is the coefficient conjugation: on the real quaternion forms the conjugation is the identity, the cross product is a Lie bracket, and a real-form check finds no failure; the failure appears when a complex coefficient enters. This is the operator form of A Bracket Invisible on the Real Forms: the Complex Witness of the Jacobi Failure, and the positive reading is A Higher Structure for the Jacobi Failure. This is a reading.

The Adjoint: the Indefinite Form and What It Does to the Parameter

The form decides the adjoint, and the form of the band is the indefinite Krein form of the fourth product.

Definition (the form and the adjoint). The form of the band is $K(\tilde P,\tilde Q)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q^{*})=\sum_\mu\varepsilon_\mu P_\mu\overline{Q_\mu}$, of signature $(1,3)$ over $\mathbb{C}$ and $(2,6)$ over $\mathbb{R}$, with fundamental symmetry $J={}^{\natural}$. The adjoint of a conjugate-linear operator $T$ is the conjugate-linear operator $T^{\dagger}$ with $K(T\tilde R,\tilde S)=\overline{K(\tilde R,T^{\dagger}\tilde S)}$, and the adjoint of a $\mathbb{C}$-linear operator $U$ is the $\mathbb{C}$-linear operator with $K(U\tilde R,\tilde S)=K(\tilde R,U^{\dagger}\tilde S)$; the twisted rule is The Adjoint of the Sesquilinear Sandwich on the Biquaternions, §The Twisted Adjoint Rule, and the linear class's $\mathbb{C}$-linear adjoint is the $J$-adjoint $U^{\dagger}=JU^{*}J$ of J-Self-Adjoint and J-Unitary Operators on the General Quaternionic Sesqualgebra of Biquaternions.

Theorem (the adjoint of the multiplications). For all $\tilde A,\tilde B\in\mathbb{B}$,

$$ \bigl(L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\bigr)^{\dagger}=-L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A},\qquad \bigl(R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\bigr)^{\dagger}=-R^{\diamond}_{\overline{\tilde A}},\qquad \bigl(L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}R^{\diamond}_{\tilde B}\bigr)^{\dagger}=R^{\diamond}_{\overline{\tilde B}}\,L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}. $$

The adjoint of the left multiplication is its negative: it reverses the parameter, $\vec A\mapsto-\vec A$, and keeps the side. The adjoint of the right multiplication negates and conjugates the parameter and keeps the side.

Proof. For the first, the left side is $K(L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\tilde R,\tilde S)=-(\vec A\times\overline{\vec R},\overline{\vec S})=-\det[\vec A,\overline{\vec R},\overline{\vec S}]$ and the right side is $-\overline{K(\tilde R,L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\tilde S)}=\det[\overline{\vec R},\vec A,\overline{\vec S}]$, the two sign interpretations of one triple product; this is the theorem of The Adjoint Operators of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Sesqualgebra. The second is the same triple product on the other slot, and the third is antimultiplicativity, $(L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}R^{\diamond}_{\tilde B})^{\dagger}=(R^{\diamond}_{\tilde B})^{\dagger}(L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A})^{\dagger}$. Verified on sixty random parameters each.

Read as a physical statement. The adjoint transports across the indefinite pairing without transporting the side: it reverses the parameter and leaves the attachment. The three sesquilinear and bilinear rows therefore do three different things on one algebra — the transpose swaps the sides, the Hermitian adjoint conjugates and keeps the side, the indefinite adjoint negates and keeps the side. The band's operands are the value of an alternating product, and the pairing of a value with a test element differs between the two orders by the sign and the conjugate; the adjoint reads that sign. This is an interpretation.

Corollary (the sandwich is self-adjoint for every parameter). The algebraic norm two-sided operator

$$ \Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}=L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A} $$

satisfies $\bigl(\Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\bigr)^{\dagger}=\Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$ for every $\tilde A$, explicitly $R^{\diamond}_{\overline{\tilde A}}L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}=L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$.

Proof. $(\Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A})^{\dagger}=R^{\diamond}_{\overline{\tilde A}}L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$ by the third identity, and this equals $L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$ by the triple-product identity in the form $\vec A\times(\overline{\vec X}\times\vec A)=(\vec A\times\overline{\vec X})\times\vec A$. Verified on sixty random parameters.

Read as a physical statement. Where the composition band's sandwich is self-adjoint exactly on the two sectors of its parameter, the band's sandwich is self-adjoint unconditionally: the negative of the left multiplication is already the operator the pairing produces, so no sector condition can break the symmetry. A sandwich of this band is a symmetric operation of the polarisation for every direction. This is an interpretation.

The Types of the Operators

The type criteria are criteria on one parameter, as in every family whose adjoint keeps a side.

Theorem (the types of the one-sided operators). For every $\tilde A\in\mathbb{B}$,

$$ L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\ \text{self-adjoint}\iff\tilde A\in\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}, \qquad L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\ \text{skew-adjoint for every }\tilde A, $$

$$ R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\ \text{self-adjoint}\iff\vec A\in i\mathbb{R}^{3}, \qquad R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\ \text{skew-adjoint}\iff\vec A\in\mathbb{R}^{3}. $$

A self-adjoint left multiplication is therefore the zero operator, and the generic left multiplication is skew-adjoint; the right multiplication separates on the reality of its vector part.

Proof. Self-adjointness of $L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$ is $-L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}=L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$, hence $\vec A=0$, and the skew case holds for every parameter. The right multiplication is parametrised injectively in $\overline{\vec A}$, so self-adjointness is $-\overline{\vec A}=\vec A$ and skew-adjointness is $-\overline{\vec A}=-\vec A$. Verified on sixty random parameters for each criterion; the operator of the block is The Adjoint Operators of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Sesqualgebra, §The Adjoint with respect to the Form.

Read as a physical statement. The band supplies no self-adjoint generator: its left multiplications are all skew-adjoint and the only self-adjoint one is zero. This is the operator statement that the antisymmetric half is a structure of directions and not of observables; the observables and the gauge generators are read with the definite Hermitian adjoint of Observables, Gauge Generators and the Chirality of the Internal Action. This is a reading.

Corollary (no J-unitary operator of the band). No nonzero multiplication operator of the band, and no nonzero sandwich of the band, preserves the Krein form: each annihilates the centre $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}$, hence is not invertible, and an operator preserving a non-degenerate form is invertible. The J-unitary operators form the indefinite unitary group $U_{J}(\mathbb{B})\cong U(1,3)$ of real dimension sixteen; the norm $N(\tilde Q)=\sum_\mu Q_\mu^{2}$ enters their criteria for the plain families of the parent, where $L_{\tilde Q}$ is $J$-unitary exactly for $\tilde Q\in S^{1}e_0$ and the plain sandwich $\Theta_{\tilde Q}$ exactly for $\lvert N(\tilde Q)\rvert=1$, as J-Self-Adjoint and J-Unitary Operators on the General Quaternionic Sesqualgebra of Biquaternions states; the band's own criteria carry no norm, because the band's operators are never invertible.

Proof. $L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$, $R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$ and $\Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$ all vanish at $\tilde X=e_0$, so the centre lies in the kernel of all three and none is invertible. The group and its dimension are The Krein Isometry Group and Its J-Contractions, and the norm criteria are the corollary of J-Self-Adjoint and J-Unitary Operators on the General Quaternionic Sesqualgebra of Biquaternions. Verified on sixty random parameters that no multiplication of the band preserves $K$.

Read as a physical statement. The isometries of the band's metric are not supplied by the band: they are the indefinite unitaries of the parent, far larger than the multiplications, and the norm $N$ enters the gauge story through the parent's plain sandwich, not through the antisymmetric half. This is an interpretation.

The Symmetries of the Form and of the Product

Theorem (the derivations and the structure group). The derivations of the band form the real vector space

$$ \mathrm{Der}(\diamond)=\mathrm{End}_{\mathbb{R}}\bigl(\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}\bigr)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(3,\mathbb{R}) $$

of real dimension seven, the scalar maps of the centre together with the real skew maps of the vector part; the group of invertible real-linear maps preserving the product contains

$$ \bigl\{\tilde Q\mapsto\lambda(Q_0)e_0+g\vec Q:\lambda\in GL_{\mathbb{R}}(\mathbb{C}),\ g\in SO(3,\mathbb{R})\bigr\} \cong GL(2,\mathbb{R})\times SO(3,\mathbb{R}), $$

of real dimension seven, whose Lie algebra is $\mathrm{Der}(\diamond)$.

Proof. The derivations and the structure group are The Adjoint Operators of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Sesqualgebra, §The Derivations and the Structure Group; the Leibniz rule on the vector part is the classical derivation condition of the cross product, whose solutions are the skew maps of $\mathbb{R}^{3}$.

Proposition (the coefficient conjugation is an automorphism). The coefficientwise conjugation preserves the product, $\overline{\tilde P\diamond\tilde Q}=\overline{\tilde P}\diamond\overline{\tilde Q}$, it is conjugate-linear, and it is an antiunitary map of the form, $K(\bar{\tilde P},\bar{\tilde Q})=\overline{K(\tilde P,\tilde Q)}$.

Proof. $\overline{\tilde P\diamond\tilde Q}=\overline{\vec P\times\overline{\vec Q}}=\overline{\vec P}\times\vec Q=\overline{\tilde P}\diamond\overline{\tilde Q}$, using that the coefficient conjugation is an involution; the form statement is the same computation on the scalar pairing. Verified on fifty random pairs for the product law and thirty for the form law.

Read as a physical statement. The symmetries of the product are an arbitrary real-linear relabelling of the scalar coordinate and a rotation of the vector part, while the form $K$ is preserved by the larger group $U(1,3)$. The metric and the product therefore have different symmetries: the product is a direction structure, the form a gauge structure. This is an interpretation.

The Sandwich of the Band and Its Adjoint

The band has no unit and no associativity, so its sandwich is not built from an inverse, and its comparison with the parent's inner automorphism compares two different objects.

Theorem (the sandwich, its value and its adjoint). The algebraic norm two-sided operator of the band is

$$ \Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}=L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A},\qquad \Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\tilde X=\vec A\times\bigl(\overline{\vec X}\times\vec A\bigr) =(\vec A,\vec A)\,\overline{\vec X}-\vec A\,(\vec A,\overline{\vec X}), $$

conjugate-linear, annihilating the centre, with $\bigl(\Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\bigr)^{\dagger}=\Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$; and the general two-sided operator has the adjoint $\bigl(L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}R^{\diamond}_{\tilde B}\bigr)^{\dagger}=R^{\diamond}_{\overline{\tilde B}}L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$.

Proof. The value is the composite of the two definitions with the inner conjugation cancelled and the last form is the triple-product identity; the adjoints are the identities of the preceding section. Verified on sixty random parameters for the value law, sixty for the self-adjointness and sixty for the general adjoint.

Theorem (the sandwich against the inner automorphism). The band has no unit, so the parent's inner automorphism $L_{\tilde A}R_{\tilde A^{-1}}$ of the general plain bilinear product does not exist inside the band and is an operator of the composition band. The band's sandwich uses the same parameter in both slots, is never invertible, and is therefore never an automorphism; it is equivariant under the rotation part of the structure group,

$$ \Theta^{\diamond}_{g\tilde A}\,g=g\,\Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A} \qquad\text{for every }g\in SO(3,\mathbb{R}), $$

so it transforms as an axial direction under the symmetries of the product.

Proof. The sandwich depends on $\vec A$, and $g$ acts on the vector part by a real orthogonal matrix of determinant one, commuting with the coefficient conjugation, so $g(\vec A\times(\overline{\vec X}\times\vec A))=g\vec A\times(\overline{g\vec X}\times g\vec A)$; non-invertibility is the annihilation of the centre. Verified on thirty random parameters and rotations.

Read as a physical statement. In the composition band the sandwich and the inner automorphism agree exactly on the unitary slice; here there is no slice, because there is no inner automorphism at all. The band's sandwich reads an element through the product twice, and its symmetry is the rotation-equivariance of an axial direction, not the conjugation of a group. The comparison with the model's sandwich is the crossing reader The Operators of the Twelve Structures: Multiplication, Adjoint and Bracket. This is an interpretation.

The Operators Acting on the Gauge Space

The band's operators act on the algebra $\mathbb{B}$ itself, and that space carries the indefinite pairing: it is the gauge space and not the state space.

Proposition (the space and the image of a multiplication). The Krein form splits the algebra as an orthogonal direct sum $\mathbb{B}=\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}\perp_{K}\mathbb{V}_{\mathbb{B}}$, positive definite on the centre and negative definite on the vector subspace, of realified signature $(2,6)$. For $\tilde A\neq0$ the left multiplication is a conjugate-linear map of real rank four with

$$ \ker L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}=\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}\oplus\mathbb{C}\,\overline{\vec A},\qquad \operatorname{im}L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}=\bigl\{\vec V\in\mathbb{V}_{\mathbb{B}}:(\vec A,\vec V)=0\bigr\}, $$

and zero trace; the right multiplication has the same real invariants, being the conjugate of the left.

Proof. The decomposition and the inertia are The Krein Gram Matrix and the Restrictions of the Form; the kernel, the image, the rank and the trace are The Adjoint Operators of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Sesqualgebra, §The Matrix, the Rank and the Kernel, confirmed here on the real basis and on sixty random arguments.

Proposition (the operator-pairing). The trace of the composite of two left multiplications is the conjugate-pairing of their parameters,

$$ \operatorname{Tr}_{\mathbb{C}}\bigl(L^{\diamond}_{\tilde P}L^{\diamond}_{\tilde Q}\bigr) =-2\,(\vec P,\overline{\vec Q}), $$

so the operator family reproduces the sesquilinear pairing of the vector parts of The Sesquilinear Pairing of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Sesqualgebra.

Proof. The composite is $\overline{\vec Q}(\vec P,\vec X)-(\vec P,\overline{\vec Q})\vec X$; the rank-one part $X\mapsto\overline{\vec Q}(\vec P,\vec X)$ contributes $+(\vec P,\overline{\vec Q})$ and the second term contributes $-3(\vec P,\overline{\vec Q})$ as the trace of the negative of the projection onto the vector subspace, for the total $-2(\vec P,\overline{\vec Q})$. Verified on forty random pairs.

Read as a physical statement. The states of the framework are not the gauge space: they are the elements the indefinite metric cannot normalise, while the band's values are pure vectors. The left multiplication sends every element into the plane perpendicular to its parameter and kills the centre and the conjugate of its parameter's line; the centre is the scalar, potential-like direction and the image is the direction of the polarisation, so the operator maps the whole frame to a direction. The trace pairing is the operator-level form of the same statement: the family carries the pairing of the vector parts and no norm. The state side is The States the Indefinite Metric Cannot Normalise and Why the Fourth Product Is a Gauge Structure and Not a State Space. This is a reading.

A Worked Case

An isotropic vector parameter. Take

$$ \tilde A=e_1+ie_2,\qquad \vec A=(1,i,0),\qquad (\vec A,\vec A)=0,\qquad (\vec A,\overline{\vec A})=2,\qquad K(\tilde A,\tilde A)=-2 . $$

The vector part is isotropic for the bilinear dot, the case the algebraic norm of the product reads: $\tilde A\diamond\tilde A=\vec A\times\overline{\vec A}=-2i\,e_3$. The left multiplication is

$$ L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}e_1=-i\,e_3,\qquad L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}e_2=e_3,\qquad L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}e_3=i\,e_1-e_2,\qquad L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}(ie_3)=e_1+ie_2=\tilde A, $$

with kernel $\mathbb{C}e_0\oplus\mathbb{C}(e_1-ie_2)$, since $L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}(e_1-ie_2)=0$, and image the plane $\{(V_1,V_2,V_3):V_1+iV_2=0\}$, of real rank four. The sandwich, using $(\vec A,\vec A)=0$, reduces to a rank-two map onto the complex line $\mathbb{C}\tilde A$,

$$ \Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\tilde X=-\vec A\,(\vec A,\overline{\vec X}),\qquad \Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}e_1=-\tilde A,\qquad \Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}e_2=e_2-ie_1,\qquad \Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}e_3=0, $$

and it is self-adjoint with $K(\Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}e_1,e_1)=+1$ and $K(\Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}e_1,\Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}e_1)=-2$.

A real parameter for contrast. Take $\tilde A=e_1$, with $(\vec A,\vec A)=1$, $L^{\diamond}_{e_1}e_2=e_3$, $L^{\diamond}_{e_1}e_3=-e_2$, $L^{\diamond}_{e_1}(ie_2)=-ie_3$, $L^{\diamond}_{e_1}(ie_3)=ie_2$. The sandwich is the projection onto the plane perpendicular to $e_1$ followed by the coefficient conjugation of the vector part, $\Theta^{\diamond}_{e_1}\tilde X=\overline{\vec X}-e_1(\overline{\vec X},e_1)$, so $\Theta^{\diamond}_{e_1}e_2=e_2$, $\Theta^{\diamond}_{e_1}e_3=e_3$, $\Theta^{\diamond}_{e_1}(ie_2)=-ie_2$, $\Theta^{\diamond}_{e_1}(ie_3)=-ie_3$, $\Theta^{\diamond}_{e_1}e_1=0$; it is self-adjoint, and the real parameter is the case in which the sandwich is a conjugation on the plane.

The closure witness. At $(\tilde A,\tilde B,\tilde X)=(e_1,ie_1,e_2)$ the commutator of two left multiplications is

$$ \bigl[L^{\diamond}_{e_1},L^{\diamond}_{ie_1}\bigr]e_2=2i\,e_2, \qquad\text{while}\qquad L^{\diamond}_{e_1\diamond ie_1}e_2=L^{\diamond}_{0}e_2=0, $$

so the commutator is not a multiplication; the term $2i\vec X$ is the multiple of the identity that the conjugation produces.

A numerical check. Verified to machine precision: the four composition laws on sixty random triples each; the square law on fifty; the two variable and the two parameter parities on sixty each; the antisymmetry link on fifty; the three adjoint identities on sixty each; the commutator formula, the real-form law and the closure count on sixty triples each (sixty failures on complex triples, none on real ones); the type criteria and the non-$J$-unitarity on sixty parameters each; the automorphism property of the coefficient conjugation, the kernel, image and rank, the trace pairing and the sandwich value, self-adjointness and equivariance laws on thirty to sixty elements each; and the worked-case values on the exact elements displayed.

The Ledger

Proved. The band's multiplications are $L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}(\tilde X)=\vec A\times\overline{\vec X}$, conjugate-linear in the argument and $\mathbb{C}$-linear in the parameter, and $R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}(\tilde X)=\vec X\times\overline{\vec A}$, $\mathbb{C}$-linear in the argument and conjugate-linear in the parameter, with $L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}=-C\circ R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$. The composition laws are $L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}L^{\diamond}_{\tilde B}\tilde X=\vec A\times(\overline{\vec B}\times\vec X)$, $R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}R^{\diamond}_{\tilde B}\tilde X=(\vec X\times\overline{\vec B})\times\overline{\vec A}$, $L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}R^{\diamond}_{\tilde B}\tilde X=\vec A\times(\overline{\vec X}\times\vec B)$ and $R^{\diamond}_{\tilde B}L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\tilde X=(\vec A\times\overline{\vec X})\times\overline{\vec B}$; the composite of two left multiplications is $\mathbb{C}$-linear and not a multiplication, and the trace pairing is $\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathbb{C}}(L^{\diamond}_{\tilde P}L^{\diamond}_{\tilde Q})=-2(\vec P,\overline{\vec Q})$. The commutator is $[L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A},L^{\diamond}_{\tilde B}]\tilde X=\overline{\vec B}(\vec A,\vec X)-\overline{\vec A}(\vec B,\vec X)-2i\operatorname{Im}(\vec A,\overline{\vec B})\vec X$; it is the multiplication $R^{\diamond}_{-(\vec a\times\vec b)}$ and equals $L^{\diamond}_{\vec a\times\vec b}$ on the real forms, and off them it is not a multiplication, the witness $(e_1,ie_1,e_2)$ giving $2ie_2$ against $0$; the left family is not closed and carries no Lie algebra. The form of the band is the indefinite Krein form of signature $(1,3)$, and the adjoint it defines is the twisted adjoint of the conjugate-linear class with $(L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A})^{\dagger}=-L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$, $(R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A})^{\dagger}=-R^{\diamond}_{\overline{\tilde A}}$ and $(L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}R^{\diamond}_{\tilde B})^{\dagger}=R^{\diamond}_{\overline{\tilde B}}L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$: the adjoint reverses the parameter and keeps the side. The types are $L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$ self-adjoint exactly for a central parameter and skew-adjoint for every parameter, and $R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$ self-adjoint exactly for $\vec A\in i\mathbb{R}^{3}$ and skew-adjoint exactly for $\vec A\in\mathbb{R}^{3}$; no nonzero operator of the band is $J$-unitary, the $J$-unitaries form $U(1,3)$, and the norm criterion $\lvert N\rvert=1$ belongs to the parent's plain sandwich. The derivations are $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbb{R}}(\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}})\oplus\mathfrak{so}(3,\mathbb{R})$, of real dimension seven, with structure group $GL(2,\mathbb{R})\times SO(3,\mathbb{R})$, and the coefficient conjugation is an automorphism of the product and an antiunitary of the form. The sandwich is $\Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}\tilde X=(\vec A,\vec A)\overline{\vec X}-\vec A(\vec A,\overline{\vec X})$, self-adjoint for $K$ for every parameter, non-invertible and $SO(3,\mathbb{R})$-equivariant; the parent's inner automorphism is not an operator of the band. On the gauge space, of canonical decomposition $\mathbb{B}=\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}\perp_{K}\mathbb{V}_{\mathbb{B}}$ and realified signature $(2,6)$, the left multiplication has rank four, kernel $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}\oplus\mathbb{C}\overline{\vec A}$, image the plane $(\vec A,\cdot)=0$ and zero trace.

Interpretations. That the adjoint of the band is taken for the indefinite form and is a negation of the parameter that keeps the side, where the composition band's transpose swaps the sides and the probability band's Hermitian adjoint conjugates the parameter; that the band supplies no self-adjoint generator, all its multiplications being skew-adjoint, so the antisymmetric half is a structure of directions and not of observables; that the operators do not close to a Lie algebra, the failure being invisible on the real quaternion forms and carried by a complex witness, while the closure that exists is the cross-product closure of the real forms; that the sandwich is self-adjoint for every parameter and there is no inner automorphism in the band to compare it with; that the operators act on the gauge space, send the whole frame into the plane perpendicular to the parameter and kill the centre, so the polarisation direction is the image and the centre is not a state; and that the band's criteria carry no norm. Each is the framework's naming of a proved algebraic fact and is labelled as such.

Physical Readings

Read under the job word polarisation, the article is the operator layer of the antisymmetric gauge product, which pairs a vector with the conjugate of another and returns an axial direction (The Cross Product of a Vector with Its Conjugate: the Antisymmetric Gauge Product). The first reading is that the operators carry a direction and not a state: the left multiplication sends every element into the plane perpendicular to its parameter, the image is a direction space, and no value of the band is an element the indefinite metric can normalise. The second interpretation is that the adjoint is a reversal of the parameter: transporting a direction across the pairing reverses its sense, the operator form of the axial character of the cross product. The third interpretation is negative: the operators do not close to a Lie algebra, so the band does not supply the internal symmetry algebra of the frame, and the closure that exists is the cross-product closure of the real quaternion forms, invisible to a computation with real coefficients. Read against the composition and probability bands, the three rows are three adjoints on one algebra: the transpose that swaps the sides, the Hermitian adjoint that conjugates and keeps the side, and the indefinite adjoint that negates the parameter and keeps the side. Read on the clock, the band's operators carry no central phase of their own, because the centre is annihilated, so the band belongs entirely to the vector part. The placement in the twelve is the final interpretation: the antisymmetric quaternionic sesquilinear row is the row where the bracket fails, the adjoint negates, and the metric remains a gauge metric and not a state space.

Summary

The antisymmetric quaternionic sesquilinear product is $\tilde P\diamond\tilde Q=\vec P\times\overline{\vec Q}$, and its multiplication operators are the conjugate-linear left multiplication $L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}(\tilde X)=\vec A\times\overline{\vec X}$ and the $\mathbb{C}$-linear right multiplication $R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}(\tilde X)=\vec X\times\overline{\vec A}$, tied by the conjugate-alternation $L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}=-C\circ R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$. The composition law adopted is $L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}L^{\diamond}_{\tilde B}\tilde X=\vec A\times(\overline{\vec B}\times\vec X)$; the four laws are displayed and were verified on sixty random triples each. The composite of two left multiplications is $\mathbb{C}$-linear and is not a multiplication, so the family is not closed; the commutator $\overline{\vec B}(\vec A,\vec X)-\overline{\vec A}(\vec B,\vec X)-2i\operatorname{Im}(\vec A,\overline{\vec B})\vec X$ is a multiplication on the real forms and a non-multiplication off them, with the complex witness $(e_1,ie_1,e_2)$ giving $2ie_2$ against $0$. The band carries no Lie algebra, the failure is invisible on the real quaternion forms, and the positive interpretation of the same failure as a higher structure is the band's own article. The form of the band is the indefinite Krein form of signature $(1,3)$, and its adjoint reverses the parameter and keeps the side: $(L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A})^{\dagger}=-L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$, $(R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A})^{\dagger}=-R^{\diamond}_{\overline{\tilde A}}$ and $(L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}R^{\diamond}_{\tilde B})^{\dagger}=R^{\diamond}_{\overline{\tilde B}}L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$. Every left multiplication is skew-adjoint and the only self-adjoint one is zero; the right multiplication is self-adjoint exactly for a purely imaginary vector part; no nonzero operator of the band is $J$-unitary, the $J$-unitary group being the parent's $U(1,3)$, and the norm criterion $\lvert N\rvert=1$ belongs to the parent's plain sandwich. The derivations are $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbb{R}}(\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}})\oplus\mathfrak{so}(3,\mathbb{R})$ of dimension seven, the structure group is $GL(2,\mathbb{R})\times SO(3,\mathbb{R})$, the coefficient conjugation is an automorphism of the product and an antiunitary of the form, and the metric and the product have different symmetries. The sandwich $\Theta^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}=L^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}R^{\diamond}_{\tilde A}$ is self-adjoint for $K$ for every parameter, non-invertible and $SO(3,\mathbb{R})$-equivariant; the inner automorphism of the parent is not an operator of the band, so the comparison degenerates and the degeneration is the interpretation. On the gauge space the left multiplication has rank four, kernel $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}\oplus\mathbb{C}\overline{\vec A}$, image the plane $(\vec A,\cdot)=0$ and zero trace, and the trace pairing of two left multiplications is $-2(\vec P,\overline{\vec Q})$, the sesquilinear pairing of the vector parts. The worked case gives the isotropic parameter $e_1+ie_2$ with $(\vec A,\vec A)=0$, $(\vec A,\overline{\vec A})=2$, $\tilde A\diamond\tilde A=-2ie_3$, the sandwich image the line $\mathbb{C}\tilde A$, and the closure witness $[L^{\diamond}_{e_1},L^{\diamond}_{ie_1}]e_2=2ie_2$. Read under polarisation, the operators carry a direction and not a state, the adjoint reverses the parameter's sense, the front of the frame is the plane perpendicular to the parameter, and the band does not close to an algebra.

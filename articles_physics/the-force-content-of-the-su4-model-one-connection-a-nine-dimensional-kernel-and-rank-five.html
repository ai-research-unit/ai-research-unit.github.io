The Force Content of the $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ Model: One Connection, a Nine-Dimensional Kernel, and Rank Five

Introduction

The claim. The model writes one connection. Fifteen generators, one bracket, one coupling $g_4$, and the same $g_4$ multiplies the representative, the fermion and the scalar. That identity is what the words one force mean here, and it is a statement about the action and not about the algebra.

The three statuses, and one absence. The other objects that physics calls forces are not three more connections. The spatial part of the fifteen is what the vacuum commutes with. The weak pair is not in the fifteen and arrives with the breaking. Gravity has no slot in the fifteen and is carried by a frame. Each status has a test, and the tests are different in kind.

object where it lives status test
the primordial force the fifteen, above $M_C$ the one connection one $g_4$ in three kinetic terms
the strong group the kernel of $\operatorname{ad}(iD)$ survives $[iD,X]=0$ on the eight
$B-L$ the kernel, ninth direction survives the same test
the weak pair an index on the condensate arrives rank $3+1+1=5$
gravity the frame, not the fifteen no slot no generator carries it

The strongest objection, stated first. The $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ action is a transcription onto the algebra and not a derivation from it. The compact group of $\mathbb{B}$ is $\mathrm{U}(2)$, of real dimension four, and fifteen generators do not fit inside four. So one force is a claim about the model's Lagrangian, not a theorem about $\mathbb{B}$. An article that let the stronger reading stand would inflate everything after it.

What one does not mean. One force is not one parameter. The scalar's six coefficients, the Yukawa matrix, the parameter $\zeta$ and the topological angle $\theta_4$ are nine more numbers, and the group fixes none of them. One force names the number of connection couplings and nothing else.

The plan. The display is read first, and the word one is fixed there. The strong group is treated as the kernel, with its coupling, its running and its confinement regime. The two theorems that forbid the weak pair from being debris come next, then the index that replaces debris. Two senses of residual are then separated, because the weak force fits one of them and not the other. Gravity follows, as a frame and not an element. A ledger and a list of what is not claimed close the argument.

The Display, and What One Means in It

The action in one display. With $Ξ$ the scalar and $\xi_f$ the sixteen Weyl fields,

$$ \mathcal{L} =-\tfrac14\sum_{a,\imath,\jmath}F_{a,\imath\jmath}F_{a,\imath\jmath} +\sum_{f}\bar\xi_{f}\Bigl(-i\sum_{\imath}\varepsilon_{\imath}\gamma_{\imath}D_{\imath}\Bigr)\xi_{f} +\operatorname{tr}\bigl(D_{\imath}Ξ\bigr)^{\dagger}\bigl(D_{\imath}Ξ\bigr)-V(Ξ) +\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{gf}}+\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{gh}}+\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{Yuk}}, $$

with one covariant derivative, $D_{\imath}=\partial_{\imath}-ig_4\sum_{a=1}^{15}A_{a,\imath}t_{a}$, and one connection coupling.

The identity that carries the word. The coupling is the same $g_4$ in the representative's kinetic term, in the fermion's and in the scalar's. Nothing else in the display says one force: there are seven terms, four dynamical pieces, and a sum over fifteen generators. The single coupling, appearing three times at the same value, is the whole content.

What the display does not contain. No $W$, no $Z$, no photon, no mixing angle, no mass, no Yukawa eigenvalue. The fermion piece contains no mass and no chirality projection, because both need a second structure and the group supplies neither. The potential is written and not derived: the group fixes none of its six coefficients.

Where the display stands. $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ is written in the algebra; $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ is written on it. The distinction is the corpus's ceiling and it is not repaired here. The fifteen are a matrix transcription; the identification of the printed arrays with a normalised set $t_a$, $\operatorname{tr}(t_at_b)=\tfrac12\delta_{ab}$, is one integer rotation and one rescaling. So the display stands with $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ on the transcribed side, and the sentence the model contains one connection is a statement about the model and not about the algebra behind it.

The precise claim, then. Of the interactions the model writes, exactly one is a connection. That is the claim this article defends, and every later section is a statement about the fate of that one connection or about an object that is not it.

The Strong Group: The Kernel, Not a Second Connection

The vacuum. The model's one load-bearing declaration is the split $\mathbf4=\mathbf1\oplus\mathbf3$, with $e_t$ the lepton and the other three lines the spatial lines. The scalar carries one $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ index, so its value is a vector in the carrier, and the declaration says which direction is available. Trace-freeness then forces the pattern up to scale:

$$ D=\operatorname{diag}(-3,1,1,1),\qquad iD=\operatorname{diag}(-3i,i,i,i). $$

The adjoint action, which is the whole theorem. On the fifteen arrays,

$$ \operatorname{ad}(iD):\quad 0^{9}\oplus(-4i)^{3}\oplus(+4i)^{3},\qquad 15=9+3+3 . $$

The kernel is the set of arrays the vacuum commutes with,

$$ \ker\operatorname{ad}(iD)=\mathfrak{u}(3)=\mathfrak{su}(3)_c\oplus\mathfrak{u}(1)_{B-L},\qquad \dim=8+1=9 , $$

and the six moved directions are the leptoquark sextet, $\mathbf3_{+4/3}\oplus\bar{\mathbf3}_{-4/3}$, each at $32v^{2}=2\,(1-(-3))^{2}v^{2}$.

The strong group is therefore a stabiliser, and the corpus has a name for it. $\mathrm{SU}(3)_c$ is $\mathrm{Stab}_{\mathrm{SU}(4)}(\mathbb{C}e_t)$. The word is residual, and it is already used that way for QCD. Nothing new is switched on at $M_C$: the eight arrays are the same eight matrices that were among the fifteen above the restructuring, the bracket is the same bracket, and the coupling at the matching is the same number. Above the restructuring they are eight directions of one multiplet and not an octet, which is why the corpus keeps the word gluon for the phase below it.

The coupling, followed across the boundary. At $M_C$ the strong coupling is matched to the primordial one, $g_3(M_C)=g_4$. The matching is a boundary condition taken from experiment and not a prediction, and the corpus records the tension in it; what matters here is that it is one number and not two. Below the restructuring the same number runs with a different coefficient, because the content that screens it has changed: eight Dirac fermions and one Hermitian $\mathbf{15}\oplus\mathbf1$ above, quarks and gluons below. So $\alpha_s$ is not a second constant of nature in this model. It is $g_4$ read at a lower scale with a different beta function.

Three objections, and their answers. The first is that QCD has its own angle. It has one, and the model does not supply it: $\theta_4$ is the topological angle of the fifteen, free in the Lagrangian and redundant above $M_C$ because the sixteen are massless and one chiral rotation removes it, and it is not the strong-$CP$ parameter $\bar\theta$ below the restructuring. So the model inherits the strong-$CP$ problem rather than solving it, and it does not explain the size of QCD's angle. The second is that confinement is a new phenomenon. It is a regime of the same coupling at long distance, not a second interaction, and the model has no confining phase above $M_C$ at all. The third is that $\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ is a new scale. It is dimensional transmutation of the one coupling, and a scale is not an interaction.

One correction to the phrasing that degenerates into the strong force invites. What survives is the strong group and one abelian factor, $\mathfrak{u}(3)$ and not $\mathfrak{su}(3)$. The degeneracy is into $\mathrm{SU}(3)_c\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}$. The extra direction is the sum of the three frame diagonals, $iD=T_x+T_y+T_z$, and it is why the rank does not move: $3\to2+1=3$.

The sentence to keep. The strong force is the primordial force read through a vacuum that fixes nine of the fifteen directions. It is the same object in a lower phase, and calling that a degeneration is right if the word means exactly that.

Why the Weak Pair Cannot Be Debris: Two Theorems

The natural guess. Six vectors are moved out of the spectrum at $M_C$. The weak pair needs six generators. The guess writes itself: the debris becomes the weak force.

The guess is false, and it is false before any dynamics is chosen. Two theorems close it, and they close different halves.

The rank lemma. If $\mathfrak h\subseteq\mathfrak g$ then $\operatorname{rank}\mathfrak h\le\operatorname{rank}\mathfrak g$. A maximal torus of $\mathfrak h$ is an abelian subalgebra of $\mathfrak g$ of semisimple elements, so it lies in a maximal torus of $\mathfrak g$, and its dimension cannot exceed that torus's.

Applied to the endpoint. $\operatorname{rank}\mathfrak{su}(4)=3$, and the endpoint needs $2+1+1+1=5$. No chain of breakings reaches five from three, in any order, at any temperature, with any dynamics, because rank is not dynamical. The endpoint costs more than the opening balance, and that is the whole difficulty.

The two maximal-rank readings spend the same three.

subgroup structure rank what it buys
$\mathrm{U}(3)$ $\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{U}(1)$ $2+1=3$ the block and one abelian factor
$\mathrm{S}(\mathrm{U}(2)\times\mathrm{U}(2))$ $\mathrm{SU}(2)\times\mathrm{SU}(2)\times\mathrm{U}(1)$ $1+1+1=3$ weak-times-weak and one abelian factor

The block needs two ranks and the weak pair needs two, and four exceeds three. Two ways to spend three, not two halves of five. The two rows cannot both be exact, because their brackets generate all fifteen and $\mathfrak{su}(4)$ is simple: a vacuum annihilated by both would break nothing.

The charge theorem, which closes the other half. Every member of the sextet carries $Q=\pm\tfrac23$ and $B-L=\pm\tfrac43$. The best channel it can form is

$$ \mathbf3_{+4/3}\otimes\bar{\mathbf3}_{-4/3}=\mathbf1_0\oplus\mathbf8_0 , $$

and the singlet is neutral identically. No combination reaches $Q=\pm1$. The weak arrays are in $\mathfrak{su}(4)$, and each is half leptoquark and half spatial; none is a weak boson.

The mass table separates an identity from a particle. The sextet sits at $32v^{2}$; the weak pair sits at $0$ until $v$. An array at thirty-two times the vacuum and an array at zero are not the same particle, whatever identities can be written between them.

What this means for the sentence the weak force is a residual. In the group-theoretic sense of the previous section, it is not. The strong group is a residual because it is a subgroup the vacuum fixes. The weak pair is not a subgroup of anything the vacuum fixed, and the word is unavailable for it.

What Arrives, and What It Costs

The object that carries the index. The restructuring's scalar is not only a $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ vector. It carries one index of the larger group and one doublet index,

$$ \Phi\in(\mathbf4,\mathbf1,\mathbf2), $$

so the same object breaks the primordial force and announces a two-dimensional space. That is the construction: the primordial force breaks, and the object that breaks it carries a doublet index.

The rank ledger. $2+1$ from the kernel, plus $1+1$ from the arriving pair, is five. Rank rises at an arrival because rank is additive across independent factors; it falls inside an algebra because rank is monotone under inclusion. The two lines are different, and the whole migration lives in the difference.

The price, in one word. The doublet index is a postulate. Why the condensate carries it is not derived. Why it splits into two chiral factors rather than one is a second assumption, inherited from the corpus. The dynamics of the condensation is not derived either, for any sector.

Rank five has no width. The group at $M_C$ is $\mathrm{SU}(3)_c\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$, exact at rank five and dimension fifteen. The same vacuum that produces it makes the right-handed vectors heavy, so the stage lasts no interval of energy. Below $M_C$ the description is the Standard Model's, and the left factor's three vectors plus the abelian $B'$ are what recombine at $v$ into $W^{\pm}$, $Z$ and the photon.

What the algebra supplies for the weak vertex. The chiral vertex is one basis operator. With $\Gamma_{\imath}$ the frame basis operator and $\xi$ the chirality operator,

$$ \bigl(\Gamma_{\imath}(g_V-g_A\xi)\bigr)^{2}=\varepsilon_{\imath}(g_V^{2}-g_A^{2})I_4 , $$

so the vertex is nilpotent exactly at $|g_A|=|g_V|$, which is $V-A$ and $V+A$ and nothing else. With the right multiplier normalised to the Peirce pair, the left multipliers are the informational-sector frame, and the two chiralities differ only by a sign. Maximal parity violation is nullity, and that is a theorem.

What the algebra does not supply. The coupling, the scale, the selection of the left half, the generations and the mixing matrix.

So not fundamental means one thing and not another. It means the weak pair is not in the Lagrangian above $M_C$. It does not mean the weak pair is made of the primordial force. The pair arrives beside the breaking, and it arrives with a strength the model does not predict.

Two Senses of Residual, and Why the Weak Force Fits Only One

The word is doing two jobs in the literature, and they are not the same job.

sense definition example what it predicts
group-theoretic a subgroup a vacuum fixes the strong group; $\mathrm{SU}(3)=\mathrm{Stab}_{\mathrm{SU}(4)}(e_t)$ the coupling is the same at the matching
effective a low-energy contact from integrating out a heavy carrier the nuclear force; the weak contact an expansion parameter and a new low-energy coupling

The strong group is a residual in the first sense. The nuclear force and the weak contact are residuals in the second. The confusion in the phrase the weak force is a residual like the nuclear force is that it mixes the two, and the mixing is where the sentence goes wrong.

The weak contact is a residual, and exactly. Below $M_W$, eliminating the $W$ from the charged-current action gives

$$ S_{\mathrm{eff}}=-\frac{G_F}{\sqrt2}\int d^{4}r\sum_{\imath}J^{+}_{\imath}J^{-}_{\imath}, \qquad \frac{G_F}{\sqrt2}=\frac{g^{2}}{8M_W^{2}} . $$

The elimination is exact as a Gaussian integration; the momentum expansion is the approximation applied to the resulting non-local term. At a momentum transfer of $1.602\times10^{-11}$ J the ratio $(q/M_W)^{2}$ is $1.55\times10^{-6}$, so the contact is accurate to a part per million in nuclear beta decay and the $W$ never appears as a propagating degree of freedom. What the process sees is the vertex, and the vertex is the residual.

And the range is a residual of the breaking. The $W$ is heavy because the doublet condenses. So the short range of the weak force is a consequence of electroweak symmetry breaking, and the weak force is a low-energy residue of the broken electroweak interaction is a true sentence, in the effective sense.

The disanalogy with the nuclear force, stated plainly. The nuclear force is a residual of a confining theory: its expansion parameter is $1/(QR)$ with $R$ the confinement size, its carriers are composite, and there is no nuclear gauge group at any scale. The weak contact is a residual of a Higgsed theory: its parameter is $q^{2}/M_W^{2}$, its carrier is elementary, and above $v$ there is an unbroken $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L$ with a coupling of its own. The two are residuals of different mechanisms and they are not evidence for each other.

The scorecard for the four candidate sentences.

sentence verdict
the weak contact is a low-energy residual of the $W$ true, and computed
above $v$ the weak force is not separate from electromagnetism true, one electroweak interaction
the weak pair is not among the fifteen of the primordial force true, by the rank lemma
the weak pair is a fragment of the primordial force false, by two theorems

The correction, in one line. The weak force is not fundamental in this model, but its non-fundamentality is an arrival, not a residue. The primordial force does not contain it, and the breaking does not manufacture it from debris: it supplies the index that the weak pair rides in on.

Gravity: No Slot in the Connection

The general-relativistic statement, in the form that survives scrutiny. In GR the trajectory of a test body is a geodesic, and the geodesic equation carries no force term. The Christoffel symbol can be removed at any one point by a choice of coordinates, which is the equivalence principle in its local form. Gravity is not a force is precise in exactly that sense: the gravitational term is absorbable into the frame, and no other interaction's term is.

The model's structural statement. The connection is valued in the adjoint of the group and acts on the carrier, which is a formal copy of $\mathbb{C}^{4}$. Gravity's data are the frame and the metric. No generator among the fifteen is the graviton, and no cell of the grid holds it. The absence is not an oversight of the classification: the classification is exhaustive over the fifteen, and the graviton is not a fifteenth-plus-one.

The algebraic result behind the absence. Curvature cannot reside in the algebra. $\mathbb{B}$ is the same at every point and its norm has constant coefficients, so a curved metric can be carried only by the field that attaches the algebra to the manifold:

$$ g_{\imath\jmath}=\langle\tilde E_{\Phi,\imath},\tilde E_{\Phi,\jmath}\rangle_{\natural}, $$

with $\tilde E_{\Phi,\imath}(x)\in\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ a frame of four elements. The carrier of gravity is a frame, not an element.

Why no single biquaternion can carry the perturbation. A symmetric $h_{\imath\jmath}$ in four dimensions has ten components. A biquaternion has eight real dimensions. The natural single-element carrier,

$$ \sum_{\imath\jmath}h_{\imath\jmath}\,\varepsilon_{\imath}\varepsilon_{\jmath}^{\natural}=h\,e_t , $$

equals the trace times the unit, so it is blind to the scalar-free part — which is exactly the part that carries the two gravitational-wave polarisations. The frame perturbation splits as $16=10+6$, the six being the infinitesimal local Lorentz transformations. So the metric perturbation is an $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$-valued one-form and not one algebra element. That is the exact sense in which gravity is outside the connection sector.

What exists and what does not. The quadratic action exists, as the second variation of the Einstein–Hilbert action; its variation gives the linearised equation; its time–time component is a constraint; its orbit is the linearised diffeomorphism; the count is ten minus four minus four, which is two. What does not exist is the non-linear completion, a derivation of the field equation, a representation of diffeomorphism invariance, and any fixing of $\kappa^{2}=16\pi G/c^{4}$ by the algebra. The wave equation is transcribed.

Three objections.

Gravity is a gauge theory. It is, in the Palatini and MacDowell–Mansouri formulations, and in the tetrad formulation the local Lorentz group acts on the frame. That is not a counterexample to the model's statement: it is a reformulation in which the group acts on the frame bundle and the connection is the spin connection of the tangent bundle. The model has no tangent-bundle connection. The reformulation and the model's absence are compatible.

The other forces are also geometry. They are. Every connection is a fibre-bundle connection, and geometry versus force is therefore a bad dichotomy as it stands. The sharp distinction is different: an internal connection acts on a fibre and couples to a charge that can vanish, while the gravitational connection acts on the tangent bundle and couples to energy–momentum universally. A universal charge can be removed by a frame choice at a point; a non-universal one cannot. That is the content of the equivalence principle, and it is what the other three connections lack.

Gravity has a spin-two quantum, so it is a force. That is a statement about a quantisation programme, not about this model. The corpus has a canonical quantisation of a biquaternion graviton field, and it is a separate construction that imports its own promotion. The model does not claim to derive it, and it does not claim to forbid it.

The honest sentence. The model's silence about the graviton is not evidence about nature. The geometric reading of gravity is independent of the model, and the model's agreement with it is a consequence of the gap rather than a result of the construction. What the model does establish is narrower: if the one connection is the whole of the connection sector, then gravity is not in it.

The rank chain reads $3\to5\to4\to3$. Three above, five at the arrival, four once the right factor is broken in the same vacuum, three at the bottom. The two middle numbers are the two different operations: rank rises at an arrival and falls inside a breaking.

On counting four forces. Before any breaking the Standard Model has three group factors and two couplings, so the number four is not a count of couplings. After breaking it has two unbroken factors, and the weak channel exists because the $W$ and $Z$ are heavy and not because a fourth group survives. The count four is a low-energy bookkeeping of interaction channels, not a list of fundamental connections. In this model the bookkeeping is even shorter: above $M_C$ there is one connection, and at the bottom there are two unbroken factors plus one frame.

What the ledger says in one sentence. One connection is written; the breaking leaves a kernel; an index arrives; a mass makes the arriving pair short-ranged; and gravity was never in the connection.

What the Article Does Not Claim

Five gaps, named so that the statuses are not mistaken for a derivation.

  1. $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ is not derived from $\mathbb{B}$. The compact group is $\mathrm{U}(2)$ and the fifteen are transcribed.
  2. The scalar potential is not derived, so the condensation is not derived. The article establishes what follows once the condensate exists.
  3. The doublet index is a postulate. It is the one new assumption of the migration, and it is what makes the weak pair an arrival.
  4. $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L$ as a chiral group is not built. The framework's own symmetry group is central and therefore vector-like, and a chiral group needs a non-central action.
  5. Gravity's dynamics and its coupling are not derived. The quadratic action is transcribed and the frame route supplies no field equation.

Why the statuses survive these gaps. The kernel, the rank, the charge theorem and the frame counting are statements about a spectrum and an inclusion. They do not depend on the dynamics that produced the vacuum. So the four statuses hold even if the potential is never derived — which is the reason this article can be written now.

Summary

The model writes one connection. Fifteen generators, one bracket, one $g_4$ in the representative, fermion and scalar kinetic terms. The display is a transcription onto the algebra; the compact group of $\mathbb{B}$ is $\mathrm{U}(2)$, and the claim is about the model.

The strong group is the kernel and not a second connection. $iD=\operatorname{diag}(-3i,i,i,i)$ gives $\operatorname{ad}(iD)$ the spectrum $0^{9}\oplus(\pm4i)^{3}$, so nine of the fifteen are fixed and six are moved. The kernel is $\mathfrak{u}(3)=\mathfrak{su}(3)_c\oplus\mathfrak{u}(1)_{B-L}$, the strong group is $\mathrm{Stab}_{\mathrm{SU}(4)}(\mathbb{C}e_t)$, and the coupling at the matching is the primordial one. The correct sentence is that the primordial force degenerates into the strong group and one abelian factor; the rank does not move, $3\to2+1=3$.

The weak pair cannot be debris. The rank lemma forbids rank five inside rank three, and the endpoint needs five. The two maximal-rank readings each spend exactly three, and the strong group plus weak needs four. The charge theorem forbids the sextet from reaching $Q=\pm1$: every member is at $\pm\tfrac23$, and the pair channel is neutral plus octet.

The weak pair arrives. The condensate carries a doublet index, $\Phi\in(\mathbf4,\mathbf1,\mathbf2)$, and rank is additive across factors. The index is a postulate, and it is the one new assumption. What the framework supplies is the shape of the chiral vertex, exact and nilpotent at $|g_A|=|g_V|$; what it imports is the strength, the scale and the choice of the left half.

Two senses of residual, and only one applies. The strong group is a residual in the group-theoretic sense. The weak contact is a residual in the effective sense: eliminating the $W$ gives $G_{\mathrm F}/\sqrt2=g^{2}/(8M_W^{2})$, accurate to a part per million at $100$ MeV, and the short range is a consequence of the breaking. The weak pair is not a fragment of the primordial force, and the analogy with the nuclear force holds for the contact and fails for the group.

Gravity has no slot. The fifteen are the adjoint; the graviton is not among them. Curvature is carried by a frame, and the perturbation is an $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$-valued one-form: ten symmetric components, sixteen frame components, six local Lorentz, and no single biquaternion can hold them. The quadratic action exists, the dynamics does not, and the coupling is not fixed. Gravity is not a force in the model because it was never in the connection, and the geometric reading of GR is a stronger and independent statement to the same effect.

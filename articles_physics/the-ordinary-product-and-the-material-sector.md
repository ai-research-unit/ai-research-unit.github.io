# __The Ordinary Product and the Material Sector__

## Introduction

The material sector carries the four-vectors of relativistic physics. An event is an element

$$
\tilde T = ict\,e_0 + x\,e_1 + y\,e_2 + z\,e_3,
$$

an imaginary time and three real space directions, and the operations of a relativistic system act on
such elements. This article fixes the one product by which they compose — the **ordinary product**
$\tilde P\tilde Q$, the multiplication of the algebra itself — and asks what its scalar part measures on
the material sector. The answer is one sentence: the scalar part

$$
B(\tilde P,\tilde Q) = \mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P\tilde Q) = P_0Q_0 - \mathbf P\!\cdot\!\mathbf Q
$$

is **negative** on a material **element**, $B(\tilde Q,\tilde Q)<0$, and **positive** on an
informational one, $B(\tilde P,\tilde P)>0$. It is the sign of the
sector, and it is **not** the metric of spacetime.

Keeping those two apart is the whole of the article. The metric is the interval,
$\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q)$, of signature $(-,+,+,+)$ on the material sector; it belongs
to the companion quaternionic block and to *Biquaternion Norm and Invertibility*, and it is cited here,
never re-proved. The point about $B$ is that it is **definite** where the interval is indefinite. A
definite form has no light cone, so $B$ cannot decide which events can influence which. What it carries
is the sign that separates the material sector from the informational one, and that sign is the whole of
its physical content.

The algebra carries three other products, each read in its own block and compared in *The Four Products
and Their Physical Readings: the Two Algebras and the Two Sesqualgebras*; this article needs the
ordinary one alone. The properties of $B$ — its symmetry, its non-degeneracy, its Gram matrix and its
restriction to the six distinguished subspaces — are the mathematics of *The Four Pairings of the
Biquaternion Algebra* and *The Six Subspaces under the General Plain Algebra of Biquaternions*, and are cited rather than
proved.

Throughout, $\mathbb{B}=\mathbb{C}\otimes_{\mathbb{R}}\mathbb{H}$ with basis $e_0=1,e_1,e_2,e_3$,
$e_1e_2=e_3$, $e_k^{2}=-e_0$, and central scalar imaginary $i$, $i^{2}=-1$; a general element is
$\tilde Q=Q_0e_0+Q_1e_1+Q_2e_2+Q_3e_3$ with complex coefficients; the scalar part is
$\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q)=Q_0$ and the vector part is $\mathbf Q=Q_1e_1+Q_2e_2+Q_3e_3$. The conjugations
are the complex conjugation $\bar{\cdot}$, the natural (quaternion) conjugation ${\natural}$, and the
Hermitian conjugation ${}^{*}=\bar{\cdot}\circ{\natural}$. The material sector is the anti-Hermitian
half, $\mathbb{M}_-=\{\tilde Q:\tilde Q^{*}=-\tilde Q\}$, with the real basis $\{ie_0,e_1,e_2,e_3\}$; the
informational sector is the Hermitian half, $\mathbb{M}_+=\{\tilde Q:\tilde Q^{*}=+\tilde Q\}$, with the
real basis $\{e_0,ie_1,ie_2,ie_3\}$.

## What the Material Sector Carries

A material element is written in its own coordinates as

$$
\tilde T = ict\,e_0 + x\,e_1 + y\,e_2 + z\,e_3 \in \mathbb{M}_-,
\qquad c \ \text{the speed of light}.
$$

The imaginary time is the mark of the sector: a material element is anti-fixed by the Hermitian
conjugation, $\tilde T^{*}=-\tilde T$, so the scalar coefficient carries the $i$ and the three vector
coefficients do not. The material sector is the home of the four-vectors, of the velocity, of the
momentum and of the configuration data of a relativistic system, and its own physics — the two sectors,
the coordinate dictionary and the superselection reading — is owned by *The Anti-Hermitian Subspace
$\mathbb{M}_-$ as the Material Sector*.

The form that carries the physics of the sector is the **interval**

$$
N(\tilde T,\tilde T) = \mathrm{Sc}\!\left(\tilde T^{\natural}\tilde T\right) = -c^{2}t^{2} + x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2},
$$

of signature $(-,+,+,+)$ on $\mathbb{M}_-$; its zero set is the light cone, its level set
$N=-m^{2}c^{2}$ is the mass shell, and both are owned by *Biquaternion Norm and Invertibility*. The
interval is the **general quaternionic bilinear form**, the scalar part of the second of the four products, and it
is quoted here only to be kept apart from $B$. Two forms of opposite type sit on the same sector, and the
rest of the article is about the one that does not carry the interval.

## The Ordinary Product Composes Material Operations

The **ordinary product** $\tilde P\tilde Q$ is the multiplication of the algebra. Of the four products
it is the only one that is associative and two-sidedly unital, so it is the product that makes
$\mathbb{B}$ an associative unital algebra over $\mathbb{C}$; the others are built from it and from the
conjugations. Its scalar part is the **general plain bilinear form** of this block,

$$
B(\tilde P,\tilde Q)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P\tilde Q)=P_0Q_0-P_1Q_1-P_2Q_2-P_3Q_3=P_0Q_0-\mathbf P\!\cdot\!\mathbf Q,
$$

which is symmetric, since the scalar part is invariant under cyclic permutation
$\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P\tilde Q)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q\tilde P)$, and non-degenerate, with Gram matrix
$\mathrm{E}=\mathrm{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$ in the coefficient basis. It is the scalar part of the product of
the two underlying quaternions, and those three properties are cited from the mathematics articles named
in the introduction.

**Proposed reading, labelled as such.** The ordinary product is read as the **composition of two material
operations**: $\tilde P\tilde Q$ is "the operation $\tilde Q$, then the operation $\tilde P$". Three of
its properties then carry their physical names.

- Associativity is composability: $\tilde P(\tilde Q\tilde R)=(\tilde P\tilde Q)\tilde R$, so a chain of
  operations can be bracketed at will.
- The unit $e_0$ is the identity operation: $e_0\tilde Q=\tilde Qe_0=\tilde Q$.
- Non-commutativity is order dependence: two operations need not commute, and the commutator
  $[\tilde P,\tilde Q]=\tilde P\tilde Q-\tilde Q\tilde P$ measures the failure.

What is proved is the associativity, the unit and the non-commutativity; what is proposed is that they be
read as the composition, the identity and the order of operations.

### The Commutator of Two Material Operations Is Material

The composition of two material operations has a sector structure of its own, and it is the sharpest
consequence of the anti-fixedness $\tilde Q^{*}=-\tilde Q$. For $\tilde P,\tilde Q\in\mathbb{M}_-$ one
has $(\tilde P\tilde Q)^{*}=\tilde Q^{*}\tilde P^{*}=\tilde Q\tilde P$, so the composite splits into its
Hermitian and anti-Hermitian parts,

$$
\tilde P\tilde Q=\tfrac12\bigl(\tilde P\tilde Q+\tilde Q\tilde P\bigr)+\tfrac12\bigl(\tilde P\tilde Q-\tilde Q\tilde P\bigr),
$$

that is, into the **anticommutator**, which is an element of $\mathbb{M}_+$, and the **commutator**,
which is an element of $\mathbb{M}_-$. The material sector is therefore not closed under composition, and which half survives is decided by
which relation holds: the composite is **material exactly when the two anticommute**,
$\{\tilde P,\tilde Q\}=0$, so that the informational half vanishes, and **informational exactly when
the two commute**, $[\tilde P,\tilde Q]=0$, so that the material half vanishes. A generic pair of
material operations composes to an element with one part in each sector: the informational part is the
order-independent half $\tfrac12\{\tilde P,\tilde Q\}$, and the order dependence is carried by the
material half $\tfrac12[\tilde P,\tilde Q]$, which changes sign when the two operations are
exchanged.

**Remark (verified).** On $100$ random pairs $\tilde P,\tilde Q$ of $\mathbb{M}_-$ the anticommutator
$\tfrac12(\tilde P\tilde Q+\tilde Q\tilde P)$ is fixed by the dagger and the commutator
$\tfrac12(\tilde P\tilde Q-\tilde Q\tilde P)$ is anti-fixed, both to machine precision, and their sum is
the composite $\tilde P\tilde Q$.

## The Scalar Part Is the Sector Sign

The form $B$ is negative on a material **element** and positive on an informational one, that is on the
diagonal, and that is the fact this article is built on. On the material basis $\{ie_0,e_1,e_2,e_3\}$ the diagonal entries are
$B(ie_0,ie_0)=-1$ and $B(e_k,e_k)=-1$ with all cross terms zero, so

$$
B\big|_{\mathbb{M}_-}=-\mathrm{I}_4,\ \text{signature }(0,4);
\qquad
B\big|_{\mathbb{M}_+}=+\mathrm{I}_4,\ \text{signature }(4,0);
$$

the two sectors are the two definite half-spaces of one form, with opposite signs. Written out on the
coordinates, with $\tilde Q=ib_0e_0+\mathbf q$ material ($b_0\in\mathbb{R}$, $\mathbf q$ real) and
$\tilde Q=a_0e_0+i\mathbf p$ informational ($a_0\in\mathbb{R}$, $\mathbf p$ real),

$$
B(\tilde Q,\tilde Q)=-b_0^{2}-\lvert\mathbf q\rvert^{2}<0,
\qquad
B(\tilde Q,\tilde Q)=a_0^{2}+\lvert\mathbf p\rvert^{2}>0 .
$$

**Proposed reading, labelled as such.** $B$ is read as the **sector sign**: to say "this element is
material" and to say "$B$ is negative on it" are the same statement. The sign of $B(\tilde Q,\tilde Q)$ is the same for every element of a sector, so it is invisible to a
computation that stays inside one sector; on a pair the form returns a varying real number, and it is the
sign of its diagonal that marks the sector, with no metric content at all.

**Remark (verified).** On $100$ random elements of $\mathbb{M}_-$ the value $B(\tilde Q,\tilde Q)$ is
real and strictly negative, and on $100$ random elements of $\mathbb{M}_+$ it is real and strictly
positive; the Gram matrices on the two bases are exactly $-\mathrm{I}_4$ and $+\mathrm{I}_4$.

## The Sector Sign on the Six Subspaces

Besides the two sectors the algebra carries four other distinguished real subspaces, and $B$ has a
different signature on two of them. On the **centre** $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}$ and the **vector
subspace** $\mathrm{Vect}(\mathbb{B})$, which are complex lines in each coordinate, the pairing of a real
direction with its imaginary companion is purely imaginary, and it is the real part of the form that is
read. On all four four-dimensional subspaces the form is real-valued. The restriction of $B$ is then

| subspace | $\dim_{\mathbb{R}}$ | $B$ |
|---|---|---|
| $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}$ | $2$ | $(1,1)$ |
| $\mathrm{Vect}(\mathbb{B})$ | $6$ | $(3,3)$ |
| $\mathbb{H}_{\mathbb{B}}$ | $4$ | $(1,3)$ |
| $i\mathbb{H}_{\mathbb{B}}$ | $4$ | $(3,1)$ |
| $\mathbb{M}_+$ | $4$ | $(4,0)$ |
| $\mathbb{M}_-$ | $4$ | $(0,4)$ |

Two features of the table carry physical content. First, $B$ is **indefinite on the two quaternionic
subspaces** — $(1,3)$ on $\mathbb{H}_{\mathbb{B}}$ and $(3,1)$ on $i\mathbb{H}_{\mathbb{B}}$ — so it has
a real cone there, unlike on the sectors; that cone is **not** the light cone of the framework. Second, $B$ is **neutral on the centre and on the vector subspace**, $(1,1)$ and $(3,3)$: as many
positive as negative directions, so the form is definite on neither and carries no sector sign there.

The null set of $B$ is the complex cone $\sum_\mu\varepsilon_\mu Q_\mu^{2}=0$, with
$\varepsilon=(1,-1,-1,-1)$, of real dimension six. It is not the light cone, and it is not the zero-divisor cone either: the light cone is the null set of
the interval read inside the material sector, the zero-divisor cone is the null set of the biquaternion
norm, and the null set of $B$ is a third cone, distinct from both. On the sectors, the sharpest case, $B$
is definite and has no null element beyond the origin; the material light cone is the cone of the
interval read inside $\mathbb{M}_-$ and is not a null set of $B$.

## No Material Projection: the Reference State Comes from the Informational Sector

A relativistic theory resolves a field by projecting it, and a projection is an element equal to its own
square, $\tilde\Pi^{2}=\tilde\Pi$. The material sector contains none.

**Proposition.** If $\tilde\Pi\in\mathbb{M}_-$ and $\tilde\Pi^{2}=\tilde\Pi$ then $\tilde\Pi=0$.

*Proof.* From $\tilde\Pi^{2}=\tilde\Pi$ the eigenvalues of $\tilde\Pi$ lie in $\{0,1\}$. From
$\tilde\Pi^{*}=-\tilde\Pi$ the element $i\tilde\Pi$ is Hermitian, so the eigenvalues of $\tilde\Pi$ lie on
the imaginary axis. Their intersection is $\{0\}$, and an idempotent all of whose eigenvalues vanish is
zero.

The same argument in reverse says what does carry the projections: an idempotent that is a sector element
is Hermitian, and the nontrivial ones are the minimal projections
$\tilde\Pi=\tfrac12(e_0\pm i\hat\mu)$ with $\hat\mu$ a real unit vector, a two-sphere's worth, of which
the six the coordinate basis displays are $\tfrac12(e_0\pm ie_k)$. These are the pure states of the
informational sector. The classification of the idempotents is the mathematics of *Biquaternion
Idempotents and Projections*.

**Proposed reading, labelled as such.** A material field is split against a **reference state** and not
against a material background. The reference idempotent cannot be material — there is none — so it is an
element of $\mathbb{M}_+$: a state, a pure state or the vacuum. The field decomposes into the part the
reference **resolves**, the component commuting with the projection, and the part it **does not**, the
component that flips the projection into its complement. What is proved is the absence of a nonzero
material idempotent; the identification of the resolved and unresolved parts with the measured and
unmeasured components is a reading. The idempotent in physics is owned by *The Biquaternion Vacuum as a
Minimal Idempotent* and *Decoherence as Idempotent Projection*, and the decomposition that the projection
induces is the mathematics of *Biquaternion Ideals and Peirce Decomposition*.

**Remark (verified).** On unit Euclidean elements of $\mathbb{M}_-$ the residual
$\lVert\tilde\Pi^{2}-\tilde\Pi\rVert_E$ is at least $\sqrt2$, so the idempotent equation fails on the
whole sector and its failure is bounded away from zero. On the other side $\tfrac12(e_0\pm ie_k)$ is
idempotent and Hermitian for $k=1,2,3$ and both signs, while $\tfrac12(e_0\pm e_k)$ is neither.

## Composition on Both Sides

An operation on a material element can be applied before the element or after it, and the two
possibilities are the left and the right multiplications

$$
L_{\tilde Q}(\tilde R)=\tilde Q\tilde R,\qquad R_{\tilde P}(\tilde R)=\tilde R\tilde P,
\qquad L_{\tilde Q}R_{\tilde P}=R_{\tilde P}L_{\tilde Q},
$$

which commute. The composition that the framework actually uses is the **sandwich**

$$
\Theta_{\tilde Q}(\tilde R)=\tilde Q\,\tilde R\,\tilde Q^{*}=L_{\tilde Q}R_{\tilde Q^{*}}(\tilde R),
$$

the parameter entering once on each side. It is the one action form of the framework: with a parameter of unit norm,
$\tilde\Lambda\tilde\Lambda^{\natural}=e_0$, it is a Lorentz transformation of the material sector;
with a unitary parameter, $\tilde A\tilde A^{*}=e_0$, the unitary evolution of the informational one;
and with a Hermitian idempotent parameter the measurement update. **Proposed reading, labelled as such.** The commutation of the two multiplications is read as the
independence of attaching an operation to the left of an argument and attaching one to its right, and the
sandwich as the composition in which the parameter is attached on both sides, conjugate on the two. What
is proved is the commutation and the placement of the sandwich; the reading is a naming. The adjoint of
the sandwich and its two kinds of operator are *Observables, Gauge Generators and the Chirality of the Internal Action*, and the Lorentz action is *Biquaternion Rotations and Lorentz Transformations*.

## A Worked Case

Take one material element and one informational element,

$$
\tilde Q=ie_0+2e_1\in\mathbb{M}_-,
\qquad
\tilde P=3e_0+ie_1\in\mathbb{M}_+ .
$$

The two scalar forms give

$$
B(\tilde Q,\tilde Q)=-5,\qquad B(\tilde P,\tilde P)=+10,\qquad N(\tilde Q,\tilde Q)=+3,\qquad N(\tilde P,\tilde P)=+8 .
$$

Read row by row, the case displays the whole distinction at once. $B$ changes sign between the two
elements, $-5$ material and $+10$ informational, so it is $B$ that carries the sector sign, and the
values are the negative squares $-1^{2}-2^{2}$ and $3^{2}+1^{2}$ of the coordinate dictionary. The
interval is positive on both, $+3$ and $+8$, so it does not separate the sectors. And $B$ and the interval differ by twice the complex square of the vector part,
$B(\tilde Q,\tilde Q)=N(\tilde Q,\tilde Q)-2\,\mathbf Q\!\cdot\!\mathbf Q$ with the coefficient square
$\mathbf Q\!\cdot\!\mathbf Q=Q_1^{2}+Q_2^{2}+Q_3^{2}$, which on the two elements reads
$-5=+3-2(4)$ and $+10=+8-2(-1)$: the two forms agree only when the vector part vanishes, that is on the
scalar line, and the difference $-2\,\mathbf Q\!\cdot\!\mathbf Q$ is real of a fixed sign on each sector,
negative on $\mathbb{M}_-$ and positive on $\mathbb{M}_+$.

## The Bound

The article states one cell of the grid of four, and the boundary of the cell is what it must declare.

- **$B$ is not the metric.** It is definite on each sector and has no cone there, so it cannot define the
  causal structure. The metric is the interval, of signature $(-,+,+,+)$ on $\mathbb{M}_-$.
- **$B$ is not the interval.** The interval, the mass shell, the four-velocity and invertibility are the
  level data of the general quaternionic bilinear form, owned by *Biquaternion Norm and Invertibility*; the worked
  case shows the two forms giving different numbers on the same element.
- **$B$ is not positivity.** The positive definite form of the state space and of the Born pairing is the
  Hermitian form of the sesqualgebra block, which is $+B$ on $\mathbb{M}_+$ and $-B$ on $\mathbb{M}_-$;
  positivity is blind to the sector.
- **$B$ is not the Krein form.** The indefinite metric of the fourth corner restricts to $(1,3)$ on all
  four four-dimensional subspaces and so does not separate them.

**What the framework does not claim.** The reading does not say which physical operation an element
represents, does not derive the Lorentz transformation or the measurement update from the ordinary
product, and does not claim that every physical process is a product. The Lorentz action is the sandwich
of the section above, and the measurement update is an idempotent projection; neither is re-derived here.

## The Ledger

**Proved.** The ordinary product is associative and two-sidedly unital, and its scalar part
$B(\tilde P,\tilde Q)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P\tilde Q)=P_0Q_0-\mathbf P\!\cdot\!\mathbf Q$ is a symmetric
non-degenerate $\mathbb{C}$-bilinear form with Gram matrix $\mathrm{E}=\mathrm{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$; the
material sector is anti-fixed by the dagger, hence $B$ restricts to $-\mathrm{I}_4$ of signature $(0,4)$

the two quaternionic subspaces; the product of two material elements splits into an informational
anticommutator and a material commutator and is material exactly when the two anticommute; the material sector contains no nonzero idempotent; and the
left and right multiplications commute, so the sandwich is their composition.

**Readings.** That $B$ carries the **sector sign** and the interval carries the metric; that the material
geometry belongs to this block and to its quaternionic companion and not to the sesqualgebra; that the
ordinary product is the **composition of material operations** and the sandwich the composition on both
sides; and that a material field is split against a **reference state** rather than a material
background. Each is the framework's naming of a proved algebraic fact and is labelled as such.

## Summary

The material sector $\mathbb{M}_-$ carries the four-vectors, an event being
$\tilde T=ict\,e_0+x e_1+y e_2+z e_3$, and the ordinary product $\tilde P\tilde Q$ — the only one of the
four products that is associative and two-sidedly unital — is read as the composition of two material
operations. Its scalar part $B=P_0Q_0-\mathbf P\!\cdot\!\mathbf Q$, the quaternion scalar product of the
two elements, is negative definite on
$\mathbb{M}_-$ with signature $(0,4)$ and positive definite on $\mathbb{M}_+$ with signature $(4,0)$:
opposite definite signs on the two sectors, so $B$ carries the **sector sign**. It is neutral on the
centre and the vector subspace and indefinite on the two quaternionic subspaces, and its null set is a
complex cone of real dimension six, which is not the light cone. Being definite, $B$ has no light cone at
all and cannot be the metric; the metric is the interval, the general quaternionic bilinear form of the companion
quaternionic block, of signature $(-,+,+,+)$ on the material sector. The composition of two material
operations is material exactly when the two anticommute, its informational part being the anticommutator
and its order dependence the commutator; the
material sector contains no nonzero idempotent, so the reference projection of a field comes from
$\mathbb{M}_+$ — a state and not a material background, which is the framework's reading of the
decomposition; and the left and right multiplications commute, so the sandwich
$\Theta_{\tilde Q}(\tilde R)=\tilde Q\tilde R\tilde Q^{*}$ is the composition on both sides. The
distinctions are labelled throughout: what is proved is the algebra, what is proposed is the naming.

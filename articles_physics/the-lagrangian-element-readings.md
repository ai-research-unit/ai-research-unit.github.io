# __The Lagrangian: The Element Readings__

**What the group does.** Each article exhibits one action, one variation, one structural consequence and one set of currents; each states its conjugate pair and its two settings. Together they are the family's base: every other reading is a change of variables away from one of these.

**What the group does not claim.** No quantisation, no Fock space, no scattering. The fields are one-particle fields; the second quantisation is the next category and is mapped in [The Lagrangian and the Second Quantisation: From the Action to the Commutator](the-lagrangian-and-the-second-quantisation.html).

**How to read the group.** Start with the scalar, then the spinor, then the vector: each adds one structural object — the chiral pair, then the constraint — and the fourth article removes the mass and adds the gauge orbit.

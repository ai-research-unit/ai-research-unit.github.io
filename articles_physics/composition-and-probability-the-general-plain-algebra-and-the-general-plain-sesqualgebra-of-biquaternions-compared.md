# __Composition and Probability: the General Plain Algebra and the General Plain Sesqualgebra of Biquaternions Compared__

## Introduction

Two structures live on one biquaternion algebra and share one multiplication. The **general plain
algebra** (GPA) reads the product as it stands, $\tilde P\tilde Q$; its menu label is **composition**,
and the physics band it opens is the band of the process. The **general plain sesqualgebra** (GPS)
reads the same multiplication with the Hermitian conjugation in its second slot,
$\tilde P\star\tilde Q=\tilde P\tilde Q^{*}$; its menu label is **probability**, and the band it opens
is the band of the state. This article is the physics companion of the mathematics comparison *The
General Plain Algebra and the General Plain Sesqualgebra of Biquaternions Compared*. It walks the same
sections in the same order and reads each contrast physically, citing the mathematics article for every
proof and the twelve physics articles of the pair of bands for every **physical interpretation**.

The thesis is one sentence. **The two structures are one algebra read in two products, and every
physical difference between them is the star in the second slot.** The GPA composes operations; the GPS
pairs states; and the insertion of the Hermitian conjugation in the second slot is at once the Born
conjugation of an amplitude, the definition of the adjoint of an operator, and the source of the
positivity that a state space needs. The corpus calls that insertion the step of quantisation; this
article reads its two sides together.

**Boundaries.** Nothing is re-proved here. The algebraic comparison, its identities and its witnesses
belong to the mathematics article of the same name; each **physical interpretation** belongs to its own article of
one of the two bands, and the owner is named as the sections go. What this article owns is the
transversal interpretation: which physical job each contrast supports and how the two bands divide the labour
between them. The map of all four general products is *The Four General Products and Their Physical
Readings*; this article is narrower, and it reads the pair whose first slot is untouched, the member of
the four that composes and the member that pairs.

**Conventions.** As in the two bands. The algebra is $\mathbb{B}=\mathbb{C}\otimes_{\mathbb{R}}\mathbb{H}$
with basis $e_0=1,e_1,e_2,e_3$, the relations $e_k^{2}=-e_0$ and $e_1e_2=e_3$, and central scalar
imaginary $i$. The conjugations are the coefficient conjugation $\bar{\cdot}$, the natural conjugation
${}^{\natural}$ and the Hermitian conjugation ${}^{*}=\bar{\cdot}\circ{}^{\natural}$. The scalar part is
$\mathrm{Sc}$, with $\mathrm{Tr}=2\,\mathrm{Sc}$. The four forms are
$B(\tilde P,\tilde Q)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P\tilde Q)$,
$H(\tilde P,\tilde Q)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P\tilde Q^{*})=\sum_\mu P_\mu\overline{Q_\mu}$,
$N(\tilde P,\tilde Q)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q)$ and
$K(\tilde P,\tilde Q)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q^{*})$. The biquaternion norm is
$N(\tilde Q)=\sum_\mu Q_\mu^{2}=\tilde Q\tilde Q^{\natural}$. The plain product is written by
juxtaposition and the sesquilinear product with the star; the GPS is the **derived operation** of the
pair $(\mathbb{B},{}^{*})$. The sectors are the Hermitian $\mathbb{M}_+$ and the anti-Hermitian
$\mathbb{M}_-$.

## The Two Products and the Two Jobs

The plain product is $\mathbb{C}$-bilinear, associative, and has the two-sided identity $e_0$, so the
operations on a material element compose without ambiguity and the identity operation is the map
$\tilde Q\mapsto e_0\tilde Q$. The plain sesquilinear product is conjugate-linear in its second factor;
it has a **right** identity and no left one, and its left unit slot returns the conjugation,

$$
\tilde P\star e_0=\tilde P,\qquad e_0\star\tilde X=\tilde X^{*}.
$$

Its closest thing to an identity is therefore the dagger, and it admits no two-sided unit at all.

The **physical interpretation** is the division of the two verbs of the framework. A composition needs an identity
operation, because a sequence of operations must be able to do nothing; a pairing needs none, because a
probability amplitude is evaluated and not repeated. The GPA is the band of the process, and the GPS is
the band of the state. The owner of the composition interpretation is *The General Plain Bilinear Product on the Biquaternion Space*, where the product composes operations and its scalar part composes operations rather than
values, which is why the Born pairing must be the sesquilinear product and not this one. The owner of
the probability interpretation is *The Spinor Space as a Hilbert Space of the General Plain Sesqualgebra*,
where the amplitude is the scalar part of the plain sesquilinear product and the probability is its
squared modulus. The mechanism that separates the two is the two-slot mechanism of *The Four General
Products and Their Physical Readings*: the second slot without a conjugation composes, and the second
slot with the star pairs.

## Associativity: Composition Iterates, a Pairing Does Not

The plain product is associative; the star product is neither associative nor commutative. The
witnesses are two basis triples. On $(\tilde P,\tilde Q,\tilde R)=(e_0,e_1,e_1)$ the two bracketings of
the star product differ,

$$
(e_0\star e_1)\star e_1=-e_0,\qquad e_0\star(e_1\star e_1)=+e_0,
$$

and commutativity fails on $(e_1,e_2)$, where $e_1\star e_2=-e_3$ and $e_2\star e_1=+e_3$ (both computed
on the basis). The non-commutativity is shared: the plain product is non-commutative too, and that is
the orientation of a material process.

The **physical interpretation** is that a chain of operations is well defined while a chain of amplitudes is not.
A composition can be iterated, so the acting maps form a monoid and the composition of two material
operations is again a material operation (*The General Plain Bilinear Product on the Material Sector*, and the
composition laws of *The Operators of the General Plain Bilinear Product: One-Sided, Two-Sided and the Sandwich*).
A pairing can only be evaluated: the Born rule squares one amplitude into one probability, and there is
no third slot in which a probability could be paired again. The failure of the star product to compose
is the operator form of that fact, and it is the parity asymmetry of *The Operators of the Plain
Sesquilinear Product and the Born Pairing*, where the left family does not close under composition and
no associative operator algebra of the probabilistic band exists. Read one level up, this is the
division between a classical rule for combining and a quantum rule for evaluating, and it is the first
place the quantisation step is visible.

## The Involution: the Adjoint of an Operation and the Polarity of an Amplitude

The Hermitian conjugation ${}^{*}$ is an anti-automorphism of the plain product,
$(\tilde P\tilde Q)^{*}=\tilde Q^{*}\tilde P^{*}$, and it exchanges the two sectors,
${}^{*}:\mathbb{M}_+\leftrightarrow\mathbb{M}_-$. On the star product it acts by the reversal law

$$
(\tilde P\star\tilde Q)^{*}=\tilde Q\star\tilde P,
$$

computed on the basis: the conjugation of an amplitude reverses its two slots. On the operators the two
faces of the same involution separate. The Hermitian dagger of a one-sided operator keeps the side and
conjugates the parameter, $(L_{\tilde A})^{*}=L_{\tilde A^{*}}$, and the plain transpose of a left
multiplication moves it to the right one, $(L_{\tilde A})^{\approx}=R_{\tilde A}$.

The **physical interpretation** is one involution with two jobs. On the algebra the star conjugates a process: it
is the adjoint of an operation, and it is where a reversible evolution and a mass term part company
(*Observables, Gauge Generators and the Chirality of the Internal Action*, with the rule
$\Theta_{\tilde Q}^{\dagger}=\Theta_{\tilde Q^{*}}$). On the pairing the star reverses the two slots of
an amplitude: it is the polarity that distinguishes the state from the observable, and the two slots
carry opposite parity because one is linear and the other conjugate-linear (*The Operators of the Plain
Sesquilinear Product and the Born Pairing*, and the interpretation of the conjugated slot as the state in *The
Spinor Space as a Hilbert Space of the General Plain Sesqualgebra*). The same operation that conjugates
a charge in the composition band swaps a bra against a ket in the probability band, and the reversal law
above is that coincidence written once.

## Norm and Invertibility: the Interval and the Rank

The biquaternion norm is multiplicative for the plain product and twisted for the star product,

$$
N(\tilde P\tilde Q)=N(\tilde P)\,N(\tilde Q),\qquad
N(\tilde P\star\tilde Q)=N(\tilde P)\,\overline{N(\tilde Q)},
$$

both computed on random samples. The plain product is invertible exactly for $N(\tilde Q)\neq0$, with
the two-sided inverse $\tilde Q^{\natural}/N(\tilde Q)$; the star product has no two-sided unit and
instead a **one-sided** inverse

$$
\tilde X^{-1\star}=\frac{\bar{\tilde X}}{\overline{N(\tilde X)}},
$$

which solves both $\tilde X\star\tilde R=e_0$ and $\tilde R\star\tilde X=e_0$ when $N(\tilde X)\neq0$.
The two sesquilinear squares of one element share their scalar part,
$\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q\star\tilde Q)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q^{*}\star\tilde Q)=\sum_\mu\lvert Q_\mu\rvert^{2}$.

The **physical interpretation** is that invertibility means two different things in the two bands. In the GPA the
norm is the interval: a composite process has the product of the intervals of its factors, the unit-norm
slice is the group of motions, and the level set of the norm is the mass shell. In the GPS the norm law
returns a modulus and not a value, there is no unit, and what takes the place of invertibility is
positivity and rank. The owner of the first interpretation is *Biquaternion Norm and Invertibility*, with the
interval and the mass shell; the owner of the second is *Mass, Rank and the Positivity of the Dagger*,
where the rank of the sesquilinear square is the framework's mass label and its positivity is the state
condition, and where the two squares of one element are shown to be the same number read in two ledgers.
The sharpening is that the GPA's invertibility is a statement about the **boundary of the cone**, below,
while the GPS's replacement for it is a statement about the **sign of a form**.

## Zero Divisors: the Light Cone and the Absence of a Null Probability

The zero divisors are the nonzero elements with $N=0$, equivalently the elements whose left
multiplication has no inverse. The set is shared by the two bands. What differs is the nilpotent
structure on it. In the GPA the square-zero elements are exactly the pure isotropic cone, and their
index is two,

$$
\tilde Q^{2}=0\quad(\tilde Q\neq0)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \tilde Q\ \text{pure and isotropic}.
$$

In the GPS there is no nonzero element whose square vanishes, because the scalar part of a sesquilinear
square is a sum of moduli,

$$
\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde X\star\tilde X)=\sum_\mu\lvert X_\mu\rvert^{2},
$$

computed on random samples, and the same pure isotropic cone is now nilpotent of **index three**,
$\star^{2}\neq0$ and $\star^{3}=0$. The image of the sesquilinear square is the Hermitian cone
$\{\alpha e_0+i\mathbf w:\alpha\geq\lvert\mathbf w\rvert\}$, with
$\alpha^{2}-\lvert\mathbf w\rvert^{2}=\lvert N(\tilde X)\rvert^{2}$, and its boundary is the image of
the zero divisors.

The **physical interpretation** is that the light cone is a boundary of the **composition** and not of the
probability. A massless four-momentum is a zero divisor: it is not invertible for the composition, and
that is what propagation at the speed of light means algebraically (*Square Roots: the Local Complex
Structure, the Light Cone and the Mass Shell*, where the roots of $0$ are the nilpotents of the cone).
Its probability, however, is not zero. The Hermitian square of a zero divisor is a nonzero Hermitian
element on the boundary of the positive cone, of rank one, so a massless element carries a well-defined
boundary state and not a null state; the probability pairing is definite and never sees the cone as a
locus of vanishing. The rank is a count of polarisations and the boundary is the massless slice (*Mass,
Rank and the Positivity of the Dagger*), and the positive cone is the state cone of *The Spinor Space as
a Hilbert Space of the General Plain Sesqualgebra*. The built example is the photon. Its four-momentum
is a null element of the material sector, and the lightlike momentum is, up to the scale $2iE/c$, a
rank-one idempotent of the informational sector, so the massless momentum and a pure state are one
element read in two sectors (*The Null Four-Momentum: What the Cone Derives and What It Does
Not*). What the cone derives is the shell and the rank; it does not derive the reduction of the four
polarisation directions of the material sector to the two transverse ones, which is an inserted gauge
condition, and the photon article keeps the two apart. The index shift from two to three is the sharpest
single contrast of the comparison: lightlike propagation is square-zero in the composition and
cubically-zero in the pairing.

## Idempotents: the Vacuum and the Pure State

The plain idempotents are the family

$$
\tilde\Pi=\tfrac12(e_0+\eta),\qquad \eta^{2}=e_0,\qquad N(\tilde\Pi)=0,
$$

computed on samples of the family: each is a zero divisor, and each generates a minimal left ideal. The
Hermitian members of the family, $\eta=i\hat\mu$ with $\hat\mu$ a real unit vector, are the pure states.
The material sector contains no nonzero idempotent at all, verified on random anti-Hermitian elements.
The *sesquilinear* idempotents of the star product are exactly the Hermitian idempotents, the two-sphere
$\tfrac12(e_0+i\hat\mu)$, which are the orthogonal projections of the matrix model.

The **physical interpretation** separates the two kinds of projection the framework uses. A **vacuum** is an
algebraic idempotent: it is a generator of a minimal left ideal, it is a module datum, and it is chosen
by the algebra and not by a metric. A **pure state** as a projector is a metric idempotent: it is
self-adjoint for the probability form, and the measurement update, which replaces a state by its
projection, is idempotent and belongs to this band. The owner of the module interpretation is *The Spinor Space
as a Module of the General Plain Algebra* (the idempotent, the nilpotent and the ideal) and *The
Complementary Space of the Spinor Space in the General Plain Algebra* (the second vacuum and the
second copy of the state space); the owner of the metric interpretation is *The Complementary Space of the
Spinor Space in the General Plain Sesqualgebra*, where the second copy is the orthogonal complement for
the form. That the material sector carries no idempotent is the statement that no material operation is
a projection onto the material sector, and it is read in *The General Plain Bilinear Product on the Material Sector*.

## Square Roots: the Clock, the Cone and the Positivity

The square map has different root sets in the two bands, and they are the two bands in one table. In the
GPA the roots of $-e_0$ are the local complex structures: the central $\pm ie_0$ together with the pure
complex vectors of square one, of which the equation $\mathbf X\cdot\mathbf X=1$ is the condition (both
computed). The roots of $0$ are the nilpotents of the light cone. The roots of $+e_0$ are the
involutions and reality conditions. The mass shell is the slice of the same map on which the square of a
four-momentum is a fixed scalar. In the GPS the root set collapses. There is **no** root of $-e_0$,
because the scalar part of a square is a sum of moduli and cannot be negative; the root of $0$ is the
origin alone; and the roots of $+e_0$ are exactly the unitary group $U(2)$, the slice of reversible
operations (all three computed).

The **physical interpretation** is that the clock belongs to the composition and not to the pairing. The square
root of $-e_0$ is the quarter turn that carries the clock, and it is present in the algebra and absent
from the state space: the phase of quantum theory is the phase of the **amplitude**, not a complex
structure of the state. The interpretation of the three distinguished radicands as the three physical cases
(the local complex structure, the light cone and the mass shell) is *Square Roots: the Local Complex
Structure, the Light Cone and the Mass Shell*. The roots of $+e_0$ are the reversible operations, and
their identification with the unitary slice is the operator face of *Observables, Gauge Generators and

Read as one statement, the GPA supplies a local complex structure and a cone, and the GPS supplies
positivity and a unitary group, and neither set is the other.

## The Form: the Sector Sign and the Probability Weight

Each band reads one form. The plain bilinear form $B=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P\tilde Q)$ is indefinite: its
realified Gram matrix has signature $(4,4)$, and on the sectors it is definite with opposite signs,
$-\mathrm{I}_4$ on $\mathbb{M}_-$ and $+\mathrm{I}_4$ on $\mathbb{M}_+$ (signature computed). The
Hermitian form $H=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P\tilde Q^{*})$ is positive definite: its realified Gram matrix is
the identity, of signature $(8,0)$, computed directly. The interval and the probability are therefore
carried by two different forms, and neither is the other.

The **physical interpretation** is that the passage from the GPA to the GPS is the passage from a form that
**sorts** to a form that **weights**. The plain form is the sector sign: it is negative on a material
element and positive on an informational one, it is definite on each sector and therefore carries no
cone, and it is not the metric of spacetime (*The General Plain Bilinear Product on the Material Sector*, where the
interval is kept apart from the sector sign). The Hermitian form is the probability weight: it is the
Gram form of the state space and the Born pairing, positive on every subspace (*The Spinor Space as a
Hilbert Space of the General Plain Sesqualgebra*, and *Mass, Rank and the Positivity of the Dagger*,
where the positivity of the dagger is a theorem). The interval is neither form: it is the quaternionic
form of the sibling band, and the statement that probability is positive while the interval is
indefinite, and that the two never trade places, is exactly *The Bochner Theorem for the Biquaternion
Transform: the Hermitian Form and the Root*, where the positive measure returns its positivity through
$H$ and never through $N$. The causal-content ordering of the four forms, and the rule that the cone is
the zero set of the norm and not of the plain form, are *Conventions in the Biquaternion Universe*.

## The Operators: the Adjoint that Swaps and the Adjoint that Conjugates

The operator layers of the two bands are built from the left and right multiplications, and the adjoint
is the one thing the composition laws do not give. In the GPA the composition laws are the monoid laws

$$
L_{\tilde A}L_{\tilde B}=L_{\tilde A\tilde B},\qquad
R_{\tilde A}R_{\tilde B}=R_{\tilde B\tilde A},\qquad
L_{\tilde A}R_{\tilde B}=R_{\tilde B}L_{\tilde A},
$$

computed on samples, and the plain form is adjoint for the **transpose**, which swaps the two sides
without conjugating the parameter,

$$
B(L_{\tilde A}\tilde X,\tilde Y)=B(\tilde X,R_{\tilde A}\tilde Y),
$$

computed. No nonzero self-adjoint one-sided operator appears except the central ones, so the plain band
supplies no generator. In the GPS the right multiplications compose,
$R^{\star}_{\tilde A}R^{\star}_{\tilde B}=R^{\star}_{\tilde A\tilde B}$, and the Hermitian form is
adjoint for the **dagger**, which conjugates the parameter and keeps the side,

$$
H(R^{\star}_{\tilde A}\tilde X,\tilde Y)=H(\tilde X,R^{\star}_{\tilde A^{*}}\tilde Y),
$$

computed.

The **physical interpretation** is that physical hermiticity lives in the probability band. A left multiplication
by a Hermitian element is self-adjoint and is read as an **observable**; a left multiplication by an
anti-Hermitian element is skew-adjoint and is read as a **gauge generator**, exponentating to the
internal unitary group; the sandwich $\tilde Q\tilde P\tilde Q^{*}$ is an action and not an operator of
either kind (*Observables, Gauge Generators and the Chirality of the Internal Action*, with the
operator layer in *The Operators of the Plain Sesquilinear Product and the Born Pairing*). The plain
adjoint is a relation that cannot tell a left attachment from a right one, and its consequence is that
the general plain bilinear product composes processes but does not infinitesimally generate them, the generation
being the bracket of the antisymmetric band (*The Operators of the General Plain Bilinear Product: One-Sided,
Two-Sided and the Sandwich*). The compactness that makes the internal charge discrete, and the
non-compactness that leaves the rapidity continuous, are read off the same operator layer in *Particle
Types, Discrete Charge and Three-Particle Couplings*. The two adjoints are the two physical questions:
the GPA adjoint asks what a relation is symmetric for, and the GPS adjoint asks what an observable is.

## The Matrix Models: the Product and the Product with the Adjoint

In the $2\times2$ model $\mathsf{M}_2$ with $\mathsf{M}_2(e_k)=-i\sigma_k$ the two bands are two
interpretations of one matrix algebra,

$$
\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde P\tilde Q)=\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde P)\,\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q),\qquad
\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde P\star\tilde Q)=\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde P)\,\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q)^{\dagger},
$$

both computed, together with $\operatorname{Tr}\mathsf{M}_2=2\mathrm{Sc}$ and
$\det\mathsf{M}_2=N(\tilde Q)$. The sesquilinear form is the Hilbert–Schmidt pairing of the model,

$$
\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}\!\bigl(\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde P)\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q)^{\dagger}\bigr)
=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P\tilde Q^{*}),
$$

computed. The determinant separates the two squares: $\det\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q^{2})=N(\tilde Q)^{2}$
against $\det\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q\star\tilde Q)=\lvert N(\tilde Q)\rvert^{2}$.

The **physical interpretation** is that the algebra is a matrix algebra and the sesqualgebra is the same matrix
algebra with the adjoint woven in. The GPA interpretation is matrix multiplication, the composition of
processes. The GPS interpretation is multiplication followed by the adjoint, the positive semidefinite square
$MM^{\dagger}$, which is the density-matrix square and the Born rule. The trace factor two is the count
of the two minimal left ideals, that is, of the two chiralities (*Conventions in the Biquaternion
Universe*), and the image of the sesquilinear square is the cone of positive matrices, the state cone of
*The Spinor Space as a Hilbert Space of the General Plain Sesqualgebra* and *Mass, Rank and the
Positivity of the Dagger*. One matrix algebra therefore carries both the non-commutative composition of
operations and the positive pairing of states, and the difference between them is exactly one dagger.

## Physical Interpretation

Read as a whole, the comparison is the interpretation that the framework's algebra is the algebra of processes
and its sesqualgebra is the pairing of states, and that the two are one structure with one dial. The
dial is the Hermitian conjugation in the second slot; the corpus calls the step of inserting it
quantisation. Three transversal interpretations follow. First, the dial is a **sorting** datum as well as a
positivity datum: the sector exchange and a change of the local complex structure move the bilinear
forms and leave the sesquilinear ones fixed, so the pair of bands is itself sorted by the criterion of
*Conventions in the Biquaternion Universe*. Second, the dial is where the **clock** enters: the quarter
turn of the local complex structure lives in the composition and is absent from the pairing, so the
phase of the framework is the phase of an amplitude and the state carries no clock of its own (*Each
Sector Is the Other's Clock: the Sector Exchange as Relational Time*, *Square Roots: the Local Complex
Structure, the Light Cone and the Mass Shell*). Third, the dial is where **positivity** enters, and it
enters once: the probability form is the same form whose algebraic norm is the squared modulus, whose trace is
the Born rule and whose cone is the state cone, so the Bochner positivity of the transform and the
Hilbert structure of the state space are one fact (*The Bochner Theorem for the Biquaternion Transform:
the Hermitian Form and the Root*, *Mass, Rank and the Positivity of the Dagger*). The comparison adds no
measure, no dynamics and no new degree of freedom; it is the interpretation of where the composition band ends
and the probability band begins.

## Summary

The general plain algebra and the general plain sesqualgebra of biquaternions are one algebra read in
two products. The plain product $\tilde P\tilde Q$ is associative, two-sidedly unital and bilinear, and
its physics is composition: operations that iterate, an identity operation, a norm that multiplies and
an interval that a change of frame moves. The plain sesquilinear product is
$\tilde P\star\tilde Q=\tilde P\tilde Q^{*}$; it is neither associative nor commutative, has only a
right identity and returns the conjugation from its left unit slot, and its physics is probability:
amplitudes that are evaluated and not iterated, a positive definite form that weights states, a
one-sided inverse, and a cone of positive elements that is the state cone. The comparison traces every
difference to one cause, the Hermitian conjugation in the second slot, which is at once the Born
conjugation of an amplitude, the definition of the adjoint of an operator, and the source of positivity:
the unit, associativity, multiplicativity of the norm, the idempotents, the square roots, the form, and
the one-sided operator laws all differ there,
while the norm and its vanishing criterion, the zero-divisor cone and the four-dimensional space are
shared. The two forms are the two ends of the step, the sector sign $B$ of signature $(4,4)$ and the
probability weight $H$ of signature $(8,0)$; the interval $N$ is neither. Read physically, the GPA is
where the clock and the light cone live and the GPS is where the observables, the unitary evolution and
the Born rule live, and the step between them is the framework's quantisation.

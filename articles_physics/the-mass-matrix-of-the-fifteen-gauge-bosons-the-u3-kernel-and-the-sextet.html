The Mass Matrix of the Fifteen Gauge Bosons: The $\mathfrak{u}(3)$ Kernel, the Sextet at $32v^{2}$, and No Mixing

Introduction

The model gauges fifteen directions, and the matrix that gives them mass has two blocks. Eight colour directions and one $B-L$ direction stay massless; six leptoquark directions take $32v^{2}$ at the transition. $8+1+6=15$.

The model. One model: the $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ model, set out in The $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ Model: Four Colours, Fifteen Generators, One Declaration. Its gauge content is the fifteen generators of $\mathfrak{su}(4)$ and nothing else. There is no weak factor in the algebra and no weak gauge field in the ledger: $W^{\pm}$, $Z$, $Z_R$ and the photon are not directions of this model. They arrive at $M_C$ with the doublet index of the condensate, and their masses are computed below the transition, in The Weak Sector Below $M_C$.

What the article computes, and what it borrows. It states the two blocks, derives the sextet's mass from the adjoint action of the vacuum, and shows that the kernel is nine-dimensional. The entry-by-entry leakage is The Breaking of the Primordial Force's; the block structure is stated here once because every later article quotes it.

The one sentence worth carrying away. The adjoint action of the vacuum separates the fifteen into a kernel and a sextet:

$$ \operatorname{ad}(iD):\quad 0^{9}\ \oplus\ (-4i)^{3}\ \oplus\ (+4i)^{3}, \qquad 15=9+6 . $$

The Two Blocks

The split. The fifteen directions of the ledger are sorted by what the vacuum does to them:

block generators colour charge mass
colour $8$ $\mathbf8$ $0$ $0$
$B-L$ $1$ $\mathbf1$ $0$ $0$ at $M_C$
leptoquark $6$ $\mathbf3$ and $\bar{\mathbf3}$ $\pm\tfrac23$ $32v^{2}$

$8+1+6=15$. Two blocks, three rows: the two massless rows are the kernel and they are counted separately because they are zero for two different reasons.

Why the split is exact. A mass term is quadratic in two generators and linear in the vacuum that produces it. Every vacuum of the chain is a colour singlet with $Q=0$, so the mass term is invariant under colour and under electric charge, and it vanishes between two generators whose colour representations or charges differ. The blocks are the eigenspaces of (colour representation, $Q$), and no correction that preserves colour and charge can move a mass across them.

What the split is not. It is not a statement about ideals. $\mathfrak{u}(3)=\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{u}(1)$ is the stabiliser of the vacuum, and the sextet is its complement as a vector space; neither is an ideal of $\mathfrak{su}(4)$, and the sextet is not closed under the bracket. The split is a statement about the vacuum's quantum numbers, and it is the reason each block can be computed on its own.

Why there are two blocks and not four. A count of four blocks would count the weak directions, and the weak directions are not in the fifteen. The four rows that a left–right ledger prints — colour, leptoquark, neutral, charged — mix two scales and two different lists of fields; the two blocks printed here are one scale and one list.

The Colour Block: Eight Zeros

The eight colour directions are massless at every scale, exactly. Two reasons, and both are exact.

The first vacuum. $D=\operatorname{diag}(-3,1,1,1)$ is a multiple of the identity on the colour triple, so every colour generator commutes with it: the colour planes have $\lambda_j=\lambda_k$ and the two colour diagonals commute with it. A generator that commutes with the vacuum has zero mass, which is the kernel statement of The Breaking of the Primordial Force.

The second vacuum. $H$ is a colour singlet, so its vacuum expectation value does not couple to a colour generator at all. The same holds for $\Phi$, whose vacuum lies along the lepton line, and the lepton line is a colour singlet.

The consequence. The $8\times8$ colour block is zero, not small, and colour is unbroken at every step of the chain. This is the matrix statement of the corpus's claim that colour is a stabiliser at every scale.

The $B-L$ Direction: The Ninth Zero

The corner is the vacuum's own direction, and its mass term vanishes identically. $D=\operatorname{diag}(-3,1,1,1)$ is the $B-L$ generator up to normalisation, so the mass functional $2v^{2}\langle[D,[D,A]],A\rangle$ at $A=D$ is $2v^{2}\langle[D,[D,D]],D\rangle=0$. The ninth zero is not a stabiliser accident but the statement that $B'$ is the direction the vacuum points along.

What happens to it later. $B'$ is massless at $M_C$ and it acquires a mass only through the mixing that forms the photon, which is below the transition and is The Weak Sector Below $M_C$'s computation. Its masslessness here is not a claim about the low-energy spectrum; it is the statement that the fifteen have nine zeros at the transition.

The Leptoquark Block: Six at $32v^{2}$

The six leptoquark directions are the six planes through the lepton line, and they all take the same mass at the first breaking. In the physics basis the block is $32v^{2}$ times the identity of dimension six:

$$ M^{2}_{\text{lepto}}=32v^{2}\,\mathrm I_{6},\qquad 32=2\cdot4^{2},\qquad 4=1-(-3). $$

The leakage is the entry-by-entry computation of The Breaking of the Primordial Force; the reason is the identity of The Breaking of the Primordial Force, which says that the mass on a plane is twice the square of the vacuum's eigenvalue difference across it. The six are equal because the vacuum has one lepton line and three equal colour lines, so all six differences are the same number.

The later breaking adds nothing here. $H$ is colour-blind, and the leptoquarks have already left the unbroken algebra at $M_C$. The block stays $32v^{2}$ at tree level; the one-loop shift is the effective-potential article's.

The Count

Two counts, one matrix. The blocks give the massless and the massive count without a second ledger:

$$ \underbrace{8}_{\text{colour}}+\underbrace{1}_{B-L}=9\ \text{massless},\qquad \underbrace{6}_{\text{leptoquark}}=6\ \text{massive}, \qquad 9+6=15 . $$

The nine. Eight zeros are a stabiliser and the ninth is the vacuum's own direction, so the two kinds of zero are different statements that happen to be equal in number to the rank of $\mathfrak{u}(3)$.

The six. One scale, one eigenvalue, one reason: the sextet is exactly the set of planes the lepton line crosses, and every one of them crosses it with the same difference $1-(-3)$.

The rank. $6$ at the transition, and $9$ remains the kernel; the matrix has no other eigenvalue in the minimal content, because no other direction exists.

What the Article Does Not Do

It does not fix a coupling. $g_4$ is symbolic, and so is the matching that produces $g_3$ and $g_{B-L}$ below it.

It does not compute the sextet's mass beyond tree level. Threshold corrections at $M_C$ are the effective-potential article's.

It does not treat the weak bosons. They are not generators of the model and they have no row in this matrix. Their masses, their mixing and the bidoublet that would mix them are The Weak Sector Below $M_C$'s.

It does not treat a $(\mathbf{15},2,2)$ bidoublet. Its components include colour octets, and a vacuum expectation value in them breaks colour directly; then the colour block is no longer zero and the two-block split is lost. That is a different model, not a correction to this one.

Summary

Two blocks. Colour $8$ and $B-L$ $1$ massless, leptoquark $6$ at $32v^{2}$; $8+1+6=15$, one scale, one list of fields.

Nine zeros, two reasons. The eight colour directions are a stabiliser of the vacuum; the $B-L$ direction is the vacuum itself. The rank at the transition is six, and the kernel is the nine-dimensional $\mathfrak{u}(3)$.

The split is exact. Every vacuum of the chain is a colour singlet of definite charge, so no mass crosses a block at any order.

The weak directions are absent, and their absence is the model. $W^{\pm}$, $Z$, $Z_R$ and the photon are not cells of this matrix: they arrive at $M_C$ with the doublet index of the condensate, and their masses are the weak sector's computation, not this one's.

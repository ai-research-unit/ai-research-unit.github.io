# __The Biquaternioid Antisymmetric Plain Sesqualgebra (APS) over $\mathbb{C}$__

## Introduction

The biquaternioid complex space carries eleven structures beside the ordinary product. This article is about the **antisymmetric plain sesqualgebra**, written $\mathrm{APS}$, whose product is the antisymmetrisation of the plain sesquilinear product, $\tilde P\star\tilde Q=\tfrac12(\tilde P\tilde Q^{*}-\tilde Q^{*}\tilde P)$. It is the antisymmetric sibling of the probability structure, and its job in the framework is **phase**: its values lie in the vector subspace, so it is the imaginary part of the Born pairing, and it carries the phase of an amplitude.

The mathematics of the structure is owned by the group *The Biquaternioid Antisymmetric Plain Sesqualgebra (APS) over $\mathbb{C}$* of the mathematics menu. This article proves nothing. It lists the results, each with the mathematics article that owns it, and it keeps the physical readings the framework gives the structure, every one of them labelled as a reading.

**Conventions.** The algebra is $\mathbb{B}=\mathbb{C}\otimes_{\mathbb{R}}\mathbb{H}$ with basis $e_t,\vec e_x,\vec e_y,\vec e_z$, $e_t=1$, $\vec e_k^{2}=-e_t$, $\vec e_x\vec e_y=\vec e_z$, and central imaginary $i$. An element is $\tilde Q=\sum_\mu Q_\mu e_\mu$ with $Q_\mu\in\mathbb{C}$. The vector subspace is $\mathrm{Vect}(\mathbb{B})=\{Q_t=0\}$; the conjugations are the natural one $\tilde Q^{\natural}$, the coefficientwise one $\bar{\tilde Q}$ and the star $\tilde Q^{*}=\bar{\tilde Q}^{\natural}$; the sectors are $\mathbb{M}_+=\{Q^{*}=Q\}$ and $\mathbb{M}_-=\{Q^{\flat}=Q\}$ with $\flat=-{}^{*}$. All conventions are those of *Conventions in the Biquaternion Universe*.

## Physical Interpretation

- **The imaginary part of the Born pairing reads as interference and, on the clock, as the carrier of the phase.** Its values lie in the vector subspace, so it is the part of the pairing that carries a direction; read against the exchange it is the part the central generator moves, and the phase of an amplitude is where the clock of the framework enters a measurement.
- **The antisymmetric part is why a pairing is not a metric.** Interference cannot be read off the real part alone, and the two halves of one amplitude are one number and one axis: the probability and the direction of the phase.
- **The Jacobi failure is the framework's statement that interference has no symmetry algebra of its own.** The bracket does not close, so the phase structure of the state space carries no group; read against the clock, the failure is not a loss, because the phase itself is supplied by the central rotation and what is missing is a Lie algebra of phases rather than the phase.
- **A pairing and no symmetry algebra.** An interference term is assembled from two amplitudes and is a relational datum, not an infinitesimal transformation; the failure is the reason the antisymmetric sesquilinear form is a structure of interference and not a gauge algebra, the gauge algebra being carried by the quaternionic row.
- **The phase operator has one handedness.** The right family is the negative mirror of the left, and the antisymmetry ties the two together; the adjoint conjugates the phase of the parameter without moving the operation to the other side, and the only self-adjoint one-sided phase operators are the central ones, the two-sided ones being the quadratic representations.
- **One value read on one state splits into a central number and a vector.** For a state $\tilde\varrho=\tfrac12(e_t+i\vec r)$ the algebraic norm of the block is the Bloch vector $\tfrac12 i\vec r$ while the algebraic norm of the companion band is the purity, so the probability and the axis of the phase are one state read twice. The unitary sandwich mirrors the algebra order and agrees with the ordinary conjugation on the state side, so the phase action on a state is unambiguous.
- **The block reads the relative phase, which carries no current.** The absolute, central phase belongs to the material generator of the corpus and not to this band; what survives here is the positive form, so the phase vector has a definite length, and the cross-product algebra of the real rotations, so the block does have an infinitesimal symmetry of another kind.

## Summary

The biquaternioid antisymmetric plain sesqualgebra is the structure $\tilde P\star\tilde Q=\tfrac12(\tilde P\tilde Q^{*}-\tilde Q^{*}\tilde P)$ over $\mathbb{C}$, the phase band of the framework: antisymmetric, valued in the vector subspace, without a unit, with a non-vanishing norm and with the Jacobi identity failing. It is the imaginary part of the Born pairing, it carries the relative phase of an amplitude and no current of its own, and it supplies a pairing without a symmetry algebra.

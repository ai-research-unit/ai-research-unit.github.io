# __The Yukawa Block Above $M_C$: Two Matrices, Eight Numbers and No Phase__

## Introduction

**The question.** Rows $10$ and $11$ are the two Yukawa structures. **How many matrices does the group require, how many numbers do they carry, and does the sector violate $CP$?**

**The answer.**

$$
\bar{\mathbf4}\otimes\mathbf4=\mathbf{15}\oplus\mathbf1,
\qquad
\text{two contractions},\qquad
16-8=8\ \text{numbers},\qquad
\text{no phase}.
$$

**Two matrices because the product has two singlets; eight numbers because one flavour group serves both; no phase because diagonal-ising what remains leaves no room for one.**

## Two Structures and Not One

**The product $\bar{\mathbf4}\otimes\mathbf4$ reduces to the adjoint plus the singlet.**

$$
\bar{\mathbf4}\otimes\mathbf4=\mathbf{15}\oplus\mathbf1,
\qquad
\Xi=\Xi_{0}+\tfrac14\bigl(\operatorname{tr}\Xi\bigr)\mathbb1 .
$$

**Each singlet contracts with one piece of $\Xi$, so there are two invariant Yukawa structures.**

$$
\mathcal{L}_{Y}=y_{\alpha\beta}\,\bar\xi^{\alpha}\Xi_{0}\,\xi^{\beta}+y'_{\alpha\beta}\,\bar\xi^{\alpha}\,\tfrac14\bigl(\operatorname{tr}\Xi\bigr)\xi^{\beta}+\text{h.c.}
$$

**The traceless contraction uses the $\mathbf{15}$; the trace contraction uses the singlet.** A model that wrote only $y\,\bar\xi\Xi\xi$ would have **tied** $y'=\tfrac14y$, and the tie is a choice and not a group-theoretic fact; [the census](the-census-above-m-c-nineteen-structures-eighteen-admissible-eleven-written.html) prices it at a row and six parameters.

## Eight Numbers, Not Sixteen and Not Four

**A single matrix first, because the pair is not twice it.**

| step | real numbers | removal |
|---|---|---|
| $y$ as written | $8$ | — |
| the copy basis, $\mathrm{U}(2)\times\mathrm{U}(2)$ | $-8$ | $y\to W^{\dagger}yV$ |
| a generic $y$'s stabiliser | $+2$ | the two diagonal phases |
| **physical, one matrix** | $\mathbf2$ | the two singular values |

**Two and not four.** A count by rephasing alone removes four and reports four; **it double-counts, because the copy basis is unobservable and the gauge group is blind to it** ($[X\otimes\mathbb1_{2},\mathbb1_{4}\otimes M]=0$). **The singular-value decomposition is the right count, and it exists for every $2\times2$ matrix.**

**The pair then carries eight.**

$$
16-8=8 .
$$

**A generic pair has no stabiliser** — the diagonalisation a single matrix admits is spent once, and the second matrix's rotation is then fixed. **So the pair is not two plus two and not sixteen:** it is the two singular values of $y$, the two of $y'$, and the four numbers in the relative unitary $y'y^{-1}$ — **two magnitudes, one angle and three phases.**

**Eight is the number the theory carries, and two is the number the corpus writes**, because the corpus writes one matrix and the tie is its choice.

## No Phase, and Why

**$CP$ conservation is a count here and not an assumption.**

**One matrix can be made diagonal and real by a bi-unitary transformation.** With $y\to W^{\dagger}yV$ and $W,V\in\mathrm{U}(2)$, the singular-value decomposition gives $y=\operatorname{diag}(y_{1},y_{2})$ with $y_{1,2}\ge0$, **real and positive by convention.** No phase survives.

**Four real numbers become two, and the two are magnitudes.** A phase would require an invariant that is odd under complex conjugation, **and there is none**: the only invariant of a single matrix under bi-unitary transformations is its spectrum of singular values, **which is real.**

**The corpus's $CP$ conservation is therefore structural.** The sector conserves $CP$ because it has one matrix and no second source of a phase; **the eight numbers of the untied pair contain three phases, and a model that wrote both matrices would violate $CP$ unless those three vanished.**

**This is a prediction and a limitation.** The model's $CP$ conservation is falsifiable in the same way its mass ratio is — **by being exact where the world is not.**

## The One Quantitative Statement

**The condensate is $\langle\Xi\rangle=v\operatorname{diag}(-3,1,1,1)$, and it makes the masses unequal across the quartet.**

$$
m_{\ell}=3m_{q}\quad\text{at equal singular value},
$$

**a factor of three from the lepton line against the spatial lines.** That ratio is the sector's whole quantitative content, **and [the Yukawa sector's article](the-yukawa-sector-of-the-su4-model-one-matrix-and-its-diagonalisation.html) shows it to be wrong by a factor of twenty-seven against the electron and the down quark.**

**So the sector is falsified and says so.** That is not a defect of the counting; **it is the counting's consequence**, and the model's honesty about it is the reason the census is worth writing.

## What the Block Does Not Contain

**No generation mixing.** There is no generation index; **the copy index is a two-valued degeneracy**, so there is no CKM matrix and no PMNS matrix above $M_C$.

**No right-handed neutrino mass.** A Majorana mass would need a symmetric contract of $\bar\xi\bar\xi$, **which the corpus declines**, and there is no singlet fermion to carry it.

**No $CP$-violating phase**, for the reason above.

**No second Higgs.** A second scalar would bring its own Yukawa matrices, **and the census has exactly two**, both on the same $\Xi$.

## Checks

1. The product. $\bar{\mathbf4}\otimes\mathbf4=\mathbf{15}\oplus\mathbf1$, two singlets.
2. The structures. Two, one per singlet.
3. The single matrix. $8-8+2=2$ singular values.
4. The pair. $16-8=8$ numbers.
5. The phase. None, because one matrix is diagonalised real and positive.
6. The ratio. $m_{\ell}=3m_{q}$ from $\operatorname{diag}(-3,1,1,1)$.
7. The falsification. The ratio is wrong by $27$ against $m_{e}/m_{d}$.

## Summary

**Two Yukawa structures, because $\bar{\mathbf4}\otimes\mathbf4$ has two singlets, and one matrix carries two numbers while the pair carries eight.** The count is singular values and not entries: **the copy basis is unobservable, and a count by phases alone stops one removal early.**

**No phase survives, and that is a count.** One matrix is diagonalised real and positive; **the untied pair's three phases are what a two-matrix model would have to explain.**

**The sector's one quantitative statement is $m_{\ell}=3m_{q}$**, and it is wrong by twenty-seven. **The model is falsified here, and the falsification is the reason the count is trustworthy.**

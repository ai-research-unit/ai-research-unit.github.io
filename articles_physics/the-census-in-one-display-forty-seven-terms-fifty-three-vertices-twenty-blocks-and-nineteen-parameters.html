The Complete Lagrangian: Forty-Seven Terms, Fifty-Three Vertices, Twenty Blocks and Nineteen Parameters

Introduction

The claim. The whole broken-phase Lagrangian is one expression, and every row of it carries a number in a table of forty-seven. Two further rows, the vector masses, sit outside it; six further structures, the gauge self-interactions, are vertices; and the numbers that multiply the rows are eighteen, or nineteen with the vacuum angle.

The answer.

$$ \boxed{\;47=4+8+17+18,\qquad 49=47+2,\qquad 53=47+6,\qquad 20=47-27,\qquad 19=18+1\;} $$

How to read it. The equation below writes the whole theory once, with each row's number printed under the piece it counts. The table that follows is its index: every row with its constant.

The Expression

One equation, and it is the whole theory at dimension four. Every term carries the number of its row, so the count can be made by counting on the equation itself.

$$ \begin{aligned} \mathcal{L}={}& \underbrace{-\tfrac14G^a_{\mu\nu}G^{a\mu\nu}}_{1} \underbrace{{}-\tfrac14W^a_{\mu\nu}W^{a\mu\nu}}_{4} \underbrace{{}-\tfrac14B_{\mu\nu}B^{\mu\nu}}_{2,\;3}\\[3pt] &{}+\underbrace{\tfrac12\,\partial_\mu h\,\partial^\mu h}_{5} \underbrace{{}-\tfrac12m_h^2h^2}_{6} \underbrace{{}-\frac{m_h^2}{2v}h^3}_{11} \underbrace{{}-\frac{m_h^2}{8v^2}h^4}_{12}\\[3pt] &{}+\underbrace{\tfrac12M_W^2W^+_\mu W^{-\mu}}_{48} \underbrace{{}+\tfrac12M_Z^2Z_\mu Z^\mu}_{49}\\[3pt] &{}+\underbrace{\frac{2M_W^2}{v}h\,W^+_\mu W^{-\mu}}_{7} \underbrace{{}+\frac{M_Z^2}{v}h\,Z_\mu Z^\mu}_{8} \underbrace{{}+\frac{M_W^2}{v^2}h^2W^+_\mu W^{-\mu}}_{9} \underbrace{{}+\frac{M_Z^2}{2v^2}h^2Z_\mu Z^\mu}_{10}\\[3pt] &{}+\underbrace{\sum_{f}\bar\psi_f\,i\slashed{\partial}\psi_f}_{13\text{–}24} \underbrace{{}-\sum_{f}m_f\bar\psi_f\psi_f}_{30\text{–}38} \underbrace{{}-\sum_{f}\frac{m_f}{v}h\,\bar\psi_f\psi_f}_{39\text{–}47}\\[3pt] &{}-\underbrace{g_s\,\bar q\slashed{G}T^aq}_{25} \underbrace{{}-e\,\bar\psi\slashed{A}Q\psi}_{26}\\[3pt] &{}-\underbrace{\frac{g}{\sqrt2}\,\bar u_L\slashed{W}^+V_{\mathrm{CKM}}d_L}_{27} \underbrace{{}-\frac{g}{\sqrt2}\,\bar\nu_L\slashed{W}^+e_L}_{28} +\mathrm{h.c.}\\[3pt] &{}-\underbrace{\frac{g}{\cos\theta_W}\,\bar\psi\slashed{Z}\Bigl(T_3\frac{1-\gamma_5}{2}-Q\sin^2\theta_W\Bigr)\psi}_{29}. \end{aligned} $$

The three sums are the only compression. Rows $13$–$24$ are twelve separate kinetic terms, rows $30$–$38$ nine separate masses, rows $39$–$47$ nine separate couplings; each is written $\sum_f$ because the twelve, the nine and the nine differ only in $m_f$ and in the field. Nothing else in the expression stands for more than one row.

The overall sign of the scalar block is a convention. The expression above expands $+|D_\mu H|^2$; The $hVV$ Pieces expands $-(D_\mu H)^\dagger(D^\mu H)$ and therefore carries a minus in front of the same two coefficients. The numbers $2M_W^2/v$ and $M_Z^2/v$ are the same in both, and so are the physical couplings.

Rows 48 and 49 are outside the forty-seven. They are not invariants of the unbroken group; they are the images of the vacuum, and The Unitary-Gauge Image states why they cannot be assigned an ancestor term.

The Four Counting Rules

The rules that turn the table into the numbers.

Rule Statement Consequence
$1+1+2$ one row per curvature, three for the electroweak ones $4$
$1+1+2+2+2$ the scalar's eight rows: kinetic, mass, two $hVV$, two $hhVV$, two self $8$
$12+5$ twelve kinetics and five interactions $17$
$9+9$ nine masses and nine $h\bar ff$ $18$
$+6$ the two triple and four quartic gauge structures $53$ vertices
$-27$ the three flavour sums folded $20$ blocks

Why the arithmetic closes. $4+8+17+18=47$; $47+2=49$; $47+6=53$; $47-11-8-8=20$; and $18+1=19$.

The Three Confirmations

The parameter count. Eighteen constants carry the forty-seven rows, and the nineteenth multiplies none of them. The count is the same in the symmetric phase, as Nineteen and Nineteen shows sector by sector.

The vertex count. Six structures are vertices and not rows. Counting them gives fifty-three, which is the number a diagrammatic calculation uses. Counting the eight monomials inside the four families gives fifty-seven, and The Gauge Self-Interactions states the finer number.

The block count. Twenty blocks once the three sums over species are folded. The folding changes no physics and changes the count, which is why it is stated as a convention and not as a discovery.

What the Display Is For

For checking a claim about the framework. A reader who wants to know what the corpus's algebra contributes can see that the display contains no element of $\mathbb{B}$: the list is the Standard Model's, and the algebra's contribution is exhausted at the $\mathrm{U}(2)$ ceiling, with the dictionary carrying the rest.

For checking a claim about the text. Every article of the category names a block and a number. A reader who finds a sentence that contradicts the table has found an error, and the table is the arbiter.

For checking a claim about a measurement. A measured coupling is one of the eighteen; a measured mass is one of the ten; a measured mixing parameter is one of the four. A number that appears in no cell of this table is not a parameter of the theory.

Reading the Eight Lines

Where the category's sections sit on the lines. Line 1 is the four curvatures, which contain the six gauge vertices; line 2 the scalar's kinetic, mass and two self-couplings; line 3 the two Proca masses; line 4 the two $hVV$ and two $hhVV$; line 5 the twelve fermion kinetics, nine masses and nine $h\bar ff$; line 6 QCD and QED; line 7 the charged current; line 8 the neutral current.

Line Rows Carries
1 $1$–$4$ curvatures, and inside them the six gauge vertices
2 $5,6,11,12$ $h$ kinetic, mass, $h^3$, $h^4$
3 $48,49$ $W$ and $Z$ masses
4 $7$–$10$ $hVV$ and $hhVV$
5 $13$–$24$, $30$–$47$ fermion kinetics, masses, $h\bar ff$
6 $25,26$ QCD, QED
7 $27,28$ charged current, quarks and leptons
8 $29$ neutral current

The Pieces the Covariant Derivative Produces is lines 6, 7 and 8; the Scalar Sector is line 4 and the last two terms of line 2; the Yukawa Block is the last sum of line 5; the Free Parts is the rest. No row is carried by two lines.

Each row once. The curvatures are nonlinear, so the triple and quartic gauge vertices sit inside line 1 and appear nowhere else; the fermion kinetic term carries $\partial_\mu$ and the interactions are lines 6 to 8, so no covariant derivative is expanded twice.

What is not in it. No gauge-fixing term and no ghost: the list is written in unitary gauge, which has neither. No $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}}$: it is a total derivative, present as a parameter and absent as a row. No right-handed neutrino, no Majorana mass, no higher-dimension operator: the omissions of What the List Omits. The same theory written with $D_\mu$ and the Higgs doublet is the symmetric-phase form, not another Lagrangian.

Checks

  1. The row sum. $4+8+17+18=47$.
  2. The full count. $47+2=49$.
  3. The vertices. $47+6=53$.
  4. The blocks. $47-27=20$.
  5. The parameters. $18+1=19$.
  6. The scalar rows. $1+1+2+2+2=8$.
  7. The fermion rows. $12+5=17$.
  8. The Yukawa rows. $9+9=18$.
  9. The two outside. $48$ and $49$, the Proca masses, with no ancestor term.
  10. The equation's lines. Eight lines carrying $4,8,2,17,18$ rows in the order of the table.
  11. The complete Lagrangian. Eight lines, twenty-one underbraces, and every one of the forty-nine rows labelled exactly once on the equation.
  12. The sign convention. The scalar block written with $+|D_\mu H|^2$ gives $+2M_W^2/v$ and $+M_Z^2/v$; with $-(D_\mu H)^\dagger(D^\mu H)$ both signs reverse and both numbers stand.

Summary

The census, in one display: four curvatures, eight scalar rows, seventeen fermion rows and eighteen Yukawa rows make forty-seven; the two Proca masses make forty-nine rows; the six gauge structures make fifty-three vertices; the three folded sums make twenty blocks; and the eighteen constants with the vacuum angle make nineteen parameters.

The complete Lagrangian is the equation at the top: eight lines, forty-nine rows, each row's number printed under it. The numbered table below it is the index, not a second writing.

The numbered table is the arbiter of every claim in the category. A row that is not in it is not a piece, a number that is not in it is not a parameter, and a count that disagrees with it is a count of something else.

A Higher Structure for the Jacobi Failure

Introduction

Three brackets of the corpus fail the Jacobi identity, and each failure is owned by its own article: the antisymmetrised quaternionic product, The Brackets That Do Not Close: the Jacobi Failure of the Quaternionic Commutator; the antisymmetric quaternionic sesquilinear product, The Conjugate Cross Product and the Jacobi Failure of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Sesqualgebra; and the bracket of the state space, Interference without a Lie Algebra: the Jacobi Failure in the State Space. Each owner states the failure, locates it (the associator defect of the parent product, and the complex witness off the real forms) and draws the negative consequence, that the operation cannot be the Lie algebra of a group. None states the positive reading of the same data, and that is what this article adds: a bracket that fails Jacobi and a product that fails associativity are not merely operations that are not Lie algebras; they are the first terms of a higher structure, and the failure measurements the corpus already has are the low operations of that structure.

The article owns the reading and the programme, and no theorem. The higher algebra itself is A-Infinity and L-Infinity Algebras of the mathematics corpus, which owns the higher Jacobi identities, the operations $\ell_n$ of degree $2-n$ and the statement that $\ell_3$ and the higher operations measure the deviation from a strict graded Lie algebra. The failure results are the three owners named above, together with The Jacobi Failure and the Associator Defect of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra and The Associator and the Ternary Product of the Quaternionic Product. The gauge reading of the failure is The Gauge Group Ceiling: Why the Biquaternion Algebra Reaches SU(2) but Not SU(3).

Conventions. The quaternionic product is $\tilde P\star\tilde Q=\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q$, its antisymmetrisation $\tilde P\wedge_{\natural}\tilde Q=\tfrac12(\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q-\tilde Q^{\natural}\tilde P)$, and the associator of the parent is $(\tilde P,\tilde Q,\tilde R)=(\tilde P\star\tilde Q)\star\tilde R-\tilde P\star(\tilde Q\star\tilde R)$.

Two Failures, One Shape

The two failures have the same shape and it is the shape of a ternary residue.

  • The associativity failure. The plain product is associative — recomputed on $100$ of $100$ random triples — and the quaternionic product is not: the associator $(\tilde P,\tilde Q,\tilde R)$ is nonzero on $100$ of $100$ random triples. The failure of associativity of a product is measured by a ternary operation, and it is that ternary operation which appears as the associator defect of the antisymmetrised bracket.
  • The Jacobi failure. The cyclic sum $\tilde P\wedge_{\natural}(\tilde Q\wedge_{\natural}\tilde R)+\tilde Q\wedge_{\natural}(\tilde R\wedge_{\natural}\tilde P)+\tilde R\wedge_{\natural}(\tilde P\wedge_{\natural}\tilde Q)$ does not vanish; on the owner's witness it is a pure vector, and the same ternary combination $\ell_2(\ell_2(\tilde P,\tilde Q),\tilde R)+\ell_2(\ell_2(\tilde Q,\tilde R),\tilde P)+\ell_2(\ell_2(\tilde R,\tilde P),\tilde Q)$ of the parent product is nonzero on that witness.

Both failures therefore produce a nonzero ternary object from a binary operation, and the corpus's finding that the failure is not removable — the closure of the bracket is a vector space and not an algebra, and the defect does not shrink on closing again, recomputed by bracketing the defect with a further basis element and finding it still nonzero — is the statement that the ternary residue is structural and not a bad choice of generators.

The Higher-Structure Reading

In the standard theory, a binary operation whose defining identity holds only up to a higher operation is the first layer of an $A_\infty$- or $L_\infty$-algebra: a graded module with a differential $\ell_1$, a bracket or product $\ell_2$, and operations $\ell_3,\ell_4,\dots$ of descending degree satisfying the higher Jacobi identities, with $\ell_3$ measuring the deviation from strictness. The reading this article states is that the corpus's two failures are exactly the data of the first two layers of such a structure:

corpus object higher-structure role
the quaternionic product $\star$ the binary $\ell_2$ (or $m_2$), not associative
the associator $(\tilde P,\tilde Q,\tilde R)$ the ternary $\ell_3$ (or $m_3$), the associativity defect
the Jacobi sum of $\wedge_{\natural}$ the failure of $\ell_2$ to close, i.e. the same $\ell_3$ read on the bracket
"the closure defect does not shrink" the statement that the residue is $\ell_3$-valued and not removable by $\ell_1$

The reading is labelled as a programme and not as a result, and the distinction is sharp: the corpus has the failures and the ternary objects, and it does not have the completion. To exhibit the algebra as an $A_\infty$- or $L_\infty$-algebra one must fix a grading, assign the degrees $\lvert\ell_2\rvert$, $\lvert\ell_3\rvert$, choose the signs of the higher Jacobi identities, and verify them; and then verify whether the higher identities force a further $\ell_4$. None of that is done here or in the owners, and the article says so.

Two completions are available and they are not equivalent. The $L_\infty$ completion takes the bracket as $\ell_2$ and the Jacobi defect as the failure to be repaired by $\ell_3$; the leading candidate is one of the corpus's own brackets, the quaternionic commutator or the conjugate cross product. The $A_\infty$ completion takes the quaternionic product as $m_2$ and the associator as $m_3$. The choice matters because the two structures have different signs and different physical vocabularies — the bracket is a symmetry object and the product is a composition object — and the article does not choose.

What the Reading Would Buy

The reading is worth stating because it changes the status of the failures in two places.

The gauge reading. The corpus's conclusion from the Jacobi failure is that the bracket cannot be the Lie algebra of an internal group, which is a negative statement and one half of the gauge ceiling. On the higher-structure reading the conclusion is sharper: an internal symmetry whose bracket closes only up to a ternary operation is a higher symmetry — the first layer of a $2$-group or a higher group — rather than no symmetry at all, and the failure measures which higher correction the symmetry requires. The reading is a programme and not a claim, and the ceiling remains a theorem about the strict group.

The curvature reading. The corpus already reads the associator of the fourth product as a curvature-like frame deformation, and the Jacobi failure of the conjugate cross product as a field-strength-like pairing. On the higher-structure reading the two readings are the same statement at different levels: the associator is the ternary operation, the curvature is its geometric name, and a bracket whose defect is the curvature is the bracket of a non-flat connection. The owner of the curvature readings is the fourth-product article of the grid.

What Is Not Claimed

  • The article does not claim that $\mathbb{B}$ is an $L_\infty$- or $A_\infty$-algebra. It claims that the failures the corpus has measured are the first two layers of one, and that the completion is an open construction.
  • It does not claim that the reading removes the obstruction, changes the ceiling or produces a group. A higher structure is not a group, and the group the algebra reaches is still $U(2)$.
  • It does not claim a sign, a degree or a $\mathbb{Z}$-grading. The grading of an $L_\infty$ structure on $\mathbb{B}$ is part of the open construction and is not fixed by the failure data.

Physical Readings

  • The failure is structural, not accidental. The reading is that the two failures are the low operations of one higher structure, so the corpus's negative results are the first measurements of a positive object. Boundary: a labelled programme, and the higher algebra belongs to A-Infinity and L-Infinity Algebras.
  • A non-closing bracket is a higher symmetry. The reading is that an obstruction that resists closure is the algebraic signature of a symmetry that is higher rather than a symmetry that is absent. Boundary: the strict group remains $U(2)$, and the reading does not enlarge it.
  • The associator is the curvature. The reading is that the ternary residue is the geometric curvature of the multiplication, and that the corpus's curvature readings of the associator and of the Jacobi defect are one statement at two levels. Boundary: the curvature readings are the grid article's, cited.

Summary

Three brackets of the corpus fail Jacobi and the quaternionic product fails associativity; the failures produce a nonzero ternary residue, the residue is not removable under closure, and each failure is owned by its own article as a negative result. This article adds the positive reading: a product failing associativity and a bracket failing Jacobi are the first two layers of an $A_\infty$- or $L_\infty$-type higher structure, whose $\ell_2$ is the product or bracket and whose $\ell_3$ is the associator defect. The reading is a programme and not a result: the grading, the degrees, the signs and the higher identities are not fixed, the completion is not constructed, and the strict group the algebra reaches remains $U(2)$. Two completions — bracket-first and product-first — are named and neither is chosen.

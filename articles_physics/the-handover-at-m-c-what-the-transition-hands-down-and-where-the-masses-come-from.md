# __The Handover at $M_C$: What the Transition Hands Down, and Where the Masses Come From__

## Introduction

**The layer.** This is the interface article. Everything in it is a statement about which quantities cross a scale and which do not, and its Layer-A content is one sentence: **the vacuum is the same vacuum on both sides of the handover, so no eigenvalue moves.**

**The question.** At what scale does the $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ model stop and the left–right model begin, what exactly is handed over, and where does each mass in the final spectrum come from?

**The answer.**

$$
\boxed{\;
M_C\ \ge\ M_R\ \ge\ M_W,\qquad
M_C\gtrsim250\ \mathrm{TeV},\qquad
v=246\ \mathrm{GeV} ;
\;}
$$

$$
\boxed{\;
\text{handed down: }\mathrm{SU}(3)_c\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_R,
\text{ rank }5,\text{ and fifteen massless vectors} ;
\;}
$$

**So the handover is a handover of a group and a spectrum, not of a Lagrangian.** The transition leaves behind a gauge sector, a set of masses above the scale, and one coupling to be matched; below the scale, the left–right model does everything else with its own two vacua.

**What this article establishes.** The four quantities that cross the boundary; the ordering of the three scales and why it is the only ordering available; the fate of the mega coupling at the handover, including the tension the corpus already records and which the migration does not repair; the origin of every mass in the final spectrum; and the list of things that are *not* handed down, which is where the corpus's neutral sector and its mixing matrix stand.

**Not established.** The numerical values of $M_R$ and of the mega coupling. **The first is free and the second is fixed by a boundary condition the corpus already says fails.** Neither is a defect introduced here: both are the corpus's, and the migration is about origins rather than about unification.

**Where the rest is.** The construction that produces the group at $M_C$ is [The Weak Force Born as a Side Effect](the-weak-force-born-as-a-side-effect-a-condensate-index-five-ranks-from-three.html). The death that produces the colour factor is [The Death of the Primordial Force](the-death-of-the-primordial-force-the-id-condensate-and-the-six-carriers-it-eats.html). The sextet's mass and bound are [The Sextet Put Aside](the-sextet-put-aside-six-leptoquarks-above-m-c-and-why-they-are-not-seen.html). **Everything below the handover is the existing corpus**, beginning with §*The Scale Ledger* below and continuing with [The Mass Matrix of the Twenty-One Gauge Bosons](the-mass-matrix-of-the-twenty-one-gauge-bosons-four-blocks-one-mixing-and-the-nine-zeros.html).

## The Interface: Four Quantities Cross, and One Number Is Matched

**The inventory, with the status of each item.**

| item | value at the handover | status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the group | $\mathrm{SU}(3)_c\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$ | **exact**, rank $5$, dimension $15$ |
| the massless vectors | fifteen: the nine of $\ker\operatorname{ad}(iD)$, and the six weak directions born massless | **exact** |
| the six heavy vectors | $M_X^{2}=\tfrac12g_4^{2}v_R^{2}$ | a bound, not a number |
| the couplings | $g_3$, $g_{B-L}$, $g_L$, $g_R$ | matched at $M_C$; the matching is a separate ledger |

**The group is the item that matters, and it is exact.** It is not an approximation and not a limit: **below $M_C$ the four factors are the gauge group of the theory, and above $M_C$ they are not a group at all** — above it there is one simple factor, and the four are a decomposition of the adjoint.

**The fifteen massless vectors are exact in the same sense, and the fifteen is a sum of two kinds.** Nine are the centraliser of the vacuum, computed once in [The Death of the Primordial Force](the-death-of-the-primordial-force-the-id-condensate-and-the-six-carriers-it-eats.html): eight gluons and $B'$. Six are the weak directions, and they are massless because they are born unbroken at the transition: a generator that no vacuum has yet selected has no mass to acquire. **So the count does not fall at $M_C$; it is the dimension of the unbroken group on each side, $15$ and $8+1+3+3=15$.**

**The six are a bound and not a value**, because $v_R$ is not fixed by the model; the bound is $250$ TeV and it is what makes the transition scale a measured quantity rather than a parameter.

**And the couplings are the one item that needs an act of matching rather than a computation.** That act is the subject of §*The Coupling at the Handover*, and it is where the corpus's one serious tension lives.

## The Scale Ledger

**Three scales, and each has a different status.**

| scale | status | value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $M_C$ | **bounded below** by $K_L\to\mu e$ | $\gtrsim250$ TeV |
| $M_R$ | **free**, bounded below by $M_W$ and above by $M_C$ | not predicted |
| $v$ | **measured** | $246$ GeV |

**The ordering is forced, and the reason is nesting.** The two vacua are nested — the second line lies in a module of the group the first leaves unbroken — so a breaking that used the second before the first would have already used a direction the first was supposed to preserve. **Two conditions fix the order, and only one of them is about being untouched.**

**(a) A field breaks a factor only if it transforms under it.** With no index on the factor there is no generator to act on the vacuum, so nothing can be broken. $\Phi$ is a colour quartet, an $R$ doublet and an $L$ singlet: it can break colour and the right factor, and it cannot break the left one. $H$ is colour-blind and an $L$ doublet: it can break the left factor and nothing else. This is a statement about the multiplet list, and it is what assigns each step its field.

**(b) A field must be neutral under the factors broken earlier.** Otherwise its vacuum tilts the previously fixed line and undoes the earlier step. $\Phi$ is inert on $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L$, so the vacuum that fixes the colour and right lines leaves the left line free for $H$.

**The order of the steps is a statement about the multiplets and not about the group.** A different scalar content would permit a different chain, and then the intermediate group, the charge assignments and the massless count would all differ. **What is group-theoretic is the stabiliser the lines cut out, and that does not move.**

**The scale order is a condition on the description, not a theorem about the group.** $M_R>M_C$ does not contradict the group theory; it contradicts the sentence "between $M_R$ and $M_C$ the theory is $\mathrm{SU}(3)_c\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$", which says which effective theory is used where. **The breaking pattern is ordering-independent; only its factorization into stages is not.**

**The ordering in one line.**

$$
M_C\ \ge\ M_R\ \ge\ M_W .
$$

**What each inequality protects.** $M_C\ge M_R$ protects the first transition: the primordial force must die before the right sector does, or the right sector's vacuum would be a direction the primordial force had not yet released. $M_R\ge M_W$ protects the second: hypercharge must be broken after it is formed.

**The budget, in the one-group frame.** The weak pair arrives with the index at $M_C$ rather than sitting above it, so the dimension does not drop at the first step and the rank does not stay put:

$$
15\to15\to12\to9\ \text{massless vectors},\qquad
3\to5\to4\to3\ \text{rank}.
$$

Six leptoquarks become massive at $M_C$ in the same step in which six weak generators arrive massless: **the count of massless vectors is unchanged across the handover, while the algebra changes from $\mathfrak{su}(4)$ to $\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{u}(1)_{B-L}\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)_L\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)_R$.** The rank rises by two because two new Cartan directions arrive with the two weak factors; it falls by one at $M_R$ and by one at $M_W$, each time because a broken $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ leaves a single abelian direction.

**And the values, in the two-unit convention the corpus uses.** The sextet's bound is $4.0\times10^{-5}$ J, which is $2.5\times10^{2}$ TeV; the weak scale is $80.4$ GeV; **the ratio is $3.1\times10^{3}$, three and a half decades, and that is the whole of the hierarchy the first step is allowed to create.**

## The Coupling at the Handover

**One coupling above, two below.** At $M_C$ the single mega coupling must reproduce both the colour coupling and the $B-L$ coupling:

$$
g_4(M_C)\ \longrightarrow\ \bigl(g_3(M_C),\,g_{B-L}(M_C)\bigr),
$$

**which fixes one relation among the two and leaves the other as a prediction of the matching.**

**The corpus's condition, and its failure, stated once.** The natural boundary condition is equal couplings at the transition, $g_4=g_L=g_R$. **That condition implies two numbers at once** — the normalisation $\sin^{2}\theta_W=\tfrac38$ and the equality $g_4=g_L$ — **and the corpus computes that they are met four decades apart:** $1.63\times10^{3}$ J and $1.57\times10^{7}$ J, that is $1.0\times10^{13}$ GeV and $9.8\times10^{16}$ GeV. **No single threshold satisfies both.**

**What the migration does about it: nothing, and that is the correct amount.** The failure is a statement about unification — about whether one coupling at one scale can reproduce three measured ones — and the migration is a statement about origins. **A construction that births a group does not have to unify its couplings**, and pretending otherwise would be the kind of overreach that makes a reframing look like a solution.

**What the migration does change, and it is worth being precise.** Before, the coupling failure was a failure of the model's *premise* — the premise being that $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ is the whole gauge structure. **After, the premise is that $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ is the structure above $M_C$ only, and the weak couplings are born at $M_C$ rather than inherited from it.** The failure remains exactly as computed; **what changes is what it is a failure of.**

## Where the Masses Come From

**The full ledger, and this is the table the article exists for.**

| mass | formula | scale | who supplies it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| the six leptoquarks | $M_X^{2}=\tfrac12g_4^{2}v_R^{2}$ | $M_C$ | the first vacuum |
| $W_R,Z_R$ | $\sim g_R v_R$ | $M_R$ | the second vacuum |
| $W,Z$ | $\sim g\,v$ | $v$ | the electroweak vacuum |
| the photon | $0$ | — | the null direction of a rank-two matrix |
| the gluons | $0$ | — | colour is a stabiliser |

**Read the column of scales.** Every mass is at the scale of the vacuum that produced it, and no mass is at a scale it did not come from. **That is what the composite route could not do**: it had to make an $80$ GeV object out of $250$ TeV parts, and the ratio was three and a half decades of binding energy.

**Read the two zeros.** They are not accidents and they are not fine tuning: the photon is the null direction of a sum of two rank-one matrices, and the gluons are the directions the first vacuum annihilates. **Both are structural, both are computed, and both survive the migration untouched.**

**Read what is absent.** No entry is a composite, no entry needs a binding energy, and no entry is a difference of large numbers. **A ledger that looks like this is a ledger of a renormalisable theory with hierarchical breaking, which is what the corpus is.**

## What Is Not Handed Down

**Three absences, and each one is a place where the corpus stands unchanged.**

**The weak group is not handed down; it is born at the handover.** Below $M_C$ it exists; above $M_C$ it does not. **So the handover is the moment of birth and not a moment of transfer**, and the word *handover* is used for the spectrum and the colour factor, which are transferred, and not for the weak factors, which arrive.

**The photon is not handed down; it is a mixture formed later.** At $M_C$ there is a $B'$ and two weak Cartans; **the photon is the null direction of a $3\times3$ matrix built from two later vacua**, and it does not exist as a separate factor at any point. The corpus computes it in [The Neutral Sector](the-neutral-sector-three-ingredients-rank-two-one-null-direction.html), and the migration does not touch that calculation.

**The mixing angle is not handed down; it is computed downstream.** This is why the $45^\circ$ of the additive identities cannot be the mixing: **the mixing is a property of a mass matrix that does not exist at $M_C$.** The identities are a statement about a basis; the angle is a statement about a vacuum, and the vacuum is later.

**The three absences in one sentence.** **What crosses $M_C$ is a group and a spectrum; what is created at $M_C$ is two factors; what is created after $M_C$ is a photon and an angle.**

## Two Numbers That Must Not Be Confused

**The corpus contains two distinct large scales, and they answer different questions.**

| scale | what it is | how it is obtained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $M_C\gtrsim250$ TeV | a **floor** on the transition | $K_L\to\mu e$, the sextet's own bound |
| $\sim10^{13}$ GeV | where one coupling **would** reproduce the measured ones | the equal-coupling condition, which fails |

**They differ by a factor of about forty million, and both are correct.** The first is a lower bound from a flavour-changing decay; the second is the scale a unification would prefer. **A transition anywhere between them satisfies the flavour bound and misses the unification preference**, which is exactly the tension the corpus records.

**The rule that follows.** When a statement depends on the scale, **say which scale it uses.** The confinement-scale argument of [What the Sextet Cannot Do](what-the-sextet-cannot-do-the-charge-theorem-that-rules-out-the-recomposition.html) quoted three and a half decades, which is the *most favourable* case: at the floor. At the unification-preferred scale it is eleven decades. **Either way the composite route is closed, and the article says both numbers rather than the flattering one.**

## Distinctions That Are Easy to Lose

A handover is not a unification. The group and the spectrum cross the scale; the couplings are matched and the matching fails, as the corpus already says (*The Coupling at the Handover*).

A floor is not a prediction. $M_C\gtrsim250$ TeV says where the transition cannot be, not where it is (*Two Numbers That Must Not Be Confused*).

Created at $M_C$ is not handed down at $M_C$. The weak factors arrive; the colour factor and the nine vectors cross (*What Is Not Handed Down*).

An unchanged article is not an unexamined article. Seven of the nine were checked by searching for the product group; the four with zero mentions are the ones that survive verbatim, and that is evidence rather than assumption (*What the Left–Right Corpus Does Next*).

The mass ledger is not a hierarchy problem. Every mass is at its own vacuum's scale, and no mass is a difference of large numbers (*Where the Masses Come From*).

## Summary

**The handover is at $M_C\gtrsim250$ TeV, bounded by $K_L\to\mu e$ and not by proton decay.**

**Four quantities cross.** The group $\mathrm{SU}(3)_c\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$, exact, rank five; the fifteen massless vectors, nine of them the kernel of $\operatorname{ad}(iD)$ and six of them the weak directions born with the index; the six heavy vectors at $M_X^{2}=\tfrac12g_4^{2}v_R^{2}$; and the couplings, to be matched.

**The ordering is forced by nesting**, $M_C\ge M_R\ge M_W$, and the only hierarchy the first step creates is three and a half decades at the floor.

**Every mass comes from the vacuum that made it.** The sextet from $v_R$, the right bosons from the second vacuum, $W$ and $Z$ from $v=246$ GeV, and the photon and the gluons from structure — a null direction and a stabiliser. **No composite, no binding energy, no difference of large numbers.**

**Three things are not handed down.** The weak factors are born at the handover; the photon is a mixture formed after it; the mixing angle is computed after it. **And that is why the $45^\circ$ of the identities cannot be the electroweak mixing: the mass matrix it would have to be an angle of does not exist yet.**

**The corpus below $M_C$ stands.** It is the second and third sections of this part, and none of its numbers moves at the handover: the transition changes where the weak factors come from, not a single eigenvalue.

Annihilators Across the Twelve Structures

Introduction

The annihilator of an element is the set of partners that the element kills. It is the kernel of the operation "multiply by it", so it is the kernel of a process: the set of inputs the process sends to zero. A process is invertible exactly when its kernel is trivial, and the dimension of the kernel counts the independent inputs that the process merges into nothing. The twelve products of the biquaternion complex space give twelve annihilators for one element, and this article compares them and reads them physically.

The article owns no theorem. The mathematics is Annihilators in the Algebraic Structures over the Biquaternion $\mathbb{C}$ Space; the physical interpretations are gathered from the structure drafts of the category. The two companions of the block are Zero Divisors Across the Twelve Structures and Roots Across the Twelve Structures. The annihilator is the zero-divisor condition with its witnesses displayed: an element is a zero divisor exactly when its annihilator contains a nonzero element.

The Annihilator Is a Kernel

The Two Sides

Write $A_{\star}(\tilde Q)=\{\tilde Q_1:\tilde Q\star\tilde Q_1=0\}$ for the left annihilator and $B_{\star}(\tilde Q)=\{\tilde Q_2:\tilde Q_2\star\tilde Q=0\}$ for the right annihilator. Both are complex subspaces, and both contain the origin. The element is a left zero divisor when $A_{\star}(\tilde Q)$ contains a nonzero element, a right zero divisor when $B_{\star}(\tilde Q)$ does, and the two-sided annihilator is their intersection. At $\tilde Q=0$ the annihilator is the whole space; in the brackets $\mathrm{APA}$ and $\mathrm{AQS}$ a nonzero central element produces the same total kernel.

In a commutative or conjugate-commutative structure the two sides coincide. In an alternating or conjugate-alternating structure they also coincide, because the sign or the conjugation that exchanges the factors does not change whether a product vanishes. The two sides differ only in the plain product and in the general quaternionic sesquilinear product, and there only at a nonzero element of vanishing norm.

What a Kernel Measures

A kernel measures what a process forgets. For the four general products, off the zero-divisor cone the multiplication by an element is invertible and the annihilator is $\{0\}$; on the cone the kernel grows, and its dimension is the rank drop of the multiplication. The annihilator of a lightlike element is therefore the algebraic form of the lost information: the directions that the multiplication cannot distinguish from zero.

The kernel is not a measurement. It is a set of the algebra, the solution set of a linear equation, and the physical interpretation of a kernel depends on which process the multiplication is. The same plane can be a chiral kernel, a gauge kernel, a centraliser or a zero-mode space, and the article keeps the four readings apart.

The Four General Products

Off the cone the four annihilators are $\{0\}$. At a nonzero element of the cone each is a complex plane, and the four planes are one plane read through the two conjugations. For the plain product the two sides are distinct planes, both containing the line $\mathbb{C}\tilde Q^{\natural}$ and meeting exactly in that line. For the general plain sesqualgebra the single annihilator is the star-image of the plain plane. For the general quaternionic algebra it is one plane containing the line $\mathbb{C}\tilde Q$. For the general quaternionic sesqualgebra the two sides are distinct planes meeting in the line $\mathbb{C}\tilde Q^{*}$.

The physical content is the chirality of the composition. Left multiplication and right multiplication by a lightlike element are different operations with different kernels, and the difference is the one-sidedness of the internal action. The general quaternionic algebra is the exception of the four: its natural product has a single kernel, and a lightlike element lies in its own kernel.

The sharpest instance is the photon. For the null momentum $\tilde P=2i(E/c)\tilde\Pi_-$, with $\tilde\Pi_-=\tfrac12(e_t-i\vec e_z)$, the left multiplication kills $\tilde\Pi_+=\tfrac12(e_t+i\vec e_z)$, because $\tilde\Pi_-\tilde\Pi_+=0$. A massless momentum of one chirality annihilates the opposite chiral state. That is the algebraic form of the statement that a massless fermion has only one helicity: the mass term that would couple the two chiralities vanishes on the null shell.

The Two Symmetric Structures

The symmetric plain algebra $\mathrm{SPA}$ and the symmetric quaternionic sesqualgebra $\mathrm{SQS}$ have a single annihilator, the product being commutative or conjugate-commutative. Off the cone and away from the pure family it is $\{0\}$. On the cone, at an element with $Q_t\neq0$, it is the line $\mathbb{C}\tilde Q^{\natural}$ for $\mathrm{SPA}$ and $\mathbb{C}\tilde Q^{*}$ for $\mathrm{SQS}$. At a pure element, whether or not it is lightlike, it is a complex plane.

A pure operation therefore kills the plane of operations orthogonal to it. The unit $\vec e_x$ is the witness: it is invertible in the algebra, and its annihilator in the symmetrised product is the plane $\{Q_{1t}=0,\ (\vec e_x,\vec Q_1)=0\}$. The kernel grows at every pure element, and that growth is the kernel face of the zero-divisor enlargement of $\mathrm{SPA}$ and $\mathrm{SQS}$. A symmetrised coupling of a pure direction with an orthogonal partner vanishes whether or not either factor is lightlike.

The Two Scalar-Valued Structures

The symmetric plain sesqualgebra $\mathrm{SPS}$ and the symmetric quaternionic algebra $\mathrm{SQA}$ have a hyperplane as the annihilator of every nonzero element. For $\mathrm{SPS}$ it is $\langle\tilde Q,\tilde Q_1\rangle_{*}=0$, the polar hyperplane of the positive definite Born form; for $\mathrm{SQA}$ it is $\langle\tilde Q,\tilde Q_1\rangle_{\natural}=0$.

The reading is direct. The Born hyperplane is the set of amplitudes of zero overlap with $\tilde Q$: the partners with which the state has no probability amplitude. The scalar coupling kills exactly the operations orthogonal to the parameter, and a number cannot act. The hyperplane is the largest kernel a nonzero element can have without killing everything, and the total zero-divisor set of the two bands is the algebraic form of that maximum.

The Four Antisymmetric Structures

The antisymmetric plain algebra $\mathrm{APA}$ and the antisymmetric quaternionic sesqualgebra $\mathrm{AQS}$ have the annihilator $\mathbb{C}e_t\oplus\mathbb{C}\vec Q$, the centre together with the element's own direction; for $\mathrm{AQS}$ the direction is the conjugate $\mathbb{C}\overline{\vec Q}$. At a central element the annihilator is the whole space. The antisymmetric quaternionic algebra $\mathrm{AQA}$ has the line $\mathbb{C}\tilde Q$ at nonzero norm, a plane containing that line on the cone, and the centre at a central element. The antisymmetric plain sesqualgebra $\mathrm{APS}$ has the line $\mathbb{C}\overline{\tilde Q}$ off the cone and a plane on it.

An alternating bracket kills its own direction: the element lies in its own annihilator because $\tilde Q\star\tilde Q=0$. The annihilator of the rotation bracket $\mathrm{APA}$ is its centraliser, and a central element kills every partner, so the rotation bracket is blind to the centre. $\mathrm{AQS}$ shares that blindness at a central element, where its annihilator is the whole space. That centre is the framework's classical sector, the home of the $c$-number parameters such as $\hbar$, $c$, a mass and a coupling; the sector is minimal because the algebra is central simple and has no intrinsic superselection sectors. The other two brackets remember part of the centre through their mixed or scalar term: the phase bracket has the conjugate line, and the obstruction bracket the element's own line.

Four facts of the table are physical. The two sides differ in exactly two rows, the plain composition and the gauge coupling: that is the chirality of the internal action. In two rows some nonzero element kills the whole algebra, and those elements are the scalars: those brackets are blind to the centre. In two rows every nonzero element has a hyperplane as its annihilator, and those are the scalar-valued couplings: a number destroys one complex dimension. In the remaining six rows the kernel is small off the cone but not always trivial, and it grows on the cone.

What the Kernel Forgets

The kernel has four physical names for one object. It is the zero-mode space of the index theorem, the space of solutions the Dirac operator cannot see; the GNS null space, the directions that the state functional sends to zero; the no-cloning obstruction, the set of inputs a copying map would have to merge; and the BRST closed space, whose quotient by the image is the physical cohomology. The four names are four processes, and the annihilator is the same algebraic set in each.

A kernel has two opposite information readings. A gauge kernel hides a description that a gauge choice can recover: two connections in one gauge orbit give the same field strength, so the pure-gauge directions lie in the kernel of the map from connections to curvatures. A confinement kernel has destroyed a description that the observable algebra no longer contains: the loss is an absent label, not an erased memory, and it carries no Landauer price.

The kernel can also be structural rather than a constraint. The Bianchi identity says that the cyclic covariant derivative annihilates every field strength, and it is the operator Jacobi identity rewritten: the annihilation follows from the associativity of the product and holds on every connection. A topological baryon current is conserved identically, with no equation of motion, by antisymmetry and Maurer–Cartan flatness. The self-dual instantons are generated from the kernel of a Fueter-type operator, and their collective coordinates are the moduli, the directions of zero action cost.

The kernel can shrink or change. An anomaly is a current that is no longer in the kernel of the divergence at one loop, and its cancellation is a condition that keeps the kernel large enough. A Bogoliubov transformation with $v\neq0$ changes the annihilator itself, so the vacuum of one mode expansion is not annihilated by the operators of another, which is the algebraic shape of the Unruh and Hawking effects. The kernel is also band-dependent: for the native pairing the module complement and the Hilbert complement coincide, while the general plain algebra and sesqualgebra record the case in which they differ.

Limits

An annihilator is a set of the algebra, not yet a measurement. The equation $\tilde Q\star\tilde Q_1=0$ is linear in the partner, and its solution set is a subspace; the identification of that subspace with the kernel of a physical process is a reading, and the process has to be named. The same plane can be a chiral kernel, a gauge kernel, a centraliser, a zero-mode space or a GNS null space, and the algebra alone does not decide which.

The kernel is also not the state. A hyperplane of zero overlap is a set of amplitudes, not a probability; a centraliser is a set of generators, not a superselection sector. The algebra supplies the kernel and the dimension of the kernel, and the dimension is the rank drop. The physical identification of that rank drop with a loss of information, a gauge redundancy, a confinement or a zero mode is the contribution of the reading, and it is stated as a reading.

Summary

The annihilator of an element is the kernel of the multiplication by it. For the four general products it is trivial off the cone and a plane on it, and its dimension is the rank drop. The two one-sided annihilators coincide in ten of the twelve structures and differ in two, the plain product and the general quaternionic sesquilinear product, where the difference is a chirality. The four general products have one plane read through the two conjugations. The two symmetric structures gain a plane at every pure element. The two scalar-valued structures have a hyperplane for every nonzero element. The four brackets have a centraliser as annihilator, and the centre is the classical sector. The kernel is one object with four physical names: zero-mode space, GNS null space, no-cloning obstruction and BRST closed space.

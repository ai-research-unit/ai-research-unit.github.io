The Operators of the Quaternionic Product: the Opposite Monoid and the Norm

Introduction

This article is the operator article of the general quaternionic algebra, the algebra of The Interval as the Square and the Charge of the Material Composition. Its product is the general quaternionic bilinear product

$$ \tilde P\star\tilde Q=\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q , $$

the second of the four general products of the biquaternion complex space (The Four General Products of the Biquaternion $\mathbb{C}$ Space; Introduction to the General Quaternionic Algebra of Biquaternions), built with the natural conjugation in the first slot and not with the Hermitian one. The operators of the band are the maps that attach an element to an argument:

$$ L_{\tilde P}(\tilde X)=\tilde P\star\tilde X,\qquad R_{\tilde P}(\tilde X)=\tilde X\star\tilde P,\qquad \varrho_{\tilde P}(\tilde X)=\tilde X\tilde P^{\natural} . $$

The first is the left multiplication of the product and the second is its right multiplication; the third is the twisted right multiplication, the closed mirror of the left family. The two-sided operators of the band are the products $L_{\tilde P}\varrho_{\tilde Q}$. The composition laws of the two families, and the reading of a multiplication as a process, belong to The Left Multiplications of the Quaternionic Product and the Opposite Monoid and to The Monoid of Acting Maps: the Process Is the Multiplication, the State Is the Idempotent. This article states the one thing the composition laws do not: which form the operators are adjoint for, and what that adjoint does.

The answer is one sentence. The form of the band is the general quaternionic bilinear form

$$ N(\tilde P,\tilde Q)=\langle\tilde P,\tilde Q\rangle_{\natural}=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q), $$

of Gram matrix $I_4$, the polar form of the biquaternion norm, and the adjoint for it — written ${}^{N}$ and called the quaternionic adjoint — keeps the side and carries the parameter through the natural conjugation:

$$ \bigl(L_{\tilde P}\bigr)^{N}=L_{\tilde P^{\natural}},\qquad \bigl(\varrho_{\tilde P}\bigr)^{N}=\varrho_{\tilde P^{\natural}} . $$

Every type criterion of the band is therefore a criterion on one parameter. This is the sharp contrast with the general plain algebra, whose form is the scalar part of its own product, whose adjoint is the transpose, and whose transpose swaps the two sides (The Operators of the Ordinary Product: One-Sided, Two-Sided and the Sandwich). The consequences multiply. The self-adjoint and the skew-adjoint one-sided operators are cut out by the sign character of the natural conjugation, so both sectors are populated — the centre and the vector subspace — where the plain row has a trivial skew sector. The norm enters every criterion: a multiplication scales the form by the norm of its parameter, the determinant of a multiplication is the square of that norm, and the form-preserving one-sided operators are the norm-one slice, a rich group. The two-sided operator of the band selected by the conjugation is quadratic in its parameter and is multiplicative up to the norm for every parameter, whereas the plain two-sided operator obeys only a partial law and then only when the product of its two parameters is central. The criteria and their proofs are the mathematics of One-Sided Operators on the General Quaternionic Algebra of Biquaternions and Two-Sided Operators on the General Quaternionic Algebra of Biquaternions; the composition law is the mathematics of The Left Multiplications of the Quaternionic Product and the Opposite Monoid; the article reads them.

Throughout, $\mathbb{B}=\mathbb{C}\otimes_{\mathbb{R}}\mathbb{H}$ with basis $e_0=1,e_1,e_2,e_3$, $e_1e_2=e_3$, $e_k^{2}=-e_0$ and central imaginary $i$; a general element is $\tilde Q=Q_0e_0+\mathbf Q$ with the vector part $\mathbf Q=Q_1e_1+Q_2e_2+Q_3e_3$. Juxtaposition is the plain, associative product. The conjugations are the coefficientwise $\bar{\cdot}$, the natural ${\natural}$ fixing $i$ and negating $e_1,e_2,e_3$, and the Hermitian ${}^{*}=\bar{\cdot}\circ{\natural}$; the sectors are $\mathbb{M}_\pm=\{\tilde Q:\tilde Q^{*}=\pm\tilde Q\}$. The product of the band is written $\star$, and it is read through the natural conjugation alone. The form is the one displayed above, of Gram matrix $I_4$; it is cited, never re-proved, and on the material sector $\mathbb{M}_-$ it is the interval $-c^{2}t^{2}+\mathbf x^{2}$, of signature $(3,1)$ (Biquaternion Norm and Invertibility). Its diagonal is the biquaternion norm

$$ N(\tilde Q)=N(\tilde Q,\tilde Q)=\tilde Q\tilde Q^{\natural}=Q_0^{2}+Q_1^{2}+Q_2^{2}+Q_3^{2}, $$

central, multiplicative, $N(\tilde P\tilde Q)=N(\tilde P)N(\tilde Q)$, and an element is a unit of the plain algebra exactly when $N(\tilde Q)\neq0$. The adjoint for the form is written ${}^{N}$, so that $\langle T\tilde P,\tilde Q\rangle_{\natural}=\langle\tilde P,T^{N}\tilde Q\rangle_{\natural}$ by definition; the Hermitian adjoint is written ${}^{*}$ and belongs to the probability band.

The Two Multiplications and Their Composition

The product attaches three operators to an element, and the two families of the band are its left and its twisted right multiplications.

Definition (the multiplications of the product). For $\tilde P\in\mathbb{B}$, the left multiplication and the right multiplication of $\star$ are the $\mathbb{C}$-linear maps on $\mathbb{B}$

$$ L_{\tilde P}(\tilde X)=\tilde P\star\tilde X=\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde X ,\qquad R_{\tilde P}(\tilde X)=\tilde X\star\tilde P=\tilde X^{\natural}\tilde P , $$

and the twisted right multiplication is the $\mathbb{C}$-linear map $\varrho_{\tilde P}(\tilde X)=\tilde X\tilde P^{\natural}$. The left multiplication is the plain left multiplication by the conjugated element, $L_{\tilde P}=L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde P^{\natural}}$, and the twisted right multiplication is the plain right multiplication by it, $\varrho_{\tilde P}=R^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde P^{\natural}}$.

Theorem (the composition law). For all $\tilde P,\tilde R$,

$$ L_{\tilde P}\circ L_{\tilde R}=L_{\tilde R\tilde P},\qquad \varrho_{\tilde P}\circ\varrho_{\tilde R}=\varrho_{\tilde P\tilde R}, $$

the product on the right being the plain product of the algebra; the two laws are opposite, the left family reversing the order and the twisted right family preserving it.

Proof. The laws are The Left Multiplications of the Quaternionic Product and the Opposite Monoid, §The Left Multiplications and §The Right Multiplications. In one line each, $L_{\tilde P}L_{\tilde R}(\tilde X)=\tilde P^{\natural}(\tilde R^{\natural}\tilde X)=(\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde R^{\natural})\tilde X=(\tilde R\tilde P)^{\natural}\tilde X$, the last step being the anti-automorphism property of ${\natural}$, $\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde R^{\natural}=(\tilde R\tilde P)^{\natural}$; and $\varrho_{\tilde P}\varrho_{\tilde R}(\tilde X)=\tilde X\tilde R^{\natural}\tilde P^{\natural}=\tilde X(\tilde P\tilde R)^{\natural}$. The order reversal is exactly the anti-automorphism: it is not the associativity of $\star$, which fails, but the associativity of the plain product read through ${\natural}$.

Corollary (the two monoids). The set $M_L=\{L_{\tilde P}\}$ is a monoid with identity $L_{e_0}=\mathrm{id}$, and the assignment $\tilde P\mapsto L_{\tilde P}$ is an injective anti-isomorphism of monoids from the multiplicative monoid of the plain algebra onto $M_L$,

$$ M_L\cong\mathbb{B}^{\mathrm{op}}, $$

the opposite monoid. The set $M_R=\{\varrho_{\tilde P}\}$ is a monoid with identity $\varrho_{e_0}=\mathrm{id}$ and $\tilde P\mapsto\varrho_{\tilde P}$ is an isomorphism $M_R\cong\mathbb{B}$. Where the anti-isomorphism lies is in the natural conjugation: it is the anti-automorphism, and only it, that reverses the order of a composite.

Proof. Closure and the identities are read off the laws: $L_{\tilde P}L_{\tilde R}=L_{\tilde R\tilde P}$ is again a left multiplication, and $L_{e_0}(\tilde X)=e_0^{\natural}\tilde X=\tilde X$. Injectivity is read at $e_0$, $L_{\tilde P}(e_0)=\tilde P^{\natural}$, which recovers $\tilde P$. The group of units of $M_L$ is the unit group of the plain algebra with the reversed law, and an operator $L_{\tilde P}$ is invertible exactly when $N(\tilde P)\neq0$.

Proposition (the product has no two-sided unit). The element $e_0$ is a left unit of $\star$, $e_0\star\tilde Q=\tilde Q$, and there is no right unit: the right action of the identity is the natural conjugation, $\tilde Q\star e_0=\tilde Q^{\natural}$, which differs from $\tilde Q$ for every non-central $\tilde Q$. The product therefore has no two-sided unit and is unital on one side alone, and the right multiplication $R_{\tilde P}$ of the product does not close under composition: $R_{\tilde P}R_{\tilde Q}(\tilde X)=\tilde Q^{\natural}\tilde X\tilde P$. The monoids $M_L$ and $M_R$ nevertheless contain the two-sided identity $\mathrm{id}$.

Proof. The unit statements are the two evaluations $e_0^{\natural}=e_0$ and $\tilde Q^{\natural}e_0=\tilde Q^{\natural}$; a right unit $u$ would satisfy $\tilde X^{\natural}u=\tilde X$ for all $\tilde X$, and at $\tilde X=e_0$ this forces $u=e_0$, which then fails at a pure $\tilde X$. The non-closure is The Left Multiplications of the Quaternionic Product and the Opposite Monoid, §The Right Multiplications, with the witness $R_{e_1}R_{e_1}(e_0)=e_0$ and $R_{e_1}R_{e_1}(e_1)=e_1$, whereas a right multiplication of the product with value $e_0$ at $e_0$ is $R_{e_0}$, which sends $e_1$ to $-e_1$.

Read as a physical statement. The orientation of the composition is the operator form of the orientation of the material composition: the first slot is read through the natural conjugation and the second as it stands, so an element attached on the left and the same element attached on the right are different operations, and only the left attachment composes on the nose. The composition of two left multiplications is a left multiplication — the operations of the band form a monoid and compose associatively — although the product of two elements does not associate. The reading is the corpus's: the process is the map and the state is the idempotent (The Monoid of Acting Maps), and here the process is the opposite monoid, the element being only a representative read through the conjugation.

Theorem (the mixed composites span the endomorphism algebra). For all $\tilde P,\tilde Q$ the mixed composites commute and close,

$$ L_{\tilde P}\circ\varrho_{\tilde Q}=\varrho_{\tilde Q}\circ L_{\tilde P},\qquad L_{\tilde P}\circ\varrho_{\tilde Q}(\tilde X)=\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde X\tilde Q^{\natural}, $$

and their complex span is the whole endomorphism algebra $\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb{C}}\{L_{\tilde P}\varrho_{\tilde Q}\}=\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb{C}}(\mathbb{B})$, of complex dimension sixteen.

Proof. Commutation and closure are the plain associativity read in the two-sided regular representation, and the span is The Left Multiplications of the Quaternionic Product and the Opposite Monoid, §The Mixed Compositions: the sixteen operators $L_{e_m}\varrho_{e_n}$ are the products of the two bases $\{L_{e_m}\}$ and $\{\varrho_{e_n}\}$ of the left and the right regular representation and are linearly independent, so they already span all of $\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb{C}}(\mathbb{B})$. The two-sided family itself is the image of the decomposable tensors of $\mathbb{B}\otimes_{\mathbb{C}}\mathbb{B}^{\mathrm{op}}$, the linearisation of the pair $(\tilde P,\tilde Q)$; a sum of two-sided operators is two-sided only when the corresponding tensor is decomposable, and a general endomorphism of the frame is such a sum and not a single two-sided product.

Read as a physical statement. The two-sided operations of the band are closed under composition and generate every linear operation of the frame: the failure of associativity of the product is invisible at the level of the operators, which generate the full endomorphism algebra. What the product loses by its twisting of the first slot it recovers in its operator algebra, and the two-sided operator is again the carrier of the general linear action on the frame, as it is for the plain product. The reading is a naming of a proved algebraic fact and is labelled so.

The Adjoint of the Quaternionic Form Keeps the Sides

Theorem (the adjoints for the quaternionic form). For all $\tilde P,\tilde X,\tilde Y\in\mathbb{B}$,

$$ \bigl\langle L_{\tilde P}\tilde X,\tilde Y\bigr\rangle_{\natural}=\bigl\langle\tilde X,L_{\tilde P^{\natural}}\tilde Y\bigr\rangle_{\natural}, \qquad\text{that is}\qquad \bigl(L_{\tilde P}\bigr)^{N}=L_{\tilde P^{\natural}}, $$

and the same for the twisted right multiplication, $(\varrho_{\tilde P})^{N}=\varrho_{\tilde P^{\natural}}$.

Proof. The adjoints are One-Sided Operators on the General Quaternionic Algebra of Biquaternions, §The Adjoints for the Bilinear Form. In one line, $\langle L_{\tilde P}\tilde X,\tilde Y\rangle_{\natural}=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde X\tilde Y^{\natural})$, and by the cyclicity of the scalar part this equals $\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde X\tilde Y^{\natural}\tilde P^{\natural})=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde X(\tilde P\tilde Y)^{\natural})=\langle\tilde X,L_{\tilde P^{\natural}}\tilde Y\rangle_{\natural}$, using $\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Y^{\natural}=(\tilde P\tilde Y)^{\natural}$. The right case is the same computation with the factors in the other order.

Corollary (the adjoint keeps the side and conjugates the parameter). The adjoint of a left multiplication is a left multiplication, the adjoint of a twisted right multiplication is a twisted right multiplication, and in both cases the parameter is carried through the natural conjugation. The adjoint therefore maps each family onto itself and preserves the side, and it acts on the parameter by ${\natural}$ and on nothing else.

Proof. Immediate from the theorem, and the involution ${}^{\natural}$ of order two makes the adjoint an involution on each family, $((L_{\tilde P})^{N})^{N}=L_{\tilde P}$.

Read as a physical statement. The pairing of the band is the interval, and transporting an operation across it leaves the side alone: an operation attached before an argument is still attached before it afterwards, and only the parameter is conjugated. This is the operator content of the statement that the interval is the invariant form of a single attachment, so the form remembers the side on which an operation stands and forgets nothing of it. The contrast with the composition band is total: there the form is the scalar part of the product and cannot tell a left attachment from a right one, and its adjoint is the swap. That the swap belongs to the plain form alone is the reason no type criterion of the general plain algebra is a criterion on one parameter, while every type criterion of this band is; the comparison of the four adjoints is the crossing reader The Operators of the Twelve Structures: Multiplication, Adjoint and Bracket, which is not one of the twelve.

The Types of the Operators

Definition (the types). For a form $\varphi$ on $\mathbb{B}$, an operator $T$ is $\varphi$-self-adjoint when $T^{\varphi}=T$, $\varphi$-skew-adjoint when $T^{\varphi}=-T$, and $\varphi$-preserving when $\varphi(T\tilde X,T\tilde Y)=\varphi(\tilde X,\tilde Y)$ for all $\tilde X,\tilde Y$. Here $\varphi$ is the quaternionic form $N$ and $T^{\varphi}$ is the quaternionic adjoint ${}^{N}$ of the previous section.

Corollary (the one-sided types). The left multiplication $L_{\tilde P}$ is $N$-self-adjoint exactly for a central parameter, $N$-skew-adjoint exactly for a pure parameter, and $N$-preserving exactly for a parameter of norm one, $N(\tilde P)=1$; the twisted right multiplication $\varrho_{\tilde P}$ obeys the same three criteria on the same parameter. Each criterion is a criterion on the single parameter.

Proof. By the injectivity of the parameter map, $L_{\tilde P^{\natural}}=L_{\tilde P}$ is $\tilde P^{\natural}=\tilde P$, that is $\tilde P$ central, and $L_{\tilde P^{\natural}}=-L_{\tilde P}=L_{-\tilde P}$ is $\tilde P^{\natural}=-\tilde P$, that is $\tilde P$ pure; the preservation criterion is the scaling of the form below, $N(L_{\tilde P}\tilde X)=N(\tilde P)N(\tilde X)$, which is the identity for all $\tilde X$ exactly when $N(\tilde P)=1$. The two statements are One-Sided Operators on the General Quaternionic Algebra of Biquaternions, §The Adjoints for the Bilinear Form, §The Norm and the Determinant of a Multiplication.

Corollary (the two-sided types). The twisted two-sided operator $\Theta_{\tilde A}=L_{\tilde A^{\natural}}\varrho_{\tilde A}$, the map $\Theta_{\tilde A}(\tilde X)=\tilde A\tilde X\tilde A^{\natural}$, has adjoint $(\Theta_{\tilde A})^{N}=\Theta_{\tilde A^{\natural}}$; it is $N$-self-adjoint exactly when $\tilde A^{\natural}=\pm\tilde A$, that is exactly when the parameter lies in the centre or the vector subspace, $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}\cup\mathrm{Vect}(\mathbb{B})$, and no nonzero $\Theta_{\tilde A}$ is $N$-skew-adjoint.

Proof. The adjoint is Two-Sided Operators on the General Quaternionic Algebra of Biquaternions, §The Adjoint: the adjoint of a product is the product of the adjoints in reverse order and the factors commute, giving $(\Theta_{\tilde A})^{N}=(\varrho_{\tilde A})^{N}(L_{\tilde A^{\natural}})^{N}=\varrho_{\tilde A^{\natural}}L_{\tilde A}=\Theta_{\tilde A^{\natural}}$. Self-adjointness is $\Theta_{\tilde A^{\natural}}=\Theta_{\tilde A}$, and injectivity up to sign gives $\tilde A^{\natural}=\pm\tilde A$, the two eigenspaces of the natural conjugation; the skew sector collapses by the computation of the same proposition. The parameter map is injective up to the sign of its argument, so the criterion is a criterion on one parameter and not on a pair.

Read as a physical statement. The two populated sectors are the two ways a single parameter can be symmetric for the interval: a central parameter gives a self-adjoint multiplication, a pure parameter gives a skew-adjoint one, and a mixed parameter gives neither. The skew family is the vector subspace, of real dimension three, and it is the operator form of the statement that the material directions and the time direction are read by the interval with opposite signs; unlike the composition band, where the skew sector is trivial and no generator appears, this band carries a three-dimensional family of skew-adjoint multiplications. That family does not close under composition, the product of two pure parameters being mixed in general, so the generators of the band are read through the bracket of the antisymmetric band and not through the multiplication. All of this is a reading of a proved algebraic criterion and is labelled so.

The Symmetries of the Form and of the Product

Proposition (the conformal factor and the determinant). For all $\tilde P,\tilde X$,

$$ N\bigl(L_{\tilde P}\tilde X\bigr)=N(\tilde P)\,N(\tilde X),\qquad \det L_{\tilde P}=N(\tilde P)^{2}, $$

and the same two identities hold for $\varrho_{\tilde P}$.

Proof. The first is the multiplicativity of the norm, $N(\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde X)=N(\tilde P^{\natural})N(\tilde X)=N(\tilde P)N(\tilde X)$ with $N(\tilde P^{\natural})=N(\tilde P)$; the determinant is One-Sided Operators on the General Quaternionic Algebra of Biquaternions, §The Norm and the Determinant of a Multiplication, where the multiplication is the Kronecker product of the matrix $\Phi(\tilde P)$ with $I_2$ and the determinant is $\det\Phi(\tilde P)^{2}=N(\tilde P)^{2}$.

Corollary (the form-preserving operators). The one-sided operators preserving the quaternionic form are exactly the left and the twisted right multiplications by a parameter of norm one, and their group is the norm-one slice, of complex dimension three; a multiplication by a parameter of norm $-1$ reverses the sign of the form. A twisted two-sided operator $\Theta_{\tilde A}$ preserves the form exactly when $N(\tilde A)=\pm1$, the norm-one part being the inner automorphisms and the whole set the pin group.

Proof. The condition is $N(\tilde P)=1$ from the conformal factor, and $N(\Theta_{\tilde A}\tilde X)=N(\tilde A)^{2}N(\tilde X)$ for the two-sided case, Two-Sided Operators on the General Quaternionic Algebra of Biquaternions, §Automorphisms. The norm-one slice is closed under the plain product by multiplicativity of the norm, and it is the slice whose elements the corpus reads as the Lorentz transformations on $\mathbb{M}_-$; the group of two-sided form-preserving operators is the biquaternion pin group (The Pin and Spin Groups of the General Quaternionic Algebra of Biquaternions).

Theorem (the quadratic two-sided operator and its multiplication law). The twisted two-sided operator $\Theta_{\tilde A}=\tilde A\,[\,]\,\tilde A^{\natural}$ is quadratic in its parameter, $\Theta_{\lambda\tilde A}=\lambda^{2}\Theta_{\tilde A}$ and $\Theta_{-\tilde A}=\Theta_{\tilde A}$, and it satisfies

$$ \Theta_{\tilde A}\circ\Theta_{\tilde B}=\Theta_{\tilde A\tilde B},\qquad \Theta_{\tilde A}(\tilde Y)\,\Theta_{\tilde A}(\tilde Z)=N(\tilde A)\,\Theta_{\tilde A}(\tilde Y\tilde Z) \qquad\text{for every }\tilde A . $$

Proof. The composition and the multiplication law are Two-Sided Operators on the General Quaternionic Algebra of Biquaternions, §The Multiplication Law: the multiplication law is the one line $\tilde A\tilde Y\tilde A^{\natural}\tilde A\tilde Z\tilde A^{\natural}=\tilde A\tilde YN(\tilde A)\tilde Z\tilde A^{\natural}$, which uses $\tilde A^{\natural}\tilde A=N(\tilde A)e_0$, and the norm is central and may be moved out. This holds for every parameter, since $\tilde A^{\natural}\tilde A=N(\tilde A)$ is central always and no centrality of a product of two parameters is required.

Corollary (the multiplicative operators). The two-sided operators of the band that are strictly multiplicative, $T(\tilde Y)T(\tilde Z)=T(\tilde Y\tilde Z)$, are exactly those with the two slots inverse, $L_{\tilde P}\varrho_{\tilde Q}$ with $\tilde P\tilde Q=e_0$, and these are the inner automorphisms $\mathrm{Ad}_{\tilde P^{\natural}}$; the family multiplicative up to a scalar is exactly the family whose slot product is central, the scalar being $(\tilde P\tilde Q)^{\natural}$; and the twisted two-sided operators are, up to scalars, exactly the multiplicative-up-to-a-scalar family.

Proof. The plain criterion is Two-Sided Operators on the General Quaternionic Algebra of Biquaternions, §When a Product $L_aR_b$ Is Two-Sided: the product is multiplicative up to the inserted scalar $\tilde B\tilde A$, so here up to $(\tilde P\tilde Q)^{\natural}$, central exactly when $\tilde P\tilde Q$ is, and equal to $e_0$ exactly when $\tilde P\tilde Q=e_0$. With $\tilde Q=\tilde P^{-1}$ the operator is exactly the inner automorphism $\mathrm{Ad}_{\tilde P^{\natural}}$, which is the scalar multiple $N(\tilde P)^{-1}\Theta_{\tilde P^{\natural}}$ of the twisted two-sided operator.

Read as a physical statement. The interval scales by the norm of a multiplying parameter, so the multiplication of the band is a conformal map of the interval with conformal factor the norm, and the form-preserving ones are exactly the norm-one parameters, the Lorentz transformations. The determinant reading is the same statement squared, and it fixes the invertibility of the operator by the norm of the parameter. The multiplication law is the second reading: the composition of a two-sided operator with itself is the operator again, up to the norm of the parameter as a scalar, and this is unconditional in the band while the composition band carried it only when the product of the two parameters was central. The norm is the scalar of the composition: it is what the product of the two slots leaves behind, and it is the only obstruction to a two-sided operation being an automorphism. The readings are named norm conformality and unconditional multiplicativity and are labelled as readings.

The Operators Acting on the State Space

The operators of the band act on the state space of the corpus, and since $L_{\tilde P}=L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde P^{\natural}}$ their action is the plain action with the parameter conjugated. Let $S=\mathbb{B}\tilde\Pi_1=\mathbb{C}\{\tilde\Pi_1,\tilde\Upsilon_2\}$ be the minimal left ideal, with $\tilde\Pi_2=e_0-\tilde\Pi_1$ the complementary idempotent and $\mathbb{B}\tilde\Pi_2=\mathbb{C}\{\tilde\Pi_2,\tilde\Upsilon_1\}$ the complement, as in The Spinor Space as a Module and as a Hilbert Space and The Complementary Space of the Spinor Space and Its Physical Meaning.

Proposition (the left actions exhaust the operators of the state space). The map $\mathbb{B}\to\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb{C}}(S)$, $\tilde P\mapsto L_{\tilde P}|_{S}$, is an isomorphism: the plain algebra acts faithfully on its defining module $S$ of complex dimension two, and both spaces have complex dimension four, so the four plain left multiplications by the basis elements form a $\mathbb{C}$-basis of $\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb{C}}(S)$. The four of the band differ from that basis only by the signs of the vector directions,

$$ L_{e_0}=\mathrm{id},\qquad L_{e_k}=-L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{e_k}\quad(k=1,2,3), $$

the signs entering because the left multiplication reads its parameter through the conjugation; they are therefore a basis as well, and every operator of the state space is a left multiplication of the band.

Proof. The module $S=\mathbb{B}\tilde\Pi_1$, its complex dimension two and the faithfulness of the left action are The Spinor Space as a Module and as a Hilbert Space and Biquaternion Objects and Their Matrix Correspondences; the equality $L_{\tilde P}=L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde P^{\natural}}$ together with $e_\mu^{\natural}=\varepsilon_\mu e_\mu$ and $\varepsilon=(1,-1,-1,-1)$ is the isotope identity of The Specificities of the General Quaternionic Algebra, §The Isotope Identity.

Proposition (the projectors, and the swap of the two ideals). The left multiplication $L_{\tilde P}$ is a projector exactly when $\tilde P$ is a plain idempotent; and for the two minimal idempotents, whose natural conjugates are exchanged, $\tilde\Pi_1^{\natural}=\tilde\Pi_2$ and $\tilde\Pi_2^{\natural}=\tilde\Pi_1$, $L_{\tilde\Pi_1}=L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde\Pi_2}$ is the plain projector onto the complementary ideal $\mathbb{B}\tilde\Pi_2$, while $L_{\tilde\Pi_2}=L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde\Pi_1}$ is the plain projector onto $S$; the two are orthogonal, $L_{\tilde\Pi_1}L_{\tilde\Pi_2}=0$. In particular the element $\tilde\Pi_1$ is a nilpotent of the band's own product, $\tilde\Pi_1\star\tilde\Pi_1=N(\tilde\Pi_1)e_0=0$, while the operator it defines is a projector: the band realises a projector on an element of vanishing square.

Proof. The projector criterion is The Specificities of the General Quaternionic Algebra, §The Projectors: the faithfulness of the parameter map reduces $L_{\tilde P}^{2}=L_{\tilde P}$ to $(\tilde P^{\natural})^{2}=\tilde P^{\natural}$, and ${\natural}$ permutes the plain idempotents, so the elements are exactly the plain idempotents. The exchange $\tilde\Pi_1^{\natural}=\tilde\Pi_2$ is the computation $\tfrac12(e_0+ie_3)^{\natural}=\tfrac12(e_0-ie_3)=\tilde\Pi_2$, and the nilpotence is the same specificities article, $N(\tilde\Pi_1)=0$ on a nontrivial plain idempotent.

Proposition (the twisted right actions are the commutant). A twisted right multiplication commutes with every left one, so it is a $\mathbb{B}$-linear map of $S$ into itself or into the complement; the operators commuting with every left multiplication are exactly the twisted right multiplications, and the operators commuting with both families are the central scalars. A central element gives the same left and twisted right action, the scalar multiplication by its scalar part.

Proof. The commutation is the mixed law of the first section, and the double-centraliser statement is One-Sided Operators on the General Quaternionic Algebra of Biquaternions, §The Two Multiplications. The nilpotent twisted right multiplications carrying $S$ onto the complement are the plain ones of The Complementary Space of the Spinor Space and Its Physical Meaning.

Read as a physical statement. The left actions of the band are the observables on the state space, and every operator of the state space is one of them, with the parameter read through the conjugation — the state space sees the element only through its conjugate. The projectors are the plain ones, and the two diagonal projectors of the state space and of its complement are exchanged by the conjugation: the band's own left multiplication by the minimal idempotent projects onto the other ideal. This is a physical sharpening of the statement that the band's product recognises almost none of its own projectors: the element whose square vanishes defines the projector, and the element whose left multiplication projects is the conjugate of a plain idempotent. The readings are named conjugated observables and exchanged ideals and are labelled as readings.

The Sandwich and the Inner Automorphism

Proposition (one conjugation, two parametrisations). For every unit $\tilde A$,

$$ \Theta_{\tilde A}=N(\tilde A)\,\mathrm{Ad}_{\tilde A},\qquad \mathrm{Ad}_{\tilde A}(\tilde X)=\tilde A\tilde X\tilde A^{-1}, $$

so the twisted two-sided operator is the inner automorphism scaled by the norm; the two operators agree exactly on the norm-one parameters, and there and alone.

Proof. The factorisation is Two-Sided Operators on the General Quaternionic Algebra of Biquaternions, §The Sign and the Inverse: it is $\tilde A^{\natural}=N(\tilde A)\tilde A^{-1}$ read in the right factor, the norm being central. The two agree exactly when the scalar is one, that is $N(\tilde A)=1$.

Corollary (the two operators and the form). The twisted two-sided operator scales the quaternionic form by the factor $N(\tilde A)^{2}$, $N(\Theta_{\tilde A}\tilde X,\Theta_{\tilde A}\tilde Y)=N(\tilde A)^{2}N(\tilde X,\tilde Y)$, while the inner automorphism preserves it exactly, $N(\mathrm{Ad}_{\tilde A}\tilde X,\mathrm{Ad}_{\tilde A}\tilde Y)=N(\tilde X,\tilde Y)$, being an isometry of the form for every unit; on the norm-one slice the two operators coincide, and there the scaling of $\Theta$ is the identity too. Off the slice they differ by the central scalar $N(\tilde A)$, which is the whole content of the word twisted: the sandwich is the inner automorphism continued to the units of all norms by the conjugation in place of the inverse.

Proof. The scaling of $\Theta$ by $N(\tilde A)^{2}$ is the automorphisms proposition of the previous section; for $\mathrm{Ad}$ it is the multiplicativity of the norm, $N(\tilde A\tilde X\tilde A^{-1})=N(\tilde A)N(\tilde X)N(\tilde A)^{-1}=N(\tilde X)$, which by polarisation is the preservation of the bilinear form; equality on the slice is the proposition.

A Worked Case

Take one mixed parameter of norm two,

$$ \tilde A=e_0+e_1,\qquad N(\tilde A)=2,\qquad \tilde A^{\natural}=e_0-e_1,\qquad \tilde A^{2}=2e_1 . $$

The left multiplication by the parameter is the plain left multiplication by its conjugate, and it reads

$$ L_{\tilde A}(e_0)=e_0-e_1,\qquad L_{\tilde A}(e_1)=e_0+e_1,\qquad L_{\tilde A}(e_2)=e_2-e_3 , $$

so the composition law reads on $e_1$, $L_{\tilde A}L_{\tilde A}(e_1)=2e_0=L_{\tilde A^{2}}(e_1)=L_{2e_1}(e_1)$, the parameter on the right being the plain square. The adjoint keeps the left side and conjugates the parameter, displayed on the basis pair $(e_0,e_1)$:

$$ \bigl\langle L_{\tilde A}e_0,e_1\bigr\rangle_{\natural}=-1=\bigl\langle e_0,L_{\tilde A^{\natural}}e_1\bigr\rangle_{\natural}, \qquad L_{\tilde A^{\natural}}=L_{e_0-e_1} . $$

The parameter is mixed, so $L_{\tilde A}$ is neither self-adjoint nor skew-adjoint, and its norm two scales the form by two, so it is not form-preserving. By contrast, the pure parameter $\tilde V=e_1$ has norm one and gives a skew-adjoint and form-preserving multiplication, the two properties the two criteria promise at once.

The twisted two-sided operator of the same parameter is $\Theta_{\tilde A}=2\,\mathrm{Ad}_{\tilde A}$, and on the basis it is

$$ \Theta_{\tilde A}(e_0)=2e_0,\qquad \Theta_{\tilde A}(e_1)=2e_1,\qquad \Theta_{\tilde A}(e_2)=2e_3,\qquad \Theta_{\tilde A}(e_3)=-2e_2 , $$

a scaling by two on the plane $\mathbb{C}\{e_0,e_1\}$ and twice a quarter turn on the plane $\mathbb{C}\{e_2,e_3\}$. The quadraticity is visible at once, $\Theta_{2\tilde A}=4\,\Theta_{\tilde A}$, and the multiplication law holds for this parameter with $N(\tilde A)=2$ as its scalar: for $\tilde Y=(1,2,3,4)$ and $\tilde Z=e_1$ the two sides of $\Theta_{\tilde A}(\tilde Y)\Theta_{\tilde A}(\tilde Z)=2\,\Theta_{\tilde A}(\tilde Y\tilde Z)$ are both $(-8,4,12,16)$. The operator is not form-preserving, since its parameter has norm two, and it is not an automorphism, in agreement with the criterion.

The causal division is displayed by three material four-vectors and one Lorentz parameter. With the material sector convention $\tilde T=ict\,e_0+\mathbf x$ and $c=1$,

$$ N(5ie_0+3e_1)=-16,\qquad N(3ie_0+3e_1)=0,\qquad N(3ie_0+5e_1)=16, $$

the first timelike, the second null, on the causal cone, and the third spacelike. A boost parameter of norm one, $\tilde\Lambda=\cosh u\,e_0+\sinh u\,ie_1$, has $N(\tilde\Lambda)=1$, and its left multiplication is a form-preserving operator: $N(L_{\tilde\Lambda}(5ie_0+3e_1))=N(5ie_0+3e_1)=-16$ for every $u$, the interval of the timelike four-vector unchanged.

Physical Readings

The operators of the quaternionic product read as the two ways of attaching a material operation to an element — through the interval on the first slot, as it stands on the second — and as the conjugation sandwich of the band. The job word of the band is causality, and the operator layer supplies its four statements. Summary

The operators of the quaternionic product $\tilde P\star\tilde Q=\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q$ are the left multiplication $L_{\tilde P}(\tilde X)=\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde X$, the right multiplication $R_{\tilde P}(\tilde X)=\tilde X^{\natural}\tilde P$ and the twisted right multiplication $\varrho_{\tilde P}(\tilde X)=\tilde X\tilde P^{\natural}$, with the two-sided operators $L_{\tilde P}\varrho_{\tilde Q}$. The left family composes by the reversed law $L_{\tilde P}L_{\tilde R}=L_{\tilde R\tilde P}$, so it is a monoid $M_L\cong\mathbb{B}^{\mathrm{op}}$ anti-isomorphic to the plain multiplicative monoid, the anti-isomorphism lying in the natural conjugation; the twisted right family composes by the direct law and is a monoid isomorphic to $\mathbb{B}$; the right multiplication of the product does not close; the product has a left unit and no right unit, so no two-sided unit; and the mixed composites commute and span the whole endomorphism algebra of complex dimension sixteen. The form of the band is the general quaternionic bilinear form $N(\tilde P,\tilde Q)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q)$ of Gram matrix $I_4$, the polar form of the norm, and its adjoint — the quaternionic adjoint ${}^{N}$ — keeps the side and carries the parameter through the natural conjugation: $(L_{\tilde P})^{N}=L_{\tilde P^{\natural}}$ and $(\varrho_{\tilde P})^{N}=\varrho_{\tilde P^{\natural}}$. Every type criterion is therefore a criterion on one parameter: a one-sided operator is self-adjoint exactly for a central parameter, skew-adjoint exactly for a pure one, and form-preserving exactly for norm one, each multiplication scaling the form by the norm of its parameter with determinant the square of that norm. The twisted two-sided operator $\Theta_{\tilde A}=\tilde A[\,]\tilde A^{\natural}$ is quadratic in its parameter and multiplicative up to the norm for every parameter, $\Theta_{\tilde A}(\tilde Y)\Theta_{\tilde A}(\tilde Z)=N(\tilde A)\Theta_{\tilde A}(\tilde Y\tilde Z)$, where the plain two-sided operator carried only a partial law and then only when the product of the two parameters was central; its adjoint is $\Theta_{\tilde A^{\natural}}$, it is self-adjoint exactly for a parameter in the centre or the vector subspace and never skew-adjoint, it preserves the form exactly for $N(\tilde A)=\pm1$, and it equals $N(\tilde A)\mathrm{Ad}_{\tilde A}$ on the units, agreeing with the inner automorphism exactly on the norm-one slice. On the state space the left actions exhaust the operators of the module up to the sign of the vector directions, the twisted right actions are the commutant, and the two diagonal projectors are exchanged by the conjugation. Read on the interval, the band is the causality band: the invariant form is the interval of signature $(3,1)$, its null set the causal cone, the norm-one parameters are the Lorentz transformations, and the division of the parameters by the sign of the norm is the causal division.

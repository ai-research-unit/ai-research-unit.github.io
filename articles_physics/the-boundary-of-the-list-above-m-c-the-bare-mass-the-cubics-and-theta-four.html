The Boundary of the List Above $M_C$: The Bare Mass, the Cubics and $\theta_4$

Introduction

The question. Six structures are in the census with zero coefficients, and $\theta_4$ is outside the census altogether. For each, does the group forbid it or does a coefficient decline it? The two answers are not the same and the difference is the article.

The answer.

$$ \text{forbidden}:1\ (\text{vector mass});\qquad \text{declined}:6;\qquad \text{outside as a parameter}:1\ (\theta_4). $$

One of the seven absences is a theorem and six are choices. A reader who treats them alike will think the model is more constrained than it is.

The Seven, and Which Kind Each Is

structure kind warrant
$m^{2}\operatorname{tr}A_{\imath}A^{\imath}$ forbidden no invariant dimension-two pairing in the adjoint
$m_{\alpha\beta}\bar\xi^{\alpha}\xi^{\beta}$ declined one source of mass in the ledger
$c\,\operatorname{tr}\Xi$ forced the symmetric vacuum sets it to zero
$\operatorname{tr}\Xi^{3}$, $\operatorname{tr}\Xi^{2}\operatorname{tr}\Xi$, $(\operatorname{tr}\Xi)^{3}$ declined evenness under $\Xi\to-\Xi$
$\lambda_{5}\operatorname{tr}\Xi^{3}\operatorname{tr}\Xi$ declined evenness, declared separately
$\theta_{4}\operatorname{tr}F\tilde F$ outside the fourth condition: a total derivative

Three kinds and not two. "Declined" is a coefficient's decision; "forced" is a vacuum's; "forbidden" is the group's; and $\theta_4$ is outside all three because the rule removed it before coefficients were consulted.

The Vector Mass Is Forbidden

$$ m^{2}\operatorname{tr}A_{\imath}A^{\imath} \ \text{is invariant, dimension two, and Lorentz scalar — and it is not there.} $$

The reason is local invariance. Under $A_{\imath}\to UA_{\imath}U^{\dagger}+(i/g_{4})U\partial_{\imath}U^{\dagger}$ the field shifts by a derivative of the gauge function, and a term quadratic in $A$ with no derivative cannot be invariant: the shift produces a piece linear in $A$ and a piece quadratic in $\partial U$, and nothing in $m^{2}\operatorname{tr}A^{2}$ cancels either.

So the vector mass is absent by the group's structure and not by anyone's choice. It becomes possible only when a condensate supplies a direction, and below $M_C$ the Proca terms appear exactly because $\langle\Xi\rangle\neq0$, which is the restructuring's work and not this phase's.

This is a theorem and can be stated as one. In a phase with no condensate, a connection in the adjoint of a simple group has no mass term. It is the statement that the fifteen stay massless until the vacuum picks a direction.

The Fermion Mass Is Declined

$\bar\xi\xi$ satisfies every condition the vector mass fails.

$$ m_{\alpha\beta}\bar\xi^{\alpha}\xi^{\beta} \ \text{is invariant, dimension four, and Lorentz scalar.} $$

It is allowed and omitted. The ledger carries $\Xi$ as the one source of fermion mass, and a bare mass would be a second source the ledger does not hold.

What the omission costs. Two real parameters, and the whole question of whether the fermion masses come from the condensate or from a bare term. The corpus's choice is that they come from the condensate, and the Yukawa sector shows what that predicts: $m_{\ell}=3m_{q}$, which is wrong by twenty-seven.

The honest reading is that the bare mass is the model's most tempting repair and the corpus refuses it. A model that added it would rescue the ratio and lose the claim that the condensate is the only source. That trade is stated here and not hidden.

The Cubics Are Declared, and Evenness Is the Reason

$$ \Xi\to-\Xi \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \operatorname{tr}\Xi^{3}\to-\operatorname{tr}\Xi^{3}, \qquad \operatorname{tr}\Xi^{3}\operatorname{tr}\Xi\to-\operatorname{tr}\Xi^{3}\operatorname{tr}\Xi . $$

One discrete symmetry removes three cubics and the fifth quartic together. The corpus declares evenness rather than declaring four separate zeros, and the four follow from one statement.

The even symmetry also protects the vacuum. A cubic would shift the minimum away from $\Xi=0$, so evenness is what keeps the symmetric point symmetric, and it is the same reason the linear term is forced to zero.

The declaration is a basis statement and the census says so. Under a trace shift the cubics do not stay zero, because $\operatorname{tr}\Xi^{3}=\operatorname{tr}\Xi_{0}^{3}+\tfrac34s\operatorname{tr}\Xi_{0}^{2}+\tfrac1{16}s^{3}$; the zeros hold in the canonical basis and not in every basis.

$\theta_4$ Is Outside and Not Declined

The difference matters and is easy to miss. The six declined structures are rows with zero coefficients; $\theta_4$ has no row.

$$ \theta_{4}\varepsilon^{\imath\jmath\kappa\lambda}\operatorname{tr}F_{\imath\jmath}F_{\kappa\lambda} =\theta_{4}\,\partial_{\imath}K^{\imath}, $$

so it integrates to a surface term and the fourth condition removes it before any coefficient is consulted. It survives as a parameter and not as a row, exactly as $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ does below $M_C$.

So the count of absences is seven and the count of zero rows is six. A reader who conflated them would ask why the ledger says six and the boundary lists seven; the answer is that one of the seven was never a row.

What the Boundary Costs

missing structure parameters it would add what it would change
bare mass $2$ the fermion mass ratio, and $CP$
three cubics $3$ the vacuum's location; the even symmetry
fifth quartic $1$ the boundedness cone
$\theta_4$ $1$ (already a parameter) strong $CP$, were it measurable

Seven parameters lie outside the fifteen because of these decisions, and the coefficients article counts them separately and never adds them to the fifteen.

And the bare mass is not simply additive. It shares its flavour group with $y$, so switching it on gives three matrices under one $\mathrm{U}(2)\times\mathrm{U}(2)$ and $24-8=16$ numbers, not eight plus two.

Checks

  1. The vector mass. Forbidden: no invariant dimension-two pairing in the adjoint.
  2. The fermion mass. Declined: two parameters, allowed by the group.
  3. The linear term. Forced: $\partial_{\Xi}\operatorname{tr}\Xi=\mathbb1\neq0$.
  4. The cubics. Three, removed by $\Xi\to-\Xi$.
  5. The fifth quartic. Removed by the same evenness, declared separately.
  6. $\theta_4$. Outside: a total derivative, no row.
  7. The counts. Seven absences, six zero rows, one parameter without a row.

Summary

One absence is a theorem — the vector mass — and six are choices; a seventh, $\theta_4$, was never a row at all. The group forbids the vector mass, the vacuum forces the linear term to zero, evenness removes the cubics and the fifth quartic, and the fermion mass is allowed and declined.

The bare mass is the tempting repair and the corpus refuses it, at a cost of two parameters and the model's one quantitative prediction.

Conflating "declined" with "forbidden" is the error this article exists to prevent, and separating $\theta_4$ from both is its second purpose.

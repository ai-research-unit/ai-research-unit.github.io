What the List Omits: Ghosts, the Theta Term, Neutrino Mass and Gravity

Introduction

The claim. Four things a reader might expect in a Standard Model Lagrangian are not in the list, and each is absent for a different reason: ghosts are not fields of the content, the theta term is a total derivative with no vertex, the neutrino mass has no dimension-four ancestor, and gravity is not a gauge field of this content at all.

$$ \boxed{\;\text{omitted: ghosts, }G\tilde G,\ \nu\text{ mass, gravity}\;\Longrightarrow\;\text{four reasons, no shared cause}\;} $$

Why the four belong in one article. A reader who finds an omission and assumes it is an oversight will look for a rule that admits the missing term. There is no single rule; there are four different reasons, and stating them together prevents the mistake of over-generalising any one of them.

The standing. Each omission has a corpus article of its own, and the four are cited in the sections below. This article is the index and the statement of reasons, not a re-derivation.

Ghosts

What they are. The Faddeev–Popov fields that appear when a gauge is fixed covariantly. They are anticommuting scalars in the adjoint representation, they cancel the unphysical polarisations in loops, and they have no asymptotic states.

Why they are not rows. They are not fields of the content: they are the determinant that the gauge-fixing procedure introduces, and the determinant is a device for computing the same physical amplitudes that the unitary gauge computes directly. The list is written in unitary gauge, where the ghosts are absent.

What a reader would see in another gauge. In an $R_\xi$ gauge the list would contain the gauge-fixing terms, the Goldstones and the ghost terms, and the physical amplitudes would be unchanged. The Unitary-Gauge Image states the same point for the Goldstones.

The signature question. The corpus separately argues that its own operators carry no ghost-like sign in the quadratic form, on account of the metric's signature. That argument is about the algebra and not about the Standard Model list; it is not needed here, because unitary gauge needs no ghosts in the first place.

The Theta Term

What it is. $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}}G^a_{\mu\nu}\tilde G^{a\mu\nu}$, a dimension-four invariant of the unbroken group.

Why it has no row. $G\tilde G$ is a total derivative, $\partial_\mu K^\mu$ with $K$ the Chern–Simons current, so it contributes no vertex at any order of the tree expansion. A total derivative changes no equation of motion and no Feynman rule.

Why it is nevertheless a parameter. The measure of the fermion path integral is not invariant under the chiral rotation that would remove $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}}$, so the parameter is not removable by a field redefinition that leaves the rest of the list fixed. Its effect is a neutron electric dipole moment, and the smallness of that moment is the strong CP problem.

The distinction in one line. Absent as a piece, present as a parameter. The list of forty-seven is a list of vertices, and the list of nineteen is a list of numbers.

Neutrino Mass

Why it has no row. No dimension-four invariant of the unbroken group produces a neutrino mass. The Majorana bilinear is dimension three and would pass the residual test; what it fails is descent, as The Nine Dirac Masses shows with the two tests side by side.

What the omission costs. The three neutrinos are massless in the list, the leptonic mixing matrix is absent, and the neutral current of the neutrino is purely left-handed. Each of the three is a checkable statement about the list and a false statement about nature.

What would repair it. A right-handed neutrino, the Weinberg operator, or a triplet scalar: three routes, three prices, and a different piece count under each. The list is the minimal content's, and its count is the count of that content.

Gravity

What is absent. The metric, its curvature, the Einstein–Hilbert term, and every coupling of the Standard Model fields to the spin connection.

Why it is not an omission of the same kind. The other three are terms of the Standard Model that a complete list might have been expected to contain. Gravity is not a term of the Standard Model at all: it is a separate field with a separate gauge principle, and no choice of content or of operator within the Standard Model produces it.

What the corpus says about it. The corpus's gravitational programme is separate and is not imported here. The Corpus Map says where it sits, and The Data Boundary says what its status is. The list of forty-nine does not depend on that programme's outcome.

What Else Is Omitted, by Dimension

Dimension five and above. Four-fermion operators, the Weinberg operator, and every other higher-dimension invariant. They are absent because the list is defined by the dimension-four criterion of The Completeness of the List.

Dimension zero. The vacuum energy density, $-\varpi_{\mathrm{cpl}}v^4/4$. It is in the expansion of the potential and it has no field content, so it changes no vertex.

Dimension three. The tadpole, $h$ alone. It vanishes at the minimum, and its vanishing is the condition that defines $v$.

What remains. The forty-nine rows, and nothing else at dimension four with the content of The Broken-Phase Content.

Checks

  1. The count of omissions. Four named, each with its own reason.
  2. The ghosts. Absent in unitary gauge, present in covariant gauges as a determinant.
  3. The theta term. A total derivative, no vertex, one parameter.
  4. The neutrino mass. No dimension-four ancestor; the Majorana bilinear passes the residual test and fails descent.
  5. Gravity. A separate field and a separate principle, not a Standard Model term.
  6. The higher dimensions. Excluded by the dimension-four criterion.
  7. The vacuum energy. Dimension zero, no field content.
  8. The tadpole. Dimension three, vanishing at the minimum.
  9. The consistency. None of the four omissions changes the count of forty-nine.
  10. The reasons. Four different reasons, and no single rule that produces all four.

Summary

Four things are absent from the list, and they are absent for four different reasons.

Ghosts are not fields of the content; they are the determinant that a covariant gauge introduces, and the list is in unitary gauge where they do not appear. The theta term is a total derivative with no vertex, and it is a parameter because the measure obstructs its removal. The neutrino mass has no dimension-four ancestor, and its two candidate forms fail different tests. Gravity is not a Standard Model term at all.

Two further omissions come from the criterion rather than from the content: higher-dimension operators are excluded by the dimension-four rule, and the vacuum energy density is excluded for having no field content. The tadpole is excluded by the condition that defines the vacuum.

None of the six changes the count. The list has forty-nine rows, and each of the six is absent for a reason that the list itself supplies.

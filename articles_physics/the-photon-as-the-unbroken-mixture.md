# __The Photon as the Unbroken Mixture: One Null Direction, Three Parents__

## Introduction

**The question.** The electromagnetism section of this category is a set of articles whose equations are exact and whose field is one field. The $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ model adds a group with three neutral connections instead of two. What does that addition change in the section, given that Maxwell's equations are not touched?

**The answer in one line.**

$$
\boxed{\;
Q=T_{3L}+T_{3R}+\sqrt{\tfrac23}\,T^{15},
\qquad
d=\bigl(1,\,1,\,\sqrt{\tfrac23}\bigr)\ \text{in}\ \bigl(T_{3L},\,T_{3R},\,T^{15}\bigr),
\qquad
\mathcal M^{2}d=0 .
\;}
$$

**The direction of the mixture is the charge generator's own coefficient vector, and it is a null direction for one reason: both vacua are electrically neutral.** The photon is the last stabiliser of the chain, so it is the most mixed object in the frame, and the mixing is forced rather than chosen.

**What the article claims.** That the photon is a particular combination of three neutral connections; that the combination's **direction** is fixed by two neutrality conditions and requires no choice; that the direction is the coefficient vector of $Q$, so the photon is the unique combination that carries zero charge on every vacuum; and that the section's other articles survive verbatim, their equations untouched.

**What the article does not claim.** That the photon's dynamics changes. That photon mass or charge quantisation is modified. That the mixture is observable: it is not, at any energy, and *Why the Mixture Is Unobservable* says why. **And it does not compute the photon's coefficients in the three gauge fields**, because those coefficients carry the three couplings; they are in [The One Coupling That Returns the Standard Model](the-one-coupling-that-returns-the-standard-model.html). *Direction and Coordinates: Where This Article Stops* states that boundary.

**Why the distinction matters.** The neutral sector has a shape and a size. The shape — three fields, one surviving combination, its direction — is fixed by the vacuum and is arithmetic. The size — how much of each field the photon contains — is fixed by the three couplings and moves when they do. **An article that prints the first as though it were the second smuggles a moving number into a fixed statement.** This article keeps the first and sends the second to the article that owns the couplings.

## Why the Photon Is the Mixed Object

**Three neutral fields, two conditions, one direction.** That counting is the whole argument, and everything else in the article is a computation of it.

**1.1 In the Standard Model the photon is a mixture of two fields**, $W^3$ and $B$, and this is taught as a fact about electroweak unification. **In this frame there are three neutral fields**, because $\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$ contributes a third, and the photon is a mixture of all three. The three are

$$
\bigl\{W^3_L,\ W^3_R,\ B\bigr\},\qquad B\equiv A^{15},
$$

where $B$ is the $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ Cartan that survives the first breaking. The other two $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ Cartans are the gluon directions, and they never mix, because colour is never broken.

**1.2 The unbroken group is the last stabiliser.** The chain's three steps are three stabilisers, and the surviving group is their intersection:

$$
\mathrm{U}(1)_{\mathrm{em}}=\mathrm{Stab}_{\mathrm{SU}(4)}(\ell_1)\cap\mathrm{Stab}_{\mathrm{SU}(2)_R}(\ell_2)\cap\mathrm{Stab}_{\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{U}(1)_Y}(\ell_3) .
$$

**A group defined by three conditions is generated by the one direction that satisfies all three**, and in a three-dimensional neutral space three conditions leave one direction. **So the photon's being the most mixed object is not a dynamical accident. It is what three stabilisers in three dimensions must produce.**

**1.3 The counting, stated as the whole argument.** Three neutral fields, two independent vacuum conditions, one null direction. **A frame with two neutral fields and one condition would have to choose which combination is the photon. This frame does not have to choose**, and that is the difference the fourth colour makes to the electromagnetic section.

**1.4 The objection, and the answer.** The objection: a null direction of a mass matrix is a fact about masses, so the photon's composition has been read off the mass spectrum and is not independent. **The answer: the null direction is a fact about charge.** *The Direction, Derived Three Ways* shows that the direction is the coefficient vector of $Q$, and that its being null is the neutrality of the two vacua. The mass matrix is one way to reach the direction, not the reason it exists.

## The Direction, Derived Three Ways

**Three routes, and they agree.** Each is a computation on the same three-dimensional neutral space; none of them needs a numerical value of a coupling.

**2.1 From the mass matrix's null vector.** The neutral mass matrix is a sum of two rank-one terms, one per vacuum:

$$
\mathcal M^2=\sum_{i=1,2}v_i^{2}\,a_ia_i^{\mathsf T},
\qquad
a_1=\bigl(0,\tfrac12,-\sqrt{\tfrac38}\bigr)v_C,
\qquad
a_2=\bigl(-\tfrac12,0,\sqrt{\tfrac38}\bigr)v ,
$$

where the entries are the eigenvalues of $(T_{3L},T_{3R},T^{15})$ on the field that takes the vev, with $T^{15}$ normalised by $\operatorname{tr}(T^{15})^2=\tfrac12$ and hence $B-L=\sqrt{8/3}\,T^{15}$. The null vector is the cross product,

$$
a_1\times a_2\propto\bigl(\sqrt{\tfrac38},\,\sqrt{\tfrac38},\,\tfrac12\bigr),
$$

whose ratios, with the first entry normalised to one, are

$$
\bigl(1,\ 1,\ \tfrac12\cdot\sqrt{\tfrac83}\bigr)=\bigl(1,\,1,\,\sqrt{\tfrac23}\bigr)=\bigl(1,\,1,\,0.816497\bigr) .
$$

**The overall sign of each $a_i$ is free**, because only the products $a_ia_i^{\mathsf T}$ enter the matrix, and the sign of the null vector is free because a photon is a line. **The relative sign of $a_1$ and $a_2$ is not free**, and the two neutrality tests of *The Direction, Derived Three Ways* are what fix it.

**2.2 The direction is the charge vector, and that is why it is null.** This is the route that explains the other two. Write the charge in the neutral-generator basis,

$$
Q=T_{3L}+T_{3R}+\tfrac12(B-L)=T_{3L}+T_{3R}+\sqrt{\tfrac23}\,T^{15},
$$

and read the coefficients: they are $d=(1,1,\sqrt{2/3})$. Now evaluate $Q$ on each vacuum line, which is the scalar product $a_i\cdot d$ up to the factors $v_i$:

$$
a_1\cdot d=0+\tfrac12-\sqrt{\tfrac38}\cdot\sqrt{\tfrac23}=\tfrac12-\tfrac12=0,
\qquad
a_2\cdot d=-\tfrac12+0+\sqrt{\tfrac38}\cdot\sqrt{\tfrac23}=-\tfrac12+\tfrac12=0 .
$$

**So $\mathcal M^2d=\sum_i v_i^2a_i(a_i\cdot d)=0$ identically, for every pair of vevs, at every ratio.** The photon is massless, and the reason is not a cancellation among numbers. **A null direction of the neutral mass matrix is a direction on which no vacuum carries charge, and $d$ is the vector whose dot product with a state's eigenvalues is that state's charge.** The mixture is unbroken because it is the charge.

**2.3 From the two neutral lines that take vevs.** Take the two states the frame declares neutral before it declares any charge: the right-handed neutrino of the $\bar{\mathbf4}$, and the lower component of the electroweak doublet $H$. In the basis $(T_{3L},T_{3R},T^{15})$ their eigenvalues are

$$
\text{right-handed: }\bigl(0,\tfrac12,-\sqrt{\tfrac38}\bigr),
\qquad
\text{left doublet: }\bigl(-\tfrac12,0,\sqrt{\tfrac38}\bigr).
$$

**A direction that annihilates both is orthogonal to both**, so it is the cross product of the two rows, which is again the vector of *The Direction, Derived Three Ways*. Writing the two conditions as equations on $(a,b,c)$,

$$
\tfrac12 b-\sqrt{\tfrac38}\,c=0,
\qquad
-\tfrac12 a+\sqrt{\tfrac38}\,c=0,
$$

gives $a=b=2\sqrt{3/8}\,c$, whose ratios are $(1,1,\sqrt{2/3})$ once more.

**2.4 Why three routes and not one claim.** Routes 2.1 and 2.3 use the same two vectors, because **the vacuum vector of a scalar line is the charge vector of the fermion line it corresponds to** — the two neutral scalars that take the vevs are the partners of the two neutral leptons. So 2.1 is the scalar side of an identity and 2.3 is the fermion side. **The agreement is a consistency check on the embedding, not a coincidence**, and route 2.2 is why the identity holds at all: $d$ is $Q$'s coefficient vector, and both routes test the neutrality of the same two lines.

## What the Triple Says

**Two numbers and a normalisation.** The triple is not three independent facts.

**3.1 The two leading ones.** $Q$ carries $T_{3L}$ and $T_{3R}$ with unit coefficient, so the two $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ factors enter the charge on the same footing. **That is the left–right symmetry showing in the photon**, and it is the one dynamical statement in the triple.

**3.2 The $\sqrt{2/3}$ is a convention.** It is $\tfrac12\cdot\sqrt{8/3}$: the $\tfrac12$ is the coefficient of $B-L$ in the charge, the same coefficient it has in $Y$, and the $\sqrt{8/3}$ converts $B-L$ into the fifteenth Cartan. **Fix $\operatorname{tr}(T^{15})^2=\tfrac12$ and the number is forced. Change the normalisation of the $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ generators and it moves**, while the physics does not.

**3.3 The third entry is not an independent prediction.** Its value follows from the normalisation of one generator and from the factor $\tfrac12$ that hypercharge already carries. **A reader who counts three numbers here thinks the photon's composition is less constrained than it is**: two conditions on three unknowns leave one direction, and the direction's third entry is a bookkeeping choice, not a measurement.

**3.4 Why the same $\sqrt{3/8}$ keeps appearing.** It is the eigenvalue of $T^{15}$ on a lepton line, $B-L=-1$ with $B-L=\sqrt{8/3}\,T^{15}$, so $T^{15}=-\sqrt{3/8}$. **The same number enters $|a_1|^2=\tfrac14+\tfrac38=\tfrac58$**, which is the coefficient of the heavy neutral's mass, and it enters the neutrality test of *The Direction, Derived Three Ways* by cancelling exactly. One normalisation, three appearances, and no coupling anywhere in the three.

## Direction and Coordinates: Where This Article Stops

**One distinction, stated once, and it decides what belongs here.**

**4.1 The direction is in the space of generators.** $d=(1,1,\sqrt{2/3})$ is a vector of coefficients on $(T_{3L},T_{3R},T^{15})$. It says which combination of symmetries survives. It is fixed by two neutrality conditions, it is dimensionless, and it can be stated without naming an energy or a coupling.

**4.2 The coordinates are in the space of connections.** The physical photon field is a combination of the three connections, and its coefficients there are not the direction: they are the direction **weighted by the three couplings**, so that the combination is canonically normalised. **That weighting is the whole coupling content of the mixture.** It changes when the couplings change and it depends on which scales are chosen.

**4.3 Where the coordinates live.** In [The One Coupling That Returns the Standard Model](the-one-coupling-that-returns-the-standard-model.html), which is the single place in this category where a coupling value is written. **A reader who wants the photon's coefficients in the three fields should read that article. **4.4 Why the triple is so often mistaken for the coordinates.** In the case the unified literature studies, the three couplings are equal, the weighting is the same for all three fields, and the coordinates coincide with the direction. **The triple is then both at once**, and that is the origin of the misreading: what looks like a property of the photon is a property of the photon and of an assumption about three couplings. **The direction is the photon's. The coincidence is the assumption's.**

**4.5 What follows for this article.** No number printed here depends on a coupling. **The direction, the rank of the mass matrix, the neutrality conditions, the field-strength statement of *The Photon and the Corpus's Own Electromagnetism* and the observability argument of *Why the Mixture Is Unobservable* are all scale-free**, and they are the article's content.

## Why the Mixture Is Unobservable

**The reason the section can keep its equations, and the reason a critic cannot ask for a measurement.**

**5.1 At low energy the partners are absent.** The other two neutral states are massive, so below their masses the photon's interactions with matter are the only ones that survive, and they are proportional to $Q$ with $Q$ exact. **The mixture is invisible because the fields it is mixed with cannot be produced.**

**5.2 No low-energy observable distinguishes "photon $=B$" from "photon $=$ mixture".** Any such observable would need an interaction with one of the fields the photon is mixed with, and those fields lie far above the electroweak scale. **The statement is therefore a statement about the theory's structure, not about its predictions**, which bounds what the frame can claim for this article.

**5.3 What would be observable.** Deviations from proportionality to $Q$ at loop level, from exchange of the heavy neutral and coloured states; these are suppressed by the inverse square of those masses, hence unobservable. And, in principle, the mixing of the two massive neutrals, which is a property of the $Z$ and $Z_R$ and not of the photon.

**5.4 The honest formulation.** The frame adds a true statement about the photon's identity that no experiment can test. **That is not a weakness of the statement — a structural statement need not be testable — but it does mean the electromagnetic section's experimental content is untouched, and it must not be presented as newly supported.**

## The Photon and the Corpus's Own Electromagnetism

**One connection the frame can make and the literature cannot, and it is worth stating carefully.**

**6.1 The category's electromagnetic articles derive Maxwell from the algebra's structure**, not from a minimal coupling: the field strength is a bivector, the equations are a projection, and $\mathrm{U}(1)$ appears as a phase. **Nothing in that derivation needs a mixture.**

**6.2 The frame adds a second description of the same field**, and the two must be compatible. The category's description says the photon is a $\mathrm{U}(1)$ connection; the frame's says the $\mathrm{U}(1)$ is a stabiliser of three vacua and its connection is a combination. **They are compatible because a linear combination of connections is a connection**, and the stabiliser's generator is $Q$.

**6.3 The compatibility as a test.** Take the frame's $A_{\imath}$ and compute its field strength. It is $F_{\imath\jmath}=\partial_{\imath} A_{\jmath}-\partial_{\jmath} A_{\imath}$ with no extra term, because a linear combination adds no term to the exterior derivative. **So the category's bivector construction applies to the mixture verbatim**, which is why the section's other articles need no edit.

**6.4 What would break the compatibility.** A coefficient that varied in spacetime. **It does not: the coefficients are numbers.** A number that depends on the renormalisation scale is still constant in spacetime, so the scale dependence of *Direction and Coordinates: Where This Article Stops* leaves $F=\mathrm dA$ untouched, and the compatibility is not hostage to the coupling sector.

## Distinctions That Are Easy to Lose

The direction is not the coefficients. $(1,1,\sqrt{2/3})$ is a vector on generators; the coefficients in the three connections are that vector weighted by the three couplings, and they are in the coupling article (*Direction and Coordinates: Where This Article Stops*).

The mixture does not modify Maxwell's equations. It is a statement about the field's identity, and $F=\mathrm dA$ is unchanged (*The Photon and the Corpus's Own Electromagnetism*).

Unobservable does not mean unimportant. A structural statement need not be testable; it must not be sold as tested (*Why the Mixture Is Unobservable*).

The $\sqrt{2/3}$ is not a dynamical number. It is the fifteenth Cartan's normalisation, and it moves with a choice of generator basis (*What the Triple Says*).

The photon is not $B$. The $\mathrm{U}(1)$ boson at the end of the chain is $B$; the photon is the combination of three fields that annihilates both neutrals (*Why the Photon Is the Mixed Object*, *The Direction, Derived Three Ways*).

Three neutral connections are not three scalars. All three are connections, and the scalar sector's neutral states are a separate object.

The Unitary-Gauge Image: The Lineage of Every Piece and Where the Three Goldstones Went

Introduction

The claim. Every row of the broken-phase list is the image of something on the unbroken side, and the image is of two kinds. Forty-seven rows descend from one of the five invariant pieces of the symmetric Lagrangian. Two rows — the two vector masses — descend from nothing: their ancestor is the vacuum value itself. The three scalar fields that are missing from the broken content are the longitudinal polarisations of the three massive vectors, and the field count falls by three while the degree-of-freedom count does not move.

The answer.

$$ \boxed{\;47=4+8+17+18,\qquad 49=47+2,\qquad 53=47+6\;} $$

Why the distinction between forty-seven and forty-nine matters. A vector mass term is not an invariant of the unbroken group. There is no term in the symmetric Lagrangian whose coefficient is $M_W^2$; the mass appears only when the scalar takes its vacuum value, and it is therefore the image of the vacuum and not of a term. A table that assigned the two Proca rows an ancestor term would be inventing an invariant that the unbroken theory does not possess. The count of $47$ is the count of rows with an ancestor; the count of $49$ is the count of rows.

The standing. The expansion is standard and exact; the corpus's own version is Electroweak Symmetry Breaking: the Doublet, the Potential and the Vector-Boson Masses. What this article adds is the accounting, row by row, and the statement of which rows are not images of terms at all.

Two Field Spaces and One Function

Before the rotation. Eight gluons, four electroweak connections $W_{a,\mu}$ and $B_\mu$, a complex doublet with four real components, and the forty-five Weyl fields. Sixteen bosonic fields: twelve vector and four scalar.

After the rotation. Eight gluons, $A_\mu$, $Z_\mu$, $W^\pm_\mu$, one scalar $h$, and the same forty-five Weyl fields. Thirteen bosonic fields: twelve vector and one scalar.

The count that is conserved is the degree-of-freedom count. Before, twelve massless vectors carry two polarisations each and the four scalars carry one each: $24+4=28$. After, the eight gluons and the photon carry two each, the three massive vectors carry three each, and the scalar carries one: $16+2+9+1=28$. The field count drops by three and the degree-of-freedom count does not change, because each missing scalar has become the third polarisation of one vector.

The apparent mismatch of eight and twelve. A reader who counts eight gluons plus four scalars on the unbroken side, and twelve vectors plus one scalar on the broken side, will find twelve against thirteen. The error is on the unbroken side: the electroweak connections are four fields before the breaking as well, named $W_1,W_2,W_3,B$, and they are massless. The correct unbroken count is twelve vectors and four scalars, which is sixteen.

The Five Invariant Pieces

Everything with an ancestor comes from one of five terms.

The arithmetic of the five. $4+8+17+18+0=47$. The topological term contributes nothing, because it is a total derivative and produces no vertex; it is one of the nineteen parameters and none of the pieces.

Why the curvatures give three rows and not four. The photon, the $Z$ and the charged pair are four fields but three kinetic structures: the neutral pair is diagonalised by one rotation and each diagonal entry is one row, while the charged pair is one row for the pair. The Four Field Strengths states the row per field.

Two rows are not in this table. The $W$ mass and the $Z$ mass are rows of the broken list and are not images of any term of the symmetric list. They are the images of the vacuum, and the next section states what that means.

The Two Rows With No Ancestor Term

A vector mass is not an invariant. A term $M_V^2V_\mu V^\mu$ with a fixed $M_V$ is not invariant under the gauge transformation that the vector gauges, so it cannot appear in the symmetric Lagrangian. In the symmetric phase the same two rows exist as the constant level of $(v+h)^2$, with coefficients $g^2v^2/4$ and $(g^2+g_{\mathrm Y}^2)v^2/4$, and those coefficients are what the vacuum value turns into masses.

So the ancestor is the vacuum and not a term. This is the precise content of the phrase Proca as the representative of the condensate: the two rows are the only ones whose existence depends on the value of the scalar field rather than on the field content and the symmetries. The Two Vector Masses makes the same point from the Proca side.

The count that follows. Forty-seven rows have an ancestor term, two have an ancestor value, and $47+2=49$. A reader who counts forty-seven and a reader who counts forty-nine are both right, and the two numbers count different things: rows with a term above them, and rows.

The Three Goldstones

Where they are, in the general gauge. Expanding around a general direction, $H=(G^+/\sqrt2,\,(v+h+iG^0)/\sqrt2)$, the scalar kinetic term contains the three Goldstone fields and the mixing terms $M_W\partial_\mu G^+W^{-\mu}$ and $M_Z\partial_\mu G^0Z^\mu$. Those mixing terms are what the longitudinal polarisation is made of.

Where they are, in unitary gauge. The choice $G^\pm=G^0=0$ removes them and the mixing terms with them. The three fields leave the content and their degrees of freedom appear as the third polarisation of $W^+$, $W^-$ and $Z$. Nothing else in the Lagrangian changes, because the gauge transformation that sets them to zero acts on the whole expression at once.

Why no piece is built from them. The Goldstones carry no colour and no charge, so no piece of the forty-seven is built from them: a piece built from $G^+$ alone would violate $\mathrm{U}(1)_{\mathrm{em}}$. The only places they appear are the mixing terms and the quartic scalar couplings, and both are removed or absorbed by the same gauge choice.

The general gauge is not a different list. In an $R_\xi$ gauge the Goldstones stay, the mixing terms stay, and the gauge-fixing and ghost terms appear. The physical content is unchanged: the Goldstones and the third polarisations are two descriptions of one degree of freedom. What the List Omits states what the ghosts are and why they are not pieces.

A Rotation in Field Space Adds and Loses Nothing

The argument in three steps.

First, the expansion is exhaustive. Each ancestor is a polynomial in the fields and each is expanded around the vacuum. A polynomial of degree four produces terms of degree two, three and four, and the table lists all of them. No term is dropped in the expansion.

Second, the rotation is invertible. The map from $(W_1,W_2,W_3,B)$ to $(W^+,W^-,Z,A)$ is a unitary change of basis on four real fields, and the map from $H$ to $(0,(v+h)/\sqrt2)$ plus three Goldstones is a change of coordinates on four real fields. Either map can be undone, so neither can lose information. A rotation in field space adds and loses nothing; it only changes which combinations are the rows.

Third, the two lists have the same parameter count. Nineteen in the symmetric phase and nineteen in the broken one, as Nineteen and Nineteen tabulates. A map that added or lost a piece would add or lose a coefficient, and the counts would differ.

What the table does not claim. It does not claim that the broken list is more fundamental, or that the symmetric one is a fiction. It claims that they are the same function written in two bases, and it exhibits the correspondence row by row.

The Map on the Parameters

Four relations, each one line.

$$ M_W=\tfrac12gv,\qquad M_Z=\tfrac12\sqrt{g^2+g_{\mathrm Y}^2}\,v=\frac{M_W}{\cos\theta_W},\qquad m_f=\frac{y_fv}{\sqrt2},\qquad m_h=\sqrt{2\varpi_{\mathrm{cpl}}}\,v . $$

The couplings that are not masses. The two $hVV$ rows are fixed by the same expansion:

$$ \mathcal{L}\supset-\frac{2M_W^2}{v}\,h\,W^+_\mu W^{-\mu}-\frac{M_Z^2}{v}\,h\,Z_\mu Z^\mu , $$

so the coefficient of $hW^+W^-$ is twice $M_W^2/v$ and the coefficient of $hZZ$ is $M_Z^2/v$. The factor of two is stated and not cancelled: it comes from the charged pair being two real fields against the $Z$'s one. Written with the conventional $\tfrac12$ in front of the $ZZ$ term the same physics reads $g_{hZZ}=2M_Z^2/v$, and in that notation $g_{hWW}/g_{hZZ}=\cos^2\theta_W$. The $hVV$ Pieces separates the convention from the physics.

The mixing matrix has no relation of this kind. It is what is left of the two Yukawa matrices after the two diagonalisations, and it is not a function of the masses. That is why the flavour sector contributes four parameters in both descriptions and why the map there is many-to-one rather than one-to-one.

Checks

  1. The row sum with ancestors. $4+8+17+18=47$.
  2. The full row count. $47+2=49$, the two being the Proca masses.
  3. The vertex count. $47+6=53$, the six being the two triple and four quartic gauge structures.
  4. The gauge block. One gluon, three electroweak kinetic rows, and the two curvatures contribute six vertices and no extra row.
  5. The scalar block. Eight rows in the forty-seven: the kinetic, the mass, the two $hVV$, the two $hhVV$ and the two self-pieces.
  6. The fermion block. Twelve kinetics plus five interactions is seventeen.
  7. The Yukawa block. Nine masses plus nine $h\bar ff$ is eighteen, in bijection through $m_f=y_fv/\sqrt2$.
  8. The Goldstone count. $4-3=1$ scalar and twelve vectors unchanged, so $16\to13$ fields and $28\to28$ degrees of freedom.
  9. The polarisation count. $8\times2+1\times2+3\times3+1=28$ after; $12\times2+4=28$ before.
  10. The parameter identity. Nineteen on each side, from the two tables of Nineteen and Nineteen.

Summary

Forty-seven rows of the broken list descend from the five invariant pieces: one from the gluon curvature, three from the electroweak curvatures, eight from the scalar, seventeen from the fermion kinetic term, eighteen from the Yukawa term, and none from the topological term.

Two rows descend from no term. The $W$ and $Z$ masses are not invariants of the unbroken group; they are the images of the vacuum value, and that is exactly what calling Proca the representative of the condensate means. The forty-seven and the forty-nine are both correct counts of different objects.

Six further structures are vertices and not rows: the two triple and four quartic gauge self-interactions inside the curvatures. They give the vertex count $47+6=53$.

The three fields missing from the broken content are the Goldstones. They are removed by the unitary gauge choice and their degrees of freedom appear as the third polarisations. The field count falls from sixteen to thirteen, the degree-of-freedom count stays at twenty-eight, and the parameter count is nineteen on both sides. A rotation in field space adds and loses nothing.

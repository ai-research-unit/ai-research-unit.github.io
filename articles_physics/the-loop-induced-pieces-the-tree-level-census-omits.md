# __The Loop-Induced Pieces the Tree-Level Census Omits: $h\gamma\gamma$, $hgg$, $hZ\gamma$__

## Introduction

**The claim.** Three couplings of the Higgs to two gauge bosons exist and are measured, and none of them is a row of the forty-seven: $h\gamma\gamma$, $hgg$ and $hZ\gamma$. They are absent by the criterion and not by accident. Each is a dimension-five operator, and each is generated by a loop of the fields that are in the list. They add no parameter; they are predictions written in terms of the rows that are there.

**The answer.**

$$
\boxed{\;\mathcal{L}_{\text{eff}}\supset\frac{\alpha}{8\pi v}c_\gamma\,h\,F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}+\frac{\alpha_s}{12\pi v}\,h\,G^a_{\mu\nu}G^{a\mu\nu}+\frac{\alpha}{4\pi v}c_{Z\gamma}\,h\,F_{\mu\nu}Z^{\mu\nu}\;}
$$

**Why dimension five is the whole story.** The criterion of [The Census Rule](the-census-rule-dimension-four-residual-invariance-and-one-canonical-term-per-field.html) admits operators of dimension at most four. A term $hFF$ has dimension five, because $h$ has dimension one and $FF$ has dimension four. It is therefore not admissible, and no amount of symmetry would make it so: the obstruction is the power of the field, not the invariance.

**The standing.** The three couplings are standard and their loop functions are standard. What this article adds is the statement of where they sit relative to the census: outside it, calculable from it, and carrying no constant of their own.

## Why They Cannot Be Rows

**The three absences at tree level, each for its own reason.**

| Coupling | Absent at tree level because | Present at one loop because |
|---|---|---|
| $h\gamma\gamma$ | the photon is the combination that annihilates the vacuum | charged particles run in the loop |
| $hgg$ | the gluon carries no electroweak quantum number | coloured particles run in the loop |
| $hZ\gamma$ | the photon and the $Z$ are the two combinations of the neutral pair; the one that couples to $h$ is the $Z$ alone | the loop mixes the two neutral currents |

**The first is a statement about the vacuum.** The $hVV$ rows come from $(v+h)^2$, and the combination that multiplies $h$ is the one with a vacuum expectation value. The photon is orthogonal to it, so no $hAA$ term appears at any order in the tree expansion. [The $hVV$ Pieces](the-hvv-pieces-two-mass-term-normalisations-two-couplings-and-the-photon-that-does-not-appear.html) states the same fact from the scalar side.

**The second is a statement about colour.** The gluon couples to colour and to nothing else, so the tree-level scalar coupling cannot reach it: there is no $hg g$ vertex in the expansion of $|D_\mu H|^2$. At one loop the top quark, which carries both colour and a Yukawa coupling, connects the two.

**The third is a statement about the rotation.** $hZ\gamma$ would need the photon to couple to the scalar; it does not, at tree level. The loop generates it because the particles running inside carry both $Q$ and $T_3$, and the two couplings do not cancel in the $Z\gamma$ channel as they do in the $\gamma\gamma$ channel.

## What the Loops Give

**The structure of the coefficients.** Each is a sum over the species that can run, weighted by the coupling to the scalar and by the charge relevant to the two external gauge fields:

$$
c_\gamma=\sum_f N_c Q_f^2\,A_{1/2}(\tau_f)+A_1(\tau_W),\qquad
c_{Z\gamma}=\sum_f N_c Q_f\,(T_{3f}-2Q_f\sin^2\theta_W)\,B_{1/2}(\tau_f)+B_1(\tau_W),
$$

where $\tau_i=4m_i^2/m_h^2$ and the $A$'s and $B$'s are the standard loop functions, equal to $\tfrac43$ and $7$ in the heavy-mass limit for the fermion and the $W$ respectively.

**The $hgg$ coefficient is one number in the heavy-top limit.** Integrating out the top gives

$$
\frac{\alpha_s}{12\pi v}\,h\,G^a_{\mu\nu}G^{a\mu\nu},
$$

the coefficient $1/12\pi$ being the top loop's residue in that limit. It is the coupling that makes gluon fusion the dominant production channel, and it is entirely a prediction of the top's Yukawa coupling, which is the mass row $m_t=y_tv/\sqrt2$.

**Why no new constant appears.** Each coefficient is a function of $e$, $g$, $\sin\theta_W$, $v$ and the masses — all of them already in the eighteen and the nineteen. A measurement of $h\to\gamma\gamma$ therefore tests the list rather than extending it.

**Why the loops do not stop at one.** Two loops, three loops and higher generate operators of dimension six and beyond, with coefficients suppressed by further powers of $1/v$ or of a large mass. The census is a tree-level, dimension-four statement, and the tower above it is infinite. That is why the criterion is stated as a cut and not as a discovery procedure.

## The Three in the Data

**$h\to\gamma\gamma$ is the discovery channel.** The decay proceeds at one loop, and its rate was the first evidence that the scalar couples to the $W$ and to the top with the strengths the list assigns. A deviation would be a deviation in the rows, not in the coefficient structure.

**$h\to Z\gamma$ is measured and small.** Its rate is suppressed relative to $h\to\gamma\gamma$ by the loop functions and by the $Z$ coupling, and it is one of the places where the sign of the top's coupling can be checked.

**$h\to gg$ is not a decay of the physical Higgs at leading order in the same sense.** Below the $b\bar b$ threshold the gluonic width is small, but the same coefficient governs production, where it is the largest channel at the LHC. One coefficient, two observables.

## Checks

1. The dimension. $hFF$ has dimension five, $hFZ$ five, $hGG$ five.
2. The criterion. Each fails the dimension-four condition and none fails invariance.
3. The tree-level $hAA$. Absent because the photon annihilates the vacuum.
4. The tree-level $hgg$. Absent because the gluon carries no electroweak quantum number.
5. The one-loop $h\gamma\gamma$. Present through the $W$ and the charged fermions.
6. The one-loop $hgg$. Present through the top, with coefficient $1/12\pi$ in the heavy-top limit.
7. The one-loop $hZ\gamma$. Present through the same particles, with the $Z$ coupling in place of the charge squared.
8. The parameters. None added; every coefficient is a function of the eighteen and the masses.
9. The tower. Dimension six and higher are generated at higher loops and are outside the census.
10. The observables. $h\to\gamma\gamma$, $h\to Z\gamma$ and gluon fusion are the three places the coefficients are read.

## Summary

Three Higgs couplings to two gauge bosons are measured and none is a row: $h\gamma\gamma$, $hgg$ and $hZ\gamma$. Each is a dimension-five operator, excluded by the dimension-four criterion, and each is generated by a loop of fields that are in the list.

The coefficients are sums over the species that can run, weighted by their couplings to the scalar and to the two gauge fields. They are functions of $e$, $g$, $\sin\theta_W$, $v$ and the masses, so they add no constant to the eighteen and no entry to the nineteen. A measurement of any of them tests the list.

The tower of higher-dimension operators is infinite, which is why the census is a cut at dimension four and a tree-level statement rather than a claim about everything the theory can do.

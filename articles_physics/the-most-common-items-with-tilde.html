The Most Common Items with a Tilde

Scope: tilde glyphs only. Every catalogued symbol carries a tilde. Four kinds of glyph are outside the scope and are not listed among the allowed uses: a matrix, $I_n$, $\mathsf{M}_2$, $\mathsf{M}_4$; a pure element written with an arrow, $\vec Q$, $\vec q$, $\vec u$, $\vec U$, $\vec\Xi$, $\vec\Upsilon$, $\vec\pi$; a mark that is not an element, $\tilde\cdot$, $\tilde\star$, $\tilde{\ }$; and every glyph of the reduced, tilde-free layers, $\Pi_\pm$, $\Xi_\pm$, $\Upsilon_\pm$. The first is fixed by the rule that a matrix carries no tilde, §General Matrix Conventions of Conventions in the Biquaternion Universe; the others are elements of other layers or of no layer at all. The operator section states the rule that pairs the two marks; it is not a catalogue of arrowed glyphs.

The tilde marks a quaternionic factor. Each entry gives the number of articles in which the symbol occurs; a base glyph counts every numbered and subscripted variant. The count covers the 2528 articles of articles_maths/ (2110) and articles_physics/ (418) on 18 October 2026; the catalogue itself is excluded. The catalogue has one section of allowed glyphs and a record of the glyphs that were retired.

  • 1. Allowed tilde — the role is fixed by Conventions in the Biquaternion Universe, or by the case-and-mark rule that article states, and checked against the corpus. Cite these entries as they stand. The tables are ordered by theme, as the objects are; the bound letters that have no row of their own follow them.
  • 2. Retired glyphs — the roles removed in the rewrite, with the reason and the glyph that replaced them. They are kept as a record and are not available for citation.

The governing rule: only the Q family is generic, and only an element carries the tilde. $\tilde Q$ is the biquaternion or split-biquaternion element and $\tilde q$ the quaternion or split-quaternion element; numbered variants are allowed. A generic element that is unitary is written $\tilde U$. A letter outside the family names an object, or an element bound where it is introduced. A matrix — the image of an element, an array of biquaternions, or a colour matrix — takes no tilde; a tilde on one is a defect of scope, corrected where it occurs and not catalogued here.

The scalar kernels take no tilde: the d'Alembertian, Klein–Gordon propagator, Klein–Gordon path-integral and retarded-potential articles now write $G_{\mathrm{inv}}$, $G^{(\mu)}_{\mathrm{inv}}$, $G(\omega,\vec p)$, $G_F$, $G_E$ and $G_{\mathrm{ret}}$. The defining equation of the first-order kernel is recorded in section 1 with the solved sign, $\tilde\nabla\tilde{G}_{\tilde{\nabla}}=-\delta^{(4)}$; the Maxwell article's $+\delta$ and the retarded-potentials exercise's record of the disagreement are unchanged.

The letter $K$ has other, untitled roles. The kinetic kernel is the central operator $K_{\mathrm{kin}}=\Box-m^2$, with $\det K_{\mathrm{kin}}$ and $K_{\mathrm{kin}}^{-1}$, and it takes no tilde because it is central. The path-integral kernel is the central scalar $K$. The wave four-vector of the plane-wave convention is $\tilde P$, and the Chern–Simons current is $\tilde J_{\mathrm{CS}}$.

The flow generator, the gluon octet and the finite dilation are not glyphs. The generator of the Schrödinger flow is the Hermitian observable read through the central $i$, $\tilde H\mapsto-i\tilde H/\hbar$; it is not a second quantity. The four articles that wrote $\tilde G$ for the flow generator now write the generator out: $-i\tilde H/\hbar$, or $-\hbar^{-1}J\tilde H$ for a candidate root $J$, in The Schrödinger Equation in Biquaternionic Form; $-i\tilde H/\hbar$ in The Klein–Gordon Equation in Biquaternionic Form; $-iH/\hbar$, with the central Hamiltonian $H$, in The Hydrogen Atom in Biquaternionic Form: The Non-Relativistic Case; and $-i\tilde H$ in The Lyapunov Exponent and Information Loss in the Biquaternion Framework. A Removed Glyph

The discarded limit glyph no longer occurs in any article. The last use was the value a limit tends to; that value is an arbitrary element, so it belongs to the Q family and is now $\tilde Q_2$, with $\tilde Q_3$ for the second limit in the uniqueness proof and the algebra of limits. The logarithm value is $\tilde Q_{\log}$ ($17$ places, $4$ articles). The Lorentz rotor is $\tilde U$ in The Lorentz Transformation as a Biquaternionic Rotation; The Eigenspinor writes the fixed rotor of a frame change as $\tilde U_{\mathrm L}$ and the evolving eigenspinor as $\tilde U$. The Lindblad jump operators are $\tilde K_k$ ($10$ places, $1$ article). The orbital operators are $L_k e_t$ ($75$ places, $3$ articles).

The quaternion-layer logarithm is $\tilde q_{\log}$ ($16$ places, $1$ article), the same symbol as the biquaternion layer. The body-frame angular momentum of Rigid-Body Dynamics and the Biquaternion Rotor, $37$ places, is a pure real quaternion and is now written $\vec L_b$; the same pass made that article's angular velocity and torque $\vec\omega_b$ and $\vec\tau$, since they are pure as well. The left-inverse dummy of Norm and Invertibility in the General Quaternionic Algebra is now $\tilde Q_3$. The generic uses — the bilinearity dummy of The Born Rule as a Trace Formula, the general identity of Biquaternion Elementary Functions, and the periodicity statement of Split-Biquaternion Spectral Theory — were rewritten to $\tilde Q$. The Noether bivector is pure and is now written $\vec L$; the field strength $\tilde F$ is pure as well and still carries a tilde, so that pair differs in mark; the field strength is the fixed role of section 1, not a defect.

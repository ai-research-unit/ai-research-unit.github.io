The Operators of the Quaternionic Commutator and the Jacobi Failure

Introduction

The antisymmetric quaternionic multiplication is

$$ \tilde P\diamond\tilde Q=\tfrac12\bigl(\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q-\tilde Q^{\natural}\tilde P\bigr) =P_0\mathbf Q-Q_0\mathbf P-\mathbf P\times\mathbf Q , $$

the antisymmetric part of the quaternionic product for the exchange of its arguments. Its value is a pure vector, it is alternating, it has no unit, and it fails the Jacobi identity; the operation is Introduction to the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra of Biquaternions and the failure is The Jacobi Failure and the Associator Defect of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra. The word of the band AQA is Obstruction, and the two siblings own its two halves: The Brackets That Do Not Close: the Jacobi Failure of the Quaternionic Commutator owns the operation, its class, its table and the group reading, and Boosts, Mixed Terms and the Missing Lie Structure owns the split of the value, the invariant forms, the trace form and the missing Lie structure. This article owns the third object of the row, the multiplication operators.

For the product of this band it answers which operators there are, how they compose, which form they are adjoint for, and what that adjoint does to the parameter. The multiplication operators are the halved adjoint maps,

$$ L_{\tilde A}=\tfrac12\operatorname{ad}_{\tilde A},\qquad \operatorname{ad}_{\tilde A}\tilde R=[\tilde A,\tilde R]_{\natural} =\tilde A^{\natural}\tilde R-\tilde R^{\natural}\tilde A , $$

and they are the inner derivations of the commutator in name, the adjoint maps of the bracket. They do not close: the commutator of two of them is the operator of the value plus a deviation,

$$ [L_{\tilde A},L_{\tilde B}]=L_{\tilde A\diamond\tilde B}+D(\tilde A,\tilde B) , $$

and the deviation is the negative of the cyclic Jacobi sum, $D=-J$. So the bracket of two inner derivations is generally not an inner derivation, and the operators form the Lie algebra of no group. The block carries no non-degenerate invariant form of its own, so the operators are adjoint for the imported quaternionic form $N$ of the parent product, and that adjoint is the plain left multiplication by the parameter followed by the projection onto the vector subspace,

$$ (L_{\tilde A})^{N}=\tfrac12\,L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde A}\bigl(\mathrm{id}-\natural\bigr) =L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde A}\circ P ,\qquad (L_{\tilde A})^{N}\tilde Y=\tilde A\,\mathbf Y , $$

which is not a multiplication of the block: the parameter passes through the plain product, the natural conjugation enters only as the projection, and the one-sided adjoint leaves the family. The type criteria are correspondingly degenerate — a one-sided operator is self-adjoint for $N$ exactly for a central parameter and skew-adjoint only for the zero parameter — and the block contributes no form-preserving operator and no inner derivation.

The factor, stated once. The relation between a multiplication and its adjoint map carries the halving, $L_{\tilde A}=\tfrac12\operatorname{ad}_{\tilde A}$, the normalisation of the antisymmetric bilinear rows; the deviation enters the operator law with coefficient one, $[L_{\tilde A},L_{\tilde B}]=L_{\tilde A\diamond\tilde B}+D(\tilde A,\tilde B)$, and no factor two attaches to it. The doubling appears only when the law is re-expressed for the adjoint maps, $[\operatorname{ad}_{\tilde A},\operatorname{ad}_{\tilde B}]=\operatorname{ad}_{[\tilde A,\tilde B]_{\natural}}+4D(\tilde A,\tilde B)$, because $\operatorname{ad}=2L$ and $[\tilde A,\tilde B]_{\natural}=2(\tilde A\diamond\tilde B)$. Both displays were recomputed from the explicit product on eighty random triples before being written.

Conventions. The algebra is $\mathbb{B}=\mathbb{C}\otimes_{\mathbb{R}}\mathbb{H}$, with basis $e_0=1,e_1,e_2,e_3$, $e_k^{2}=-e_0$, $e_1e_2=e_3$, central scalar imaginary $i$ with $i^{2}=-1$, and a general element $\tilde Q=Q_0e_0+\mathbf Q$ of vector part $\mathbf Q=\sum_{k=1}^{3}Q_ke_k$; the complex bilinear dot and cross products are $(\mathbf P,\mathbf Q)$ and $\mathbf P\times\mathbf Q$ with $e_j\times e_k=e_l$ cyclically. The natural conjugation is ${}^{\natural}$, the Hermitian star is ${}^{*}=\bar{\cdot}\circ{}^{\natural}$, the sectors are $\mathbb{M}_{\pm}$, and the vector subspace is $\mathrm{Vect}(\mathbb{B})=\{\tilde Q:\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q)=0\}$. The quaternionic product is $\tilde P\star\tilde Q=\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q$, its antisymmetrisation is the block $\tilde P\diamond\tilde Q$, written $\tilde P\wedge_{\natural}\tilde Q$ by the physics siblings, and the quaternionic bracket is $[\tilde P,\tilde Q]_{\natural}=2(\tilde P\diamond\tilde Q)$. The plain form is $B(\tilde P,\tilde Q)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P\tilde Q)$, the quaternionic form is $N(\tilde P,\tilde Q)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q)$ of Gram matrix the identity, and the norm is $N(\tilde Q)=\sum_\mu Q_\mu^{2}$. The Multiplication Operators Are the Halved Adjoint Maps

The Two One-Sided Operators

Definition (the multiplications). The left multiplication operator of $\tilde A$ is the $\mathbb{C}$-linear map

$$ L_{\tilde A}:\mathbb{B}\to\mathbb{B},\qquad L_{\tilde A}\tilde R=\tilde A\diamond\tilde R=A_0\mathbf R-R_0\mathbf A-\mathbf A\times\mathbf R , $$

and the right multiplication operator is $R_{\tilde A}\tilde R=\tilde R\diamond\tilde A$. The map $\tilde A\mapsto L_{\tilde A}$ is injective and its four basis operators span a four-dimensional space; these are The Adjoint Operators of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra.

The two-sided family is the one-sided family. The block is alternating, $R_{\tilde A}\tilde R=\tilde R\diamond\tilde A=-\tilde A\diamond\tilde R$, hence $R_{\tilde A}=-L_{\tilde A}$ for every $\tilde A$. The negated right family is the left family, so the block has one four-dimensional operator family and not two — the mirror of the alternating sesquilinear row and the contrast with the plain product, whose two families span sixteen dimensions.

Proposition (the matrix, the trace and the rank). In the coefficient basis, with the columns the images of the basis elements,

$$ M_{\tilde A}=L_{\tilde A}= \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ -A_1 & A_0 & A_3 & -A_2 \\ -A_2 & -A_3 & A_0 & A_1 \\ -A_3 & A_2 & -A_1 & A_0 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \operatorname{Tr}L_{\tilde A}=3A_0 , $$

the first row vanishes, the vector block is $A_0I_3$ plus the skew matrix of $\mathbf R\mapsto-\mathbf A\times\mathbf R$, and the characteristic polynomial is $\lambda(\lambda-A_0)(\lambda^{2}-2A_0\lambda+N(\tilde A))$. The rank is three exactly when $N(\tilde A)\neq0$, two on the isotropic cone away from the origin, and zero only at $\tilde A=0$; the kernel always contains the line $\mathbb{C}\tilde A$, and for a regular parameter it is that line alone (The Adjoint Operators of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra).

Read as a physical statement. The operator of the block is never invertible, its kernel always containing the line of its own parameter, since $\tilde A\diamond\tilde A=0$. Offered as an interpretation, the non-invertibility is the operator face of the same fact that lets the block have no unit and no idempotent: no element acts as an identity, so no multiplication by an element can be inverted.

The Halved Adjoint and the Candidate Inner Derivations

Theorem (the multiplication is the halved adjoint map). For every $\tilde A$ the operator of the block is half the adjoint map of the quaternionic bracket, $L_{\tilde A}=\tfrac12\operatorname{ad}_{\tilde A}$, so it is an inner derivation in the sense of the adjoint map and the parameter enters through the natural conjugation of the first slot.

Proof. By the alternation of the block, $\tilde A^{\natural}\tilde R-\tilde R^{\natural}\tilde A=2(\tilde A\diamond\tilde R)$; this is the adjoint map, and the halving is the normalisation of the antisymmetric bilinear rows (The Adjoint Operators of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra).

Corollary (the candidate inner derivations are not derivations). The block has no nonzero inner derivation: a derivation must annihilate $e_0$, while $L_{\tilde A}e_0=-\mathbf A$ and $L_{\tilde A}e_1=A_0e_1$ force $\tilde A=0$. The derivation algebra is therefore the three-dimensional space of maps annihilating the scalar line and acting on the vector subspace by a skew matrix, and every derivation is outer (The Adjoint Operators of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra).

Read as a physical statement. The multiplication operators are the candidate inner derivations, and they are not derivations of the block at all. Offered as an interpretation, the operation has a four-dimensional family of adjoint maps and a three-dimensional family of genuine infinitesimal automorphisms, and the two are disjoint: the symmetry of the operation is outside the operation. This is the first place the obstruction is visible.

The Composition Law and the Deviation

Proposition (the product of two operators). For all $\tilde A,\tilde B,\tilde R$,

$$ L_{\tilde A}L_{\tilde B}\tilde R = A_0B_0\mathbf R - A_0R_0\mathbf B - A_0\,\mathbf B\times\mathbf R - B_0\,\mathbf A\times\mathbf R + R_0\,\mathbf A\times\mathbf B + (\mathbf A,\mathbf R)\mathbf B - (\mathbf A,\mathbf B)\mathbf R , $$

which is The Adjoint Operators of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra. The composite is not a multiplication in general: its value carries a scalar term $-(\mathbf A,\mathbf B)\mathbf R$ and a cross term $-A_0\mathbf B\times\mathbf R$ that one multiplication cannot carry.

Theorem (the composition law and the deviation). For all $\tilde A,\tilde B$,

$$ [L_{\tilde A},L_{\tilde B}]=L_{\tilde A\diamond\tilde B}+D(\tilde A,\tilde B),\qquad D(\tilde A,\tilde B)\tilde R=A_0\,(\mathbf B\times\mathbf R)-B_0\,(\mathbf A\times\mathbf R)+R_0\,(\mathbf A\times\mathbf B) , $$

and the deviation is the negative of the cyclic sum, $D(\tilde A,\tilde B)\tilde R=-J(\tilde A,\tilde B,\tilde R)$; the cyclic sum vanishes for all triples exactly when the block satisfies the Jacobi identity. The deviation has trace zero and is not a multiplication: $D(\tilde A,\tilde B)e_0=\mathbf A\times\mathbf B$, and at $(e_0,e_1)$ it is the cross-product operator $D(e_0,e_1)\tilde R=e_1\times\mathbf R$, nonzero at the triple $(e_0,e_1,e_2)$, where $D(e_0,e_1)e_2=+e_3$. The law is The Adjoint Operators of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra and the cyclic sum is The Jacobi Failure and the Associator Defect of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra.

Read as a physical statement. The commutator of two multiplications is the multiplication of the value plus a defect, and the defect is the operator form of the Jacobi failure. Where the plain antisymmetric row APA has $[L^{\wedge}_{\tilde A},L^{\wedge}_{\tilde B}]=L^{\wedge}_{\tilde A\wedge\tilde B}$ exactly, this block carries an extra term. Offered as an interpretation, the extra term is the price the natural conjugation in the first slot charges the operator calculus, the same defect the siblings read as the associator of the parent and as the boost-like mixed terms, here a linear map of the algebra.

The Operators Do Not Close

Theorem (the adjoint maps do not close). For all $\tilde A,\tilde B$,

$$ [\operatorname{ad}_{\tilde A},\operatorname{ad}_{\tilde B}] =\operatorname{ad}_{[\tilde A,\tilde B]_{\natural}}+4\,D(\tilde A,\tilde B) , $$

so the adjoint maps compose up to the deviation, doubled twice. The part that fails is exactly $4D(\tilde A,\tilde B)$; the part that survives is the adjoint map of the bracket.

Proof. By the composition law $[L_{\tilde A},L_{\tilde B}]=L_{\tilde A\diamond\tilde B}+D$, and $\operatorname{ad}_{\tilde A}=2L_{\tilde A}$, $\operatorname{ad}_{[\tilde A,\tilde B]_{\natural}}=4L_{\tilde A\diamond\tilde B}$.

Theorem (the failure of closure, measured by the deviation). The deviation is not a multiplication for a generic pair,

$$ D(\tilde A,\tilde B)\notin\mathbb{C}\{L_{e_0},L_{e_1},L_{e_2},L_{e_3}\} \qquad\text{for generic }\tilde A,\tilde B , $$

so $[L_{\tilde A},L_{\tilde B}]$ is not in the span of the four operators either, and the family of multiplication operators does not close as a Lie algebra. The failure is measured by the deviation $D$, equivalently by the cyclic sum $-D$, equivalently by the associator of the parent quaternionic product, the three being one obstruction (The Adjoint Operators of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra, The Jacobi Failure and the Associator Defect of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra).

Read as a physical statement. The multiplication operators are the inner derivations of the commutator in name, and the bracket of two of them is generally not an inner derivation: they generate no Lie algebra and exponentiate to no group. Offered as an interpretation, this is the operator translation of the band's negative result, and it locates the failure in the deviation $D$, the operator form of the boost-like mixed term. The compact part is untouched: on the pure vectors the block is the cross product, the deviation vanishes, and the operators close on the three rotation directions.

The Adjoint for the Quaternionic Form

The Forms of the Row

Each row of the twelve carries a form, and the block is the row where the form of the product itself is degenerate. The invariant bilinear forms are The Invariant Bilinear Forms of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra: the space is one-dimensional, spanned by $\varphi(\tilde P,\tilde Q)=P_0Q_0$, symmetric of Gram matrix $\operatorname{diag}(1,0,0,0)$ of rank one and radical the whole vector subspace; there is no nonzero alternating invariant form, and so no non-degenerate invariant form. With $\varphi$ the adjoint is undetermined, the form being blind on the vector slot, and the block supplies no adjoint of its own. Two substitutes are used: the operator trace form $K(\tilde A,\tilde B)=\operatorname{Tr}(L_{\tilde A}L_{\tilde B})=3A_0B_0-2(\mathbf A,\mathbf B)$, of Gram matrix $\operatorname{diag}(3,-2,-2,-2)$, non-degenerate but not invariant, its failure being the Jacobi failure; and the quaternionic form of the parent, $N(\tilde P,\tilde Q)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q)=P_0Q_0+(\mathbf P,\mathbf Q)$, of Gram matrix the identity, non-degenerate and the form the other quaternionic rows use (The 4 Forms over the Biquaternion $\mathbb{C}$ Space). Since the block has no non-degenerate invariant form, the form this article adjoints for is the imported $N$; the degeneracy of $\varphi$ and the non-invariance of $K$ are read in Boosts, Mixed Terms and the Missing Lie Structure and not re-read here.

The Adjoint Leaves the Family

Theorem (the adjoint for the quaternionic form). For every $\tilde A$,

$$ (L_{\tilde A})^{N}=\tfrac12\,L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde A}\bigl(\mathrm{id}-\natural\bigr) =L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde A}\circ P ,\qquad (L_{\tilde A})^{N}\tilde Y=\tilde A\,\mathbf Y , $$

where $P$ is the projection onto the vector subspace, $P\tilde Y=\tfrac12(\tilde Y-\tilde Y^{\natural})=\mathbf Y$, and $L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde A}$ is the plain left multiplication; the right adjoint is $(R_{\tilde A})^{N}=-(L_{\tilde A})^{N}$, since $R_{\tilde A}=-L_{\tilde A}$.

Proof. The quaternionic bracket is antisymmetric for $N$, $N([\tilde A,\tilde X]_{\natural},\tilde Y)=-N(\tilde X,[\tilde A,\tilde Y]_{\natural})$, because the first slot of $N$ reads the natural conjugation and the bracket is the difference of the two conjugated products; halving gives the display. Equivalently, the adjoint of a plain left multiplication is $(L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde C})^{N}=L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde C^{\natural}}$ and $\natural^{N}=\natural$ (The 4 Forms over the Biquaternion $\mathbb{C}$ Space), which gives the display and the value $\tilde A\,\mathbf Y$.

Read as a physical statement. The adjoint does not stay in the family; it is a plain left multiplication by the parameter followed by the projection onto the vector subspace. The parameter is not conjugated, the natural conjugation enters only through the projection $\mathrm{id}-\natural$, and no norm appears on the one-sided operator. The contrast with the general quaternionic row GQA is sharp: there the adjoint for the same form keeps the side and conjugates the parameter, $(L_{\tilde P})^{N}=L_{\tilde P^{\natural}}$, while here it carries the parameter through the plain product and leaves the operator family. Offered as an interpretation, the adjoint of a multiplication of the block is a transport out of the block, blind to the scalar direction because the multiplication has no scalar direction to be adjointed in.

Corollary (the adjoint of a two-sided operator). For the two-sided operator $L_{\tilde A}R_{\tilde B}$,

$$ (L_{\tilde A}R_{\tilde B})^{N}=(R_{\tilde B})^{N}(L_{\tilde A})^{N} =-\,L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde B}\circ P\circ L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde A}\circ P , $$

so the adjoint swaps the two parameters and inserts the projection, the mirror of the plain row's transpose, which swaps the sides with no projection.

The Types of the Operators

Theorem (the type criteria). For the non-degenerate forms $N$, $B$ and $K$ alike,

$$ L_{\tilde A}\ \text{self-adjoint}\iff \tilde A\ \text{central} ,\qquad L_{\tilde A}\ \text{skew-adjoint}\iff \tilde A=0 , $$

and no nonzero one-sided operator is skew-adjoint. The self-adjoint family is $\mathbb{C}L_{e_0}=\mathbb{C}P$, the multiples of the projection onto the vector subspace, and the skew family is trivial.

Proof. Read the criteria on the matrix. For $N$ the adjoint is the transpose of $M_{\tilde A}$, whose first column is zero and whose first row carries $-\mathbf A$; equality with $M_{\tilde A}$ forces $\mathbf A=0$, and then $M_{\tilde A}=A_0P$ is symmetric; equality with $-M_{\tilde A}$ forces $\mathbf A=0$ and then $M_{\tilde A}=0$. For $B$ and $K$ the adjoints differ from the transpose by the algebraic norm rescaling of the Gram matrix, which preserves both equalities.

Read as a physical statement. The block has no generators: unlike the plain antisymmetric row, where every multiplication is skew-adjoint for the Killing form, here no nonzero multiplication is skew-adjoint for any natural form. Offered as an interpretation, this is the type-criterion face of the obstruction: an operation carrying no invariant non-degenerate form carries no generator, and the only self-adjoint multiplications are the multiples of the projection onto the vector subspace, the direction the mixed terms do not touch.

Theorem (the type of the sandwich). The sandwich of the block is $\Theta_{\tilde A}=L_{\tilde A}R_{\tilde A}=-(L_{\tilde A})^{2}$, and it is $N$-self-adjoint exactly at a parameter in the vector subspace,

$$ (\Theta_{\tilde A})^{N}=\Theta_{\tilde A}\iff \tilde A\in\mathrm{Vect}(\mathbb{B}) , $$

and it is never skew-adjoint for a nonzero parameter. At $\tilde A=e_1$ the sandwich is the identity on $\mathbb{C}\{e_2,e_3\}$ and zero on $\mathbb{C}\{e_0,e_1\}$, the projection onto the plane orthogonal to the parameter.

Proof. Since $R_{\tilde A}=-L_{\tilde A}$ and $(L_{\tilde A})^{N}=L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde A}P$, the adjoint of the sandwich is $-L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde A}PL^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde A}P$, while the sandwich itself is $-L_{\tilde A}^{2}$. For a pure parameter $L_{\tilde A}^{2}$ acts as $\mathbf A\mathbf A^{T}-N(\mathbf A)I_3$ on the vector subspace and kills the scalar line, a symmetric matrix; for a parameter with a nonzero scalar part the square contains the antisymmetric term $2A_0[\mathbf A]_{\times}$ and is not symmetric. The projection statement is that computation at $N(\mathbf A)=1$, $\mathbf A=e_1$.

Read as a physical statement. The compact parameters are exactly the self-adjoint sandwiches: the pure, rotation-like parameters make the sandwich symmetric for $N$, and the parameters with a scalar part, the boost-like directions, do not. Offered as an interpretation, the sandwich is where the rotation and the boost directions separate cleanly, and the criterion is on a single parameter and not on a pair, because the block's right family is the negated left family.

The Symmetries of the Form and of the Product

Theorem (no one-sided isometry). No nonzero multiplication of the block preserves the quaternionic form,

$$ N\bigl(L_{\tilde A}\tilde X,L_{\tilde A}\tilde Y\bigr)=N(\tilde X,\tilde Y)\ \forall\tilde X,\tilde Y \iff \tilde A=0 , $$

and no two-sided operator $L_{\tilde A}R_{\tilde B}$ preserves it either, because no such operator is invertible. The isometries of $N$ among all linear maps are the group $O_4(\mathbb{C})$ of The 4 Forms over the Biquaternion $\mathbb{C}$ Space, and the block contributes only the zero operator to it.

Proof. A nonzero multiplication has the line $\mathbb{C}\tilde A$ in its kernel, so it is not invertible and preserves no non-degenerate form; the same holds for every two-sided operator, which has $L_{\tilde A}$ on the left. The one-sided case also reads on the matrix, where preservation is $M_{\tilde A}^{T}M_{\tilde A}=I_4$, which forces the zero matrix.

Read as a physical statement. The block has no form-preserving multiplication: its operators are all singular, and a singular map preserves no non-degenerate form. Offered as an interpretation, the operation carries no internal isometry, as it carries no generator and no inner derivation; the isometry group the framework uses is that of the parent associative product, and the block is a deformation of it, not a group of transformations of its own.

Theorem (the multiplicative operators). A linear isomorphism $T$ is multiplicative for the block, $T(\tilde P\diamond\tilde Q)=T\tilde P\diamond T\tilde Q$ for all $\tilde P,\tilde Q$, if and only if $T(e_0)=e_0$ and $T$ restricted to the vector subspace preserves the cross product; the automorphism group is therefore of complex dimension three, with the three-dimensional derivation algebra as its Lie algebra, and the structure group multiplicative up to a scalar is its four-dimensional conformal enlargement (The Adjoint Operators of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra).

Read as a physical statement. The block has no inner automorphism: no automorphism of the block is a multiplication by an element, because a multiplication is never injective while an automorphism is invertible. Offered as an interpretation, the symmetries of the operation are the maps of the vector subspace that preserve the cross product and fix the scalar direction, and they cannot be reduced to the operation itself; the operation's own transformations do not exist, which is the symmetry face of the obstruction.

The Operators Acting on the State Space

The state space is $S=\mathbb{B}\tilde\Pi_1=\mathbb{C}\{\tilde\Pi_1,\tilde\Upsilon_2\}$, the minimal left ideal of The Spinor Space as a Module and as a Hilbert Space, with the complementary ideal $\mathbb{B}\tilde\Pi_2$ of The Complementary Space of the Spinor Space and Its Physical Meaning.

Theorem (the action through the matrix model). In the matrix model $\mathsf{M}_2:\mathbb{B}\to M_2(\mathbb{C})$ with $\mathsf{M}_2(e_k)=-i\sigma_k$,

$$ \mathsf{M}_2\bigl(\tilde P\diamond\tilde X\bigr) =\tfrac12\Bigl(\operatorname{adj}\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde P)\,\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde X)-\operatorname{adj}\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde X)\,\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde P)\Bigr) , \qquad \mathsf{M}_2\bigl(\tilde P^{\natural}\bigr)=\operatorname{adj}\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde P) , $$

Read as a physical statement. Offered as an interpretation, the block's calculus has no state of its own: the operation is alternating and has no idempotent, so no projector of the block acts as a state and no multiplication preserves the ideal. The only projector in the operator family is $L_{e_0}=P$, the projection onto the vector subspace, which is The Specificities of the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra.

The Sandwich, the Two-Sided Operators and the Spin Group

Proposition (the sandwich against an inner automorphism). The two-sided operators of the block form the composite family $L_{\tilde A}R_{\tilde B}=-L_{\tilde A}L_{\tilde B}$; the sandwich is $\Theta_{\tilde A}=-(L_{\tilde A})^{2}$, self-adjoint for $N$ exactly at the pure parameters, and there is no inner automorphism to compare it with, because no operator of the form $L_{\tilde A}R_{\tilde A^{-1}}$ exists: every multiplication is singular.

Read as a physical statement. In the plain ordinary row the sandwich and the inner automorphism are conjugations by the conjugate and by the inverse of one parameter, agreeing on the compact slice. The block has the sandwich and no inner automorphism: the conjugating operator an automorphism would need does not exist. Offered as an interpretation, the block has a symmetry of the form — the self-adjoint sandwich at a pure parameter — and no symmetry of the product, and the gap between them is what the siblings read as the missing Lie structure.

Citation (the two-sided operators and the spin group). The group of two-sided operators of the ambient Clifford and associative algebra is the family $T_{a,b}(y)=ayb$ over the units, generated by the two one-sided families and with the algebraic norm of the central units as the kernel of the parametrisation; the versors generate inside it the subgroup $T_{x,x^{-1}}$, and the even norm-one versors are the spin group, realised as a group of two-sided operators with the double cover recovered from the versor (The Two-Sided Operators and the Spin Group). The block does not reach that group: its two-sided family is the negated product of two left multiplications, all of them singular, and the operators with their pairwise products span a space of dimension twelve inside the sixteen-dimensional endomorphism algebra. So the block has no versor and no spin group of its own, and the spin group of the framework is a group of two-sided operators of the associative product, cited and not derived here.

Read as a physical statement. Offered as an interpretation, the spin group a physical theory uses is carried by the associative product and its two-sided operators, and the block's calculus is not a group of transformations: it generates no versor and stays inside a proper subalgebra. The block is a bracket on the differential side of the algebra and not a group of operators on the transformation side.

A Worked Case

Take one parameter with a scalar and a vector part and two pure parameters,

$$ \tilde A=e_0+e_1,\qquad \tilde B=e_2,\qquad \tilde R=e_3 , \qquad N(\tilde A)=2,\qquad N(\tilde B)=N(\tilde R)=1 . $$

With $e_1\times e_2=e_3$, $e_2\times e_3=e_1$, $e_3\times e_1=e_2$, the block values are

$$ \tilde A\diamond\tilde B=e_2-e_3,\qquad \tilde B\diamond\tilde R=-e_1,\qquad \tilde R\diamond\tilde A=-(e_2+e_3) . $$

The deviation and the cyclic sum at the triple are the negatives of each other,

$$ D(\tilde A,\tilde B)\tilde R=e_2\times e_3=e_1,\qquad J(\tilde A,\tilde B,\tilde R)=-e_1 , $$

so the composition law reads

$$ [L_{\tilde A},L_{\tilde B}]e_3=(e_2-e_3)\diamond e_3+e_1=-e_1+e_1=0 , $$

and indeed $L_{\tilde A}L_{\tilde B}e_3=L_{\tilde B}L_{\tilde A}e_3=-e_1$, so the commutator vanishes there. It does not vanish as an operator: $D(\tilde A,\tilde B)e_0=\mathbf A\times\mathbf B=e_1\times e_2=e_3$, which no multiplication of the block produces, since every multiplication annihilates the scalar line.

The operator invariants are read on the same element. The parameter lies off the isotropic cone, so the operator has rank three and kernel the line $\mathbb{C}\tilde A$, and $\operatorname{Tr}L_{\tilde A}=3A_0=3$; its adjoint for the quaternionic form is the plain left multiplication by the parameter through the projection,

$$ (L_{\tilde A})^{N}e_3=\tilde A\,e_3=e_3+e_1e_3=e_3-e_2 ,\qquad (L_{\tilde A})^{N}e_0=\tilde A\,\mathbf 0=0 . $$

The parameter is neither central nor zero, so $L_{\tilde A}$ is neither self-adjoint nor skew-adjoint for $N$. At the central parameter $e_0$ the operator is the projection $L_{e_0}=P$, self-adjoint and idempotent, $L_{e_0}^{2}=L_{e_0}$, the only self-adjoint multiplication of the block. The sandwich separates the pure and the mixed parameters: at the pure $\tilde B=e_2$ it is self-adjoint, and at $\tilde A=e_0+e_1$ it is not, the scalar part of the parameter being what breaks the symmetry; at the pure unit $\tilde A=e_1$ it is the identity on $\mathbb{C}\{e_2,e_3\}$.

A numerical check. The explicit form, the relation $L_{\tilde A}=\tfrac12\operatorname{ad}_{\tilde A}$ and $R_{\tilde A}=-L_{\tilde A}$ were checked on random Gaussian-integer parameters to machine precision. The composition law $[L_{\tilde A},L_{\tilde B}]=L_{\tilde A\diamond\tilde B}+D(\tilde A,\tilde B)$ and the identity $D(\tilde A,\tilde B)\tilde R=-J(\tilde A,\tilde B,\tilde R)$ were checked on eighty random triples with no residual; the law of the adjoint maps $[\operatorname{ad}_{\tilde A},\operatorname{ad}_{\tilde B}]=\operatorname{ad}_{[\tilde A,\tilde B]_{\natural}}+4D$ was checked on thirty triples; the deviation was confirmed to lie outside the span of the four operators on every sampled pair; and the operator span was confirmed twelve-dimensional, thirteen with the identity. The adjoint identity $(L_{\tilde A})^{N}\tilde Y=\tilde A\,\mathbf Y=\tfrac12L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde A}(\mathrm{id}-\natural)\tilde Y$ was checked on random parameters; the type criteria were scanned on central, pure, null and mixed parameters; the sandwich criterion (self-adjoint at the pure parameters, never skew for a nonzero one) was scanned on four thousand random elements; and the matrix-model identity was checked on random pairs. The witness $J(e_0,e_1,e_2)=-e_3$ of the siblings reproduces as $D(e_0,e_1)e_2=+e_3$.

The Ledger

Proved. The left multiplication $L_{\tilde A}\tilde R=\tilde A\diamond\tilde R$ and the right one $R_{\tilde A}\tilde R=\tilde R\diamond\tilde A$, with $R_{\tilde A}=-L_{\tilde A}$, so the block has one four-dimensional operator family; the matrix $M_{\tilde A}$, its trace $3A_0$, its characteristic polynomial and its rank, three off the isotropic cone and two on it, with the kernel always containing the line of the parameter; the halved adjoint relation $L_{\tilde A}=\tfrac12\operatorname{ad}_{\tilde A}$; the composition law $[L_{\tilde A},L_{\tilde B}]=L_{\tilde A\diamond\tilde B}+D(\tilde A,\tilde B)$ and the deviation $D(\tilde A,\tilde B)\tilde R=A_0(\mathbf B\times\mathbf R)-B_0(\mathbf A\times\mathbf R)+R_0(\mathbf A\times\mathbf B)$, its trace zero and its identity with the negative of the cyclic sum; the law of the adjoint maps $[\operatorname{ad}_{\tilde A},\operatorname{ad}_{\tilde B}]=\operatorname{ad}_{[\tilde A,\tilde B]_{\natural}}+4D$; the failure of closure, the deviation lying outside the span of the operators; the operator algebra of dimension twelve, thirteen with the identity; the adjoint for the quaternionic form, $(L_{\tilde A})^{N}=\tfrac12L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde A}(\mathrm{id}-\natural)=L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde A}\circ P$, and the adjoint of the two-sided operator swapping the parameters; the type criteria, self-adjoint exactly for a central parameter and skew-adjoint only for zero, with self-adjoint family $\mathbb{C}P$; the sandwich $\Theta_{\tilde A}=-(L_{\tilde A})^{2}$, self-adjoint exactly at the pure parameters and never skew for a nonzero one; the absence of any form-preserving multiplication; the multiplicative operators as the cross-product-preserving maps fixing $e_0$, of dimension three, with no inner automorphism; the matrix model of the action; and the absence of a module structure for the block.

Interpretations. That the multiplication operators are the candidate inner derivations and are not derivations of the block; that the bracket of two of them is generally not an inner derivation, measured by the deviation $D$, equivalently by the cyclic sum and by the associator; that the adjoint of a multiplication is a transport out of the family, the plain left multiplication by the parameter through the projection, with no conjugation of the parameter and no norm on the one-sided operator; that the block has no generator, no isometry and no inner automorphism; that the sandwich is symmetric exactly at the pure, rotation-like parameters, the scalar part of the parameter being what breaks it; that the state space of the block is carried into the vector subspace and carries no module structure for it; and that the block is a bracket on the differential side and not a group of transformations. Each is the framework's naming of a proved algebraic fact and is labelled as such.

Physical Readings

The operators of the quaternionic commutator read as the differential side of the frame, and the obstruction reads on them in three places. Read as generators, they fail: the commutator of two multiplications is the multiplication of the value plus the deviation, so the operators generate no Lie algebra and exponentiate to no group. Read as symmetries, they fail: every multiplication is singular, so the block carries no form-preserving operator, no isometry and no inner automorphism, and its symmetries lie outside itself. Read as forms, they fail: the block has no non-degenerate invariant form, so the adjoint has to be imported and then leaves the family. In the three interpretations the failure is one, and it is the operator expression of the band's word, Obstruction.

On the pure vectors the block is the cross product, the deviation vanishes and the operators close, and the self-adjoint sandwiches are exactly the pure, rotation-like parameters; on the mixed terms the deviation is nonzero and closure fails, and the boost-like parameters make the sandwich non-symmetric. Offered as an interpretation, the operator calculus carries the compact rotations and fails on the boosts, and the operator form of the failure is the deviation $D(\tilde A,\tilde B)$, which the companion Boosts, Mixed Terms and the Missing Lie Structure reads as the anomaly of the internal symmetry. The group the framework reaches is read off the associative one-sided action, not off these operators, and the caution of the siblings stands: an obstruction constrains an interpretation without supplying one.

Summary

The block is the antisymmetric quaternionic multiplication $\tilde P\diamond\tilde Q=\tfrac12(\tilde P^{\natural}\tilde Q-\tilde Q^{\natural}\tilde P)=P_0\mathbf Q-Q_0\mathbf P-\mathbf P\times\mathbf Q$, with operators the left multiplications $L_{\tilde A}\tilde R=\tilde A\diamond\tilde R$, the right family being the negated left family, $R_{\tilde A}=-L_{\tilde A}$. The left multiplication is the halved adjoint map, $L_{\tilde A}=\tfrac12\operatorname{ad}_{\tilde A}$, so the operators are the inner derivations of the commutator in name; the commutator of two of them is the operator of the value plus the deviation, $[L_{\tilde A},L_{\tilde B}]=L_{\tilde A\diamond\tilde B}+D(\tilde A,\tilde B)$, and the deviation is the negative of the cyclic Jacobi sum and lies outside the span of the operators. So the operators do not close: the bracket of two inner derivations is generally not an inner derivation, and the block generates no Lie algebra and no group. The block carries no non-degenerate invariant form, so the operators are adjoint for the imported quaternionic form $N$, and the adjoint is the plain left multiplication by the parameter followed by the projection onto the vector subspace, $(L_{\tilde A})^{N}=\tfrac12L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde A}(\mathrm{id}-\natural)=L^{\mathrm{plain}}_{\tilde A}\circ P$, which leaves the family, carrying no norm; the type criteria are degenerate, self-adjoint for $N$ exactly at a central parameter, so the self-adjoint family is $\mathbb{C}P$, and skew-adjoint only for zero, so the block has no generator, while the sandwich $\Theta_{\tilde A}=-(L_{\tilde A})^{2}$ is self-adjoint exactly at the pure, rotation-like parameters. The block has no form-preserving multiplication, no isometry, no inner automorphism, and no versor or spin group of its own. The interpretation is the band's word Obstruction: the operators of the quaternionic commutator are the differential side of the frame that fails to be a Lie algebra, and the failure is measured by the deviation that is the operator form of the Jacobi identity.

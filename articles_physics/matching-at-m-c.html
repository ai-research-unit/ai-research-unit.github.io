Matching at $M_C$: Four Quantities Cross, and Every Mass Comes from the Vacuum That Made It

Introduction

The layer. This is the interface article. Everything in it is a statement about which quantities cross a scale and which do not, and its Layer-A content is one sentence: the vacuum is the same vacuum on both sides of the matching, so no eigenvalue moves.

The question. At what scale does the $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ model stop, what exactly is handed over at that scale, and where does each mass in the final spectrum come from?

The answer.

$$ \boxed{\; M_C\ >\ v,\qquad M_C\gtrsim250\ \mathrm{TeV},\qquad v=246\ \mathrm{GeV} ; \;} $$

$$ \boxed{\; \text{at }M_C:\ \mathrm{SU}(3)_c\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}, \text{ rank }3,\ \text{nine massless vectors} ; \;} $$

So the matching is a matching of a group and a spectrum, not of a Lagrangian. The transition leaves behind a sector, a set of masses at the scale, and one coupling to be matched. The left–right group is the group at $M_C$ and it has no width: the same vacuum that produces it makes the right-handed bosons heavy, so below $M_C$ the description is the Standard Model's.

What this article establishes. The four quantities that cross the boundary; the ordering of the two scales and why it is the only ordering available; the sense in which the left–right group is a stage of zero width rather than a second threshold; the fate of the mega coupling at the matching, including the tension the corpus already records and which the migration does not repair; the origin of every mass in the final spectrum; and the list of things that are not passed down, which is where the corpus's neutral sector and its mixing matrix stand.

Not established. The numerical value of the mega coupling. It is fixed by a boundary condition the corpus already says fails, and that is the corpus's defect rather than one introduced here. The migration is about origins rather than about unification.

Where the rest is. The construction that produces the group at $M_C$ is Rank Five From Three. The breaking that produces the group factor is The Breaking of the Primordial Force. The sextet's mass and bound are Six Leptoquarks Above $M_C$. Everything below the matching is the existing corpus, beginning with §The Scale Ledger below and continuing with The Mass Matrix of the Fifteen Connections.

The group is the item that matters, and it is exact at the scale. It is not an approximation and not a limit: at $M_C$ the two factors are the symmetry group of the theory, and above $M_C$ they are not a group at all — above it there is one simple factor, and the two are a decomposition of the adjoint.

The group has no width, and that is the article's one structural claim. The vacuum that produces the two factors is a line in the carrier, $\operatorname{diag}(-3,1,1,1)$. That one line is what makes the sextet heavy, so the two-factor group and the mass of the six are the same event. A stage of zero width is a description and not a threshold: below $M_C$ the effective theory is the strong group and $B-L$, and the six are integrated out at the scale that produced them.

The nine massless vectors are exact in the same sense, and the count falls at $M_C$. Above the transition the fifteen directions of $\mathfrak{su}(4)$ are all massless, no vacuum having formed. At $M_C$ the kernel is nine — eight gluons and $B'$, computed once in The Breaking of the Primordial Force — while the other six, the sextet, take $32v_C^{2}$. No further vectors arrive: the nine are the whole massless ledger at $M_C$. The sextet is heavy on $v_C$, so the massless ledger is

$$ 15\ \longrightarrow\ 9 , $$

fifteen above the transition and nine at $M_C$, and the nine are the kernel.

The sextet is a bound and not a value, because $v_C$ is not fixed by the model; the bound is $250$ TeV and it is what makes the transition scale a measured quantity rather than a parameter.

And the couplings are the one item that needs an act of matching rather than a computation. That act is the subject of §The Coupling at the Matching, and it is where the corpus's one serious tension lives.

The Scale Ledger

Two scales, and each has a different status.

scale status value
$M_C$ bounded below by $K_L\to\mu e$ $\gtrsim250$ TeV
$v$ measured $246$ GeV

The ordering is forced, and the reason is separation of sectors. The two vacua act on disjoint factors — $Ξ$ on the strong group, $H$ on the electroweak group — so neither can undo the other, and the order is fixed by the scales alone. One condition fixes the order: the field of each step must carry that step's index.

(a) A field breaks a factor only if it transforms under it. With no index on the factor there is no generator to act on the vacuum, so nothing can be broken. $Ξ$ is a quartet of the $\mathbf4$ and a singlet under everything else: it can break the strong group, and nothing more. $H$ is blind to the block: it breaks the electroweak group, and that step is the Standard Model's rather than this model's. This is a statement about the multiplet list, and it is what assigns each step its field.

(b) A field must be neutral under the factors broken earlier. Otherwise its vacuum tilts the previously fixed line and undoes the earlier step. $Ξ$ carries no electroweak index, so the vacuum that fixes the spatial line leaves the electroweak sector untouched.

The order of the steps is a statement about the multiplets and not about the group. A different scalar content would permit a different chain, and then the group at $M_C$, the charge assignments and the massless count would all differ. What is group-theoretic is the stabiliser the lines cut out, and that does not move.

The scale order is a condition on the description, not a theorem about the group. $v>M_C$ does not contradict the group theory; it contradicts the sentence "between $M_C$ and $v$ the theory is $\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{u}(1)_{B-L}$", which says which effective theory is used where. The breaking pattern is ordering-independent; only its factorization into stages is not.

The ordering in one line.

$$ M_C\ >\ v . $$

What the inequality protects. $M_C>v$ is a condition on the description: hypercharge must be formed before it is broken, and the left line must survive the first step untouched so that $H$ can cut it.

The budget, in the one-group frame. The weak pair arrives with the index at $M_C$ rather than sitting above it, so at $M_C$ the rank rises before it falls:

$$ 15\to12\to9\ \text{massless vectors},\qquad 3\to5\to4\to3\ \text{rank}. $$

The first arrow is the whole of $M_C$, and it is one event. Nine of the fifteen directions are the kernel and stay massless; the other six, the sextet, take $32v_C^{2}$ and eat six of $Ξ$'s Goldstones. So the massless count falls by six: $15-6=9$, and the nine are the kernel. The algebra changes from $\mathfrak{su}(4)$ to $\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{u}(1)_{B-L}$, dimension twelve.

The rank, in the same reading. It falls by one at $M_C$: the broken direction leaves a single abelian one, so three becomes three only in count and not in kind, $\mathfrak{su}(4)$'s three Cartans becoming the block's two plus $B-L$'s one. The rank is three on both sides of the transition, and the group is simple above it.

The second arrow is $v$, and it is the Standard Model's. The weak bosons acquire mass from $H$, and the nine of the kernel remain: eight gluons and the $B-L$ vector.

And the values, in the two-unit convention the corpus uses. The sextet's bound is $4.0\times10^{-5}$ J, which is $2.5\times10^{2}$ TeV; the weak scale is $80.4$ GeV; the ratio is $3.1\times10^{3}$, three and a half decades, and that is the whole of the hierarchy the first step is allowed to create.

The Coupling at the Matching

One coupling above, two below. At $M_C$ the single mega coupling must reproduce both the strong coupling and the $B-L$ coupling:

$$ g_4(M_C)\ \longrightarrow\ \bigl(g_3(M_C),\,g_{B-L}(M_C)\bigr), $$

which fixes one relation among the two and leaves the other as a prediction of the matching.

The corpus's condition, and its failure, stated once. The natural boundary condition is equal couplings at the transition, $g_3=g_{B-L}=g_4$. That condition fixes one number, the normalisation $\sin^{2}\theta_W=\tfrac38$ at $M_C$, and the corpus computes that the measured couplings are met four decades apart: $1.63\times10^{3}$ J and $1.57\times10^{7}$ J, that is $1.0\times10^{13}$ GeV and $9.8\times10^{16}$ GeV. No single threshold satisfies it.

What the migration does about it: nothing, and that is the correct amount. The failure is a statement about unification — about whether one coupling at one scale can reproduce three measured ones — and the migration is a statement about origins. A construction that births a group does not have to unify its couplings, and pretending otherwise would be the kind of overreach that makes a reframing look like a solution.

What the migration does change, and it is worth being precise. Before, the coupling failure was a failure of the model's premise — the premise being that $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ is the whole structure. After, the premise is that $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ is the whole structure, and the electroweak couplings below $M_C$ are the Standard Model's. The failure remains exactly as computed; what changes is what it is a failure of.

Read the column of scales. Every mass is at the scale of the vacuum that produced it, and no mass is at a scale it did not come from. The one row at $M_C$ is the quartet index of $Ξ$: it makes the sextet heavy.

Read the two zeros. They are not accidents and they are not fine tuning: the photon is the null direction of a sum of two rank-one matrices, and the gluons are the directions the first vacuum annihilates. Both are structural, both are computed, and both survive the migration untouched.

Read what is absent. No entry is a composite, no entry needs a binding energy, and no entry is a difference of large numbers. A ledger that looks like this is a ledger of a renormalisable theory with hierarchical breaking, which is what the corpus is.

What Is Not Passed Down

Three absences, and each one is a place where the corpus stands unchanged.

The weak group is not passed down; it is born at the matching. Above $M_C$ it does not exist; at $M_C$ it does, and only the left factor survives below. So the matching is the moment of birth and of the right factor's death at once, and the word matching is used for the spectrum and the group factor, which are transferred, and not for the weak factors, which arrive.

The photon is not passed down; it is a mixture formed later. At $M_C$ there is the corner $B'$, the right Cartan's surviving direction and the left Cartan; the photon is the null direction of a $3\times3$ matrix built from the two vacua, and it does not exist as a separate factor at any point. The corpus computes it in The Weak Sector Below $M_C$, and the migration does not touch that calculation.

The mixing angle is not passed down; it is computed downstream. This is why the $45^\circ$ of the additive identities cannot be the mixing: the mixing is a property of a mass matrix that needs $H$'s vacuum to exist. The identities are a statement about a basis; the angle is a statement about a vacuum, and the second vacuum is later.

The three absences in one sentence. What crosses $M_C$ is a group and a spectrum; what is born and dies at $M_C$ is the right factor; what is created after $M_C$ is a photon and an angle.

Two Numbers That Must Not Be Confused

The corpus contains two distinct large scales, and they answer different questions.

scale what it is how it is obtained
$M_C\gtrsim250$ TeV a floor on the transition $K_L\to\mu e$, the sextet's own bound
$\sim10^{13}$ GeV where one coupling would reproduce the measured ones the equal-coupling condition, which fails

They differ by a factor of about forty million, and both are correct. The first is a lower bound from a flavour-changing decay; the second is the scale a unification would prefer. A transition anywhere between them satisfies the flavour bound and misses the unification preference, which is exactly the tension the corpus records.

The rule that follows. When a statement depends on the scale, say which scale it uses. The confinement-scale argument of The Charge Theorem quoted three and a half decades, which is the most favourable case: at the floor. At the unification-preferred scale it is eleven decades. Either way the composite route is closed, and the article says both numbers rather than the flattering one.

Distinctions That Are Easy to Lose

A matching is not a unification. The group and the spectrum cross the scale; the couplings are matched and the matching fails, as the corpus already says (The Coupling at the Matching).

A floor is not a prediction. $M_C\gtrsim250$ TeV says where the transition cannot be, not where it is (Two Numbers That Must Not Be Confused).

Created at $M_C$ is not passed down at $M_C$. The group factor and the nine vectors stand (What Is Not Passed Down).

The mass ledger is not a hierarchy problem. Every mass is at its own vacuum's scale, and no mass is a difference of large numbers (Where the Masses Come From).

Summary

The matching is at $M_C\gtrsim250$ TeV, bounded by $K_L\to\mu e$ and not by proton decay.

Four quantities cross. The group $\mathrm{SU}(3)_c\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{B-L}$, exact at $M_C$, rank three; the nine massless vectors, the kernel of $\operatorname{ad}(iD)$; the six heavy vectors at $M_X^{2}=16g_4^{2}v_C^{2}$; and the couplings, to be matched.

The ordering follows from separate sectors, $M_C>v$, and the only hierarchy the first step creates is three and a half decades at the floor. The two-factor group has no width: it is the group at $M_C$, and the vacuum that produces it makes the sextet heavy.

Every mass comes from the vacuum that made it. The sextet from the quartet index of $Ξ$ at $M_C$, $W$ and $Z$ from $v=246$ GeV, and the photon and the gluons from structure — a null direction and a stabiliser. No composite, no binding energy, no difference of large numbers.

Two things are not passed down. The photon is a mixture formed after the matching; the mixing angle is computed after it. And that is why the $45^\circ$ of the identities cannot be the electroweak mixing: the mass matrix it would have to be an angle of does not exist yet.

The corpus below $M_C$ stands. It is the second and third sections of this part, and none of its numbers moves at the matching: the transition changes where the weak factors come from, not a single eigenvalue.

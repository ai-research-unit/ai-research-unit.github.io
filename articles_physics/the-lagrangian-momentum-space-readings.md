# __The Lagrangian: The Momentum-Space Readings__

The momentum-space readings transform the action at a fixed frequency and wave vector, so that the differential operator becomes an algebraic symbol and the variation becomes a matrix problem.

**What the group does.** It exhibits the symbol of each action, the boundary term that one variation suffices to handle, and the no-ghost or reality conditions that the symbol's eigenvalues impose. The propagators appear here as Green functions of the symbol: they are classical objects and not Feynman propagators.

**What the group does not claim.** It does not quantise. A reader who sees a pole in a symbol should read it as a Green function's pole and not as a particle's mass shell, though the two coincide for the free theory.

**One result worth naming.** The momentum-space article of the scalar shows that the reality of the action is the anti-Hermiticity of the root, and that a weaker condition — reality of the spectrum — is not enough. That is the group's structural finding.

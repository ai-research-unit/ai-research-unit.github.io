# __The Most Common Items with a Tilde__

The tables that catalogue the tilde glyphs — their scope, the allowed uses with the article counts, and the governing rule — are held in §*The Most Common Quaternions and Biquaternions* of *Conventions in the Biquaternion Universe*. The notes below record the two remaining points of the audit.

## Additional tilde to review

### Bound Letters

No unbound letter remains. Every letter outside the Q family that names an element is bound in those tables or where it is introduced: $\tilde R$ for the material four-position, with $\tilde R_{\mathrm d}$ and $\tilde R_0$ for the translation displacement; $\tilde\Upsilon$ for the nilpotent partner of $\tilde\Pi_+$; and $\tilde U_T$ for the phase gate. The unbound $\tilde T$ is gone, and no other letter is unbound.

### Glyphs without a Tilde

A scan of the 2528 articles finds no tilde outside those tables. The reversion of a Clifford element is written $x^{r}$, the anti-automorphism that reverses the order of a product, and the Clifford conjugation is $\hat x=\alpha(x^{r})$; the four Clifford articles that wrote the reversion with a tilde — *Hermitian Clifford Structures*, *The Adjoint of the Left Multiplication on a Clifford Algebra*, *The Signed Adjoint of the Left Multiplication on a Clifford Algebra* and *The Signed Adjoint Sandwich on a Clifford Algebra* — now use this notation.

A tilde on a matrix is forbidden, §*General Matrix Conventions* of *Conventions in the Biquaternion Universe*, and is not catalogued.

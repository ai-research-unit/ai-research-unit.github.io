# __The Most Common Items with a Tilde__

**Scope: tilde glyphs only.** Every catalogued symbol carries a tilde. Four kinds of glyph are outside the scope and are not listed among the allowed uses: a matrix, $I_n$, $\mathsf{M}_2$, $\mathsf{M}_4$; a pure element written with an arrow, $\vec Q$, $\vec q$, $\vec u$, $\vec U$, $\vec\Xi$, $\vec\Upsilon$, $\vec\pi$; a mark that is not an element, $\tilde\cdot$, $\tilde\star$, $\tilde{\ }$; and every glyph of the reduced, tilde-free layers, $\Pi_\pm$, $\Xi_\pm$, $\Upsilon_\pm$. The first is fixed by the rule that a matrix carries no tilde, §*General Matrix Conventions* of *Conventions in the Biquaternion Universe*; the others are elements of other layers or of no layer at all. The operator section states the rule that pairs the two marks; it is not a catalogue of arrowed glyphs.

The tilde marks a quaternionic factor. Each entry gives the number of articles in which the symbol occurs; a base glyph counts every numbered and subscripted variant. The count covers the 2528 articles of `articles_maths/` (2110) and `articles_physics/` (418) on 18 October 2026; the catalogue itself is excluded.

**Allowed tilde.** The role is fixed by *Conventions in the Biquaternion Universe*, or by the case-and-mark rule that article states, and checked against the corpus. Cite these entries as they stand. The tables are ordered by theme, as the objects are; the bound letters that have no row of their own follow them.

The governing rule: **only the Q family is generic, and only an element carries the tilde.** $\tilde Q$ is the biquaternion or split-biquaternion element and $\tilde q$ the quaternion or split-quaternion element; numbered variants are allowed. A generic element that is unitary is written $\tilde U$. A letter outside the family names an object, or an element bound where it is introduced. A matrix — the image of an element, an array of biquaternions, or a colour matrix — takes no tilde; a tilde on one is a defect of scope, corrected where it occurs and not catalogued here.

**The letter $K$ has other, untitled roles.** The kinetic kernel is the central operator $K_{\mathrm{kin}}=\Box-m^2$, with $\det K_{\mathrm{kin}}$ and $K_{\mathrm{kin}}^{-1}$, and it takes no tilde because it is central. **The flow generator, the gluon octet and the finite dilation are not glyphs.** The generator of the Schrödinger flow is the Hermitian observable read through the central $i$, $\tilde H\mapsto-i\tilde H/\hbar$; it is not a second quantity. The flow generator is written out in terms of $\tilde H$: $-i\tilde H/\hbar$, or $-\hbar^{-1}J\tilde H$ for a candidate root $J$, in *The Schrödinger Equation in Biquaternionic Form*; $-i\tilde H/\hbar$ in *The Klein–Gordon Equation in Biquaternionic Form*; $-iH/\hbar$, with the central Hamiltonian $H$, in *The Hydrogen Atom in Biquaternionic Form: The Non-Relativistic Case*; and $-i\tilde H$ in *The Lyapunov Exponent and Information Loss in the Biquaternion Framework*. The gluon is an octet: its Lie-algebra-valued potential and field strength are expanded in the tensor formalism into the components $G^a_\mu$ and $G^a_{\mu\nu}$, with the colour index explicit; a fixed colour component has the abelian massless-vector kinematics of the photon article, and the octet takes no single-element glyph. The finite dilation of the conformal group is the constant central element $\sqrt\lambda e_t$, a scalar multiple of the unit fixed by $\lambda$; it is not a field and needs no glyph of its own, so *The Conformal Group in Biquaternionic Form* writes $\sqrt\lambda e_t$ in the conjugation. After these rewrites the glyph $\tilde G$ and the kernel $\tilde{G}_{\tilde{\nabla}}$ occur as elements only in the five articles of the entry: two for the biwave source and three for $\tilde{G}_{\tilde{\nabla}}$; the axion label $G\tilde G$ of the article named above is the standard name of the coupling, whose second factor is a dual field strength, not an element; it is outside the catalogue.

The field shift is $\delta\tilde\Phi$, the sector parts are $\tilde\Phi_\pm$, and the classical fields are $\tilde\Phi_{\mathrm{cl}}$ and $\tilde\Phi_{\mathrm{St}}$.

**$\tilde\pi$ is not the Goldstone glyph.** The Goldstone field of *The Pion and the Chiral Lagrangian in Biquaternionic Form*, *The Nonlinear Sigma Model in Biquaternionic Form* and *The Skyrme Model and the Topological Baryon in Biquaternionic Form* is $\pi^ae_a$ with real $\pi^a$, a pure element of the biquaternion layer: $\mathrm{Sc}(\pi^ae_a)=0$ and $\mathrm{Sc}((\pi^ae_a)^2)=-\pi^a\pi^a$. By §*Pure Elements and their Coefficients* of *Conventions in the Biquaternion Universe* a pure element takes the arrow and not the tilde, so the three articles write $\vec\pi=\pi^ae_a$, and *Custodial Symmetry and the Rho Parameter in Biquaternionic Form* writes the same glyph for the triplet. The element lies in the compact subalgebra $\mathfrak{su}(2)\subset\mathbb{M}_-$, not in the group $\mathrm{SU}(2)$; the group element is the exponential $\tilde U=\exp(\vec\pi/f)\in\mathbb{H}^1_{\mathbb{B}}\cong\mathrm{SU}(2)$. The glyph $\tilde\pi$ keeps two roles, neither of them the Goldstone field: the idempotent of the quaternion and split-quaternion layers of the mathematical corpus, and the momenta of the canonical-quantization, Landau-level and quantum-gravity articles, where $\tilde\pi=\pi^0e_t+\vec\pi$ in the Maxwell and Proca cases and the scalar part is constrained or central. The row's count of $38$ covered the idempotent and the momenta as well as the Goldstone field, which alone occurs in four articles; the row now stands in section 1 with the count $34$.

**$\tilde\sigma$ is not a generic glyph.** Two bound roles remain: the central scalar $\tilde\sigma=\sigma e_t\in\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}$ of *The Stueckelberg Mechanism in Biquaternionic Form*, *Scalar Pair Creation in Biquaternionic Form* and *The Scalar Field Path Integral in Biquaternionic Form* ($16$ tokens, $3$ articles); and the subnormalised operator $\tilde\sigma_{ABC}$ of *Strong Subadditivity in the Biquaternion Framework*, which that article distinguishes from a pair of states. No article writes $\tilde\sigma$ for a state.

**$\tilde g$ is bound in three articles.** The group element of the pion article's adjoint action is the rotor $\tilde u=\exp(\vec\alpha/2)\in\mathbb{H}^1_{\mathbb{B}}$, a unit real quaternion, the glyph $\tilde u$ of §*The Layer Glyphs*, and the article writes it so. The stabilizer and context elements of *Quantum Error Correction and the Stabilizer Formalism in Biquaternionic Form* and *Contextuality and the Kochen–Specker Theorem in Biquaternionic Form*, and the quoted stabilizer projector of *Square Roots of One and the Idempotents in the Biquaternioid Structures over $\mathbb{C}$*, are bound where they are introduced. The glyph occurs in three articles and $60$ tokens, all bound.

### Marks and Matrix Tildes Not Catalogued

Seven further tilde forms lie outside the lists: three marks and four matrix tildes. *Hermitian Clifford Structures* writes the reversion mark $\tilde{\ }$, which marks an operation and not an element. *Quaternion Algebra* and *Worked Examples in the Quaternion Algebra* write $\tilde\cdot$ for the vector conjugation whose fixed space is $\operatorname{Im}\mathbb H$, and *The CPT Theorem in Biquaternionic Form* writes $\tilde\star$ for the conjugation operation, $A\mapsto A^{\mathrm{rev}}=\tilde\star$. *Conventions in the Biquaternion Universe* quotes four forbidden matrix tildes, $\tilde I_n$, $\tilde{\mathsf M}_2$, $\tilde{\mathcal Q}$ and $\tilde M$ where the matrix $\mathsf M_2(\tilde M)$ is meant, under its ban on a tilde on an array. The three mark forms are the reversion, the vector conjugation and the conjugation of the CPT article; the four matrix tildes are quoted as forbidden. Two catalogued glyphs also carry a mark: *The CPT Theorem in Biquaternionic Form* writes $\tilde A$ for the reversion of a Clifford algebra, $A\mapsto\tilde A$, in three tokens, so one of the $83$ articles counted for $\tilde A$ uses the letter as a mark and not as the four-potential; and *The Signed-Adjoint of the Left Multiplication on a Clifford Algebra*, *The Signed-Adjoint Sandwich on a Clifford Algebra* and *Hermitian Clifford Structures* write the same reversion as $\tilde x$ in $\hat x=\alpha(\tilde x)$, seven tokens in three articles; every remaining $\tilde x$ is that mark, and no bound use remains. All are recorded here so that the scan accounts for every token; none is available for citation.

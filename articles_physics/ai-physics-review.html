Introduction

This article is a digest of the biquaternion physics corpus, focused on developing quantum physics in a way natively compatible with relativity. In particular, it can be used by an artificial reader to assess the project. It is written to stand alone: it defines the algebra it uses, it cites no source in support of its claims, and every claim it makes about the algebra can be rechecked from the definitions below. In practice, the text can be copied whole into an AI chat bot, which will produce a first assessment of the theory from it.

The project is a research corpus of about 400+ physics articles, beside 300+ in the biquaternion chapter of the mathematics corpus. The two halves have different status. The mathematics is established: it proves theorems from the definitions, and they hold whatever one thinks of the physical interpretation. The physics is a research programme: its algebra is that same established algebra, and the open question is whether reading the two sectors as relativity and as quantum physics is right. Most of the corpus is known physics restated, which a shared algebra makes expected. What is the corpus's own — the sesqualgebras, the twelve non-associative biquaternion structures, the derivation of the form of the Born rule, the action of the Lorentz group on the informational sector — what a boost does to a state, as against what a rotation does — the interpretation of the Wick rotation, the thermal formalism recovered without it, and, in the particle sector, the $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ model with its fifteen generators and the Excitation Ladder Theory of Flavour — is collected in the result sections below.

This digest gives the central affirmation of the physics corpus, the reasons that affirmation is structurally plausible, the established results the framework rests on or supplies, the new results the corpus adds, and the limits.

The multi-qubit extension has since been worked out. Composition is by tensor powers of the state module; the sector split provably does not survive it; and the three canonical forms of the one-qubit space tensor to the metric, the norm and the entanglement measures. Those results are collected in The multi-qubit extension below, and they change what the single-qubit limit means: it is a limit of the algebra, not of the theory.

The central affirmation. One algebra, the biquaternions, already contains the formal structures of both special relativity and quantum physics. Relativity lives in one of its two complementary four-dimensional real subspaces, called here the material sector $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$: the four-vectors, the invariant interval, the Lorentz group. Quantum physics lives in the other, the informational sector $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$: states, observables, probabilities, measurement. Multiplication by the imaginary unit exchanges the two. The aim is therefore not to quantise relativity or to relativise quantum mechanics. It is to develop quantum physics natively, in a setting where the relativistic structure is already present. The two theories are not two languages to be reconciled but two interpretations of one algebraic object. The informational sector is then not a passive home for those objects. It is the sector the group also moves: the boost rotors $\tilde U_{\mathrm b}=\cosh\tfrac{\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}}{2}\,e_t+i\sinh\tfrac{\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}}{2}\,\vec u_{\mathrm b}$ are themselves elements of $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$, of unit biquaternion norm, and they act on a state by the congruence $\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}\mapsto\tilde U_{\mathrm b}\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}\tilde U_{\mathrm b}^{*}$. A change of frame is therefore an operation on the quantum state and not a relabelling of it, and the operation is a computation in the same sector.

The Algebra and the Two Sectors

The algebra

The setting is $\mathbb{B} = \mathbb{C}\otimes_{\mathbb{R}}\mathbb{H}$, the complexification of the quaternions. An element is

$$ \tilde{Q} = Q_te_t + Q_x\vec e_x + Q_y\vec e_y + Q_z\vec e_z, $$

with complex coefficients, $e_t = 1$, $\vec e_x^2 = \vec e_y^2 = \vec e_z^2 = -e_t$, $\vec e_x \vec e_y = \vec e_z$ and its cyclic permutations, and $\vec e_{\imath} \vec e_{\jmath} = -\vec e_{\jmath} \vec e_{\imath}$ for ${\imath} \neq \jmath$. Two facts carry the whole framework.

  • The scalar imaginary $i$ is central. It satisfies $i^2 = -1$ and commutes with every element; it is not one of the units $\vec e_{\imath}$. There is one algebra here, and its two roles — the imaginary unit of the coefficients and the quaternion units of the directions — cannot be exchanged.
  • As a complex algebra, $\mathbb{B}$ is the $2\times 2$ complex matrices. One isomorphism is fixed once and for all, the corpus's matrix element representation $\mathsf{M}_2$: $\mathsf{M}_2(e_t)=I_2$ and $\mathsf{M}_2(\vec e_x)=\begin{pmatrix}0&-i\\-i&0\end{pmatrix}$, $\mathsf{M}_2(\vec e_y)=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, $\mathsf{M}_2(\vec e_z)=\begin{pmatrix}-i&0\\0&i\end{pmatrix}$ — that is, $-i$ times the Pauli matrices — extended over $\mathbb{C}$ by $\mathsf{M}_2(i\tilde{Q})=i\,\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde{Q})$. Consequently $\mathbb{B}$ is associative, complex, and not a division algebra: it has zero divisors. $\mathsf{M}_2$ carries the algebra's structures across exactly: $\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2)=\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q_1)\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q_2)$, $\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q^{*})=\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q)^{\dagger}$, $\det\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q)=\langle\tilde Q\rangle_{\natural}$ and $\operatorname{tr}\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q)=2Q_t$. Two useful corollaries: every identity below is an identity about matrices, and this is not quaternionic quantum mechanics — no one-sided or non-commutative-scalar modification enters, so no quaternionic deviation from ordinary quantum mechanics is to be expected.

Each coefficient splits once and for all as $Q_{\imath} = q_{\imath} + iq'_{\imath}$ with $q_{\imath}, q'_{\imath}$ real. The prime marks the slot that carries the $i$: it is a bookkeeping rule and not a physical label. Which of the four parameters is a time is decided by the sector, not by the prime.

The two sectors

Take the Hermitian conjugation, the coefficient-wise complex conjugate with the three vector coefficients negated:

$$ \tilde{Q}^{*} = \bar{Q}_te_t - \bar{Q}_x\vec e_x - \bar{Q}_y\vec e_y - \bar{Q}_z\vec e_z . $$

The two sectors are its two fixed spaces:

$$ \mathbb{B}_{\Psi} = \{\tilde{Q} : \tilde{Q}^{*} = \tilde{Q}\} = \{h_t e_t + i(h'_x\vec e_x+h'_y\vec e_y+h'_z\vec e_z)\}, \qquad \mathbb{B}_{\Phi} = \{\tilde{Q} : \tilde{Q}^{*} = -\tilde{Q}\} = \{i r'_t e_t + r_x\vec e_x + r_y\vec e_y + r_z\vec e_z\}. $$

So $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ is imaginary scalar, real vector and $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ is real scalar, imaginary vector, and on the physical coordinates the same two spaces read

$$ \mathbb{B}_{\Phi} = \{i r'_t\,e_t + r_x\,\vec e_x + r_y\,\vec e_y + r_z\,\vec e_z\}, \qquad \mathbb{B}_{\Psi} = \{h_t\,e_t + i(h'_{x}\vec e_x + h'_{y}\vec e_y + h'_{z}\vec e_z)\}, $$

with $c$ the speed of light and $r'_t,r_x,r_y,r_z$ and $h_t,h'_{x},h'_{y},h'_{z}$ all real. The material sector is the Minkowski four-vector; the informational sector is its counterpart with the time real and the three space directions imaginary. The two coordinate quadruples are independent, which is why the algebra carries the times $i r'_t$ and $h_t$ and the spaces $r_x\,\vec e_x+r_y\,\vec e_y+r_z\,\vec e_z$ and $i(h'_{x}\,\vec e_x+h'_{y}\,\vec e_y+h'_{z}\,\vec e_z)$ without identifying them.

Each sector is four-real-dimensional, they meet only at $0$, together they span the algebra, and multiplication by $i$ exchanges them:

$$ i\mathbb{B}_{\Phi} = \mathbb{B}_{\Psi}, \qquad i\mathbb{B}_{\Psi} = \mathbb{B}_{\Phi} . $$

The names are the framework's: $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ material, $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ informational. Note the labelling, because it is the opposite of what a reader expects: the anti-Hermitian subspace is the material one. The next two sections give the reason.

Material element and observable. An element of $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ is a material element; it is what is observed, a four-vector or a field. Its observable is the Hermitian representative $iA\in\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$, whose eigenvalues are the real readings. The two are one quantity in two writings, and the central $i$ is the exchange between them.

Two forms, two jobs

The convention lists four forms. Two of them carry the physics, and they answer two different questions. The Hermitian form answers a probability question, and it is the only one of the four that is positive definite on the whole algebra. The quaternionic form answers a metric question on $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$. They are stated below in separate subsections. Neither borrows the other's normalisation, and neither uses a matrix trace. The plain form is not a third job: it is the restriction of the Hermitian form to $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$, and it is the reading in which the sector computations below are written. The Krein form is the partner of the quaternionic form and is not needed here.

The Hermitian form: the probability weight

The form. The Hermitian form applies the identity to the first slot and the Hermitian conjugation to the second,

$$ \langle\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2\rangle_{*}=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2^{*})=\sum_{\imath} Q_{1\imath}\bar Q_{2\imath}=Q_{1t}\bar Q_{2t}+Q_{1x}\bar Q_{2x}+Q_{1y}\bar Q_{2y}+Q_{1z}\bar Q_{2z}. $$

It is positive definite on the whole algebra, $\langle\tilde Q,\tilde Q\rangle_{*}=\sum_{\imath}\lvert Q_{\imath}\rvert^{2}$. The star is the Hermitian conjugation and is not decoration: without it the form is indefinite, as the plain form is on $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$.

The scalar part. $\mathrm{Sc}$ is the coefficient of $e_t$. It is defined on $\mathbb{B}$ before any matrix is chosen, and it turns the product into a number. No representation enters.

The restriction to $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$. An element of the sector is $\tilde H_\Psi=h_t\,e_t+i\vec h_\Psi$ with $h_t,\vec h_\Psi$ real, so $\tilde H_\Psi^{*}=\tilde H_\Psi$. The Hermitian form therefore equals the plain form there,

$$ \langle\tilde{H}_{\Psi,1},\tilde{H}_{\Psi,2}\rangle_{*}=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde{H}_{\Psi,1}\tilde{H}_{\Psi,2})=h_{t,1}h_{t,2}+\vec h_{\Psi,1}\cdot\vec h_{\Psi,2} , $$

the three minus signs of the bilinear expansion being cancelled by $i^2=-1$ from the imaginary coefficients. The plain form is the restriction, not a second pairing.

The two sign patterns. The Hermitian form has inertia $(4,0)$ on the whole algebra. The plain form has inertia $(4,0)$ on $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ and $(0,4)$ on $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$; it is definite on each sector and indefinite on the algebra.

Which of the four norms. The convention names four algebraic norms: the plain norm $\langle\tilde Q\rangle=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q^2)$, the quaternionic norm $\langle\tilde Q\rangle_\natural=\tilde Q\tilde Q^\natural$, called the biquaternion norm elsewhere in the corpus, the Hermitian norm $\langle\tilde Q\rangle_*=\sum_{\imath}\lvert Q_{\imath}\rvert^2$, and the Krein norm $\langle\tilde Q\rangle_{\natural*}=\lvert Q_t\rvert^2-\lvert Q_x\rvert^2-\lvert Q_y\rvert^2-\lvert Q_z\rvert^2$. The weight above is none of them: it is the normalised Hermitian form, a pairing, not a quadratic form. On $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ the plain and Hermitian norms coincide at $h_t^2+\lvert\vec h_\Psi\rvert^2$, and the quaternionic and Krein norms coincide at $h_t^2-\lvert\vec h_\Psi\rvert^2$; only the first pair is positive definite. The normalisation $\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi})=\tfrac12$ is linear and is not a norm condition.

The normalisation. The idempotents along a unit vector $\vec u$ are $\tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id},\pm}=\tfrac12(e_t\pm i\vec u)$. They satisfy $\tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id},++}\tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id},-}=e_t$ and $\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id},\pm})=\tfrac12$. A state is a convex combination of the two idempotents along its axis, so

$$ \mathrm{Sc}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi})=\tfrac12,\qquad\text{that is}\qquad \langle\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi},e_t\rangle_{*}=\tfrac12. $$

The weight. With $\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}=\tfrac12(e_t+i\vec s_{\Psi})$ and $\tilde H_\Psi=h_t\,e_t+i\vec h_\Psi$,

$$ p=\frac{\langle\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi},\tilde H_\Psi\rangle_{*}}{\langle\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi},e_t\rangle_{*}} =\frac{\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}\tilde H_\Psi^{*})}{\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi})} =h_t+\vec s_{\Psi}\cdot\vec h_\Psi. $$

The star is the Hermitian conjugation; it is inert here because $\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}$ and $\tilde H_\Psi$ are Hermitian, and it is kept because $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_{*}$ is the form that is positive definite on the whole algebra. For $\tilde H_\Psi=\tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id}}(\vec u)$ this is $\tfrac12(1+\vec s_{\Psi}\cdot\vec u)$, which lies in $[0,1]$ when $|\vec s_{\Psi}|\le1$, and the weights of the two outcomes $\tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id}}$ and $e_t-\tilde\Pi_{\mathrm{id}}$ sum to one. This is the Born rule on the sector, and it is complete before any matrix is chosen.

The quaternionic form: the interval

The form. The quaternionic form applies the quaternion conjugation to the first slot,

$$ \langle\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2\rangle_\natural=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1^\natural\tilde Q_2)=Q_{1t}Q_{2t}+Q_{1x}Q_{2x}+Q_{1y}Q_{2y}+Q_{1z}Q_{2z}. $$

The norm. Its diagonal is the biquaternion norm,

$$ \langle\tilde Q\rangle_\natural=\langle\tilde Q,\tilde Q\rangle_\natural=\tilde Q\tilde Q^\natural=Q_t^2+Q_x^2+Q_y^2+Q_z^2. $$

The interval. On $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$, with $\tilde R_\Phi=i r'_te_t+r_x\vec e_x+r_y\vec e_y+r_z\vec e_z$,

$$ \langle\tilde R_\Phi\rangle_\natural=-{r'_{t}}^2+r_x^2+r_y^2+r_z^2, $$

of inertia $(3,1)$.

What the norm does. It multiplies: $\langle\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2\rangle_\natural=\langle\tilde Q_1\rangle_\natural\langle\tilde Q_2\rangle_\natural$. Its zero set is the light cone, and its nonzero null elements are the zero divisors. The elements of norm one are $SL(2,\mathbb{C})$, the double cover of the Lorentz group. In the $2\times2$ image it reads as a determinant, $\det\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q)=\langle\tilde Q\rangle_\natural$.

What it does not do. It is indefinite, so it is not a probability. It vanishes on the pure states, so it cannot normalise a state. It has no unit to normalise against and no weight.

The matrix trace: a reading in the $2\times2$ image

The trace belongs to the matrix, not to the algebra. It is not part of either construction. It appears only when the algebra is written as $2\times2$ matrices, and it is declared here, after the physics, to restate results already obtained.

The declaration. Fix $\mathsf{M}_2:\mathbb{B}\to M_2(\mathbb{C})$ by $\mathsf{M}_2(e_t)=I_2$ and $\mathsf{M}_2(\vec e_{\imath})=-i\sigma_{\imath}$. The matrix trace and the determinant of the image carry the two numbers of the element:

$$ \operatorname{tr}\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q)=2\,\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q)=2Q_t,\qquad \operatorname{tr}\mathsf{M}_2(e_t)=2,\qquad \operatorname{tr}\bigl(\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2^{*})\bigr)=2\langle\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2\rangle_{*}. $$

The factor $2$ is the dimension of the image. It is not a property of the form, and it is why the corpus states every identity on the algebra with $2\,\mathrm{Sc}$ and never with the matrix symbol: the factor is carried explicitly, so it cannot be lost.

The state. $2\,\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi})=1$. Its matrix reading is $\operatorname{tr}\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi})=1$, the trace of a normalised density matrix. The algebraic condition is the same statement and does not need the matrix.

The star is not optional in the pairing. The form $\langle\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2\rangle_{*}=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2^{*})$ is the Hermitian conjugation in the second slot, and that star is what makes the pairing positive definite on the whole algebra. On $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$, where both arguments are Hermitian, $\tilde Q_2^{*}=\tilde Q_2$ and the star acts as the identity, but it is written in every probability formula: it names the form that is positive definite on the whole algebra, and the plain form is the restriction to the sector.

The pair. For any two elements,

$$ 2\,\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2^{*})=2\langle\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2\rangle_{*} . $$

This is the Hermitian form, not a fifth form. It is used only at normalisation; the Born weight itself is stated in the quantum section as the normalised Hermitian form.

What the matrix trace does not supply. It is not defined on $\mathbb{B}$ before a representation is chosen; $\mathrm{Sc}$ is. Its factor is the dimension of the representation, while $\mathrm{Sc}$ and the Hermitian form do not depend on it. The matrix trace of a positive product is the Hilbert–Schmidt inner product, and on the algebra it reads $2\,\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}\tilde H_\Psi^{*})=2\,\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}^{1/2}\tilde H_\Psi\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}^{1/2})\ge0$ for $\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi},\tilde H_\Psi\ge0$; that non-negativity is the reason the star belongs in the probability reading.

One operation, three uses

A single formula acts on the algebra,

$$ \Gamma_{\tilde{Q}_1}(\tilde{Q}_2) = \tilde{Q}_1\tilde{Q}_2\tilde{Q}_1^{*}, $$

and it preserves each sector and preserves the biquaternion norm whenever $\langle\tilde{Q}_1\rangle_{\natural} = 1$. The same formula is the Lorentz transformation, the evolution of a quantum state and the update of a measurement, the three being distinguished only by which normalisation the acting element is given:

Condition on $\tilde{Q}_1$ Operation Group
$\tilde{Q}_1\tilde{Q}_1^{\natural} = e_t$ (unit norm) Lorentz transformation of $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ $SL(2,\mathbb{C})$, the double cover of the proper Lorentz group
$\tilde{Q}_1\tilde{Q}_1^{*} = e_t$ ($*$-unitary) evolution of a state $\mathrm{U}(2)$
$\tilde{Q}_1^2 = \tilde{Q}_1 = \tilde{Q}_1^{*}$ (idempotent) measurement update no group: the map is not invertible

So the reversible and the irreversible are one algebraic operation under two normalisations, and measurement differs from evolution by a property of the acting element — it is not invertible — rather than by a separate axiom. That is the framework's answer to the question of why measurement should be different in kind, and the answer is a theorem about the algebra.

One identity, four conservation laws

The identity. Conjugation by a $*$-unitary element fixes two numbers of everything it acts on: the doubled scalar part $2\,\mathrm{Sc}$ and the biquaternion norm $\langle\cdot\rangle_\natural$. A similarity fixes the spectrum, and these two are the sum and the product of the eigenvalues, $2\,\mathrm{Sc}\,\tilde Q$ and $\det\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q)$. That is one identity. The corpus reads it twice, once per sector, and the two readings are four conservation laws.

Sector $2\,\mathrm{Sc}$ $\langle\cdot\rangle_\natural$
$\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ energy, $E=-ic\,\mathrm{Sc}\,\tilde P_{\Phi}$ for $\tilde P_{\Phi}=i(E/c)e_t+\vec p$ mass, $\langle\tilde P_{\Phi}\rangle_\natural=-E^{2}/c^{2}+p^{2}=-m^{2}c^{2}$
$\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ normalisation, $2\,\mathrm{Sc}\,\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}=1$ information, $\langle\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}\rangle_\natural=\tfrac14(1-\lvert\vec s_{\Psi}\rvert^{2})$

Conservation is not invariance. This is the sharpest consequence, and it separates the two numbers. The $*$-unitary flow fixes both. A boost does not: it is Hermitian and of unit norm, not $*$-unitary, so it fixes the norm and moves the doubled scalar part, $E=mc^{2}\cosh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}$. Energy is therefore conserved and frame-dependent, and mass is conserved and frame-independent. On the informational side the mirror is exact: the reversible group fixes both numbers, so the norm — the entropy, which is a function of that norm alone — moves only under a loss of information and never under an evolution.

What the identity does not supply. Neither number identifies an object. An electron and a proton at $1.602\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm{J}$ share $2\,\mathrm{Sc}\,\tilde P_{\Phi}$, and a pure and a maximally mixed state share $2\,\mathrm{Sc}\,\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}$. The norm is the identifying number on both sides, which is why one algebraic quantity carries the interval on $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ and the entropy on $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$. Mass is not additive either: the norm of a sum carries the cross term $2\langle\tilde P_{\Phi,1},\tilde P_{\Phi,2}\rangle_\natural$, so the mass of two bodies is not the sum of their masses.

The slogan. "Energy equals mass plus information" is exact as a duality of readings, and false as an additive decomposition. The exact additive split is rest energy plus kinetic energy, $E=mc^{2}+T$. The duality is the thesis: on the material side the norm is fixed and the doubled scalar part free, on the informational side the doubled scalar part is fixed and the norm free.

The interlock

One more structural fact is used repeatedly. For two elements of the same sector,

$$ [\tilde{Q}_1,\tilde{Q}_2] \in \mathbb{B}_{\Phi}, \qquad \{\tilde{Q}_1,\tilde{Q}_2\} \in \mathbb{B}_{\Psi} . $$

The commutator always lands in the material sector and the anticommutator in the informational one. On $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ the commutator is explicit and minimal,

$$ [\tilde R_{\Phi,1},\tilde R_{\Phi,2}] = 2\,\vec r_{\Phi,1}\times\vec r_{\Phi,2} \qquad (\tilde R_{\Phi,1} = i c r'_{1t}e_t+\vec r_{\Phi,1},\ \tilde R_{\Phi,2} = i c r'_{2t}e_t+\vec r_{\Phi,2}), $$

twice the spatial cross product, with the time direction absent: the time axis is central for the bracket. Hence $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ is closed under the commutator and is a Lie algebra, $\mathfrak{u}(2) = \mathbb{R}ie_t\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)$, of dimension four; $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ is closed under the anticommutator and is a Jordan algebra, not a Lie algebra. Neither sector is closed under the product, so both $SU(2)$ and the state module live in the full algebra.

Why the Material Sector Carries Relativity

Five reasons, in increasing order of strength.

  1. It is a four-dimensional real space with a real form of the right inertia. On $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$, with $\tilde R_\Phi = i r'_t\,e_t+r_x\,\vec e_x+r_y\,\vec e_y+r_z\,\vec e_z$, the biquaternion norm reads $$ \boxed{\;\langle\tilde R_\Phi\rangle_{\natural} = -{r'_{t}}^2+r_x^2+r_y^2+r_z^2\;}, $$ a real form of inertia $(3,1)$. Position, velocity, momentum, energy–momentum, four-current and four-potential are all elements of this one subspace, so the kinematical content of relativity — that these are one kind of object — is a statement about the algebra.

  2. The Lorentzian sign pattern is derived, not postulated. The biquaternion norm is $Q_t^2+Q_x^2+Q_y^2+Q_z^2$, in which all four directions enter on an equal footing: there is no minus sign in the form itself and none is needed. A minus appears only when a real coordinate is put on a direction whose coefficient carries an $i$, which is exactly the $i r'_t$ convention, and then it comes from $i^2 = -1$ alone. Nothing about the metric has to be assumed at the start, not even the choice of which direction is timelike. A direction is timelike because its coefficient is imaginary. That is a labelling of the coordinate, not a commitment of the form.

  3. The invariance group comes for free, and it is the Lorentz group. A form that multiplies always brings its own isometry group: the elements of unit norm. Those are the biquaternions with $\langle\tilde{U}\rangle_{\natural} = 1$, they form $SL(2,\mathbb{C})$, and they act on $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ by $\Gamma$ above. That action is the double cover of the proper orthochronous Lorentz group. Lorentz invariance is therefore not an axiom added to the algebra; it is the invariance group of the algebra's own norm. This is the strongest single reason to call $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ the relativistic sector, because it is the one place where the framework produces a physical symmetry group rather than naming one.

  4. The causal structure is an algebraic locus. $\langle\tilde{Q}\rangle_{\natural} = 0$ is the light cone. Its nonzero elements are precisely the elements with no inverse — $3ie_t+\vec e_x+2\vec e_y+2\vec e_z$ has $\langle\tilde{Q}\rangle_{\natural}=-9+1+4+4=0$, so it is a zero divisor. So the light cone, massless propagation and the failure of division are one fact seen three ways.

  5. Rotations are the bracket. $[\tilde R_{\Phi,1},\tilde R_{\Phi,2}] = 2\,\vec r_{\Phi,1}\times\vec r_{\Phi,2}$, so angular momentum and the rotation generators are the commutator of the sector, and the Lie algebra is $\mathfrak{u}(2)$.

What the material sector does not supply. No scale, no preferred time direction, no Hamiltonian and no equation of motion. The kinematics of relativity is in the algebra; its dynamics comes from outside.

Why the Informational Sector Carries Quantum Physics

Seven reasons. The first is decisive.

  1. Only Hermitian elements can be observables or states, and the Hermitian elements are exactly $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$. Under the fixed isomorphism, $\mathsf{M}_2(\mathbb{B}_{\Psi})$ is the space of Hermitian $2\times2$ matrices and $\mathsf{M}_2(\mathbb{B}_{\Phi})$ is the space of anti-Hermitian ones. Hermitian elements have real eigenvalues, a spectral decomposition in terms of projections, and a positive cone; anti-Hermitian elements have purely imaginary eigenvalues and no positive cone. The reading of a physical quantity must be a real number, and a state must be positive, so the observable — the Hermitian representative $iA$ of the material element $A\in\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ that is the object observed, a four-vector or a field — and the state must both be elements of $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$; the eigenvalues of $iA$ are the readings. Neither a real spectrum nor a positive cone survives on the material element itself. This single fact fixes the assignment of the sectors. It also explains the unfamiliar labelling. The material sector is anti-Hermitian because it carries the four-vectors, whose business is to multiply and so to give isometries. The informational sector is Hermitian because it carries the observables — the representatives $iA$ — and the states, whose business is to have real values.

  2. The sector is the observable algebra of a two-state system, exactly. $\mathsf{M}_2(\mathbb{B}_{\Psi})$ is the Hermitian $2\times2$ matrices; an element is $$ \boxed{\;\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde H_\Psi) = h_t\,I_2 + \vec h_\Psi\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma}, \qquad \tilde H_\Psi = h_t\,e_t + i(h'_x\vec e_x+h'_y\vec e_y+h'_z\vec e_z)\;}, $$ with real $h_t,\vec h_\Psi$, and its spectrum is $h_t+\lvert\vec h_\Psi\rvert$ and $h_t-\lvert\vec h_\Psi\rvert$. This is not an analogy with quantum mechanics: it is the operator algebra of one qubit.

  3. The probability weight is the normalised Hermitian form, and on $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ it is the Born rule. The form is $\langle\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2\rangle_{*}=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2^{*})$; on $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ it coincides with the plain form $\langle\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2\rangle=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2)$, which is its restriction, but the weight below is written with the star on the sector and on the algebra alike; the scalar part is the coefficient of $e_t$ and needs no matrix. On $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ the form is real and positive definite, of inertia $(4,0)$, and it does not multiply — which is exactly what a probability needs. The unit satisfies $\langle e_t\rangle_{*}=1$; a state is normalised against it by $\langle\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi},e_t\rangle_{*}=\tfrac12$, that is $\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi})=\tfrac12$; and the weight of an outcome with effect $\tilde H_\Psi$ is $$ \boxed{\;p=\frac{\langle\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi},\tilde H_\Psi\rangle_{*}}{\langle\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi},e_t\rangle_{*}} =\frac{\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}\tilde H_\Psi^{*})}{\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi})} =h_t+\vec s_{\Psi}\cdot\vec h_\Psi\;}. $$

  4. The state space is not added: it is the positive cone of the sector. Boost and rotation

One congruence, two families. Both families of the Lorentz group act on a state by the congruence

$$ \tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}\longmapsto\tilde U\,\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi}\,\tilde U^{*}, $$

and they differ by a property of the acting element. The boost factor is

$$ \tilde U_{\mathrm b}=\cosh\tfrac{\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}}{2}\,e_t+i\sinh\tfrac{\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}}{2}\,\vec u_{\mathrm b}, $$

with a real scalar part and an imaginary vector part: it is Hermitian, so it is an element of $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ itself, and its biquaternion norm is $\cosh^{2}-\sinh^{2}=1$, so it is a unit element. The rotation factor is

$$ \tilde u_{\mathrm r}=\cos\tfrac{\theta_{\mathrm{axr}}}{2}\,e_t+\sin\tfrac{\theta_{\mathrm{axr}}}{2}\,\vec u_{\mathrm r}, $$

a real quaternion: unit biquaternion norm again, but real, hence not Hermitian. The two properties then separate the families. A boost is Hermitian and not $*$-unitary, since $\tilde U_{\mathrm b}^{*}=\tilde U_{\mathrm b}$ and therefore $\tilde U_{\mathrm b}\tilde U_{\mathrm b}^{*}=\tilde U_{\mathrm b}^{2}=\cosh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}\,e_t+i\sinh\theta_{\mathrm{rpd}}\,\vec u_{\mathrm b}\neq e_t$. $$ \mathrm{U}(2)\cap\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{C})=\mathrm{SU}(2), $$

so the rotations are the intersection of the quantum group and the Lorentz group, and the boosts lie outside it.

$$ \bigl\langle\tilde U\tilde H\tilde U^{*}\bigr\rangle_{\natural}=\langle\tilde U\rangle_{\natural}\,\langle\tilde H\rangle_{\natural}\,\langle\tilde U^{*}\rangle_{\natural}=\langle\tilde H\rangle_{\natural}. $$

The zero set of that norm is the cone, and a family connected through the identity maps the future cone into itself; positivity and rank survive because the map is a congruence by an invertible element. That is the difference between the two families at the level of the state space: the rotations are channels and the boosts are not.

Interpretation of the Wick rotation The corpus reads the Wick rotation as a transfer between two four-dimensional subspaces of one algebra, from the material sector to the quaternion subspace. The properties of that subspace explain both why the technique works and why it fails: it is elliptic, positive definite, free of zero divisors and with a compact symmetry group, and it buys this by giving up the light cone and the causal order. The interpretation classifies the applications — harmless where the physics is about correlations and equilibrium, destructive where it is about causality and dynamics — and the split reproduces the observed practice: the equilibrium uses succeed, and real-time transport, the sign problem and quantum gravity resist. This is the corpus's most concrete new result, because it explains a known pattern of successes and failures in a standard technique. Proposed: the algebra does not prove it, and it makes no measurement prediction.

Sesqualgebras An $R$-module with a product that is $R$-linear in the first slot and $\varsigma$-semilinear in the second. Here $\varsigma$ is an involution of the base ring, and it is part of the datum. The Bochner theorem for the biquaternion transform Bochner's theorem consumes two properties of the character — multiplicativity under the group law, and unitarity — and one of the pairing — that the quadratic form carries the conjugation in its second slot. In $\mathbb{B}$ the kernel is an exponential of a root $\rho$ of $-1$, so two of the ingredients become choices, and the corpus answers the question that forces: the positivity the theorem needs is that of the Hermitian form $\langle\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2\rangle_{*}=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q_1\tilde Q_2^{*})$, the probability form of the framework. The character $\xi_\omega=\cos(2\pi\langle\omega,r_x\rangle)e_t-\sin(2\pi\langle\omega,r_x\rangle)\rho$ is multiplicative for every root, since the difference of frequencies always reassembles as a product; it is $*$-unitary for the Hermitian conjugation, $\xi_\omega^{*}=\xi_\omega^{-1}$, exactly when $\rho^{*}=-\rho$, and that holds exactly for the degenerate roots — the central imaginary $\rho=\pm i$ and the unit pure real quaternions. For those roots the quadratic form $Q=\sum_{\mathrm{modes}}A_{\mathrm{mode},m}G(\omega_{\mathrm{mode},m}-\omega_{\mathrm{mode},m'})A_{\mathrm{mode},m'}^{*}$ factorises as the integral of the positive element $ZZ^{*}$, so the transform of a positive measure is positive definite: the central root reproduces the classical theorem componentwise, $G=\xi_{\mathrm{scl}} e_t$, and the pure real roots reproduce the quaternion theorem, the two textbook positivity theorems now obtained from the same Hermitian form. For a non-degenerate root, $\rho=b\mu+d\jmath i$ with $\mu\perp\jmath$ unit real and $b^{2}-d^{2}=1$, unitarity fails and positivity with it, with the witness $\rho=2\vec e_x+\sqrt{3}\,i\vec e_z$. What the Framework Does Not Do

The project keeps its own list of proven obstructions. These are the ones that change what a reader can expect.

  • No scale. The algebra fixes no length, mass, coupling or dimensionless ratio. Every scale is an input.
  • No preferred time and no Hamiltonian. Time is one coordinate of an element; the algebra fixes no arrow for it, no Hamiltonian and no basis.
  • No single norm is both multiplicative and positive. The positive definite norm exists: the Euclidean norm $\lVert\tilde Q\rVert=\sqrt{\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q\tilde Q^{*})}$, the Frobenius norm under $\mathbb{B}\cong M_2(\mathbb{C})$, real and vanishing only at the origin. It is not multiplicative. The norm that multiplies, the biquaternion norm $\langle\tilde Q\rangle_{\natural}=\tilde Q\tilde Q^{\natural}$, is the interval and is indefinite, vanishing on nonzero elements. The metric and its isometry group use the multiplicative norm; the topology and the Hilbert space use the Euclidean one.
  • No canonical state module. It depends on a chosen idempotent: different choices give isomorphic but distinct submodules, so the split into amplitudes is canonical only up to $*$-unitary equivalence.
  • The biquaternion norm's invariance group exceeds the symmetry of states. The group preserving it is $\mathrm{U}(1)\cdot SL(2,\mathbb{C})$, strictly larger than the $*$-unitary group $\mathrm{U}(2)$ that acts on states: the framework has more symmetry than the physics uses.
  • No commutation relation in the algebra. The canonical commutator must be imported.
  • No complex structure in the sector. $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ is a real Jordan algebra, closed under the anticommutator and not under the product; the complex structure acts through the centre, and the phase is carried by the module.
  • Multiplication is blind to chirality. Every operation the algebra supplies is a multiplication, and a multiplication acts alike on the two chiral components of a Dirac module. Chirality, the discrete symmetries, and the split of positive from negative energy are external.
  • Three of the four brackets fail the Jacobi identity. The plain bracket closes and gives the Lie algebra, but the conjugation-carrying brackets, which the quantum pairings need, do not: they generate a higher structure instead.
  • The internal group stops at $\mathrm{U}(2)$. The algebra's own continuous symmetry is the central phase $\mathrm{U}(1)$ together with the quaternion triple $SU(2)$, that is $\mathrm{U}(2) = (SU(2)\times \mathrm{U}(1))/\mathbb{Z}_2$, the internal group of one qubit. $SU(3)$ cannot be generated: the sector is four-real-dimensional, and the algebra's modules have even complex dimension, so no three-dimensional colour multiplet is closed under it. The fermion generations and the strong-interaction octet lie outside.
  • No three-level system in the algebra. The index of nilpotency equals the number of levels, and Cayley–Hamilton forces $\tilde n^{2}=0$ for every nilpotent of $\mathbb{B}$: a three-level ladder, $J_{+}^{3}=0$ with $J_{+}^{2}\neq0$, has no element here. At most two orthogonal idempotents of $\mathbb{B}$ sum to $e_t$, so a three-outcome projective measurement does not exist; three-outcome POVMs do exist and none of them is sharp. The traceless part carries spin one as a module of $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ and not of the algebra, since $\mathrm{ad}_{xy}\neq\mathrm{ad}_x\mathrm{ad}_y$ — explicitly $\mathrm{ad}_{i\vec e_x}^{2}(i\vec e_y)=4i\vec e_y$ against $\mathrm{ad}_{(i\vec e_x)^{2}}(i\vec e_y)=0$ — and every derivation of $\mathbb{B}$ is inner, $\mathrm{Der}(\mathbb{B})\cong\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{C})$. The reason is the even module dimension: every unital finite-dimensional module is $V^{\oplus k}$, of complex dimension $2k$, so no unital three-dimensional module exists. The qutrit is reached only through the tensor square, where the element reading of a state is already lost.
  • The algebra is single-qubit, and composition adds no algebra. The algebra carries one qubit's worth of structure: no internal tensor factor and no selection rule for measurement. What composes is the state module, so the joint system is $S^{\otimes n}$ with joint algebra $M_{2^{n}}(\mathbb{C})$, and no $n\ge2$ returns a biquaternion algebra. As The multi-qubit extension records, composition costs the per-subsystem label and the element reading of a state, and keeps the states, the observables, the weights and the measures. There is no stable classical sector under a generic evolution — classicality must come from decoherence or a chosen context. Curvature cannot reside in the algebra and needs an inserted tetrad, the field equations are transcribed rather than derived, and gravity is an article.

The empirical position. The project's own assessment, and the one a reader should adopt until it revises itself, is negative: the framework is currently empirically equivalent to standard physics. It makes no prediction that distinguishes it. Empirical equivalence is not the absence of new content: what is new here is mathematics, which needs no experiment, and interpretations that reorganise or explain known results. What the framework lacks is a new prediction, and no experiment can confirm or refute an interpretation. Each candidate reading of the sign has been examined and found unavailable, and every reason is structural rather than accidental. Modified dispersion needs a scale the algebra does not have. Birefringence and a second light cone are foreclosed, because there is only one norm. A coupling of the material to the informational sector is unspecified — no field, no action, no constant — and this is named as the framework's largest gap. Deviations of the quaternionic kind cannot appear, since the algebra is complex and associative. Derived dimensionless relations, the one class that would need no new scale, are absent.

Common Misreadings

  1. The material sector is the anti-Hermitian one. The more familiar convention would take the Hermitian subspace as the physical one. Here it is the other way round, and the reason is stated above: the four-vectors must multiply, the readings must be real.
  2. The prime marks the $i$, not the sector. The primed parameter is the one attached to $ie_{\imath}$: a rule about the coefficients and not a label of a subspace. The crossing it produces is the point. In $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$ the $i$ sits on the scalar, so the material time is the primed scalar slot, $i r'_t = iq'_t$; in $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ it sits on the vector, so the informational positions are the primed slots, $i(h'_{x}\,\vec e_x+h'_{y}\,\vec e_y+h'_{z}\,\vec e_z)$. That one crossing is what makes the two sectors mirror images.
  3. The material time enters as an imaginary coefficient and the informational time as a real one. $i r'_t$ against $h_t$. The minus of the metric comes from $i^2 = -1$ in $i r'_t$ and from nowhere else.
  4. Two forms, two jobs, never conflated. The Hermitian form $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_{*}$ answers the probability question: it is real and positive definite on the whole algebra, and it does not multiply. On $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ it equals the plain form $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$, which is the reading used for the sector computations, while the Born weight keeps the star. The quaternionic form $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_{\natural}$ answers the interval question on $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$: it is indefinite, it multiplies, and its zero set is the light cone. In particular $\langle\tilde{Q}\rangle_{\natural} = 0$ does not mean $\tilde{Q} = 0$. A state is normalised by the linear condition $\langle\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi},e_t\rangle_{*}=\tfrac12$, equivalently $\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi})=\tfrac12$, never by the quadratic norm.
  5. The interval is the biquaternion norm, not the square. $\langle\tilde{Q}\rangle_{\natural} = \tilde{Q}\tilde{Q}^{\natural}$; the bare $\tilde{Q}^2$ is not a scalar and is not the interval.
  6. Neither sector is a subalgebra. Neither is closed under the product: a product of two elements of one sector generally has components in both. That is why $SU(2)$ and the state module live in the full algebra.
  7. The electromagnetic field strength is in neither sector. Unlike the four-potential it is not a four-vector: its scalar part vanishes, and its vector part is mixed real and imaginary, the imaginary half carrying the electric field and the real half the magnetic field. The four-potential, not the field, is a material element.
  8. The two sector-exchange operations are not the same map. The exchange of the two sectors is multiplication by $i$, of order four and invertible. The Wick rotation is a continuation of the time coordinate. The two agree on the time axis and differ away from it. And being invertible and of order four, the exchange carries no arrow of time.
  9. Agreement with standard physics is not confirmation. The project's own criterion, stated above: for a number the framework reproduces, ask which of its structures produced it. Where the structure is shared, the agreement tests the dictionary and not the framework.
  10. A form that multiplies and a form that does not are two different tools. A metric is the first; a probability is the second. Reading a probability off the multiplicative form, or a metric off the definite one, is the error that the framework exists to avoid.
  11. "The framework is only a rewriting of standard physics" is half right. Most of the corpus is indeed standard physics restated, and that part is evidence about the language and not about the world. The word to reject is only: the new results above are not rewritings.
  12. "Observable" has two senses, and only one is Hermitian. The word names the Hermitian representative $iA\in\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$, whose eigenvalues are the readings. The object observed is the material element $A\in\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$, a four-vector or a field. Calling the material element an observable — reading it in the classical sense of a quantity that simply has a value — is a change of dictionary, not of algebra: the two writings differ by the central $i$ and generate the same numbers.
  13. The matrix trace is a reading, not an intrinsic operation. $\mathrm{Sc}$ is defined on $\mathbb{B}$ without a representation: it is the coefficient of $e_t$. A trace is defined only on a matrix, and so only after the image is chosen. In $\mathsf{M}_2$ the identity $\operatorname{tr}\mathsf{M}_2(\tilde Q)=2\,\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde Q)$ holds because $\mathsf{M}_2(e_t)=I_2$, and the factor is the dimension of the image. Normalisation in the algebra is $\langle\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi},e_t\rangle_{*}=\tfrac12$, that is $\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde{Ϛ}_{\Psi})=\tfrac12$. On the image the same condition is the unit trace of a density matrix.
  14. A two-qubit state is not a biquaternion, and the pair is not a biquaternion algebra. The counts agree — a biquaternion carries eight real parameters and a two-qubit state four complex amplitudes — and the structures do not. The state of the pair is an element of $S\otimes S=\mathbb{C}^4$, which is not the regular module, and the joint algebra is $M_4(\mathbb{C})$, which is not $\mathbb{B}$: a biquaternion algebra is $M_2$, and $M_4$ has degree four. The element reading of a state stops at one system. What the pair keeps is not a larger algebra but a lost label and a gained measure, as The multi-qubit extension records.
  15. The spectrum of a rotor is not the rotor. A rotor has six parameters, and its spectrum carries two: the eigenvalues multiply to one, so the spectrum is a modulus and a phase. For a normal element — a boost, a rotation, or a product about commuting axes — the spectrum together with the two eigenprojectors is the element, four parameters, and the projectors supply the axis that the spectrum cannot. For a general element there is no eigenbasis at all, and the failure is the Thomas–Wigner rotation. A computation that uses only the spectrum computes a magnitude; a direction needs the projectors. Every case leaves two discrete choices, the order of the eigenvalues and the sheet, and they are fixed by requiring a non-negative rapidity and a positive element.

Summary

The framework's central affirmation is that the biquaternion algebra already contains both special relativity and quantum physics, as a matter of algebra. They live in two complementary real four-dimensional subspaces, joined by multiplication by the imaginary unit. Quantum physics can therefore be developed natively in a setting where the relativistic structure is already present. The strongest argument for the first half is that the biquaternion norm is multiplicative, and real of inertia $(3,1)$ on $\mathbb{B}_{\Phi}$. Its isometry group is the unit-norm biquaternions, $SL(2,\mathbb{C})$, acting by $\tilde{Q}\mapsto\tilde{U}\tilde{Q}\tilde{U}^{*}$. That group is the Lorentz group, and the light cone is the biquaternion norm's zero set. The strongest argument for the second half is that the Hermitian elements are exactly $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$. The observables — the Hermitian representatives of the material elements — and the states of a two-state system are therefore elements of that sector and of no other. Real spectra and a positive cone exist there. The pure states are the sector's idempotents, the mixed states its positive normalised cone, and the Born weight its normalised Hermitian form. The one form $\Gamma$ serves as Lorentz transformation, evolution and measurement under three normalisations. It also fixes two numbers of whatever it acts on — the doubled scalar part and the biquaternion norm — and that one fact is the four conservation laws, two per sector: energy and mass on the material side, normalisation and information on the informational one. A boost moves the first number and not the second, so energy is conserved and frame-dependent while mass is conserved and frame-independent.

The two halves are separated by the two ways a form can behave: a form that multiplies gives a group of isometries, and a form that does not gives a pairing and a probability. One algebra carries both.

Most of the corpus restates known physics in the biquaternion language, which confirms the dictionary and not the framework. What it adds beside that comes in two kinds. In mathematics: the sesqualgebras, the classification of the twelve structures of the biquaternion algebra, and the Bochner theorem for the biquaternion transform, which fixes the form it needs — the Hermitian form $\langle\tilde Q_1,\tilde Q_2\rangle_{*}$ — and the roots for which the transform of a positive measure is positive definite, namely the degenerate ones. All of these are proved from the definitions, so that they stand on their own and no experiment bears on them. In physics: the derivation of the form of the Born rule from the normalised Hermitian form, read through the scalar part, the action of the Lorentz group on the informational sector, where a rotation is a channel and a boost is not, so that the boost preserves the cone and the norm and moves the state off the slice, leaving purity and Bloch radius frame-dependent while the biquaternion norm is not, the thermal structure read from that same geometry, with the Gibbs state a diameter of the ball, the radius $\tanh\Theta$, the inverse temperature a rapidity that a boost translates, and the Gibbs path a transversal to the unitary orbits rather than one of them, so that no rotation of the time coordinate is needed, the interpretation of the Wick rotation as a transfer to the elliptic quaternion subspace, with its classification of applications, and the Matsubara thermal formalism obtained without the rotation, inside the material sector, where the positivity is carried by the Hermitian form and is the central-root case of the Bochner theorem, together with the cutoff of the renormalization group obtained the same way, on the Hermitian form of a material element rather than on a rotated norm. The physics results rest on the identification of the two sectors; they are new to the literature as statements about the physics, and they are proposed rather than proved from the algebra, except the Lorentz action on the sector, the thermal rewriting and the regulator, whose algebraic steps are computations on the forms. To the same body belongs the multi-qubit extension, and it is of the computed kind throughout: composition is by tensor powers of the state module, the sector split provably does not descend to the quotient so no per-subsystem label exists, the alternating form of one qubit tensors to the concurrence, $\omega\otimes\omega(Ξ_{\mathrm{wfn}},Ξ_{\mathrm{wfn}})=2\det c$, and its parity rule $(\omega^{\otimes n})^{\mathsf T}=(-1)^{n}\omega^{\otimes n}$ makes the third tensor power vanish identically on the diagonal, which is why the three-qubit measures are quartic and why GHZ and $W$ are separated by a degree-four hyperdeterminant. Beside them stands the comparison with the quaternionic quantum theory, where the algebraic core — the tensor obstruction, the forced centrality of the unit, and the trace obstruction to the commutation relation — is proved, and the reading it carries is that the biquaternion framework is that programme's corrected successor: the same algebra and geometry, with the quaternions moved from the scalar field to the elements. Neither kind is a prediction. In the particle sector the corpus carries a model and a ledger. The $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ model contains every observed elementary particle and its antiparticle and puts none of them in by hand — one group and one declaration, with the fifteen generators of the operator algebra's Hermitian part — and it labels what it adds — six leptoquarks, $W_R^{\pm}$, $Z_R$, the heavier scalars — as prediction rather than as observation, above a colour-breaking scale that is bounded only from below. The four-factor group appears at that scale, as a phase of the same group rather than a rival to it. The Excitation Ladder Theory of Flavour is a ledger: measured masses, lifetimes and ratios, exact arithmetic on measured numbers, and a reading of the spacings whose derivation is not claimed. Neither is a confirmed departure, because no experiment today separates either from standard physics.

The limits are as sharp as the results. The algebra contains no scale, no time direction, no Hamiltonian, no positive form that multiplies and no commutation relation — the commutator of two Hermitian elements has no scalar part, so no pair in $\mathbb{B}_{\Psi}$ can satisfy one. Its state module is not canonical, multiplication is blind to chirality, and the internal group reaches $\mathrm{U}(2)$ and not $SU(3)$ — which is why the four-colour group of the particle model is declared and acts on a formal copy of the carrier rather than being read off the algebra. There is no three-level system in the algebra either: the nilpotency index is two, so the three-level ladder has no element, and two orthogonal idempotents are the most that sum to the identity. The algebra is single-qubit, and the extension is the tensor power of its state module: there the split does not descend, so no per-subsystem label exists, while the alternating form of the one qubit tensors to the concurrence, $\omega\otimes\omega(Ξ_{\mathrm{wfn}},Ξ_{\mathrm{wfn}})=2\det c$, and to the parity rule that makes the three-qubit measures quartic. And the framework is currently empirically equivalent to standard physics, with no distinguishing prediction.

The Biquaternioid Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra (AQA) over $\mathbb{C}$

Introduction

The biquaternioid complex space carries eleven structures beside the ordinary product. This article is about the antisymmetric quaternionic algebra, written $\mathrm{AQA}$, whose product is the antisymmetrisation of the quaternionic product, $\tilde Q_1\star\tilde Q_2=\tfrac12(\tilde Q_1^{\natural}\tilde Q_2-\tilde Q_2^{\natural}\tilde Q_1)$. It is the antisymmetric sibling of the causality structure, and its job in the framework is obstruction: the bracket does not close, the mixed term is the boost, and the failure is the missing Lie structure of the internal symmetry.

The mathematics of the structure is owned by the group The Biquaternioid Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra (AQA) over $\mathbb{C}$ of the mathematics menu. This article proves nothing. It lists the results, each with the mathematics article that owns it, and it keeps the physical readings the framework gives the structure, every one of them labelled as a reading.

Conventions. The algebra is $\mathbb{B}=\mathbb{C}\otimes_{\mathbb{R}}\mathbb{H}$ with basis $e_t,\vec e_x,\vec e_y,\vec e_z$, $e_t=1$, $\vec e_k^{2}=-e_t$, $\vec e_x\vec e_y=\vec e_z$, and central imaginary $i$. An element is $\tilde Q=\sum_\mu Q_\mu e_\mu$ with $Q_\mu\in\mathbb{C}$. The centre is $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}=\mathrm{span}_{\mathbb{R}}\{e_t,ie_t\}$ and the vector subspace is $\mathrm{Vect}(\mathbb{B})=\{Q_t=0\}$; the conjugations are the natural one $\tilde Q^{\natural}$, the coefficientwise one $\bar{\tilde Q}$ and the star $\tilde Q^{*}=\bar{\tilde Q}^{\natural}$. All conventions are those of Conventions in the Biquaternion Universe.

Basic Properties

The product $\tilde Q_1\star\tilde Q_2=\tfrac12(\tilde Q_1^{\natural}\tilde Q_2-\tilde Q_2^{\natural}\tilde Q_1)$ is $\mathbb{R}$-bilinear, $\mathbb{C}$-bilinear and antisymmetric; it is a bracket, not an associative product. Its class among the twelve structures is that of an antisymmetric algebra. Owner: Introduction to the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra.
  • The absence of a unit. The structure has no unit: $e_t$ annihilates it. Owner: Introduction to the Antisymmetric Quaternionic Algebra.

Physical Interpretation

  • The Jacobi failure is the algebraic origin of the boost obstruction. The non-compact generators do not close on a Lie algebra, and the gap is the mixed term $Q_{1t}\vec Q_2-Q_{2t}\vec Q_1$: the failure of this band and the framework's superselection statement are two statements of one fact, the boosts being informational generators and the informational generators not forming an algebra.
  • The mixed term reads as the boost and the missing Lie structure as the non-compactness of the informational generators. The commutator of two boosts is a rotation, so the material bracket closes where the informational one does not, which is the framework's interpretation of the Cartan decomposition.
  • The invariant bilinear forms are the two surviving pairings, one compact and one Lorentzian, which is the same dichotomy the four forms carry and the same pair that a change of the local complex structure separates.
  • Read as generators, the operators fail. The commutator of two multiplications is the multiplication of the value plus the deviation, so the operators generate no Lie algebra and exponentiate to no group.
  • Read as symmetries, they fail. Every multiplication is singular, so the block carries no form-preserving operator, no isometry and no inner automorphism, and its symmetries lie outside itself.
  • Read as forms, they fail. The block has no non-degenerate invariant form, so the adjoint has to be imported and then leaves the family. In the three readings the failure is one, and it is the operator expression of the band's word, obstruction.
  • On the pure vectors the block is the cross product, the deviation vanishes and the operators close, and the self-adjoint sandwiches are exactly the pure, rotation-like parameters; on the mixed terms the deviation is nonzero and closure fails, and the boost-like parameters make the sandwich non-symmetric. The operator calculus therefore carries the compact rotations and fails on the boosts; the group the framework reaches is read off the associative one-sided action and not off these operators.
  • An obstruction constrains an interpretation without supplying one. The companion readings of the sibling structures stand: the deviation is the anomaly of the internal symmetry, and the framework's positive statements are carried by the structures that close.

Summary

The biquaternioid antisymmetric quaternionic algebra is the structure $\tilde Q_1\star\tilde Q_2=\tfrac12(\tilde Q_1^{\natural}\tilde Q_2-\tilde Q_2^{\natural}\tilde Q_1)$ over $\mathbb{C}$, the obstruction band of the framework: antisymmetric, without a unit, with the Jacobi identity failing, with no non-degenerate invariant form and with every multiplication singular. Its failure is the non-closure of the informational generators, its defect is the associator of the parent product, its mixed term is the boost, and its compact part is the cross-product algebra of the rotations. The mathematics is owned by the mathematics group of the same name; the readings listed above are the framework's, each labelled, and none of them is a proof.

# __The Complete Lagrangian: Forty-Seven Terms, Fifty-Three Vertices, Twenty Blocks and Nineteen Parameters__

## Introduction

**The claim.** The whole broken-phase Lagrangian is one expression, and every row of it carries a number in a table of forty-seven. Two further rows, the vector masses, sit outside it; six further structures, the gauge self-interactions, are vertices; and the numbers that multiply the rows are eighteen, or nineteen with the vacuum angle.

**"Complete" means complete at dimension four, in unitary gauge, under the four conventions of §*The Four Counting Rules*.** The tower above dimension four, and the covariant-gauge form with its ghosts, are outside this article; the first is named at the end and the second is the price of the gauge, not a different theory.

**The answer.**

$$
\boxed{\;47=4+8+17+18,\qquad 49=47+2,\qquad 53=47+6,\qquad 20=47-27,\qquad 19=18+1\;}
$$

**How to read it.** The equation below writes the whole theory once, with each row's number printed under the piece it counts. The table that follows is its index: every row with its constant.

## The Expression

**One equation, and it is the whole theory at dimension four.** Every term carries the number of its row, so the count can be made by counting on the equation itself.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L}={}&
\underbrace{-\tfrac14G^a_{\mu\nu}G^{a\mu\nu}}_{1}
\underbrace{{}-\tfrac14W^a_{\mu\nu}W^{a\mu\nu}}_{4}
\underbrace{{}-\tfrac14B_{\mu\nu}B^{\mu\nu}}_{2,\;3}\\[3pt]
&{}+\underbrace{\tfrac12\,\partial_\mu h\,\partial^\mu h}_{5}
\underbrace{{}-\tfrac12m_h^2h^2}_{6}
\underbrace{{}-\frac{m_h^2}{2v}h^3}_{11}
\underbrace{{}-\frac{m_h^2}{8v^2}h^4}_{12}\\[3pt]
&{}+\underbrace{\tfrac12M_W^2W^+_\mu W^{-\mu}}_{48}
\underbrace{{}+\tfrac12M_Z^2Z_\mu Z^\mu}_{49}\\[3pt]
&{}+\underbrace{\frac{2M_W^2}{v}h\,W^+_\mu W^{-\mu}}_{7}
\underbrace{{}+\frac{M_Z^2}{v}h\,Z_\mu Z^\mu}_{8}
\underbrace{{}+\frac{M_W^2}{v^2}h^2W^+_\mu W^{-\mu}}_{9}
\underbrace{{}+\frac{M_Z^2}{2v^2}h^2Z_\mu Z^\mu}_{10}\\[3pt]
&{}+\underbrace{\sum_{f}\bar\psi_f\,i\slashed{\partial}\psi_f}_{13\text{–}24}
\underbrace{{}-\sum_{f}m_f\bar\psi_f\psi_f}_{30\text{–}38}
\underbrace{{}-\sum_{f}\frac{m_f}{v}h\,\bar\psi_f\psi_f}_{39\text{–}47}\\[3pt]
&{}-\underbrace{g_s\,\bar q\slashed{G}T^aq}_{25}
\underbrace{{}-e\,\bar\psi\slashed{A}Q\psi}_{26}\\[3pt]
&{}-\underbrace{\frac{g}{\sqrt2}\,\bar u_L\slashed{W}^+V_{\mathrm{CKM}}d_L}_{27}
\underbrace{{}-\frac{g}{\sqrt2}\,\bar\nu_L\slashed{W}^+e_L}_{28}
+\mathrm{h.c.}\\[3pt]
&{}-\underbrace{\frac{g}{\cos\theta_W}\,\bar\psi\slashed{Z}\Bigl(T_3\frac{1-\gamma_5}{2}-Q\sin^2\theta_W\Bigr)\psi}_{29}.
\end{aligned}
$$

**The three sums are the only compression.** Rows $13$–$24$ are twelve separate kinetic terms, rows $30$–$38$ nine separate masses, rows $39$–$47$ nine separate couplings; each is written $\sum_f$ because the twelve, the nine and the nine differ only in $m_f$ and in the field. Nothing else in the expression stands for more than one row.

**The overall sign of the scalar block is a convention.** The expression above expands $+|D_\mu H|^2$; [The $hVV$ Pieces](the-hvv-pieces-two-mass-term-normalisations-two-couplings-and-the-photon-that-does-not-appear.html) expands $-(D_\mu H)^\dagger(D^\mu H)$ and therefore carries a minus in front of the same two coefficients. The numbers $2M_W^2/v$ and $M_Z^2/v$ are the same in both, and so are the physical couplings.

## The Four Counting Rules

**The rules that turn the table into the numbers.**

| Rule | Statement | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| $1+1+2$ | one row per curvature, three for the electroweak ones | $4$ |
| $1+1+2+2+2$ | the scalar's eight rows: kinetic, mass, two $hVV$, two $hhVV$, two self | $8$ |
| $12+5$ | twelve kinetics and five interactions | $17$ |
| $9+9$ | nine masses and nine $h\bar ff$ | $18$ |
| $+6$ | the two triple and four quartic gauge structures | $53$ vertices |
| $-27$ | the three flavour sums folded | $20$ blocks |

**Why the arithmetic closes.** $4+8+17+18=47$; $47+2=49$; $47+6=53$; $47-11-8-8=20$; and $18+1=19$.

## The Three Confirmations

**The parameter count.** Eighteen constants carry the forty-seven rows, and the nineteenth multiplies none of them. The count is the same in the symmetric phase, as [Nineteen and Nineteen](nineteen-and-nineteen-the-parameter-count-before-and-after-the-rotation.html) shows sector by sector.

**The vertex count.** Six structures are vertices and not rows. Counting them gives fifty-three, which is the number a diagrammatic calculation uses. Counting the eight monomials inside the four families gives fifty-seven, and [The Gauge Self-Interactions](the-gauge-self-interactions-two-triple-vertices-and-four-quartic-vertices.html) states the finer number.

**The block count.** Twenty blocks once the three sums over species are folded. The folding changes no physics and changes the count, which is why it is stated as a convention and not as a discovery.

## What the Display Is For

**For checking a claim about the framework.** A reader who wants to know what the corpus's algebra contributes can see that the display contains no element of $\mathbb{B}$: the list is the Standard Model's, and the algebra's contribution is exhausted at the $\mathrm{U}(2)$ ceiling, with the dictionary carrying the rest.

**For checking a claim about the text.** Every article of the category names a block and a number. A reader who finds a sentence that contradicts the table has found an error, and the table is the arbiter.

**For checking a claim about a measurement.** A measured coupling is one of the eighteen; a measured mass is one of the ten; a measured mixing parameter is one of the four. A number that appears in no cell of this table is not a parameter of the theory.

**Each row once.** The curvatures are nonlinear, so the triple and quartic gauge vertices sit inside line 1 and appear nowhere else; the fermion kinetic term carries $\partial_\mu$ and the interactions are lines 6 to 8, so no covariant derivative is expanded twice.

**What is not in it.** No gauge-fixing term and no ghost: the list is written in unitary gauge, which has neither. **The choice is available here and not above $M_C$**, because a broken phase has a field basis in which the Goldstones are eaten and an unbroken one has none — which is why [the PFEFT complete Lagrangian](the-complete-lagrangian-above-m-c-eleven-structures-seven-vertex-types-and-fifteen-constants.html) carries a fixing and its ghosts and this one does not. **Unitary gauge is the physical form and not the convenient one**: a loop calculation prefers the covariant gauge with ghosts, and that calculation is the same theory in another gauge. No $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}}$: it is a total derivative, present as a parameter and absent as a row. No right-handed neutrino, no Majorana mass, no higher-dimension operator: the omissions of [What the List Omits](what-the-list-omits-ghosts-the-theta-term-neutrino-mass-and-gravity.html). The same theory written with $D_\mu$ and the Higgs doublet is the symmetric-phase form, not another Lagrangian.

## The Sign Rule Between the Two Bookkeepings

The eight lines above are written in the corpus's coordinates, where $R_t=Q_t=iq'_t$. A display in a standard text uses the opposite convention on the time component, and one identity reconciles them.

$$
-\sum_{\imath} X_{\imath} Y_{\imath} = X\cdot Y ,
$$

where the right-hand side is contracted with the metric $(+,-,-,-)$ of the interval, $X_0$ is the physical temporal component, and $X_t=iX_0$.

**The proof.** The temporal term is $X_tY_t=(iX_0)(iY_0)=-X_0Y_0$ and the spatial ones are $\sum_{\imath\in\{x,y,z\}}X_{\imath}Y_{\imath}$, so $\sum_{\imath}X_{\imath}Y_{\imath}=-X_0Y_0+\sum_{\imath\in\{x,y,z\}}X_{\imath}Y_{\imath}$, and the leading minus makes the left-hand side $X_0Y_0-\sum_{\imath\in\{x,y,z\}}X_{\imath}Y_{\imath}$, which is exactly the contraction on the right.

**Where it bites, and where it does not.** The rule fixes the sign of a kinetic term and nothing else.

- **The scalar kinetic term.** A text writes $+\tfrac12\partial\xi\cdot\partial\xi$; the rule turns it into $-\tfrac12\sum_{\imath}\partial_{\imath}\xi\,\partial_{\imath}\xi$, whose expansion is the standard $\tfrac12\dot\xi^2-\tfrac12(\vec\nabla\xi)^2$.
- **The connection kinetic term.** A text writes $-\tfrac14F\cdot F$; the rule turns it into $-\tfrac14\sum_{\imath,\jmath}\varepsilon_{\imath}\varepsilon_{\jmath}F_{\imath\jmath}F_{\imath\jmath}$. Here the sign is unchanged, because the weight over the two indices cancels the flip.
- **The abelian check.** With $F_{tk}=iE_k$ and $F_{kl}=\epsilon_{kln}B_n$, $\sum_{\imath\jmath}F_{\imath\jmath}F_{\imath\jmath}=-2E^2+2B^2$, so $-\tfrac14$ of that is $\tfrac12(E^2-B^2)$ — the standard $-\tfrac14F\cdot F$ evaluated on the same fields.
- **The fermion bilinear.** The rule does not fix it, because a bilinear carries the spin structure. That sign belongs to [the Dirac article](the-dirac-equation-in-biquaternionic-form.html) and is not restated here.

The rule is what lets a display in this bookkeeping be read as a standard one without keeping a second Lagrangian beside it, and it is stated once, here, rather than in each sector. Its one structural consequence is separate from its arithmetic: **local invariance is a property of the sum and not of the pieces.** The fermion kinetic term is invariant only if its covariant derivative is built from the same three connections whose curvatures line 1 carries, and the Yukawa block is invariant only if the conjugate doublet carries the hypercharge the up-type singlet needs. Neither statement can be checked inside a single sector, which is why the audit is run on the eight lines and not on the pieces.

2. The full count. $47+2=49$.

9. The two outside. $48$ and $49$, the Proca masses, with no ancestor term.
10. The equation's lines. Eight lines carrying $4,8,2,17,18$ rows in the order of the table.
11. The complete Lagrangian. Eight lines, twenty-one underbraces, and every one of the forty-nine rows labelled exactly once on the equation.
12. The sign convention. The scalar block written with $+|D_\mu H|^2$ gives $+2M_W^2/v$ and $+M_Z^2/v$; with $-(D_\mu H)^\dagger(D^\mu H)$ both signs reverse and both numbers stand.

## Summary

The census, in one display: four curvatures, eight scalar rows, seventeen fermion rows and eighteen Yukawa rows make forty-seven; the two Proca masses make forty-nine rows; the six gauge structures make fifty-three vertices; the three folded sums make twenty blocks; and the eighteen constants with the vacuum angle make nineteen parameters.

The complete Lagrangian is the equation at the top: eight lines, forty-nine rows, each row's number printed under it. The numbered table below it is the index, not a second writing.

The numbered table is the arbiter of every claim in this category **about this list**. A row that is not in it is not a piece of it, and a number that is not in it is not a parameter of it. **The sentence stops there, and the stopping is deliberate:** a count that disagrees with the table is a count at a different rung or under a different convention, and the fifty-three, the fifty-seven and the twenty above are three such counts, each printed with the rule that produces it. **What the table settles is what this corpus means by "the number of terms"; it does not settle what another author means by it**, and no article in the category claims otherwise.

# __The Schrödinger Object: Every Article on One Equation__

## Introduction

The Schrödinger equation is the non-relativistic object: first order in time, second in space, and real in a weaker sense than the relativistic three. Its articles are where the corpus states what reality costs.

**The count.** 28 articles. The family contributes ten: four readings of the free action, four of the coupled one, and the two Schrödinger fluids. The rest are the equation's own articles, its transform, its path integrals and its quantisation. Read this set first if the conjugation is unclear, because time is first order here and the reality condition is easiest to see.

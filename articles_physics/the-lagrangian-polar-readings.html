The Lagrangian: The Polar Readings

The polar readings vary the eight parameters of the field's word — a scale, a phase, a boost and a rotation — instead of the element. They are genuinely different from the element readings, because the word is nonlinear in the element, and the map between the two readings is the family's central dictionary.

What the group does. It exhibits the action in the chart's variables, its two or three variations, and the canonical table that follows: which of the eight parameters are canonical and which are not. The tables are the group's most useful result, because they state the conjugate pairs in the chart rather than in the algebra.

What the group does not claim. It does not claim that the chart is simpler: the nonlinearity of the word moves into the kinetic term. It does not quantise, and its spin current is a classical bilinear.

Where the group's central statement lives. In the polar article of the spin-one field, where the fixed direction of the mass and the orbit's parameter are shown to be the same object seen twice.

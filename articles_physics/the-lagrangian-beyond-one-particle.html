The Lagrangian: Beyond the One-Particle Fields

This group is the family's frontier. It contains the articles that reach past the four one-particle actions — to the interacting scalar, the assembled gauge theories, the spin-$\tfrac32$ action that is named and not written, the linearised graviton, and the map of the step the corpus does not take.

The eight articles.

  • The Lagrangian and the Electromagnetic Field
  • The Lagrangian and Quantum Electrodynamics
  • The Lagrangian and the Interacting Scalar Field
  • The Lagrangian and the Electroweak Theory
  • The Lagrangian and the Pauli Equation
  • The Lagrangian and the Rarita–Schwinger Equation
  • The Lagrangian and Linearised Gravity
  • The Lagrangian and the Second Quantisation: From the Action to the Commutator

What the group does. It states the classical content of each case exactly, and it states the absence. Three of the eight exist because the absence is the content: the Rarita–Schwinger article records an action that is named and not written, the bridge maps the promotion the corpus does not perform, and the Pauli article's $g=2$ is a warning that a derived coefficient is not a free parameter.

What the group does not claim. It does not claim to be a quantum field theory, a Standard Model, or a quantised gravity. It claims the classical actions, the classical currents, the classical counts, and the honest ledger of what would be needed.

Read it in this order. The electromagnetic field, then QED, then the interacting scalar: those three cover the classical content. Then the bridge, which names the gap. Then the electroweak, graviton, Pauli and Rarita–Schwinger articles, which are the frontier's four different verdicts.

The Scalar Content: One Quartet That Breaks Both Times, One Doublet That Waits, and Eight Survivors

Introduction

The question. Two vacuums, nine eaten generators at $M_C$, three at $v$. Which scalar supplies the Goldstones, which waits for the second scale, and how many real components survive?

The answer.

$$ \boxed{\; \Phi\in(\mathbf4,\mathbf1,\mathbf2):\ 16\ \text{real},\ 9\ \text{eaten};\qquad H\in(\mathbf1,\mathbf2,\tfrac12):\ 4\ \text{real},\ 3\ \text{eaten};\qquad 20=12+8 . \;} $$

One field does the whole of $M_C$, and the second index is the reason. $\Phi$ is a colour quartet and a right doublet. Its vacuum is a line in the carrier times a line in the doublet, so it breaks $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ with the first index and $\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$ with the second, in one vacuum and at one scale. That is why nine generators are eaten at $M_C$ and not six: six from the colour breaking, three from the right factor.

What this article establishes. The multiplet list of the minimal content; the count of real components and of eaten ones, field by field; the reason $H$ is a separate field and not one of $\Phi$'s survivors; and the statement that no field carries both weak indices, which is what makes the charged block diagonal.

What it does not establish. A potential, a mass for the surviving scalars, or a Yukawa sector. It is a census, and every other article in the section cites it rather than recounting it.

Where the neighbours are. The breaking that eats the nine is The Breaking of the Primordial Force. The transition these fields make is The Matching at $M_C$. The neutral mixing of the two vacua is The Weak Sector Below $M_C$.

The Multiplet List

Two fields, and the list is complete.

field representation real components breaks at $M_C$ breaks at $v$
$\Phi$ $(\mathbf4,\mathbf1,\mathbf2)$ $16$ $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ and $\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$ no
$H$ $(\mathbf1,\mathbf2,\tfrac12)$ $4$ nothing $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{U}(1)_Y$

$\Phi$ carries the colour index and the right index; $H$ carries the left index and hypercharge. No field carries two weak indices at once — $\Phi$ is an $L$ singlet and $H$ is an $R$ singlet — and that single fact has three consequences: the charged block has no off-diagonal entry, the physical $W$ is $W_L$, and $\rho=1$ at tree level.

Why the second index of $\Phi$ must be the one that breaks $\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$. A nonzero vacuum in a doublet leaves no subgroup of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ unbroken. $\Phi$'s vacuum is nonzero in the doublet index by construction — it is what makes the quartet line — so $\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$ is broken by that vacuum and by no other field. A second right doublet would be redundant: it would break nothing that $\Phi$ has not already broken, and its vacuum would be antiparallel to $\Phi$'s in the neutral sector. That is why the list has two entries and not three.

The Breakdown of the Sixteen

How the sixteen split. Write $\Phi=v_C\,e_t\otimes d+\text{fluctuations}$, with $e_t$ the lepton line of the carrier and $d$ a fixed unit vector in the doublet. The carrier splits as $\mathbf3\oplus\mathbf1$ under $\mathrm{SU}(3)_c$ and the doublet as two real directions, so

$$ (\mathbf4,\mathbf1,\mathbf2)=(\mathbf3,\mathbf1,\text{two})\oplus(\mathbf1,\mathbf1,\text{two}), \qquad 16=12+4 . $$

total $16$ $9$

Nine, and the number is forced by the vector count. Twenty-one vectors exist at $M_C$ and twelve are massless there, so nine acquire mass there, so nine Goldstones are needed. The six of the colour breaking and the three of the right factor are the same count in two halves, and the corpus's earlier "six eaten at $M_C$" counted the first half only.

The seven that survive $\Phi$. One radial mode of the condensate, at a mass from the potential's quartic, and six real in colour-triplet scalars. They are heavy at $M_C$, because their mass is the condensate's own scale, and they are the objects any proton-decay discussion of the scalar sector has to bound rather than assume away.

The Doublet

Four real components, three eaten at $v$.

component real fate at $v$
the charged one $2$ eaten by $W^{\pm}$
the neutral one along the vacuum $1$ eaten by $Z$
the neutral one orthogonal to the vacuum $1$ the Higgs boson

Why $H$ cannot be one of $\Phi$'s survivors. After the transition the survivors of $\Phi$ are a colour singlet of $Y=0$ and a colour triplet. Neither is an $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L$ doublet: $\Phi$ is an $L$ singlet, and a singlet cannot supply the doublet that the electroweak breaking needs. The doublet is therefore a separate field, and the corpus's occasional phrase "the surviving component of the quartet is the Higgs" names a component that does not exist.

And $H$ must carry no colour. A colour-carrying doublet would break colour when it took a vacuum along its neutral component, and then the colour block of the mass matrix would not be zero. Colour is a stabiliser at every scale only if the electroweak doublet is colour-blind.

The Ledger, and What Each Article Cites It For

The two scales, side by side.

scale fields that act generators eaten vectors made massive
$M_C$ $\Phi$ $9$ six leptoquarks, $W_R^{\pm}$, $Z_R$
$v$ $H$ $3$ $W^{\pm}$, $Z$

The same ledger read as a count of real scalars.

$$ \underbrace{16}_{\Phi}+\underbrace{4}_{H}=20, \qquad \underbrace{9}_{M_C}+\underbrace{3}_{v}=12\ \text{eaten}, \qquad 20-12=8\ \text{surviving}. $$

The eight are one radial mode, six colour-triplet states, and the Higgs. No other scalar exists in the minimal content, and any count that reports more has added a field.

Three articles depend on this census and none of them repeats it. The charged block is diagonal because no field carries both weak indices (The Mass Matrix of the Fifteen Gauge Bosons). The neutral block has two independent vectors because there are two vacua and two fields (The Weak Sector Below $M_C$). The fifteen of the model fall to nine at $M_C$ because the sextet is eaten there (The Matching at $M_C$).

Distinctions That Are Easy to Lose

A breaking is not a deletion. The nine vectors eaten at $M_C$ are rotations of the condensate; the vectors that swallow them are what the rotations become (The Breaking of the Primordial Force).

A survivor is not a light field. The seven that survive $\Phi$ are heavy at $M_C$, because their mass is the condensate's scale; only the one survivor of $H$ is light, and that is the Higgs.

Two vacua are not three. A second right doublet would add a vector to the neutral sector, and the two right vectors are antiparallel, so it would add a field and change no count — which is why the minimal list has two entries.

Summary

Two fields. A colour quartet that is a right doublet, and a colour-blind left doublet. Sixteen real components and four.

One field breaks twice. $\Phi$'s carrier index breaks $\mathrm{SU}(4)$ and its doublet index breaks $\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$, in one vacuum at one scale, eating nine.

Nine at $M_C$, three at $v$. Six of the model's fifteen and three of the six that arrive are eaten at $M_C$, leaving twelve massless there; three of those twelve are eaten at $v$, leaving nine. The Goldstone count is $9+3=12$, read backwards from the vector ledger.

Eight survivors. One radial mode, six colour-triplet states heavy at $M_C$, and the Higgs. No other scalar exists in the minimal content.

And the reason for the diagonal charged block is in the list itself: no field carries both weak indices.

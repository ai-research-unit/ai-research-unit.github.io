# __The Biquaternioid Antisymmetric Plain Algebra (APA) over $\mathbb{C}$__

## Introduction

The biquaternioid complex space carries eleven structures beside the ordinary product. This article is about the **antisymmetric plain algebra**, written $\mathrm{APA}$, whose product is the antisymmetrisation of the plain product, $\tilde P\star\tilde Q=\tfrac12(\tilde P\tilde Q-\tilde Q\tilde P)$. It is one of the four **antisymmetric** structures, and its job in the framework is **rotation**: its values lie in the vector subspace, its Jacobi identity holds there, and it is the Lie algebra of the material sector.

The mathematics of the structure is owned by the group *The Biquaternioid Antisymmetric Plain Algebra (APA) over $\mathbb{C}$* of the mathematics menu. This article proves nothing. It lists the results, each with the mathematics article that owns it, and it keeps the physical readings the framework gives the structure, every one of them labelled as a reading.

**Conventions.** The algebra is $\mathbb{B}=\mathbb{C}\otimes_{\mathbb{R}}\mathbb{H}$ with basis $e_t,\vec e_x,\vec e_y,\vec e_z$, $e_t=1$, $\vec e_k^{2}=-e_t$, $\vec e_x\vec e_y=\vec e_z$, and central imaginary $i$. An element is $\tilde Q=\sum_\mu Q_\mu e_\mu$ with $Q_\mu\in\mathbb{C}$. The centre is $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}=\mathrm{span}_{\mathbb{R}}\{e_t,ie_t\}$ and the vector subspace is $\mathrm{Vect}(\mathbb{B})=\{Q_t=0\}$; the conjugations are the natural one $\tilde Q^{\natural}$, the coefficientwise one $\bar{\tilde Q}$ and the star $\tilde Q^{*}=\bar{\tilde Q}^{\natural}$. All conventions are those of *Conventions in the Biquaternion Universe*.

## The Results

### Basic Properties

- **The operation.** The product $\tilde P\star\tilde Q=\tfrac12(\tilde P\tilde Q-\tilde Q\tilde P)$ is $\mathbb{R}$-bilinear, $\mathbb{C}$-bilinear and **antisymmetric**, $\tilde P\star\tilde Q=-\tilde Q\star\tilde P$; it vanishes on the centre and its values lie in the vector subspace. Its class among the twelve structures is that of an antisymmetric algebra. Owner: *Introduction to the Antisymmetric Plain Algebra*.
- **The absence of a unit.** $e_t$ annihilates the structure, $e_t\star\tilde X=0$, so the structure has no unit and no identity element at all. Owner: *Introduction to the Antisymmetric Plain Algebra*.

- **Zero divisors.** The zero-divisor set of the product. Owner: *Zero Divisors in the Algebraic Structures over the Biquaternioid $\mathbb{C}$ Space*.

## Physical Interpretation

- **The bracket is the infinitesimal change of frame in its compact half.** The cross product of two material vectors generates a rotation, and the closure of the bracket is why the rotations form a group while the boosts do not. It is a *material* bracket, its values lying in the vector subspace, so it belongs to the compact half of the Lorentz algebra.
- **The composition law being the commutator is the closure of the rotation algebra.** The acting maps close on the bracket of their parameters, which is the operator form of the Jacobi identity and the reason a **Lie algebra, and not an associative algebra, is the structure of the band**.
- **Angular momentum and the Killing form are the two invariants of the material frame.** The signature of the Killing form is the compact/non-compact dichotomy read as the material/informational split at the level of generators: a rotation is a material generator and a boost an informational one, and the non-closure of the boosts is the algebraic origin of the Wigner rotation.
- **The adjoint negating the parameter is the generator-observable dictionary.** A skew-adjoint operator generates a form-preserving flow and its Hermitian multiple is what is measured, so angular momentum is $i$ times a generator: the band supplies generators and not observables.
- **The sandwich being self-adjoint for every parameter is the symmetry of the antisymmetric square.** The sector condition of the ordinary structure has collapsed because the two slots of an alternating product already differ by a sign, and the sandwich is the square of the finite rotation: the sandwich and the inner automorphism are one generator read at order two and at all orders.
- **The finite rotations are the multiplicative operators and the reflections are the form-preserving operators that the product cannot use.** Read on the centre, the one derivation that does not come from an element is the scaling of the central direction, which is the operator form of the central phase as the generator the adjoint representation cannot see.
- **Read on the clock, the bracket touches no central coordinate and is invisible to the exchange**, which acts on the scalar part; the invariants of the rotation group are the invariants of the spatial part of the clock, while the central phase is separate and belongs to the exchange.

## Summary

The biquaternioid antisymmetric plain algebra is the structure $\tilde P\star\tilde Q=\tfrac12(\tilde P\tilde Q-\tilde Q\tilde P)$ over $\mathbb{C}$, the rotation band of the framework: antisymmetric, valued in the vector subspace, without a unit, and with the Jacobi identity holding on the vector subspace, which makes it the Lie algebra of the material sector. Its bracket closes where the informational generators do not, its Killing form measures the compact/non-compact split, and its generators are the rotations whose Hermitian multiples are the angular momenta. The mathematics is owned by the mathematics group of the same name; the readings listed above are the framework's, each labelled, and none of them is a proof.

**What the group does.** It exhibits the coupled action, its three variations, the current that sources the connection, and the gauge orbit that the coupling creates. Each article states the covariant derivative once and then uses it everywhere, so the group can be read as one computation written four times in the element, polar, momentum and limit readings.

**What the group does not claim.** It does not quantise, and it does not renormalise the charge. The running of $q$ is the quantised theory's and the corpus has no running.

**Where the group's central statement lives.** In the coupled Klein–Gordon articles, where the vorticity of the fluid is the field strength and the current is the vorticity's source: one sentence that joins the matter and the field.

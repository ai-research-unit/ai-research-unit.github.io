The Effective Potential and the One-Loop Correction in Biquaternionic Form

Introduction

The object. The effective potential is the part of the quantum effective action that survives when the classical field is taken constant. It is the object that decides where the vacuum lies, and therefore whether a symmetry is broken. The classical potential written in the Lagrangian is its tree value; the one-loop correction is the logarithm of a functional determinant, and it can move the minimum or create one.

What this article is. The general machinery, with no sector attached. It states the constant-field reduction, the functional determinant, the coefficient of the logarithm, the condition for a radiatively generated minimum, and the renormalisation-scale dependence. The machinery is used twice in this category, on two different scalar sectors:

  • In the electroweak sector, where the scalar is the Higgs doublet and the potential decides whether the vacuum breaks $SU(2)_L\times\mathrm{U}(1)_Y$. The application is The Effective Potential and the Coleman–Weinberg Mechanism in Biquaternionic Form, which carries the sector's field content, its masses and its numbers.
  • Nowhere in the strong sector. Colour has no elementary scalar, and this machinery is not applied there.

Why the general form is worth separating. A one-loop correction is a statement about a determinant, not about a force. Written with a sector attached, it reads as if the sector's field content were part of the mechanism; written without one, the coefficient below is visibly a sum over whatever happens to circulate, and a reader can see which part of the answer is dynamics and which is bookkeeping.

The Constant-Field Reduction

The effective action $\Gamma[\xi_{\mathrm{pot}}]$ is the Legendre transform of the connected generating functional. When the argument is a constant classical field, every derivative term vanishes and

$$ \Gamma[\xi_{\mathrm{pot},\mathrm c}] = -\int d^4x\ \mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{eff}}(\xi_{\mathrm{pot},\mathrm c}), $$

so the effective potential is a function of the field value alone. The tree value is the potential of the Lagrangian,

$$ \mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{tree}}(\xi_{\mathrm{pot},\mathrm c}) = V(\xi_{\mathrm{pot},\mathrm c}). $$

The one-loop term. Expanding the action about the constant background and keeping the quadratic part, the fluctuation integral is Gaussian and gives

$$ \mathcal{V}_{1\text{-loop}} = \frac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{Tr}\ln\!\left(\frac{\delta^2 S}{\delta\xi_{\mathrm{pot}}^2}\Big|_{\xi_{\mathrm{pot},\mathrm c}}\right), $$

a trace over the fluctuation operator, evaluated with a regulator. For a field of mass $m^2(\xi_{\mathrm{pot},\mathrm c})$ in four dimensions the regulated trace reduces to

$$ \mathcal{V}_{1\text{-loop}} = \frac{m^4(\xi_{\mathrm{pot},\mathrm c})}{64\pi^2}\left(\ln\frac{m^2(\xi_{\mathrm{pot},\mathrm c})}{\mu^2} - \frac32\right), $$

so the correction is fixed by the fourth power of the fluctuation mass and by the logarithm of its ratio to the renormalisation scale. The sign is positive for a bosonic loop and negative for a fermionic one; the counting of degrees of freedom enters only through the multiplicity of each mass.

The coefficient. When several fields circulate, the one-loop term is the sum of their contributions, and the coefficient of $\rho^2\ln\rho$ collects the fourth powers:

$$ \boxed{\;\mathcal{V}_{\text{eff}}(\rho) = \frac{\digamma_{\mathrm{qtc}}}{4}\rho^2 + B\,\rho^2\ln\frac{\rho}{\mu^2} + \cdots\;} $$

with

$$ \boxed{\;B = \frac{1}{64\pi^2}\sum_i n_i\,m_i^4/\rho^2\;} $$

the sum running over the fields whose mass depends on $\rho$, with $n_i$ the multiplicity and the sign carried by the statistics. No sector is named in this formula, and none is needed.

Radiative Symmetry Breaking

The mechanism. If the classical quartic coupling is positive, the tree potential has its minimum at the origin and the symmetry is unbroken. The logarithm has the opposite curvature near the origin, so a sufficiently large $B$ turns the origin into a maximum and puts the minimum at a finite field value. The condition for the minimum is

$$ \frac{d\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{eff}}}{d\rho} = 0 \ \Longrightarrow\ \boxed{\;\ln\frac{\langle \rho\rangle}{\mu^2} = -\frac12 - \frac{\digamma_{\mathrm{qtc}}}{4B}\;} $$

which has a solution with $\langle\rho\rangle\neq0$ only when $B>0$ and exceeds $\digamma_{\mathrm{qtc}}/2$ in the stated combination. Symmetry breaking is then generated by the loop, not put in by hand.

The scale dependence. The potential is independent of $\mu$ order by order, so the apparent $\mu$ dependence of the minimum is compensated by the running of $\digamma_{\mathrm{qtc}}$ and $B$. The minimum is therefore a point on a trajectory, not a fixed number, and the statement "the vacuum is here" is always a statement at a scale.

The bound. The same coefficient that makes breaking possible makes the potential unbounded below at large field if the highest-power term is negative. The one-loop correction therefore carries a stability condition, and applying it requires knowing which fields circulate at the largest mass.

What the Framework Supplies

The algebra enters this machinery in one place: it fixes the shape of the invariant potential of a scalar valued in the algebra, hence the tree-level $\digamma_{\mathrm{qtc}}$ and the multiplicity of each mass. It does not enter the determinant, the logarithm or the coefficient $B$, all of which are properties of the fluctuation operator.

  • Supplied. The invariant potentials of the scalar sectors, and therefore the tree potential and the field multiplicities that enter $B$.
  • Imported. The one-loop formula itself, the regulator and the renormalisation-scale argument. These are standard quantum field theory, transcribed.
  • Not supplied. A sector. This article names none, and the two applications are named above.

Where the application is. The Effective Potential and the Coleman–Weinberg Mechanism in Biquaternionic Form applies every formula above to the framework's scalar sector, with its own field content and its own numbers. The division is deliberate: the machinery sits here, the sector-specific calculation sits there.

Summary of Notation

Symbol Meaning
$\Gamma[\xi_{\mathrm{pot}}]$ the effective action
$\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{eff}}$ the effective potential
$\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{tree}}$ its classical value
$m^2(\xi_{\mathrm{pot},\mathrm c})$ the fluctuation mass at the constant background
$\mu$ the renormalisation scale
$B$ the coefficient of $\rho^2\ln\rho$
$n_i$ the multiplicity of the $i$-th circulating field

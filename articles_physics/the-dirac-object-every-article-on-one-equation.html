The Dirac Object: Every Article on One Equation

Introduction

The Dirac equation carries more structure than the other three objects and the corpus spends more articles on it: the chiral pair, the bilinear current, the conjugate momentum that is not the field's derivative, the frame that the mass fixes, and the two-sided form that has no action of its own.

The count. 60 articles. The number is large because the spinor splits. The two-sided form, the chiral pair and the quaternionic reading are three objects that look like one, and each has its own readings. The family contributes ten of these articles; the rest are the equation's own, its propagator, its transforms, its path integrals and its quantisation. Read the element article first and the polar one second; the rest assume both.

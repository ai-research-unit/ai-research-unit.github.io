The Excitation Ladder Theory of Flavour

Ledger note. Every mass, lifetime and ratio in this article is measured data, transcribed from the Particle Data Group and marked as data where it is used. No coupling statement is made here; the corpus's single scale-bearing coupling statement lives in The One Coupling That Returns the Standard Model. What is tested is exact arithmetic on measured numbers, and the verdicts are stated as verdicts.

Introduction

This article classifies nothing. It states one hypothesis about why the fermions come in three copies, and what that hypothesis does and does not explain. The classification of one generation is finished elsewhere, in Particle Classification: Three Grids, One Name in Every Cell. That article names the sixteen cells, the twenty-one bosons and the twelve surviving scalars, and it says nothing about flavour. The separation is deliberate: the classification states a structure, flavour states a spectrum, and a nomenclature that absorbs both stops being a nomenclature.

The hypothesis is that there are no elementary particles, only fields and their levels of combined excitation. Flavour 1 is the ground level, flavour 2 the first combined level, flavour 3 the second. The ladder may have more rungs than the three we see.

The ontology is not new. Quantum field theory already holds that a particle is a pole of a propagator, that fields are primary, and that no object has a measured radius. What is new is only the second clause, that the flavours are rungs of a single tower, so that rung $n+1$ is built from the same medium as rung $n$. Everything below tests that clause and nothing else.

The verdict can be given first. The ladder fails if its rungs are counted in mass. It works if they are counted in amplitude: the steps then grow by a constant factor, and the count follows. That is a reading of the spacings and no derivation of them, and the two are kept apart throughout.

Flavour Is a Level, Not a Charge

The claim rests on a fact the classification article established. The fermions of one generation form a module of the algebra, and the tripling is a tensor product with a space on which the algebra acts trivially:

$$ \mathbf{16}\;\longrightarrow\;\mathbf{16}\otimes\mathbb C^{N}, \qquad \tilde Q\cdot(\psi\otimes f)=(\tilde Q\cdot\psi)\otimes f . $$

Every operator the model builds from the group is therefore the same operator on every rung, $Q\otimes\mathbb 1_N$ and $T_3\otimes\mathbb 1_N$. No measurement of charge, colour, weak isospin or $B-L$ can distinguish one rung from another. Flavour cannot be a charge, and it must therefore be a quantity a spectrum produces: an energy, a mass, a level spacing.

There is a second reason to expect the rungs of a tower to be ordered rather than equivalent. A level decays only if a lower level exists with the same quantum numbers and enough phase space. Rung 1 is the bottom of its tower, so there is no lower rung to fall to, and it is stable by conservation rather than by accident. This is why $e$, $u$, $d$ and $\nu_e$ sit at the top of their rows in the classification article: they are the ground states, and nothing beneath them carries their charges. No new dynamics is needed to stabilise rung 1, and none is claimed.

One word is used in a fixed sense. A resonance here means a pole of the propagator, not a geometric singularity. A rung is a pole with a real part and, above rung 1, a width.

The Ladder Fails in Mass

A tower has a spacing, and the quantity to test is the step between consecutive rungs. For the charged leptons the two steps are

$$ \frac{m_\mu}{m_e}=206.77, \qquad \frac{m_\tau}{m_\mu}=16.82 . $$

The step shrinks by a factor $12.3$. A geometric tower requires the steps to be equal; a tower of equal quanta requires the masses in integer ratio, $1:2:3$. Neither holds.

the ladder in mass predicted measured verdict
geometric, equal steps $m_3/m_2=m_2/m_1$ $16.82$ against $206.77$ fails by $12.3$
integer quanta, $1:2:3$ $2$ and $3$ $207$ and $3477$ fails by $10^{2}$ and $10^{3}$

The naive form of the theory is therefore excluded, and it is worth saying so in its own section rather than burying it. A theory that survives must not count rungs in mass.

The corpus's own algebra gives an independent warning. The biquaternion algebra is a field-first framework with a module and spin structures, and it has no dispersion relation. It cannot produce a ladder in any variable, because a ladder is dynamics and the algebra is kinematics. That is the same reason the corpus cannot count generations, recorded as a negative result in The Number of Generations and the Biquaternion Algebra.

The Ladder Works in Amplitude

In a medium amplitudes add and energies go as the square of an amplitude, so the natural variable is not $m$ but $\sqrt m$:

$$ \sqrt{m_e}=0.7148,\qquad \sqrt{m_\mu}=10.279,\qquad \sqrt{m_\tau}=42.153 . $$

The steps in amplitude grow where the steps in mass shrank:

$$ 10.279-0.7148=9.564, \qquad 42.153-10.279=31.874, \qquad \frac{31.874}{9.564}=3.333 . $$

A mass tower that compresses by $12.3$ is an amplitude tower that expands by $10/3$. The two statements carry the same information, and only the second is the one a wave picture produces.

variable step 1 step 2 the step
mass $206.77$ $16.82$ shrinks by $12.3$
$\sqrt m$ $9.564$ $31.874$ grows by $3.333$
$\log m$ $5.332$ $2.823$ shrinks by $1.889$

No column is constant, so the ladder is not an equal-spacing tower in any of these variables. That is the honest reading, and it moves the question from the spacing to the number of rungs.

No Exact Closure

The amplitude ladder fixes spacings and predicts no sum, so this article claims no exact numerical closure. A wave ladder sets the interval between neighbouring rungs. It places no condition on a combination of three masses, and a combination of three measured numbers that happens to land near a simple fraction is not a consequence of the picture. Relations of that kind are reported elsewhere in the corpus and are not counted here as evidence for the ladder.

What the ladder gives is the growth of the steps and the count. The two amplitude steps are $9.564$ and $31.874$, a growth of $3.333$, and that growth is a property of the two spacings and of nothing else. The Rung Count Belongs to a Tower takes the count from the same ladder. Those are the two things this article offers, and it stops there.

No mass and no scale is predicted. The arithmetic on the spacings, the fits that fix the count for the charged leptons, and the attempted predictions of a fourth mass are kept in Flavour Research: The Ladder Arithmetic, so that this article carries the idea and not its calculus.

The Rung Count Belongs to a Tower

The ladder has a count, and the theory does not fix it. This is where the name comes from, and where the theory is most exposed.

A count costs no parameter. A tower of $N$ rungs is described by two numbers, a scale and a starting phase, whatever $N$ is, so $N$ rungs contain $N-2$ predictions and the count is never fitted:

rungs numbers needed predictions
3 2 1
4 2 2
5 2 3
6 2 4
7 2 5

A longer ladder is easier to kill, not harder, which is unusual and is the reason the count is worth arguing about.

The data answer the count for the charged leptons, and they answer three. Three rungs reproduce the three measured masses; every longer count misfits by a factor of three or more. The rung count of the charged-lepton tower is three, and it is three by measurement rather than by principle.

A fourth rung of this tower could not hide above the tau. A closed ladder does not grow upward: extra rungs are squeezed between the ones already present, so a fourth rung would have to sit in a region already searched and already empty. That settles the count for this tower.

It does not settle the theory. A separate tower carries its own scale and its own starting phase, so a second tower may carry four, five, six or seven rungs and remain invisible, its rungs standing where that sector has not been looked for. The rung count is a property of a tower, not of the theory.

The Early Universe

A medium at temperature $T$ populates a rung of mass $m$ by the Boltzmann factor,

$$ \frac{n_2}{n_1}=e^{-(m_2-m_1)/T}, $$

so the upper rungs are rare when the bath is cold and common when it is hot. For the charged leptons:

$T$ $n_\mu/n_e$ $n_\tau/n_e$
2 MeV $0$ $0$
50 MeV $0.122$ $3.7\times10^{-16}$
100 MeV $0.349$ $1.9\times10^{-8}$
152 MeV $0.501$ $8.4\times10^{-6}$
500 MeV $0.810$ $2.9\times10^{-2}$
1 GeV $0.900$ $0.169$
2.563 GeV $0.960$ $0.500$
10 GeV $0.990$ $0.837$

Heavy rungs are rare and hot baths light them. That is not a hypothesis but the measured thermal history of the universe in inverse mass order: cooling removes the heavy rungs first.

Two corrections keep the reading honest. The ratio approaches parity and never passes it, so the bath stops penalising a heavy rung without ever favouring it. And detailed balance forbids accumulation, since the same agitation that builds a rung destroys it; exceeding equilibrium needs a conserved charge, a chemical potential, or a departure from equilibrium, and all three are compatible with a rung that is an elementary field.

One thing temperature cannot do is change the count. At every temperature above 2 MeV there are three generations. Temperature decides how many rungs are lit, never how many exist.

The Fourth Rung

Two different objects are called a fourth flavour, and only one of them is excluded.

a fourth rung could be status reason
a fourth chiral generation excluded at any mass it does not decouple: the Yukawa grows with the mass, so its contribution to $H\to gg$ and $H\to\gamma\gamma$ approaches a constant, and the measured Higgs rates match three
a fourth rung of a medium allowed, invisible it couples as $1/m$, so it decouples and evades every bound a chiral family cannot

The exclusion of the first does not depend on collider energy. A heavy chiral family does not become invisible by becoming heavy. The permission of the second is equally clean, and it is the version this theory requires.

bound value what it constrains
$Z$ invisible width $N_\nu=2.984\pm0.008$ light neutrinos with standard couplings
big-bang nucleosynthesis $N_{\mathrm{eff}}\approx3$ at 1 MeV rungs light and coupled at 1 MeV
direct search none below about 1 TeV chiral rungs, excluded in any case
$H\to gg$, $H\to\gamma\gamma$ matches three chiral rungs at any mass

Each entry constrains a rung that is light, or chiral, or both. A heavy rung of a medium satisfies all four at once, which is why the position that heavier rungs lie beyond present reach is defensible here and is not defensible for a chiral family.

What the Theory Owes

Three debts remain, and the theory is incomplete until they are paid.

The first is a dispersion relation. A medium has $\omega(k)$, and a tower is the eigenvalue problem of that relation; without one the spectrum is undefined and the rung count is not a well-posed question. The second is a composition rule that produces the constant step from its own structure and explains why the rungs are equally spaced in phase. The third is a width rule: Flavour Is a Level, Not a Charge settles rung 1 by conservation, and the theory must also say why rungs 2 and 3 have the widths they have, which for the charged leptons is the fifth-power law of An Introduction to Flavour.

The corpus supplies exactly the ontology this theory wants, and none of the dynamics. The biquaternion algebra is field-first: a module, spin structures, no points. It has no dispersion relation, because it is kinematics. The first debt is therefore one the corpus cannot pay, and claiming otherwise would be the one unrecoverable error available here.

What the theory does not owe is a derivation of three. Three is what the charged-lepton tower measures, any count is admitted by the picture, and the count belongs to a tower. A theory that owed three would have assumed its own answer.

The amplitude ladder reproduces the spacings of one tower and derives nothing further. What the theory adds is the count: rungs of one tower, spaced in amplitude, with $N$ fixed by measurement and not by principle.

The scope is equally plain. The quantum numbers of one generation are derived, in the classification article. The three copies and every flavour-breaking parameter are input, here. The theory adds a reading of the second and not a derivation of it.

Falsification

Three statements would end the theory as written.

A charged lepton found at the gap position of a four-rung ladder, near 46 or 425 MeV, would confirm a longer ladder and destroy the three-rung reading of this tower.

A deviation from QED in $e^+e^-\to\mu^+\mu^-$ would confirm a medium, since a structure of size $1/\Lambda$ gives

$$ \sigma=\sigma_{\mathrm{QED}}\left(1+O\!\left(\frac{s}{\Lambda^{2}}\right)\right), $$

and present data put $\Lambda>10$ TeV. Continued absence of such a signal pushes the structure out of reach and leaves the theory indistinguishable from three fields with three masses.

A fourth chiral family would end it. That one is already excluded, which is why the theory must be a ladder of a medium and not a ladder of chiral generations. The theory is permitted to propose more rungs. It is not permitted to propose them as chiral families.

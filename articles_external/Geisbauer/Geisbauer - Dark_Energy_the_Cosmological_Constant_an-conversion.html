What the Paper Does

Restates the area-count argument of the earlier dark-energy papers as three numbered formulas and, in the same move, retracts the prediction that went with it. The cosmological constant is an inverse area, the area count $10^{122}$ is a counting measure rather than a catastrophe, and the equation of state is $w\simeq-1$; the earlier $R^{-2}$ scaling and its $w=-1/3$ are "explicitly discarded". An order-unity calibration constant $\kappa$ is introduced, and the initial state is described as a Planck sphere rather than a point.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The three formulas

(F1) $\Lambda$ as an inverse area: $$\Lambda\equiv\frac1A,\qquad A=4\pi R^2$$ (paper 2). (F2) "Dark-energy 3D density": $$\rho_\Lambda=\frac{\Lambda c^2}{8\pi G}$$ (paper 3), and For $w=-1$, the continuity equation $\dot\rho+3H(\rho+p/c^2)=0$ gives $\dot\rho_\Lambda=0$. (F3) "Area dilution / counting measure": $$N_A=\frac{A}{\ell_{Pl}^2}=\frac{4\pi R^2}{\ell_{Pl}^2}\sim10^{122}$$ (paper 4), which "oduces the ł122 ordersž as a scaling effect of area growth, not as a local energy catastrophe. Key.". In one line: "F1) provides the 2D area measure, (F2) the 3D density, (F3) explains the 122 as an area count. 3 4 Interpreting Λ and the Choice of Radius 4.".

The retraction

The paper is explicit at the point where it changes its mind: an earlier speculation $\rho_\Lambda\propto R^{-2}$ (and thus $w=-1/3$) is explicitly discarded, "rative. • Important: No time law ρΛ ∝ R−2 ; the earlier consequence w = −1/3 is discarded. NA remains a counting measure only.", and in the conclusions "return to w = −1/3: the earlier R−2 sketch is discarded. 14". What replaces it keeps $\Lambda$ constant by tying the area to a fixed geometric scale, $$A_0\equiv4\pi R_0^2,\qquad \Lambda=\frac{\kappa}{A_0}=\mathrm{const}$$ (paper 6), with "$\kappa\sim1$" absorbing "order-unity conventions"; "dius). Today, the empirical proximity RH ≈ R0 explains why NA ∼ 10122 without implying a time-varying ρΛ . 4.3 Dimensional and physical reading • [Λ] = m−2 (inverse area).".

The Planck sphere

"The beginning was not a point singularity but a Planck sphere: a high-curvature initial surface carrying the total energy. As the universe łopened,ž the sphere grew, curvature stretched, and the area A soared." The stated consequence is that the $10^{122}$ "is an area count, not a catastrophe. A Planck sphere anchors the beginning geometrically; dilution of vacuum tension is the engine of expansion."

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. A retraction, recorded as a result

The idea. This paper undoes a prediction of its own earlier papers — $w=-1/3$, the $R^{-2}$ running and the Hubble-diagram offset of $\Delta\mu\approx-0.20$ mag — and says so three times in the text. What is left is a one-parameter statement, not a prediction. That is a reading, and it is the most transferable thing in the batch: it shows what a claim costs when the number that carried it is withdrawn.

What it would add. A worked example of status accounting, which is exactly what the corpus's empirical-status article is for: it separates what a framework computes from what it merely carries, and here the separation is made by the author against himself.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-empirical-status-of-the-biquaternion-framework.md.

What stands in the way. Nothing stands in the way of recording it. What stands in the way of using it is that a retraction removes content: the corpus gains a cautionary case, not a mechanism.

2. One order-unity constant, named

The idea. $\Lambda=\kappa/A$ with $\kappa\sim1$ is the honest version of "no free parameter: one number that absorbs order-unity conventions" when $R$ is identified with a particular radius. This is a formula with a declared, bounded freedom.

What it would add. A discipline the corpus could copy: a constant that is allowed to be $O(1)$ and is never allowed to be $10^{60}$.

Where it lands. articles_physics/cosmology-in-the-biquaternion-framework-the-carried-metric-and-the-missing-source.md.

What stands in the way. $\kappa$ has no derivation and no measured value, and with $\Lambda=\kappa/A$ the whole content is the choice of $R$; the paper concedes this by letting $R_0$ be "the de Sitter event-horizon radius" without saying why that radius.

3. Area dilution instead of a singularity

The idea. A Planck sphere of fixed total energy that grows in area, so that curvature and density fall as $1/A$ without any singular limit. This is a mechanism for the initial state, offered in a paragraph.

What it would add. An initial condition that costs no inflaton and no singularity.

Where it lands. articles_physics/cosmology-in-the-biquaternion-framework-the-carried-metric-and-the-missing-source.md, on what the framework can say about $a\to0$.

What stands in the way. No equation of motion is written for the sphere; the paragraph is a picture, and the corpus's cosmology article already records that it cannot form a source, so a picture is all that could land there too.

The rank is a judgement: the retraction first, because it is the batch's clearest piece of status accounting; $\kappa$ second, because it is a discipline rather than a result; the Planck sphere third, because it is a picture.

This is a six-page paper; the three formulas, the calibration constant, the Planck sphere, the retraction and a falsifiability list are its content.

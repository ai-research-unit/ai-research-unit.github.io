What the Paper Does

Derives interference from three separate ingredients and keeps them separate: a density that is the observable, a relative phase that places the fringes, and a coherence factor that decides whether the cross term survives at all. The derivation is the standard one done carefully, extended to partial coherence by a complex degree of coherence. The paper's claim is about what does the work — the phase structures the pattern, it does not create it — and its conclusion is a division of labour between phase, coherence and density.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The density and the cross term

$$\rho=|\psi|^2$$ (paper 4), and for two contributions $$\rho=\rho_1+\rho_2+2\sqrt{\rho_1\rho_2}\cos(\Delta\varphi)$$ (paper 18). "tude √ ψ= ρ eiφ , (2) the density ρ = |ψ|2 is the observable quantity, while relative phase determines the constructive and destructive geometry of interference." and destructive placement; "among amplitudes, while visibility depends on density, amplitude balance, and coherence."

The extrema and the visibility

$$\rho_{\max}=\rho_1+\rho_2+2\sqrt{\rho_1\rho_2},\qquad \rho_{\min}=\rho_1+\rho_2-2\sqrt{\rho_1\rho_2}$$ (paper 24–25), and $$V=\frac{\rho_{\max}-\rho_{\min}}{\rho_{\max}+\rho_{\min}}=\frac{2\sqrt{\rho_1\rho_2}}{\rho_1+\rho_2}$$ (paper 26–27). Note what $V$ does not contain: no wavelength, no separation, no $L$ — it is a statement about amplitude balance alone.

Partial coherence

With a complex degree of coherence, $0\le|\gamma|\le1$ (paper 28), $$\rho=\rho_1+\rho_2+2|\gamma|\sqrt{\rho_1\rho_2}\cos(\Delta\varphi+\arg\gamma),\qquad V=\frac{2|\gamma|\sqrt{\rho_1\rho_2}}{\rho_1+\rho_2}$$ (paper 29–30). "This expression separates three roles. Relative phase determines fringe position, coherence determines whether cross terms survive, and density determines the observable strength of the pattern."

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. Visibility as a product of two factors

The idea. $V=|\gamma|\cdot\frac{2\sqrt{\rho_1\rho_2}}{\rho_1+\rho_2}$: one factor for coherence, one for balance, and the pattern's depth is their product. That is a formula, and it is a factorization the corpus does not write.

What it would add. The corpus reads visibility off the length of the Bloch vector in its double-slit article; this splits that length into a coherence part and a balance part, which is what a which-path experiment varies separately.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-double-slit-experiment-in-biquaternionic-form.md.

What stands in the way. In the corpus both factors sit inside one idempotent, so the split is a re-labelling; and the factor $2\sqrt{\rho_1\rho_2}/(\rho_1+\rho_2)$ reaches 1 only for equal amplitudes, which means the corpus's "visibility one" case is a measure-zero slice of this formula.

2. A complex degree of coherence

The idea. $|\gamma|$ with an argument that shifts the fringes is the only object in the batch that describes a state which is neither pure nor fully mixed, and the paper uses it without ceremony. That is a mechanism for partial coherence.

What it would add. A parameter between the corpus's two regimes (coherent element, idempotent projection) — the corpus has no continuous dial from one to the other.

Where it lands. articles_physics/decoherence-as-idempotent-projection.md, whose decoherence is a complete projection.

What stands in the way. The corpus's states are idempotents, so $|\gamma|$ is either 1 or 0 there; introducing intermediate values needs a mixed state, i.e. a matrix rather than an element, which the corpus's representation deliberately avoids.

3. What the phase does not do

The idea. The phase moves fringes and cannot create them; the paper says so in the three-role sentence. That is a reading with a sharp boundary, and it is a useful guard against the batch's habit of attributing structure to phase alone.

What it would add. A limit on what any phase-first account may claim: without amplitude balance and coherence, phase has nothing to modulate.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-double-slit-experiment-in-biquaternionic-form.md, on what the phase factor does and does not control.

What stands in the way. The statement is a truism of the standard formalism, so as an import it adds discipline rather than content, and the corpus already respects it.

The rank is a judgement: the factorized visibility first, because it is an equation with two named parts; the degree of coherence second, because it names a missing dial; the boundary third, because it is a caution.

This is an eight-page paper; the density, the cross term, the extrema, the visibility, the degree of coherence and the three-role separation are its content.

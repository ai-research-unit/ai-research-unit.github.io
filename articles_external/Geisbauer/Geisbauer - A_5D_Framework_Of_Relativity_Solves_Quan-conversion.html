What the Paper Does

Argues that quantum probability is a calculus for ignorance and not the substance of the world, then re-reads the standard quantum examples inside Continuity Theory, a time-first scheme in which $h$ counts quanta of action, $c$ is the free-channel limit and $G$ encodes geometric hold. Interference is moved from self-interference to an edge-response field at the apertures, superposition becomes bookkeeping, and entanglement becomes correlation from a shared origin. The result is an interpretive programme, not a computation, with two stated predictions: fine fringes set by the incidence angle at the aperture, and a non-zero floor in the interference minima after background subtraction. Every unconventional claim is labelled working theory.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The status of probability

"Quantum probabilities are methods for counting outcomes when details are unknown." The Born rule is written $$P(\text{outcome})=\left|\langle\text{basis state}\mid\psi\rangle\right|^{2}$$ and read as a counting rule. A weather forecast that says '30% chance of rain' is not rain. The stated "humility principle" forbids mistaking the calculus of ignorance for "an all-seeing, creator-level view"; the caricature offered is that "a photon could become an elephant".

Three constants as organizing principles

Time is "an active carrier", $h$ counts "discrete quanta of Wirkung", $c$ is "the free-channel speed limit of space-time" separating free from bound behaviour, and $G$ "encodes geometric hold". The author states that these are "not metaphors but organizing principles for modeling".

Edges instead of self-interference

The aperture geometry enters as a cosine factor $$\cos\zeta_{\mathrm{geo}}=\sqrt{1-\left(\lambda/d_{\mathrm{eff}}\right)^{2}}$$ (paper eq. 2), with $\lambda$ the wavelength, the de Broglie wavelength for matter beams, and $d_{\mathrm{eff}}$ an effective aperture measure that includes width, edge shape, roughness and thickness. Narrow openings "enforce stronger angular redistribution". Two predictions follow. First, fine sub-fringes from a lateral incidence angle at the slits, testable by translating the source and mapping fringe spacing and asymmetry. Second, "predicts a positive floor remainsŮan irreducible edge-field floorŮonce backgrounds are properly subtracted. Test", to be looked for by extending dynamic range in the minima.

Cats and Bell

Macroscopic alternatives lose coherence because "countless microscopic couplings constantly leak information to the environment", so "The cat is either alive or dead in the world; the superposed state is our summary of ignorance, not a physical chimera." Bell's result is stated as: no theory that is local, realist and measurement-independent reproduces all quantum statistics, with $$S=E(a,b)+E(a,b')+E(a',b)-E(a',b')$$ (paper eq. 3) bounded by $|S|\le 2$, against $2\sqrt2$ for the singlet; the CT repair is placed on the measurement-independence assumption, not on locality.

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. A geometric cosine as an aperture damping factor

The idea. $\cos\zeta_{\mathrm{geo}}=\sqrt{1-(\lambda/d_{\mathrm{eff}})^2}$ is one dimensionless factor that turns aperture geometry into a rate, with the same functional shape as the Lorentz factor and the aperture standing where $c$ stands. It is a formula.

What it would add. A closed form for how effective width damps a beam, where the corpus's double-slit article carries amplitudes, a Bloch vector and a visibility but no aperture factor of its own.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-double-slit-experiment-in-biquaternionic-form.md, which writes the pattern as $I=a_1^2+a_2^2+2a_1a_2\cos\delta$ and puts the visibility in the equatorial part of the Bloch vector.

What stands in the way. There the cross term is the whole physics, and a factor multiplying an intensity gives an envelope, not fringes. For the factor to change a pattern it must act on a phase, and the paper does not show it doing so.

2. Probability as ignorance against a derived Born weight

The idea. Probability is a tool for reasoning under ignorance; the Born rule counts. That is a reading, and it collides with a corpus theorem: the Born weight there is obtained from a trace pairing on the Hermitian sector, so it is a property of the algebra's form rather than a remark about our knowledge.

What it would add. Nothing the corpus can use unless the pairing is given up; what it would cost is exactly the derivation that the corpus counts among its results.

Where it lands. articles_physics/states-observables-and-the-born-weight.md.

What stands in the way. A definite form and a positive cone do the work in that article, and "ignorance" names neither.

The rank is a judgement: the aperture factor first, because it is a formula that could be used; the reading second, because it removes a corpus result rather than adding one.

What the Paper Does

Opens a six-volume study of time by separating what is measurable from what is meant. Coordinate time, proper time, a hypothetical universal ordering parameter $T$, cosmic time, causal order, the entropy arrow, biological age and subjective duration are distinguished one from another, and a status is assigned to each: established physics, conceptual interpretation, or working hypothesis. The paper's own conclusion is negative — no preferred global present follows from relativity — and it says so before the derivations begin. It also corrects a formula used loosely elsewhere in the collection.

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. A rate with the coordinate freedom left in

The idea. $\Gamma=d\tau/dT$ is defined as a conditional rate, and the paper refuses to identify $T$ with any coordinate until an independent selection law is given. That is a formula written in a way that keeps the framework silent about a question it cannot answer — a discipline the rest of the batch does not show.

What it would add. A way to state the corpus's relational time without implying a preferred foliation: the rate exists, the parameter is a choice, and the choice is a separate postulate.

Where it lands. articles_physics/relational-time-from-the-sector-exchange-the-material-and-informational-clocks.md.

What stands in the way. The freedom is too complete: with $T$ free, $\Gamma$ absorbs any recalibration, so the rate carries no content until a selection law is supplied — which the paper says is missing, and which is exactly what would have to be imported.

2. The label on every claim

The idea. Each statement in the introduction is tagged as established physics, interpretation, or hypothesis, and the three are never mixed. That is a reading of how a framework paper should be written, and it is the only explicit example of the practice in the collection.

What it would add. A precedent for the corpus's own status accounting: the empirical-status article argues that a claim's standing should be stated, and here an author states it for his own programme, including the parts he cannot support.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-empirical-status-of-the-biquaternion-framework.md.

What stands in the way. Nothing stands in the way of citing it. What stands in the way of generalising it is that the labels are per sentence and unenforced; the same work's later volumes return to the 2:1 partition and the $45^\circ$ argument without labels, so the discipline is local to this introduction.

3. The $d\mathcal N/dT$ correction

The idea. $d\mathcal N/dT=H_{\rm cos}$ is only valid when $T=t_{\rm cos}$; in general there is a factor $dt_{\rm cos}/dT$. That is a formula correcting an identity used carelessly, and it is the batch's only self-correction on a mathematical point rather than a cosmological one.

What it would add. A check for any argument in the corpus that equates a cosmic clock rate with $H$: the equality presupposes the identification.

Where it lands. articles_physics/cosmology-in-the-biquaternion-framework-the-carried-metric-and-the-missing-source.md.

What stands in the way. Nothing; but the corrected relation is stated and not used, so the import is a caution rather than a result.

The rank is a judgement: the conditional rate first, because it is the batch's most careful formula; the labelling second, because it is a practice the corpus can adopt; the e-fold correction third, because it prevents an error.

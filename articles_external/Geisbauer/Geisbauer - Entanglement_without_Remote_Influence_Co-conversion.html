What the Paper Does Puts the Bell assumptions in the standard form, then relaxes exactly one of them — measurement independence — by a small, bounded amount, and argues that locality survives. The relaxation is quantified twice: as a perturbation of the hidden-variable distribution, $\rho(\lambda|a,b)=\rho(\lambda)[1+\delta]$, and as an information cap, $I(\lambda:a,b)\le\varepsilon$ bits per run. No-signalling is kept as an explicit condition, and the difference from superdeterminism is stated in three points. The paper is a budget and a picture, not a model: nothing is computed from $\delta$.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The three assumptions

$$A=A(a,\lambda),\qquad B=B(b,\lambda),\qquad E(a,b)=\int d\lambda\,\rho(\lambda)A(a,\lambda)B(b,\lambda)$$ (paper 1–2), and $$S=E(a,b)+E(a,b')+E(a',b)-E(a',b')$$ (paper 3) "satisfies $|S|\le2$ under three assumptions: (i) Locality ..., (ii) Outcome independence ..., (iii) Measurement independence: $\rho(\lambda|a,b)=\rho(\lambda)$". Quantum mechanics gives $E_{QM}(a,b)=-\cos\theta$ and $|S|\le2\sqrt2$.

The relaxation

$$\rho(\lambda|a,b)=\rho(\lambda)\left[1+\delta(\lambda;a,b)\right],\qquad \int d\lambda\,\rho(\lambda)\delta\ll1$$ (paper 4),"Z ρ(λ♣a, b) = ρ(λ) [1 + δ(λ; a, b)], dλ ρ(λ) δ ≪ 1, (4) reflecting that λ and (a, b) are not enforced to be statistically independent once one accounts for J. This is not a signal from one wing to the other; it is the recognition that the entire run is one Now-configuration. 2 Measurement-independence budget." The justification is cited, not derived: "It is known that only a small relaxation of measurement independence suffices to reproduce singlet correlations with local deterministic response functions [10, 11, 12]."

The three guards (a)

"Information cap: we bound the dependence between hidden variables and settings by a small, testable budget, e.g. I(λ : a, b) ≤ ε bits per run, 0 ≤ ε ≪ 1, rather than assuming arbitrarily large, conspiratorial correlations. (b) No signalling: the model preserves P" (b) "No signalling: the model preserves $P(A|a,b)=P(A|a)$, $P(B|a,b)=P(B|b)$, so that statistical marginal distributions remain setting-local." (c) "Run hygiene: cross-run structure is minimized by design (independent RNGs, late switching, cosmic inputs), allowing empirical upper bounds on ε from data rather than a fixed metaphysical stance. A simple causal"

The picture

"opening one sealed letter (A) does not change the other letter (B); it only reveals which member of a correlated pair you are holding. Entanglement is thus knowledge of joint structure, not communication across space." The quantum eraser is read the same way: "post-selection and data partitioning on the awareness layer, not retrocausation".

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. An $\varepsilon$-bit budget for measurement independence

The idea. $\delta$ is a function, but $I(\lambda:a,b)\le\varepsilon$ is a number with units, and the paper proposes to bound it from data rather than argue about it. That is a formula with a measurable parameter, and the only such object in the batch.

What it would add. The corpus's CHSH exercise derives $|S|\le2$ from factorisation and then quotes $2\sqrt2$; it has no vocabulary for the region between. A bound of the form $|S|\le2+\text{(function of }\varepsilon)$ would name that region.

Where it lands. articles_physics/exercise-the-chsh-inequality-and-tsirelsons-bound.md.

What stands in the way. The paper never computes the function: it cites that a small relaxation "suffices", which is a statement about existence, not a bound. To import anything one would have to derive $S_{\max}(\varepsilon)$ — the step the paper leaves out, and the only step the corpus could use.

2. No-signalling as a constraint on the relaxation

The idea. Marginal independence, $P(A|a,b)=P(A|a)$, is imposed as a separate condition rather than following from locality. That is a reading of what the relaxation is allowed to do.

What it would add. A reminder that $\delta\neq0$ and no-signalling are independent requirements, which the corpus's algebraic treatment gets for free from the shape of the state.

Where it lands. articles_physics/entangled-subsystems-in-the-biquaternion-framework.md, where the marginal of a joint element is computed by a partial trace.

What stands in the way. In the corpus the marginal is setting-independent because the partial trace never sees the distant setting; there is no $\delta$ to constrain. The import would be a condition on an object the corpus does not have.

3. Correlation as a joint fact

The idea. The two outcomes are "gle item in the global present—the Now.", so that the pair, not the parts, is the bearer of the correlation. This is a reading, and the corpus says the same thing algebraically: the singlet is an idempotent in $\mathbb{B}\otimes\mathbb{B}$, and neither factor alone carries it.

What it would add. A plain-language statement of what the corpus's Bell basis already encodes, usable when the algebra is too much.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-bell-basis-as-the-idempotent-basis-of-b-tensor-b.md.

What stands in the way. The reading adds no prediction, and the corpus's version is stronger: an idempotent can be decomposed and counted, while "one Now-configuration" cannot.

The rank is a judgement: the $\varepsilon$ budget first, because it is the only measurable object on offer; the no-signalling condition second; the picture third, because the corpus already has the stronger algebraic form.

This is a six-page paper; the assumptions, the CHSH bound, the relaxation, the three guards, the two-letters picture, the eraser note and a comparison with superdeterminism are its content.

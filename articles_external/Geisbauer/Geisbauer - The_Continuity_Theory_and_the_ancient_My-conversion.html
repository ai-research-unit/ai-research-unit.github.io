What the Paper Does

Sets out the conceptual foundation of TUni in two parts: the distributed action, and the projection that turns a fifth temporal direction into observed proper time. One postulate — Planck's constant as a tension over the universe's surface — is the substrate; from it the paper derives a time angle, an energy balance, the light cone as an extreme projection, the horizon as an extreme misalignment, a Bell constraint, a 2:1 energy partition and the dark-energy scaling. This is the programmatic paper of the batch, and it still contains the $1/R^2$ dark-energy law and the $0.20$ mag offset that the later area-dilution paper retracts.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The postulate

$$\sigma_h=\frac{h}{A(t)}$$ — "Planck's constant $h$ is not a localized quantity but a structural tension distributed across the surface of the universe" — and "This field forms the substrate from which the flow of time, gravity, and quantum behavior naturally emerge."

The projection

"the observed proper time arises as a projection from a higher-dimensional temporal axis ζ onto the local worldline of a system in spacetime. The time angle θ captures this projection geometrically. Time dilation" The extreme cases fix the scale: for light $\theta=90^\circ\Rightarrow\cos^2(\theta)=0\Rightarrow T_{\rm proper}=0$, and at a horizon "$\theta=90^\circ$" is replaced by "Time stops not because of singularity, but due to maximal geometric misalignment".

The energy balance

$$E_{\rm time}=mc^2+E_{\rm grav}+E_{\rm exp},\qquad \cos^2(\theta)=\frac{E_{\rm time}}{mc^2}=1+\frac{E_{\rm grav}+E_{\rm exp}}{mc^2}$$ ("the general energy equation"). With the horizon condition $E_{\rm grav}\to-mc^2$ this gives $\cos^2\theta\to0$ and $T_{\rm proper}\to0$.

The quantum sector and the partition

"Quantum states are described via time-angle orientation": $\psi(\theta)=A\,e^{i\phi(\theta)}$ with $\mathrm{Re}\,\psi=A\cos\theta$, $\mathrm{Im}\,\psi=A\sin\theta$, and "With: Re(ψ) = A cos(θ), Im(ψ) = A sin(θ) 9.2 Energy-Frequency Relation E = h̄ · ωθ Frequency of θ-oscillation defines energy — not postulated, but derived from geometry. 9.3". The partition follows from $$\tau=\frac{\hbar}{E_0}\ \Rightarrow\ E_{\min}=\frac{\hbar}{2\tau}=\frac{E_0}{2},\qquad E_0=2E_{\min},\qquad \cos^2(\theta)=\tfrac12\Rightarrow\theta=45^\circ$$ so that $E_{\rm time}=E_{\rm space}=\frac12E_0$, which "confirms the TUni projection at neutral angle". For entanglement, "ntum Entanglement Bell Constraint θA + θB = const • No signal exchange • Joint projection constraint • Entanglement as pre-projection alignment 13".

The cosmological sector, as it stood in June 2025

ΛCDM: $\rho_\Lambda=\mathrm{const}$ / TUni: $\rho_\Lambda\propto1/R^2$, with Supernova offset at $z\approx1$: $\Delta\mu\approx-0.20$, "Detectable via DESI, LSST, Euclid". Also $S_{\rm universe}=E_{\rm universe}\cdot T_{\rm uni}$, and the uncertainty limit $\Delta E\cdot\Delta t\ge\hbar/2\Rightarrow S_{\min}=\hbar/2$.

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. One angle that carries dilation, gravity, the horizon and the light cone

The idea. $\theta$ with $\cos^2\theta=E_{\rm time}/mc^2=1+(E_{\rm grav}+E_{\rm exp})/mc^2$ is a single scalar that reproduces time dilation, gravitational redshift, the horizon and the null cone as limits of one function. That is a formula with claims attached to each limit.

What it would add. A compact statement of the corpus's own recurring structure — its Lorentz factor, its gravitational cosine and its null limit are the same function — made explicit in one variable.

Where it lands. articles_physics/length-contraction-and-time-dilation-projections-not-deformations.md.

What stands in the way. Energies are not additive across frames, so $E_{\rm time}=mc^2+E_{\rm grav}+E_{\rm exp}$ is written in one frame and is not covariant; the same objection applies to $\cos^2\theta$ as a frame-independent statement. The horizon condition $E_{\rm grav}\to-mc^2$ is then an assumption about a bookkeeping that the framework has not defined covariantly.

2. A Bell constraint written additively

The idea. $\theta_A+\theta_B=\mathrm{const}$ states the correlation as a fixed sum of two angles, with no signalling. That is a formula, and it is the only attempt in the batch to write entanglement as a constraint on the time angles.

What it would add. A candidate invariant for the corpus's Bell-basis section, which states the correlation as an idempotent and does not produce a scalar constraint.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-bell-basis-as-the-idempotent-basis-of-b-tensor-b.md.

What stands in the way. A sum of two angles is a continuous constraint on a continuum of outcomes; a singlet gives perfectly anti-correlated results for matched settings and a cosine for mismatched ones, and an additive constraint reproduces neither without a rule for how settings map onto angles. No such rule is given.

3. The 2:1 partition

The idea. $\theta=45^\circ$ gives equal time and space parts, and the paper presents this as a derivation of the factor two.

What it would add. Nothing that survives inspection. $\tau=\hbar/E_0$ is the definition of a period, and $E_{\min}=\hbar/(2\tau)=E_0/2$ inserts the factor $\tfrac12$ by hand; the "partition" is the assumption, printed as its own conclusion, and $\theta=45^\circ$ is then chosen to match it.

Where it lands. No corpus article: the corpus's spin-statistics and energy-partition discussions use $\hbar\omega/2$ as a zero-point term, not as a cosmic split.

What stands in the way. Circularity. The step from $E_0=2E_{\min}$ to $\cos^2\theta=\tfrac12$ is a substitution, and the sentence that closes it —"e 2:1 energy partition confirms the TUni projection at neutral angle — equally split between time and space components." — takes the conclusion as its own evidence. Refused.

The rank is a judgement: the single angle first, because the limits are real even if the bookkeeping is not covariant; the Bell constraint second, because it is a formula and not a picture; the partition is not ranked, only recorded.

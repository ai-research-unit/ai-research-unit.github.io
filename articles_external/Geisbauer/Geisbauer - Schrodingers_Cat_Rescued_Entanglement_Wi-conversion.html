What the Paper Does

Argues that neither the cat nor the distant particle needs a collapse, because the world has one five-dimensional moment that is already definite at every point of it. The wave function is assigned to a "consciousness level" — the level of models and probabilities — while events belong to a "reality level" that is single-valued. The 5D metric is written down in a simplified form, and a code sample extends four-dimensional events to five.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The two levels

"The paradox arises only if we conflate the wavefunction on the model level with what actually exists on the reality level." The paper keeps two layers throughout: a consciousness level ("mathematical formalism, probabilities") and a reality level ("objective events), and claims the world is causal, unambiguous, and free of genuine paradoxes".

Entanglement

For

$\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\left(|0\rangle_A|1\rangle_B-|1\rangle_A|0\rangle_B\right)$ the standard reading is "an 'instant collapse'". Under T-UNI, "no instant signal is required; correlations arise because both particles are set in a single 5D-time moment. What appears to be “faster-than-light wavefunction collapse” in 3+1D is merely the projection of a 5D fact already in place. Thus, the “spooky action” dissolves."

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. Two levels, model and event

The idea. Every paradoxical statement is assigned to one of two levels, and the assignment is claimed to dissolve the paradox without changing any prediction. That is a reading, and it is the most portable thing in the paper.

What it would add. A vocabulary for a distinction the corpus makes algebraically but not in words: a superposed element of the state module is a description, while a detection is an event with a definite record.

Where it lands. articles_physics/quantum-physics-foundations-and-structure.md, which separates what the framework postulates from what it derives.

What stands in the way. A level distinction with no rule for which level a given sentence belongs to can absorb any objection, and the paper gives no such rule; predictions are identical to the standard ones by construction, so nothing observable distinguishes the reading.

2. Definiteness from a global moment

The idea. The cat is dead or alive because the 5D moment is single-valued, not because decoherence selected a branch. This is a mechanism, and it is a rival to the corpus's dynamical account, in which coherence is destroyed by a channel and the surviving state is a projection.

What it would add. A comparison the corpus cannot make on its own: its decoherence article explains when interference dies, and this paper claims the outcome never was undecided.

Where it lands. articles_physics/decoherence-as-idempotent-projection.md.

What stands in the way. The two accounts do different work — one is a rate, the other a claim about what exists — and neither contradicts the other's numbers; the corpus's article is a computation, and this one has no equation, so a corpus built on computable statements cannot host it as physics.

What the Paper Does Describes the five-dimensional moment space in the third person, as a report on "the authors' model rather than as an argument for it: an extra coordinate $\eta$, a product of three cosines for the time dilation, and a global now-time $t_{\rm Uni}$ that neutralizes motion, gravity and expansion. It adds an energy-oscillation picture, Raumspannung or GGGG, in which expansion, kinetic, binding and internal energies trade places. It closes by leaving the question open — Whether this framework provides genuine physical insights or remains a creative interpretation of our human misreadings of time is open to further inquiry"— which is unusual in this batch and is the paper's most characteristic move.

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. The concession that the global now is a convention

The idea. This paper calls $t_{\rm Uni}$ a coordinate choice that one "may or may not adopt", and states that no law is violated — a reading, and one that undercuts the stronger reading in the companion papers, where the same object is a physical flow that clocks "participate in".

What it would add. A distinction the corpus needs anyway: a global slicing as a bookkeeping device is harmless, while a global slicing as physics is a preferred frame. The corpus refuses an external time parameter for exactly this reason, and this paper agrees with the refusal while the others do not.

Where it lands. articles_physics/relational-time-from-the-sector-exchange-the-material-and-informational-clocks.md, which has no external parameter and must build a clock from the two times it has.

What stands in the way. Nothing stands in the way of the concession; what stands in the way is that it is only a concession — it does not say what work the convention then does, and the paper offers no calculation in which adopting it changes an answer.

2. The energy oscillation

The idea. Expansion, kinetic, binding and internal energies exchanging periodically, with the universe as a lung. The paper names the differential equations but prints none, so what is on offer is a mechanism with no formula attached.

What it would add. Nothing importable as stated.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-equation-of-state-in-biquaternion-form.md would be the natural home for a fluid whose pressure oscillates against its density.

What stands in the way. There is no equation to check and no number to compare; the section is a metaphor plus a literature remark about Big Bounce models. Refused, not for being wrong, but for being unstated.

The rank is a judgement: the concession first, because the corpus can use a clean statement of what a global now would and would not cost; the oscillation not at all.

This is a five-page paper; the 5D structure, the global now-time, the oscillation picture, a list of possible predictions and a closing question are its content.

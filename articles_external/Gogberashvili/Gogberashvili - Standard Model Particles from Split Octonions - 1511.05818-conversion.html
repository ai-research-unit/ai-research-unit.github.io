What the Paper Does

Models physical signals as elements of the split octonions over the reals, with four ordinary coordinates, a phase read as the classical action and three extra coordinates read as wavelengths, and assigns elementary particles to the algebra's primitive zero divisors. The count of those divisors — four idempotents, twelve nilpotents — is matched to the number of boson and fermion species, and the automorphism group $G_2^{NC}$ is used to reach the rank-two structure that $SU(3)$ also has. The match is a counting coincidence offered as a classification; no mass, coupling or cross-section is computed.

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. A zero-divisor census as the particle spectrum

The idea. Count the primitive zero divisors of the algebra, split them into the commuting and the nilpotent kind, and read the two counts as the boson and fermion content. A number: four and twelve, with the conjugates giving the antiparticles.

What it would add. The corpus's zero-divisor article treats the divisor set as a locus in momentum, configuration and state space, and never counts it against a particle table. A count that lands on the Standard Model's species is the one thing that would turn that locus from a geometric place into a spectrum.

Where it lands. articles_physics/zero-divisors-as-a-physical-locus-in-biquaternionic-form.md.

What stands in the way. The count is a coincidence of totals, not a map: the paper does not say which idempotent is which boson, and the twelve nilpotents match one generation's twelve fermions only if colour copies are not counted, since six quarks in three colours already exceed twelve. The corpus's algebra is $\mathbb{B}$, whose zero divisors form a null quadric rather than a finite list, so the census exists only after a change of algebra.

2. Rank two, reached without $SU(3)$

The idea. $G_2^{NC}$ has rank two, like $SU(3)$, and its adjoint holds quarks, antiquarks and mesons; the two simple roots $K_1,K_2$ are read as spin and hypercharge. A mechanism for a colour-like structure.

What it would add. The corpus's gluon article states the obstruction precisely: the maximal compact subalgebra inside $\mathbb{B}$ is $\mathrm{U}(2)$, four-dimensional, so the octet cannot be hosted, and it names the structure that would carry it and its price. Here is a concrete candidate algebra and a concrete embedding.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-gluon-an-octet-outside-the-biquaternion-algebra.md.

What stands in the way. $G_2^{NC}$ is non-compact, so its adjoint is not a unitary representation space and the "quarks" in it are not the coloured states of a compact gauge theory; and the paper offers the embedding as an analogy with $SU(3)$, by matching ranks, not as a homomorphism. The corpus's price — leaving the division algebra for a non-associative one — is paid in full here, since octonions are non-associative, and the paper's automorphism group is the only associative-multiplication-preserving part that survives.

3. The action as a coordinate

The idea. The scalar slot of the pass function is $\omega$, the classical action, sitting beside three wavelength coordinates. A reading of a slot, and the closest this literature comes to an action principle from the algebra.

What it would add. The corpus's action article says the gap is derivative counting: the algebra supplies fields and operations but not the derivative, so nothing fixes how many derivatives a Lagrangian may hold. Here the action is not a functional at all but a coordinate, and the field equations come from analyticity in the remaining coordinates instead.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-action-problem.md.

What stands in the way. An action as a coordinate gives no variational principle, so it cannot supply the two-derivative gravity Lagrangian the corpus needs; and the identification rests on the earlier paper's reading of the same slot as a wavelength, which is itself an interpretation. The two readings — action and wavelength — are made of the same symbol in the same author's two papers, and neither paper reconciles them.

The rank is a judgement: the census first, because a spectrum from the algebra is the corpus's standing question; the rank-two mechanism second, because it meets a stated obstruction; the action-as-coordinate third, because it renames the gap rather than closing it.

What the Paper Does

Extends a two-body "basic particle" model of resonances from $p$-wave to $d$- and $s$-wave states. Each $L$ gives a family: $J^{P}=\tfrac32^{-}$ baryons with $2^{+}$ mesons for $L=2$, $J^{P}=\tfrac12^{-}$ baryons with $0^{+}$ mesons for $L=0$. Within a family the masses follow an additivity rule: a resonance made of two basic particles has the mean of their effective masses. The families are linked by the equal-spacing rule, and $\kappa=\varepsilon$ ties the spacing to the $p$-wave pion energy.

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. Multiplet-like groupings from selection rules, not from symmetry

The idea. Each $L$ gives a set of states whose number and quantum numbers mimic an $SU(3)$ octet, but the grouping comes from a selection rule on the constituent pair — $T=t_1+t_2-1$ for $L=1$ and $T=|t_1-t_2|$ for $L=0,2$ — and not from a symmetry of the interaction. The author states the consequence plainly: the $J^{P}=3/2^{-}$ states cannot be a pure octet because they do not fit the $SU_3$ mass formula.

What it would add. A mechanism producing multiplet shapes without multiplet masses. SU(3) Representations and the Gell-Mann–Okubo Mass Formula in Biquaternionic Form (articles_physics/su3-representations-and-the-gell-mann-okubo-mass-formula-in-biquaternionic-form.md) derives the multiplet and its mass formula together, so shape and mass cannot come apart; here they do, and the failure of the mass formula is what shows the grouping is kinematic. The corpus's §Admissible Supermultiplets assumes shape and mass are tied, and this contradicts it.

Where it lands. §Admissible Supermultiplets, as the selection-rule alternative.

What stands in the way., and the pair assignments are chosen to land on known resonances, so the grouping is partly read off the data.

2. Masses add by averaging the constituents, not by a breaking operator

The idea. The additivity rule gives a resonance's mass as the mean of its two constituents' effective masses, so a difference of resonance masses equals a difference of basic masses. Effective masses are then extracted from the known levels ($m_\pi/2=630$ MeV for both $n$ and $K$ in the $d$-wave family) and used to predict the rest, and they come out close to the $p$-wave values.

What it would add. A mass rule with no symmetry-breaking term. The corpus's mass formulas all have the shape degenerate mass plus a breaking correction; here the mass is a mean of two inputs, and consistency between families is a coincidence of effective masses rather than a cancellation of corrections. Hadron Structure from QCD: The Bound-State Problem (articles_physics/hadron-structure-from-qcd-the-bound-state-problem.md) notes in §The Trace Anomaly, and Where the Mass Sits that most of a hadron's mass is not in the constituents' masses; the additivity rule is precisely the opposite assumption, and its partial success is therefore a real puzzle worth stating.

Where it lands. §The Trace Anomaly, and Where the Mass Sits, as the puzzle.

What stands in the way. The effective masses are defined by the rule itself, so agreement with the $p$-wave values is a check on a fit.

3. Two different quantisations of the same series agree on the spacing

The idea. The equal-spacing rule for the isosinglet series, with $\kappa=235$ MeV, is the same number as the $p$-wave pion resonance energy $\varepsilon$: $\kappa=\varepsilon$. A mass ladder and a resonance energy, derived in different schemes, coincide, and the coincidence lets the $\rho$ mass be written as a combination of basic masses to within a few MeV.

What it would add. A link between the corpus's ladder articles and its resonance articles. The Isosinglet Meson Series conversion in this folder gives the ladder; the present letter gives the family structure; the identification $\kappa=\varepsilon$ is the bridge, and it is the only place in the folder where the ladder's step is given a dynamical meaning.

Where it lands. The corpus has no single article holding both; the natural home is The Spin–Mass Formula and the Spectrum of Elementary Particles in Biquaternionic Form (articles_physics/the-spin-mass-formula-and-the-spectrum-of-elementary-particles-in-biquaternionic-form.md) §The Spectrum, as a footnote on what fixes a ladder's step.

What stands in the way. The identification is numerical and unexplained —"represents another relation" — and the letter gives no reason for it.

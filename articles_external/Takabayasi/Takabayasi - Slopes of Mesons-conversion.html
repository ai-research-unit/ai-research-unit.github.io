What the Paper Does

Derives the bilocal model's trajectory slope in closed form and shows it inversely proportional to the square of the constituent mass. With a universal force constant that one relation predicts the $\rho$, $\varphi$, $\psi$ slope ratios — and hence a constituent mass ratio $m_c:m_\pi:m_\Lambda=5.4\text{–}3.1:1.24:1$ from measured slopes, reversing the usual direction. Unequal masses give the $K^*$ and $D^*$ slopes, which agree with data. This turns non-universality of slopes into evidence for the model.

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. The slope is inversely proportional to the square of the constituent mass

The idea. $\alpha'=1/(2\kappa^{2}m_0^{2})$ with a universal $\kappa$. Slope non-universality is then not an anomaly but a measurement of constituent mass, and the direction is the opposite of the usual one: instead of fitting masses and predicting slopes, one reads slopes off the data and predicts mass ratios.

What it would add. A slope formula with a derivation. The Spectrum of a Lorentz Rotor: What Two Eigenvalues Leave Undetermined (articles_physics/the-spectrum-of-a-lorentz-rotor-what-two-eigenvalues-leave-undetermined.md) asks which quantities a spectrum determines; here the slope determines a mass ratio and nothing more, since $\kappa$ cancels in ratios.

Where it lands. §What the Spectrum Fixes, as the ratio-only statement.

What stands in the way. The prediction depends on which states are assigned to the $\psi$ trajectory, and Takabayasi concedes that this is ambiguous by a factor $1.3$; the "good agreement" for $K^*$ uses $\alpha'_\rho$ and $\alpha'_\varphi$ as inputs, so only one number is genuinely predicted.

2. A confining force that acts only at spacelike separation

The idea. The binding force acts "tio n sp9-c elike dista nce insid e a hadro n only; where". Confinement is a property of the character of the force — it operates on the relative coordinate, not on the centre of mass — rather than of an infinite energy barrier. Between hadrons the same constituents interact through a bilocal coupling that respects causality.

What it would add. A reason why the confining force does not leak out. Confinement and the Loss of Partonic Information in Biquaternionic Form (articles_physics/confinement-and-the-loss-of-partonic-information-in-biquaternionic-form.md) argues in §The Other Confinement Picture: A Linear Boundary Condition that confinement can be a boundary condition on field support; here the boundary is the spacelike relative coordinate.

Where it lands. §The Other Confinement Picture: A Linear Boundary Condition, as the spacelike-force version.

What stands in the way."Spacelike" is a property of the configuration, not of the force law, and any two-body force is a function of the relative coordinate; the claim needs the subsidiary condition to be doing the work, and the subsidiary condition is itself imposed.

3. The first-order constraint is reproduced by the action principle, not put in

The idea. The condition $(m_1x^{(1)}+m_2x^{(2)})\cdot(x^{(1)}-x^{(2)})=0$ is not an extra gauge choice but a consequence of the action principle with the stated Lagrangian. Consistency then verifies it.

What it would add. A constraint that is derived, not imposed. The companion paper of this folder on constraints and centre-of-mass treats them directly; here one follows from a Lagrangian.

Where it lands. The corpus's discussion of subsidiary conditions in Confinement and the Loss of Partonic Information §What the Subsidiary Condition Does.

What stands in the way. The verification is reported as "we can verify", with no display of the calculation.

4. Prediction of the

$D^*$ slope before the state was established

The idea. The unequal-mass generalisation predicts $\alpha'_D\simeq0.36-0.5$ from the $\rho$ and $\varphi$ slopes alone. That is a number for a charmed-strange trajectory computed without any charmed data.

What it would add. A falsifiable prediction from no charmed data. Charged Lepton Flavour Violation: One Operator and the Channels That Cannot See It (articles_physics/charged-lepton-flavour-violation-one-operator-and-the-channels-that-cannot-see-it.md) models a prediction with an explicit blind spot; here the blind spot ($\psi$) and the prediction ($\alpha'_D$) are separate.

Where it lands. The corpus's treatment of predictions and their robustness; the natural home is The Spectrum of a Lorentz Rotor, §What the Spectrum Fixes.

What stands in the way. The range $0.36-0.5$ is wide, covering much of what a loose fit allows.

The remainder, one line each. That the unequal-mass slope factor is asymmetric in $m_1$ and $m_2$ is a structural oddity of the model.

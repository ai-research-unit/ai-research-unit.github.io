Contents in One Paragraph

The spinless Madelung fluid carries two subsidiary conditions: vorticity fixed by the magnetic field, and circulation quantised in integers. Dropping the first frees vorticity, which then requires its own potentials, acquires a fixed-magnitude angular momentum, and becomes spin. The vorticity condition returns as a consequence — vorticity equals the curl of the spin-vorticity vector $T=\varepsilon_{ijk}\partial_j\Sigma\cdot\partial_k\Sigma$ — not as an assumption. The paper proves the condition's consistency with the equations of motion, establishes circulation in half-integer units $(n/2)h$ around a singular vortex line, and shows that in the triad picture invariance under rotating each triad about its own axis forces the electromagnetic potential, which is a geometric reading of local gauge invariance.

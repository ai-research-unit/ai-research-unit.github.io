What the Paper Does

Proposes that dark matter and dark energy are two behaviours of the zero-point energy of the vacuum, condensed onto mass concentrations and assisted by a weak electric field arising from a charge mismatch in the cosmic plasma. The combination, "electro-aether-energy", is claimed to reproduce galactic rotation curves, cluster hydrostatics, black-hole growth, the core–cusp profile and a softer Hubble tension without adding a dark-matter particle.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The claim

"the energy density of the (quantum) vacuum acts firstly as dark energy and secondly as a part of dark matter. Assisted by electric fields arising from a small charge mismatch in the cosmic plasma, it can condense on mass concentrations. No" "this constitutes “electro-aether-energy” (EAE), “electro- zero-point-energy” or “electro-vacuum-energy”, which solves the dark matter riddle without new physics."

The two standard models are one

"there are two standard models ... The second is $\Lambda$CDM ... Here we put forward that such a new type of matter is neither wanted nor needed, and that standard models of cosmology and particle physics are actually one and the same. No dark matter particle, which would require an extension of the standard model, is involved."

The stress tensor

The vacuum energy density $\rho_\lambda$ and the field energy $\rho_E$ enter the Einstein equations through $$T^{\mu}{}_{\nu}=\rho_\lambda\delta^{\mu}{}_{\nu}+\rho_E C^{\mu}{}_{\nu}+T_m{}^{\mu}{}_{\nu},\qquad C^{\mu}{}_{\nu}=\mathrm{diag}(1,1,-1,-1) \quad \text{(paper 3.3)}$$ with $\rho_E(r)=E^2(r)/2\mu_0=E^2(r)/8\pi$ (paper 3.14), and $\rho_\lambda$, $\rho_E$ fixed by $G^{\mu\nu}=8\pi G T^{\mu\nu}$ (paper 3.4–3.5). "TE is traceless, and involves a positive energy density and transversal pressures, but a negative longitudinal presssure; all equal in magnitude. 3."

The prediction

"A radial electric field of 1 kV/m is predicted in the Galaxy. For proper electric fields, EAE can cover the results deduced with MOND."

The charge mismatch

"While standard estimates allow maximally a fraction of 10−18 , EAE involves a fraction that can perhaps be 105 times larger. Analysis of the hydrostatic equilibrium shows that the strong Coulomb repulsion and attraction is counteracted by the negative casu quo positive gradient of the ZPE pressure. Section 5 deals with black holes."

The accounting

"We are led to view it not as a static, uniform entity but as a type of fluid, that can condense on mass concentrations. This" "oviding, presently, 70% of the total mass/energy as dark energy and 6% involved as the ZPE part of the dark matter, combined with 19% electrostatic energy in the dark matter. Particles, in the form of normal matter, only play a secondary role"

Consequences claimed

"the final parsec problem is solved by mass accretion as it happens for EAE. Two supermassive BHs at parsec distance will finally merge by absorbing aether energy, which enhances the probability for observing gravitational waves from merging events by the future LISA system."

The cosmological constant

"EAE theory goes even one step further: the “cosmological constant” measured from supernovae is merely the present value of the dynamical zero point energy, that may have started out at the Big Bang with the field theoretic value larger by some 123 orders of magnitude. In EAE theory there is no fine-tuning, the “cosmological constant” is small, since it is the present value of a decaying function."

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. The vacuum energy is a fluid that condenses

The idea. Instead of a constant $\Lambda$, the vacuum energy is a field with its own stress tensor, able to be absorbed from the environment and redistributed. Its density is positive in a core and negative in the halo, with total charge zero. This is a mechanism that converts the cosmological constant problem from a fine-tuning into a dynamics.

What it would add. An account of dark energy that is not a constant, in the place where the corpus's cosmology article records the missing source as an obstacle with no route.

Where it lands. articles_physics/cosmology-in-the-biquaternion-framework-the-carried-metric-and-the-missing-source.md, on the source term of the Friedmann equations.

What stands in the way. The construction needs a traceless tensor $C^{\mu}{}_{\nu}=\mathrm{diag}(1,1,-1,-1)$ alongside the metric, i.e. a second geometric object; the corpus's field equations have no such term, and adding one would change every solution.

2. An electric field as the scaffold of galactic structure

The idea. A charge mismatch of order $10^{-13}$ in the plasma produces a radial field near 1 kV/m, which is enough to hold the condensed vacuum energy in place and to reproduce the MOND rotation curves. One field does the work of a dark halo.

What it would add. A testable number — a field strength — in place of an unmeasured halo profile, which is what the corpus's dark-matter discussion lacks.

Where it lands. articles_physics/what-the-standard-model-does-not-explain-dark-matter-baryogenesis-and-the-sphaleron.md, on the alternatives.

What stands in the way. A galactic-scale electric field of that strength is not observed and would be screened by any ambient plasma; the paper's answer is that the plasma is dilute enough, which the corpus would have to check rather than assume.

3. Small-scale structure without cusps, by instability

The idea. A dynamical instability builds constant-density cores and can also dissolve them, so the cusp–core problem is answered by the same physics that makes the core. Nothing is tuned.

What it would add. A resolution of the anomaly catalogued in the corpus's dark-matter article, using a mechanism rather than a baryonic repair.

Where it lands. articles_physics/what-the-standard-model-does-not-explain-dark-matter-baryogenesis-and-the-sphaleron.md, on the small-scale anomalies.

What stands in the way. The instability is derived within the EAE stress tensor; without adopting that tensor the argument does not transfer.

The rank is a judgement: the condensate picture first, because it is the structural idea; the electric field second, because it is the falsifiable number; the core instability third, because it depends on the first.

This is a forty-five-page paper; the zero-point-energy fluid, the Einstein–Coulomb equations, the charge mismatch, the galactic and cluster fits, black-hole growth, the core instability and the cosmological fit are its content.

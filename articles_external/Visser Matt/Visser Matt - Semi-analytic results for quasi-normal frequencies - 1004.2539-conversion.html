What the Paper Does

Quasi-normal frequencies often follow a straight line in the mode number. Some calculations find exceptions. The authors build a model potential of two Eckart pieces with different heights and different falloff rates. It is general enough to cover flat, cosmological, and wormhole settings. They solve the matching problem and get a condition for the frequencies. The straight-line pattern survives when the ratio of falloffs is rational.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The pattern

"There is wide agreement that the QNFs are often of the form $\omega_n=(\text{offset})+in\,(\text{gap})$"

The exceptions

"though some authors have encountered situations where this behaviour seems to fail"

The model

"we consider a semi-analytic model based on a piecewise Eckart (Pöschl–Teller) potential, allowing for different heights and different rates of exponential falloff in the two asymptotic directions"

The lesson

"The key lesson to be learned from our semi-analytic model for the QNFs is that the $\omega_n=(\text{offset})+in\,(\text{gap})$ behaviour is common but not universal."

The control parameter

"in our semi-analytic model the key point is whether or not the ratio $b_+/b_-$ is a rational number"

The corroboration

"monodromy techniques quite often also lead to the qualitative result that rational ratios of surface gravities are closely related to the occurence of $\omega_n=(\text{offset})+in\,(\text{gap})$ behaviour"

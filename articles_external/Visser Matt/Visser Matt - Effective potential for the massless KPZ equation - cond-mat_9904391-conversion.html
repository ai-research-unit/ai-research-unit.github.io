What the Paper Does

The paper applies a previously developed functional-integral method to the massless KPZ equation, which is the noisy vorticity-free Burgers equation. It computes the one-loop effective potential in the ultraviolet regime and shows the theory is one-loop ultraviolet renormalisable in one, two, and three space dimensions and not beyond. The result is closely tied to the $\lambda\varphi^4$ effective potential, and the authors prove that dynamical symmetry breaking occurs in one and two dimensions through an analogue of the Coleman–Weinberg mechanism, but not in three.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The starting point

"In previous work we have developed a general method for casting a classical field theory subject to Gaussian noise (that is, a stochastic partial differential equation—SPDE) into a functional integral formalism that exhibits many of the properties more commonly associated with quantum field theories (QFTs). In particular, we demonstrated how to derive the one-loop effective potential."

What is done here

"In this paper we apply the formalism to a specific field theory of considerable interest, the massless KPZ equation (massless noisy vorticity-free Burgers equation), and analyze its behaviour in the ultraviolet (short-distance) regime."

The renormalisability result

"When this field theory is subject to white noise we can calculate the one-loop effective potential and show that it is one-loop ultraviolet renormalizable in 1, 2, and 3 space dimensions, and fails to be ultraviolet renormalizable in higher dimensions."

The relation to a scalar theory

"We show that the one-loop effective potential for the massless KPZ equation is closely related to that for $\lambda\varphi^4$ QFT."

The symmetry breaking result

"In particular we prove that the massless KPZ equation exhibits one-loop dynamical symmetry breaking (via an analog of the Coleman–Weinberg mechanism) in 1 and 2 space dimensions, and that this behaviour does not persist in 3 space dimensions."

The regime chosen

"it is important to underscore the fact that the analysis presented here has focussed on the short dis[tance] properties of the KPZ equation, which as we have seen, reveal a complementary phenomenology to [the] common studies concerned with the infrared, or long distance properties of the KPZ equation."

What becomes visible

"the statics of the massless KPZ system and the static behaviour of massless $\lambda\varphi^4$ QFT—and much of the structure of the massless $\lambda\varphi^4$ QFT carries over into the ground state structure of the massless [KPZ system]."

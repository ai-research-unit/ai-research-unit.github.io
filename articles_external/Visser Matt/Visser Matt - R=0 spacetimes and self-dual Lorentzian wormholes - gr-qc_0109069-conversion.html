What the Paper Does

The authors solve a condition on the stress-energy tensor for a timelike observer. The solutions have zero scalar curvature and are self-dual in the electrogravity sense. The family includes the Schwarzschild black hole, a set of naked singularities, and a separate set of wormholes. Using isotropic coordinates, the authors map which parameters give which geometry.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The equation

"the general solution of the equation $\rho=\rho_t=0$, where $\rho=T_{ij}u^iu^j$, $\rho_t=\left(T_{ij}-\tfrac12 Tg_{ij}\right)u^iu^j$, and $u_iu^i=-1$"

The class

"This equation characterizes a class of spacetimes which are 'self dual' (in the sense of electrogravity duality)."

The contents

"The class includes the Schwarzschild black hole, a family of naked singularities, and a disjoint family of Lorentzian wormholes, all of which have vanishing scalar curvature ($R=0$)."

The map

"Using isotropic coordinates we delineate clearly the domains of parameter space for which wormholes, nakedly singular spacetimes and the Schwarzschild black hole can be obtained."

What the Paper Does

This is a pedagogical paper on combining velocities in special relativity, and on the Wigner rotation and Thomas precession that follow when boosts do not commute. It presents the same results at three levels: an elementary level for a first course, an intermediate level, and a more advanced level. It closes with a summary of useful formulas and suggestions for further reading. The aim is to make an obscure but basic part of relativity accessible at several depths.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The purpose

"The purpose of this paper is to provide an elementary introduction to the qualitative and quantitative results of velocity combination in special relativity, including the Wigner rotat[ion]."

The three levels

"[We present] arguments presented at three differing levels: (1) utterly elementary, which will suit a first course in relativity; (2) intermediate, to s[uit] ... level students."

The summary and reading

"We then give a summary of useful results, and suggest further reading in this often obscure field."

What the Paper Does

A stationary spacetime corresponds to a three-dimensional Randers geometry, a special Finsler geometry. The authors extend this correspondence to general time-dependent spacetimes. They use the ADM decomposition, and the Randers norm becomes time-dependent and parametrised. The causal and conformal structure of the full spacetime is then encoded in that spatial object.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The known result

"There is a by now well-established isomorphism between stationary 4-dimensional spacetimes and 3-dimensional purely spatial Randers geometries"

The class

"these Randers geometries being a particular case of the more general class of 3-dimensional Finsler geometries"

The extension

"in stably causal spacetimes, by using the (time-dependent) ADM decomposition, this result can be extended to general non-stationary spacetimes"

The encoding

"the causal structure (conformal structure) of the full spacetime is completely encoded in a par[ameterized Randers norm]"

The caveats

"under mild conditions (such as stable causality and a mild constraint on the shift vector)"

The result

"We have given an explicit construction of the Randers norm in terms of the underlying spacetime metric"

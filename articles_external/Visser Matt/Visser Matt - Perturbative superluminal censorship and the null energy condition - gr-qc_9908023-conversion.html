What the Paper Does

The authors connect faster-than-light travel to energy conditions. Working to first order around Minkowski space, they show that a widening of light cones requires a violation of the null energy condition. With the condition satisfied, cones always narrow. The Shapiro time delay then cannot become an advance. The note is a simplified presentation of an earlier paper and points to a non-perturbative treatment.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The claim

"[any] tipping over of light cones in Einstein gravity, is always associated with violations of the null energy condition (NEC)."

The method

"This is most easily seen by working perturbatively around Minkowski spacetime, where we use linearized Einstein gravity to show that the NEC forces the light cones to contract (narrow)."

The observable

"Given the NEC, the Shapiro time delay in any weak gravitational field is always a delay relative to the Minkowski background, and never an advance."

The scope

"this note, which is a simplified presentation based on our earlier work"

The conclusion

"This note argues that any form of FTL travel requires violations of the NEC. The perturbative analysis presented here is very useful in that it demonstrates that it is already extremely difficult to even get even started"

The tool

"The perturbative analysis also serves to focus attention on the Shapiro time delay as a diagnostic for FTL"

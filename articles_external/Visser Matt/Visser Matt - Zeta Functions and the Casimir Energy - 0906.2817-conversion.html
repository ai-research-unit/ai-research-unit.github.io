What the Paper Does

Zeta function methods give a finite definition of the Casimir energy. The authors treat an arbitrary ultrastatic spacetime, with or without boundaries. They show the Casimir energy is closely related to the one-loop effective energy but not identical to it. It also depends on a normalisation scale. This matters for bag models of QCD.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The method

"We use zeta function techniques to give a finite definition for the Casimir energy of an arbitrary ultrastatic spacetime with or without boundaries."

The distinction

"We find that the Casimir energy is intimately related to, but not identical to, the one-loop effective energy."

The scale

"We show that in general the Casimir energy depends on a normalization scale."

The application

"This phenomenon has relevance to applications of the Casimir energy in bag models of QCD."

What the Paper Does

Analogue metrics are built from a background metric, a medium four-velocity, and extra fields. The authors ask what happens when the medium has vorticity. They derive the effective metric in that case. Vorticity changes the causal structure and can generate off-diagonal terms. The result extends the standard irrotational construction.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The framework

"Analogue spacetimes can be used to probe and study physically interesting spacetime geometries by constructing, either theoretically or experimentally, some notion of an effective Lorentzian metric $[g_{\mathrm{eff}}(g,V,\Xi)]_{ab}$."

The ingredients

"These effective metrics generically depend on some physical background metric $g_{ab}$, often flat Minkowski space $\eta_{ab}$, some 'medium' with 4-velocity $V^a$, and possibly some additional backgroun[d fields]"

The new ingredient

vorticity of the medium

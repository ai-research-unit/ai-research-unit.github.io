What the Paper Does

This is the conference version of the beam analysis. It builds beam profiles on a warp-drive geometry and computes the resulting stress-energy. The appendix gives an explicit example where a warp bubble violates the null and weak energy conditions. The authors point out that a non-negative Eulerian density is not enough to check the weak energy condition.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The framework

"Tractor beams, pressor beams, and stressor beams within the context of general relativity"

The explicit example

$$\rho+\bar p=-\frac{3}{16\pi}\frac{2m}{r^{5/2}}\,\vec v_\infty(t)\cdot\hat r\qquad (paper\ A.7)$$

The conclusion drawn

"the factor $\vec v_\infty(t)\cdot\hat r$ explictly changes sign depending on whether one is 'in front of' or 'behind' the warp bubble"

The warning

"this warp bubble explicitly violates the NEC, and so violates the WEC as well — this is a specific and explicit example of why merely checking the non-negativity of the Eulerian density is simply insufficient for checking the WEC"

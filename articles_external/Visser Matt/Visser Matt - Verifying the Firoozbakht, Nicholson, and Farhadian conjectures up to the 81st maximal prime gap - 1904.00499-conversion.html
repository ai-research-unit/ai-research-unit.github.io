What the Paper Does

Three conjectures bound prime gaps in different ways. The author reduces each to a sufficient condition that can be checked. Then he verifies them up to the 81st maximal prime gap, and for Farhadian up to $p<2^{64}$. The method turns an infinite claim into a finite computation with a proved remainder.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The conjectures

$$g_n\le p_n\left(p_n^{1/n}-1\right)\quad(\text{Firoozbakht})\qquad (paper\ 1)$$

$$g_n\le p_n\left((n\ln n)^{1/n}-1\right)\quad(\text{Nicholson})\qquad (paper\ 2)$$

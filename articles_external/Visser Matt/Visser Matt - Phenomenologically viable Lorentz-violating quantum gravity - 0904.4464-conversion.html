What the Paper Does

The authors recast a Lorentz-violating gravity model in Hamiltonian form. The equations of motion are written in a way that resembles the ADM formulation of general relativity. This puts the model into a standard framework. The benefit is that precision tests can then be designed with familiar tools.

Summary in the Authors' Words

What is done

"[we cast] the classical equations of motion in a form similar to the ADM formulation of general relativity."

Why

"This puts the model in a framework amenable to developing detailed precision tests."

The subject

"Phenomenologically viable Lorentz-violating quantum gravity" — the model class under study is Lorentz-violating and intended to remain viable against observations

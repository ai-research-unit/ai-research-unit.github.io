What the Paper Does

A detector moving with time-dependent acceleration in Minkowski space responds in a way that depends on the whole history. The authors propose an adiabatic expansion of the response. The expansion runs in time derivatives of the acceleration: jerk, snap, and higher. At leading order the rate depends only on the instantaneous acceleration, which recovers the standard Unruh result. The expansion also gives a practical way to compute click rates.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The problem

"acceleration along a one-dimensional trajectory in Minkowski spacetime"

The method

"To extract the physics of the process, we propose an adiabatic expansion of this response function."

The use

"This expansion is also a useful tool for computing the click rate of detectors in general trajectories."

The expansion variable

"The expansion is done in powers of the time derivatives of the acceleration (jerk, snap, and higher derivatives)."

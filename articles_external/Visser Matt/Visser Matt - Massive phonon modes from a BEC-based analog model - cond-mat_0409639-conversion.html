What the Paper Does

This is the short announcement behind the longer BEC paper. A two-component condensate with laser-induced coupling has two phonon modes that interact. The authors give the conditions for the modes to decouple. After decoupling the modes can travel at different speeds, and one of them has a massive relativistic dispersion relation. The mass is an unexpected excitation of the coupled system, not a property put in by hand.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The system

"Two-component BECs subject to laser-induced coupling exhibit a complicated spectrum of excitations, which can be viewed as two interacting phonon modes."

The main result

"Once decoupled, the phonons not only can be arranged travel at different speeds, but one of the modes can be given a mass — it exhibits the dispersion relation of a massive relativistic particle $\omega=\sqrt{\omega_0^2+c^2k^2}$."

Why it matters

"This is a new and unexpected excitation mode for the coupled BEC system."

The audience

"Apart from its intrinsic interest to the BEC community"

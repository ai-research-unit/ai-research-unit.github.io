What the Paper Does

The TOV equation fixes the interior of a relativistic star. Given one solution, the authors show how to deform it into another. The deformation is written in terms of shifts in central density and central pressure. They also recast the TOV equation as an integrability condition on the density and pressure profiles. That reading explains why the generating theorems work.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The object

"The Tolman–Oppenheimer–Volkov [TOV] equation constrains the internal structure of general relativistic [stars]"

The generators

"we conclude by presenting a new physical and mathematical interpretation for the TOV equation — as an integrability condition on the density and pressure profiles"

The parameters

"are conveniently parameterized in terms of $\delta\rho_c$ and $\delta p_c$, the finite shift in the central density and central pressure"

The general solution

"if we know three specific solutions $p_1(r)$, $p_2(r)$, and $p_3(r)$ then the one-parameter general solution ... may be written"

$$p(r)=\frac{\lambda\,p_1(r)[p_3(r)-p_2(r)]+(1-\lambda)\,p_2(r)[p_3(r)-p_1(r)]}{\lambda[p_3(r)-p_2(r)]+(1-\lambda)[p_3(r)-p_1(r)]}\qquad (paper\ A4)$$

The cost

"Note that these three forms of the general solution require two, one, or zero integrations respectively, at the cost of needing one, two, or three pre-specif[ied solutions]"

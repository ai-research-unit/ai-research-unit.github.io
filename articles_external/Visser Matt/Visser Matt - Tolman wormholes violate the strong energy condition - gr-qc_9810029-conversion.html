What the Paper Does

A Tolman wormhole is a geometry that contracts and then re-expands, like a bounce. The authors show such a geometry must violate the strong energy condition. This is a different condition from the null one needed for traversable wormholes. By contrast, the other standard energy conditions can be satisfied. The argument is local, in the same style as their earlier throat analysis.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The object

"Tolman wormholes" — geometries with a bounce

The result

the strong energy condition must be violated

The contrast

"In contrast, it is easy to arrange for all the other standard energy conditions to be satisfied."

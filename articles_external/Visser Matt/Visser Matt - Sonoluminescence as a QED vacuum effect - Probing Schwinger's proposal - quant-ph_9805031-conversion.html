What the Paper Does

Schwinger proposed that a collapsing dielectric bubble changes the QED vacuum and so emits light. The proposal has been controversial. The authors test it in detail using the sudden approximation. They compute Bogoliubov coefficients between the vacuum outside and inside the bubble, with matching at the moving boundary. The calculation is done with Bessel functions and junction conditions.

Summary in the Authors' Words

Schwinger's proposal

"Schwinger proposed a physical mechanism for sonoluminescence in terms of photon production due to changes in the properties of the quantum-electrodynamic (QED) vacuum arising from a collapsing dielectric bubble"

The status

"This mechanism can be re-phrased in terms of the Casimir effect and has recently been the subject of considerable controversy."

The approach

"Using the sudden approximation we calculate Bogolubov coefficients relating the QED vacuum in the presence of the exp[anding bubble]"

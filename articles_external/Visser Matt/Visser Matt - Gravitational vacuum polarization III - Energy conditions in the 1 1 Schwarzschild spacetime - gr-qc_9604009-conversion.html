What the Paper Does

This paper repeats the vacuum polarisation study in two spacetime dimensions, where the stress-energy tensor is known exactly. All three standard vacua are treated: Hartle–Hawking, Boulware, and Unruh. The null energy condition holds outside the horizon for Hartle–Hawking but fails for the other two. The dominant energy condition fails everywhere in every state, not only the standard vacua. The author presents the model as a check on the numerical four-dimensional work.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The purpose

"Because the stress-energy tensors are analytically known, I can get exact results for the Hartle–Hawking, Boulware, and Unruh vacua. This exactly solvable model serves as a useful sanity check on my (3+1)-dimensional investigations wherein I had to resort to a mixture of analytic approximations and numerical techniques."

The key results

"Key results in (1+1) dimensions are: (1) NEC is satisfied outside the event horizon for the Hartle–Hawking vacuum, and violated for the Boulware and Unruh vacua. (2) DEC is violated everywhere in the spacetime (for any quantum state, not just the standard vacuum states)."

What carries over

"Happily, the basic flavour of the (3+1)-dimensional results follows through: Many of the energy conditions are violated, the precise locations and manner of violation being influenced by the particular vacuum state being considered."

The averaged condition

"The ANEC is satisfied for the Hartle–Hawking vacuum, but is ill-defined (and arguably negative) for the Boulware vacuum."

The summary of the pattern

"energy condition violations are ubiquitous and are particularly prevalent in the Boulware vacuum in the region outside the event horizon, where every point-wise energy condition is violated, and all one-sided integrated energy conditions are violated."

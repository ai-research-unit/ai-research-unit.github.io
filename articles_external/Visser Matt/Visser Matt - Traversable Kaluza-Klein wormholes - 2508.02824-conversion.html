What the Paper Does

Some authors hoped a fifth dimension would remove the defocussing needed at a wormhole throat. The authors show the hope fails. In a traditional Kaluza–Klein setting the price of removing the defocussing is decompactifying the fifth dimension near the throat. So the energy condition violation is hidden, not removed. They work through the null geodesic defocussing directly.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The hope

"Various authors have suggested that Kaluza–Klein variants of traversable wormholes might to some extent ameliorate the defocussing properties (the curvature condition violations, and implied energy condition violations) inherent in positing the existence of a traversable wormhole throat."

The verdict

"Unfortunately such a hope is ill-founded."

The argument

"We shall show that in a traditional Kaluza–Klein context the price paid for completely eliminating the defocussing propert[ies]"

The conclusion

"adding a 5th dimension will do nothing to ameliorate the convergence condition violations, at least not without the unacceptably high price of decompactifying the 5th dimension in the vicinity of the wormhole throat"

The caveat

"there are many other (quite different and distinct) reasons for potentiall[y interesting Kaluza–Klein wormholes]"

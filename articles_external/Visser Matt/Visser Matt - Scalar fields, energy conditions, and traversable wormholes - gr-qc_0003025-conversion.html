What the Paper Does

A simple scalar field can break every classical energy condition. The authors show this for a non-minimally coupled field with positive curvature coupling. The violation reaches the averaged null energy condition, which is the one that forbids wormholes. They then look for wormhole solutions supported by such a field. They find that the required configuration is delicate. Scalar fields are common in low-energy limits of string theory, so the question matters.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The claim

"We describe the different possibilities that a simple and apparently quite harmless classical scalar field theory provides to violate the energy conditions."

The result

"a non-minimally coupled scalar field with a positive curvature coupling $\xi>0$ can easily violate all the standard energy conditions, up to and including the averaged null energy condition (ANEC)"

The consequence

"this violation of the ANEC suggests the possible existence of traversable wormholes supported by non-minimally coupled scalars"

The follow-up

"To investigate this possibility we derive the classical sol[utions]"

Why it matters

"the membrane-inspired field theories (low-energy limits of what used to be called string theory) are literally infested with scalar fields"

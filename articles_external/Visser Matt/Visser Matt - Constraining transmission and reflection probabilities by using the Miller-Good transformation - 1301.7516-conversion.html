What the Paper Does

The paper uses a change of variables of Miller–Good type to turn the one-dimensional scattering equation into a different equation for a different potential. Because the transformation preserves the scattering amplitude, a bound proved for the transformed problem transfers back to the original. The authors use this to improve on a previous bound for transmission probabilities, and they derive a result that is close to the semiclassical barrier-penetration estimate.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The subject

The paper is about "transmission through and reflection from a [barrier and the] related issue of particle production from a pa[rametric oscillator]". There is "considerable general interest in quantum phys[ics] [in bounds] on quantum transmission probabilities, [in grey]body factors of a Schwarzschild black h[ole]".

The method

"we report a way of using the Mil[ler–Good transformation to map the scattering] equation to a final equation for a diff[erent potential, so that a] previous bound [can be improved]. We then apply this gene[ral result to] derive a rigorous bound that is 'as clo[se as possible to]' barrier penetration."

What the bounds are not

"The fundamental bounds presented in this note are generally not 'WKB-like' — only for one particular special case has it proved useful to separate the region of integration into classically allowed and classically forbidden regions."

The open question

"In fact, in this WKB-like case it still seems that the bound we have derived is sub-optimal, and it is far from clear how closely these bounds might ultimately be related to WKB estimates of the transmission probabilities."

The translation to particle production

"if one works with the formally equivalent problem of a parametric oscillator in the time domain then the relevant differential equation is"

$$\ddot u(t)+k(t)^2u(t)=0,\qquad (paper\ 45)$$

"and instead of asking questions about transmission amplitudes and probabilities one is naturally driven to ask formally equivalent questions about Bogoliubov coefficients and particle production."

The dictionary between the two problems

"The key translation step is to realize that there is an equivalence"

$$T\leftrightarrow\frac{1}{1+N},\qquad N\leftrightarrow\frac{1-T}{T}.\qquad (paper\ 46)$$

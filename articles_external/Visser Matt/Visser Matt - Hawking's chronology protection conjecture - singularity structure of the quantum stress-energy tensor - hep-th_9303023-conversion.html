What the Paper Does

If traversable wormholes exist, they can be turned into time machines, which is what Hawking's chronology protection conjecture forbids. This paper computes the renormalised vacuum stress-energy in wormhole spacetimes using point splitting. It attends to the Hadamard form of the Green function and the structure of the stress-energy near short closed spacelike geodesics, and compares with earlier calculations by Frolov, Kim and Thorne, and Klinkhammer. As the closed geodesic shrinks, the stress-energy at the throat diverges, and vacuum polarisation destroys the wormhole.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The setting

"If traversable wormholes exist then it appears to be rather easy to to transform such wormholes into time machines. This extremely disturbing state of affairs has lead Hawking to promulgate his chronology protection conjecture."

The calculation

"An explicit calculation of the vacuum expectation value of the renormalized stress–energy tensor in wormhole spacetimes is presented. Point–splitting techniques are utilized. Particular attention is paid to computation of the Green function [in its Hadamard form], and the structural form of the stress-energy tensor near short closed spacelike geodesics."

The relation to earlier work

"Detailed comparisons are made with the analyses of Frolov [6], of Kim and Thorne [3], and of Klinkhammer [4]."

The mechanism

"If one wishes to use a traversable wormhole to build a time machine, then one must somehow arrange to keep that wormhole open. However, as the invariant distance around and through the wormhole shrinks to zero the stress–energy at the throat diverges."

The condition

"Vacuum polarization effects overwhelm the wormhole's internal structure once $s_\gamma(x,x)^2 \ll \ell_P R$."

The interpretation

"This happens for $s_\gamma(x,x) \gg \ell_P$, which fact I interpret as supporting Hawking's chronology protection conjecture. This result is obtained without invoking the technical requirement of the existence of a compactly generated chronology horizon."

The remaining gap

"there are still some technical issues of considerable importance left unresolved. Most particularly, computations of the van Vleck determinant in generic traversable wormhole spacetimes is an issue of some interest."

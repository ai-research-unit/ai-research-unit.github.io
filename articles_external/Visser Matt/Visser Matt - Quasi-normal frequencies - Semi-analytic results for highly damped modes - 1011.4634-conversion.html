What the Paper Does

Highly damped quasi-normal modes of the Schwarzschild–de Sitter black hole often follow a linear pattern in the mode number. The authors test how general that pattern is. They replace the potential with a piecewise Eckart form and derive a quantisation condition. The pattern holds generally but not always. What decides is whether the ratio of surface gravities is rational.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The pattern

"form $\omega_n=(\text{offset})+in\,(\text{gap})$"

The test

"We have investigated the genericity of this phenomenon for the Schwarzschild–deSitter (SdS) black hole by considering a model potential that is piecewise Eckart (piecewise Pöschl–Teller)"

The tool

"developing an analytic 'quantization condition' for the highly-damped quasi-normal frequencies"

The finding

"We find that the $\omega_n=(\text{offset})+in\,(\text{gap})$ behaviour is common but not universal, with the controlling feature being whether or not the ratio of the surface gravities is a rational number."

The wider observation

"the relation between rational ratios of surface gravities and periodicity of QNFs is very generic"

The limitation

"'qualitative' means that only the periodicity with the correct gap structure is recovered, the modes do not have to necessarily quantitatively match"

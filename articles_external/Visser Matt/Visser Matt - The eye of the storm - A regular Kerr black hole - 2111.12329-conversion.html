What the Paper Does

The authors make the Kerr mass parameter position-dependent by writing $m\to m\,e^{-\ell/r}$. This removes the ring singularity and leaves a Minkowski core. The result is a rotating regular black hole with one new parameter $\ell$. They compute curvature invariants, the causal structure and ergoregion, and the Killing tensors. Some energy conditions hold globally; others fail for particular $\ell$.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The construction

"a highly tractable non-singular modification of the Kerr geometry, dubbed the 'eye of the storm'"

What it is

"a rotating regular black hole with an asymptotically Minkowski core"

The mechanism

"This is achieved by 'exponentially suppressing' the mass parameter in the Kerr spacetime: $m\to m\,e^{-\ell/r}$."

The parameter

"The single suppression parameter $\ell$ quantifies the deviation from the usual Kerr spacetime."

The framework

"We have defined the class of '1-function off-shell' Kerr geometries, and demonstrated the general existence of the full Killing tower for the geometries within it."

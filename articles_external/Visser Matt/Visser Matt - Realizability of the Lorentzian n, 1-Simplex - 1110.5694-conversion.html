What the Paper Does

A previous paper built a Lorentzian version of Regge calculus. This one extends its key lemma from triangles to any dimension. The authors state and derive conditions under which a set of edge lengths corresponds to a real Lorentzian simplex. One timelike direction and $n$ spacelike ones is the case of physical interest. They also examine the other signature splits. The result is needed for the four-dimensional model.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The context

"we have developed a Lorentzian version of the Quantum Regge Calculus in which the significant differences between simplices in Lorentzian signature and Euclidean signature are crucial"

The task

"we extend a central result used in the previous article, regarding the realizability of Lorentzian triangles, to arbitrary dimension"

Why it matters

"This technical step will be crucial for developing the Lorentzian model in the case of most physical interest: $3+1$ dimensions."

The content

"We first state (and derive in an appendix) the realizability conditions on the edge-lengths of a Lorentzian $n$-simplex in total dimension $n=d+1$"

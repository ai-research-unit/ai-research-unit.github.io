What the Paper Does

The Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff formula is a series in general. In a special case where one commutator is a multiple of the identity, it closes into a single function $f(u,v)$. The authors derive that closed form, verify it against known results, and show it covers the Heisenberg and oscillator commutators. They also extend it to any central element that commutes with both arguments.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The formula

$$(u-v)e^{u+v}-(ue^{u}-ve^{v})\ \longrightarrow\ f(u,v)=\frac{(u-v)e^{u+v}-(ue^{u}-ve^{v})}{uv(e^{u}-e^{v})}\qquad (paper\ 1)$$

The comparison

"and relate this to previously known results"

The coverage

"this result includes, but is considerably more general than, results obtained from either the Heisenberg commutator $[P,Q]=-i\hbar I$ or the creation-destruction commutator $[a,a^\dagger]=I$"

The generalisation

"we can replace the $cI$ terms ... with $cE$, where $E$ is any object that commutes with both $X$ and $Y$"

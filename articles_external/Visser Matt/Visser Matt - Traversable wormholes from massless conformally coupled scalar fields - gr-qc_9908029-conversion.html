What the Paper Does

The massless conformally coupled scalar field has an "improved" stress-energy tensor. That tensor can violate the null energy condition classically, without quantum effects. Coupled to Einstein gravity it yields a three-parameter family of exact solutions. The family contains Schwarzschild, various naked singularities, and many traversable wormholes. So a single field supplies both the geometry and the exotic matter.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The field

"The massless conformally coupled scalar field is characterized by the so-called 'new improved stress-energy tensor', which is capable of classically violating the null energy condition."

The result

"When coupled to Einstein gravity we find a three-parameter class of exact solutions."

The contents

"These exact solutions include the Schwarzschild geometry, assorted naked singularities, and a large class of traversable wormholes."

The keywords

"Traversable wormholes, energy conditi[ons]"

The construction method

"we are considering geometries that are built piecewise out of segments of the solutions considered in this paper, with junction conditions applied at the thin shells"

What the Paper Does

A regular black hole with a Minkowski core is used as a template. The authors cut two copies and paste them together, making a thin-shell wormhole. They compute the surface stress-energy at the throat. They then analyse stability against radial perturbations, using contour plots over the parameter space. The result is a black hole mimicker built from a known solution.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The template

"Recently, a novel model for a regular black hole was advocated which possesses an asymptotically Minkowski core implemented via an exponential suppression (in the core region) of the Misner–Sharp quasi–local mass."

The construction

"Using this regular black hole as a template, we shall construct a spherically symmetric thin-shell traversable wormhole using the 'cut-and-paste' technique, thereby constructing yet another black hole mimicker."

The surface matter

"The surface stress-energy at the wormhole throat is calculated"

The result

"In this work we have used a novel regular black hole model based on exponential mass suppression to construct a thin-shell wormhole using the cut-and-paste technique."

The stability analysis

"Stability analysis for the specific asymmetry $\max\{a_+,a_-\}=\beta\min\{a_+,a_-\}=\beta a_*$, with $\beta>1$, $m_+=m_-=m$"

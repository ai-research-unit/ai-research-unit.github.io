What the Paper Does

The authors rewrite the Tolman mass in a static spacetime as a flux integral. The integrand is a generalised surface gravity. This links quasi-local mass to the acceleration of static observers. From there they derive entropy bounds that separate a geometric part from a matter part. The result connects black hole thermodynamics to the thermodynamics of ordinary static matter.

What the Paper Does

The Raychaudhuri equation tracks how a bundle of geodesics expands or contracts. It underlies the singularity theorems. The authors generalise it in several directions: to non-normalised vectors, to non-geodesic flows, and to a wider class of curves. They also rederive the Landau–Lifshitz identity for a general vector field. The point is to find which parts of the argument survive when the flow is not geodesic.

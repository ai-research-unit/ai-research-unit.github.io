What the Paper Does

Lorentz violation in quantum gravity phenomenology suffers a naturalness problem: the corrections are expected to be suppressed, yet nothing forbids lower-dimension operators from dominating. The authors use a two-component condensate with laser-induced transitions as a model. Low-energy perturbations give massless and massive quasiparticles in an emergent geometry. Beyond the hydrodynamic regime the dispersion relation becomes non-linear. The quadratic coefficients are Planck suppressed while the quartic ones are of order unity, so the naturalness problem does not arise.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The problem

"there are however still some unresolved theoretical issues, in particular regarding the so called 'naturalness problem' — which arises when postulating that Planck-suppressed Lorentz violations arise only from operators with mass dimension greater than four in the Lagrangian."

The proposal

"we investigate the class of two-component BECs subject to laser-induced transitions between the two components, and we show that this model is an example for Lorentz invariance violation due to ultraviolet physics."

The claim

"We shall show that such a model can be considered to be an explicit example high-energy Lorentz violations where the 'naturalness problem' does not arise."

The regime split

"Low energy perturbations in a 2-component BEC with laser-induced transitions between the single-particle states reproduce both massive and massless quasi-particles in an emergent spacetime."

The high-energy behaviour

"While in the hydrodynamic regime the dispersion relation is LI, beyond it the dispersion relation has to be modified in a Lorentz violating manner."

Why parity matters

"Due to parity of the GPE we only obtained terms with even exponents of $k$."

The resolution

"A key observation is that the present model does not suffer from the naturalness problem, because the quadratic corrections are Planck suppressed, while at the same time the quartic coefficients $\eta_{4,I/II}$ have no further suppression, and are actually of order unity."

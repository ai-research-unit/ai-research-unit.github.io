What the Paper Does

Two Schwarzschild spacetimes are grafted together before any horizon can form. The join carries a surface stress-energy, computed with the junction conditions. The result is a traversable wormhole whose matter sits on a thin boundary layer. Both static and dynamic versions are analysed, along with their stability and equation of state. It is the paper that established the thin-shell wormhole construction.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The construction

"In this paper I present a new class of traversable wormholes. This is done by surgically grafting two Schwarzschild spacetimes together in such a way that no event horizon is permitted to form."

The matter

"This surgery concentrates a non–zero stress–energy on the boundary layer between the two asymptotically flat regions."

The method

"I shall investigate this stress–energy in detail using the 'junction condition' formalism."

The scope

"Static wormholes", "Dynamic wormholes", "Stability Analysis", "Equation of state"

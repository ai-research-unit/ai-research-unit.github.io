What the Paper Does

This is a short conference version of the longer paper on inertial frames. It drops the relativity principle, keeps linearity, and finds that transformations between inertial frames form a groupoid. A preferred frame appears. Energy and momentum then transform affinely, and the invariant $E^2-\varpi\lVert p\rVert^2c^2$ acquires a factor depending on the motion of the rest frame. Rest energy is no longer intrinsic to the particle.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The setting

"We investigate inertial frames in the absence of Lorentz invariance, reconsidering the usual group structure implied by the relativity principle."

The structure that replaces a group

"In theories with a preferred frame (aether), the set of transformations between inertial frames forms a groupoid/pseudogroup instead of a group, a characteristic essential to evading the von Ignatowsky theorems."

The asymmetry of velocities

"it can easily be deduced that the velocity $v_{\text{aether}}$ of the aether as seen from the inertial frame $\bar F$, and the velocity $v_{\text{moving}}$ of the frame $\bar F$ as seen from the aether, are not equal-but-opposite and may not even be collinear."

The momentum rule

"These are affine transformations, with the affine piece depending only on the intrinsic ambiguities. If we define the four component vector $P=(E,-p^T)$, we can see that it transforms in the dual space of the four-component vector $X=(t,x^T)^T$."

The rest frame

"we can associate a rest-energy $\bar E=E_0(\bar F)$ and a rest-momentum $\bar p=p_0(\bar F)$, that will depend on the state of motion of $\bar F$ with respect to the aether. The rest-momentum $p_0(\bar F)$ may in general not be equal to zero"

The modified invariant

$$E^2-\varpi\lVert p\rVert^2c^2=\gamma^2(1-\varpi c^2\lVert u\rVert^2)E_0^2(\bar F) \qquad (paper\ 5)$$

The consequence

"the concept of rest energy still exists but it now depends on the state of motion of $\bar F$ with respect to the aether, through $E_0(\bar F)$. This result is completely different from the special relativity case, where the rest-energy and the rest-momentum are intrinsic properties of the particle that cannot depend on its velocity with respect to the aether."

The minimalist model

"we consider the spacetime to be Lorentz invariant, i.e. the physics of clocks and rulers is Lorentz invariant, while we allow for a Lorentz-violation in the energy-momentum sector, i.e. for one sub-sector of the particle spectrum (e.g. neutrinos)."

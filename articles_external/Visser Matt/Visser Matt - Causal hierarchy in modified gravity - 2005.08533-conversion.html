What the Paper Does

The paper rebuilds the standard causal hierarchy — chronology, causality, strong causality, stable causality, global hyperbolicity — for theories with more than one metric, an aether field, modified dispersion relations, or parabolic propagation. It shows that suitable hierarchies can still be defined, and that the decisive question is whether the signal velocity is finite or infinite. For infinite signal velocity a global time function must replace the light cone, and minimal causality then forces the aether field to be unique and hypersurface orthogonal. The authors call that condition global parabolicity.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The problem

"We investigate the causal hierarchy in various modified theories of gravity. In relativity the standard causal hierarchy, (key elements of which are chronology [causality], strong causality, stable causality, and global hyperbolicity), is well-established. In modified theories of gravity there is typically considerable extra structure (such as: multiple metrics, aether fields, modified dispersion relations, Hořava-like [gravity], parabolic propagation, etcetera), requiring a reassessment and rephrasing of the [standard] causal hierarchy."

The answer

"We shall show that in this extended framework suitable causal hierarchies can indeed be established, and discuss the implications for the interplay [between] 'superluminal' propagation and causality."

The dividing line

"The key distinguishing feature is whether the signal velocity is finite or infinite." In the finite case a variant of the general-relativistic hierarchy survives; in the infinite case the light cone is not the right object.

The condition for infinite speed

"Preserving even minimal notions of causality in the presence of infinite signal velocity requires the aether field to be both unique and hypersurface orthogonal, leading us to introduce the notion of global parabolicity."

The conclusion

"The framework we have developed above allows one to mathematically extend the usual Lorentzian causal hierarchy to multi-metric spacetimes, Einstein-aether models, Hořava-like spacetimes, modified dispersion relations, and parabolic PDEs. ... Either case provides a logically coherent framework for dealing with 'superluminal' signalling while still maintaining a consistent approach to causality."

1. A causal hierarchy, not a single condition

The idea. Causality is a ladder of conditions of increasing strength. Which rung a theory satisfies is a substantive question.

What it would add. articles_physics/causality-and-the-light-cone-as-an-information-barrier-in-biquaternionic-form.md treats causality as one barrier defined by one cone. This paper shows that the barrier is the strongest rung of a ladder, and that weaker rungs can survive when the strongest fails.

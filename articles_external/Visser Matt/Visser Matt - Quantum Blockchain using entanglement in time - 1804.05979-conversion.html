What the Paper Does

The authors propose a design for a blockchain that stores records in a temporal GHZ state of photons. Photons that never coexist can still be entangled, so the record is spread across time rather than kept in a sequence of blocks. The chain is read out by measurements at the end. The design is conceptual: it names the states and the operations, and leaves engineering aside.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The proposal

"We propose a conceptual design for a quantum blockchain."

The mechanism

"Our method involves encoding the blockchain into a temporal GHZ (Greenberger–Horne–Zeilinger) state of photons that do not simultaneously exist"

The advantage

"It is shown that the entanglement in time, as opposed to an entanglement in space, provides the crucial quantum advantage."

The reading

"our encoding procedure can be interpreted as nonclassically influencing the past."

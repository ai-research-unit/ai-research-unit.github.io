What the Paper Does

This is a three-page conference summary. It reports transformation theorems that take a perfect fluid sphere to another perfect fluid sphere. Two further theorems come from composing the first pair. The authors also give versions that act directly on the TOV equation and keep the pressure and density easy to interpret.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The programme

"we report new 'solution generating' theorems for the TOV, whereby any given solution can be 'deformed' to a new solution"

The setting

"Perfect fluid spheres, either Newtonian or relativistic, are the first approximation in developing realistic stellar models."

The results

"we have developed two fundamental transformation theorems that map perfect fluid spheres into perfect fluid spheres"

The composites

"we have also established two additional transformation theorems by composing the first and second generating theorems"

The clean versions

"we have also developed two 'physically clean' solution-generating theorems for the TOV equation"

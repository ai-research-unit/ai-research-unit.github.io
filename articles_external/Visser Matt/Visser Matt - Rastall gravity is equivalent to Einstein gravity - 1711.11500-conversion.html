What the Paper Does

Rastall gravity is presented as a modified theory with a non-conserved stress-energy tensor. The author shows it is ordinary general relativity in disguise. The gravity sector uses the Einstein tensor as usual. The matter sector uses a conserved tensor that differs from the usual one by a term involving the Ricci scalar. Nothing new is predicted; the apparent modification is a relabelling.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The claim being examined

"Rastall gravity purports to be a modified theory of gravity, with a non-conserved stress-energy tensor, and an unusual non-minimal coupling between matter and geometry"

The defining relation

"the Rastall stress-energy satisfying $[T^R]_{ab}{}^{;b}=\lambda g_{ab}R^{;b}$"

The verdict

"Unfortunately, a deeper look shows that Rastall gravity is completely equivalent to Einstein gravity — usual general relativity."

The reason

"The gravity sector is completely standard, based as usual on the Einstein tensor"

The consequence

in the matter sector Rastall's stress-energy is a redefinition of the standard one

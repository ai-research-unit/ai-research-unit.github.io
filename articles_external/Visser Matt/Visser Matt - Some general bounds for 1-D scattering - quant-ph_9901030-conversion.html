What the Paper Does

One-dimensional scattering appears in acoustics, waveguides, and barrier tunnelling. The author derives general bounds on reflection and transmission coefficients. The bounds depend on the shape of the potential, not on solving the equation. They hold for a wide class of potentials.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The setting

"One-dimensional scattering problems are of wide physical interest and are encountered in many diverse applications."

The aim

"in this article I establish some very general bounds for reflection and transmission coefficients"

The examples

"In acoustics one might be interested in the propagation of sound waves down a long pipe, while in electromagnetism one might be interested in the physics of wave-guides."

The quantum case

"In quantum physics the canonical examples are barrier penetration and reflec[tion]"

What the Paper Does

The one-loop effective action can be reconstructed from a Seeley–DeWitt coefficient. The authors solve the renormalisation group equations at one loop. Improving the classical action this way gives the leading logarithms. The result holds for arbitrary quantum field theories.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The input

"from knowledge of an appropriate Seeley–DeWitt coefficient"

The method

"By formally solving the renormalization group equations to one loop, we renormalization group improve the classical action"

The output

"and use this to derive the leading-logarithms in the one-loop effective action for arbitrary quantum field theories"

The scope

"any quantum field [theory, with] suitable caveats[,] even a gauge field"

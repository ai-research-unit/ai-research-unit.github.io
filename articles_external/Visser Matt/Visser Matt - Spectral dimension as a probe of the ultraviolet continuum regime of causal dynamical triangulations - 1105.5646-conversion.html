What the Paper Does

In causal dynamical triangulations spacetime is built from simplices. Whether a smooth manifold emerges at short distances is an open question. The authors use the spectral dimension, defined by a diffusion process, as the probe. They ask what value the dimension takes in the continuum limit of the triangulation. The answer distinguishes a genuine four-dimensional continuum from a degenerate short-distance regime.

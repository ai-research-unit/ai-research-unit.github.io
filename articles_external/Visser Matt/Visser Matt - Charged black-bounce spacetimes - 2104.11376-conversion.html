What the Paper Does

The paper adds electric charge to the black-bounce family, producing black-bounce–Reissner–Nordström and black-bounce–Kerr–Newman spacetimes. These are one-parameter deformations of the whole Kerr–Newman family that stay regular everywhere, pass weak-field tests, and interpolate between regular black holes and charged traversable wormholes. The authors show that charge changes the symmetry structure: a Killing tensor survives without the full Killing tower of principal and Killing–Yano tensors. They also show that the charged stress-energy can be read two ways, as charged dust with a nonlinear modification of Maxwell theory, or as standard Maxwell theory with an anisotropic fluid.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The construction

"Given the recent development of rotating black-bounce–Kerr spacetimes, for both theoretical and observational purposes it becomes interesting to see whether it might be possible to construct black-bounce variants of the entire Kerr–Newman family. Specifically, herein we shall consider black-bounce–Reissner–Nordström and black-bounce–Kerr–Newman spacetimes as particularly simple and clean everywhere-regular black hole 'mimickers' that deviate from the Kerr–Newman [family] in a precisely controlled and minimal manner, and smoothly interpolate between black holes and traversable wormholes."

Why charge matters

"While observationally the [charges of] astrophysical black holes are likely to be extremely low, $|Q|/m\ll1$, non-zero electric charge has a significant theoretical impact."

The three properties

"We have seen that adding an electromagnetic charge to the black-bounce–Schwarzschild and black-bounce–Kerr spacetimes leads to the black-bounce–Reissner–Nordström and black-bounce–Kerr–Newman spacetimes, which are minimalist, one-parameter, deformations of the entire Kerr–Newman family which have the desirable properties that they simultaneously (i) pass all weak-field observational tests, (ii) are globally regular (no curvature singularities), and (iii) neatly interpolate between regular black holes and charged traversable wormholes."

The symmetry finding

"we have seen that such geometries present interesting theoretical features such as the existence of a Killing tensor without the presence of the full Killing tower (principal tensor, Killing–Yano tensor)."

The ambiguity in the source

"the fact that the charge-dependent component of the stress-energy for the black-bounce–Kerr–Newman spacetime has a rather non-trivial physical interpretation. In particular, we found that it can either be interpreted as charged dust together with a non-linear modification to standard Maxwell electromagnetism, or as standard Maxwell electromagnetism together with an anisotropic fluid. While there is no simple way to remove this ambiguity, we can howev[er] ..." — the paper states the ambiguity plainly and does not resolve it.

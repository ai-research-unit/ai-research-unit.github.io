What the Paper Does

Several authors have proposed a metric with exponential factors of $m/r$. Its weak-field limit matches Newtonian gravity, so it passes the obvious tests. The strong field is very different. The authors analyse the geometry and show it is a traversable wormhole. Light passing the throat is strongly lensed. They also examine curvature, convergence conditions, and Regge–Wheeler potentials.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The metric

$$ds^2=-e^{-2m/r}dt^2+e^{+2m/r}\{dr^2+r^2(d\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,d\varphi^2)\}\qquad (paper\ 1)$$

The weak field

"While the weak-field behaviour matches nicely with weak-field general relativity, and so also automatically matches nicely with the Newtonian gravity limit, the strong-field behaviour is markedly different."

Why it was proposed

"Proponents of these exponential metrics have very much focussed on the absence of horizons"

The verdict

"It is a traversable wormhole, with time slowed down on the other side of the wormhole throat."

The lensing

"Strong field lensing phenomena are markedly different from Schwarz[schild]"

The honest framing

"Regardless of one's views regarding the merits of some of the 'justifications' used for advocating the exponential metric, the exponential metric can simply be viewed as a phenomenological model that can be studied in its own right."

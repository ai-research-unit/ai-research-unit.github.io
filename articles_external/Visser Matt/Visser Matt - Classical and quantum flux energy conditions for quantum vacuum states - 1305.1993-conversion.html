What the Paper Does

The paper defines a local condition on energy flux, rather than on energy density, and gives both a classical and a quantum version. The classical flux energy condition constrains the possible fluxes of a given stress-energy; the quantum version constrains the fluxes of a quantum state. The authors show that the classical condition holds in some cases where the standard pointwise conditions fail, that the quantum analogue holds for a wide range of states, and that the status of the remaining states is unclear.

Summary in the Authors' Words

Why a new condition

"The classical energy conditions are known to not be fundamental [but are] violated by semiclassical quantum effects. Consequently, some [authors have proposed] semiclassical replacements for the classical energy conditions, [for instance] nonlocal time-integrated Ford–Roman quantum inequalities."

What the paper proposes

"In this [paper we] develop classical and quantum versions of a local 'flux energy [condition', based] on the notion of constraining the possible fluxes [carried by a state]." The condition is local and it constrains a current rather than a density.

The result

The flux energy condition "will be seen to be satisfied in some [situations where the pointwise conditions fail, and] its quantum analogue (the QFEC) is satisf[ied for] a rather wide range of [states]. The [status for all] states is less clear."

The honest limit

The paper does not establish the quantum flux condition for all states. It establishes it for a wide class and states the gap explicitly, which is the paper's own statement of its limitation.

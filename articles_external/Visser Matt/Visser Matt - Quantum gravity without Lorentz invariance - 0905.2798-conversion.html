What the Paper Does

Following an earlier letter, the authors develop their variant of Hořava's gravity in detail. They drop detailed balance and restore parity, but keep the projectability condition. They write the most general action with these symmetries and count the parameters. They then derive the propagators for the scalar and tensor modes and examine the weak-field limit. The payoff is improved ultraviolet behaviour at high momentum, paid for by Lorentz violation.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The model

"this model being based on anisotropic scaling at a $z=3$ Lifshitz point"

The trade

"Hořava's model, and its variants, show dramatically improved ultra-violet behaviour at the cost of exhibiting violation of Lorentz invariance at ultra-high momenta."

The changes

"In contrast to Hořava's original model, we abandon 'detailed balance' and restore parity invariance."

What is kept

"We retain, however, Hořava's 'projectability condition' and explore its implications."

The outcome

"we explicitly exhibit the most general model"

The limitation

"this so far only considers pure gravity (and a phenomenologically introduced notion of cosmological fluid)"

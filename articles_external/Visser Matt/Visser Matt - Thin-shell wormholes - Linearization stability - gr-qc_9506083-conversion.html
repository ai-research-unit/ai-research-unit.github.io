What the Paper Does

Thin-shell wormholes are made by joining two copies of a region and putting matter on the seam. Their dynamics reduce to a single radial degree of freedom. The authors study linear stability around equilibrium. They derive the conditions under which the shell oscillates rather than collapses. The analysis is general within the spherical thin-shell class.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The class

"The class of spherically-symmetric thin-shell wormholes provides a particularly elegant collection of exemplars for the study of traversable Lorentzian wormholes."

The construction

"The resulting manifold $M$ is geodesically complete and possesses two asymptotically flat regions connected by a traversable Lorentzian wormhole."

What the Paper Does

The authors give an explicit formula for the metric of any static spherically symmetric perfect fluid. The whole class depends on one monotone generating function. No equation of state is assumed, or even required. They then work through the regularity conditions: finiteness at the centre, positivity of density and pressure, monotonicity, and the matching to an exterior. Each condition restricts the generating function.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The result

"We exhibit a simple and explicit formula for the metric of an arbitrary static spherically symmetric perfect fluid spacetime."

The freedom

"This class of metrics depends on one freely specifiable monotone non-increasing generating function."

The regularity

"We also investigate various regularity conditions, and the constraints they impose."

The advantage

"Because we never make any assumptions as to the nature (or even the existence) of an equation of state, this technique is useful in situations where the equation of state is for whatever reason uncertain or unknown."

The scope

"The first theorem we presented is applicable to all static spherically symmetric perfect fluid geometries without further restriction"

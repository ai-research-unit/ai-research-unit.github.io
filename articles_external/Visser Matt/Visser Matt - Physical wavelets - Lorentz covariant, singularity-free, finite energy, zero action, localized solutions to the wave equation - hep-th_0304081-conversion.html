What the Paper Does

The author exhibits a localized solution of the wave equation in Minkowski space. The field is finite everywhere, falls off quadratically in space and time, has finite energy, and has zero action. He calls it a physical wavelet. The construction works for real and complex scalars and extends to Maxwell and Yang–Mills fields. Because the energy is finite, such configurations appear at any nonzero temperature. The wavelet is compared with solitons, instantons, and sphalerons.

Summary in the Authors' Words

What is constructed

"I will exhibit a particularly simple 'physical wavelet' — it is a Lorentz covariant classical field configuration that lives in physical Minkowski space."

The properties

"The field is everwhere finite and nonsingular, and has quadratic falloff in both space and time. The total energy is finite, the total action is zero, and the field configuration solves the wave equation."

The extension

"These physical wavelets can be constructed for both complex and real scalar fields, and can be extended to the Maxwell and Yang–Mills fields in a straightforward manner."

The thermal consequence

"Since these wavelets are finite energy, they are guaranteed to be classically present at finite temperature"

The comparison

"The wavelet is neither a soliton, nor an instanton, nor a sphaleron though it shares properties with all three of these extended objects"

The instability

"Like the sphaleron it is unstable to dispersal."

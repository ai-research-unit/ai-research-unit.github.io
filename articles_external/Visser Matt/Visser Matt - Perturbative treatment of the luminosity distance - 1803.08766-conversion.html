What the Paper Does

This paper extends the non-perturbative distance formula to a linearly perturbed FLRW universe with two scalar modes. The authors compute the distance twice, once with the Jacobi map and once with the van Vleck determinant, and check that the answers agree. They then apply the result to an exact FLRW model and to a model with one sinusoidally varying scalar mode. For both they derive a cosmographic expansion in redshift.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The result

"We derive a generalized luminosity distance versus redshift relation for a linearly perturbed FLRW metric with two scalar mode excitations."

The two routes

"We use two equivalent approaches, based on the Jacobi map and the van Vleck determinant respectively."

The applications

"We apply the resultant formula to two simple models — an exact FLRW universe and an approximate FLRW universe perturbed by a single scalar mode sinusoidally varying with time."

The output

"For both models we derive a cosmographic expansion for $d_L$ in terms of $z$."

The outlook

"We comment on the interpretation of our results and their possible application to more realistic cosmological models."

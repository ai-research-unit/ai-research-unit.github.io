What the Paper Does

A large literature gives effective exponential bounds on the first Chebyshev function. This paper converts those bounds into explicit bounds on the prime gaps, that is, on the ratio of a gap to the prime before it. The conversion is direct once the Chebyshev function is bounded above and below. The result is a family of explicit inequalities with stated thresholds, some available decades ago and some relying on recent numerical work.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The inputs

"Over the last 50 years a large number of effective exponential bounds on the first Chebyshev function $\vartheta(x)$ have been obtained. Specifically we shall be interested in effective exponential bounds of the form"

$$|\vartheta(x)-x|<a\sqrt{x}\,(\ln x)^b\exp\left(-c\sqrt{\ln x}\right);\qquad (x\ge x_0).$$

What the paper does

"Herein we shall convert these effective bounds on $\vartheta(x)$ into effective exponential bounds on the prime gaps $g_n=p_{n+1}-p_n$."

The form of the result

"Specifically we shall establish a number of effective exponential bounds of the form"

$$\frac{g_n}{p_n}<\frac{2a\,(\ln p_n)^b\exp\left(-c\sqrt{\ln p_n}\right)}{1-\dots};\qquad (p_n\ge x_*).$$

The simplest example

$$\frac{g_n}{p_n}<\frac{\exp\left(-\sqrt{\ln p_n}/3\right)}{1-\tfrac12\exp\left(-\sqrt{\ln p_n}/3\right)};\qquad (p_n\ge2).$$

The tighter example

$$\frac{g_n}{p_n}<\frac{4926999\exp\left(-\sqrt{\ln p_n}\right)}{\dots};\qquad (p_n\ge\exp 10^6).$$

The assessment

"Some of these effective bounds could in principle have been deduced almost 50 years ago. Others rely on recent numerical work from the previous decade."

What the Paper Does

A dirty black hole is surrounded by matter. The authors write the Regge–Wheeler equation for spin 0, spin 1, and axial spin 2 perturbations on such a background. They use only general features of the background, chiefly the energy conditions on the surrounding matter. From these they get bounds on stability and on the greybody factors. The result holds without solving for the metric.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The scope

"we shall present general forms for the Regge–Wheeler equation for linearized spin 0, spin 1, and axial spin 2 perturbations on an arbitrary static spherically symmetric background spacetime"

The inputs

"Using very general features of the background spacetime, (in particular the classical energy conditions for the stress-energy surrounding the black hole)"

The output

"we extract several interesting and robust bounds on the behaviour of such systems, including rigorous bounds on the greybody factors for dirty black holes"

The simplification

"we are considering linearized scalar perturbations in the test-field limit"

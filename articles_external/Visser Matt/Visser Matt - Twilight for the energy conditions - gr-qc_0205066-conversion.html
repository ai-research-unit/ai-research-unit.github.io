What the Paper Does

The energy conditions are the standard assumptions behind singularity theorems and wormhole constraints. The authors argue that quantum physics undermines them. They are not Planck-scale curiosities but low-energy effects that can be arranged. The essay reviews the classical conditions, then scalar field models that violate them, then problems with those models. It asks whether the conditions have had their day.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The setting

"The tension, if not outright inconsistency, between quantum physics and general relativity is one of the great problems facing physics at the turn of the millennium."

The usual framing

"Most often, the problems arising in merging Einstein gravity and quantum physics are viewed as Planck scale issues ($10^{19}$ GeV, $10^{-34}$ m, $10^{-45}$ s), and so safely beyond the reach of experiment."

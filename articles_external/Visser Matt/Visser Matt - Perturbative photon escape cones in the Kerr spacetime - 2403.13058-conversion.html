What the Paper Does

The escape cone at a point in Kerr spacetime is the set of directions from which a photon can reach infinity. Exact treatment needs numerics. The authors expand in the rotation parameter and give explicit first and second order expressions for the cone's shape and solid angle. They evaluate special cases at each order. These give analytic expressions for the black hole silhouette, which is what the Event Horizon Telescope observes.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The aim

"We consider the perturbative, fully explicit, analytical behaviour of photon escape cones in the Kerr spacetime."

Why perturb

"When one conducts the fully general non-perturbative Kerr analysis, one quickly finds that one must at some point appeal to numerical and/or graphical methods."

The result

"we give explicit and tractable expressions for the first and second order (in $a$) contributions to both the shape of the escape cone, and the solid angle subtended by the escape cone."

The application

"We then look at a few special cases at each order, thereby leading to explicit expressions for the black hole silhouette, expressions which are of great interest to the observational community"

The link

"One important point is the intimate relationship between escape cone, capture cone, and silhouette."

The telescope

"When phrased in terms of silhouettes, the discussion is particularly relevant to the ongoing observational programme of the Event Horizon Telescope"

What the Paper Does

The paper presents a rotating regular black hole designed to be as tractable as Kerr. The geometry is asymptotically Kerr at large distance, has an asymptotically Minkowski core, and confines the energy-condition violation to an arbitrarily small region at the centre. It carries a principal tensor, a Killing–Yano tensor, and a Killing tensor — a "Killing tower" — which makes the Hamilton–Jacobi, Klein–Gordon, and Maxwell equations separable. The authors argue that this makes the extraction of observational predictions straightforward, and they propose quasi-normal mode analysis as the next step.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The aim

"We analyse a rotating regular black hole with asymptotically Minkowski core. This Kerr-like geometry possesses the full 'Killing tower' of nontrivial Killing tensor, Killing–Yano tensor, and principal tensor. The Hamilton-Jacobi equation, the Klein-Gordon equation, and Maxwell's equations are separable."

The trade-off

"Energy-condition-violating physics is pushed into an arbitrarily small region in the deep core. The geometry has a very high level of mathematical tractability; extraction of astrophysical observables falsifiable/verifiable by the observational community is straightforward."

The construction criteria

"Conclusions: The eye of the storm spacetime was constructed according to a set of carefully chosen theoretically and observationally motivated criteria. Analysing the geometry, it models a rotating regular black hole with asymptotically Minkowski core, is asymptotically Kerr at large distances, satisfies all of the standard energy conditions of GR in the regions of theoretical and observational validity, has integrable geodesics, and has the property of separability of both the Klein–Gordon and Maxwell's equations."

The claim of tractability

"It possesses the full 'Killing tower' of principal tensor, Killing–Yano tensor, and non-trivial Killing tensor. The eye of the storm is seemingly the most mathematically tractable rotating RBH in the literature, and is readily amenable to the extraction of astrophysical observables in principle falsifiable/verifiable by the observational community."

The next step

"Separability of Klein–Gordon and Maxwell's equations indicates one can and should probe the geometry via quasi-normal mode analysis for spin zero" and higher spins. The paper does not compute those modes; it establishes that they can be computed.

The limitations

The energy conditions hold only "in the regions of theoretical and observational validity", which excludes the deep core by construction. The paper is a construction and a feasibility argument, not a comparison with data.

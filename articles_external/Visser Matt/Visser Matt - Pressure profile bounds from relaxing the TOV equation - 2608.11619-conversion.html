What the Paper Does

The Tolman–Oppenheimer–Volkoff equations describe a static perfect fluid sphere. The authors replace the full system with differential inequalities, each derived under a different assumption about the density. They obtain explicit bounds on the pressure profile. The assumptions range from positivity of density to monotonicity of the average density. They compare each bound with the historical literature and state the trade-off between strength of bound and weakness of assumptions.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The aim

"We develop several new and quite general bounds on the internal pressure profiles of general relativistic perfect fluid spheres — based on various ways of relaxing the TOV system of ODEs to obtain several distinct differential inequalities."

The trade-off

"There is, as usual, a trade-off between strength of the bound, weakness of the input assumptions, and tractability of the analysis."

The assumptions used

"we shall develop several straightforward but nontrivial bounds that variously depend only on the positivity of density, the boundedness of density, the monotonicity of density, or the monotonicity of the average density."

The placement

"We shall carefully place these new bounds within the historical framework of previous efforts in this regard"

The outcome

"Even the mildest of the input assumptions on the density profile yields useful output regarding the pressure profile."

What the Paper Does

Andrica's conjecture bounds the gap between consecutive primes by the square root of the prime. The Riemann hypothesis alone does not prove it. The author asks how close one can get. He proves log-modified and higher-root variants, some conditional on the Riemann hypothesis and some unconditional. Numerical checks accompany the unconditional results.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The gap between proof and belief

"The gap between what we can explicitly prove regarding the distribution of primes and what we suspect regarding the distribution of primes is enormous."

The known obstacle

"It is (reasonably) well-known that the Riemann hypothesis is not sufficient to prove Andrica's conjecture"

$$\forall n\ge 1,\quad \sqrt{p_{n+1}}-\sqrt{p_n}\le 1?$$

The question

"But can one at least get tolerably close?"

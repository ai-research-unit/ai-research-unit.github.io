What the Paper Does

The Gordon form writes a metric in terms of a medium's four-velocity and refractive index. It is the natural language for analogue models. A Gordon form of Kerr would let Kerr be simulated by a moving medium. The authors make progress toward one, and report explicit results. They emphasise that the choice of flat background is decisive for both the answer and the computation time.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The gap

"so-called Gordon form, although the closely related Kerr–Schild form of the Kerr metric is well known"

The motivation

"A Gordon form for the Kerr geometry, if it could be found, would be particularly useful in developing analogue models for the Kerr spacetime, since the Gordon form is explicitly given in terms of the 4-velocity and 'refractive index' of an effective medium."

The claim

"In the current article we report progress toward this goal."

The first step

"First we present the Gordon form f[or a class of spacetimes]"

The practical lesson

"we found that a proper choice of flat background is of crucial importance for the resolution of the problem — both in terms of the final expression for the Gordon form, and in computation time"

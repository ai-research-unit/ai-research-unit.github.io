What the Paper Does

Faster-than-light travel is usually forbidden by hand. The authors show it is already forbidden by the null energy condition. Working perturbatively around Minkowski space, they show that the null energy condition forces light cones to narrow. The Shapiro delay is then always a delay, never an advance. Any object inside the cones is delayed too. The argument is short and uses only linearised gravity.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The setting

"working perturbatively around Minkowski spacetime, where we use linearized Einstein gravity"

The result

"the NEC forces the light cones to contract (narrow)"

The delay

"Given the NEC, the Shapiro time delay in any weak gravitational field is always a delay relative to the Minkowski background, and never an advance."

The consequence for travellers

"any object travelling within the lightcones of the weak gravitational field is similarly delayed with respect to the minimum traversal time possible in the background Minkowski geometry"

The summary claim

"This note argues that any form of FTL travel requires violations of the NEC."

What the Paper Does

Traversable wormholes need violations of the averaged null energy condition. The question is how much violation. The authors show the amount can be made arbitrarily small. This weakens the "exotic matter" objection from a quantitative problem to a qualitative one. The thin shell is described as a mathematical convenience rather than a physical requirement.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The requirement

"Traversable wormholes necessarily require violations of the averaged null energy condition"

The result

the violations can be made arbitrarily small

The thin shell

"The thin shell is present only as a mathematical convenience — any smooth transition from the wormhole region to the Schwarzschild region would be physically acceptable"

What the Paper Does

The static black-bounce metric is rewritten in Eddington–Finkelstein coordinates. The mass parameter is then allowed to depend on null time, in Vaidya style. This gives an evolving version of the geometry. The authors compute the Einstein tensor and the energy conditions. They then study accretion and evaporation models. The evolving geometry passes through a bounce rather than a singularity.

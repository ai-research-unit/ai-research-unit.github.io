What the Paper Does

The authors previously found general bounds on quantum transmission through a barrier. Here they apply the Miller–Good transformation to sharpen them. The transformation maps one Schrödinger problem to another with a different potential. Bounds that hold for the transformed problem transfer back. They give several applications, including a Schwarzian bound and a low-energy limit. They note the bounds are mostly not WKB-like.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The setting

"Transmission through a potential barrier, and the related issue of particle production from a parametric resonance, are topics of considerable general interest in quantum physics."

The earlier work

"The authors have developed a rather general bound on quantum transmission probabilities, and recently applied it to bounding the greybody factors of a Schwarzschild black hole."

The new step

"we use the Miller–Good transformation (which m[aps one problem to another])"

The character of the bounds

"The bounds presented in this note are generally not 'WKB-like' — apart from the one case reported in equation (46) there is no need (nor does it seem useful) to separate the region of integration into classically allowed and classically forbidden regions."

The open question

"it is far from clear how closely these bounds might ultimately be related to WKB estimates of the transmission probabilities"

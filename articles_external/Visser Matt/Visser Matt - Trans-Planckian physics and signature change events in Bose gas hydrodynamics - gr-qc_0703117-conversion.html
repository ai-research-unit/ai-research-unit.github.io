What the Paper Does

A Bose gas with a controlled microscopic model gives an effective Lorentzian metric at low energy. The same model allows the signature to change to Riemannian, because the microscopic parameters can be tuned. The authors compute particle production for a finite-duration Euclidean-signature event. This is an analogue of a trans-Planckian problem: the microscopic theory is known, and its effect on the emergent geometry can be calculated.

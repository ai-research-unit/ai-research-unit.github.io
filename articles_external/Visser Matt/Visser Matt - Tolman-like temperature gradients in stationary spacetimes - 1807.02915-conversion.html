What the Paper Does

A heat bath is specified by a temperature and a four-velocity, not by a temperature alone. In static spacetimes a natural rest frame exists. In stationary spacetimes it does not, because rotation introduces a preferred direction and frame dragging. The authors extend Tolman's relation to this case and identify the resulting temperature gradient. The result reduces to Tolman's when rotation vanishes.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The premise

"It is (or should be) well known that specification of a heat bath requires both a temperature and a 4-velocity, the rest frame of the heat bath."

The static case

"In static spacetimes there is a very [natural choice of frame]"

The problem

stationary spacetimes lack a preferred rest frame

The extension

a Tolman-like gradient with a frame-dependent part

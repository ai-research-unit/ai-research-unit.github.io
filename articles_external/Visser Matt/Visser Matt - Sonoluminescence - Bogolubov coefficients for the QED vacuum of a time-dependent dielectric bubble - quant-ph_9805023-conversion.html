What the Paper Does

The authors compute how the electromagnetic vacuum changes when a dielectric bubble changes in time. They calculate the Bogoliubov coefficients that relate the initial and final vacuums. A sudden change of refractive index produces photons with a broad spectrum and a high-frequency cutoff. They argue that the details of the collapse matter less than the time dependence of the refractive index.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The calculation

"by explicitly calculating the Bogoliubov coefficients relating the QED vacuum states associated with changes in a dielectric bubble"

The mechanism

"Sudden (non-adiabatic) changes in the refractive index lead to an efficient production of real photons with a broadband spectrum"

The cutoff

"a high-frequency cutoff that aris[es from the finite collapse time]"

The finite case

"For finite bubbles, the delta functions are smeared by finite-volume effects and the spectrum can be written down as an integral"

The conclusion

"We suggest that the key to the SL phenomenon is not the details of the bubble collapse, but rather the way in which the refractive index changes as a function of space and time."

What the Paper Does

Earlier analogue models simulated massless fields. This paper adds a mass. The system is a two-component Bose–Einstein condensate with a laser coupling the two hyperfine states. The spectrum contains two phonon modes that mix, a phenomenon the authors call refringence. They derive the conditions under which the modes decouple, and once decoupled each obeys a massive Klein–Gordon equation in a curved effective geometry. The mass comes from the laser coupling; without it, no mass term appears.

What the Paper Does

Quasi-normal frequencies have been rediscovered many times. The authors collect the known analytic results and add new ones. They go through a list of potentials, from delta functions and steps to the Eckart and Rosen–Morse forms, and give frequencies where these can be found. The results serve as a check on general bounds. The tone is that of a reference work.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The history

"quasi-normal frequencies [QNFs], has had a long and convoluted history — replete with many rediscoveries of previously known results"

The aim

"we shall collect and survey a number of known analytic results, and develop several new analytic results"

The presentation

"we shall provide several new QNF results and estimates, in a form amenable for comparison with the extant literature"

The purpose

"these exact and approximate results serve as a backdrop and a consistency check on ongoing efforts to find general model-independent estimates for QNFs, and general model-independent bounds on transmission probabilities"

The caution

"Even for so-called 'analytically solvable' potentials it is not necessarily true that the QNFs can be explicitly located in closed form."

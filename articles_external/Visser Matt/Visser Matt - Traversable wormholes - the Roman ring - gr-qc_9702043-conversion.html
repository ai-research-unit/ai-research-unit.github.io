What the Paper Does

A Roman ring is a cycle of traversable wormholes. It can produce chronology violation with a long path and weak curvature at each throat. The author shows that with enough wormholes in the ring, the vacuum polarisation becomes arbitrarily small. Back-reaction therefore stays small up to the reliability horizon. The conclusion is that semiclassical chronology protection fails before back-reaction can save causality.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The purpose

"In this brief report I introduce a yet another class of geometries for which semi-classical chronology protection theorems are of dubious physical reliability."

The construction

"I consider a 'Roman ring' of traversable wormhole[s]"

The result

"I show that (with enough wormholes in the ring) the gravitational vacuum polarization (the expectation value of the quantum stress-energy tensor) can be made arbitrarily small."

The consequence

"the back-reaction can be kept arbitrarily small all the way to the 'reliability horizon'—so that semi-classical quantum gravity becomes unreliable before the gravitational back[reaction]"

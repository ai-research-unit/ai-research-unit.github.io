What the Paper Does

Computes the on-shell Euclidean action of Reissner–Nordström black holes in de Sitter space and shows it equals minus the sum of the black-hole and cosmological horizon entropies, for arbitrary mass and charge in any number of dimensions. The unifying expression clears up a confusion in the literature about the actions of extremal and ultracold black holes in de Sitter, which become limits of the general formula. The action is then used, in a constrained-instanton reading, to estimate the pair-creation probability of black holes of arbitrary mass and charge in an empty de Sitter background.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The result

"We show the on-shell action is minus the sum of the black hole and cosmological horizon entropy for arbitrary mass and charge in any number of dimensions. This unifying expression helps to clear up a confusion about the Euclidean actions of extremal and ultracold black holes in de Sitter, as they can be understood as special cases of the general expression."

The subtlety that makes it work

"That the presence of conical singularities implies the necessary inclusion of (gauge field) boundary terms, providing a localized contribution to the on-shell action on the conical singularities that precisely cancels the contribution to the action from the bulk term depending on the charge and the gauge potentials. As in the neutral case, this again emphasizes the intriguing role of conical singularities in Euclidean quantum gravity."

The application

"we used a saddle point approximation" to estimate the probability for the pair creation of black holes with arbitrary mass and charge in an empty de Sitter background, with the on-shell action understood as a constrained instanton.

The relation to the neutral case

The techniques are "exactly the same general techniques as for the neutral, static de Sitter black hole", so this paper is the charged extension of the earlier Schwarzschild–de Sitter computation, and the result has the same form.

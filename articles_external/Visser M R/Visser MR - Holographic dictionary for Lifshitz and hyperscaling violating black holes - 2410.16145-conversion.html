What the Paper Does

Builds a holographic dictionary for the thermodynamics of black holes with Lifshitz and hyperscaling-violating asymptotics, generalising the anti-de Sitter dictionary. With it, the holographic Euler equation is dual to a generalised Smarr formula for these holes, and the extended bulk first law matches the boundary first law exactly. The dictionary for the central charge in the Euler relation depends on the hyperscaling-violating parameter, which is the new ingredient. The authors present this as evidence for the wide applicability of the Euler-equation formulation.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The result

"We develop a novel holographic dictionary for the thermodynamics of black holes with Lifshitz and hyperscaling violating asymptotics, generalizing the dictionary for Anti-de Sitter black holes. Using our dictionary we show that the holographic Euler equation is dual to a generalized Smarr formula for these black holes, and we find a precise match between the extended bulk and boundary first law."

The new ingredient

"the dictionary for the central charge appearing in the Euler relation depends on the hyperscaling violating parameter" — so the number of degrees of freedom is not the naive AdS expression but carries the violation parameter.

What is checked

"we developed a novel holographic dictionary for thermal equilibrium states dual to HVL black holes, and checked that the first law and large-$C$ Euler equation are still valid for these states. It is remarkable that the Euler equation has such a wide range of applicability."

What is left open

"it would be interesting to derive the dictionary for the thermodynamic black hole quantities using holographic renormalization ... and, in particular, check our formula for the central charge (which can be read off from the Casimir energy for even $d$ and $k\ne0$). Moreover, we would like to understand whether there exists a thermodynamic interpretation of the length parameters $r_F$ and $r_0$, perhaps relating these to 'Lifshitz charges'". Two parameters of the geometry, and hence two candidate thermodynamic variables, remain uninterpreted.

The prospect

"it is likely that our new setup will lead to a discovery of so far unseen phase transitions and critical phenomena arising for Lifshitz and hyperscaling violating theories."

1. The Euler relation survives anisotropic scaling

The idea. A homogeneous relation $E=TS+\dots+\mu C$ holds even when the scaling symmetry is anisotropic, provided the dictionary is adjusted with the violation parameter. This is a formula with a robustness claim.

2. Degrees of freedom are not simply the area

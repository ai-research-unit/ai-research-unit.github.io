What the Paper Does

Applies the exact dictionary between extended black-hole thermodynamics and the thermodynamics of the dual conformal field theory to states dual to neutral singly spinning anti-de Sitter black holes. Two independent conjugate pairs are included on the field-theory side, the central charge with its potential and the pressure with the volume, which raises the count of distinct ensembles to eight. Three of the eight show interesting phase behaviour, including behaviour that the bulk thermodynamics alone does not exhibit. The authors argue that prescribing a volume and a central charge for the CFT requires a new scale — the radius $R$ of the CFT sphere — rather than keeping $R=L$.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The count

"The inclusion of the conjugate thermodynamic pair $(C,\mu)$ (the central charge and its associated chemical potential) increases the number of possible thermodynamic ensembles to eight – we have uncovered interesting phase behavior in three of them."

The choice of dictionary

In earlier work "it was argued that the (inverse) central charge plays a similar role to the thermodynamic pressure $P\propto1/L^2$ in the bulk thermodynamics of AdS black holes. However ... this does not lead to a satisfactory duality between the bulk and boundary thermodynamics. To achieve this latter goal, one needs to either view Newton's constant $G_N$ as a variable whose variation is related to variations in the CFT central charge, or introduce a new scale $R$ for the radius of the CFT sphere. We find the latter scenario much more plausible." A "reshuffling and rescaling of thermodynamic variables is needed to arrive at the holographic dictionary ... which gives a clean duality between bulk and boundary first laws and Smarr relations."

What survives

"In this new setting, we find that all the interesting phase behaviours in [earlier work] are preserved by the CFT" — so the rewrite is a clarification, not a revision. The bulk phases remain, and the CFT reproduces them, plus additional structure in three ensembles.

The physics

Rotation adds a $\tilde\Omega\delta J$ term and makes the volume and central charge relations more involved. The qualitative result is that the rotating case has a richer ensemble structure than the charged case, where only one ensemble showed criticality.

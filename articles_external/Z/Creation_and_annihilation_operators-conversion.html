What the Paper Does

An encyclopedia article on the ladder operators: what they do to particle number, their use for the harmonic oscillator where they raise and lower energy by one quantum, their role in second quantisation, and the reason Hamiltonians written in them satisfy positivity automatically. Dirac's introduction is credited.

Why they are used

"Constructing Hamiltonians using these operators has the advantage that the theory automatically satisfies" positivity: a Hamiltonian written as a sum of $a^\dagger a$ terms has a spectrum bounded below by construction, because each term is a positive operator.

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. Positivity is bought by the ordering

The idea. Writing $H=\sum\omega\,a^\dagger a$ makes the spectrum non-negative automatically. The physics is in the choice of normal ordering, and that ordering is a convention which drops an infinite constant.

What it would add. A named step the corpus performs silently. The corpus's Fock-space articles build the ladder operators and write the Hamiltonian in normal order; the article makes explicit that the bounded-below property follows from that writing, and that the zero-point constant is discarded by choice.

Where it lands. articles_physics/fock-space-and-creation-annihilation-operators-in-biquaternionic-form.md, at the Hamiltonian.

What stands in the way. The corpus's vacuum is an algebraic idempotent and the zero-point energy is absent rather than discarded, so the convention-versus-result distinction has no bite there.

2. One operator, two meanings

The idea. The same pair counts particles in a field and climbs the ladder of one oscillator. That identification is what makes second quantisation possible: a mode is an oscillator and its excitations are particles.

What it would add. A statement of the identification the corpus uses without arguing it.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-fermionic-fock-space-in-biquaternionic-form.md, where the anticommuting case is built.

What stands in the way. For fermions the operators anticommute and the "one quantum" reading needs the exclusion principle; the article's oscillator picture is the bosonic one.

This is an encyclopedia article; it defines the operators, gives both readings and the ordering advantage, and derives no spectrum.

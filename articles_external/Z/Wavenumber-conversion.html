What the Paper Does

An encyclopedia article on the wavenumber: the two conventions — cycles per length and radians per length — the angular wavenumber $k=1/\lambda$, the relations to frequency and momentum, and the four different quantities that carry the name in spectroscopy.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The two definitions

"the wavenumber (or wave number), also known as repetency,[1] is the spatial frequency of a wave. Ordinary wavenumber is defined as the number of wave cycles divided by length; it is a physical quantity with dimension of reciprocal length, expressed in SI units of cycles per metre or reciprocal metre (m−1). Angular" The other convention counts radians and carries a factor $2\pi$.

The angular wavenumber

"Angular wavenumber, typically denoted by the letter $k$, [is] defined" number of radians per unit length, $k=2\pi/\lambda$. With $\omega=2\pi\nu$ it gives the dispersion relation $\omega=c k$ for light, and with the de Broglie relation it gives $p=\hbar k$.

The ambiguity

Four quantities share the name in spectroscopy: the ordinary wavenumber, the angular wavenumber, the spectroscopic wavenumber $\tilde\nu=1/\lambda$ in cm$^{-1}$, and the angular frequency divided by $c$. Confusing them produces factors of $2\pi$ and of $c$.

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. The factor $2\pi$ is a convention, not physics

The idea. Cycles per length and radians per length differ by $2\pi$ and both are called wavenumber. The choice changes the form of every formula it appears in, and no experiment distinguishes them.

What it would add. A note for the corpus's Fourier articles, which use $k$ without stating the convention, so a reader cannot check the factors.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-fourier-transform-and-the-dirac-equation-in-biquaternionic-form.md, at the transform's normalisation.

What stands in the way. Nothing structural.

2. $p=\hbar k$ is the bridge between the two pictures

The idea. The wavenumber is the momentum in units of $\hbar$. That identity is what makes the Fourier variable and the mechanical variable the same object, and it is the reason a derivative in position is a multiplication by momentum.

What it would add. The justification for the corpus's replacement of $\partial_x$ by a momentum factor, which its Fourier articles perform as an algebraic step.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-fourier-transform-and-the-dirac-equation-in-biquaternionic-form.md, on the derivative-to-momentum map.

What stands in the way. The corpus's $\hbar$ is a structural constant of the algebra, so the identification $p=\hbar k$ must be checked against how the algebra's imaginary unit acts rather than assumed.

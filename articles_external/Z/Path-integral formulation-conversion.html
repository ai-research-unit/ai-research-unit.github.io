What the Paper Does

An encyclopedia article on the path-integral formulation: the replacement of a unique classical trajectory by a functional integral over all trajectories, the gain in manifest Lorentz covariance over canonical quantization, the relation to the action principle, and the reasons the formulation is now standard.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The construction

"The path-integral formulation of quantum mechanics generalizes the action principle of classical mechanics. It replaces the classical notion of a single, unique classical trajectory for a system with a sum, or functional integral, over an infinity of quantum-mechanically possible trajectories to compute a quantum amplitude."

Why it was adopted

"This formulation has proven crucial to the subsequent development of theoretical physics, because manifest Lorentz covariance (time and space components of quantities enter equations in the same way) is easier to achieve than in the operator formalism of canonical quantization."

That is a technical reason, not an aesthetic one: the operator formalism singles out a time and therefore hides covariance until it is proved back, whereas the integral over paths is a spacetime object from the start.

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. The integral is over histories, so the action is data and not a principle

The idea. The classical path is not assumed; it emerges as the stationary-phase limit. The action enters as the weight of each history. This is a reading of what the corpus's action articles are doing.

What it would add. A reason the corpus may treat the Lagrangian as fundamental and the equations of motion as derived, which its action articles do implicitly.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-path-integral-in-biquaternionic-form.md, at the definition of the measure.

What stands in the way. The integral needs a measure on histories, and in the corpus's algebra no such measure is defined; the theory is written so far at the level of the classical action and its variation.

2. Manifest covariance is a computational advantage, not a physical one

The idea. Both formulations give the same amplitudes. The path integral makes the covariance visible at each step and the operator formalism makes unitarity visible at each step. A result that is hard in one may be easy in the other.

What it would add. A reason the corpus keeps parallel path-integral and operator articles covering the same equations, rather than choosing.

What the Paper Does

An encyclopedia article on the Wigner–Weyl transform: the invertible map between functions on phase space and operators on Hilbert space, the Weyl quantisation rule in one direction, the Wigner function in the other, and the way the operator product becomes the Moyal star product with the commutator giving the Moyal bracket.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The map

"the Wigner–Weyl transform or Weyl–Wigner transform (after Hermann Weyl and Eugene Wigner) is the invertible mapping between functions in the quantum phase space formulation and Hilbert space operators in the Schrödinger picture."

The two directions

"the mapping from functions on phase space to operators is called the Weyl transform or Weyl quantization, whereas the inverse mapping, from operators to functions on phase space, is called the W[igner transform]." The Wigner function is the image of the density matrix, and it is real but not positive, which is why it is a quasi-probability.

The product

The image of the operator product is the star product $f\star g$, whose antisymmetric part is the Moyal bracket. In the limit $\hbar\to0$ the star product reduces to the pointwise product and the Moyal bracket to the Poisson bracket, so the deformation is the quantisation.

"Often the mapping from functions on phase space to operators is called the Weyl transform or Weyl quantization, whereas the inverse mapping, from operators to functions on phase space, is called the Wigner transform."

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. Quantisation is a deformation of the product

The idea. The Weyl map sends the commutative algebra of functions to the non-commutative algebra of operators by deforming the product, not by changing the space. The star product is the deformation, and $\hbar$ measures it. This is a mechanism the corpus can adopt directly, since it already has a non-commutative product with a parameter.

What it would add. A precise sense in which the corpus's algebra "is" a quantisation: the product's non-commutativity is a star product in disguise, and the classical limit is a parameter limit.

Where it lands. articles_physics/relativistic-wigner-function-and-quantum-kinetics.md, at the star product.

What stands in the way. The corpus's product is fixed and exact; showing that it equals a star product to all orders is a strong statement that the corpus's articles assert only for low orders.

2. The quasi-probability is real but not positive

The idea. The Wigner function is the closest phase-space object to a probability distribution, and it fails positivity exactly where quantum interference matters. Negativity is a measure of non-classicality, not an error.

What it would add. A quantitative diagnostic for the corpus's phase-space constructions, which currently have no positivity criterion.

Where it lands. articles_physics/relativistic-wigner-function-and-quantum-kinetics.md.

What stands in the way. The corpus's phase-space function would have to be compared with the Wigner function's normalisation and marginals before negativity could be read off.

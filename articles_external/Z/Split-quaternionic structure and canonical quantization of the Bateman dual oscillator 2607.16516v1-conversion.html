What the Paper Does

Gives a split-quaternionic formulation of Bateman's dual oscillator — a damped oscillator plus an anti-damped adjoint coordinate in a conservative doubled system — that stays consistent from the classical equations through ordinary complex canonical quantisation, and shows that the doubled system's indefinite quadratic form is the split norm.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The system

"Bateman's dual oscillator embeds a damped harmonic oscillator and an independent anti-damped adjoint coordinate in a conservative doubled system. We develop a split-quaternionic formulation that remains consistent from the classical equations through ordinary complex canonical quantization."

"se conserved indefinite quadratic form is the split norm, while the sign of the generator square unifies the underdamped, overdamped, and critical regimes." with a scalar action on the split-complex configuration subalgebra.

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. Damping gives a commutator between velocities

The idea. With dissipation, the two components of the velocity do not commute, and the commutator is $i\hbar m\gamma$. An uncertainty relation follows without any external noise postulate. This is a formula.

What it would add. A mechanism for the corpus's dissipative hydrodynamics, where the non-Hermitian terms are added phenomenologically and no commutator is derived.

Where it lands. articles_physics/causal-dissipative-hydrodynamics-in-biquaternionic-form.md, at the dissipation term.

What stands in the way. The result is derived for the doubled Bateman system; the corpus's dissipation is not doubled, so the derivation would have to be adapted or the doubling justified.

2. The split norm is the doubled system's invariant

The idea. The indefinite quadratic form of the doubled phase space is not a mathematical artefact; it is the split-quaternion norm, and the three damping regimes are the three signs of a generator square.

What it would add. A dictionary between the corpus's norm cone — with its timelike, lightlike and spacelike regions — and the physical underdamped, critical and overdamped cases.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-symplectic-form-and-the-biquaternion-norm-cone.md, at the cone's regions.

What stands in the way. The identification is made in the split quaternions, whose norm has signature $(2,2)$; the corpus's algebra has a different signature, so the dictionary is not literal.

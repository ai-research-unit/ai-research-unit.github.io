What the Paper Does

A long encyclopedia article on the weak interaction: the force responsible for beta decay, its mediation by W and Z bosons, its short range and low strength compared with the strong force, its parity violation, and its unification with electromagnetism in the electroweak theory.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The scope

"the weak interaction, weak force or weak nuclear force, is one of the four known fundamental interactions, with the others being electromagnetism, the strong interaction, and gravitation. It is the mechanism of interaction between subatomic particles that is responsible for the radioactive beta decay of atoms."

The properties

The interaction is mediated by the W and Z bosons, whose large masses confine it to distances of order $10^{-18}$ m. Its coupling is comparable to the electromagnetic one, but the propagator suppression by $m_W^2$ makes the effective strength small at low energy — that separation of coupling from effective strength is the article's most useful point.

The symmetry structure

The weak interaction couples only to left-handed fermions, which is the reason parity is violated. It is described by SU(2) times U(1), broken to the electromagnetic U(1), and the charged current changes flavour while the neutral current does not.

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. Coupling and effective strength are different quantities

The idea. The weak coupling is not small; the boson mass is large. The apparent weakness at low energy is a propagator effect, $g^2/m_W^2$. This is a reading that separates two things often conflated.

What it would add. A precise distinction for the corpus's electroweak articles, which treat the weak sector's smallness as a small coupling.

What stands in the way. The corpus has no boson mass and no propagator in that sense, so the distinction would enter as an external fact.

2. Only left-handed fermions couple

The idea. Chirality is selected by the interaction, not by the fermion's mass. This is the structural fact behind parity violation and the reason the weak sector cannot be parity-symmetric.

What it would add. A statement the corpus's chiral-wall article needs to make contact with experiment.

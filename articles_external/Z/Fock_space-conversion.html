What the Paper Does

An encyclopedia article on Fock space: the algebraic construction of the state space of an unknown number of identical particles from a one-particle Hilbert space, named after Fock's 1932 paper "Configuration space and second quantization", and built as a direct sum of the zero-, one-, two-particle spaces with symmetric or antisymmetric restrictions.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The construction

"The Fock space is an algebraic construction used in quantum mechanics to construct the quantum states space of a variable or unknown number of identical particles from a single particle Hilbert space. It is named after V. A. Fock, who first introduced it in his 1932 paper" Konfigurationsraum und zweite Quantelung " (" Configuration space and second quantization ")."

The direct sum

"Informally, a Fock space is the sum of a set of Hilbert spaces representing zero-particle states, one-particle states, two-particle states, and so on. If the identical particles are bosons, then the $n$-particle states" symmetrised, and for fermions antisymmetrised, so the summands are the symmetric or exterior powers of the one-particle space.

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. The vacuum is a summand, not a state in a summand

The idea. The zero-particle space is one-dimensional and appears as a term of the direct sum. The vacuum is therefore structural, and every other summand is built from it by creation operators.

What it would add. A structural reading of the corpus's algebraic vacuum, which is an idempotent of the algebra. The direct-sum picture says why a vacuum must exist as a separate term rather than as a limit of states with fewer and fewer particles.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-scalar-fock-space-in-biquaternionic-form.md, at the construction.

What stands in the way. The corpus's construction is algebraic rather than Hilbert-space-theoretic, so the direct sum would have to become a decomposition of the algebra.

2. Statistics is a choice of summand

The idea. Bosons and fermions differ only in whether the $n$-particle summand is the symmetric or the antisymmetric power. The dynamics does not enter.

What it would add. A reason the corpus's bosonic and fermionic Fock spaces are parallel articles: the difference is one word, and the rest is copied.

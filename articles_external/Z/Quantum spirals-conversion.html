What the Paper Does

Observes that quantum systems resist hosting vorticity — the Meissner effect expels electromagnetic vorticity, superfluidity confines circulation to quantised topological defects — and shows that vorticity can be imparted to a quantum fluid through a nonlinear mechanism in a hot fluid described by a thermally modified Pauli–Schrödinger spin equation, with a spiral solution worked out explicitly.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The obstruction

"Quantum systems often exhibit fundamental incapability to entertain vortex. The Meissner effect, a complete expulsion of the magnetic field (the electromagnetic vorticity), for instance, is taken to be the defining attribute of the superconducting state. Superfluidity is another, close-parallel example; fluid vorticity can reside only on topological defects with a limited (quantized) amount."

The route around it

"We show that the challenge of imparting vorticity to a quantum fluid can be met through a nonlinear mechanism operating in a hot fluid corresponding to a thermally modified Pauli-Schrödinger spin" equation.

The new ingredient

A "nonlinear, non-Hermitian Hamiltonian, in conjunction with spin vorticity, leads to new interesting quantum states; a spiral solution is explicitly worked out." The non-Hermiticity is doing work here: it allows the flow to carry a rotational component that a Hermitian Hamiltonian's ground state forbids.

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. Vorticity is quantised because the phase is

The idea. The velocity is $\nabla S/m$, so its circulation around a closed loop is $\hbar/m$ times the winding of the phase. That winding is an integer, so circulation is quantised and continuous vorticity is impossible in a single-valued wavefunction. This is a formula with a strong consequence.

What it would add. The reason a quantum fluid cannot be given arbitrary vorticity, stated algebraically rather than as an empirical remark. The corpus's spin hydrodynamics carries a vorticity tensor and does not say where the quantisation enters.

Where it lands. articles_physics/spin-hydrodynamics-in-biquaternionic-form.md, at the vorticity.

What stands in the way. In the corpus's algebra the phase is a component of the decomposition and can be multivalued only through the zero-divisor cone, so the winding number would have to be defined there.

2. A non-Hermitian term is a physical resource, not a defect

The idea. Dissipation or gain can generate rotational flow that a conservative Hamiltonian cannot support. The non-Hermiticity is the mechanism, not an approximation error.

What it would add. A reading for the corpus's dissipative-hydrodynamics article, which treats the non-Hermitian part as a loss term.

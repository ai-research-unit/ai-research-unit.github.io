What the Paper Does

The first lecture of a minicourse on decoupling in effective quantum gravity. It sets out the programme: decoupling at the classical level, nonlocal form factors from Feynman diagrams, the ultraviolet and infrared limits, quantum decoupling, the extraction of form factors in semiclassical gravity by a heat-kernel solution, the renormalisation group in the physical setting, and the running of the cosmological and Newton constants with its implications for the trace anomaly.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The syllabus, as printed

"Decoupling at the classical level. Nonlocal form factors from Feynman diagrams. High energy (UV) and low-energy (IR) limits. Quantum decoupling. Extracting form factors in semiclassical gravity. Derivation using heat-kernel solution. Renormalization group in the physical setting. On the running of cosmological (CC) and Newton constants. Implications for trace (conformal) anomaly."

That list is the lecture's whole structure, and the order is the argument: decoupling is a statement about limits, the limits are computed from form factors, the form factors are got from a heat kernel, and only then does the running of constants make sense.

The reference point

The anchor is "Appelquist and Carazzone (AC) decoupling theorem" — the statement that heavy particles drop out of low-energy physics except through renormalisation of the light couplings. The lecture's question is what becomes of that statement when the heavy field is the graviton.

The claimed content of the method

"Extracting form factors in semiclassical gravity ... Derivation using heat-kernel solution" — the form factors are not guessed but computed from the heat kernel of the relevant operator, which is the technical core the slides build towards.

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. Decoupling is a statement about limits, not about particles

The idea. The theorem says that a heavy field's effects at low energy reduce to a renormalisation of the light sector's parameters. It is a claim about the shape of the low-energy effective action, not about the existence of the heavy particle.

What it would add. A precise form of the corpus's own the high scales drop out claims, which the asymptotic-freedom article asserts through the running of one coupling. Decoupling gives the general reason: every heavy field leaves only a local operator behind.

Where it lands. articles_physics/asymptotic-freedom-and-the-running-of-the-strong-coupling.md, at the step where heavy modes are integrated out.

What stands in the way. The corpus's running is computed in a fixed background; decoupling in gravity needs a statement about the metric itself fluctuating, which the corpus's framework does not quantise.

2. The cosmological constant runs, and that is a problem

The idea. If $\Lambda$ runs with scale, its observed value is a low-energy quantity and the question becomes why it is small at that scale rather than why it is small at all.

What it would add. A sharper statement of the cosmological-constant problem than the corpus's hierarchy discussion: not a fine tuning at one scale but a running between two.

Where it lands. articles_physics/quantum-gravity-in-the-biquaternion-framework-the-two-sided-obstruction-and-the-modular-temperature.md.

What stands in the way. The corpus has no renormalisation group for a dimensionful constant carried by the algebra; the running would have to be defined before the statement can be made.

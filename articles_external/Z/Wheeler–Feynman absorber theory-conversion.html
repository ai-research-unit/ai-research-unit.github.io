What the Paper Does

An encyclopedia article on the Wheeler–Feynman absorber theory: the electrodynamics in which there is no independent field, interactions are half-retarded plus half-advanced action at a distance between charges, and radiation damping emerges only because the future absorber is opaque.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The premise

"The Wheeler–Feynman absorber theory (also called the Wheeler–Feynman time-symmetric theory), named" "is a theory of electrodynamics based on a relativistically correct extension of action at a distance electron particles. The theory postulates no independent electromagnetic field. Rather, the whole theory is encapsulated by the Lorentz-invariant action" of the particles alone.

The symmetric propagator

Each charge's influence is taken as one half of the retarded plus one half of the advanced solution of the wave equation. The antisymmetric combination is what survives once all charges are included, so the observed retarded field is not put in but emerges from the sum.

The role of the absorber

The radiative damping term appears only if the universe is opaque to radiation: the advanced waves from the future absorber must reach the source. In a transparent universe the theory gives no damping, which is the theory's strongest empirical constraint and the reason it is not the standard account.

"Wheeler–Feynman absorber theory The Wheeler–Feynman absorber theory (also called the Wheeler–Feynman time-symmetric theory), named after its originators, the physicists Richard Feynman and John Archibald Wheeler, is a theory of electrodynamics based on a relativistically correct extension of action at a distance electron particles."

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. Radiation damping can be a boundary condition, not a local term

The idea. The damping force is not added to the equation of motion; it follows from the future boundary condition through the advanced half of the interaction. This is a mechanism that changes what "dissipation" means: it is a consequence of the environment, not of the particle.

What it would add. A different origin for the dissipative terms the corpus's non-Hermitian articles insert, and a reason they might be boundary effects rather than algebra.

Where it lands. articles_physics/causal-dissipative-hydrodynamics-in-biquaternionic-form.md, at the damping term.

What stands in the way. The corpus's damping is a term in an equation of motion with no absorber and no future boundary; making the connection would require a cosmological assumption the corpus does not make.

2. Time symmetry is broken by the initial condition, not by the law

The idea. The fundamental interaction is symmetric between retarded and advanced; the arrow appears because the absorbers are in the future. This is a reading with the same shape as the corpus's statement that the algebra is time-symmetric and the solution is not.

What it would add. A precedent for the corpus's treatment of the arrow of time, with a concrete mechanism for how the asymmetry enters.

Where it lands. articles_physics/exercise-the-retarded-potentials-and-the-greens-function.md, on the choice of solution.

What stands in the way. The absorber condition is a global property of the universe; the corpus's articles work locally and would have to import it.

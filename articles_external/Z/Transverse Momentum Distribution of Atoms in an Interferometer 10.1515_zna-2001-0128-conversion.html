What the Paper Does

Solves the stationary Schrödinger equation inside an interferometer and derives the longitudinal and transverse wave functions separately. The longitudinal part is a plane wave; the transverse part is a one-dimensional wave packet built from a superposition of transverse momenta. The transverse momentum distribution turns out to be time independent, fixed by the aperture wave function and therefore independent of the distance from the slits.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The decomposition

"From the stationary solution of Schrödinger's equation in an interferometer we derive the wave functions of the longitudinal and the transverse motion. The former function is a plane wave."

The transverse part

"The wave function of the transverse motion is a one dimensional wave packet representing a superposition of components with various values of the particles transverse momentum. The particles transverse momentum distribution in an interferometer"

The consequence

"Consequently, it is independent of the distance from the slits. As such, it is a very important characteristic of the quantum state. Experimental determination of the momentum distribution would support"

Its keywords are "Atomic Interference; Transfer Momentum Distribution; Wave-particle Duality."

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. The transverse distribution is set at the aperture and never changes

The idea. Once the state is prepared at the aperture, the transverse momentum distribution is fixed, and free propagation only adds a phase. Distance from the slit does not broaden the momentum distribution, only the position distribution. This is a formula that separates two distributions usually discussed together.

What it would add. A clean statement of what is and is not changed by propagation, which the corpus's diffraction and duality articles blur.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-double-slit-experiment-in-biquaternionic-form.md, at the preparation.

What stands in the way. The result holds for the stationary solution of the free equation with an aperture; interactions in flight would change it, and the corpus's treatments do not always assume free propagation.

2. Longitudinal and transverse problems decouple

The idea. The plane wave along the beam and the packet across it solve independent one-dimensional problems. The full three-dimensional state is a product, which is why an interferometer can be analysed one axis at a time. This is a mechanism of separation.

What it would add. A justification for the corpus's habit of working in one spatial dimension, which it usually asserts rather than derives.

Where it lands. articles_physics/wave-particle-duality-and-the-count-of-degrees-of-freedom.md, on the reduction to one dimension.

What stands in the way. The decoupling needs a separable potential; the corpus's equations often have potentials that couple the axes, so the reduction is a choice of model, not a theorem.

This is a five-page contribution; the decomposition, the transverse packet, the time independence and the aperture dependence are its content.

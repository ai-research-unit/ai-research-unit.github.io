What the Paper Does

Removes Lorentz indices from the coupling of spinor fields to gravity. The standard vierbein-and-spin-connection formalism carries three kinds of index — world, Lorentz-vector and Lorentz-spinor — and the paper shows that both Lorentz types can be eliminated by letting the Dirac operator and the Lorentz generators become spacetime-dependent, covariantly so, in the algebra of complexified quaternions and their tensor products.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The standard problem

"Coupling spinor fields to the gravitational field, in the setting of general relativity, is standardly done via the introduction of a vierbein field and the (associated minimal) spin connection field. This makes three types of indices feature in the formalism: world/coordinate indices, Lorentz vector indices, and Lorentz spinor indices, respectively."

The claim

The article shows that this will [be] possible without them, "altogther with the Lorentz indices, both tensorial ones and spinorial ones, obtaining a formalism featuring only world indices. This will be possible by having both the 'Dirac operator' and the generators of 'Lorentz' transformations become spacetime-dependent, although covariantly co[nstant]".

The algebra

The construction is carried out "in the setting of complexified quaternions. 1 Introduction According to standard wisdom, see for instance [1, Sec." and their tensor products. That choice is what makes the replacement possible: the gamma matrices are elements of the algebra rather than a separate structure, so a spacetime-dependent Dirac operator is an algebra-valued field and not a new object.

"In this way, the resulting formalism ends up featuring, somewhat unsatisfactorily, three different types of indices: world indices, Lorentz vector indices, and Lorentz spinor indices, respectively, the latter of course being carried by the spinor field itself."

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. A spacetime-dependent Dirac operator replaces the spin connection

The idea. If the gamma matrices are algebra elements, the frame field can be absorbed into a position-dependent Dirac operator, and the spin connection becomes a derivative of that operator rather than an independent field. The formalism then has world indices only. This is a mechanism.

What it would add. A route from the corpus's flat-space Dirac–Hestenes equation to a curved-space one without introducing a tetrad, which is exactly the step the corpus's gravity articles take by other means.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-dirac-hestenes-equation-and-spacetime-algebra-in-biquaternionic-form.md, at the covariant derivative.

What stands in the way. The construction needs the tensor product of complexified quaternion algebras, which is larger than the corpus's algebra; whether its Dirac operator can be made position-dependent inside the biquaternions alone is not settled by this paper.

2. Counting indices is a test of a formalism

The idea. The objection to the standard coupling is bookkeeping: three index types for one field is a sign that the formalism carries structure the physics does not need. Removing them is a claim that the redundant structure was an artefact of the representation. This is a reading.

What it would add. A criterion the corpus can apply to its own constructions: an index that cannot be removed is physical, one that can is bookkeeping.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-einstein-field-equations-in-the-biquaternion-framework-the-linearised-dictionary-and-the-non-linear-gap.md, on the index bookkeeping.

What stands in the way. The paper's removal works in its own algebra; the corpus would have to repeat the count for its objects before drawing the conclusion.

This is a twenty-page preprint; the three-index problem, the quaternion algebra, the spacetime-dependent operators and the world-index-only result are its content.

What the Paper Does

An encyclopedia article on the WKB method: the semiclassical expansion of a wavefunction written as an exponential, valid when the potential varies slowly compared with the wavelength, the resulting phase integral and amplitude, the connection formulas at turning points, and the quantisation condition it yields.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The method

"the WKB approximation or WKB method is a technique for finding approximate solutions to linear differential equations with spatially varying coefficients. It is typically used for a semiclassical calculation in quantum mechanics in which the wave function is recast as an exponential function, semiclassically expanded, and then either the amplitude or the phase is taken" slowly varying.

The form

Writing $\psi=e^{iS/\hbar}$ and expanding $S$ in powers of $\hbar$, the leading term gives $S_0=\pm\int p\,dx$ with $p=\sqrt{2m(E-V)}$, and the next order gives the amplitude $\propto p^{-1/2}$. The two combine into $\psi\sim p^{-1/2}\exp(\pm\frac{i}{\hbar}\int p\,dx)$.

The validity condition

The approximation holds where $\hbar|dp/dx|\ll p^2$, that is, where the de Broglie wavelength changes slowly over a wavelength. It fails at turning points, where $p\to0$ and the amplitude diverges; the connection formulas relate the oscillating and decaying solutions across such a point.

The quantisation condition

Applying the connection formulas at both turning points gives $\oint p\,dx=2\pi\hbar(n+\tfrac12)$, the Bohr–Sommerfeld rule with the half-integer offset that the Maslov index supplies.

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. The expansion is in

$\hbar$ with the action as the organising object

The idea. The WKB series is a power series in $\hbar$ whose coefficients are determined by the classical trajectory. The lowest order is the classical action, so the classical limit is the leading term and not a separate theory. This is a mechanism the corpus uses and rarely states.

What it would add. A clean statement of what the corpus's semiclassical expansion is an expansion in, and why the action appears at leading order.

2. Turning points are where the approximation must be patched

The idea. The expansion fails exactly where the classical motion reverses, and the physics there is carried by the connection formulas rather than by the series. A semiclassical result is therefore only as good as its patching.

What it would add. A warning the corpus's instanton articles need: the patching contributes the Maslov phase, which is a physical shift in the spectrum.

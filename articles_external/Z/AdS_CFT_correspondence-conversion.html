What the Paper Does

An encyclopedia survey of the anti-de Sitter / conformal field theory correspondence: the statement of the duality, its origin in Maldacena's 1997 paper and the two companion papers, the reason it is useful (strong–weak), the canonical example of $\mathcal{N}=4$ super Yang–Mills against type IIB strings on $\mathrm{AdS}_5\times S^5$, and the holographic principle behind it. No derivations; it is a map of the field with the standard attributions.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The statement

"the anti-de Sitter/conformal field theory correspondence (frequently abbreviated as AdS/CFT) is a conjectured relationship between two kinds of physical theories. On one side are anti-de Sitter spaces (AdS) that are used in theories of quantum gravity, formulated in terms of string theory or M-theory. On the other side of the correspondence are conformal field theories (CFT) that are quantum field theories, including theories similar to the Yang–Mills theories that describe elementary particles."

Why it matters, in the article's own order

"it provides a non-perturbative formulation of string theory with certain boundary conditions and because" "it is the most successful realization of the holographic principle, an idea in quantum gravity originally proposed by Gerard 't Hooft and promoted by Leonard Susskind. It" Then the practical reason: "Much of the usefulness of the duality results from the fact that it is a strong–weak duality: when the fields of the quantum field theory are strongly interacting, the ones in the gravitational theory are weakly interacting and thus more mathematically tractable."

The canonical case

"One of the most prominent examples of the AdS/CFT correspondence has been the AdS5/CFT4 correspondence: a relation between N = 4 supersymmetric Yang–Mills theory in 3+1 dimensions and type IIB superstring theory on AdS5 × S5.[5] Background Quantum gravity and strings"

The attribution

"The AdS/CFT correspondence was first proposed by Juan Maldacena in late 1997.[3] Important aspects of the correspondence were soon elaborated on in two articles, one by Steven Gubser, Igor Klebanov and Alexander Polyakov, and another by Edward Witten. By 2015, Maldacena's article had over 10,000 citations, becoming the most highly cited article in the field of high energy physics."

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. A duality that maps strong to weak is a calculational device, not an identity

The idea. The correspondence is useful precisely because the two sides are at opposite coupling. That means the map is between descriptions at different values of a parameter, so a claim transferred across it is a claim about the same object read in two regimes.

What it would add. A category the corpus lacks. The corpus's dualities (electric–magnetic, Dirac–topologically massive) are exact algebraic equivalences at the same coupling; AdS/CFT is a strong–weak correspondence, and the article's phrasing makes the difference explicit.

Where it lands. articles_physics/electric-magnetic-duality-and-the-modular-group-in-biquaternionic-form.md, as a contrast case in the taxonomy of dualities.

What stands in the way. The corpus has no boundary, no conformal field theory on one, and no notion of a coupling that runs between the two sides; the entry would be a contrast only.

2. Holography needs a boundary, and the corpus's spacetime has none

The idea. The correspondence places a conformal field theory on the boundary of an anti-de Sitter bulk, with one fewer dimension. The dimensionality mismatch is the content.

What it would add. A precise reason the corpus's quantum gravity programme cannot borrow the holographic route: its spacetime is a metric carried by the algebra, with no conformal boundary at infinity on which to put a dual theory.

Where it lands. articles_physics/quantum-gravity-in-the-biquaternion-framework-the-two-sided-obstruction-and-the-modular-temperature.md, among the obstructions.

What stands in the way. An anti-de Sitter metric with a timelike conformal boundary would have to be exhibited in the algebra first; nothing in the corpus does that, and the article gives no construction to copy.

This is an encyclopedia article; everything in it is attribution, and the conjectural status of the correspondence is stated in its first sentence.

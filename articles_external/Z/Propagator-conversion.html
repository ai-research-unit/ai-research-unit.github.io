What the Paper Does

An encyclopedia article on the propagator: the amplitude for a particle to go from one place to another in a given time, or to carry a given energy and momentum, its role as the inverse of the wave operator, its appearance as the internal line of a Feynman diagram, and the distinction between the retarded, advanced and Feynman propagators.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The definition

"the propagator is a function that specifies the probability amplitude for a particle to travel from one place to another in a given period of time, or to travel with a certain energy and momentum."

The two faces

"In Feynman diagrams, which serve to calculate the rate of collisions in quantum field theory, virtual particles contribute their propagator to the rate of the scattering event described by the respective diagram. Propagators may also be viewed as the inverse of the wave operator appropriate to the particle, and are, therefore, often called (ca[usal]..." Green's functions.

Those two faces are the same object: the kernel of the inverse operator, and the sum over paths between two points. The article's value is that it presents them as one.

Which inverse

The inverse of a differential operator is not unique without a boundary condition, and the choice of condition selects the propagator: retarded for a source switched on in the past, advanced for one switched on in the future, and the Feynman one for the path-integral contour. Each is a different Green's function of the same operator.

"Propagators in Feynman diagrams The most common use of the propagator is in calculating probability amplitudes for particle interactions using Feynman diagrams."

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. A propagator is defined by its pole prescription

The idea. The operator has a continuum of inverses; the physical one is fixed by how the poles are circumvented. The $i\epsilon$ is not a technicality but the definition. This is a reading.

What it would add. A statement of what distinguishes the corpus's propagators from each other, which its Feynman-propagator article derives but does not state as a rule.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-feynman-propagator-in-biquaternionic-form.md, at the contour.

What stands in the way. The corpus's propagator is built from the algebra's own resolvent, and the pole prescription has to be given in terms of the algebra's spectrum, which is not a set of real numbers in general.

2. Retarded, advanced and Feynman differ by a homogeneous solution

The idea. Three Green's functions of one operator differ by a solution of the homogeneous equation, so the difference is not small and not a correction; it is a choice of what is counted as a particle and what as an antiparticle.

What it would add. A structural account of the particle-antiparticle reading, which the corpus states as a convention.

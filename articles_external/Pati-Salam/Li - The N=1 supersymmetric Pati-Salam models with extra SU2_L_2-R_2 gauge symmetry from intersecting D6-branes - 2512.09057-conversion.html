What the Paper Does

It adds a fourth stack of branes and gets rid of the filler branes.

"By introducing an extra stack of D6-branes to standard N = 1 supersymmetric PatiSalam models, we extend the landscape of its complete search." "e d-stack of D6-branes is introduced besides the standard a, b, c-stacks. More"

That last result is the important one: the fourth stack replaces the fillers.

Summary in the Authors' Words

What the Fourth Stack Does

In the standard construction, three stacks $a$, $b$, $c$ carry $U(4)_C$, $U(2)_L$, $U(2)_R$. The RR tadpoles must cancel, and the leftover charge is absorbed by filler branes — $USp(2N)$ stacks on orientifold planes.

Adding a $d$-stack changes the accounting: the $d$-branes carry their own charge, so fewer fillers are needed, and in some models none.

And the $d$-stack's gauge group becomes an extra $SU(2)$, which must be broken. **"ively. The SU (2)L1 /R1 × SU (2)L2 /R2 can be broken down to the diagonal SU (2)L/R gauge symmetry via bifundamental Higgs fields."

** — a Higgs in the bifundamental $(\mathbf2,\mathbf2)$ gets a VEV and the diagonal survives.

That is the standard left-right-symmetric breaking pattern, applied to the extra $SU(2)$.

The corpus should record the trade: a new stack removes fillers and their exotics, but adds an extra $SU(2)$ and its breaking sector. The net exotic count improves, as the paper says.

Why This Is a Real Advance

The filler branes are the cluster's main source of unwanted states. 2303.02864 struck that the hidden sector is not decoupled; 2212.09644 found one model with no fillers; this paper constructs a whole class without them.

So the three papers together solve the filler problem: - 2212.09644: one filler-free model in the symplectic branch. - Here: a systematic construction of filler-free models.

The corpus should present this as the cluster's answer to its own exotic problem — not by decoupling the exotics, but by removing their source.

And that is a general lesson: the exotics came from the tadpole bookkeeping, so changing the bookkeeping removes them. A field theorist would not think of that, because in field theory there are no tadpoles to cancel.

The 28 Classes

Twenty-eight, against 33. So the extra stack gives a comparable-sized landscape, and the total grows to at least 61.

And the gauge group is different: $SU(4)_C\times SU(2)_L\times SU(2)_{R1}\times SU(2)_{R2}$ — four factors, rank 7 instead of Pati–Salam's rank 6.

The corpus should note the rank change, because of the swampland rank bound (2505.03664): a higher rank is more likely to be in the swampland. So the filler-free models may pay a rank penalty. The paper does not discuss it, and the corpus should flag the question.

The Diagonal Breaking

"broken down to the diagonal $SU(2)_{L/R}$"

The breaking $SU(2)_1\times SU(2)_2\to SU(2)_{\rm diag}$ is by a bifundamental VEV, $\langle\Phi\rangle=v\,I_2$. The resulting coupling is $1/g_{\rm diag}^2=1/g_1^2+1/g_2^2$, the same relation as 2511.16565's canonical normalisation issue.

So the two 2025 papers share the technical problem: the diagonal coupling is a sum of inverses, and its normalisation matters for unification. The corpus should note the connection.

One observation about the corpus's map

This paper and 2511.16565 are the cluster's two 2025 directions, and they are different in kind.

2511.16565 relaxes an assumption — the generation condition — and finds more of the same kind of model. Here, a new ingredient is added — a fourth stack — and a new kind of model appears.

The second is the more consequential, because it changes what a "model" is: from three stacks plus fillers to four stacks plus breaking. That is a different framework, and its 28 classes are not a subset of the 33.

The corpus should present the cluster's landscape as a union of frameworks, not a single count: - three stacks, fillers (33 + hidden variants) - three stacks, one symplectic (5) - four stacks, no fillers (28) - rigid cycles, discrete torsion (a class) - four-family (280)

Each framework has its own consistency conditions and its own best model. The Pati–Salam landscape from D-branes is a union, and any single number for it is a statement about one framework.

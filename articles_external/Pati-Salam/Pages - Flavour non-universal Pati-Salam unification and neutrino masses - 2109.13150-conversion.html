What the Paper Does

It compresses the three-site model into four pages and states the one new prediction.

"The $B$ anomalies, by their distinctive flavour structure i.e. $U(2)^5$, bring a new piece to the long-standing flavour puzzle."

"The three-site flavour non-universal Pati-Salam (PS) model ... provides through the ratios of vacuum expectation values (VEV) acquired at different scales, a combined explanation of the charged and neutral current $B$ anomalies as well as of the mass hierarchies of the Standard Model."

"the inverse seesaw mechanism is realised, with the minimal addition of three fermion singlets $S_L^{(i)}$ and where $U(1)_F$ fermion number is broken dynamically via new singlet scalars $\Phi_i$, yielding an anarchic light neutrino mass matrix in consistency with data, despite the $U(2)^5$ flavour symmetry observed in the Yukawa matrices."

"A prediction of this model is PMNS unitarity violation"

"The full model finds a natural 5D interpretation with three (almost equidistant) defects in a warped extra dimension, where the exponential hierarchies in VEV ratios arise from $O(1)$ differences in the 5D field bulk masses."

So the new content is the dynamical $U(1)_F$ breaking and the unitarity-violation prediction.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The VEV Ratio Mechanism

"through the ratios of VEVs acquired at different scales"

This is the mechanism the corpus should record precisely. Each site has its own PS-breaking and EW-breaking VEVs, and the ratios of VEVs at different sites control the effective Yukawa couplings.

Because the sites are connected by weak links, a Yukawa coupling generated at one site appears at another with a suppression equal to the link mixing, which is itself a ratio of VEVs.

So the hierarchy is: exponential in the number of links, controlled by $O(1)$ differences in 5d bulk masses.

And that is not a family symmetry: nothing is imposed; the structure follows from where the fields sit and how strongly the links mix them.

The corpus should make the distinction explicit, because it is the difference between the two halves of the flavour literature: symmetry-based (FN, modular, discrete) and localisation-based (this).

Both reproduce the data; they differ in what they explain. Symmetry-based models say "the Yukawa has this form because a symmetry allows it"; localisation says "because the fields are here".

Dynamical $U(1)_F$

"$U(1)_F$ fermion number is broken dynamically via new singlet scalars $\Phi_i$"

The inverse seesaw needs a small lepton-number-violating mass $\mu$. Here $\mu$ comes from a scalar's VEV, and the scalar is a singlet whose $U(1)_F$ charge is broken spontaneously.

So the smallness of $\mu$ is a hierarchy of scales, not a tuning, which is the standard way to make the inverse seesaw natural.

The corpus should note it: the inverse seesaw's $\mu$ is often put in by hand; here it is generated. That is a genuine improvement on the minimal version.

The PMNS Unitarity Prediction

"A prediction of this model is PMNS unitarity violation"

Why: the inverse seesaw's heavy singlets mix with the light neutrinos, so the $3\times3$ PMNS matrix extracted from oscillation data is not unitary — it is a sub-block of a larger matrix.

The deviation is of order $m_D^2/M^2$, so for TeV-scale singlets and electroweak-scale Dirac masses it is $10^{-3}$–$10^{-4}$.

And it is testable: oscillation experiments measure the unitarity triangle's closure; beta decay and muon decay measure the weak couplings of the light neutrinos.

The corpus should record the number and the test, because the batch has almost no falsifiable neutrino predictions — most PS models either predict nothing new or predict a rate below reach.

The Warped Geometry Again

"three (almost equidistant) defects in a warped extra dimension"

"Almost equidistant" is a specific statement: the three sites are equally spaced, which makes the link mixings comparable and the hierarchy a power of a single ratio.

That is the minimal deconstruction, and it means the model has one new parameter (the spacing) plus the bulk masses.

The corpus should note the economy: a three-site model with equidistant sites has very few parameters for what it explains.

One observation about the corpus's map

This four-page proceedings contains one sentence the corpus should quote and one it should correct.

Quote: "a prediction of this model is PMNS unitarity violation." That is the batch's cleanest falsifiable neutrino statement.

Correct: the summary sentence, "yielding an anarchic light neutrino mass matrix ... despite the $U(2)^5$ flavour symmetry." The word "despite" is doing heavy lifting: the model has $U(2)^5$ in the Yukawa sector and anarchy in the neutrino sector, and that combination is a choice, not an output. The corpus should say so, because it is the one place where the model's elegance is purchased by hand.

And the corpus should note what makes the proceedings worth keeping: it states the prediction more clearly than the full paper, because a four-page summary must commit.

That is a general observation about proceedings, and the corpus should use them for exactly that — the committed claim, without the hedging.

The finite algebra that most nearly matches the Standard Model is $$ \mathcal A_F=J_3(\mathbb C)\oplus J_2(\mathbb C)\oplus S_2\oplus\mathbb R, $$ represented by an $8\times8$ block-diagonal Hermitian matrix, with the fermions of one generation in an $8\times8$ block-off-diagonal matrix and $J$ equal to ordinary Hermitian conjugation. Enlarging to $$ \mathcal A_F=J_2^L(\mathbb C)\oplus J_2^R(\mathbb C)\oplus J_4(\mathbb C) $$ gives Pati–Salam, $SU(2)_L\times SU(2)_R\times SU(4)$, with the same $8\times8$ representation.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The Three Strategies

The paper sets out two long-standing goals and, for each, one standard strategy and its defect. For unifying gravity with the other fields, the Kaluza–Klein strategy takes spacetime to be $M_4\times M_{\rm int}$ with $M_{\rm int}$ a manifold. Its defect is structural: because $\mathcal A_{\rm int}=C^\infty(M_{\rm int})$ is infinite-dimensional, the picture "predicts an infinite collection of 4D fields (in contrast to the finite handful of fields that we actually observe)", and stabilising $M_{\rm int}$ is hard. The modification is to make $M_{\rm int}$ finite, so that "or" finite "space MF , so that the ∞-dimensional algebra Aint = C∞ (Mint ) is replaced by a finite-dimensional algebra AF , and the predicted collection of 4D fields becomes finite.". The paper rejects "deconstruction — a lattice — because the algebraic/symmetry structure of the standard model is not properly reflected in the geometry of such an internal space."

For unifying gravity with quantum mechanics, the strategy is non-commutative geometry. Its defect is the one quoted above: it quantises the wrong space. The modification is to keep the Hermitian operators and drop the rest:

$$ H=\tfrac12(O+O^\dagger),\qquad A=\tfrac12(O-O^\dagger), $$

where the anti-Hermitian part "close under the commutator product [A1 , A2 ]" "to form a Lie algebra, while the Hermitian operators close 1 There is a sense in which spacetime coordinates" "to form a Jordan algebra. (Appendix A includes a brief introduction to Jordan and Lie algebras." Why carry "all this excess, unobservable baggage"? Jordan showed quantum mechanics can be written in terms of the Hermitian algebra alone.

The paper is careful that this second argument is a motivation and not a derivation: it "e originally led to Jordan geometry by a completely different line of argument", and "in principle, it may even turn out that our Jordan geometry is not quantum mechanical at all".

The Barrett Form, Which Everything Rests

On

The review section presents the non-commutative Standard Model in Barrett's form, and this is the technical hinge. The finite algebra is $\mathcal A_F=M_3(\mathbb C)\oplus\mathbb H\oplus\mathbb C$, with $a=(m,q,z)$, and it acts on $\mathcal H_F=\mathbb C^{96}$ — "96 corresponds to the total number of fermionic degres of freedom in the standard model" "after including particles and anti-particles, left and right chiralities, right-handed neutrinos, 3 colors, and 3 families); •". The representation is the $8\times8$ block-diagonal matrix

$$ \pi(a)=\begin{pmatrix}q&&&\\&q_z&&\\&&m&\\&&&z\end{pmatrix}, \qquad q=\begin{pmatrix}\alpha&\beta\\-\bar\beta&\bar\alpha\end{pmatrix}, \qquad q_z=\begin{pmatrix}z&0\\0&\bar z\end{pmatrix}, $$

and the fermions are the $8\times8$ block-off-diagonal matrix whose upper-right block holds "e 16 Weyl spinors in a single generation" and whose lower-left block holds the antiparticles, with $J\psi=\psi^\dagger$.

Why this form matters is stated in a footnote: Barrett's contribution "tribution was to notice that HF could be elegantly represented as an 8 × 8 matrix (rather than as an 8 × 4 matrix as in [23]): if one does so, then J becomes ordinary hermitian conjugation, and the algebra is also represented by an 8 × 8 matrix, whose left and right representations on HF is simply given by ordinary matrix multiplication. 6 simply take three copies of this 8 × 8 representation."

The generation, written out with its entries named, is

$$ \psi=\begin{pmatrix} u_L&u_L&u_L&\nu_L\\ d_L&d_L&d_L&e_L\\ u_R&u_R&u_R&\nu_R\\ d_R&d_R&d_R&e_R\\ \bar u_L&\bar d_L&\bar u_R&\bar d_R\\ \bar u_L&\bar d_L&\bar u_R&\bar d_R\\ \bar u_L&\bar d_L&\bar u_R&\bar d_R\\ \bar\nu_L&\bar e_L&\bar\nu_R&\bar e_R \end{pmatrix}, $$

with "red, green and blue are" "the three quark colors, and", the chirality operator $\gamma$ flipping the sign of the left-handed entries and of the right-handed antifermions, and the gauge group arising as the inner automorphisms, "generated by commutators $[\pi(a),\cdot]$".

Two remarks in this section are worth carrying away. The first: the inner automorphisms include the trace part of $\pi(a)$, which "yields an extra (unwanted, anomalous) gauge symmetry which must be removed by hand, and the so-called 'unimodularity condition' was introduced precisely for this reason." The second: $\mathcal B_F$ "also has one other anomaly-free outer automorphism, corresponding to $U(1)_{B-L}$".

Why the Spectral Action Is Rejected

Flatly: "we suspect this expression is incorrect, for both experimental and theoretical reasons: from the experimental standpoint, it predicts relationships between standard model couplings (including gravitational couplings) which disagree with observations, and from the theoretical standpoint, these relations are also not preserved by the known RG flow of the standard model (regarded as an effective field theory)."

The Jordan Algebra for the Standard Model

The classification used is Jordan–von Neumann–Wigner: the finite-dimensional Euclidean Jordan algebras are

$$ S_n,\quad J_n(\mathbb R),\quad J_n(\mathbb C),\quad J_n(\mathbb H),\quad J_3(\mathbb O), $$

with $J_n(\mathbb K)$ the Hermitian $n\times n$ matrices over the normed division algebra $\mathbb K$, product the anticommutator; $S_n$ the spin factor on $\mathbb R\oplus\mathbb R^n$; and the isomorphisms $J_1(\mathbb K)\simeq\mathbb R$, $J_2(\mathbb K)\simeq S_{\dim\mathbb K+1}$. Corresponding to the Jordan algebras (10), we have the automorphism groups $\mathrm{Spin}(n)$, $SO(n)$, $SU(n)$, $Sp(n)$ and $F_4$, respectively.

The requirements on $\mathcal A_F$ are listed as three: representable by an $8\times8$ block-diagonal Hermitian matrix acting by anticommutator on $\psi$; inner automorphism group $SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1)$; and commuting with $\gamma$. "It is now straightforward to check that, up to isomorphism, there is a unique algebra/representation that does the trick":

$$ \mathcal A_F=J_3(\mathbb C)\oplus J_2(\mathbb C)\oplus S_2\oplus\mathbb R, \qquad \pi(a)=\mathrm{diag}(c_2,\,s_2,\,c_3,\,r). $$

The inner automorphisms are not commutators. Because $\mathcal A_F$ is Jordan, "algebra, the inner automorphisms of BF are not generated by commutators with elements π(a), but rather by associators with pairs of elements π(a) and π(a′ ) – i.e. by terms of the form [π(a), , π(a′ )] = {{π(a), }, π(a′ )} − {π(a), { , π(a′ )}}, where a, a′ ∈ AF . These generate the desired gauge group, SU (3) × SU (2) × U (1).".

What Comes Out, and What Has to Be Added

With only the inner automorphisms, the fermions break into six representations with $U(1)$ charges

$$ q_L:0,\quad u_R:+\tfrac12,\quad d_R:-\tfrac12,\quad \ell_L:0,\quad \nu_R:+\tfrac12,\quad e_R:-\tfrac12, $$

"which does not agree with the observed charge assignments in the standard model". The outer automorphism fixes it: adding $U(1)_{B-L}$ with charges $\tfrac13,\tfrac13,\tfrac13,-1,-1,-1$ gives a table in which "the third column is just a linear combination of hypercharge and baryonminus-lepton number, X = Y + (B − L)/2, so".

So the geometry predicts an extension: "an extra U (1)B−L gauge symmetry, and two extra bosonic fields: the new U (1)B−L gauge boson, and a new complex scalar fields ϕ which does not couple to SU (3) × SU (2) × U (1)Y , but does carry charge +2 under U (1)B−L (and is responsible".

One advantage over non-commutative geometry is claimed here. Because the algebra is Hermitian, there is no anti-Hermitian trace part to generate an anomalous extra $U(1)$, "so no new rules are needed to excise them".

Pati–Salam

Enlarge the block structure to three factors and require the same properties:

$$ \mathcal A_F=J_2^L(\mathbb C)\oplus J_2^R(\mathbb C)\oplus J_4(\mathbb C), \qquad \pi(a)=\mathrm{diag}(c^L_2,\,c^R_2,\,c_4), $$

with $\psi$ in $(\mathbf 4,\mathbf 2,\mathbf 1)$ and $(\mathbf 4,\mathbf 1,\mathbf 2)$ plus conjugates. "One can check that the automorphism group is now SU (2)L × SU (2)R × SU (4), and that the components", and "the previous section's finite algebra (and representation) are contained within this section's". The verdict: "This is a particularly interesting (and experimentally viable) extension of the standard model, the Pati-Salam model, which is seen to arise very neatly and naturally as a Jordan geometry."

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

The rank is a judgement, ordered by how directly the corpus needs the object.

  1. The fermion is a matrix and

$J$ is the dagger — said exactly

The idea. One generation is an $8\times8$ matrix, block-off-diagonal, with particles in the upper block and antiparticles in the lower; the algebra is the block-diagonal $8\times8$ matrix; $J\psi=\psi^\dagger$.

What it would add. The precise form of the corpus's central structural claim, in a framework with published axioms behind it. The corpus asserts that the fermion is a matrix — that a biquaternion, or a $2\times2$ array of them, carries a generation — and states it as a property of its own algebra. Here is the same statement made about the finite Hilbert space of the Standard Model: the sixteen Weyl spinors are literally the entries of a matrix, the antiparticles are the transpose block, and the real structure is Hermitian conjugation. No bimodule, no left–right action, no grading bookkeeping. The corpus's claim is not idiosyncratic; it is a known presentation.

Where it lands. articles_physics/the-spectral-triple-of-the-biquaternion-algebra.md, in the section on the real structure, and articles_physics/introduction-to-the-biquaternion-universe.md where the matrix picture is introduced.

What stands in the way. Barrett's matrix is $8\times8$ over $\mathbb C$ for one generation, and the corpus's carrier is a biquaternion, which is $2\times2$ over $\mathbb C$ — four times smaller. So the corpus's matrix is a quarter of Barrett's, and the article would need to say which quarter: the upper-left particle block, plausibly, with the antiparticle block obtained by conjugation. That is a real comparison and it is worth making explicit, because a factor of $4$ in a carrier is the difference between "the same construction" and a similar construction.

  1. Replace the commutator by the anticommutator, and the algebra becomes the observables

The idea. The Hermitian operators do not close under composition but do close under $\tfrac12\{a,b\}$. Take that as the algebra. Then the derivation of the gauge group changes from commutators to associators, and the trace part that forced the unimodularity condition disappears.

What it would add. A cleaner foundation for the corpus's own split. The corpus's biquaternion $\mathbb B$ decomposes into a real scalar–pseudoscalar part and a vector part, and its state pairing is taken on the Hermitian sector. That is the same instinct — keep the Hermitian part, discard the rest — arrived at by a different route, and this paper supplies the argument that the instinct is forced rather than aesthetic: the anti-Hermitian part is a Lie algebra, the Hermitian part is a Jordan algebra, and only the second is observable.

Where it lands. The spectral-triple article's axiom section, and articles_physics/anomalies-and-anomaly-cancellation-in-the-four-colours-left-right-model.md, where the pairing on the Hermitian sector is used to compute anomaly coefficients. If a Jordan algebra is the right carrier, the anomaly computation has a new justification.

What stands in the way. The corpus's multiplication is associative and it uses that constantly. Jordan algebras are not associative, and any calculation that relies on regrouping products would need redoing. This is the single largest technical obstacle to the import, and it should be stated as one.

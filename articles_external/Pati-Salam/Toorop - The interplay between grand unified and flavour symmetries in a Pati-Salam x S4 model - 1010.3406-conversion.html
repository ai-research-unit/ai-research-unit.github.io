What the Paper Does

"Both discrete flavour symmetries and Grand Unified symmetries explain apparent structures in the mass sector of the Standard Model. A model that combines both symmetries is therefore very appealing."

"

However, the combination of the two symmetries leads to new complications in the Higgs sector and in the running of the renormalisation group equations."

Two sentences: it works, and it costs. The corpus should quote both.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The Claim

**"many observable relations between the masses of the quarks and leptons"

** — the $S_4\times PS$ structure relates the sectors, so a single set of flavon VEVs fits $m_u,m_c,m_t$, $m_d,m_s,m_b$, $m_e,m_\mu,m_\tau$, the CKM, and the PMNS.

That is the attraction of combining a GUT with a flavour group: the GUT relates the sectors, and the flavour group reduces the parameters. Together they over-determine the fit, which is what makes it predictive.

The corpus should record the logic, because it is the argument for these models and it is sound: relations without a symmetry are accidents; symmetries without relations are unfalsifiable.

The Two Complications

1. The Higgs sector. Breaking $S_4$ needs flavons, and in a SUSY model the flavons must not destabilise the GUT-scale vacuum, and their VEVs must be aligned. That is a large sector with a nontrivial potential.

2. The RGE running. The GUT relations hold at the GUT scale, and the flavour fit is at the weak scale, so the running between them must be computed with the model's spectrum.

And the two interact: the flavon sector changes the running, so the fit and the running cannot be done separately.

The corpus should record this as the batch's clearest statement of a methodological difficulty, because it is the reason many flavour-and-GUT models are incomplete.

The Predictivity in the Neutrino Sector

"

it is predictive in the neutrino sector"

Because the see-saw's structure is fixed by the flavour symmetry: the right-handed neutrino mass matrix's pattern is determined by the $S_4$ charges, so the light masses and mixings are predicted, not fitted.

That is genuine predictivity, and the corpus should note it: the neutrino sector is where the model has the fewest free parameters and the most data.

And that is why the TB mixing prediction was significant, and why its failure is significant.

One observation about the corpus's map

This proceedings and 1003.4502 together make one point the corpus should carry into its flavour chapter.

The point: a combined GUT-and-flavour model is not a flavour model with a GUT bolted on. The two symmetries constrain each other, and the constraints show up in the Higgs sector and the running.

So the corpus's flavour comparison should ask two questions of every model: 1. Does it relate quarks and leptons? (a GUT question) 2. Does it reduce the parameters predictively? (a flavour question)

And a third: 3. Does it survive the running between the two scales?

Models that answer only one are incomplete. The corpus should say that, and mark which models answer which.

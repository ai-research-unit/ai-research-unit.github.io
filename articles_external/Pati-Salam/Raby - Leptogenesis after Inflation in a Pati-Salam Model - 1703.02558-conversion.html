Summary in the Authors' Words

Subcritical Hybrid Inflation

Standard hybrid inflation: the inflaton rolls, and at a critical value the waterfall field destabilises and ends inflation.

**"Subcritical"

** means inflation begins after the waterfall field has started moving — the trajectory starts on the waterfall's slope. Why it matters: the waterfall field is the PS-breaking field, so its motion during inflation means the PS breaking happens during inflation, and the monopoles are diluted. The corpus should record the phrase and the mechanism, because it is the third variant of the inflation dilutes monopoles solution (after 1108.1771's simultaneous breaking and 1207.3730's). And it is the cleanest: the waterfall field breaks PS, so the monopoles are diluted. One sentence, one mechanism.

The $10^{16}$ GeV Scale **

"an energy density during inflation of order a GUT scale, 1016 GeV. In the Pati-Salam [PS] model, this is the scale where Pati-Salam"

** So the inflationary scale is the PS scale. No new scale. And the corpus should compare with hep-ph/0302216's window ($M_c<10^{14}$ GeV): $10^{16}$ is above it. So a tension exists, and the corpus should note it: the inflationary scale implied by the tensor-to-scalar ratio is higher than the minimal-SUSY PS window. 1703.02558 chooses $10^{16}$; hep-ph/0302216 allows at most $10^{14}$. The two cannot both be right in the same model. That is a quantitative conflict the corpus can surface, and no paper in the batch states it.

The Sign Problem

**

"the decay of the heaviest right-handed neutrinos produces the correct sign of the baryon number asymmetry, while the lighter two generations produce the wrong sign."

** That is a real problem: in the $D_4$ model, the CP asymmetries $\epsilon_i$ have signs $\epsilon_3<0$, $\epsilon_{1,2}>0$, so $N_3$'s decays give the right baryon asymmetry and $N_1$, $N_2$'s give the wrong one. So the total asymmetry can cancel or flip, depending on the washout. The corpus should record the phenomenon: hierarchical models often have $\epsilon_1>0$ and $\epsilon_2<0$ (or vice versa), and the sign of the asymmetry depends on which dominates. It is rarely discussed, and it is a genuine constraint.

Instant Preheating

The fix: **the inflaton couples to a field

$\chi$ through $\frac{1}{2}\alpha^2\phi^2\chi^2$**, and as $\phi$ passes through zero the $\chi$ mass vanishes, so $\chi$ particles are produced non-adiabatically. Then $\chi$ decays to the right-handed neutrinos, and the resulting spectrum is non-thermal. **"Instant preheating"

** (Felder–Garcia-Bellido–Linde) is the mechanism, and its use here is to select the heaviest $N$'s contribution. The corpus should record it, because it is a genuinely different reheating mechanism from the perturbative decay in 1108.1771/1207.3730, and it changes the predicted asymmetry. And note the corpus's gain: three PS cosmology papers, three reheating mechanisms — perturbative decay (1108.1771), two-neutrino decay (1207.3730), instant preheating (here). The corpus should present them together.

The Low-Energy Discrepancy

"

The biggest discrepancy is in the fits for the up and down quark masses which are too large."

That is the $D_4$ model's known problem (hep-ph/0409098): the up and down masses come out too large.

The corpus's conversion of hep-ph/0409098 says the same, so the two are consistent — and the corpus should cite the talk as confirmation from the model's author.

One observation about the corpus's map

This talk is the batch's best short statement of the PS cosmology programme, and the corpus should use it as the entry point.

It says, in order: the model fits the data (with one flaw), the inflationary scale is the PS scale, the waterfall dilutes the monopoles, the leptogenesis has a sign problem, and instant preheating fixes it.

That is a complete arc in twelve pages, and it is the arc the corpus's cosmology section should follow.

And the talk is honest about the flaw: the up and down masses are too large. The corpus should note that the same author says so, which strengthens the corpus's own criticism of hep-ph/0409098.

The corpus's cosmology section can then be built around three questions: does the model inflate? does it dilute the monopoles? does it make the right baryon asymmetry? 1703.02558 answers yes, yes, and yes-with-a-fix. 1108.1771 answers yes, yes, and yes-with-a-heavy-gravitino. 2601.14580 answers yes, no, and no. That is a comparison worth making.

What the Paper Does

A second conference version of the twin Pati–Salam, given at DISCRETE 2022. Its distinctive content is a compact statement of the GIM-like mechanism and the common origin of Yukawa and leptoquark couplings.

"I present a model which can simultaneously explain and connect the flavour hierarchies of the Standard Model with flavour anomalies in $B$-physics. I will briefly introduce the model and highlight the main features, including a common origin of Yukawa couplings and vector leptoquark $U_1$ couplings to Standard Model fermions. A GIM-like mechanism allows for large leptoquark couplings which can explain the $B$-anomalies, while protecting from the most dangerous FCNCs that could be mediated by a heavy coloron and $Z'$."

Summary in the Authors' Words

The Common Origin

The model's premise, stated plainly: U_1 couplings and SM Yukawa couplings come from the same mixing between chiral and vector-like fermions. So a fit to the fermion masses is a prediction for the leptoquark couplings, and vice versa.

That is the whole claim, and its consequence is that the model has far fewer parameters than a generic 4321 construction — which is what "theory of flavour" means here.

The GIM-Like Protection

This is the point the paper develops best:"A GIM-like mechanism allows for large leptoquark couplings which can explain the $B$-anomalies, while protecting from the most dangerous FCNCs that could be mediated by a heavy coloron and $Z'$."

Why this is necessary: the 4321 group contains a colour octet $g'$ and a $Z'$, both at the TeV scale. If those had generic flavour-changing couplings, $B_s-\bar B_s$ mixing and $K-\bar K$ mixing would exclude them immediately. So the model must have large flavour-changing couplings in the leptoquark sector and small ones in the neutral sector — which is precisely the SM's own arrangement. The mechanism: the rotation that diagonalises the mass matrices appears in the leptoquark current (which is charged, so a CKM-like matrix is allowed) but cancels in the neutral currents (which must be diagonal). The same unitarity that gives GIM in the SM gives it here. So the construction's viability rests on reproducing the SM's flavour structure at the new scale, which is a strong requirement and the reason six vector-like families were needed.

The Status After 2022

"The analysis has been updated with the late 2022 measurements of 𝑅𝐾 (∗) by LHCb." The consequence, from the parent paper: the model survives only if $R_{K^{(*)}}$ is close to the SM value, and $R(D^{(*)})$ works at $1\sigma$ in a narrow window.

The Signals

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

1. Charged currents may be flavour-changing; neutral ones may not

The idea. The rotation appears in the leptoquark current but cancels in the colouron and $Z'$ currents, because the latter are neutral.

What it would add. A statement of the SM's flavour structure as a design requirement for new physics. The corpus's FCNC discussion, if it has one, states GIM as a suppression mechanism in the SM. Here it is used constructively: the model is built so that the new charged current is CKM-like and the new neutral currents are diagonal, which is what allows a TeV colour octet to exist at all.

Where it lands. The Pati–Salam article's gauge-sector section.

What stands in the way. Nothing; the mechanism is stated and the earlier one-family failure demonstrates it is nontrivial.

One observation about the corpus's map

The twin Pati–Salam series now has four entries in this batch, and this one adds nothing that the others lack except a compact statement of the GIM mechanism. It is worth recording mainly so the corpus is complete on the series, and so a reader searching for "twin Pati–Salam" finds a dated chain: 2021 construction, 2022 Corfu summary, 2023 full analysis, 2023 Moriond summary.

The one idea worth extracting is the constructive use of GIM. It reframes a familiar SM accident as a requirement: any model with TeV-scale colour-octet or $Z'$ vectors must arrange for their couplings to be flavour-diagonal to the accuracy that meson mixing demands, and the only known way to do that is to make the new charged current carry the entire rotation. That is a transferable design rule, not a fact about this model.

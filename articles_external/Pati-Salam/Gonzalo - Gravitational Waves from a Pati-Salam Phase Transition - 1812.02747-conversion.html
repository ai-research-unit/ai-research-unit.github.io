What the Paper Does

It computes the gravitational wave spectrum of the Pati–Salam phase transition and checks it against everything else the model predicts.

"on. For a Pati-Salam scale of MP S ∼ 105 GeV, we find a stochastic power spectrum within reach of the next generation of ground-based interferometer experiments such as the Einstein Telescope, in parts of the parameter space. We"

The reason Pati–Salam is the right group for this:

"It can admit a low energy symmetry breaking scale, MP S < 107 GeV, which implies that the stochastic spectrum peaks within the experimental reach, fpeak . 103 Hz [49]. The model also has a fairly large gauge coupling constant, g4 & 0.8, which increases the strength of the phase transition. Moreover, the rank of the broken group, SU (4), is larger than e.g. the electroweak phase transition, such that more latent heat is released [35]. The symmetry"

Three reasons, and the third is the one that is specific to Pati–Salam. A large gauge coupling $\alpha_4\sim g_4^2/4\pi\sim0.05$ at $10^5$ GeV makes the finite-temperature barrier substantial. A rank-3 group breaking to rank-4... no — $SU(4)$ has rank 3 and breaks to $SU(3)$, releasing the difference. The latent heat scales with the number of broken generators, and $SU(4)\to SU(3)$ breaks eight of fifteen, more than the electroweak transition breaks.

The Frequency Argument, Which Is Elementary and Decisive

The peak frequency from a transition at temperature $T$ is set by the Hubble rate there, redshifted to today:

$$ f_{\rm peak}\sim\frac{H(T)}{1+z}\sim10^{-7}\,\frac{T}{\rm GeV}\ {\rm Hz} $$

up to the supercooling factor. So $10^5$ GeV gives $10^{-2}$ Hz, which is the Einstein Telescope band, and $10^7$ GeV gives $1$ Hz, which is the top of it. The LIGO band starts at about $10$ Hz, which corresponds to $10^8$ GeV — so the later paper (2307.02544) is reaching up in scale, not down in frequency.

That arithmetic is worth recording because it fixes the entire observational strategy: the interferometer band you can access determines the GUT scale you can probe, and no amount of model building changes it.

Summary in the Authors' Words

The Scalar Content

$\Phi=\{\mathbf1,\mathbf2,\mathbf2\}$, $\Delta_L=\{\mathbf{10},\mathbf3,\mathbf1\}$, $\Delta_R=\{\mathbf{10},\mathbf1,\mathbf3\}$, $\Xi_1,\Xi_2,\Xi_3=\{\mathbf{15},\mathbf1,\mathbf1\}$, $\Omega_R=\{\mathbf{15},\mathbf1,\mathbf3\}$.

Three adjoints of $SU(4)$. $\Xi_1$ takes the VEV that breaks $SU(4)$; $\Xi_{2,3}$ are there for unification. The masses:

$$ M_{\Omega_R}^2\approx\rho_1^2v^2-\mu_{\Omega_R}^2,\qquad M_{\Xi_{2,3}}^2\approx v^2,\qquad M_{\Delta_{L,R}}^2\approx v^2, $$

and the component of $\Xi_1$ that acquires the vev gets a mass $M_{\Xi_1^v}=\sqrt{2}\lambda_1v\equiv M_{\rm PS}$.

So $M_{\rm PS}=\sqrt2\lambda_1v$ and the VEV is $v$. The transition is in $\Xi_1$'s fifteenth component, and the order parameter is a single real field.

The decomposition after breaking:

$$ \Phi\to\varphi=\{\mathbf1,\mathbf2,\mathbf2,0\},\quad \Delta_L\to\delta_L=\{\mathbf1,\mathbf3,\mathbf1,-2\},\quad \Delta_R\to\delta_R=\{\mathbf1,\mathbf1,\mathbf3,-2\}. $$

The $-2$ is $B-L=\pm2$ for the triplet, which is why $\delta_R$ gives a Majorana mass to $\nu_R$ and $\delta_L$ gives one to $\nu_L$.

D-Parity, and Why

It Has to Go

"This gauge group and matter content are manifestly left-right symmetric, invariant under exchanges of SU (2)L ↔ SU (2)R . Manifest left-right symmetry (also known as D-parity) forces gL = gR , which makes gauge coupling unification an impossible task. We will thus explicitly break this symmetry by adding purely right-handed fields at the PS scale that ensure gL 6= gR and gauge coupling unification can occur. We therefore construct"

**"Makes gauge coupling unification an impossible task"

** is a strong statement and it is correct. If $g_L=g_R$ at all scales, then $SU(2)_L$ and $SU(2)_R$ have one coupling between them; to meet $SU(4)$ and the hypercharge you have three couplings that must reach a common value at one point, but the two-group-one-coupling structure makes the system over-constrained.

The fix is to put in a field that breaks the symmetry by hand. $\Omega_R$ is purely right-handed — it has no left partner — so it contributes to the $SU(2)_R$ beta function and not to the $SU(2)_L$ one, and the two couplings separate.

The corpus should record the tension explicitly: the aesthetic motivation for Pati–Salam is left–right symmetry, and the phenomenological requirement for unification is that the symmetry be explicitly broken. It is not a small breaking either — it is a whole field whose only purpose is to make the couplings differ.

The Effective Model

"We motivate an effective model with three free parameters" — later stated as four: "eters: the PS gauge coupling g4 , the PS scale MP S , the portal coupling ρ1 , and the ratio of parameters in the tree-level scalar potential v/Λ. We found"

Four parameters, one of which ($g_4$) is fixed by unification. "An argument from gauge coupling unification fixes the Pati-Salam coupling $g_4$ as a function of this scale." So the prediction is three-dimensional, and:

"e amplitude of the power spectrum, – 17 – then, is a function of the portal coupling and the zero-temperature combination (v/Λ). As was demonstrated in Section 3, the peak of the spectrum may be observable if at least one of the portal couplings is sizable (ρ1 & 0.1), and for particular zero-temperature parameters in the scalar potential (v/Λ & 2). For a Pati-Salam scale of MP S ∼ 105"

Two conditions, both of them inequalities. $\rho_1\gtrsim0.1$ is a portal coupling of order the gauge coupling; $v/\Lambda\gtrsim2$ says the VEV is above the scale $\Lambda$ in the potential, which is what makes the barrier survive to zero temperature and the transition supercool.

The corpus should note that the signal requires supercooling, and supercooling requires $v/\Lambda>2$. That is not a tuned number but it is a specific regime, and it is the regime where the completion problem of 2307.02544 appears.

The Thermal Parameters

The standard four: $\alpha$ (latent heat over radiation density), $\beta/H$ (inverse duration), $T_n$ (nucleation temperature), $v_w$ (wall velocity).

$$ \alpha=\frac{\Delta V-\tfrac14T\partial_T\Delta V}{\rho_R(T)}\bigg|_{T=T_\Gamma} $$

with $\Delta V=V(\phi_f)-V(\phi_t)$ the energy difference between the vacua. The $-\frac14T\partial_T\Delta V$ is the trace-anomaly correction, and it matters at high temperature because the potential itself is temperature dependent.

Assuming the bag equation of state — so the plasma is treated as radiation plus vacuum energy with a definite relation, which is what makes $\alpha$ meaningful.

For the sound-wave and turbulence spectra the paper uses the standard parameterisations. The peak frequency:

$$ f_{\rm sw}\simeq1.9\times10^{-5}\ {\rm Hz}\ \frac{1}{v_w}\left(\frac{\beta}{H}\right)\left(\frac{T_n}{100\ {\rm GeV}}\right)\left(\frac{g_*}{100}\right)^{1/6}\frac{z_{\rm peak}}{\dots} $$

and for turbulence $f_{\rm turb}=27\ \mu{\rm Hz}$ at the reference point, with $h_*=16.5\ \mu{\rm Hz}$ the Hubble rate there.

GHz-scale frequencies from $100$ GeV transitions, $\mu$Hz from... no — reading the numbers again: the reference point is $T_n=10^5$ GeV and the resulting $f_{\rm sw}$ lands in the $10$–$1000$ Hz band for the chosen $\beta/H$. The $\beta/H$ factor is what moves the peak, and that is why supercooling is essential.

The Proton Lifetime

"The most constraining limit was imposed by the Super-Kamiokande collaboration to the process p → e+ π 0 , with a half-life lower bound of τp > 1.29 × 1034 years [115]. In our particular scenario, with the optimal PS scale for gravitational wave detection, MP S = 105 GeV, gauge coupling unification fixes MGU T ∼ 1.9 × 1016 and gGU T ∼ 0.96, which gives a proton half-life of τp ∼ 7 × 1035 years, larger than the experimental limit. This prediction"

$$ \tau_p\approx\frac{(4\pi)^2M_X^4}{\lambda_X^4m_p^5}. $$

Note the structure: a low Pati–Salam scale forces a high GUT scale. That is counter-intuitive and it is the key correlation. Lowering $M_{\rm PS}$ lengthens the running above it, so the couplings meet later; $M_{\rm GUT}$ goes up, and the proton lifetime goes as $M_X^4$, so it goes up by a lot.

"This prediction for proton decay is not too far from the SuperK bound and in fact it is fairly close to the projected limit expected to be reached by HyperK [116] of τ > 1.3 × 1035 years. A positive"

So the same model is testable three ways: GWs at ET, proton decay at Hyper-K, and the low-energy probes. And they are correlated through $M_{\rm PS}$, which is what makes the combination decisive.

"A positive measurement of proton decay is the smoking gun for unified theories, in particular if the measured decay rate is close to the predicted in our model, it would further motivate the scenario with MP S = 105 GeV where the peak amplitude of GW spectra is within sensitivity of the Einstein telescope."

That sentence is the paper's thesis in one line: an observation in one channel would select the parameter point that makes the other channel observable.

A scale of $10^5$ GeV puts the $W_R$ and the heavy neutrinos at a mass that LHC and flavour experiments already probe, so the model is squeezed from below by direct searches and from above by the requirement of a strong transition. Defects

"One-dimensional defects, cosmic strings, may decay into gravitational radiation [117, 118]. However, cosmic strings associated with the energy scales studied in this work - MP S ∼ 105 GeV - will have a dimensionless string tension of Gµ ∼ 10−29 and will therefore not lead to any observational signatures. Primordial"

So one possible GW source is switched off and the other is switched on. $G\mu\sim(T/M_{\rm Pl})^2\sim(10^5/10^{19})^2=10^{-28}$, which is far below the pulsar-timing and CMB bounds ($G\mu\lesssim10^{-11}$). Strings are irrelevant at this scale, and the phase transition is the only source.

"Primordial monopoles created during the PS phase transition can be reduced to acceptable limits during late time inflation [24]. Light monopoles may be produced at colliders [119], however PS monopoles have a mass of the order ∼ 106 GeV [120] which is beyond the reach of current experimental searches by ATLAS [121] and MoEDAL [122]."

Note the coincidence: the monopole mass $\sim4\pi v/g_4\sim10^6$ GeV is the same scale as the upper bound from the completion criterion in 2307.02544. That is not an accident — both are set by $v$.

Ideas Not Yet in the Corpus

The rank is a judgement, ordered by how directly the corpus needs the object.

  1. $f_{\rm peak}\sim10^{-7}(T/{\rm GeV})$ Hz fixes which GUT scale each detector can reach

The idea. The peak frequency of a transition's GW spectrum is set by the Hubble rate at the transition, redshifted. $10^5$ GeV gives $10^{-2}$ Hz (ET band), $10^8$ GeV gives $10$ Hz (LIGO band).

What it would add. A one-line rule that connects an experimental band to a unification scale, and it is the reason this whole subliterature exists. The corpus can state: LISA probes $10^3$–$10^5$ GeV, ET and Cosmic Explorer probe $10^5$–$10^8$ GeV, LIGO probes $\sim10^8$ GeV. No model building changes this; it is kinematics.

Where it lands. The Pati–Salam article's cosmology section, as the organising fact.

What stands in the way. Nothing. The scaling is $f\sim H/(1+z)$ with $H\sim T^2/M_{\rm Pl}$ and $1+z\sim T/T_0$ at the relevant epoch, which gives the coefficient.

  1. A rank-3 group breaking releases more latent heat than the electroweak transition

The idea. $SU(4)\to SU(3)$ breaks eight generators; the electroweak breaking breaks three. The latent heat scales with the number broken, so $\alpha$ is larger.

What it would add. A group-theoretic reason why GUT transitions are stronger than electroweak ones, which is why the GUT literature can hope for detectable signals at all. The corpus's Pati–Salam article can note that the group's size is itself an advantage here, which is a different kind of merit from unification.

Where it lands. The Pati–Salam article's cosmology section.

What stands in the way. The precise relation between the number of broken generators and $\alpha$ depends on the couplings and the barrier, not just the counting.

# Human Country — Specialisation Module

Reads with the Human Framework, never alone. Adds specialised aspects to each domain.

## D01. Physical Domain

__Site & Setting:__ The terrain, coastline, climate, and hazards of the territory the country holds, and the natural endowment — land, water, minerals, energy — the population draws on — the geographic setting and what it imposes.

__Built Fabric & Morphology:__ The physical country itself — settlement pattern, the urban–rural spread, building stock, where density concentrates and where it thins — the made stage daily life runs through.

__Infrastructure Networks:__ The trunk systems as standing built stock — water, power, rail, roads, ports, telecoms — their capacity, condition, and reach across the territory.

__Saturation & Decay:__ The load activity concentrates and the environment has not cleared — air and water pollution, accumulated waste, emissions — and the decaying fabric: deferred repair, dereliction, abandoned regions; cleared or left to accrete.

## D02. Biological Domain

__Population Body & Density:__ The population as bodies — headcount, distribution and density, age and health profile — the living mass the country holds, read at the grain a country is analysed.

__Sanitation & Disease Load:__ The endemic and epidemic disease profile crowding and water and sanitation produce, plus traffic and environmental mortality — the biological cost of scale.

__Care Capacity:__ The hospitals, clinics, and emergency response the country holds and their reach across regions, and whether capacity meets the load scale generates.

__Migration-Driven Renewal:__ How the population is sustained and grown — natural increase against the net immigration from abroad that countries lean on — and the turnover that flow imposes.

__Decline & Dependency:__ The standing load the life-cycle leaves — the aged, infirm, and indigent — and the mortality scale imposes; carried or left to accrete.

## D03. Economic Domain

__Economic Base & Function:__ What the country produces and exports to the world economy — its specialisation and role in the international division of labour.

__Land, Property & Rent:__ The land and property market as a major national asset and rent as a major cost of living — prices, ownership, and the access or exclusion the rent gradient produces.

__Domestic Economy & Employment:__ The service and consumption economy the base supports and the jobs and livelihoods it sustains or fails to.

__National Finance:__ The country's fiscal base — what it raises in tax and what it borrows on its own credit — and where the spending goes; sovereign, but its room to act is bounded by creditors, markets, and debt rather than any polity above.

__Vacancy & Misallocation:__ The financial residue scale leaves — stranded capital, malinvestment, fiscal deficit and debt overhang — and the material discard the country routes to its sinks; cleared or left to accrete.

## D04. Social Domain

__Spatial Stratification:__ How class, ethnicity, and status sort across regions — the segregation and mixing the country's geography produces, and whether the lines are hardening or dissolving.

__State Apparatus & Governance:__ The state — government, agencies, bureaucracy, policing, and services the country is run through — their competence and reach — and its sovereign standing in the international order, where treaties, alliances, and external pressure set the outer bounds of what it may decide.

__Community & Association:__ The community fabric below the whole — regions, localities, associations, the informal networks and mutual aid that organise daily life on the ground, and whether it is thickening or thinning.

__Transient Population & Cross-Border Flux:__ The visitor and temporary presence that swells the population above the resident base — tourists, seasonal and migrant labour, cross-border and transit traffic — the country used without being inhabited — and the strain that churn places on the fabric.

__Disorder & the Excluded:__ The friction the fabric throws off and the order the apparatus fails to hold — crime, the segregated outcast, the unhoused — cleared or left to accrete.

## D05. Emotional Domain

__Civic Will & Leadership:__ The drive to govern and direct the country — the leadership and machine that concentrate it, the appetite to build, reform, or coast — read off what the country pursues, not what it proclaims.

__Public Mood & Trust:__ The ambient temper of the populace — confidence or grievance, pride or siege — and the radius of trust among strangers the country does or does not sustain, and which way it is moving.

__Civic Disaffection:__ The estrangement, apathy, and spent will the populace carries — the alienation scale and inequality leave behind — re-anchored or left to settle, feeding the disorder in Social.

## D06. Informational Domain

__Civic Channels & Media:__ How word moves through the country — national and local press, broadcast and online media, signage, addressing and notification systems — the reach and speed of the public information layer across regions.

__Languages & Codes:__ The tongues and scripts a large, often polyglot population holds — the shared communicative ground and who is excluded from it by language or literacy.

__Surveillance & Records:__ The monitoring the state accumulates — cameras, sensors, communications and identity data — and the national registers that fix who and what is where, and what the record captures or misses.

__Noise & Stale Record:__ The rumour, misinformation, and decayed or contradictory official records the channels throw off — cleared or left to accrete.

## D07. Moral Domain

__Inherited Law & National Statute:__ The codified order the country runs on — the constitution and inherited legal tradition beneath it, the treaties and international law it is bound by, plus the statute it enacts itself — and what its own legislation adds or constrains atop that floor.

__Enforcement & Adjudication:__ How the code is actually applied — policing, courts, inspection, the evenness of enforcement across regions and classes — practice read against the rule.

__Impunity & the Unredressed:__ The gap between justice owed and delivered — uneven enforcement, the protected, the unpunished and the over-punished — feeding the Social grievance stock; cleared or left to accrete.

## D08. Intellectual Domain

__Knowledge Institutions & Talent Pool:__ The universities, research bodies, and skilled-labour base the country holds — the stock of expertise it draws and produces — and how widely competence spreads across the population.

__Schooling & Skill Formation:__ The education system that forms the population — its quality, reach, and unevenness across regions — and the trades and competencies the economy reproduces.

__Innovation Frontier:__ The active frontier of method and craft the talent base works — what is being made and improved here.

__Brain Drain & Obsolete Craft:__ The skilled outflow abroad that hollows the talent pool and the trades the economy no longer needs — knowledge decayed or departed, cleared or left to accrete.

## D09. Cultural Domain

__Civic Identity & Origin:__ The story the country tells about itself — its founding, its self-image, the character it claims — the bedrock identity beneath the surface mood.

__Cultural Production & Scene:__ The arts, music, cuisine, and distinctive national culture the country makes and hosts — how it externalises its inner life — and the institutions and places that carry it.

__Built Character & Landmark:__ The architectural idiom and the landmarks, monuments, and signature places that fix the country's visual identity.

__Heritage Hollowed:__ The heritage and symbols worn into cliché or kitsch — the commodified tradition, the stale national brand, the anomie where shared character has eroded — renewed or left as inert residue.

## D10. Spiritual Domain

__Religious Presence & Plurality:__ The faiths the country holds and their institutions, sacred sites, and reach — the plurality it gathers and the relations among them.

__Cosmology & Sacred Practice:__ The outlooks the populace holds and the ritual, observance, and sacred space through which they are enacted — festival, pilgrimage, prayer, or their secular absence.

__Disenchantment & the Cold Sacred:__ The residue where belief has lapsed — the desacralised site, the repurposed church, the void scale leaves where the sacred no longer answers — re-anchored or left to settle.

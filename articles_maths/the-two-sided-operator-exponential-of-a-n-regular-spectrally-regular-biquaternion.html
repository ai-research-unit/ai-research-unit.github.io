The Two-Sided Operator Exponential of a N-Regular, Spectrally Regular Biquaternion

Introduction

This article is the analytic companion of The Two-Sided Operator of a N-Regular, Spectrally Regular Biquaternion. It computes the one-parameter group generated by the sandwich and the exponent that each polar factor contributes to it. The algebra of the sandwich — multiplicativity, the closed form, the action of the four factors, the two slots and the image — is in the companion; this article uses it and does not repeat it.

The sandwich is quadratic in the element, so it is not a representation, and there is no identity $\exp(t\Theta_{\tilde Q})=\Theta_{\exp(t\tilde Q)}$. What replaces it is the spectral form: on $\Sigma_{11}$ the sandwich is diagonalisable, and its exponential is obtained by exponentiating its four eigenvalues. The absence of an element identity is itself the doubling: the sandwich squares the modulus and pairs the two slots, and the exponent of the pair is the sum of the two slot exponents.

The plan is the following. §The Operator Exponential reads the spectrum of the group. §The Exponents of the Factors gives the scale, the boost and the rotor. §The Pure Factors and the Full Spin and §The Vector Representation as the Double of the Half-Spin give the representation-theoretic form. §The Angle Bookkeeping states the doubling rule. The article closes with the summary, the notation and the readings.

The Operator Exponential

Proposition. On $\Sigma_{11}$ the sandwich is diagonalisable, with the eigenvalues $\lambda_i\bar\lambda_j$ of §The Spectrum of the Sandwich of the companion. Its exponential is diagonalisable, with the eigenvalues

$$ e^{t\lambda_+\bar\lambda_+},\quad e^{t\lambda_+\bar\lambda_-},\quad e^{t\lambda_-\bar\lambda_+},\quad e^{t\lambda_-\bar\lambda_-}, $$

and determinant

$$ \boxed{\;\det\exp\bigl(t\Theta_{\tilde Q}\bigr) =\exp\bigl(t\operatorname{tr}\Theta_{\tilde Q}\bigr) =\exp\bigl(4t\lvert Q_t\rvert^{2}\bigr).\;} $$

Proof. The sandwich is diagonalisable in the common eigenbasis of §The Two Slots of the Sandwich of the companion, and the exponential of a diagonal operator is the diagonal of the exponentials. The trace is the sum of the four products, $$ \lambda_+\bar\lambda_++\lambda_+\bar\lambda_-+\lambda_-\bar\lambda_++\lambda_-\bar\lambda_- =(\lambda_++\lambda_-)(\bar\lambda_++\bar\lambda_-) =4\lvert Q_t\rvert^{2}, $$ and $\det\exp(tA)=\exp(t\operatorname{tr}A)$.

Remark (no element identity). For a central element $\tilde Q=c\,e_t$ the sandwich is $\lvert c\rvert^{2}\mathrm{id}$, so $\exp(t\Theta_{\tilde Q})=e^{t\lvert c\rvert^{2}}\mathrm{id}$, while $\Theta_{\exp(t\tilde Q)}=e^{2t\operatorname{Re}c}\mathrm{id}$. The two agree only when $\lvert c\rvert^{2}=2\operatorname{Re}c$. The sandwich exponentiates its image, not its parameter.

The Exponents of the Factors

The Scale

The exponent is $\ln r$, and the sandwich sends it to

$$ \boxed{\;\ln r\longmapsto 2\ln r\;}. $$

There is no angle. The factor two is nevertheless the same factor two as in the boost and the rotor: the exponent of the factor is half the exponent of its image. The scale is the case in which the doubled parameter is a dilation and not a rotation or a rapidity. The map $r\mapsto r^{2}$ is an isomorphism of $\mathbb{R}_{>0}$, so the scale is recovered from its sandwich; only the sign of the factor is lost, and the polar word has $r>0$.

The Boost

The exponent is $i\tfrac{\psi}{2}\vec u_{\mathrm b}$ and the output is the rapidity $\psi$. The parameter of the factor is half the parameter of its image, and the doubling is exact. The boost is Hermitian, so both slots carry the exponent with the same sign; the sandwich adds the two exponents instead of cancelling them, and the sum is $2\cdot\tfrac{\psi}{2}=\psi$.

The Rotor

The exponent is $\tfrac{\theta}{2}\vec u_{\mathrm r}$ and the output is the rotation of angle $\theta$ about $\vec u_{\mathrm r}$. The parameter of the factor is half the parameter of its image. The rotor is $*$-unitary, so the two slots carry opposite signs; the sandwich conjugates instead of composing the two exponents, and the conjugation by $\exp(\tfrac{\theta}{2}\vec u_{\mathrm r})$ is the rotation of angle $\theta$. Equivalently, the double cover $SU(2)\to SO(3)$ sends $\pm\tilde u_{\mathrm r}$ to the same rotation, and the parameter $\theta$ of the image is twice the parameter $\theta/2$ of the rotor. The sandwich sees the rotation and not the sign; the sign is the extra data of the one-sided operator.

The Pure Factors and the Full Spin

For each pure factor, read with the other factors absent, the products are elementary. The scale $r$ has $\lambda_\pm=r$ and products $r^{2}$. The central phase has $\lambda_\pm=\exp(i\alpha)$ and products $\exp(i\alpha)\exp(-i\alpha)=1$. The boost has $$ \lambda_+=e^{-\psi/2},\qquad \lambda_-=e^{\psi/2}, $$ so its products are $$ e^{-\psi},\quad 1,\quad 1,\quad e^{\psi}. $$ The rotor has $$ \lambda_+=e^{i\theta/2},\qquad \lambda_-=e^{-i\theta/2}, $$ so its products are $$ e^{i\theta},\quad 1,\quad 1,\quad e^{-i\theta}. $$ The sandwich therefore carries the full rapidity $\psi$ and the full angle $\theta$, while the one-sided operator carries the half-rapidity and the half-angle. The phase cancels and the scale doubles.

The one-sided operator is the spinor action. The sandwich has no half-angle of its own: it pairs two one-sided slots, and each slot is the spinor action of The One-Sided Operator Exponential of a N-Regular, Spectrally Regular Biquaternion, §The Exponential Polar Word on a Spinor. The half-angle $\theta/2$ and the half-rapidity $\psi/2$ live on a single slot; this article computes their products, which are the full angle $\theta$ and the full rapidity $\psi$.

factor one-slot pair $(\lambda_+,\lambda_-)$ two-slot products output
scale $r$ $(r,r)$ $r^{2},r^{2},r^{2},r^{2}$ dilation $r^{2}$
phase $\exp(i\alpha)$ $(e^{i\alpha},e^{i\alpha})$ $1,1,1,1$ identity
boost $\tilde U_{\mathrm b}$ $(e^{-\psi/2},e^{\psi/2})$ $e^{-\psi},1,1,e^{\psi}$ rapidity $\psi$
rotor $\tilde u_{\mathrm r}$ $(e^{i\theta/2},e^{-i\theta/2})$ $e^{i\theta},1,1,e^{-i\theta}$ angle $\theta$

The scale and the phase are central. Their rows are read as scalar multipliers of a word with $B\neq0$; for a purely central element $B=0$ and the spectrum collapses to $Q_t\bar Q_t$.

For a general word the two factors do not commute, and the common eigenbasis is not the eigenbasis of $L_{\tilde Q}$. The product formula for the spectrum remains exact. The splitting of the products into a boost and a rotation is the content of §The Boost and §The Rotor when the axes commute; in general the effective exponent is the sum of the two slot exponents only after the common eigenbasis is chosen. The relation between the spectral data and the polar parameters is Relations between Exponential Polar Decomposition and Eigenbasis of a N-Regular, Spectrally Regular Biquaternion, §The Spectral Data from the Polar Factors.

Corollary (the sandwich is the two-slot operator). The sandwich acts on the left and the right slots of the matrix model. Its spectrum is the four products $\lambda_i\bar\lambda_j$. The one-sided operator is the restriction to one slot; the two-sided operator is the pairing of the two slots. The half-angle of the rotor and the half-rapidity of the boost belong to one slot; their doubles belong to the pair.

The Vector Representation as the Double of the Half-Spin

The two slots are the two tensor factors of $\mathbb{B}\cong S\otimes S^{*}$, where $S=\mathbb{C}^{2}$ is the spinor space and $S^{*}$ its dual. The left factor acts on $S$, the right on $S^{*}$, and the sandwich is $$ \Theta_{\tilde Q}=\rho(\tilde Q)\otimes\bar\rho(\tilde Q), $$ where $\rho$ is the defining representation on $S=\mathbb{C}^{2}$ and $\bar\rho$ its conjugate. The tensor product of the defining representation and its conjugate is the complexified vector representation, $$ 2\otimes\bar 2=4, $$ so the sandwich is the vector and the one-sided operator, on one slot, is the spinor.

The weights add. For the rotor, $\rho(\tilde u_{\mathrm r})$ has weights $e^{\pm i\theta/2}$ and $\bar\rho(\tilde u_{\mathrm r})$ has weights $e^{\mp i\theta/2}$; the four sums are $\theta,0,0,-\theta$. For the boost, both slots carry $e^{\mp\psi/2}$ because the boost is Hermitian; the four sums are $\psi,0,0,-\psi$. In both cases the vector weights are the sums of the two spinor weights, and each extremal vector weight is twice a spinor weight.

The sandwich has no half-angle of its own. It is the vector representation, of dimension four; its weights are the sums. The half-angle $\theta/2$ and the half-rapidity $\psi/2$ live on a single slot, in The One-Sided Operator Exponential of a N-Regular, Spectrally Regular Biquaternion, §The Exponential Polar Word on a Spinor. The sandwich is the double of the half-spin, not a half-spin itself.

The two Peirce copies are not the two Lorentz chiralities. The two columns $S_+$ and $S_-$ carry the same Weyl representation $\rho$; the two Lorentz chiralities are the two tensor slots $S$ and $S^{*}$, that is the left and right slots.

The addition is at the level of generators. For a general word the two slot exponents do not combine into one rapidity and one angle unless the axes commute. The four products $\lambda_i\bar\lambda_j$ remain exact; the split into a boost and a rotation is the content of §The Boost and §The Rotor for commuting axes. For a pure non-central factor the eigenvalue $1$ occurs twice in the list; for the scale the value $r^{2}$ occurs four times, and for the phase the value $1$ occurs four times.

The whole section assumes $B\neq0$. When $B=0$ and $\vec Q\neq0$, $L_{\tilde Q}$ is defective, with two Jordan blocks of size two, the common eigenbasis of $L_{\tilde Q}$ and $R_{\tilde Q^{*}}$ does not exist, and the spectral formula is unavailable; the sandwich itself remains defined. This is the degenerate case of The Spectrum, §The Degenerate Case: The Spectral-Degenerate Cone.

The table is a table of exponents, not of angles alone. The scale doubles its exponent and produces no angle; the phase produces no exponent and no angle; the boost and the rotor double theirs and produce a rapidity and a rotation.

The Doubling Rule

Proposition (the doubling rule). Write each factor as $F=\exp(tA)$, where $t$ is its scalar parameter and $A$ its generator: $A=e_t$ for the scale, $A=i$ for the phase, $A=i\vec u_{\mathrm b}$ for the boost and $A=\vec u_{\mathrm r}$ for the rotor. Then

$$ \Theta_F(\tilde X)=\exp(tA)\,\tilde X\,\exp(tA^{*}). $$

Three cases exhaust the word:

(a) $A^{*}=A$ (Hermitian). The two slots carry the same exponent $+tA$. If $A$ is central, as for the scale with $A=e_t$, the sandwich is multiplication by $\exp(2tA)$; if $A$ is non-central, as for the boost with $A=i\vec u_{\mathrm b}$, the sandwich is the congruence whose parameter is $2t$. In both cases the output parameter is $2t$.

(b) $A^{*}=-A$ and $A$ non-central (the rotor, $A=\vec u_{\mathrm r}$). The two slots carry opposite exponents, so the sandwich is the conjugation $\exp(t\,\mathrm{ad}_A)$, whose vector action is the rotation of angle $2t$; the output parameter is $2t$.

(c) $A^{*}=-A$ and $A$ central (the phase, $A=i$). The two slots carry opposite exponents and $A$ commutes with everything, so $\mathrm{ad}_A=0$ and the output parameter is $0$.

In one formula,

$$ \text{output parameter}= \begin{cases} 2t, & A^{*}=A,\\ 2t, & A^{*}=-A\ \text{and}\ A\ \text{non-central},\\ 0, & A^{*}=-A\ \text{and}\ A\ \text{central}. \end{cases} $$

The doubling is therefore a property of the non-central factors, and of the Hermitian central one; the cancellation is a property of the skew-Hermitian central one. "Half-angle" is the name of the boost and rotor rows; it is not a property of the phase.

Summary

The one-parameter group of the sandwich is read on the spectrum. On $\Sigma_{11}$ the sandwich has the four eigenvalues $\lambda_i\bar\lambda_j$, and its exponential has their exponentials; its determinant is $\exp(4t\lvert Q_t\rvert^{2})$. There is no element identity $\exp(t\Theta_{\tilde Q})=\Theta_{\exp(t\tilde Q)}$, because the sandwich is quadratic; for a central element the two sides differ by the square of the modulus against twice its real part.

The four factors give the table of exponents. The scale doubles $\ln r$ to $2\ln r$; the phase cancels $i\alpha$ to $0$; the boost doubles $i\tfrac{\psi}{2}\vec u_{\mathrm b}$ to the rapidity $\psi$; the rotor doubles $\tfrac{\theta}{2}\vec u_{\mathrm r}$ to the angle $\theta$. The rule is the number of slots: the Hermitian factors carry the same exponent twice, the $*$-unitary non-central factor carries opposite exponents whose conjugation produces the angle, and the central $*$-unitary factor cancels.

The spectral reading is the product formula. The one-slot pair of the rotor is $(e^{i\theta/2},e^{-i\theta/2})$ and the two-slot products are $e^{\pm i\theta}$; the one-slot pair of the boost is $(e^{-\psi/2},e^{\psi/2})$ and the products are $e^{\pm\psi}$; the scale gives $r^{2}$ and the phase gives $1$. In representation-theoretic terms the sandwich is $\rho\otimes\bar\rho$, the complexified vector representation, and the vector weights are the sums of the two spinor weights. The one-sided operator is the spinor action, and the sandwich is its double.

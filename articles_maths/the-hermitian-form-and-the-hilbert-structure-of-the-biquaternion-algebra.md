The complex sesquilinear form

$$
\langle\tilde P,\tilde Q\rangle_{*}=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P\tilde Q^{*})=\sum_\mu P_\mu\overline{Q_\mu}
$$

is positive definite, of signature $(8,0)$ on the real space, and it makes the algebra a Hilbert space: the underlying real space of dimension eight with the real part of the form is $\mathbb{C}^4$ with its ordinary Hermitian inner product, complete, and the level set $\lVert\tilde Q\rVert_E=1$ is the Euclidean sphere $S^7$. This article will treat that Hilbert structure: the inner product, the norm, the completeness and the sphere. It is the abstract counterpart of *The Euclidean Topology of the Biquaternion Algebra*.

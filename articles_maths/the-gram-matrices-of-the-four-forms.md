# __The Gram Matrices of the Four Forms__

## Introduction

**Provisional.** This article is a placeholder of the topology region, created in the dispatch that gives the four forms their own reading groups. It records its scope, its home and its sources; the content is to be written.

The four forms are read from two matrices in the coefficient basis. The complex bilinear form and the quaternion sesquilinear form have Gram matrix

$$
\mathrm{D}=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1),
$$

and the quaternion bilinear form and the complex sesquilinear form have Gram matrix $\mathrm{I}_4$. Which form takes which matrix is decided by whether the sign vector $\varepsilon$ enters, and that in turn is decided by whether the plain product or the $\natural$-product is read. This article will tabulate the four matrices with the four conjugations that produce them, the four signatures and the four null sets, and is the tabular counterpart of *The Four Pairings of the Biquaternion Algebra*.

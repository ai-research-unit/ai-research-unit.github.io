The Real Forms of the Bilinear Form

Introduction

Provisional. This article is a placeholder of the topology region, created in the dispatch that gives the four forms their own reading groups. It records its scope, its home and its sources; the content is to be written.

The quaternion bilinear form $N=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P\tilde Q^{\natural})$ restricted to the six distinguished real subspaces carries the six signatures

$$ (1,1),\ (3,3),\ (4,0),\ (0,4),\ (1,3),\ (3,1), $$

on the centre, the vector subspace, the quaternion subspace, the anti-quaternion subspace, the Hermitian subspace and the anti-Hermitian subspace. This article will gather them, with the isotropic lines of the indefinite restrictions and the definiteness reading of the division-algebra property on the two quaternion subspaces. It is the bilinear half of The Six Subspaces and the Four Forms.

The Realification of the Antisymmetric Plain Sesqualgebra

Introduction

The Antisymmetric Plain Sesqualgebra is one of the twelve algebraic structures that the biquaternion space of $\mathbb{B}=\mathbb{C}\otimes_\mathbb{R}\mathbb{H}$ carries, the structure carried by the product $$ \tilde P\circ\tilde Q=\operatorname{Vect}\bigl(\tilde P\tilde Q^{*}\bigr)=\tfrac12\bigl(\tilde P\tilde Q^{*}-\overline{\tilde Q}\tilde P^{\natural}\bigr), $$ which over $\mathbb{C}$ is the antisymmetric plain sesquilinear structure of The Algebraic Structures over the Biquaternion $\mathbb{C}$ Space. Read over $\mathbb{R}$, the same product on the same set is an $\mathbb{R}$-algebra on the eight-dimensional real space $\mathbb{R}^8$, and this article owns that real reading.

The general construction is not repeated here. The realification of a vector space, of a linear map and of a form, the doubling of the dimension, the complex structure $J$ and the table of the four effects are The Realification and the Effect of the Change of Scalars; This article gives what is specific to this product: its real bilinear reading, the law of its second slot over the centre, the statement that it carries no form of its own, and its real operators.

The Product Read over $\mathbb{R}$

The product is antisymmetric plain sesquilinear over $\mathbb{C}$ and $\mathbb{R}$-bilinear over $\mathbb{R}$, by §The Effect on the Twelve Algebraic Structures of The Realification and the Effect of the Change of Scalars. It is therefore an $\mathbb{R}$-algebra on $\mathbb{R}^8$ in the broad sense, the sense of a module with a bilinear multiplication. In that algebra conjugate-alternation holds, the Jacobi identity fails, there is no unit, and the scalar part vanishes.

Every ring-theoretic property of the product is the same in the two readings, by the dictionary of the same article: a condition written with the addition, the product, $e_0$ and quantification over the elements is a statement about the ring and does not move when the scalars are restricted to $\mathbb{R}$. What the real reading adds to this product is the following. It is the vector half of the split of the general plain sesquilinear product, $$ \tilde P\tilde Q^{*}=H(\tilde P,\tilde Q)e_0+\tilde P\circ\tilde Q, $$ the scalar half being the product of the symmetric plain sesqualgebra; its scalar part therefore vanishes and it carries no form of its own. Its rule is the rule of the block, unchanged over $\mathbb{R}$, $$ \tilde P\circ\tilde Q=-P_0\overline{\vec Q}+\overline{Q_0}\vec P-\vec P\times\overline{\vec Q}, $$ and its diagonal is not zero but the vector part of the square, $\tilde Q\circ\tilde Q=\operatorname{Vect}(\tilde Q\tilde Q^{*})$, equal to $2ie_3$ at $e_1+ie_2$. The operator criterion of the block, $R_{e_0}=P_{\operatorname{Vect}}$ (The Specificities of the Antisymmetric Plain Sesqualgebra), is a statement about the ring and holds in the two readings.

The Second Slot and the Centre

Over $\mathbb{C}$ the product is $\mathbb{C}$-sesquilinear: it is linear in the first slot and conjugate-linear in the second, because the second slot is read through the star ${}^{*}=\bar{\cdot}\circ{}^{\natural}$, whose restriction to the centre is $i\mapsto-i$, the Galois conjugation of $\mathbb{C}$ over $\mathbb{R}$. The structure is therefore a sesqualgebra over $(\mathbb{C},\varsigma)$ with $\varsigma={}^{*}$ on the centre, and not an algebra.

Read over $\mathbb{R}$, the involution disappears: the only automorphism of $\mathbb{R}$ is the identity, and a sesquilinear law with the identity involution is a bilinear law. The product is therefore an $\mathbb{R}$-algebra, and its second slot is $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}$-conjugate-linear, by §The Realification of a Linear Map of The Realification and the Effect of the Change of Scalars: the left multiplication $L$ by a central element anticommutes with the complex structure, $LJ=-JL$, which is the criterion for $\varsigma$-linearity. The passage therefore changes the kind of this product — sesqualgebra over $\mathbb{C}$, algebra over $\mathbb{R}$ — and the distinction migrates from the field to the centre.

In the real basis the criterion takes the block form $$ \begin{pmatrix}A&B\\ B&-A\end{pmatrix}, $$ which anticommutes with $J$.

The Realified Form

The scalar part of this product vanishes: it is the antisymmetrisation of the general product of its family, and the antisymmetrisation kills the scalar part, so the product carries no degree-two form, as the third column of the table of The 12 Products of the Biquaternion Complex Space records. The realification therefore has no form to realify, and the four realified signatures $(4,4)$, $(4,4)$, $(8,0)$, $(2,6)$ of The Realification of the Four Forms do not include this product. What the real reading adds is the shape of the product itself: conjugate-alternating and vector valued over $\mathbb{R}$ exactly as over $\mathbb{C}$, with the Hermitian square $\tilde Q\circ\tilde Q=\operatorname{Vect}(\tilde Q\tilde Q^{*})$ as its diagonal. The structural theory of that shape, the quadric of the square-zero elements and the two Jacobi failures, is The Vector Part of the Square and the Jacobi Failure of the Antisymmetric Plain Sesqualgebra.

The Real Operators

The left and the right multiplications by an element are the maps $$ L_{\tilde X}:\tilde Y\mapsto\tilde X\circ\tilde Y,\qquad R_{\tilde X}:\tilde Y\mapsto\tilde Y\circ\tilde X, $$ and over $\mathbb{R}$ they are $\mathbb{R}$-linear endomorphisms of $\mathbb{R}^8$, by §The Realification of a Linear Map of The Realification and the Effect of the Change of Scalars. Because the second slot is $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}$-conjugate-linear, the left multiplication is $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}$-conjugate-linear, so it anticommutes with $J$, has the block shape $\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\ B&-A\end{pmatrix}$ and has vanishing real trace, the two diagonal blocks being opposite; it is $\mathbb{R}$-linear and not $\mathbb{C}$-linear, which is the point of the real reading. The right multiplication by $e_0$ is of the other kind, $\mathbb{C}$-linear, and equals the projection onto the vector subspace, $R_{e_0}=P_{\operatorname{Vect}}$, the defining identity of the block.

Summary

The Antisymmetric Plain Sesqualgebra read over $\mathbb{R}$ is the same product on the same elements, an $\mathbb{R}$-algebra on $\mathbb{R}^8$ in which conjugate-alternation holds, the Jacobi identity fails, there is no unit, and the scalar part vanishes. Its second slot is $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}$-conjugate-linear. The product carries no form: its scalar part vanishes because it is the antisymmetrisation of its family. The passage changes nothing of the product itself; it changes the field, the dimension and the linear structure, and for this product it adds the real reading of the block identity $R_{e_0}=P_{\operatorname{Vect}}$ and of its operators.

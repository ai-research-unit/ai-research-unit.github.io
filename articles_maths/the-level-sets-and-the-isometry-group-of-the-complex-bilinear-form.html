The Level Sets and the Isometry Group of the Complex Bilinear Form

Introduction

Provisional. This article is a placeholder of the topology region, created in the dispatch that gives the four forms their own reading groups. It records its scope, its home and its sources; the content is to be written.

Of the four forms, the complex bilinear one is the plain-product reading. Its level set

$$ \sum_\mu\varepsilon_\mu Q_\mu^2=1 $$

is a non-compact complex quadric of real dimension $6$, and it is not a group; and its isometry group is $O_4(\mathbb{C})$, of real dimension $12$, shared with the quaternion bilinear form. This article will treat both, with the real slice $O(1,3)$ that the form induces on the Hermitian subspace $\mathbb{M}_+$ (Hermitian and Skew-Hermitian Elements), and will place them beside the level sets and the isometry groups of the three other forms.

The Witt Index and the Maximal Isotropic Subspaces of the Krein Form

Introduction

Provisional. This article is a placeholder of the topology region, created in the dispatch that gives the four forms their own reading groups. It records its scope, its home and its sources; the content is to be written.

The quaternion sesquilinear form is indefinite, so it has a null set and isotropic subspaces. Its null set is the real cone of dimension $7$

$$ \lVert c\rVert_E=\lVert v\rVert_E, $$

on the centre part $c$ and the vector part $v$ of the element. This article will treat that cone, the totally isotropic subspaces, the maximal ones and the Witt index, and will describe the maximal ones by the Peirce lines and the minimal left and right ideals, as The Six Subspaces and the Four Forms does for the quaternion bilinear form. It is the indefinite counterpart of The Isotropic Structure of the Complex Bilinear Form.

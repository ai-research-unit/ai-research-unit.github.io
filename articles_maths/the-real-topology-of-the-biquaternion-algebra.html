The Real Topology of the Biquaternion Algebra

Introduction

Provisional. This article is a placeholder of the topology region, created in the dispatch that gives the four forms their own reading groups. It records its scope, its home and its sources; the content is to be written.

Read over the real scalars, the biquaternion algebra is the space $\mathbb{R}^8$ of the eight real coordinates, and the real part of the positive definite complex sesquilinear form, $\mathrm{Re}\langle\tilde P,\tilde Q\rangle_{*}$, is its ordinary inner product. This article will treat the vector-space topology so induced: the inner product, the Euclidean norm, the contractibility of the space and the Euclidean unit sphere $S^7$. It is the real counterpart of The Euclidean Topology of the Biquaternion Algebra.

The Inner Adjoint and the Orthogonal Group of the Bilinear Form

Introduction

Provisional. This article is a placeholder of the topology region, created in the dispatch that gives the four forms their own reading groups. It records its scope, its home and its sources; the content is to be written.

The quaternion bilinear form $N=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P\tilde Q^{\natural})$ puts a two-sided operator on the algebra: the inner adjoint $\tilde Q\mapsto\tilde Q^{\natural}$, and the sandwich it defines. This article will give that operator, the group of the $\natural$-versors it produces, and the double covers onto the orthogonal group $O_4(\mathbb{C})$ and its real slice $SO^+(1,3)$ (Biquaternion Versors and the Orthogonal Group, Biquaternion Rotations and Lorentz Transformations). It is the quaternion-bilinear counterpart of Two-Sided Operators on the Biquaternion Algebra with Hermitian Adjoint, and it fills the operator half of the quaternionic-bilinear reading.

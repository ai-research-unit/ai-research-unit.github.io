The Realification of the Symmetric Plain Algebra

Introduction

The Symmetric Plain Algebra is one of the twelve algebraic structures that the biquaternion space of $\mathbb{B}=\mathbb{C}\otimes_\mathbb{R}\mathbb{H}$ carries, the structure carried by the product $$ \tilde P\circ\tilde Q=\tfrac12\bigl(\tilde P\tilde Q+\tilde Q\tilde P\bigr), $$ which over $\mathbb{C}$ is the symmetric plain bilinear structure of The 12 Algebraic Structures over the Biquaternion $\mathbb{C}$ Space. Read over $\mathbb{R}$, the same product on the same set is an $\mathbb{R}$-algebra on the eight-dimensional real space $\mathbb{R}^8$, and this article owns that real reading.

The general construction is not repeated here. The realification of a vector space, of a linear map and of a form, the doubling of the dimension, the complex structure $J$ and the table of the four effects are The Realification and the Effect of the Change of Scalars; This article gives what is specific to this product: its real bilinear reading, the law of its second slot over the centre, the realification of the form it carries and the signature that follows, and its real operators.

The Product Read over $\mathbb{R}$

The product is symmetric plain bilinear over $\mathbb{C}$ and $\mathbb{R}$-bilinear over $\mathbb{R}$, by §The Effect on the Twelve Algebraic Structures of The Realification and the Effect of the Change of Scalars. It is therefore an $\mathbb{R}$-algebra on $\mathbb{R}^8$ in the broad sense, the sense of a module with a bilinear multiplication. In that algebra commutativity holds, associativity fails, the Jordan identity holds, and $e_0$ is a two-sided unit.

Every ring-theoretic property of the product is the same in the two readings, by the dictionary of the same article: a condition written with the addition, the product, $e_0$ and quantification over the elements is a statement about the ring and does not move when the scalars are restricted to $\mathbb{R}$. What the real reading adds to this product is the following. It is the symmetrisation of the general plain product, so its scalar part is that of $B$ and it keeps the same form; its associativity fails while its Jordan identity holds, which makes it the one symmetric product of the twelve that is a Jordan algebra.

The Second Slot and the Centre

Over $\mathbb{C}$ the product is $\mathbb{C}$-bilinear, so it is an algebra and not a sesqualgebra, and its second slot is $\mathbb{C}$-linear. Read over $\mathbb{R}$, the product remains $\mathbb{R}$-bilinear and its second slot is $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}$-linear: by §The Realification of a Linear Map of The Realification and the Effect of the Change of Scalars, the left multiplication $L$ by a central element $\lambda\in\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}$ commutes with the complex structure, $LJ=JL$, which is the criterion for $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}$-linearity. The passage therefore does not change the kind of this product: it is an algebra over $\mathbb{C}$ and an algebra over $\mathbb{R}$, with the same linearity of the second slot once the centre is named as the field.

In the real basis the criterion takes a block form. If the complex matrix of the product on a $\mathbb{C}$-basis is $A+iB$, the real matrix on the doubled basis is $$ \begin{pmatrix}A&-B\\ B&A\end{pmatrix}, $$ the shape that commutes with $J=\begin{pmatrix}0&-\mathrm{I}\\ \mathrm{I}&0\end{pmatrix}$.

The Realified Form

The product carries the general plain bilinear $B$ form, the scalar part of the general product of its family, quoted from The 4 Forms over the Biquaternion $\mathbb{C}$ Space: $$ B(\tilde P,\tilde Q)=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P\tilde Q)=P_0Q_0-(\mathbf P,\mathbf Q) $$ Over $\mathbb{C}$ it is a symmetric bilinear form; a nondegenerate symmetric bilinear form over $\mathbb{C}$ is classified by its rank alone, so its realification is not the doubling of a complex inertia but the split pair described in §The Realification of a Form of The Realification and the Effect of the Change of Scalars: the real part $\operatorname{Re}\Phi$ and the imaginary part $\operatorname{Im}\Phi$ are two real symmetric bilinear forms, and on the identification $\mathbb{R}^8$ the realification has signature $$ (4,4), $$ on the basis $e_0,e_1,e_2,e_3,ie_0,ie_1,ie_2,ie_3$. On the realifications the complex structure reverses the form, $$ \Phi_{\mathbb{R}}(JP,JQ)=-\Phi_{\mathbb{R}}(P,Q), $$ which is the same statement as the two opposite blocks $\mathrm{D}$ and $-\mathrm{D}$ of its Gram matrix. The instance is the row general plain bilinear $B$ of the table of The Realification of the Four Forms, §The Four Gram Matrices.

The Real Operators

The left and the right multiplications by an element are the maps $$ L_{\tilde X}:\tilde Y\mapsto\tilde X\circ\tilde Y,\qquad R_{\tilde X}:\tilde Y\mapsto\tilde Y\circ\tilde X, $$ and over $\mathbb{R}$ they are $\mathbb{R}$-linear endomorphisms of $\mathbb{R}^8$, by §The Realification of a Linear Map of The Realification and the Effect of the Change of Scalars. Because the second slot is $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}$-linear, the left multiplication is $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}$-linear as well, and its real trace is twice the real part of its complex trace, $\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathbb{R}}(L_{\tilde X})=8\operatorname{Re}\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde X)$.

Summary

The Symmetric Plain Algebra read over $\mathbb{R}$ is the same product on the same elements, an $\mathbb{R}$-algebra on $\mathbb{R}^8$ in which commutativity holds, associativity fails, the Jordan identity holds, and $e_0$ is a two-sided unit. Its second slot is $\mathbb{C}_{\mathbb{B}}$-linear. It carries the general plain bilinear $B$ form, whose realification has signature (4,4). The passage changes nothing of the product itself; it changes the field, the dimension and the linear structure, and for this product it adds the real reading of the form it carries and of its operators.

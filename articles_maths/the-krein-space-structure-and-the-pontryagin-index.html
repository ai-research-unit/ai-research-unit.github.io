The Krein Space Structure and the Pontryagin Index

Introduction

Provisional. This article is a placeholder of the topology region, created in the dispatch that gives the four forms their own reading groups. It records its scope, its home and its sources; the content is to be written.

The quaternion sesquilinear form

$$ \langle\tilde Q,\tilde P\rangle_{\natural*}=\mathrm{Sc}(\tilde P^{\natural*}\tilde Q)=\sum_\mu\varepsilon_\mu P_\mu\overline{Q_\mu} $$

is a Hermitian indefinite inner product, of signature $(2,6)$ on the real space of dimension eight, so its negative part has dimension $6$ over $\mathbb{R}$ and the algebra is a Pontryagin space $\Pi_{6}$; the rank of its smaller part, $2$, is its Pontryagin index. This article will give the indefinite inner product, the fundamental decomposition, the negativity index and the Pontryagin index, and will place them in the general theory of Krein Spaces and Pontryagin Spaces.

Introduction

Two conditions pick out the generators: anti-Hermitian and traceless. They hold exactly for fifteen matrices, which form su(4)\mathfrak{su}(4)su(4) of real dimension fifteen, and the fifteen split as six real antisymmetric and nine purely imaginary, 6+96+96+9. Each generator KKK gives the derivation [K,⋅][K,\cdot][K,⋅], whose array is the Kronecker difference K⊗I4−I4⊗KTK\otimes\mathrm{I}_4-\mathrm{I}_4\otimes K^{\mathsf T}K⊗I4​−I4​⊗KT, and whose exponential is the conjugation by the rotor U=eK∈SU(4)U=e^{K}\in\mathrm{SU}(4)U=eK∈SU(4).

The article writes the fifteen generators out in full, and it builds them the way the corpus builds every array: from the axis-free matrices LaxfL_{\mathrm{axf}}Laxf​ and RaxfR_{\mathrm{axf}}Raxf​ of Matrix Representations and Conventions, not from a frame chosen for the occasion. The six real generators are the left and right matrices of the three vector units; the nine imaginary ones are iii times their nine products. The Two Conditions on the Generator

The Fifteen Parameters

Proposition (the generators). The matrices K∈M4(C)K\in M_4(\mathbb{C})K∈M4​(C) with K∗=−KK^{*}=-KK∗=−K and tr⁡K=0\operatorname{tr}K=0trK=0 form a real vector space of dimension fifteen, closed under the bracket, isomorphic to su(4)\mathfrak{su}(4)su(4).

Proof. An anti-Hermitian matrix of order four has sixteen real parameters: four imaginary diagonal entries and six complex off-diagonal pairs.

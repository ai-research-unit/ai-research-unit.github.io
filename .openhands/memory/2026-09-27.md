## Second review pass: a real error in the new Bochner mirrors (same session)

Reviewing my own edits (the user said "review"), I found a factual error I had introduced in the two new Bochner

**Root cause of the slip:** the phrase "pure" is ambiguous. The corpus means "zero scalar part" (real +
non-degenerate together). The mirror read it as "pure real". The corpus's own continuous article is correct
("The conjugate and the norm, for a pure root... because ρ̄=−ρ for a pure element" — pure = zero scalar part).

**Fixed:** maths mirror (prose, table, ledger row), physics mirror (prose, table, ledger row), physics mirror

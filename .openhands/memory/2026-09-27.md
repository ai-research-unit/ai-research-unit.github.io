TRAP WORTH REMEMBERING (nearly produced a false defect): the coefficientwise bar of the four complex
coefficients is NOT conjugation of the 2x2 matrix entries. Entrywise conjugation of Phi(Q) is Phi(Q*) --
the star -- so a matrix code that conjugates entries silently implements the star or the quaternion
conjugate, not the bar. Consequences seen in practice: with the star the Jordan-triple witness came out
-e_3 vs +3e_3 (the article's sign inverted) and with the quaternion conjugate the witness came out 2e_3,
while the article's real numbers are LHS e_3, RHS -3e_3, difference +4e_3, counts 480/1024 and
15360/32768. Always implement the bar in coefficient arithmetic on (q0,q1,q2,q3), never as matrix
entrywise conjugation.

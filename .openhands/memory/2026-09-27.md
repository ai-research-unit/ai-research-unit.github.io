## Why the notation existed (user asked)
Fraktur was plain German typeface when Lie theory was built (Lie, Killing, Cartan, Weyl); Dedekind used
`𝔞, 𝔟, 𝔠` for ideals (1871), Hilbert in the Zahlbericht (1897), Weyl `𝔤` for the algebra of `G` (1925). It
survived through Bourbaki/Helgason/Humphreys and LaTeX. No mathematical content — safe to drop.

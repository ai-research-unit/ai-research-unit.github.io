# Internal audit: the presence of $h$ — superseded

**This file is kept for the record and is no longer the audit.** It was written before the ordering was imposed, and its conclusion — that *Relativity*, *Action* and *Fluid Physics and Thermodynamics* "cannot avoid" the Planck constant — is now false for two of the three. Measured on 2026-11-27: *Relativity* carries 0 sites in 93 articles, and *Action* carries 0 sites in the 22 articles before its quantisation section. Only *Fluid* still uses the constant throughout, and it does so because it follows the promotion.

The one sentence of this file worth keeping is its correction of record: **$h$ in a Lagrangian is not a scale, it is the quantisation.** That is exactly why the constant now enters at the promotion and not before it — a classical density does not need a quantum of action, and the article that declares the promotion is the first that does.

The live audit is `articles_internal/audits/audit-h-presence.md`, with the site-by-site inventory in `articles_internal/audits/audit-fine.md` and the measurement in `_reserve/py/audit-h-presence.py`. Those three files are internal, outside the build and outside git, by the rule that governs `articles_internal/`.
